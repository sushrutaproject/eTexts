# Mādhavanidāna: word spacing and verse tagging — spots to check

Word spacing was added in 2026-11 by 54 agents, one per piece of the TEI file
(tools/spacing/, see its README). Each agent reported up to five places it was
unsure of; they are listed below as "NNN l.LINE", where NNN is the piece and
LINE the line within that piece. Use the form quoted to find the place in
madhavanidana-full.xml (search for it; after the avagraha pass te'pi is te 'pi).

Decisions taken across the whole text (the same as for the Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā):
- the editor's notes are not spaced.
- avagraha: a space before ' after e/o everywhere (te 'pi, khaṇḍo 'yaṃ), as in
  the Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha; ā' (tathā'pi, vā'pi) stays joined.
- particles split everywhere: tathā ca, tathā hi, yad vā, na hi, na ca, na tu,
  yady api, ity arthaḥ, ity āha, yad uktaṃ, tad uktaṃ, yad āha, tad yathā …;
  joined: tadanu, tadanantaraṃ, etadanantaraṃ, ityādi and its cases, kintu,
  parantu, athavā, itastataḥ.
- forms where the doubled consonant at a word junction is written once
  (ālasyamanyāstambha…, kaṇḍūraktapitta…) are left joined; typos are left
  uncorrected and are usually left joined.
- commentary lemmata (pratīka) with a fused vowel stay joined (yathetyādi);
  with a consonant junction they are split (kaṭv ityādi, vātād ityādi).

New policy questions for the editor (decided provisionally as stated):
- kiñca is split kiñ ca (26 places), kiṃvā is split kiṃ vā (25 places), by
  analogy with kiṃ ca and yad vā; they could instead stay joined like kintu, athavā.
- ityantena (and other cases of ityanta-) is kept joined, like ityādinā (12 places).
- vāraṃvāram is split vāraṃ vāram as a repeated word; upary upari is split where
  it stands alone, joined inside a compound (uparyuparisaṃsthitaiḥ).
- -vat forms (śukravat, kāṣṭhavat, maṇḍalavat, tadvat …) are split off as adverbs
  before a participle or adjective; several agents flagged that they could be
  compound members.
- pṛthak, prāk, manāk, punar, sadyas before a participle or noun are split or
  joined by context (rule 5), and are treated differently in different places:
  pṛthagdoṣaiḥ ×5 / pṛthag doṣaiḥ ×3, pṛthagabhidhānaṃ ×3 / pṛthag abhidhānaṃ ×2,
  pṛthagupādānaṃ ×1 / pṛthag upādānaṃ ×4, punarukta- ×5 / punar ukta- ×5,
  prāgukta- / prāg ukta-. Left as the agents had them.
- tat/tad/etad before a noun is split or joined by context (rule 5):
  tatpūrvarūpaṃ ×2 / tat pūrvarūpaṃ ×1, tadrūpam / tad rūpam, tadanye / tad anye.
- list items that may be dvandvas are treated differently in different places:
  kṣuttṛṣṇā ×2 / kṣut tṛṣṇā ×1, tvaṅmāṃsaṃ ×4 / tvaṅ māṃsaṃ ×1, hṛduṇḍukaḥ ×3 /
  hṛd uṇḍukaḥ ×1, bahustokam / bahu stokam, ṣaḍarbudāni ×2 / ṣaḍ arbudāni ×1,
  kaṭvamlamuṣṇaṃ / kaṭvamlam uṣṇaṃ. Left so.
- the source has one untransliterated (ASCII) passage in adhyāya 21
  (vyAyAmAder niShiddhakAlAdiShu …); it was spaced like the rest.

Root-text segments the verse tagger left as prose (source references such as
"(su. u. taṃ. a. 39) |", headings, colophons, and about 45 half-verses that do
not scan, mostly hypermetric, defective or elided with "...") are listed in
madhavanidana-prose.txt.
