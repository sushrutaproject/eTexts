pañcanidānalakṣaṇam | praṇamya jagadutpattisthitisaṃhārakāraṇam | svargāpavargayordvāraṃ trailokyaśaraṇaṃ śivam ||1||
śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ| śaśiruciraharārdhavyaktasaktārdhadeho diśatu ghanaghanābhaḥ padmanābhaḥ śriyaṃ vaḥ| tridaśasaridaśītadyotajāvārimadhyabhramibhavamiva nābhau vārijaṃ yasya reje||1|| bhaṭṭārajejjaṭagadādhara 2 vāpyacandraśrīcakrapāṇibakuleśvarasenabhojaiḥ| īśānakārtikasukīra 3 sudhīravaidyairmaitreyamādhavamukhairlikhitaṃ vicintya||2|| tantrāntarāṇyapi vilokya mamaiṣa yatnaḥ sadbhirvidheya iha doṣavidhau samādhiḥ| martyairasarvavidurairvihite kva nāma granthe'sti doṣavirahaḥ sucirantane'pi||3|| tattadgranthatarubhyo 4 vyākhyākusumarasaleśamāhṛtya| bhramareṇeva mayā'yaṃ vyākhyāmadhukośa ārabdhaḥ||4|| upayuktamihānuktaṃ nidānaṃ mādhavena yat| granthavyākhyāprasaṅgena mayā tadapi likhyate||5|| atha prathitasarvāyurvedabodhaviśuddhabuddhiḥ śrīmādhavakaro vikāranikarahetvāditattvabubhutsotsukacikitsakajanānujighṛkṣayā vidhitsitagranthasandarbhārambhe tatpratyūhavyūhavyapohahetuṃ paramāptācāraparamparāpariprāptaṃ sveṣṭadevatāpraṇāmaṃ prāk prāṇaiṣīt; 5 granthaśrotṝṇāmapi viśveśvaramaheśvarasya praṇāmānuvādamātrādapi 6 vighnopaśamo bhavatītyabhiprāyeṇa taṃ granthādau nibaddhavān– praṇamyetyādi| atra praśabdo bhaktyatiśayakhyāpakaḥ| nibandhanakriyāpekṣayā praṇāmasya pūrvakālabhāvitvāt praṇameḥ ktvāpratyayaḥ| jagacchabdenotpattimantaḥ 7 kṣityādayo'bhidhīyante; teṣāmutpattau svakāraṇasamavāye, sthitau katicitkālāvasthāne, saṃhāre pradhvaṃse, kāraṇaṃ kartāram| svargaḥ sukham, apavargo mokṣaḥ ātyantikaduḥkhanivṛttilakṣaṇaḥ, tayordvāramupāyaṃ pradhānakāraṇam| etena sakalapuruṣārthahetutvamuktaṃ, sukhāvāptiduḥkhahānivyatiriktasya puruṣārthasyābhāvāt| ata eva trailokyaśaraṇaṃ trailokyarakṣitāram| na coktajagatsthitikāraṇatvena paunaruktyaṃ, jagacchabdenotpattimatāmevābhidhānāt| atra tu trailokyaśabdo bhuvanatrayavarticetanācetanasamūhavācī, lokaśabdasya bhuvanajanayorabhidhāyakatvāt| tathā cāmaraḥ– “lokastu bhuvane jane” (a. ko. kā. 3 va. 3) iti| trailokyamiti svārthe ṣyañ; 8 cāturvarṇyavat| harādiparyāyān parityajya śivapadanirdeśena granthasya tadadhyetṝṇāṃ ca sakalakalyāṇamabhilaṣan śivapadaṃ nibaddhavān; śivakāritvenaiva maheśvarasya śivapadābhidhānamiti||1||
tulasīkusumaṃ dadhattamālapratimaṃ ballavigopabālasaktam| kimapi tribhavaikamūlakandaṃ paramānandamahaṃ mahaḥ smarāmi||1|| śrīhammīranareśvaraḥ 9 samabhavatprauḍhapratāpānala- jvālājālakarāladagdhapavanaprāgbhāraśuṣkendhanaḥ| yasya prauḍhasabhāṅgaṇeṣvapi yaśaḥsvarlokakallolinī- kallolāmalamaṅganāḥ sumanasāṃ gāyanti romāñcitāḥ||2|| pratyarthikṣitināthasārthavanitāvaidhavyadīkṣāguroḥ śauryaṃ yasya raṇāṅgaṇe suragaṇaiḥ paśyan surāṇāṃ patiḥ| labdhvā netrasahasrajaṃ phalamalaṃ smṛtvā nijābhyarthitaṃ rāmaṃ rāvaṇaghātinaṃ ca samabhūttuṣṭo vilakṣaḥ punaḥ||3|| tasyābhavadvaidyavaraḥ prabhūtaviśvāsapātraṃ viditaścaritraiḥ| pramodanāmā sakalapramodī kalāvilāsaikanidhirdvijendraḥ||4|| jyeṣṭho'bhavattasya sutaḥ praśastaśāstrārthavettā śivasaktacetāḥ| 10 raiśarmanāmā prathitaḥ kalābhirmahanmadakṣoṇipateḥ sabhāyām||5|| anyaśca tasyāsti sutaḥ kanīyānvācaspatiḥ sattvaguṇaikasaṅgaḥ|| sahodaraprītatamaḥ samastā yo vaidyavidyāḥ svayamadhyajīgapat||6|| 11 guruvacanavicāraiścārucañcaccaritraścarakacaturacetāḥ suśrute suśruto yaḥ| kapilamatavayasye brahmavidyārahasye prakaṭitapaṭubuddhirbuddhakāṇādasiddhiḥ||7|| tenojjvalā vivṛtiralpadhiyāṃ hitāya sambaddhyate vibudhamādhavasaṅgrahasya| vijñāya tārkikavaraikavicāravedyaṃ vaiṣamyagumphanavaśānmadhukoṣabandham||8|| tatra granthārambhe prārabdhagranthaparisamāptyarthaṃ granthaśrotṝṇāmapi 12 pratyūhavyūhavyudāsārthaṃ śiṣṭācāraparipālanārthaṃ granthakāraḥ prāgviśiṣṭeṣṭadevatāpraṇāmaṃ nibaddhavān– praṇamyetyādi| yugmam| mayā rogaviniścayo grantho nibaddhyata iti sambandhaḥ| kiṃ kṛtvā? praṇamya| 13 atra prakarṣārthadyotakena praśabdena granthakṛdbhaktyatiśayaṃ khyāpayati| kaṃ? śivam| sakalabhuvanapatermaheśvarasya nāmabāhulye'pi tadadhyāpakādhyetṝṇāṃ kalyāṇamabhilaṣan granthakāraḥ śivapadaṃ nibaddhavān| kīdṛśaṃ śivaṃ? jagadutpattisthitisaṃhārakāraṇam| jagacchabdenotpattimantaḥ pṛthivyādayo'bhidhīyante; teṣāmutpattau svakāraṇasamavāye, sthitau katicitkālāvasthāne, saṃhāre saṃharaṇe, kāraṇaṃ hetum| punaḥ kīdṛśaṃ? svargāpavargayordvāraṃ; svargaḥ sukham, apavargo mokṣaḥ, 14 tayordvāram upāyaṃ pradhānakāraṇam| punaḥ kīdṛśaṃ? trailokyaśaraṇaṃ bhuvanatrayasya rakṣitāram||1||
nānāmunīnāṃ vacanairidānīṃ samāsataḥ sadbhiṣajāṃ niyogāt | sopadravāriṣṭanidānaliṅgo nibadhyate rogaviniścayo'yam ||2|| nānātantravihīnānāṃ bhiṣajāmalpamedhasām | sukhaṃ vijñātumātaṅkamayameva bhaviṣyati ||3||
abhidheyasambandhaprayojanopadeśamantareṇa prekṣāvatāṃ na pravṛttiḥ 15 syāt iti, atastadabhidhānārthaṃ ślokadvayamāha– nānetyādi| rogāṇāṃ viśeṣeṇa 16 vātajatvādisādhyāsādhyatvādirūpeṇa niścayo jñānaṃ yena sa rogaviniścayo grantho nibadhyate'bhidhīyate, ‘asmābhiḥ’ iti śeṣaḥ| tasya viśeṣaṇam– sopadravetyādi| saha upadravādibhirvartate yaḥ sa tathā; 17 etena viśeṣaṇadvāreṇa upadravāriṣṭanidānaliṅgasvarūpamabhidheyamuktaṃ bhavati, 18 etadvyatiriktasyābhidheyasyābhāvāt; tathā sati upadravādibhirabhidheyaiḥ saha granthasya vācyavācakalakṣaṇaḥ sambandho'yabhihitaḥ| tatra, upadravo rogārambhakadoṣaprakopajanyo'nyavikāraḥ, 19 uktaṃ ca carake– “vyādherupari yo vyādhirbhavatyuttarakālajaḥ| upakramāvirodhī ca sa upadrava ucyate||” iti; niyatamaraṇakhyāpakaṃ liṅgamariṣṭaṃ; nidānaṃ rogotpādako hetuḥ; liṅgaṃ rogakhyāpako hetuḥ| tena ‘liṅgyate jñāyate vyādhiranena’ iti pā.[ “liṅgyante jñāyante vyādhayo'nena” iti vyutpattyā pūrvarūparūpopaśayasamprāptayo'bhidhīyante| yadyapi nidānamapi rogaviśeṣaṃ bodhayati, tathā'pyutpattijñaptihetutvena kāraṇadvaividhyapratipādanārthaṃ tasya pṛthagabhidhānam| eṣāṃ copadravādīnāṃ vistarābhidhānaṃ yathāvasaraṃ kariṣyate| nanu, roganidānāditattvamatisūkṣmatvenāsarvajñasya 20 jñānaviṣayaḥ, tat kathaṃ tadupadeśe prekṣāvatāṃ pravṛttirityata āha– nānāmunīnāmityādi| etena granthasya prāmāṇyaṃ pravṛttyaṅgatvamuktaṃ bhavati; munayo hi tapoyogardhibalāttraikālikanikhilajñānaśālinaḥ puruṣātiśayā ucyante| nanu, yadyevambhūtaḥ 21 kasyacidgrantho'nyo'pyāste, tenaiva vyavahārasiddheḥ kṛtakāryatvenāsya niṣprayojanatā syādityata āha– idānīmityādi| idānīmasmābhireva 22 prāthamyena nānāmunīnāṃ vacanairevaṃvidho nibandhaḥ kriyate| samāsata iti saṅkṣepataḥ| 23 etenālpabuddhīnāmativistaratvenāpravṛttyaṅgatādoṣaḥ parihṛto bhavati| nanu, kṛte'pi granthe'nupādeyapuruṣapraṇītatvena na kaścidbhiṣak pravartiṣyata iti granthasya vaiyarthyaṃ syādityata āha– sadbhiṣajāṃ niyogāditi| niyogo niyojanam “asmadupakārāya granthaḥ kriyatām” ityevaṃ prārthanetyarthaḥ; athavā niyoga ājñā, etenātmanaḥ savinayatvamuktaṃ bhavatītyarthaḥ| 24 nānātantretyādi| sukhaṃ yathā bhavati tathā ātaṅkaṃ rogaṃ vijñātumayameva grantho bhaviṣyati, ‘kāraṇam’ iti śeṣaḥ| etena rogajñānaṃ prayojanamityuktaṃ, phalaṃ cāsya cikitsitamiti mantavyam yaduktaṃ carake– “rogamādau parīkṣeta tato'nantaramauṣadham| tataḥ karma bhiṣak paścājjñānapūrvaṃ samācaret||” iti (ca. sū. a. 20)| nanu, nānāmunīnāṃ vacanaireva rogajñānaṃ bhaviṣyati, kimanena tadupajīvinā granthenetyata āha– alpamedhasāmiti alpamedhasām alpabuddhīnām; mahābuddhayo hi 25 ativistaraduradhigamanātantrādhyayanakṣamā bhavanti, na tvalpabuddhaya iti| mahādhiyāmapi ālasyānāsāditadurupapādāśeṣasaṃhitānāmayameva rogajñānāya bhaviṣyatītyāha– nānātantretyādi||2-3||
nānāmunīnāmityādi| ayaṃ rogaviniścayo grantho nibadhyate vidhīyate, rogāṇāṃ viniścayo jñānaṃ yasmātsa tathā| kiṃviśiṣṭaḥ? sopadravāriṣṭanidānaliṅgaḥ; sahopadravādibhirvartate 26 iti sopadravaḥ, upadravo rogārambhakadoṣaprakopajanyo vikāraḥ| uktaṃ ca carake– “vyādherupari yo vyādhirbhavatyuttarakālajaḥ| upakramāvirodhī ca sa upadrava ucyate||” 27 iti; niyatamaraṇakhyāpakaṃ liṅgamariṣṭaṃ; nidānaṃ rogotpādako hetuḥ, niścīyate 28 pratipādyate vyādhiraneneti nidānaṃ; liṅgaṃ rogakhyāpako hetuḥ, tena liṅgyate jñāyate'neneti vyutpattyā pūrvarūparūpopaśayasamprāptayo vijñāyante iti| nanu, sarvajñajñānaviṣaye paramasūkṣmanidānāditattvajñāne'sarvajñapuruṣavākyaiḥ kathaṃ buddhimatāṃ pravṛttirityata āha– nānāmunīnāṃ 29 vacanairiti| yogardhibalāttraikālikajñānadarśī 30 puruṣātiśayo munirucyate| idānīmiti– asmābhireva prādhānyena nānāmunīnāṃ vacanairevaṃvidho granthaḥ kriyate; anyairācāryairevaṃvidho grantho na kṛta ityarthaḥ| samāsata iti saṅkṣepāt| nanu, kṛte'pi granthe na kaścidbhiṣak pravartiṣyata iti granthasya vaiyarthyaṃ syādityata āha– sadbhiṣajāṃ 31 niyogāditi| niyogo niyojanam, asmadupakārāya granthaḥ kriyatāmevambhūtā prārthanetyarthaḥ 32 granthasyāsyotkṛṣṭatvamāha– nānetyādi| mahābuddhayo hi duradhigamatantrādhyayanakṣamā bhavanti, ato nānātantravihīnānāmalpabuddhīnāṃ bhiṣajāṃ sukhaṃ yathā bhavati tathā ātaṅkaṃ rogaṃ vijñātumayameva grantho 33 bhaviṣyatīti; ‘kāraṇam’ iti śeṣaḥ| 34 uktaṃ ca– “rogajñānārthamevādau yatnaḥ kāryo bhiṣagvaraiḥ| sati tasmin kriyārambhaḥ puṇyāya yaśase śriyai||” rogamādau parīkṣeta tato'nantaramauṣadham| tataḥ karma bhiṣak paścājjñānapūrvaṃ samācaret iti||2-3||
nidānaṃ pūrvarūpāṇi rūpāṇyupaśayastathā | samprāptiśceti vijñānaṃ rogāṇāṃ pañcadhā smṛtam ||4|| (vā. ni. a. 1) |
vyādherjñātavyasya 35 pañca jñānopāyā bhavantīti tānāha– nidānamityādi| ete pañca vyastāḥ samastāśca vyādhibodhakāḥ| na ca samastapakṣe kṛtakaraṇatvaṃ vācyaṃ, 36 pramāṇasamplavasyāpi dṛṣṭatvāt| ( 37 anumitavahnau pratyakṣopamānaśabdānāmapi sthitisattvāt) na yo 38 hyanumānena pratīto vahniḥ sa eva pratyakṣāgamābhyāṃ nopalabhyate [ 39 na vā nidānādīni prekṣāvanto ye naivamālocayeyuḥ; ( 40 kintu ye prekṣāvante nidānādīni syurityeva vetsyanti) 41 ekenaiva pratipādito vyādhiriti vayamihodāsmahe ( 42 na manyāmahe)| kiñca, 43 ekena pratipādite'pi vyādhāvapare'vaśyamabhidhātavyāḥ, bhinnaprayojanatvāt| tathāhi– yadi nidānaṃ nocyate tadā tatparivarjanaṃ kathaṃ labhyate? uktaṃ hi suśrute– “ 44 vātādīnāṃ pratīkāraḥ prokto vistarataḥ punaḥ| saṅkṣepataḥ kriyāyogo nidānaparivarjanam||” iti (su. u. taṃ. a. 1)| kiñca, yathā– mṛdbhakṣaṇāt pāṇḍurogaḥ, makṣikābhakṣaṇācca chardiravasīyate; na 45 tu tathā nidānena sarvatra niyatarogādhyavasāyaḥ, 46 jvaragulmādīnāmekakāraṇatvāt| yadāha carakaḥ– “eko heturanekasya tathaikasyaika eva hi| vyādherekasya 47 bahavo bahūnāṃ bahavastathā||” iti (ca. ni. a. 8)| api ca kadācit pratyāsannaṃ nidānaṃ bādhitvā viprakṛṣṭanidānakṛto doṣasañcayo vyādhiṃ kuryāt, tasmāt 48 kevalānnidānānna vyādhijñānaṃ bhavatīti pūrvarūpādīnāmupādānamiti 49 vāpyacandraḥ| asati pūrvarūpābhidhāne tatroktaḥ kriyāviśeṣo na saṅgacchate| uktaṃ hi carake– “ 50 jvarasya pūrvarūpe laghvaśanamapatarpaṇaṃ vā||” iti (ca. ni. a. 1)| tathā ca suśrute– 51 vātikajvarapūrvarūpe ghṛtapānamiti| tathā'sādhyatvaṃ ca nopalabhyeta| uktaṃ ca carake– “pūrvarūpāṇi sarvāṇi jvaroktānyatimātrayā| yaṃ viśanti viśatyenaṃ mṛtyurjvarapuraḥsaraḥ|| anyasyāpi ca rogasya pūrvarūpāṇi yaṃ naram| viśantyanena kalpena tasyāpi maraṇaṃ dhruvam||” (ca. i. a. 5) iti| tathā raktapittapramehayorviśeṣajñānaṃ ca na jāyate| uktaṃ ca carake– “hāridravarṇaṃ rudhiraṃ ca mūtraṃ vinā pramehasya hi pūrvarūpaiḥ| yo mūtrayettaṃ na vadet pramehaṃ raktasya pittasya hi sa prakopaḥ||” iti (ca. ci. a. 6)| asati rūpābhidhāne 52 vyādheraśeṣeṇa svarūpameva na vyavacchidyate; kiṃ ca sādhyāsādhyatvaṃ ca na jñāyate| tathā hi sukhasādhyalakṣaṇe carakaḥ– “hetavaḥ pūrvarūpāṇi rūpāṇyalpāni yasya vai| na ca tulyaguṇo dūṣyo na doṣaḥ prakṛtirbhavet||” iti (ca. sū. a. 10); kaṣṭasādhyalakṣaṇe carakaḥ– “nimittapūrvarūpāṇāṃ rūpāṇāṃ madhyame bale|” (ca. sū. a. 10) iti; tathā– “sarvasampūrṇalakṣaṇaḥ| sannipātajvaro'sādhyaḥ” (ca. sū. a. 3) iti| asatyupaśayābhidhāne saṅkīrṇalakṣaṇe'nabhivyaktaklaṣaṇe vā vyādhau viśeṣabodho na syāt| taduktaṃ carake– “gūḍhaliṅgaṃ vyādhimupaśayānupaśayābhyāṃ” (parīkṣeta) (ca. vi. a. 4) iti| asatyāṃ ca samprāptau pūrvarūpādipratītasyāpi vyādheścikitsopayoginoṃ'śāṃśavikalpanābalakālāderapratīteścikitsāviśeṣo na syāt| tasmāt pañcāpi nidānādayo vaktavyāḥ| pañcavidhamapyetadvyādhyutpattijñaptihetubhūtaṃ nidānaśabdenocyate| yadāha suśrutaḥ– “hetulakṣaṇanirdeśānnidānāni” (su. sū. a. 3) iti| tatraivaṃ 53 nidānaśabdaniruktiḥ– “nirdiśyate vyādhiraneneti nidānaṃ”; “diśeḥ pṛṣodarāditvādrūpasiddhiḥ|” iti gadādharaḥ; “niścitya dīyate pratipādyate vyādhiraneneti nidānam||” iti jejjaṭaḥ| bhaṭṭāraharicandreṇāpi 54 tantrayuktyādivivaraṇaprastāve eṣaiva niruktiruktā| niśabdo niścaye| tathā ca vararucerupasargasūtram– “ni niścayaniṣedhayoḥ” iti| loke'pi ‘adya te nidānaṃ kariṣyāmi’ ityukte niścayaṃ 55 kariṣyāmītyavagamyate| nidānamiti karaṇe lyuṭ; tena ‘vyādhiniścayakaraṇaṃ nidānam’ iti nidānādipañcakasāmānyalakṣaṇam| nidānaśabdo'yaṃ 56 nidānaviśeṣe jātau ca vartate; yathā– tṛṇaśabdaḥ tṛṇaviśeṣe tṛṇajātau ca vartate| yattu, 57 bhaṭṭāraharicandreṇa nidānasthāne– “yā gauḥ sudohā bhavati na tāṃ 58 nidadīta|” iti vyāsaprayogamupanyasya nibandhārtho nidānaśabdo vyākhyātaḥ ‘nidīyate nibadhyate 59 hetvādisambaddho vyādhiranena’ iti kṛtvā;
