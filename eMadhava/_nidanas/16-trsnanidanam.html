chardestṛṣṇopadravatvācchardyanantaraṃ tṛṣṇānidānaṃ, tasyāḥ samprāptimāha– 7 bhayaśramābhyāmityādi| pittavivardhanaiḥ kaṭvamloṣṇādibhiḥ krodhopavāsādibhiśca svasthāne hṛdi sañcitaṃ pittaṃ, vātaśca bhayaśramabalakṣayaiḥ kupitaḥ, ūrdhvaṃ prasaran pipāsāṃ janayet| tāluprapannamiti savātaṃ pittaṃ tālugatamityarthaḥ| tālvityupalakṣaṇam; tena klomagatamapi boddhavyaṃ, 8 tasya pipāsāsthānatvāt| uktaṃ ca carake– “rasavāhinīśca dhamanīrjihvāmūlagalatālukaklomnaḥ| saṃśoṣya nṛṇāṃ dehe kurutastṛṣāmatibalau tau tu” iti| annakaphajāmajānāṃ samprāptimāha– srotaḥsvityādi| nanu srotaḥsviti bahuvacanaṃ viruddhaṃ yaduktaṃ suśrute– “dve udakavahe” iti| maivaṃ, tayorevānekapratānayogāditi| 9 annakaphāmaiḥ, duṣṭikartṛtvādduṣṭadoṣasambandhādannāmayorapi doṣatvamiti gayadāsaḥ| tisra iti vātādibhiḥ pratyekaṃ, kṣatajeti kṣatanimittā vraṇināṃ yā bhavati, caturthītyanena coktānāṃ tisṛṇāṃ 10 sādhyatvaṃ bodhayati, anyāsāṃ tu kaṣṭasādhyatvamiti| kṣayādanyā pañcamī, āmasamudbhavā ṣaṣṭhī, bhaktodbhavā saptamiketi, krameṇa tāsāṃ lakṣaṇāni jānīhi||1-2||
