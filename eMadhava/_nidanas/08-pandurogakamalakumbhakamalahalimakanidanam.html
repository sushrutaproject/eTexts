--- title: "8. pāṇḍurogakāmalākumbhakāmalāhalīmakanidānam" adhyaya: 8 order: 8 ---
atha pāṇḍurogakāmalākumbhakāmalāhalīmakanidānam | pāṇḍurogāḥ smṛtāḥ pañca vātapittakaphaistrayaḥ | caturthaḥ sannipātena pañcamo bhakṣaṇānmṛdaḥ ||1|| (ca. ci. a. 16) |
purīṣajāḥ krimayaḥ sūkṣmāḥ pāṇḍutāṃ janayanti, ataḥ krimeranantaraṃ pāṇḍurogamāha– pāṇḍurogā ityādi| pāṇḍutvenopalakṣito rogaḥ pāṇḍurogaḥ| carake aṣṭodarīyādhyāye “pañca pāṇḍurogāḥ” (ca. sū. a. 19)ityabhidhāyāpi “pāṇḍurogāḥ smṛtāḥ pañca” (ca. ci. a. 16) iti yadetatpunaścarakavacanaṃ tat “pañcānāmapi sādhyatvaṃ bodhayati, natu pañconmādeṣviva sānnipātikasyāsādhyatvamiti” jejjaṭaḥ; “nyūnasaṅkhyāvyavacchedārthamiti” cakraḥ| nanu, suśrute hi mṛttikājo na paṭhitaḥ; mṛttikā'pi doṣaprakopadvāreṇaiva pāṇḍurogaṃ janayatīti; yaduktam– “kaṣāyā mārutaṃ, pittamūṣarā, madhurā kapham|” (ca. ci. a. 16) iti; nidānabhedācca rogabhede rogānantyaprasaṅgaḥ, vātajasyāpi rūkṣaśītādyanekavātanidānakupitavātajanyatvāt| ucyate– doṣajatvāviśeṣe'pi viśiṣṭarūpacikitsāpratipādanārthaṃ pṛthagabhidhānaṃ, 1 mūtrāntravṛddhivat; suśrutena tu parādhikāreṣu na vistaroktirityabhiprāyeṇa na pṛthakkṛtaḥ| cikitsā tu 2 doṣacikitsayā'pi bhavatīti||1||
purīṣajāḥ kṛmayo sūkṣmāḥ pāṇḍutāṃ janayanti, ataḥ kṛmeranantaraṃ pāṇḍurogamāha pāṇḍurogā ityādi| pāṇḍutvenopalakṣyate rogaḥ pāṇḍurogaḥ| sa ca pañcavidhaḥ– vātapittakaphaistrayaḥ, sannipātenaikaḥ, mṛdbhakṣaṇādekaḥ, evaṃ pañca| nanu, suśrutena mṛttikājanito na paṭhitaḥ, yato mṛttikā'pi 3 tridoṣakopakāraṇenaiva pāṇḍurogaṃ janayati, uktaṃ ca– “kaṣāyā mārutaṃ pittamūṣarā madhurā kapham” iti, yadi nidānabhedādrogabhedastadā rogāṇāmānantyaprasaṅgaḥ? ucyate– 4 doṣajatvāviśeṣe'pi viśiṣṭarūpacikitsāpratipādanārthaṃ pṛthagabhidhānam||1|| 5
vyāyāmamamlaṃ lavaṇāni madyaṃ mṛdaṃ divāsvapnamatīva tīkṣṇam | 6 niṣevamāṇasya pradūṣya raktaṃ doṣāstvacaṃ pāṇḍuratāṃ nayanti ||2|| (su. u. taṃ. a. 44) |
samprāptimāha– vyāyāmamityādi| raktamityupalakṣaṇaṃ, tena ‘tvaṅmāṃsamapi’ dūṣyatvena dṛḍhabalena paṭhitaṃ, hārītena raso'pi, iti||2||
pāṇḍuroganidānamāha– 7 vyavāyamityādi| vyavāyādīni niṣevamāṇasya puṃso duṣṭā doṣā raktaṃ 8 vidūṣya tvaci pāṇḍubhāvaṃ kurvantīti piṇḍārthaḥ| 9 raktamityupalakṣaṇaṃ; tvacaṃ māṃsamapi dūṣayantīti dṛḍhabalena paṭhitaṃ, hārītena tathoktatvāt| vyavāyaḥ strīsevā, anye ‘vyāyāmam’ iti paṭhanti| lavaṇānīti bahuvacanena kṣārāṇyapi boddhavyāni| mṛdaṃ mṛttikām| tīkṣṇaṃ rājikādi||2||
tvaksphoṭanaṣṭhīvanagātrasādamṛdbhakṣaṇaprekṣaṇakūṭaśothāḥ | viṇmūtrapītatvamathāvipāko bhaviṣyatastasya puraḥsarāṇi ||3|| (su. u. taṃ. a. 44) |
pūrvarūpamāha– tvagityādi| tvaksphoṭanaṃ tvacaḥ kiñcidvidāraṇam| mṛdbhakṣaṇaṃ mṛdbhakṣaṇecchā| prekṣaṇakūṭaśotho akṣigolake śothaḥ| avipāka āhārasya| puraḥsarāṇi pūrvarūpāṇi||3||
pūrvarūpamāha– tvagityādi| tvaksphoṭanaṃ tvacaḥ kiñcidvidāraṇam| anye ‘tvakpāṭaniṣṭhīvanagātrasādāḥ’ iti paṭhanti, tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ| niṣṭhīvanaṃ mukhena, ‘thugathugīti’ loke| mṛdbhakṣaṇaṃ mṛdbhakṣaṇecchā, prekṣaṇakūṭaśothaḥ akṣigolakaśothaḥ, avipāka āhārasya, puraḥsarāṇi pūrvarūpāṇi, tasya bhāvinaḥ bhavanti||3||
tvaṅmūtranayanādīnāṃ 10 rūkṣakṛṣṇāruṇābhatāḥ | vātapāṇḍvāmaye todakampānāhabhramādayaḥ ||4||
vātikalakṣaṇamāha– tvagityādi| atra kṛṣṇāruṇābhatā na pāṇḍutāmatikrāmati, anyathā pāṇḍurogatvābhāvaḥ| uktaṃ ca suśrute– “sarveṣu caiteṣviha pāṇḍubhāvo yato'dhiko'taḥ khula pāṇḍurogaḥ|” (su. u. taṃ. a. 44) iti| bhramādaya iti ādiśabdena bhedaśūlādīnāṃ grahaṇam||4||
vātikapāṇḍurogamāha– tvagityādi| kṛṣṇāruṇaprabhā pāṇḍutvamatikramya na bhavati, anyathā pāṇḍurogatvābhāvāt| uktaṃ hi suśrute– “sarveṣu caiteṣviha pāṇḍubhāvo yato'dhiko'taḥ khalu pāṇḍurogaḥ” iti| todaḥ pīḍābhedaḥ, ānāha udarāṭopaḥ, bhramaścakrārūḍhasyeva| ādigrahaṇādbhedaśūlādīnāṃ grahaṇam||4||
pītamūtraśakṛnnetro dāhatṛṣṇājvarānvitaḥ | bhinnaviṭko'tipītābhaḥ pittapāṇḍvāmayī naraḥ ||5||
paittikalakṣaṇamāha– pītetyādi| nanu, pittapāṇḍvāmayīti vaktuṃ na yuktaṃ, pāṇḍurogasya pittakāryatvādeva| ucyate– itaradoṣāsaṃśliṣṭaprabalapittajanyatvena paittikābhidhānaṃ, yathā– paittikaraktapittamiti||5||
paittikamāha– pītetyādi| bhinnaviṭkaḥ sadravamalaḥ, atipītābhaḥ atiśayena pītaḥ| nanu pittapāṇḍvāmayīti vaktuṃ na yuktaṃ, pāṇḍurogasya| ucyate, itaradoṣāsaṃsṛṣṭaprabalapittajanyatvena paittikābhidhānaṃ, paittikaraktapittavat||5||
kaphaprasekaśvayathutandrālasyātigauravaiḥ | pāṇḍurogī kaphācchuklaistvaṅmūtranayanānanaiḥ ||6||
ślaiṣmikalakṣaṇamāha– kaphapraseketyādi| kaphādyaḥ pāṇḍurogī sa śuklaistvaṅmūtranayanānanairupalakṣita iti yojyam||6||
ślaiṣmikamāha– kaphetyādi| kahāttu yaḥ pāṇḍurogī sa śuklaiḥ tvabdhūtranayanādibhirupalakṣyate yojyam| kaphaprasekaḥ kaphasrāvaḥ, sānnipātikastu samasamavetairvātādilakṣaṇaireva bodhyaḥ| uktaṃ hi carake “sarvānnasevinaḥ sarve duṣṭā doṣāstridoṣajam| tridoṣaliṅgaṃ kurvanti pāṇḍurogaṃ suduḥsaham” iti||6||
jvarārocakahṛllāsaccharditṛṣṇāklamānvitaḥ | pāṇḍurogī tribhirdoṣaistyājyaḥ kṣīṇo hatendriyaḥ ||7||
sānnipātikastu 11 prakṛtisamasamavetatvenauktavātajādilakṣaṇaireva boddhavyaḥ| uktaṃ hi carake– “sarvānnasevinaḥ sarve duṣṭā doṣāstridoṣajam| triliṅgaṃ samprakurvanti pāṇḍurogaṃ suduḥsaham||” (ca. ci. a. 16) iti| tasyaiva sopadravasyāsādhyatvamāha– jvarārocaketyādi| hatendriyaḥ svaviṣayāgrāhakendriyaḥ||7||
tasyaiva sopadravasyāsādhyatvamāha– jvaretyādi| tridoṣajaḥ pāṇḍurogam| etairupadravairyuktastyājyaḥ asādhya ityarthaḥ| hatendriyaḥ svaviṣayāgrāhakendriyaḥ||7||
mṛttikādanaśīlasya kupyatyanyatamo malaḥ | kaṣāyā mārutaṃ, pittamūṣarā, madhurā kapham ||8|| kopayenmṛdrasādīṃśca raukṣyādbhuktaṃ ca rūkṣayet | pūrayatyavipakvaiva srotāṃsi niruṇaddhyapi ||9|| indriyāṇāṃ balaṃ hatvā tejo vīryaujasī tathā | pāṇḍurogaṃ karotyāśu balavarṇāgnināśanam ||10|| (ca. ci. a. 16) |
mṛjjasamprāptimāha– mṛttiketyādi| anyatamo malo vātādiḥ| ūṣarā sakṣārā| rasādīn rūkṣayet, bhuktaṃ 12 ca rūkṣayediti yojyam| 13 raukṣyāt prākṛtikodbhūtaraukṣyaguṇāt| avipakvaiva koṣṭhadhātvagnibhiḥ pākaṃ na gatvaiva, srotāṃsi rasavahādīni, pūrayati ruṇaddhi ca| indriyāṇāṃ balaṃ svaviṣayagrahaṇaśaktim| “tejo dīptiḥ” iti jejjaṭaḥ, ‘ūṣmā’ iti cakraḥ| vīryaṃ śaktiḥ| ‘ojaḥ 14 sarvadhātusāraḥ, 15 sarvadhātusārabhūtaṃ hṛdayastham’ iti parāśaraḥ, ‘parābhibhavecchā’ iti jejjaṭaḥ||8-10||
mṛttikājanitapāṇḍurogamāha– 16 mṛttiketyādi| mṛttikābhakṣaṇaśīlasya puruṣasya anyatamo malo doṣo vātādiḥ kopaṃ gacchati| mṛttikāyāstridoṣaprakopane hetuṃ darśayati– 17 kaṣāyetyādi| kaṣāyā mṛttikā mārutaṃ prakopayet, ūṣarā sakṣārā pittam, madhurā kaphaṃ, raukṣyācca rasādīn dhātūn rūkṣayet bhuktaṃ ca rūkṣayet, tasyāḥ svabhāvodbhūtarūkṣaguṇatvāt| apakvaivetyādi na koṣṭhadhātvagnibhiḥ pākaṃ gacchati kintu apakvaiva rasavāhīni srotāṃsi pūrayati niruṇaddhi ca| indriyāṇāṃ balaṃ svaviṣayagrahaṇaśaktiṃ hatvā pāṇḍurogaṃ karoti| tejo dīptiriti jejjaṭaḥ, ūṣmeti cakraḥ| vīryaṃ śaktiḥ, ojaḥ sarvadhātusārabhūtaṃ hṛdayasthaṃ, parābhibhaveccheti jejjaṭaḥ| balavarṇāgnināśanamityatra balaṃ śarīre sāmarthyam||8-10||
śūnākṣikūṭagaṇḍabhrūḥ śūnapānnābhimehanaḥ | krimikoṣṭho'tisāryeta malaṃ sāsṛkkaphānvitam ||11|| (ca. ci. a. 16) |
mṛjjasya lakṣaṇamāha-śūnākṣītyādi| ‘sarvapāṇḍurogeṣu krimikoṣṭhatā yadā syāttadaitallakṣaṇam’ iti jejjaṭaḥ, mṛttikājānantarapaṭhitatvena tasyaiva lakṣaṇamityanye| videhe tu paṭhyate– “mṛdbhakṣaṇādbhavetpāṇḍustandrālasyanipīḍitaḥ| saśvāsakāsaśoṣārśaḥ 18 sādārucisamanvitaḥ|| śūnapādānanakaraḥ kṛśāṅgaḥ kṛśapāvakaḥ|” iti||11||
mṛjjanitasyopadravamāha– śūnetyādi| akṣikūṭo'kṣigolakaḥ, gaṇḍaḥ kapolaḥ, eteṣu śvayathuḥ| tathā śūnapānnābhimehanaḥ śūnaścaraṇo nābhirmehanaṃ ca yasya sa tathā| tathā ca kṛmikoṣṭhaḥ puruṣaḥ, sarudhiraṃ kaphaṃ purīṣamatisāryeta| 19 sarvapāṇḍurogeṣu kṛmikoṣṭhatā yadā syāttadaitallakṣaṇamiti jejjaṭaḥ; mṛttikājānantaraṃ paṭhitatvāttasyaiva lakṣaṇamityanye| uktaṃ ca videhena– 20 “mṛdbhakṣaṇādbhavetpāṇḍustandrālasyanipīḍitaḥ| saśvāsakāsaśothārśaḥ 21 sādārucisamanvitaḥ|| śūnapādānanakaraḥ kṛṣṇāṅgaḥ kṛśapāvakaḥ” iti||11||
pāṇḍurogaścirotpannaḥ kharībhūto na sidhyati | 22 kālaprakarṣācchūnānāṃ yo vā pītāni paśyati ||12|| baddhālpaviṭ saharitaṃ sakaphaṃ yo'tisāryate | dīnaḥ śvetātidigdhāṅgaśchardimūrchātṛḍarditaḥ ||13|| sa nāstyasṛkkṣayādyaśca pāṇḍuḥ śvetatvamāpnuyāt | (ca. ci. a. 16) | pāṇḍudantanakho yastu pāṇḍunetraśca yo bhavet | pāṇḍusaṅghātadarśī ca pāṇḍurogī vinaśyati ||14|| (su. sū. a. 33) | anteṣu śūnaṃ parihīṇamadhyaṃ mlānaṃ tathā'nteṣu ca madhyaśūnam | gude ca śephasyatha muṣkayośca śūnaṃ pratāmyantamasañjñakalpam | vivarjayetpāṇḍukinaṃ yaśo'rthī tathā'tisārajvarapīḍitaṃ ca ||15|| (su. u. taṃ. a. 44) |
asādhyalakṣaṇamāha– pāṇḍuroga ityādi| ‘pāṇḍurogaścirotpannaḥ kālaprakarṣāt kharībhūto jaṭharatāṃ gato na sidhyati, acirotpanno'pi śūnānāṃ madhye yo vā pītāni paśyati sa 23 pāṇḍurogī na sidhyatītyaparamasādhyalakṣaṇam’ iti jejjaṭasya yojanā| 24 cakrastvāha– ‘cirotpannaḥ kharībhūto'tyartharūkṣitasarvadhāturna sidhyati, tathā kālaprakarṣādityādinā'paramasādhyalakṣaṇam’ iti| atra śūnānāṃ śothavatāṃ madhye yo vā pītāni paśyati sa na sidhyatīti| ‘śūno nā’ iti pāṭhāntare nā puruṣaḥ| ‘śūnāṅgo yo vā pītāni paśyati’ iti pāṭhāntaraṃ sugamam| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– baddhetyādi| atra sakaphatve'pi baddhatvālpatvaharitatvāni vyādhiprabhāvāt; baddhālpasthāne bahulamiti pāṭhāntaram| viṭśabdo 25 napuṃsako'pyastītyetannirdeśādevonneyamityāhuḥ| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– dīna ityādi| dīnaḥ glānaḥ| śvetātidigdhāṅga iti śvetavarṇaliptāṅga ivetyarthaḥ| sa nāsti naṣṭa iva asādhya ityarthaḥ| aparamāha– asṛgityāda| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– pāṇḍudantetyādi| pāṇḍusaṅghātadarśīti nayanaraśmisahacaritaṃ bahirnirgataṃ pittaṃ sampiṇḍitaṃ paśyati| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– anteṣvityādi| anteṣu bāhujaṅghāśiraḥsu, 26 śūnaṃ śothayuktam| parihīṇamadhyaṃ durbalamadhyadeham| etadvaiparītyenāparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– mlānamityādi| mlānaṃ durbalam| asañjñakalpaṃ mṛtaprāyam| evaṃvidhaṃ pāṇḍukinaṃ pāṇḍurogiṇaṃ yaśo'rthī vaidyo vivarjayediti| atra sauśrutaśloke ‘pāṇḍukinam’ ityatra “ 27 pālakinam” iti pāṭhāntaraṃ, yuktaṃ caitat; evaṃ hi 28 paṭhyamāne pāṇḍurogāvasthāviśeṣasya pālakino lakṣaṇamapi kṛtaṃ syāt| uktaṃ hi suśrute– “ 29 sakāmalāpālakipāṇḍurogaḥ kumbhāhvayo” lāghavako'lasākhyaḥ| (su. u. taṃ. a. 44) iti| anenaivābhiprāyeṇa kaścidabhiyukto likhitavān– “ante śūnaḥ kṛśo madhye'nyathā ca gudaśephasi| śūno 30 jvarātisārārto bhṛtakalpastu pālakī||” iti||12-15||
asādhyalakṣaṇamāha– pāṇḍvityādi| pāṇḍurogaścirotpannaḥ kālaprakarṣāccirakālātkharībhūto jaṭharatāṃ gato na sidhyati, acirotpanno'pi pāṇḍurogaḥ śūnāṅgo yo vā puruṣaḥ pītāni paśyati sa pāṇḍurogī na sidhyati, ityaparamasādhyalakṣaṇamiti jejjaṭaḥ| cakrastvanyathā vyācaṣṭe– cirotpannaḥ kharībhūto 31 atyarthā virūkṣitasarvadhāturna sidhyati, tathā kālaprakarṣādityādinā aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamiti| atra ‘śūnānāṃ yo vā pītāni paśyati’ iti pāṭhāntare śūnānāṃ śothavatāṃ madhye yaḥ pītāni paśyati sa na sidhyati, anye ‘śūno nā’ iti paṭhanti, tatra nā puruṣaḥ| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– baddhetyādi| atra sakaphatve'pi ca bahutvamalpatvaṃ haritatvaṃ 32 vyāpisvabhāvāt| haritaṃ haritaśākavarṇam| anye bahvalpasthāne bahulamiti pāṭhaṃ paṭhanti| bahulaṃ viṭ sāryate iti viṭśabdo napuṃsako'pyastītyetannirdeśādunneyamityāhuḥ| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– dīna ityādi| dīno glānaḥ| śvetātidigdhāṅga iti śvetavarṇātiliptāṅga ityarthaḥ| sa puruṣo nāsti naṣṭaḥ asādhya ityarthaḥ| atraivāparamapyasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– asṛkkṣayāt yaḥ pāṇḍurogī śvetatvamāpnuyātso'pyasādhyaḥ| aparamapyasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– pāṇḍvityādi| nayanaraśmisahacaritaṃ bahirnirgataṃ pittaṃ tadyogādakhilamapi paśyan vastu pītavarṇapiṇḍitaṃ paśyati| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– anteṣvityādi| bāhu jaṅghāśirassu śūnaṃ śothayuktaṃ, parihīṇamadhyaṃ durbalamadhyadehaṃ, 33 glānamityādinā paramasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– 34 glānaṃ durbalaṃ madhyadehaṃ śūnaṃ śvayathuyuktaṃ yasya sa tādṛśastam| aparamasādhyalakṣaṇamāha– gude śephasi muṣkayośca śūnaṃ śothayuktaṃ 35 pratāmyantaṃ mohamupagacchantaṃ mūrchantamityarthaḥ| asañjñakalpaṃ mṛtaprāyam| evaṃvidhaṃ pāṇḍurogiṇaṃ yaśorthī bhiṣak vivarjayet| suśrute asmin śloke pāṇḍukinamityatra pālakinamiti paṭhanti, yuktaṃ caitat| evaṃ hi paṭhyamāne pāṇḍurogāvasthāviśeṣasya pālakino lakṣaṇamapyuktaṃ syāt| yaduktaṃ suśrute– “sakāmalāpālakipāṇḍurogaḥ kumbhāhvayo lāghaviko'lasākhyaḥ” iti| anenaivābhiprāyeṇa kaścidabhiyukto likhitavān “ante śūnaḥ kṛśo madhye tvatha vā gudaśephasi| śūno jvarātisārādyairmṛtakalpastu pālakī” iti||12-15||
