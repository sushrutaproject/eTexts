--- title: "64. mūḍhagarbhanidānam" adhyaya: 64 order: 64 ---
atha mūḍhagarbhanidānam | bhayābhighātāttīkṣṇoṣṇapānāśananiṣevaṇāt | garbhe patati raktasya saśūlaṃ darśanaṃ bhavet ||1|| 1
yonisthānavikārānuvṛtteḥ strīroganidānārambhaḥ| tatra garbhapātanidānamāha– bhayābhighātādityādi| etaccopalakṣaṇaṃ, tenānye'pi suśrutoktā grāmyadharmayānavāhanapatanaskhalanādayo boddhavyāḥ| patatīti sraṃsamāne, tena srāvapātayorapi saśūlaṃ raktadarśanaṃ bhavati, etattu pūrvarūpamiti darśayati||1||
atha strīvikārādhikārātstrīroganidānam| tatra garbhapātahetumāha– bhayetyādi| etaccopalakṣaṇam| tenānye'pi suśrutoktā grāmyadharmayānavāhanapatanaskhalanādayo grāhyāḥ| patatīti sraṃsamāne, saśūlaṃ raktadarśanaṃ 2 bhavatīti pūrvarūpakathanam||1||
ācaturthāttato māsāt prasravedgarbhavidravaḥ | tataḥ sthiraśarīrasya pātaḥ pañcamaṣaṣṭhayoḥ ||2|| (su. ni. a. 8) |
etayoḥ kālabhedamāha– ācaturthādityādi| garbhavidrava iti anatighanāvayavatvena viśeṣeṇa dravarūpatayā garbhavidravo bhaṇyate; 3 srāvo nātighanatvāt, pātastu ghanatvāt| yattu bhoje'bhihitam– “ātṛtīyāttato māsādgarbhaḥ sravati śoṇitam| ūrdhvaṃ 4 saṅghātabhūtastu garbhaḥ patati yoṣitām||” iti| 5 saṅghātabhūtaḥ komalāṅgaḥ, piṇḍitāvastha ityarthaḥ| ata eva suśrute caturthamāse'pyadṛḍhatvāt srāvaḥ kathitaḥ| sthiraśarīrasyeti kaṭhinaśarīrāvayavasya| pañcamaṣaṣṭhayoriti saptame 6 anuguṇajanane jīvadarśanāyoktaṃ, 7 viguṇajanane tu 8 saptamādimāseṣvapi garbhapātaḥ| anye tu pañcamaṣaṣṭhayoreva pātaḥ, saptamādiṣu doṣavaiguṇyāddhi prasava iti ācāryaprāmāṇyādvyavahārācca manyante||2||
srāvapātayoridānīṃ kālaniyamamāha– ācaturthādityādi| anatighanāvayavatvena viśeṣeṇa dravarūpatayā garbhavidravo bhaṇyate| yaduktaṃ bhoje– “ātṛtīyāttato māsādgarbhaḥ sravati śoṇitam| ūrdhvaṃ saṅghātabhūtastu garbhaḥ patati yoṣitām||” iti| taccaturthe māsi saṃyāvarūpatayā anabhivyaktadṛṣṭāṅgatayā suśrutena srāvapakṣe nikṣiptam| sthiraśarīrasyeti kaṭhinaśarīrāvayavasya| pañcamaṣaṣṭhayoriti saptame anuguṇajanane jīvanadarśanāyoktiḥ| viguṇajanane tu 9 saptamādimāseṣvapi pātaḥ| saptamādiṣu tu doṣavaiguṇyāddhi prasava iti ācāryaprāmāṇyādvyavahārācca manyante||2||
garbho'bhighātaviṣamāśanapīḍanādyaiḥ pakvaṃ drumādiva phalaṃ patati kṣaṇena |3|
garbhasyākālapāte nidānapūrvakaṃ dṛṣṭāntamāha– garbho'bhighātetyādi| pakvaṃ drumādiveti dṛṣṭāntenaiva darśayati– yathā vṛntalagnaṃ pakvaphalamabhighātenākāla eva patati, tathoktahetubhirakāle garbhapātaḥ|3|–
garbhasyācirapātaṃ sadṛṣṭāntamāha– garbha ityādi| pūrṇasarvāvayavastu prasavaḥ samucite kāle|3|–
mūḍhaḥ karoti pavanaḥ khalu mūḍhagarbhaṃ śūlaṃ ca yonijaṭharādiṣu mūtrasaṅgam ||3||
ucitaprasavakāle yathā mūḍho garbhaḥ syāttadāha– mūḍhaḥ karotītyādi| mūḍho vyāsaktagatiḥ, śūlaṃ ca yonijaṭharādiṣu 10 ‘karoti’ iti śeṣaḥ; 11 mūtrasaṅgamityatra karotīti sambadhyate||3||
yathā mūḍho garbho bhavati tadāha– mūḍha ityādi| mūḍho vyāsaktagatiḥ, 12 mūḍhagarbhaṃ karoti, yonijaṭharādiṣu śūlaṃ ca karoti, mūtrasaṅgaṃ ca karotīti sambadhyate||3||
bhugno'nilena viguṇena tataḥ sa garbhaḥ saṅkhyāmatītya bahudhā samupaiti yonim | dvāraṃ nirudhya śirasā jaṭhareṇa kaścit kaściccharīraparivartitakubjadehaḥ ||4|| ekena kaścidaparastu bhujadvayena tiryaggato bhavati kaścidavāṅmukho'nyaḥ | pārśvāpavṛttagatireti tathaiva kaścidityaṣṭadhā gatiriyaṃ hyaparā caturdhā ||5||
viguṇānilatvādasaṅkhyeyatve'pi 13 viśiṣṭā aṣṭau gatīrāha– bhugno'nilenetyādi| bhugno viguṇīkṛtaḥ| bahudheti kathitaprakārādapyadhikaṃ darśayati| 14 dvāraṃ nirudhya śirasetyekaḥ prakāraḥ, śirasā vipulena dvāraṃ yonimukhaṃ pidhāya lagno bhavatītyarthaḥ| jaṭhareṇa kaścidityaparaḥ, jaṭhareṇodareṇa yonidvāraṃ pidhāya sakto bhavatītyarthaḥ| kaścit śarīraparivartitakubjadeha iti śarīraparivartanena kubjadehaḥ kaścit sakto bhavati, 15 anenāntaḥkubjapṛṣṭhakubjayoḥ parigrahaḥ| ekeneti bāhunā, ayaṃ caturthaḥ| tiryaggata iti argalāyamānaḥ| kaścidavāṅmukho'nya iti 16 grīvābhaṅgādadhaḥ saṃlagnaḥ| pārśvāpavṛttagatiriti pārśvabhaṅgena 17 viguṇīkṛtaḥ; pārśvanata iti yāvat| etīti svasthānādapaiti, tathaiveti avāṅmukhaḥ san| 18 suśrute'pyaṣṭadhā gatireva paṭhyate| yathā– “kaściddvābhyāṃ sakthibhyāṃ yonimukhaṃ prapadyate, kaścidābhugnaikasakthirekena sakthnā, kaścidābhugnasakthiśarīraḥ sphigdeśena 19 tiryagāgataḥ, kaścidudarapṛṣṭhapārśvānāmanyatamena yonidvāraṃ pidhāyāvatiṣṭhate, antaḥpārśvāpavṛttaśirāḥ kaścidekena bāhunā, 20 kaścidābhugnaśirā bāhudvayena, kaścidābhugnamadhyo hastapādaśirobhiḥ, kaścidekena sakthnā yonimukhamabhiprapadyate apareṇa pāyum|” (su. ni. a. 8) iti||4-5|| 21
viguṇānilayogādasaṅkhyeyāṃ gatimāha– bhugna ityādi| bhugno viparītīkṛtaḥ, bahudheti 22 uktaprakārādadhikam| tatra vyavahārayogyānaṣṭau prakārānāha– dvāramiti| vipulena śirasā yonimukhaṃ pidhāya lagno bhavati, ityekaḥ prakāraḥ| aparo dhmātena jaṭhareṇodareṇa 23 yonikubjamukhaṃ pidhāya sakto bhavatīti dvitīyaḥ prakāraḥ| kaścit śarīraparivartanena viparītavartanena 24 dehaḥ sakto bhavati, anena cāntaḥkubjapṛṣṭhakubjayoḥ parigrahaḥ, ayaṃ tṛtīyaḥ prakāraḥ| kaścidekena bāhunā iti caturthaḥ prakāraḥ| aparo bāhudvayeneti pañcamaḥ prakāraḥ| pārśvāpavṛttagatiriti pārśvabhaṅgena viguṇīkṛtaḥ pārśvanata iti yāvat| etīti svasthānādapaiti| tathaivetyavāṅmukhaḥ san, evamaṣṭadhā gatirbhavati| garbhasya caturaḥ prakārān punarāha– apyaparāścaturdhā ityādi| saṅkīlakādibhedena aparā vakṣyamāṇāścatuḥprakārāḥ||4-5||
saṅkīlakaḥ pratikhuraḥ parigho'tha bījasteṣūrdhvabāhucaraṇaiḥ śirasā ca yonim | saṅgī ca yo bhavati kīlakavat sa kīlo dṛśyaiḥ khuraiḥ pratikhuraṃ sa hi kāyasaṅgī | gacchedbhujadvayaśirāḥ sa ca bījakākhyo yonau sthitaḥ sa parighaḥ parigheṇa tulyaḥ ||6|| 25
ityaṣṭavidhā gatīḥ pradarśya catuḥprakāreṇa 26 ye gativiśeṣāḥ 27 kathitāstānāha– saṅkīlaka ityādi| 28 samyak kīlavat saṅkīlakaḥ, svārthe kan| tenordhvabāhucaraṇaśirobhiḥ kīlakavallagno 29 yonyāṃ saṅkīlakaḥ| 30 pṛṣṭhena yonyāṃ tathaitadviparītena dṛśyaiḥ hastapādaśirobhiḥ pratikhuraḥ khurasādharmyāt; khuraśabdena hastapādāvucyete| gacchedbhujadvayaśirā iti 31 bhujadvayopahitaṃ śiro yasya sa 32 tathābhūtaḥ san yo gacchet sa bījakaḥ kāyasaṅgī| bhoje'pyetā gatayaḥ paṭhyante| tathāhi– “ūrdhvabāhuśiraḥpādo rundhyādyonimukhaṃ tu yaḥ| pratikīlopamasthityā 33 sa ca kīlakasañjñitaḥ|| 34 adhastāt pārśvato vā'pi tathaivākuñcito'pi vā| yo niḥsṛtya mukhaṃ yonerjñeyaḥ pratikhurastu saḥ|| yonidvārāttu nirgacchedyaścaikaḥ saśirobhujaḥ| tamāhurbījakaṃ nāma 35 mūḍhagarbhacikitsakāḥ|| yonimāvṛtya yastiṣṭhet parigho gopuraṃ yathā| 36 tathā'ntargarbhamāyāntaṃ vidyāt parighasañjñitam||” iti||6||
tānāha– saṅkīlaka ityādi| ūrdhvabāhucaraṇaśirobhiḥ kīlitaiḥ yonyāṃ kāyalagnaḥ saṅkīlakaḥ| yonimiti yoniṃ pratikhurairiti khurasādharmyātkhuraśabdena hastapādā ucyante, tena hastapādaiḥ bahirgataiḥ pratikhura ucyate, sa pratikhuraḥ kāyasaṅgī niruddhakāyo bhavati| bījakamāha– gacchedityādi| bhujadvayopahitaṃ śiro yasya sa tathābhūtaḥ san yo gacchet sa bījakaḥ| parighamāha– yo yonimāvṛtya tiṣṭhetsa parighaḥ| parigheṇa tulya iti| parigho'rgalādaṇḍaḥ| bhoje'pyetā gatayaḥ paṭhyante– “ūrdhvabāhuśiraḥpādo rujanyonimukhaṃ tu yaḥ| pratikīlopamaḥ sthitvā saṅkīla iti sañjñitaḥ|| adhaḥpārśvagato vāpi tathaivākuñcito'pi vā| yo niḥsṛtya mukhaṃ yonerjñeyaḥ pratikhurastu saḥ|| yonidvārāttu nirgacchedyaścaikāṃsaśirobhujaḥ| tamāhurbījakaṃ nāma mūḍhagarbhacikitsakāḥ| yonimāvṛtya yastiṣṭhetparigho gopuraṃ yathā| tathā'ntargarbhamāyāntaṃ vidyātparighasañjñitam||” 37 iti||6||
apaviddhaśirā yā tu śītāṅgī nirapatrapā | 38 nīlodgatasirā hanti sā garbhaṃ sa ca tāṃ tathā ||7|| (su. ni. a. 8) |
asādhyamūḍhagarbhagarbhiṇyorlakṣaṇamāha– apaviddhaśirā yā tvityādi| apaviddhaśirā śiro dhārayitumaśaktetyarthaḥ, avanataśirā iti gadādharaḥ| nirapatrapā lajjāśūnyā| 39 nīlodgatasirā iti 40 nīlavarṇā udgatāḥ sirāḥ kukṣau yasyāḥ sā tathā| sa ceti garbhaḥ||7||
asādhyamūḍhagarbhagarbhiṇyorlakṣaṇamāha– apaviddhetyādi| apaviddhaśirāḥ śiro dhārayitumaśaktā| avanataśirā iti gadādharaḥ| nirapatrapā lajjārahitā| nīlodgatasireti nīlā udgagatāḥ 41 sirāḥ kukṣau yasyāḥ sā tathā, sā garbhaṃ hanti sa ceti 42 garbhastāmityarthaḥ||7||
garbhāspandanamāvīnāṃ praṇāśaḥ śyāvapāṇḍutā | bhaveducchvāsapūtitvaṃ śūnatā'ntarmṛte śiśau ||8|| (su. ni. a. 8) |
mṛtagarbhalakṣaṇamāha 43 garbhāspandanamityādi| aspandanaṃ niścalatvaṃ, 44 jīvato garbhasyāvayavacalanaṃ bhavati| āvīnāṃ praṇāśaḥ prasavavedanānāmabhāvaḥ; athavā āvīśabdena prasavaliṅgānyucyante, tāni 45 ca mūtrakaphaprasekādīni, teṣāṃ nāśaḥ| śūnateti ucchūnatā, antargatasya mūḍhagarbhasyādhmāpanena||8||
atha garbhiṇīrakṣārthaṃ mṛtasya mūḍhagarbhasya paripīḍyākarṣaṇārthaṃ lakṣaṇamāha– garbhetyādi| garbhāspandanaṃ garbhasyā'calatvam| āvīnāṃ praṇāśa iti prasavavedanānāmabhāvaḥ, athavā āvīśabdena prasavaliṅgāni bhaṇyante, tāni ca mūtrakaphaprasekādīni, teṣāṃ nāśaḥ| śūnateti uddhatatā, mṛtasya garbhasyādhmāpanena||8||
mānasāgantubhirmāturupatāpaiḥ prapīḍitaḥ | garbho vyāpadyate kukṣau vyādhibhiśca nipīḍitaḥ ||9|| (su. ni. a. 8) | 46
tasyāntargatasya mānasāgantuduḥkhavyādhibhedena dvividhaṃ maraṇahetumāha– mānasāgantubhirityādi| upatāpaiḥ duḥkhaiḥ||9||
tasyāntarmṛtasya mānasāgantuduṣṭavyādhibhedena dvividhaṃ hetumāha– mānasetyādi| upatāpairiti duḥkhaiḥ, tathāgantubhirapi pīḍitaḥ||9||
yonisaṃvaraṇaṃ saṅgaḥ kukṣau makkalla eva ca | hanyuḥ striyaṃ mūḍhagarbhāṃ yathoktāścāpyupadravāḥ ||10|| vāyuḥ prakupitaḥ kuryāt saṃrudhya rudhiraṃ srutam | sūtāyā hṛcchirobastiśūlaṃ makkallasañjñakam ||11||
aparamasādhyagarbhiṇīlakṣaṇamāha– yonisaṃvaraṇamityādi| yonisaṃvaraṇaṃ tantrāntarapaṭhito rogaḥ| tathāhi– “vātalānyannapānāni grāmyadharmaṃ prajāgaram| atyarthaṃ sevamānāyā garbhiṇyā yonimārgagaḥ|| mātariśvā prakupito yonidvārasya saṃvṛtim| kurute ruddhamārgatvāt punarantargato'nilaḥ|| niruṇaddhyāśayadvāraṃ pīḍayan 47 garbhasaṃsthitim| niruddhavadanocchvāso garbhaścāśu vipadyate|| baddhāṃ saṃruddhahṛdayāṃ 48 nāśayatyāśu garbhiṇīm| yonisaṃvaraṇaṃ vidyādvyādhimenaṃ sudāruṇam|| antakapratimaṃ ghoraṃ nārabheta cikitsitam|” iti| saṅgaḥ kukṣāviti yonisaṃvaraṇe 49 pratinivṛtto vāyurgarbhāśayaṃ yadā niruṇaddhi tadā garbhaḥ kukṣau sakto bhavati| 50 sa ucyate– saṅgaḥ kukṣāviti| makkallo raktamārutajaḥ śūlaviśeṣaḥ| yadyapi prasūtāyāḥ śūlaṃ makkallamuktaṃ 51 suśrute– “ 52 prajātāyāścottarakālaṃ tīkṣṇairaviśodhitaṃ raktaṃ makkallaṃ karoti|” (su. śā. a. 10) iti, tathā'pi prajātāyāśceti cakāreṇāprajātāyā api śūlaṃ makkallamiti| yathoktāścāpyupadravā iti yathoktā ye ye uktāste punarākṣepakaśvāsādayaḥ||10-11||
aparamasādhyagarbhiṇīlakṣaṇamāha– yonītyādi| yonisaṃvaraṇe sati pratinivṛtto vāyurgarbhāśayadvāraṃ yadā ruṇaddhi, tadā garbhaḥ kukṣau sakto bhavati sa ucyate– saṅgaḥ kukṣāviti| makkallo raktamārutajaḥ śūlaviśeṣaḥ| yonisaṃvaraṇaṃ tantrāntare rogaviśeṣaḥ| yathā “vātalānyannapānāni grāmyadharmaṃ prajāgaram| atyarthaṃ sevamānāyāṃ 53 garbhiṇyā yonimārgagaḥ|| mātariśvā prakupito yonidvārasya saṃvṛtim| kurute ruddhamārgatvātpunarantargato'nilaḥ| niruṇaddhyāśayadvāraṃ pīḍayan garbhasaṃsthitam| niruddhavadanocchvāso garbhaścāśu vipadyate|| vipannaḥ śūnasarvāṅgaḥ sarvāṇyevāyanāni ca| baddhāṃ saṃruddhahṛdayāṃ nāśayatyāśu garbhiṇīm|| yonisaṃvaraṇaṃ nāma vyādhimenaṃ sudāruṇam| antakapratimaṃ ghoraṃ nārabheta cikitsitam||” iti| yathoktāścāpyupadravāste ca punarākṣepakakāsaśvāsādayaḥ| “pūtigandhi tathā svedo jihvā tālu ca śuṣyati| vepate bhrāmyati tathā jīvitaṃ coparudhyate|| etairliṅgairvijānīyānmūḍhagarbhaṃ cikitsakaḥ||” makkallamāha– vāyuḥ prakupita ityādi| yadyapi prasūtāyāḥ śūlaṃ makkallamuktam| yaduktaṃ suśrute– “prajātāyāścottarakālaṃ tīkṣṇairavaśoṣitaṃ raktaṃ makkalaṃ karoti” iti, tathāpi prajātāyāśceti cakāreṇāprajātāyā api śūlaṃ makkallamiti||10-11||
iti śrīmādhavakaraviracite mādhavanidāne mūḍhagarbhanidānaṃ samāptam ||64||
iti śrīkaṇṭhadattakṛtāyāṃ madhukośavyākhyāyāṃ mūḍhagarbhanidānaṃ samāptam||64|| 54
iti śrīvācaspativaidyaviracitāyāṃ mādhavanidānavyākhyāyāmātaṅkadarpaṇākhyāyāṃ mūḍhagarbhanidānam||64||
