Sa. stokasattvasya puṃsaḥ kleśairviṣayotthaiḥ pīḍyamānasya rāgiṇaḥ-kāminaḥ, śarīrakṣayarakṣārthaṃ vājīkaraṇmucyate| anena śāstrakṛtā, aparaiśca tantrakāraiḥ|

Sa. kalpa(lya)sya-samarthasya, tathā yauvanasthasya, vājīkaraṇaṃ satataṃ kurvataḥ, tasya sarveṣveva ṛtuṣu dine dine vyavāyo maithunaṃ, na niṣidhyate|

Sa. atha-kṛtamaṅgalasya, snigdhasya tathā viśuddhasya tathā kṣīrādyāśino yogavit-vaidyaḥ, prāṅh nirūhān sānuvāsanān ghṛtādisaṃyuktān kārayet| tataḥ-anantaraṃ, vājīkarān yogān yojayet| kimbhūtān ? śukrādipradān|
