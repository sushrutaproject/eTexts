----------------------------------------------------| kaphapitte tu pūrvotthaṃ durnāmāśritya kupyataḥ||15|| arśomūle tataḥ śophaḥ kaṇḍūdāhādimān bhavet| sa śīghraṃ pakvabhinno'sya kledayanmūlamarśasaḥ||16|| sravatyajasraṃ gatibhirayamarśobhagandaraḥ| ----------------------------------------------------|17|
Sa. pūrvotthaṃ durnāma samāśritya kaphapitte yadā kupyataḥ, tataḥ-tatkopāt, arśomūle śophaḥ kaṇḍvādimān syāt| saḥ-śophaḥ, pakvabhinno'sya-arśaso, mūlamāśveva kledayan gatibhirajasraṃ sravati| sa śopho'rśobhagandara ucyate|

Sa. sannipātotthaḥ śambukāvartasaṃjñaḥ śambukāvartasamaḥ syāt| asmin-sarvaje bhagandare, rugvegaistīvrairgatayo gudaṃ dārayanti|
----------------------------------------------------| asthileśo'bhyavahṛto māṃsagṛddhyā yadā gudam||18|| kṣiṇoti tiryaṅnirgacchannunmārgaṃ kṣatato gatiḥ| syāttataḥ pūyadīrṇāyāṃ māṃsakothena tatra ca||19|| jāyante kṛmayastasya khādantaḥ parito gudam| vidārayanti nacirādunmārgī kṣatajaśca saḥ||20||
Sa. asthilavo māṃsagārdhyena bhukto yadā tiryak-unmārgaṃ, nirgacchan gudaṃ kṣiṇoti, tadā kṣātādgatiḥ syāt| tataḥ pūyadīrṇāyāṃ gatau māṃsakothena ca tasmin kṛmayo bhavanti| te ca kṛmayaḥ tasya-bhagandariṇo, gudaṃ bhakṣayantaḥ parito vidārayanti śīghrameva| asāvunmārgī kṣatajaścocyate|
teṣu rugdāhakaṇḍvādīn vidyād vraṇaniṣedhataḥ| ----------------------------------------------------|21|
Sa. teṣu-bhagandareṣu, vraṇaniṣedhato-vraṇapratiṣedhāt, rugādīn vidyāt|
----------------------------------------------------| ṣaṭ kṛcchrasādhanāsteṣāṃ, nicayakṣatajau tyajet||21|| pravāhiṇīṃ valīṃ prāptaṃ sevanīṃ vā samāśritam| ----------------------------------------------------|22|

----------------------------------------------------| athāsya piṭikāmeva tathā yatnādupācaret||22|| śudhdyasṛksrutisekādyairyathā pākaṃ na gacchati| ----------------------------------------------------|23|
Sa. athāsya-bhagandarasya, piṭikāmeva-piṭikāvasthāmeva, yatnaṃ kṛtvā śudhdyādibhistathopakramet yathā pākaṃ na prāpnoti|
----------------------------------------------------| pāke punarupasnigdhaṃ sveditaṃ cāvagāhataḥ||23|| yantrayitvā'rśasamiva paśyetsamyagbhagandaram| arvācīnaṃ parācīnamantarmukhabahirmukham||24||
Sa. pāke tu satyupasnigdhamavagāhena ca sveditaṃ tato'rśasamiva yantrayitvā suṣṭhu bhagandaraṃ paśyet-avāṅmukhaṃ parāṅmukhaṃ vā, antarmukhaṃ bahirmukhaṃ vā, ityevaṃ suṣṭhu nirūpya| arvācīnamiti "vibhāṣāñceradik striyām" iti svārthe khaḥ|
athāntarmukhameṣitvā samyak śastreṇa pāṭayet| bahirmukhaṃ ca niḥśeṣaṃ tataḥ kṣāreṇa sādhayet||25|| agninā vā bhiṣak sādhu kṣāreṇaivoṣṭrakandharam| ----------------------------------------------------|26|
Sa. anantaramantarmukhameṣiṇyā eṣitvā samyak śastreṇa pāṭayet| bahirmukhaṃ ca bhagandaraṃ niḥśeṣaṃ pāṭayitvā tataḥ kṣāreṇa sādhayet jvalanena vā| uṣṭrakandharaṃ-uṣṭragrīvaṃ, kṣāreṇaiva sādhu kṛtvā vaidyaḥ sādhayet-na tvagninā|
----------------------------------------------------| nāḍīrekāntarāḥ kṛtvā pāṭayecchataponakam||26|| tāsu rūḍhāsu śoṣāśca, mṛtyurdīrṇe gude'nyathā| ----------------------------------------------------|27|
Sa. śataponakākhyamekāntarā nāḍīḥ kṛtvā pāṭayet| tāsu rūḍhāsu śeṣā api nāḍīḥ pāṭayet| anyathā-ekahelayaiva, gude pāṭite sati mṛtyuḥ syāt|
----------------------------------------------------| parikṣepiṇī cāpyevaṃ nāḍyuktaiḥ kṣārasūtrakaiḥ||27||

----------------------------------------------------| tyaktvopacaryaḥ kṣatajaḥ śalyaṃ śalyavatastataḥ||28|| āhārecca tathā dadyāt kṛmighnaṃ lepabhojanam| piṇḍanāḍyādayaḥ svedāḥ susnigdhā ruji pūjitāḥ||29||
