------------------------------------------------------| teṣāṃ grahīṣyatāṃ rūpaṃ pratataṃ rodanaṃ jvaraḥ||3||

Sa. saṃjñānāśādikaṃ pūyaśoṇitagandhāntaṃ skandāpasmāralakṣaṇam|
ādhmānaṃ pāṇipādasya spandanaṃ phenanirvamaḥ| tṛṇmuṣṭibandhātīsārasvaradainyavivarṇatāḥ||12|| kūjanaṃ stananaṃ chardiḥ kāsahidhmāprajāgarāḥ| oṣṭhadaṃśāṅgasaṅkocastambhabastābhagandhatāḥ||13|| ūrdhvaṃ nirīkṣya hasanaṃ, madhye vinamanaṃ, jvaraḥ| mūrcchaikanetraśophaśca naigameṣagrahākṛtiḥ||14||
Sa. meṣasya naigameṣāparasaṃjñasyādhmānādi ekanetraśophāntaṃ lakṣaṇam|
