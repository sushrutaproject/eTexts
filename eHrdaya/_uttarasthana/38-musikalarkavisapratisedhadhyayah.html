-----------------------------------------------| śunaḥ śleṣmolbaṇāṃ doṣāḥ saṃjñāṃ saṃjñāvahāśritāḥ||8|| muṣṇantaḥ kurvate kṣobhaṃ dhātūnāmatidāruṇam| lālāvānandhabadhiraḥ sarvataḥ so'bhidhāvati||9|| srustapucchahanuskandhaḥ śiroduḥkhī natānanaḥ| -----------------------------------------------|10|
Sa. śunaḥ-sārameyasya, śleṣmādhikā doṣāḥ saṃjñāvahāni srotāṃsyāśritāḥ saṃjñāmapaharanto dhātūnāmatighoraṃ kṣobhaṃ kurvanti| saḥ-śvā, lālāyuto'ndhabadhiro dhvastapucchahanuskandhaḥ śiro duḥkhī natamukhaḥ sarvato dhāvati|
-----------------------------------------------| daṃśastena vidaṣṭasya suptaḥ kṛṣṇaṃ kṣaratyasṛk||10|| hṛcchirorugjvarastambhatṛṣṇāmūrcchādbhavo'nu ca| -----------------------------------------------|11|
Sa. tena-śunā, vidaṣṭasya daṃśaḥ suptaḥ-acetanaḥ, kṛṣṇaṃ raktaṃ sravati| paścād hṛtpīḍādyudbhūtiḥ syāt|
-----------------------------------------------| anenānye'pi boddhavyā vyālā daṃṣṭrāprahāriṇaḥ||11|| śṛgālāśvatarāśvarkṣadvīpivyāghravṛkādayaḥ| -----------------------------------------------|12|
Sa. anena-daṃśādisvarūpeṇa, apare vyālāḥ-duṣṭāḥ, daṃṣṭrāprahāriṇaḥ śṛgālādaya evaṃ vedyāḥ|
-----------------------------------------------| kaṇḍūnistodavaivarṇyasuptikledajvarabhramāḥ||12|| viḍaharāgarukpākaśophagranthivikuñcanam| daṃśāvadaraṇaṃ sphoṭāḥ karṇikā maṇḍalāni ca||13|| sarvatra saviṣe liṅgaṃ, viparītaṃ tu nirviṣe| -----------------------------------------------|14|
Sa. sarvatraiva saviṣe daṃśe kaṇḍvādayo liṅgam| nirviṣe ca daṃśe'to viparītaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ vidyāt|

Sa. yena prāṇinā daṣṭastacceṣṭārutaṃ kurvan mriyate| tameva daṣṭāraṃ, ādarśakādiṣu sahasā paśyan vinaśyati|
-----------------------------------------------| yo'dbhyastrasyedadaṣṭo'pi śabdasaṃsparśadarśanaiḥ||15|| jalasantrāsanāmānaṃ daṣṭaṃ tamapi varjayet| -----------------------------------------------|16|
Sa. yo jalebhyo'daṣṭo'pi santrasyet śabdasaṃsparśadarśanairhetubhiḥ, taṃ jalasantrāsasaṃjñaṃ daṣṭaṃ varjayet|
-----------------------------------------------| ākhunā daṣṭamātrasya daṃśaṃ kāṇḍena dāhayet||16|| darpaṇonāthavā, tīvrarujā syātkarṇikā'nyathā| -----------------------------------------------|17|
Sa. ākhunā daṣṭamātrasya narasya sato daṃśaṃ kāṇḍena dāhayet| darpaṇena vā dāhayet| anyathā-evamadagdhe sati, karṇikā dāruṇapīḍā syāt|
-----------------------------------------------| dagdhaṃ visrāvayeddaṃśaṃ pracchitaṃ ca pralepayet||17|| śirīṣarajanīvakrakuṅkumāmṛtavallibhiḥ| -----------------------------------------------|18|
Sa. daṃśaṃ dagdhaṃ pracchitaṃ visrāvayet| śirīṣabījādibhiḥ pralepayecca|
-----------------------------------------------| agāradhūmamañjiṣṭhārajanīlavaṇottamaiḥ||18|| lepo jayatyākhuviṣaṃ karṇikāyāśca pātanaḥ| -----------------------------------------------|19|

-----------------------------------------------| tato'mlaiḥ kṣālayitvā'nu toyairanu ca lepayet||19|| pālindīśvetakaṭabhībilvamūlaguḍūcibhiḥ| anyaiśca viṣaśophaghnaiḥ sirāṃ vā mokṣayeddrutam||20||
Sa. anantaramamlaiḥ kṣālayitvā'nu jalaiḥ kṣālayet| paścāt pālindyādibhiraparaiśca viṣaghnaiḥ śvayathughnaiśca lepayet| athavā śīghraṃ sirāṃ vyadhayet| pālindī-mālavikā trivṛt|

-----------------------------------------------| kośātakyāḥ śukākhyāyāḥ phalaṃ jīmūtakasya ca||21|| madanasya ca sañcūrṇya dadhnā pītvā viṣaṃ vamet| -----------------------------------------------|22|
Sa. kośātakyādīnāṃ phalāni sañcūrṇya dadhnā pītvā viṣaṃ vamet|
-----------------------------------------------| vacāmadanajīmūtakuṣṭhaṃ va mūtrapeṣitam||22|| pūrvakalpena pātavyaṃ sarvonduraviṣāpaham| virecanaṃ trivṛnnīlītriphalākalka iṣyate||23||
Sa. vacādiṃ vā gomūtreṇa sañcūrṇya pūrvakalpena-dadhnā, pītvā sarvonduraviṣaṃ hanti| virecanaṃ trivṛtādikalka iṣyate|
