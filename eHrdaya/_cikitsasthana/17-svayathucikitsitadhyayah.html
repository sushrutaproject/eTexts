--- title: "17. śvayathucikitsitādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 17 order: 17 ---
Sa. pāṇḍurogacikitsitādanantaraṃ śophacikitsitaṃ vyākhyātuṃ prakramyate| sambandhaścāsya nidāna evokta ityāha-
athātaḥ śvayathucikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ| iti ha smāhurātreyādayo maharṣayaḥ| (gadyasūtre||2|| ) sarvatra sarvāṅgasare doṣaje śvayathau purā| sāme viśoṣito bhuktvā laghu koṣṇāmbhasā pibet||1|| nāgarātiviṣādāruviḍaṅgendrayavoṣaṇam| athavā vijayāśuṇṭhīdevadārupunarnavam||2|| navāyasaṃ vā doṣāḍhyaḥ śuddhyai mūtraharītakīḥ| varākvāthena kaṭukākumbhāyastryūṣaṇāni vā||3|| athavā gugguluṃ tadvajjatu vā śailasambhavam| ---------------------------------------------|4|
Sa. sarvatra doṣaje śvayathau sarvāṅgaje tathā sāme viśoṣitaḥ san laghu bhuktvā koṣṇena jalena nāgarādi pibet| viśoṣaṇamatropavāsaḥ| athavā, vijayādīn koṣṇāmbhasā pibet| doṣabahulo navāyasaṃ-pāṇḍurogoktaṃ (hṛ. ci. 16|14) , pibet| śudhyarthaṃ gomūtreṇa harītakīḥ śīlayet| triphalākvāthena saha kaṭukātrivṛtālohacūrṇatrikaṭukāṇi śīlayet| gugguluṃ vā śilājatu vā triphalākvāthenaiva śīlayet|
---------------------------------------------| mandāgniḥ śīlayedāmagurubhinnavibaddhaviṭ||4|| takraṃ sauvarcalavyoṣakṣaudrayuktaṃ guḍābhayām| takrānupānamathavā tadvadvā guḍanāgaram||5||
Sa. mandāgnirāmagurubhinnavibaddhaviṭ san takraṃ sauvarcalādiyutaṃ śīlayet| guḍayutāmabhayāṃ takrānupānāṃ vā| tathaiva guḍanāgaraṃ takrānupānaṃ śīlayet|
ārdrakaṃ vā samaguḍaṃ prakuñcārdhavivardhitam| paraṃ pañcapalaṃ māsaṃ yūṣakṣīrarasāśanaḥ||6|| gulmodarārśaḥśvayathupramehān śvāsapratiśyālasakāvipākān| sakāmalāśoṣamanovikārān kāsaṃ kaphaṃ caiva jayetprayogaḥ||7||
Sa. ārdrakaṃ vā tulyaguḍaṃ palārdhena pratidinaṃ vardhitaṃ paraṃ pañcapalaṃ yāvat| ekasminneva dine palapañcakapramāṇaṃ syāt, punarapi hrāsayedardhapalaṃ yāvat| evaṃ māsaṃ yāvadeṣa prayogaḥ| yathocitaṃ kaphapittavātādhiko yūṣādibhojī syāt| eṣa ca prayogo gulmādīṃścaturdaśa kaphāntān jayet|
ghṛtamārdrakanāgarasya kalka- svarasābhyāṃ payasā ca sādhayitvā| śvayathukṣavathūdarāgnisādai- rabhibhūto'pi piban bhavatyarogaḥ||8||
Sa. ghṛtamārdrakanāgarasya kalkasvarasābhyāṃ payasā ca sādhayitvā-paktvā, piban śophādibhirabhibhūto'pi nirāmayaḥ syāt| ārdrakanāgaramārdrakamucyate| upajātyaupacchandasike vṛtte|
nirāmo baddhaśamalaḥ pibecchvayathupīḍitaḥ| trikaṭutrivṛtādantīcitrakaiḥ sādhitaṃ payaḥ||9|| mūtraṃ gorvā mahiṣyā vā sakṣīraṃ kṣīrabhojanaḥ| saptāhaṃ māsamathavā syāduṣṭrakṣīravartanaḥ||10||
Sa. nirāmo baddhapurīṣaḥ śophārtaḥ san trivṛtā (trikaṭvā)- dibhiḥ sādhitaṃ payaḥ pibet| gormahiṣyā vā sambandhi mūtraṃ sa[kṣīraṃ] kṣīrabhojanaḥ san pibet| tathā, uṣṭrakṣīrabhojanaḥ pānāhārābhyāṃ vinā saptāhamathavā māsaṃ syāt| uṣṭrakṣīravartana ityatra "kukkuṭyādīnāmaṇḍādiṣu" puṃvadbhāvo vaktavya ityuṣṭrīśabdasya puṃvadbhāvaḥ| keciduṣṭrīkṣīreti jaguḥ|
yavānakaṃ yavakṣāraṃ yavānīṃ pañcakolakam| maricaṃ dāḍimaṃ pāṭhāṃ dhānyakāmamlavetasam||11|| bālabilvaṃ ca karṣāṃśaṃ sādhayetsalilāḍhake| tena pakvo ghṛtaprasthaḥ śophārśogulmamehahā||12||
Sa. yavānakādi karṣāṃśaṃ jalāḍhake pacet| tena-kvāthena, ghṛtaprasthaḥ pakvaḥ śophādihā syāt| brahmādiśabdasya prāyikatvācchophādiśabdottarādapi hanteḥ kvip|
dadhnaścitrakagarbhādvā ghṛtaṃ tattakrasaṃyutam| pakvaṃ sacitrakaṃ tadvadguṇaiḥ-----------------|13|
Sa. citrakagarbhāddadhnaḥ-citrakacūrṇamiśritātkṣīrādyajjātaṃ dadhi tasmānmathyamānāt, tattakreṇa-citrakatakreṇa, saṃyuktaṃ pakvaṃ tadvat-yavānakādighṛtasadṛśaṃ, guṇaiḥ|
---------------------------------------------| ------------------yuñjyācca kālavit||13|| dhānvantaraṃ mahātiktaṃ kalyāṇamabhayāghṛtam| ---------------------------------------------|14|
Sa. kālavit-avasthādoṣādivit, dhānvantaraṃ (hṛ. ci. a. 12|19) , mahātiktaṃ (hṛ. ci. a.19/8) kalyāṇakaṃ (hṛ. u. a.6/27) abhayāghṛtaṃ (hṛ. ci. a. 15|28) yuñjyāt|
---------------------------------------------| daśamūlakaṣāyasya kaṃse pathyāśataṃ pacet||14|| dattvā guḍatulāṃ tasmin lehe dadyādvicūrṇitam| trijātakaṃ trikaṭukaṃ kiñcicca yavaśūkajam||15|| prasthārdhaṃ ca hime kṣaudrāttannihantyupayojitam| pravṛddhaśophajvaramehagulma- kārśyāmavātāmlakaraktapittam| vaivarṇyamūtrānilaśukradoṣa- śvāsāruciplīhagarodaraṃ ca||16||
Sa. daśamūlakaṣāyasyāḍhake pathyāśataṃ pacet| atra ca sāmānyaparibhāṣayā kvāthyadravyasya ca daśamūlasya dvātriṃśat palāni bhavanti| evaṃ ca tantrāntare virodhaḥ| tathā ca tatraivaṃ kathyate, (ca. ci. a. 12|50)- "dvipañcamūlasya tulākaṣāye kaṃse'bhayānāṃ ca śataṃ guḍācca|" iti| tadevaṃ virodhaṃ paśyanto vyākhyāmevaṃvidhāṃ vidvāṃso vidadhati,-daśamūlasya tulā, tasyāḥ kaṣāyo daśamūlakaṣāya iti| mayūravyaṃsakāditvāduttarapadalopi samāsaḥ| evaṃ daśamūlasya palaśataṃ tatkvāthasyāḍhake pathyāśataṃ pacet| guḍatulāṃ dattvā tasmin lehe vicūrṇitaṃ trijātakādi dadyāt| eteṣāṃ ca pramāṇaṃ vāsiṣṭhalehavet (hṛ. ci. a. 3|133) kalpyam| yavakṣārasya karṣamātraṃ deyam| tantrāntare hyuktam-"vyoṣāccatuṣpalaṃ dadyāttripalaṃ trisugandhataḥ| karṣamātraṃ yavakṣārāt" iti| śīte ca sati mākṣikāt prasthārdhaṃ deyam| tat-etat, upayojitaṃ pravṛddhaśophādīn hanti| kharanādastu jagāda-"daśamūlāḍhakamabhayāśatasahitaṃ pācayedapāṃ droṇe| avaśeṣitacaturaṃśe tasmin punaḥ prakṣipet pathyāḥ||" iti| upajātivṛttam|
---------------------------------------------| purāṇayavaśālyannaṃ daśamūlāmbusādhitam||17|| alpamalpapaṭusnehaṃ bhojanaṃ śvayathorhitam| kṣāravyoṣānvitairmaudgaiḥ kaulatthaiḥ sakaṇai rasaiḥ||18|| tathā jāṅgalajaiḥ kūrmagodhāśalyakajairapi| anamlaṃ mathitaṃ pāne madyānyauṣadhavanti ca||19||
Sa. śvayathoḥ purāṇayavādi daśamūlākvāthasādhitamalpaṃ tathā'lpalavaṇasnehaṃ bhojanaṃ hitam| kaiḥ saha ? maudgai rasaiḥ| kimbhūtaiḥ ? kṣāratryūṣaṇayuktaiḥ| tathā kaulatthai rasaiḥ| kimbhūtaiḥ ? sapippalīkaiḥ| tathā ca muniḥ (ca. ci. a. 12|62)- "kulatthayūṣastu sapippalīko maudgastu satryūṣaṇayāvaśūkaḥ| rasastathā jāṅgalaviṣkirāṇāṃ sakūrmagodhāśikhiśallakānām||" iti| atra ca śikhino jāṅgalaviṣkiratvena gṛhītā api pathyataratvapratipādanārthaṃ punarupāttāḥ| tathā'yameva tantrakṛdaṣṭāṅgāvatāre'dhyagīṣṭa (?)-"daśamūlāmbhasā siddhairvyoṣakṣārarajonvitaiḥ| rasairjāṅgalajairmaudnaiḥ kaulatthairmaulakairapi||" iti| tathetyādi| tathā jāṅgalamāṃsajaistathā kacchapādijaiśca| pāne mathitamanamlaṃ madyāni ca sauṣadhāni hitāni|
ajājīśaṭhījīvantīkāravīpauṣkarāgnikaiḥ| bilvamadhyayavakṣāravṛkṣāmlairbadaronmitaiḥ||20|| kṛtā peyā''jyatailābhyāṃ yuktibhṛṣṭā paraṃ hitā| śophātisārahṛdrogagulmārśolpāgnimehinām||21||
Sa. ajājīprabhṛtibhiḥ peyā ghṛtatailābhyāṃ yuktyā bhṝṣṭā'tiśayena śophādimatāṃ hitā|
guṇaistadvacca pāṭhāyāḥ pañcakolena sādhitā| ---------------------------------------------|22|

---------------------------------------------| śaileyakuṣṭhasthauṇeyareṇukāgurupadmakaiḥ||22|| śrīveṣṭakanakhaspṛkkādevadārupriyaṅgubhiḥ| māṃsīmāgādhikāvanyadhānyadhyāmakavālakaiḥ||23|| caturjātakatālīsamustāgandhapalāśakaiḥ| kuryādabhyañjanaṃ tailaṃ lepaṃ snānāya tūdakam||24||

snānaṃ vā nimbavarṣābhūnaktamālārkavāriṇā| ---------------------------------------------|25|

---------------------------------------------| ekāṅgaśophe varṣābhūkaravīrakakiṃśukaiḥ||25|| viśālātriphalārodhranalikādevadārubhiḥ| hiṃsrākośātakīmādrītālaparṇījayantibhiḥ||26|| sthūlakākādanīśālanākulīvṛṣaparṇibhiḥ| vṛdhdyārddhihastikarṇaiśca sukhoṣṇairlepanaṃ hitam||27||
