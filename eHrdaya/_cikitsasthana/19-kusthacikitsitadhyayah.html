Sa. asmādanantaraṃ kuṣṭhacikitsitamārabhyate| sambandhaścāsya nidāna evokta ityāha-
athātaḥ kuṣṭhacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ| iti ha smāhurātreyādayo maharṣayaḥ| (gadyasūtre||2|| )||3||
Sa. tvagraktamāṃsādidūṣaṇāttatsravaṇādāmayasvabhāvācca kāyakārśyaṃ kuṣṭhinaḥ prāyeṇāvaśyaṃ bhāvi| anenaivānusāreṇa vakṣyati (ślo. 16)- "snehairāpyāyayeccainaṃ kuṣṭhaghnairantarā'ntarā|" ityāha-
kuṣṭhinaṃ snehapānena pūrvaṃ sarvamupācaret| ---------------------------------------------|1|

---------------------------------------------| tatra vātottare tailaṃ ghṛtaṃ vā sādhitaṃ hitam||1|| daśamūlāmṛtairaṇḍaśārṅgeṣṭāmeṣaśṛṅgibhiḥ| ---------------------------------------------|2|

---------------------------------------------| paṭolanimbakaṭukādārvīpāṭhādurālabhāḥ||2|| parpaṭaṃ trāyamāṇāṃ ca palāṃśaṃ pācayedapām| dvyāḍhake'ṣṭāṃśaśeṣeṇa tena karṣonmitaistathā||3|| trāyantīmustabhūnimbakaliṅgakaṇacandanaiḥ| sarpiṣo dvādaśapalaṃ pacettattiktakaṃ jayet||4|| pittakuṣṭhaparīsarpapiṭikādāhatṛḍbhramān| kaṇḍupāṇḍvāmayān gaṇḍān duṣṭanāḍīvraṇāpacīḥ||5|| visphoṭavidradhīgulmaśophonmādamadānapi| hṛdrogatimiravyaṅgagrahaṇīśvitrakāmalāḥ||6|| bhagandaramapasmāramudaraṃ pradaraṃ garam| arśo'srapittamanyāṃśca sukṛcchrān pittajān gadān||7||

---------------------------------------------| āragvadhasya mūlena śatakṛtvaḥ śṛtaṃ ghṛtam||13|| piben kuṣṭhaṃ jayatyāśu bhajan sakhadiraṃ jalam| ---------------------------------------------|14|

--------------------------------------------| ebhireva yathāsvaṃ ca snehairabhyañjanaṃ hitam||14||

---------------------------------------------| lalāṭahastapādeṣu śirāścāsya vimokṣayet||15|| pracchānamalpake kuṣṭhe śṛṅgādyāśca yathāyatham| ---------------------------------------------|16|
Sa. lalāṭādiṣu cāsyāturasya balāpekṣī vaidyo śirāṃ vimokṣayet| atyalpe kuṣṭhe pracchānaṃ syāt| yathāyathaṃ-yathāsvaṃ, śṛṅgādyāśca syuḥ|
---------------------------------------------| snehairāpyāyayeccainaṃ kuṣṭhaghnairantarā'ntarā||16|| muktaraktaviriktasya riktakoṣṭhasya kuṣṭhinaḥ| prabhañjanastathā hyasya na syāddehaprabhañjanaḥ||17||

dantyāḍhakamapāṃ droṇe paktvā tena ghṛtaṃ pacet| dhāmārgavapale pītaṃ tadūrdhvādho viśuddhikṛt||21||
Sa. dantyāścatuḥṣaṣṭipalāni jaladroṇe pacet| tena kvāthena rājakośātakīpale ghṛtaṃ pacet| tatpītamūrdhvādho viśuddhikaram|
āvartakītulāṃ droṇe pacedaṣṭāṃśaśeṣitam| tanmūlaistatra niryūhe ghṛtaprasthaṃ vipācayet||22|| pītvā tadekadivasāntaritaṃ sujīrṇe bhuñjīta kodravamasaṃskṛtakāñjikena| kuṣṭhaṃ kilāsamapacīṃ ca vijetumicchan icchan prajāṃ ca vipulāṃ grahaṇaṃ smṛtiṃ ca||23||
Sa. āvartakyāḥ śataṃ droṇe'pāmaṣṭāṃśaśeṣitaṃ pacet| ttrakvāthe, tasyā mūlaiḥ kalkīkṛtairghṛtaprasthaṃ vipācayet| taddhṛtamekadivasāntaritaṃ pītvā suṣṭhu jīrṇe ca ghṛte kodravamasaṃskṛtena kāñjikena saha bhuñjīta| kuṣṭhādīn vijigīṣuḥ, prajāṃ ca vistīrṇāmicchan, grahaṇaṃ smṛtiṃ cābhilaṣan| vasantatilakāvṛttam|
