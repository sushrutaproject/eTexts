# Bhelasaṃhitā: word spacing — spots to check

The text (bhelasamhita.xml) was already partly word-spaced. In 2026-11 the remaining
run-together words were spaced by 14 agents, one per piece of the file (tools/spacing/,
see its README); 4,923 spaces were added. "NNN l.LINE" below = piece and line within
the piece; search for the quoted form in bhelasamhita.xml.

Two things were done differently from the other texts:
- Before the spacing, the heading and opening formula of each chapter ("|| pañcamo '
  dhyāyaḥ || athāto … vyākhyāsyāma iti ha smāha bhagavān ātreyaḥ |"), which the source
  had at the end of the last <l> of the preceding chapter, were moved to the start of
  the first <l> of their own chapter, at all 97 chapter boundaries (at the 7 sthāna
  boundaries with the sthāna title). This is a separate commit. In the lacunose
  chapters everything from the heading onward was moved, including verse fragments.
  3.1.28/3.3.1: the heading "|tṛtīyo ' dhyāyaḥ ||" follows "ity āha bhagavān ātreyaḥ ||"
  without "iti bhele"; it was taken as the heading of chapter 3.3.
- The verse markup was left as it is (one <l xml:id> with <label> and <caesura/> per
  verse; no <lg>); verse.py was not run, and there is no -prose.txt file.

Decisions taken across the whole text (see tools/spacing/INSTRUCTIONS.md):
- only spaces were added; no existing space was removed, even a wrong one (below).
- the 2882 notes are all empty (<note/>). Where a word boundary falls at
  a note the space was put before the note ("pallavāḥ <note/>śvetā"; 1239 places now,
  41 in the source); the 481 places where the source has the space after the note
  are unchanged; 31 notes stand inside a word and have no space.
- bracketed alternative readings are spaced like the text; "+" and "++", lacuna dots,
  "(?)", "Bhṣ…" references and typos (palāśaw, nẏṇām, sūtrasthānanam, ekadaśo,
  aṣṭhamo, ṣaṣṭo) are left as they are.
