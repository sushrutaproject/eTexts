# About this site

This site presents the **Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā** of **Śārṅgadhara**, a
Sanskrit treatise on medicine and pharmacy, together with two
commentaries

## The three texts

The root text is set in larger type at the full measure. Two commentaries
follow it, each set smaller and indented behind a rule: **Āḍhamalla's**
*Dīpikā* and **Kāśīrāma's** *Gūḍhārthadīpikā*. 

## Editor's notes

The source carries the editor's notes (variant readings marked *pā.*,
remarks on the Calcutta printing, passages judged interpolated, etc.)
embedded in the running text. Here they are numbered in the text and
gathered at the foot of each chapter.

## Source

The Sanskrit text originates from the 1931 edition of Paraśurāma Śāstrī, as transcribed for the e-book reading room of the Central Council for
Research in Ayurvedic Sciences (CCRAS), Ministry of Ayush, Government of
India: <http://ccras.res.in/ccras_ebooks/readSam>. 
The NIIHM website is extremely well designed and implemented, and users 
are recommended to use it.  The present, simplified derivative is created for 
long-term archival preservation, stability, and ease of local consultation. 

### Known limitations

- A handful of comments in the source lack the abbreviation. Where their
  place in the sequence (the source gives the *Dīpikā* before the
  *Gūḍhārthadīpikā*) or a colophon naming the author settles the matter,
  they are assigned accordingly; two that remain uncertain (Uttarakhaṇḍa 3
  and 4) are shown in commentary type without a label. One passage of root
  text misfiled among the comments (Uttarakhaṇḍa 11) has been restored to
  the root text, and a stray line from the *Carakasaṃhitā* at the end of
  Pūrvakhaṇḍa 7 has been omitted.
