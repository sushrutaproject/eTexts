athedānīṃ kṛtasnehasvedasya śodhanādhikāraḥ | uktaṃca vāgbhaṭe - ‘snehaklinnāḥ koṣṭhagā dhātugā vā srotolīnā ye ca śākhā'sthisaṃsthāḥ | doṣāḥ svedaiste dravīkṛtya koṣṭhaṃ nītāḥ samyak śuddhibhirnirhriyante’ iti | atra śuddhibhiriti bahuvacanagrahaṇātpañcakarmaṇāṃ grahaṇam | tenā'trādau sthānānurodhātprāk vacanaṃ paścādvirecanādīti | ata evāha -
vamanapradhāno vidhiḥ vamanavidhiḥ śodhanaprakāraḥ | ata evāha - śaratkāle vasante ca prāvṛṭkāle ca dehinām | vamanaṃ recanaṃ caiva kārayetkuśalo bhiṣak || 1 ||
Dī. ūrdhvabhāgadoṣanirharaṇaṃ vamanam, adhobhāgadoṣanirharaṇaṃ virecanam | cakārādvastinasyādayaḥ | vamanādīnāṃ kālamāha - śaratkāle ityādi | śaratkālādiśabdaistatsamīpasādhāraṇakāla upalakṣyate | uktaṃca - ’śrāvaṇe kārtike caiva māsi sādhāraṇe kramāt | grīṣmavarṣāhimacitān vāyvādīnāśu nirharet’ iti | kuśalo bhiṣagiti | doṣadeśakālasātmyaprakṛtivayovahridhātumalaviśeṣajñāne śodhane ca kuśala: iti || 1 ||
Gū. svedānantaraṃ vamanasyoktatvādvamanamāha - tatrādau vamanakālamāha - śaraditi | śaratkāle vasante prāvṛṭkāle eṣu triṣveva ca dehināṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ vamanaṃ recanaṃ ca kārayet | yaḥ kuśalo bhiṣak pravīṇavaidyaḥ sañcayaṃ jñātvā vamanonmukhaṃ doṣaṃ jñātvā, anyathā svasthaviṣaye doṣadarśanāt | tathācoktaṃ granthāntare - ’bhiṣajaḥ śodhanaṃ prāhurvarjyaḥ svasthena sarvadā | pannagasya vadhovaṃkṣṇe doṣasya samudīraṇam’ iti | nanu śaratkāle pittasyā'pi vamanaṃ yuktaṃ vasante kaphasyā'pi vamanaṃ recanaṃ yuktam | dvayoḥ prāvṛṭkāle vā hetutvātkathaṃ vamanaṃ recanaṃ dīyate tatra vātādhikyaṃ kathaṃ syāt ? ucyate - varṣākāle niṣedho, na prāvṛṭkāle | tathācoktam agniveśena - ‘ativiṣṭenātigharme prāvṛḍityabhidhīyate | tatraiva śodhanaṃ pathyaṃ varṣāsu kadācana’ iti | śodhanena sarvadoṣanāśo bhavatītyeke | tathācoktaṃ tīsaṭe - ’prāvṛṭghanātyayavasantadineṣu yasmāt prādurbhavanti pavanādirujastataśca | vātādidoṣavigamācca śarīraśuddhiḥ’ iti | ambudāgamaśaranmadhuvāsareṣu agniveśena triṣveva ca śodhanaṃ nānyeṣu | tathācoktaṃ - ’triṣu tvanuśītatvātpreṣṭhaṃ śeṣeṣu śodhanam | hitaṃ bhavati yasmācca tatra hetuṃ nibodha me’ iti || 1 ||
vāmyāḥ - balavantaṃ kaphavyāptaṃ hṛllāsādinipīḍitam | tathā vamanasātmyaṃ ca dhīracittaṃ ca vāmayet ||2||
Dī. saṃprativamanārhatvamāha - balavantamiti vamanasāmarthyayuktaṃ | kaphavyāptamiti kapholbaṇagadāvyāptahṛllāsaḥ uparisthitivamanatā tadādirogapīḍitamityarthaḥ | ādiśabdāt kāsaśvāsātmapitta1 prabhṛtayaḥ | vamanasātmyamiti | vamanaṃ sātmyaṃ yasya tattathā te | puruṣamiti śeṣaḥ | dhīracittaṃ na kātaramityaryaḥ || 2 ||
Gū. yeṣu vamanaṃ deyaṃ tānāha - hṛllāso hṛdayanirgamaḥ | upasthitavamanamityanye | vamanasātmyaṃ vamanasahajam || 2 || vamanārhā rogāḥ -
vamanārhā rogāḥ - viṣadoṣe stanyadoṣe mando'gnau śrlīpade'rbude | hṛdroga-kuṣṭha-vīsarpa-mehā-jīrṇa-bhrameṣu ca || 3 || vidārikā-pacī-kāsa-śvāsa-pīnasa-vṛddhiṣu | apasmāre jvaronmāde tathā raktātisāriṣu ||4 || nāsā-tālvoṣṭha-pākeṣu karṇasrāve dvijihvake | galaśuṇḍyāmatīsāre pittaśleṣmagade tathā || 5 || medogade'rucau caiva vamanaṃ kārayet bhiṣak |
Dī. atha ye rogā vāmyāstānāha - viṣa iti | nanu apasmārādīnāmanilapradhānatvātkathamatra vamanaṃ yuktam ? ucyate, pāṇḍurogī kṛmivyāpta iti vakṣyamāṇavyāptaśabdena pravṛddhapāṇḍuroge niṣiddhopakrame kaphāvasthāyāṃ ca mṛdu jñeyaṃ | yaduktaṃ ‘pāṇḍuroge bhiṣak kuryādūrdhvādhaḥśodhanaṃ sadā’ iti | atra ūrṣvaśodhanaṃ vamanādikam | adhaḥ śodhanaṃ virecanaṃ | virecanaṃ cātra tīkṣṇameva, ‘vātasya tīkṣṇānyanulomanānī’ti vacanāt || 3-5 ||
Gū. stanyadoṣe mātṛdugdhodbhave | vidārī rogaviśeṣaḥ | spaṣṭamanyat || 3-5 ||
athāvāmyānāha - na vāmanīyastimirī na gulmī nodarī kṛśaḥ || 6 || nātivṛddho garbhiṇīṃ ca na sthūlo na kṣudhāturaḥ | madārto bālako rūkṣaḥ śudhitaśca nirūhitaḥ || 7 || udāvartyūrdhvaraktī ca duśchardī kevalānilī | pāṇḍarogī kṛmivyāptaḥ paṭhanātsvaraghātakaḥ || 8 ||
Dī. timiraprabhṛtibhirvyādhibhirvyāptāḥ puruṣā na vāmanīyā ityabhiprāyaḥ | yataḥ -‘avamyavamanādrogāḥ kṛcchratāṃ yānti dehinaḥ | asādhyatāṃ vā gacchanti naite vāmyā hyatiśrutā’ iti | duśchardirityanena yatsāmānyaṃ tathāpi bahudoṣānvitaśchardyāṃ vamanaṃ kāryaṃ na doṣaḥ | yaduktaṃ suśrute -‘chardiṣu bahudoṣāsu vamanaṃ hitamucyate’ iti || 6-8 ||
Gū. yeṣāṃ vamanaṃ na deyaṃ tānāha - ete timiryādayaḥ na vāmanīyāḥ || 6-8 ||
atha yadyapyete'pyajīrṇādivyāpitāstadā vāmanīyā iti vicāryāha - ete'pyajīrṇavyathitā vāmyā ye viṣapīḍitāḥ | kaphavyāptāśca te vāmyā madhukvāthasya pānataḥ || 9 || sukumāraṃ kṛśaṃ bālaṃ vṛddhaṃ bhīruṃ ca vāmayet | pīttvā yavāgūmākaṇṭhaṃ kṣīratakradadhīni vā || 10 || asātmyaiḥ śleṣmalairbhojyairdoṣānutkliśya dehinaḥ | snigdhasvinnāya vamanaṃ dattaṃ samyak pravartate ||11 ||
Dī. ete pūrvoktāstimiryādayaḥ te yadā ajīrṇavyathitā upaviṣapīḍitāḥ kaphavyāptā vā bhavanti tadā vāmyā ityabhiprāyaḥ | kaphavyāptā iti niyatam | tathāhi - ‘kaphaje vamanaṃ śastaṃ recanaṃ pittaje gade | bastirvātagate kāye āmaye tvāmapācana’miti | kena vāmyāstamāha - madhukvāthasya pānata iti | madhukvātho madhuyaṣṭīkvāthaḥ | yadvā madhu kṣaudraṃ | kvāthaṃ kvathitaṃ jalaṃ | anyadvihitadravyaśṛtaṃ vā prakaraṇatvāt | kaṣāyaścātra sarvaḥ koṣṇaḥ kārya iti carakavacanāt | uṣṇavirodhitvena madhūṣṇaṃ niṣiddhamiti vivakṣāyāṃ na cātra madhūṣṇaṃ viruddhaṃ | yaduktaṃ - ‘pracchardane nirūhe ca madhūṣṇaṃ na nivārayet | alaghvapākamāśveva tayostasmānnivartate’ iti | ata eva yātyadhodoṣamādāya pacyamānaṃ virecanaṃ | guṇotkarṣaṇātdūrdhvamapakvaṃ vamanaṃ punariti | yadā'jīrṇādipīḍitatimirādayo'pi sukumārādiṣu bhavanti tadā vakṣyamāṇaprayogenaiva vāmyā ityata āha - vamanasādhyavikārapīḍitāḥ sukumārādayo'pi yavāgvādīnāmanyatamenākaṇṭhaṃ pāyayitvā vāmayedityarthaḥ | yavāgvapyanena kaphakaratvāt kaphotkleśanārthamityabhiprāyaḥ | yaduktaṃ ‘pāṃśudhānye yathā vṛṣṭiḥ kledayatyatikardamam | tathā śleṣmaṇi saṃvṛddhe yavāgū śleṣmavardhanī’ti | ityabhiprāyaviṣayaṃ vakṣyamāṇenaiva caritārthatvāt | kṣīratakradadhi yathādoṣāpekṣayā vyastaṃ deyaṃ | pūrvamuktaṃ vakṣyamāṇenaiva caritārthatvāttānāha - ‘asātmyaiḥ śleṣmalairbhojyairdoṣānukteśca dehinaḥ | snigdhasvinnāya vamanaṃ dattaṃ samyak pravartate | snigdhasvinnāyeti snehapānapūrvakāya khedapūrvakāya puruṣāyetyarthaḥ | na tu snehasvedāyeti, yataḥ snehapānādekāhamuparatamaparādinā etatsvinnamiti prasiddhiḥ | bhālukinā snehapānāt tryahena vamanaṃ darśitaṃ | tadyathā ‘snigdhasvinnaśarīrasya dadyātpracchardanaṃ tryahāt | daśarātre gate vānte bhūyo dadyādvirecana’miti || 9-11 ||
Gū. eteṣvapi ajīrṇavyathitā vāmyāḥ, kaphavyāptāśca vāmyāḥ | madhūko guḍapuṣpaḥ tatkvāthapānataḥ | kṛśabālavṛddhānāmapi punaruktiviśeṣaniṣedhārthaḥ | vamanādau yadvihitaṃ tadāha - ‘naraḥ śvo vamanaṃ pātā bhuñjīta kaphavardhanam | sujaraṃ dravabhūyiṣṭhaṃ laghu śītaṃ virecanam ||’ iti | yavāgūm ākaṇṭhaṃ pītvā asātmyaiḥ arucyaiḥ śleṣmavardhakai: bhojyairvā doṣānutkleśya dehinaḥ puruṣasya snigdhasvinnāya vamanaṃ dattaṃ samyak pravartate | snigdhāya kṛtaghṛtapānāya sveditāya | nanu snehapānātsvedātkadā vamanaṃ deyam ? tadāha granthāntarāt - atra nāsti | tathāca bheḍaḥ ‘ekāhaṃ parataḥ snehāt sthitvā pracchardanaṃ pibet | bhuktvā grāmyodakairmāṃsairanūpaiśca susaṃskṛtaiḥ | trirātrāt paratastadvat svedātprakledanaṃ pibet iti || 9-11 ||
?madhusaindhavayoḥ prādhānyam - vamaneṣu ca sarveṣu saindhavaṃ madhu2 vā hitam |
Dī. atha vamanadravyeṣu madhusaindhavayorviśeṣeṇaivopādeyatvamāha | madhusaindhavayorabhimantraṇaṃ ca sarvatraiva jñeyaṃ | yaduttaṃ ‘yathāvyādhi ca vihitaṃ madhusaindhavasaṃyutaṃ | koṣṭhaṃ vibhajya bhaiṣajyamātrāṃ maṃtrābhimantritā’mityādi | atroṣṇe'pi kaṣāye madhusaindhave na viruddhe | namanasyāpakvasyaivordhvagamanatvāt | vaidyāstvatra yoge pākasaṃbhavaṃ manyamānā madhu na prakṣipante iti kecit | mantraśca ‘brahmadakṣāśvirudrendre’tyādiśloko jñeyaḥ |
Gū. carakāt vamaneṣu yaddhitaṃ tadāha - vamaneṣu vamanadravyeṣu sarveṣu vakṣyamāṇeṣu saindhavaṃ madhu vā hitam ||
vamanārhaṃ dravyam - bībhatsaṃ vamanaṃ dadyāt viparītaṃ virecanam || 12 ||
Dī. kīdṛśaṃ vamanārhaṃ dravyaṃ deyaṃ tadāha - bībhatsavikṛtarasavarṇaprāyaṃ vamanaṃ vamanadravyamityarthaḥ | tena lālādullāsakaradravyamityabhiprāyaḥ | viparītaṃ virecanamiti viparītaṃ bībhatsadravyāt | tena bhakṣaṇe manojñamavikṛtamityarthaḥ || 12 ||
Gū. vamanaṃ bībhatsaṃ saghṛṇaṃ, virecanaṃ ghṛṇādirahitam || 12 ||
kvāthyakvāthayormānam - kvāthyadravyasya kuḍavaṃ śrapayitvā jalāḍhake | ardhabhāgāvaśiṣṭaṃ ca vamaneṣvavacārayet || 13 ||
Dī. idānīṃ kvāthyakvāthayormānamāha - kvāthyadravyaṃ paṭolavāsānimbaprabhṛtikaṃ, teṣu kuḍavamitaṃ catuḥpalapramāṇaṃ | jalāḍhaka iti catuḥṣaṣṭipalamite pānīye ardhabhāgāvaśiṣṭamiti prasthadvayamitaṃ grāhyamityarthaḥ | ayaṃ parimāṇo'pyaikadaiva pānāśaktatvādvibhajya deyaḥ | kvāthabahutve tu sajalaguṇo yadyayogaḥ syāttadā sa eva punaḥ peya iti vyavahārādayamevāvagantavyaḥ | kaṣāyamapi pūrṇaśarāvaṃ pāpayedityuktaṃ tathehā'pi kaṣāyapānavyavastheti kecit || 13 ||
Gū. vamane kvāthyadravyaparimāṇamāha - kuḍavaṃ catuḥpalam | kvāthyadravyasya jalādeḥ catuḥprasthake śrapayitvā ardhaṃ śritaṃ vamaneṣvavacārayet || 13 ||
bheḍamatena vamane kvāthapānamātrā - kvāthapāne navaprasthā jyeṣṭhā mātrā prakīrtitā | madhyamā ṣaṇmitā proktā triprasthā ca kanīyasī ||14||
Dī. atha kvāthyakvāthādīnāṃ pāne'tra mānamāha - ṣaṇmiteti | ṣaṭprasthā kanīyasī laghvī | etatparimāṇaṃ paścājjalapānasyāhuḥ | anyathā pūrvāparayoravyavasthā syāt | yadvā pūrvaparimāṇaṃ tu yavāgvādīnāṃ pācanārthamabhihitaṃ | paścāt kvāthapānasyetyarthaḥ | tatra yavāgvā dyupakalpayediti pāṭhāntare vṛndoktamātrayā vyavahāraḥ iti cakra: | carake tu ‘madanaphalakaṣāyamātrāṇāṃ pramāṇaṃ tu khalu sarvasaṃśodhanamātrāpramāṇāni pratipuruṣamavekṣitavyāni bhavanti | yāvaddhi yasya saṃśodhanaṃ pītaṃ vaikārikadoṣaharaṇāyopapadyate | na cātiyogāya tāvadasya mātrāpramāṇaṃ veditavyam’ iti (ca.sū.15|12) | tatra vāgbhaṭena koṣṭhaṃ vibhajya bhaiṣajyamātrāmityādivacanenaiva darśitaṃ koṣṭhaśca pratipuruṣaṃ bhinna eva | dṛḍhabalena śarāvamātrā coktā | ekavāraṃ pānārthaṃ jalāḍhakāviśiṣṭaṃ praśastaṃ ca | dvitrivāraṃ pānamityeke | śarāvasya pūrṇatvābhidhānaṃ maṅgalamiti jaijjaṭacakrau iti || 14 ||
Gū. kvāthapāne navaprasthā jyeṣṭhā mātrā madhyamā ṣaṭprasthā triprasthā kanīyasī idaṃ kvāthapānapramāṇam | nanu3 śārṅgadhareṇa kuḍavadravye āḍhakaṃ jalaṃ datvā ardhāvaśiṣṭaṃ kvāthaṃ vamane deyamityuktaṃ punaḥ kvāthasya navaprasthapramāṇā jyeṣṭhā mātrā likhitā tatkathaṃ na virodhaḥ ? ucyate, etatpramāṇe śārṅgadhareṇa bheḍamataṃ likhitaṃ tadā - kaṇṭhapānārthaṃ viṣādau jñeyam | mahākāyaviṣayaṃ vā | tathāca bheḍaḥ ‘navaprasthā tu jyeṣṭhā syānmadhyamā ṣaṭprakīrtitā | niḥkvāthasya traya: prasthā māgadhī tu kanīyasī’ | carake tvevaṃ likhitaṃ ‘madhumadhukasaindhavaphāṇitopahatāṃ madanakalkakaṣāyamātrāṃ pāyayet | madanaphalakaṣāyamātrāpramāṇaṃ tu sarvasaṃśodhanamātrāpramāṇāni ca pratipuruṣāpekṣitavyāni bhavanti yāvadviṣasya śodhanaṃ pītaṃ vaikārikadoṣaharaṇāyopapadyate bhavati na cātiyogāsya tāvadasya mātrā pramāṇaṃ veditavyam’ || 14 ||
kalkādimānam - kalkacūrṇāvalehānāṃ viphalaṃ śreṣṭhamātrayā | madhyamāṃ dvipalāṃ vidyātkanīyā palasaṃmitā ||15||
Dī. aparamapi dravyaviśeṣeṣu mānaviśeṣamāha - iyaṃ tu doṣavyādhīnavekṣya prayoktavyā | kalkaṃ dṛṣadi peṣitam | cūrṇaṃ vastragālitam | avaleho lehyadravyam||15||
Gū. vamane kvāthaparimāṇamuktvā kalkādīnāṃ parimāṇamāha - spaṣṭam || 15 ||
vamanavegāḥ - vamane cā'pi vegāḥ syuraṣṭau pittāntamuttamāḥ | ṣaḍvegā madhyamā vegāścatvārastvavare smṛtāḥ ||16||
Dī. atha vamanasaṃkhyāmāha - vamane tu madhyamottamāvaravegānāṃ ṣaḍaṣṭau catvāro vihitāḥ | iyameva doṣādivyavasthā kalpitā | aṣṭau vegā iti aṣṭavāraṃ yadvāmayedityabhiprāyaḥ | evaṃ ṣaṭcatvāro'vagantavyāḥ | pittānte iti kaphe | athavā pittāntaṃ yāvat ityanena suvāntalakṣaṇaṃ pratipāditameva | yaduktaṃ - ‘pitte kaphasyānumukhaṃ pravṛtte śuddheṣu hṛtkaṇṭhaśiraḥsu cāpi | laghau ca dehe kaphasaṃsrave ca sthite suvātaṃ puruṣaṃ vyavasye’diti || 16 ||

Dī. athedānīṃ vegānāṃ parimāṇamāha - ardhamityanena dviguṇitaṃ jñeyo'yamiti dviguṇitaṃ tathāhi - ‘jaghanyamadhyaprabaleṣu vegāścatvāra iṣṭā vamane ṣaḍaṣṭau | daśaiva te dvitriguṇā vireke svasthastathā śoṇitamokṣaṇe ce’ti || 17 ||
Gū. ardhenādhikena vartamānaṃ trayodaśapalamityucyate || 17 ||
