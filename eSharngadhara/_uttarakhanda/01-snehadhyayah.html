Dī. atha uttarakhaṇḍaṃ vyākhyātuṃ pratijānīte | tatrā'yaṃ sambandhaḥ madhyamakhaṇḍānte rasāyanavājīkaraṇārthaṃ rasā uktāḥ, te cāviśuddhaśarīre na svakāryakaraṇakṣamāḥ; uktaṃ ca vāgbhaṭe - ‘aviśuddhe śarīre hi yukto rāsāyano vidhiḥ | vājīkaro vā maline vastre raṅga ivāphalaḥ’ ( vā. u. 39-4) iti | ’tasmāccharīraśuddhyarthaṃ pañcakarmāṇi yojayet | tāni ca snehasvedābhyāṃ vinā na saṃbhavanti, uktaṃ ca - ’snehasvedāvanabhyasya kuryāt saṃśodhanaṃ tu yaḥ | dāruśuṣkamivāyāme śarīraṃ tasya dīryate (vā. sū. 18-59) iti tasmāt snehā'dhyāyameva prathamaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ --
atha snehapānavidhiḥ |
Dī. snehasya ghṛtādeḥ pānasya vidhirvidhānaṃ, sa ca vidhiścarake yathā sapraśna uktastamāha - ?sāṃkhyaiḥ saṃkhyātasaṃkhyeyaiḥ sahāsīnaṃ punarvasum | jagaddhitārthaṃ papraccha vahniveśaḥ svasaṃśayam || kiṃyonayaḥ kati snehāḥ ke ca snehaguṇāḥ pṛthak | kālānupāne kaḥ kasya katidhā ca vicāraṇāḥ || kati mātrāḥ kathaṃ mānāḥ kā ca keṣūpadiśyate | kaśca kebhyo hitaḥ snehaḥ prakarṣaḥ snehane ca kaḥ || snehyāḥ ke ke ca na snehyāḥ, snigdhā'tisnigdhalakṣaṇam | kiṃ pānātprathamaṃ pīte jīrṇe kiṃ ca hitāhitam || ke mṛdukrūrakoṣṭhāḥ kā vyāpadaḥ siddhayaśca kāḥ | śodhane śamane caiva snehe kā vṛttiriṣyate || nareṣu keṣu vidhinā kena yojyā vicāraṇāḥ | snehasyāpyamitajñāna ! jñānamicchāmi veditum? (ca. sū. 13,1-6) iti | ’tatra saṃkhyānamadhīyante vindate vā te sāṃkhyāḥ, taiḥ; kiṃviśiṣṭaiḥ ? saṃkhyātasaṃkhyeyaiḥ, saṃkhyātā saṃkhyā yaiste saṃkhyātasaṃkhyeyāḥ | jñātajñeyā ityarthaḥ | taiḥ saha āsīnam, uta cāsaṃkhyātasaṃkhyeyaṃ sahāsīnaṃ punarvasum iti pāṭhaḥ. | etaduktaṃ bhavati - sākhyaiḥ sahāsīnaṃ punarvasuṃ saṃkhyāvikārakāyāṃ kathāyāṃ vartamānaṃ tatkālānurūpam agniveśo'pi svasaṃśayaṃ pṛṣṭavān | dvikarmako'yam | tatra ’ kati snehāḥ ? etasyottaramāha -
snehaścaturvidhaḥ prokto ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ tathā vasā | majjā ca -
Dī. sa snehaḥ catuḥprakāraḥ, ghṛtādibhedāt | tatra ghṛtaṃ gavyaṃ śreṣṭhatvāt; tailaṃ tilasnehaḥ; vasā-’śuddhamāṃsasya yaḥ snehaḥ sā vasā parikīrtitā iti | majjā iti majjā mastakamajjā asthimajjā vā | tathāca - ’sthūlā'sthiṣu viśeṣeṇa majjā tvabhyantarāśritā | athetareṣu sarveṣu saraktaṃ meda ucyate’ iti | taduktaṃ carake - ’tilāḥ priyālābhiṣukau bibhītakaścitrābhayairaṇḍamadhūkasarṣapāḥ | kusumbhabilvārukamūlakātasī nikocakākṣoṭakarañjaśigrujāḥ || snehāśayāḥ sthāvarajaṅgamāstathā syurjaṅgamā matsyamṛgāḥ sapakṣiṇaḥ | teṣāṃ dadhi kṣīraghṛtāmiṣaṃ vasā sneheṣu majjā ca tathopadiśyate’ iti | abhayā harītakī, citrā dantī iti || ( ca. sū. 13,8)
Gū. athottarakhaṇḍaḥ procyate | atha pañcakarmādau snehapānaṃ ghṛtaṃ, tailaṃ, vasā, majjā, iti caturvidhaḥ snehaḥ proktaḥ kathitaḥ | caturvidhasneheṣu ghṛtasya mukhyatvādādāvabhidhānaṃ, tatastailaṃ tilasaṃbhūtaṃ, tato vasā māṃsasnehaḥ, (tato) majjā, asthimadhyasnehaḥ | nanu pañcakarmādau vamanaṃ tadyathā – ‘vamanaṃ recanaṃ nasyaṃ nirūhamanuvāsanam | jñeyaṃ pañcavidhaṃ karma vidhānaṃ tasya kathyate’ iti | tatkathaṃ snehapānamādau ucyate ? ( satyam, snehapānaṃ vinā vamanādisaṃśodhanānāṃ hānikaratvāt ) pañcakarmādau snehapānaṃ, tataḥ svedaḥ, tato vamanaṃ, tato recanam; ayaṃ vidhiḥ ||
kaḥ kāla ’ etasya uttaramāha -- taṃ pibenmartyaḥ kiṃcidabhyudite ravau || 1 ||
Dī. ’ śītakāle divā snehamuṣṇakāle pibenniśi ’ iti || 1 ||
Gū. taṃ caturvidhaṃ snehaṃ kiṃcidabhyudite ravau pibet || 1 ||
‘kiṃyonayaḥ’ etasyottaramāha - sthāvaro jaṃgamaścaiva dviyoniḥ sneha ucyate |
Dī. tatra sthāvarāścaturvidhāḥ, vanaspatayaḥ, vṛkṣāḥ, vīrudhaḥ, oṣadhaya iti | jaṃgamāḥ khalvapi caturvidhāḥ – jarāyujā-ṇḍaja-svedajo-dbhijjāḥ eteṣāṃ svarūpamanyato jñātavyam | tathāca carake – ’snehānāṃ dvividhā saumya yoniḥ sthāvarajaṅgamā’ (ca. sū. 13,7) iti |
Gū. sthāvarajaṅgamabhedena snehasya dvividhatvamāha | sugamam ||
varāvaratvamāha - tilatailaṃ sthāvareṣu jaṅgameṣu ghṛtaṃ varam || 2 ||
Dī. tailaghṛtayorvaratvaṃ ca saṃskārasyānuvartanāditi | uktaṃca - ’tailaṃ saṃyogasaṃskārātsarvarogāpahaṃ matam’ iti | atraivodāharaṇaṃ candanādyaṃ tailam | tat ghṛtamapi śāstre vyavahārārtham || 2 ||
militasnehanāmānyāha - dvābhyāṃ tribhiścaturbhistairyamakastrivṛto mahān |
Dī. yo dvābhyāṃ snehābhyāṃ milito yogaḥ sa yamakaḥ kathitaḥ | yastribhiḥ snehairmilitaḥ sa trivṛtaḥ | yaścaturbhiḥ snehaiḥ saṃyutaḥ sa mahānkathita ityabhiprāyaḥ |
Gū. yamakādisnehabhedānāha - dvābhyāṃ ghṛtatailābhyāṃ yamakaḥ | tribhirghṛtatailavasābhistrivṛtaḥ | caturbhirghṛtatailavasāmajjābhirmahānityarthaḥ ||
snehasātmye kālāvadhimāha -- pibetryahaṃ caturahaṃ pañcāhaṃ ṣaḍahaṃ tathā || 3 || sāptarātrātparaṃ snehaḥ sātmībhavati sevitaḥ |
Dī. kutracitsnehaḥ sevitaḥ san tryahātparaṃ sātmyībhavati | kvacit caturahaṃ, pañcāhaṃ, ṣaḍahaṃ ca | saptāhātparamiti saptadinaṃ yāvat | iyam aharādimaryādā koṣṭhasya mṛdumadhyakṛtā jñeyā, paraṃ saptarātrādūrdhvaṃ sevitaḥ sātmībhavati | sātmībhūtasya snehasya guṇāḥ vāgbhaṭe – ’sātmībhūto hi kurute na malānāmudīraṇam’ iti | carake tu - ’snehanasya prakarṣau tu saptarātratrirātrakau ( ca. sū. 13,49) iti | vāgbhaṭe tu - ’tryahamacchaṃ mṛdukoṣṭhe krūre saptadinaṃ pibet’ iti ( vā. sū. 16,29) ihatyavirodhaḥ? maivaṃ - sarveṣāṃ viṣayabhedāt, mṛdu-mṛdutara-madhya-madhyatara-krūra-krūratara-koṣṭhaviṣayāṇi vacanāni jñātavyāni || 3 ||
Gū. pibet ghṛtaṃ tryahaṃ, tailaṃ caturahaṃ, vasā pañcāhaṃ, majjā ṣaḍaham evaṃ krameṇa snehaḥ kadā sātmībhavatītyāha - sneho ghṛtādiḥ sevitaḥ saptarātrātparaṃ sātmībhavati || 3 ||
atha snehapānamātrāviṣayamāha - doṣakālāgnivayasāṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā prayojayet || 4 || hīnāṃ ca madhyamāṃ jyeṣṭhāṃ mātrāṃ snehasya buddhimān |
Dī. mātrālakṣaṇamagre vakṣyamāṇam | doṣāstrividhāścaturvidhā vā teṣāṃ balam utkṛṣṭa-madhyā-lpādibhedena jñeyam | kālāḥ śītoṣṇavarṣālakṣaṇāstrividhāḥ eṣāṃ balaṃ hīnamadhyātimātrasvarūpaṃ, bheṣajagrahaṇakālaṃ vā | agnirapi sama-viṣama-manda-tīkṣṇamedena caturvidhaḥ asya balaṃ pācakāpācakarūpam | vayo'pi bāla-madhyottarabhedena trividhaṃ tasyāpi balaṃ svaśaktisāmarthyam | buddhimāniti sadvaidya iti śeṣaḥ | tathāhi - ’yathādoṣaṃ yathākālaṃ yathāvyādhiṃ yathābalaṃ | snehapakvamapakvaṃ vā pākaṃ yuñjyāccikitsakaḥ’ iti | doṣādīnapekṣya snehasya hīnādimātrāṃ prayojayet, hīnatarāmapi prayojayet | uktaṃca - hīnamadhyottamā mātrā tāstābhyaśca hṛsīyasīm | kalpayedvīkṣya doṣādīn prāgeva tu kanīyasīm || ( vā. sū 16|17) ‘ajñātakoṣṭhe hi bahu kuryājjīvitasaṃśayam’ iti || 4 ||
Gū. taṃ doṣādīnāṃ balaṃ sāmarthyaṃ dṛṣṭvā buddhimānpaṇḍitaḥ snehasya hīnāṃ madhyamāṃ jyeṣṭhāṃ mātrāṃ prayojayet | yasmindoṣe, yasminkāle, yasminnagnau yasminvayasi yaducitaṃ tat deyam || 4 ||
amātrādisevitasya snehasya doṣamāha - amātrayā tathā'kāle mithyāhāravihārataḥ || 5 || snehaḥ karoti śophārśastandrānidrāvisaṃjñatāḥ |

Dī. dīptāgnaye iti pradīptakoṣṭhapuruṣāyetyarthaḥ | jaghanyasaṃjñādoṣaviśeṣeṣu manyante tadapi saṃgataṃ tantrāntaradarśanāt | mātrā evaṃ madhyamāya trikārṣikī syāt ,jaghanyāya hīnabalānalāya dvikārṣikī | eke prabalamadhyajaghanyasaṃjñā doṣaviṣeṣu manyante tadapi saṃgataṃ tantrāntaradarśanāt | uktaṃ ca --’uttamasya palaṃ mātrā tribhiścākṣaiśca madhyamā | jaghanyasya palārdhena snehakvāthauṣadheṣu ca’ iti || 7 ||
Gū. dīptāgnyādiṣu kīdṛśī mātrā deyā tāmāha | madhyamāya madhyamāgnaye, jaghanyāya alpāgnaye || 7 ||
atha vikalpāntaravihitāṃ mātrāṃ darśayannāha - athavā snehamātrāḥ syustisro'nyāḥ sarvasaṃmatāḥ | ahorātreṇa mahatī jīryatyahni tu madhyamā || 8 || jīryatyalpā dinārdhena sā vijñeyā sukhāvahā |
Dī. athaveti prakāreṇa kālabhedena ityarthaḥ | anyāḥ palādibhyo'nyāḥ vilakṣaṇāḥ, vailakṣaṇyatvādasaṃmatatvamityata āha - sarvasaṃmatā iti | carakavāgbhaṭādisaṃmatā | uktaṃca carake -‘ahorātramahaḥ kṛtsnamardhāhaṃ ca pratīkṣyate | pradhānā madhyamā hrasvā snehamātrā jarāṃ prati | iti tisraḥ samuddiṣṭāḥ mātrāḥ snehasya mānataḥ ( ca. sū 13, 27 | 28) iti | vāgbhaṭe'pi - ’ dvābhyāṃ caturbhiraṣṭābhiryāmairjīryanti yāḥ kramāt | hasvamadhyottamā mātrā ’ (vā. sū 16,17) iti | ahorātreṇa mahatī jīryatīti | nanvahorātragrahaṇaṃ sahṛdayahṛdayāhlādakaraṃ na bhavati, kutaḥ ? ahani dvayornāḍikayoranantaraṃ pītasya snehasya pare'hni ghaṭikādvayānantaraṃ pākaḥ sacāhorātragrahaṇenaiva gṛhyate | ahorātragrahaṇam aṣṭau praharapramāṇakālaparaṃ jñeyam | evaṃ paramacatureṇa vāgbhaṭenāṣṭau praharādipramāṇamevoktaṃ tadvadatrāpyahorātramaṣṭau praharaparaṃ jñeyam | evamuttaratrā'pi vyākhyeyam | seti | sā alpā hrasvā sukhāvahā jñātavyā | sukhakāriṇītyarthaḥ | śīghrapākatvāt || 8 ||
Gū. alpāṃ mātrāmāha - athavā pakṣāntare | anyāstisraḥ snehamātrā āha - mahatī ahorātreṇa pacyate sā palapramāṇā, madhyamā trikarṣā yā sā ahni yāmacatuṣṭayena jīryati, alpā dvikārṣikī dinārdhena yāmadvayena jīryati sā sukhāvahā vijñeyā || 8 ||
alpādīnāṃ guṇānāha - alpā syāddīpanī vṛṣyā svalpadoṣeṣu pūjitā || 9 || madhyamā snehanī jñeyā bṛṃhaṇī bhramahāriṇī | jyeṣṭhā kuṣṭhaviṣonmādagrahāpasmāranāśinī || 10 ||
Dī. atha tāsāṃ viṣayamāha - alpā mātrā snehasya dīpanī bhavati agnidīptikarītyarthaḥ | vṛṣyā śukravṛddhikarī, svalpadoṣeṣu hīnabaleṣu doṣeṣu vā pūjitā | kāraṇe kāryopacārāt | svalpadoṣe svalparogeṣu vā iti svalpanidānenālpasaṃcite | madhyamā catuḥpraharairyā pākaṃ prāpnoti sā madhyamā | sā snehanī rūkṣāṇāṃ | bṛṃhaṇī sarvadhātūnām | bhramaścakrārūḍhasyevārthasaṃvittiḥ | vastudarśanam | jyeṣṭheti | jyeṣṭhā uttamā kuṣṭhādihāriṇī bhavati | carake tu hrasvādimātrāṇāṃ viṣayaguṇāścoktā yathā -
