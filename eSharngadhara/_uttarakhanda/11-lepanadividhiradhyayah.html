ekādaśo'dhyāyaḥ | atha kavalādīnāṃ vidhimabhidhāya saṃpratilepamūrddhatailakarṇapūraṇavidhimārabhyate | tatra tāvatpralepanāmānyāha - ālepasya ca nāmāni lipto lepaśca lepanam | doṣaghno viṣahā varṇyo mukhalepastridhā mataḥ || 1 || tripramāṇaścaturbhāgatribhāgā'rdhāṅgulonnata: | ārdro vyādhiharaḥ sa syācchuṣko dūṣayati cchavim ||2||
Dī. tridhā mata ityanena eko doṣaghnaḥ dvitīyo viṣahā kathitaḥ aparastu varṇya iti tridhā saṃjño'sminmate'nyatrāpi prasiddha eva | sa eva varṇyo mukhalepaḥ kathitaḥ | na tu catvāro bhedāḥ vakṣyamāṇatvāt tripramāṇa iti teṣāṃ doṣaghnādīnāṃ trayāṇāṃ traya eva parimāṇā yathā'nukrameṇa boddhavyāḥ | sa kathaṃbhūta: | caturbhāgavibhāgārdhāṅgulonnata iti | etena kimuktaṃ | doṣaghno lepaḥ aṅgulasya caturbhāgapramāṇenotsedhaḥ kuryādityabhiprāyaḥ | evaṃ viṣahālepastu tribhāgotsedhaḥ kāryaḥ | tribhāgatāṅgulasyeti saṃbandhaḥ | varṇyo lepaścārdhāṅgulonnata eva kuryāt | athārdraśuṣkayoḥ phalamāha | ārdro vyādhihara ityādi | sa pūrvokto lepo yāvadārdro bhavati tāvadvyādhiharo jñeyaḥ | yadā śuṣko bhavati tadā na vidūṣayati | chaviḥ chāyā sā ca varṇāprabhopalakṣitā | ‘ tathāca varṇaprabhāśritā yā ca chāyā sā parikīrtite’ti | nanu chāyāprabhayostu ko bhedaḥ | ucyate ‘varṇamātrapraticchāyā prabhā varṇaprakāśinī | āsannāt lakṣyate chāyā prabhā dūrācca lakṣyate’ iti | na kevalaṃ varṇaprabhācchāyānāṃ lakṣaṇabhedaḥ | saṃkhyābhedopyastīti | tadyathā caturdhā varṇaḥ gauraḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śyāmaḥ gauraśyāma iti | prabhā ca saptavidhā raktā pītā sitā śyāmā haritā pāṇḍurā siteti | chāyāpi pañcavidhā snigdhā vimalā rūkṣā malinā saṃkṣiptā iti || 1 || 2 ||
Gū. atha mūrdhatailakarṇapūraṇavidhiḥ | tatrādau lepaḥ lipto lepaḥ lepanaṃ tāni trīṇi ālepasya nāmāni bhavanti | nāmakathanaṃ saṃjñārthaṃ paryāyakathanārthaṃ ca | sa lepastridhā triprakāraḥ mataḥ kathitaḥ | ekaḥ doṣaghnaḥ śothādināśakaḥ | doṣaśabdenāpi rogā ucyante | dvitīyo viṣahā bhallātādiviṣanāśakaḥ| tṛtīyo varṇyaḥ mukhavyaṅgādivarṇakaraḥ | evaṃbhūto mukhalepaḥ mukhyo lepaḥ tridhā | mukhaśabdena mukhya ucyate | tathāca viśvaḥ ‘mukhaṃ ca vadane mukhye ādau dvārābhyupāyayoḥ’ iti | punaḥ lepastripramāṇaḥ trīṇi pramāṇāni yasya saḥ | tadvidhānamāha - caturbhāgaḥ aṅgulasya catvāro bhāgā yasya saḥ pūrṇāṅgula ityarthaḥ | sa tu doṣaghnaḥ | tribhāgaḥ trīṇi aṅgulāni yasya saḥ tribhāgaḥ sa tu viṣahā | ardhāṅgulottamaḥ ardham aṅgulapramāṇam unnata uccaḥ, sa tu varṇyaḥ varṇakaraḥ | sa ārdraḥ aśuṣkaḥ chaviṃ kāntiṃ dūṣayati || 1 || 2 ||
doṣaghno lepo yathā - punarnavāṃ dāru śuṇṭhīṃ siddhārthaṃ śigrumeva ca | piṣṭvā caivāranālena pralepa: sarvaśothajit || 3 ||
Dī. pūrvamuktaṃ doṣaghno viṣahā varṇyo lepastridhā | tatra tāvaddoṣaghno lepo yathā | siddhārthaṃ sarṣapāḥ śigruḥ śobhāñjanaḥ āranālaṃ kāñjikabhedaḥ tadabhāve'mlatakraṃ grāhyamiti sarvatra | ayaṃ lepaḥ sarvaśothajidityanena vātakaphaprāyaṃ śothaṃ viśeṣeṇa jayatīti pracaratīti | śotho'tra doṣajanitaḥ śopharogasaṃbhavaḥ | na tu vraṇaśothaḥ tatrāpyāgantujāpīti saṃbandhāt || 3 ||
Gū. punarnavā dāru devadāru śuṇṭhī siddhārthaṃ sarṣapaḥ śigruḥ śobhāñjanaḥ āranālena kāñjikena | tadabhāve gomūtreṇa vā lepaḥ kāryaḥ sa lepaḥ sarvaśothahṛdbhavati sarvaśothena ca viṣaśotha: || 3 ||
atha dāhe lepaḥ vibhītaphalamajjāyā lepo dāhārtināśanaḥ |
Dī. athāparaḥ pittajadāhanāśano lepastamāha - atrāpi āranālena peṣaṇamiti saṃbandho jñeyaḥ ||
Gū. bibhītaphalaṃ bibhītakaphalaṃ tasya majjālepaḥ ||
atha daśāṅgalepo visarpaśothavraṇādau – (daśāṅgalepaḥ) śirīṣaṃ madhuyaṣṭī ca tagaraṃ raktacandanam || 4 || elā māṃsī niśāyugmaṃ kuṣṭhaṃ vālakameva ca | iti saṃcūrṇya lopo'yaṃ pañcamāṃśaghṛtapluta: || 5 || jalena kriyate sujñairdaśāṅga iti saṃjñitaḥ | visarpānviṣavisphoṭāñśothānduṣṭavraṇāñjayet ||6||
Dī. atha daśāṅgalepasaṃjñakaṃ lepamapyāha - atra śirīṣaḥ śirīṣavṛkṣatvak | eke pañcāṅgaśirīṣamatra yojayanti prayogāntaradarśanāt | tathāhi - ‘śirīṣapañcāṃgatuṣodakena kalkīkṛtaṃ caiva ghṛtena yuktam | nihanti visphoṭakamugrarūpaṃ sadāhapākasrutirāgayuktam’ iti | yatra tvaṅmūlapuṣpāṇi phalānyaṅgāni pañca veti | niśāyugmamiti haridrādvayaṃ | pañcamāṃśaghṛtapluta iti | sakaladravyāṇāṃ samabhāgamilitānāṃ cūrṇaṃ kṛtvā tadanu jalena saṃpiṣya paścātpañcamāṃśena ghṛtena saṃplāvya lepaḥ kāryaḥ | pañcamāṃśamatra dravyaparimāṇāt || 4-6 ||
Gū. śirīṣaṃ, māṃsī jaṭāmāṃsī, niśāyugmaṃ haridrādvayaṃ, vālakam udīcyaṃ, cūrṇātpañcamāṃśaṃ ghṛtaṃ daśāṅga iti daśa aṅgāni auṣadhāni yasya saḥ ghṛtaṃ tu prakṣepakadravyaṃ tadbhinnam || 4-6 ||
viṣaghno lepo yathā - ajādugdhatilairlepo navanītena saṃyutaḥ | śothamāruṣkaraṃ hanti lepo vā kṛṣṇamṛttikaiḥ ||7||
Dī. atha viṣahā lepamāha - āruṣkaro bhallātakaḥ tajjanitaḥ śotha iti || 7 ||
Gū. āruṣkaraṃ bhallātakajanitaṃ | spaṣṭhamanyat ||7||
atha kīṭaghnalepaḥ - lāṅgalyativiṣālābujālinīmūlabījakaiḥ | lepo dhānyāmbusaṃpiṣṭa: kīṭavisphoṭanāśanaḥ || 8 ||
Dī. aparamapyāha - lāṅgalī kalikārikā | ativiṣā ‘atīsa’ śabdavācyā | alābu kaṭutumbī jālinī ‘toraī’ śabdavācyā | mūlakaṃ prasiddhaṃ tasya bījāni | eke alābuprabhṛtīnāṃ trayāṇāṃ bījāni gṛhanti | dhānyāmbusaṃpiṣṭa iti | dhānyāmbu kāñjikabhedaḥ | kīṭavisphoṭaḥ saviṣakīṭadaṣṭajanitavisphoṭa ityarthaḥ | yathā lūtādayaḥ || 8 ||
Gū. lāṅgalī kalihārī, ativiṣā prasiddhā alābu tumbī jālinī kaṭutumbībījāni mūlakabījāni dhānyāmbu kāñjikam || 8 ||
varṇyo lepo yathā - raktacandanamañjiṣṭhālodhrakuṣṭhapriyaṅgavaḥ | vaṭāṅkurā masūrāśca vyaṅgaghnā mukhakāntidāḥ || 9 || mātuluṅgajaṭā sarpiḥ śilā gośakṛto rasaḥ | mukhakāntikaro lepaḥ piṭikāvyaṅgakālajit ||10|| lodhradhānyavacālepastāruṇyapiṭikāpahaḥ | tadvadgorocanāyuktaṃ maricaṃ mukhalepanam || 11 || siddhārthakavacālodhrasaindhavaiśca pralepanam | vyaṅgeṣu cārjunatvagvā mañjiṣṭhā vā samākṣikā || 12|| lepaḥ sanavatīto vā śvetāśvakhurajā maṣī | arkakṣīraharidrābhyāṃ mardayitvā vilepanāt || 13 || mukhakārṣṇyaṃ śamaṃ yāti cirakālodbhavaṃ dhruvam | vaṭasya pāṇḍupatrāṇi mālatī raktacandanam || 14 || kuṣṭhaṃ kālīyakaṃ lodhramebhirlepaṃ prayojayet | tāruṇyapiṭikāvyaṅganīlikādivināśanam || 15 ||
Dī. atha viṣahā lepamabhidhāya samprati varṇyalepamāha - raktacandanādīni vyastāni samastāni saṃgṛhya jalenāpiṣya lepaḥ kārya iti saṃbandhaḥ | atra priyaṅgurlatāviśeṣaḥ tasyāḥ phalāni | vaṭāṅkurā iti vaṭasyābhinavāḥ patramukulāḥ | masūro'nnaviśeṣaḥ | mātuluṅgo bījapūrakaḥ tasya jaṭā mūlaṃ | śilā manaḥśilā | gośakṛto rasa iti | gośakṛt gopurīṣaṃ tasya rasa ityarthaḥ | dhānyaṃ dhānaśabdavācyam | tadvadatitāruṇyapiḍakāpahopyayaṃ lepo boddhavyaḥ | gorocanā prasiddhā siddhārthaka iti | atrāpi tāruṇyapiḍakāpaha iti saṃbandhaḥ | tena siddhārthādidravyacatuṣkairlepo yauvanapiḍakāpaho bhavatīti matāntarāt | arjuno vṛkṣaviśeṣaḥ sa ca kauhāśabdavācyaḥ tasya tvak challī | mañjiṣṭhā prasiddhā | samākṣikā dhātumākṣikasaṃyuktā | sacārjunatvak mañjiṣṭhā vā | tena vikalpatvāt tvaca eko yogaḥ mañjiṣṭhāyā dvitīyo yogaḥ | aśvakhurajanitasya kajjalasya tṛtīya iti | eke śvetā aparājiteti vyākhyānayanti | eke arkakṣīramiti bhinnapadaṃ haridrābhyāmiti dvivacanatvena haridrā dāruharidrā yojyeti vyākhyānayanti | etatsarvatra na pracarati tenārkakṣīraharidrābhyāmeva yogaḥ sādhuḥ| vaṭasya vaṭavṛkṣasya pāṇḍupatrāṇīti | pariṇatatvātteṣu pāṇḍuratvaṃ tādṛśānītyarthaḥ | mālatī jātī tasyāḥ patrāṇi kālīyakam agurubhedaṃ pītasāramiti loke | kecidvaṭaśca pāṇḍupatrāṇītī paṭhanti tatra vaṭa ityanena vaṭavṛkṣasya patrāṇi pāṇḍuḥ paṭolastasyāpi patrāṇītyarthaḥ | etanna sarvamatam | aprasiddhatvāt || 9-15 ||
Gū. raktacandaneti sugamaṃ | vyaṅgalepaḥ vyaṅgaśchāyā | atha tāruṇyapiḍakādau - mātuluṅgo bījapūramūlaṃ, sarpirghṛtaṃ, śilā manaḥśilā gośakṛt gopurīṣaṃ tadrasaḥ kālaṃ kālimā | atha tāruṇyapiḍakādau - dhānyaṃ prasiddham | siddhārthakaṃ sarṣapāḥ ete trayo yogāḥ piḍakādau | atha vyaṅge-arjunatvak arjunavṛkṣatvak mākṣikaṃ madhu śveto yo'śvaḥ ghoṭakastat khurajanyā maṣī bhasma mukhaśyāmatāyāṃ punaḥ - mālatī jātī kālīyakaṃ dāruharidrā || 9-15 ||
athāruṃṣikāyāṃ lepaḥ - purāṇamatha piṇyākaṃ purīṣaṃ kukkuṭasya ca | mūtrapiṣṭa: pralepo'yaṃ śīghraṃ hanyādaruṃṣikām ||16|| khādirāriṣṭajambūnāṃ tvagbhirvā mūtrasaṃyutaiḥ | kuṭajatvaksaindhavaṃ ca lepo hanyādaruṃṣikām ||17||
Dī. piṇyākaṃ tilakalkajanitaṃ khaliśabdavācyaṃ | tacca purātanaṃ varṣoṣitaṃ grāhyaṃ | kukkuṭaḥ tāmracūḍaḥ tasya purīṣaṃ | mūtramatra gavyam || 16 || 17 ||
Gū. priṇyākaṃ tilakhalaḥ anyaṃ ca ariṣṭo nimba: aruṃṣikā apiṭakāḥ || 16 || 17 ||
dāruṇe lepaḥ - priyālabījamadhukakuṣṭhamāṣaiḥ sasaindhavaiḥ | kāryo dāruṇake mūrdhni pralepo madhusaṃyutaḥ || 18 || dugdhena khākhasaṃ bījaṃ pralepāddāruṇaṃ jayet | āmrabījasya cūrṇaṃ tu śivācūrṇasamaṃ dvayam || 19 || dugdhapiṣṭha: pralepo'yaṃ dāruṇaṃ hanti dāruṇam |
Dī. priyālabījaṃ cirauñjīti prasiddham | madhukaṃ yaṣṭīmadhu | māṣo'nnaviśeṣaḥ | madhusaṃyuta ityanena priyālabījādiboddhavyam | jalena saṃpiṣya manāk madhunā saṃsrāvya lepaḥ kāryaḥ | dugdhamatra gavyaṃ | khākhasaṃ bījamiti dānaśabdavācyam | āmrabījasya cūrṇamiti | āmraphalamadhyasthitamajjāviśeṣaḥ | śivā harītakī | dāruṇaṃ rogaviśeṣaṃ | dvitīyaṃ dāruṇaṃ duḥsādhyamiti viśeṣaṇam || 18 || 19 ||
Gū. priyālabījaṃ cirauñjīti prasiddhaṃ, madhukaṃ madhuyaṣṭī māṣo'nnaviśeṣaḥ | khākhasaṃ śivā harītakī dāruṇe || 18 || 19 ||
athendralupte - rasastiktapaṭolasya patrāṇāṃ tadvilepanāt || 20 || indraluptaṃ śamaṃ yāti tribhireva dinairdhruvam | indraluptāpaho lepo madhunā bṛhatīrasaḥ || 21 || guñjāmūlaṃ phalaṃ vāpi bhallātakaraso'pi vā | gokṣurastilapuṣpāṇi tulye ca madhusarpiṣī || 22 || śiraḥpralepanaṃ tena keśasaṃvardhanaṃ param | hastidantamaṣīṃ kṛtvā chāgīdugdhaṃ rasāñjanam ||29|| romāṇyanena jāyante lepātpāṇitaleṣvapi | yaṣṭīndīvaramṛdvīkātailājyakṣīralepanaiḥ || 24 || indraphalaṃ śamaṃ yāti keśāḥ syuḥ saghanā dṛḍhā: | catuṣpadānāṃ tvagromanakhaśrṛṅgāsthibhasmabhiḥ || 25 || tailena saha lepo'yaṃ romasaṃjananaḥ paraḥ |
Dī. tiktapaṭolaḥ kaṭukapaṭolaḥ tasya patrāṇāṃ rasa ityarthaḥ | tadvilepanāditi | tattasya rasasya lepanāt | tribhireva dinairityanenāsya viśiṣṭatvaṃ darśitam | nātra tridinaireva niyatapramāṇaṃ yato bahudinasevyo'yaṃ prayogaḥ | bṛhatīrasa iti pakkabṛhatīphalajanitarasasyaiko yogaḥ | guñjāyā mūlaṃ phalaṃ vā dvitīyo yogaḥ | atrā'pi madhupracāraḥ | bhallātakaphalaṃ tṛtīyo yogaḥ | eke bhallātakarasopi vetyasya sthāne'vikṣīreṇa saṃyutamiti paṭhanti | tena bheḍīdugdhena guñjāphalaṃ saṃghṛṣya lepaḥ kārya iti saṃgataḥ | saṃpradāye tu indraluptasthānaṃ prasthayitvā tadabhāve saṃghṛṣya paścāllepaḥ kārya iti sādhuḥ | uktamanyatrāpi - ‘bṛhatīphalarasasaṃpiṣṭaṃ guñjāphalamūlamindraluptasya | kanakavighṛṣṭasya tato dātavyaṃ prasthitasya sade’ti | gokṣuraḥ prasiddhaḥ | tilapuṣpāṇi vikhyātāni | tulye sāmye gokṣure satilapuṣpe ityarthaḥ | madhusarpiṣī ca | tulye'tra grāhye | maṣīkajjalaviśeṣaṃ hastidantaṃ dagdhvā kajjalaṃ kāryam | rasāñjanaṃ dvividham ekaṃ kṛṣṇapāṣāṇasadṛśaṃ dravyaviśeṣam | aparaṃ dārvīkvāthasamudbhavam | tadabhāve dāruharidraiva grāhyā | ayaṃ lepaḥ keśavardhanārthamityāhuḥ | yaṣṭī madhuyaṣṭī indīvaraṃ nīlotpalaṃ tadabhāve padmabījamapi grāhyam | padmabījaṃ gaṭāśabdavācyam| mṛdvīkā gostanī drākṣā | eke mṛdvīsthāne mūrveti pāṭhāntaraṃ paṭhanti vyākhyānayanti ca | tailāni prasiddhāni | ājyaṃ ghṛtaṃ kṣīraṃ gopayaḥ | tilājyakṣīrasthāne tilājakṣīralepanairitipāṭhaḥ | tatrā'jakṣīramityarthaḥ tilājyakṣīramārkavairityapi pāṭhāntaraṃ saṃgatam | tathāhyuktaṃ ‘madhukendīvaramūrvātilājyagokṣīrabhṛṅgalepena | acirādbhavanti keśā ghanadṛḍhamūlāyatā mṛdava’ iti | catuṣpadāḥ go'śvāvyajebheṇakharoṣṭrādayaḥ | teṣāṃ tvak carma romanakhaśṛṅgāsthīni prasiddhāni | tena tvagādīnāṃ dagdhvā bhasma kāryaṃ tailaṃ tilasaṃbhavam || 20-25 ||
