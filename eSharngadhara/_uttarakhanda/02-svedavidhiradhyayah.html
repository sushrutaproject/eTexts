Dī. atha snehādhyāyānantaraṃ svedavidhiṃ vyākhyātuṃ pratijānīte -

Dī. athaśabda ānantarye | snehavidhikathanānantaraṃ svedavidhiḥ | svedasya vidhirvidhānam | uktaṃca prāk - ’ snehasvedāvanabhyasya kuryāt saṃśodhanaṃ tu yaḥ | dāruśuṣkamivānāme śarīraṃ tasya dīryate’ iti | carake'pri snehapūrvaprayuktena svedamāvarjite'nile | purīṣamūtraretāṃsi na sajjanti kadācana’ iti | vāgbhaṭe'pi - śuṣkānyapi hi kāṣṭhāni snehasvedopapādanaiḥ | śakyaṃ karmaṇyatāṃ netuṃ kimu gātrāṇi jīvatām’ iti | tena yuktaṃ snehānantaraṃ svedā'bhidhānam |
Gū. snehapānānantaraṃ svedasya vihitatvāt ||
tatra tāvatpūrvaṃ svedasaṃkhyāmāha - svedaścaturvidhaḥ proktastāpoṣmasvedasaṃjñakau | upanāho dravaḥ svedaḥ sarve vātārtihāriṇaḥ || 1 || svedau tāpoṣmajau prāyaḥ śleṣmaghnau samudīritau | upanāhastu vātaghnaḥ pittasaṅge dravo hitaḥ || 2 ||
Dī. sveda iti | svidyate'neneti vyutpattyā svedo'gniḥ, tasya vidhiḥ karmacāturī | caturvidhatvamāha - ekastāpasaṃjñaḥ svedaḥ| dvitīyastūṣmāsaṃjñaḥ| tṛtīya upanāhaḥ upanāho nibandhanamiti pralepena | asya bhedaḥ sālvaṇa iti | caturtho dravasveda iti | eteṣāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ boddhavyaṃ anyatrāpyuktaṃ ca ’kṣāmaḥsaikatapārṣṇikāsyavasanaiḥ svedo'thavāṃgārakairlepairvātaharaiḥ sahāmlalavaṇaiḥ snehaiḥ sukhomlairbhavet | evaṃ taptapayombuvātaśamanakvāthādisekādibhistaptaistoyaniṣecanāhatamahābāṣpaiḥ sitādyaiḥ kramā’diti | eṣveva caturṣu yathākramaṃ carakoktasaṃkarasvedādyavarodhaḥ | śaṃkarādisvedā yathā ’śaṃkaraḥ prastaro nāḍī pariṣeko'vagāhanaṃ | jetākoṣmaghanaḥ karṣaḥ kuṭī bhūḥ kumbhireva ca | kūpo dolaka ityete svedayanti trayodaśa iti1 ’ | pittasaṃge dravo hita iti | pittayukte vāte dravasvedo hitaḥ pittasaṃge'tra svedasaṃghāto vihitaḥ | vātakaphayorevaṃ svedaśaṃkaraḥ | yaduktaṃ carake - vātaśleṣmaṇi vāte vā kaphe vā sveda iṣyata iti | alpe pittasaṃsarge vāte dravasvedaḥ | sacoṣṇatvāt prabalau vātakaphau hanti | hīnapittaṃ tu dravatvena saukṣmyatvāt | vāgbhaṭe'pi - ‘ svedastāpopanāhoṣmadravabhedāścaturvidhāḥ ’ iti ||1||2||
Gū. svedaścaturvidhaḥ proktaḥ kathitaḥ | caturvidhatvamāha – tāpoṣmau etau dvau svedasaṃjñitau bhavataḥ | upanāho dravaśca etau dvau , evaṃ catvāraḥ sarvato vātaharāḥ vātahāriṇo bhavanti | caturvidhasvedasya vātanāśakatve'pi viśeṣamāha – svedāviti | topoṣmajau svedau śleṣmaghnau samudīritau kathitau | upanāhaḥ svedaḥ vātaghnaḥ pittasaṃyoge dravo dravaḥ svedyamatipittasaṅge vā tau | tathāca suśrutaḥ - ‘tatra tāpoṣmasvedau viśeṣataḥ śleṣmaghnau, upanāhasvedo vātaghnaḥ | anyatarasmin pittasaṃsṛṣṭe dravasvedaḥ’ iti | anyatarasaṃsṛṣṭe vātamiśrite pitte dravaḥ svedo'bhihitaḥ iti suśrutā'bhiprāyaḥ | kevalapitte tu sarva eva svedā niṣiddhāḥ | kiṃtu śītalajalāvagāha eva praśastaḥ tathā coktaṃ - ‘pariṣekāvagāheṣu vyañjanānāṃ ca sevanam | śasyate śiśiraṃ toyaṃ tṛṣṇāpittoṣmaśāntaye’ iti ||1||2||
atha trividhasvedasya viśeṣamāha - mahābale mahāvyādhau śīte svedo mahānsmṛtaḥ | durbale durbalaḥ svedo madhye madhyatamo mataḥ || 3 ||
Dī. mahābalapuruṣe mahāvyādhau ca kapholbaṇavātavikārādau | śīte śītale śītārditapuruṣe vā mahān svedo vihitaḥ | durbale puruṣe pittaprāye vā durbalaḥ svedo dravaprāyo vihitaḥ | madhye madhyamabalapuruṣe vātaprāye vā madhyaḥ madhyamaḥ sveda upanāhaprāyo vihitaḥ | tathācoktaṃ -‘tāpopanāhadravabāṣpapūrvāḥ svedāstato'ntyaḥ prathamau kaphe staḥ | vāyau dvitīyaḥ pavanaṃ kaphe ca pittopasṛṣṭe vihitastṛtīyaḥ ’ iti || 3 ||
atha doṣaviśeṣeṇa svedaviśeṣamāha – balāse rūkṣaṇasvedo rūkṣasnigdhaṃ kaphānile | kaphamedovṛte vāte koṣṇaṃ gehaṃ raveḥ karān ||4|| niyuddhaṃ mārgagamanaṃ guruprāvaraṇaṃ kṣudhaḥ | cintāvyāyāmabhārāṃśca sevetāmayamuktaye ||5||
Dī. balāse kaphe |rūkṣasvedaṃ vālukāvastrakapālāṃgārādinimittaṃ | kaphānile iti kaphānilasaṃsarge rūkṣasnigdhamiti | tacca śālvaṇaprabhṛtikaṃ | kaphamedovṛte vāte iti | medo hi sarvaśarīrāṇāmudarastham aṇvasthiṣu ca | tathāhi ‘sthūlāsthiṣu viśeṣeṇa majjātvabhyantarasthitā | athetareṣu sarveṣu saraktaṃ meda ucyate ’ iti | anena sakaphenāvṛte vāyau koṣṇagehādikaṃ kuryādityabhiprāyaḥ | koṣṇagehamaṅgārādibhiḥ saṃtaptamityarthaḥ | raveḥ karān sūryakiraṇān | niyuddhaṃ bāhuyuddhaṃ | guruprāvaraṇamiti pravarājinakauśeyapraveṇīkuthakaprabhṛtikaṃ | kruddhaṃ kopaṃ pittajanakaṃ cintācittabhrāṃtikaraṃ vidhānaṃ medonāśakaratvāt | vyāyāmamiti śarīrāyāsajananam | viyuddhādivyatiriktaṃ karma etān āmayamuktaye kaphamedovṛtavātajanitarogaśāntyai seveta ātura iti śeṣaḥ | cintāsthāne bhayamiti kecit | tenāpi manaḥsaṃtāpatvāt krodhavat | tathāhi ‘vyāyāmoṣṇagṛhakṣudgurubalakrodhamānabhayasamarāḥ | upanāhasūryatāpau daśāgniguṇavarjitāḥ svedāḥ’ | bhayaṃ tu yathā unmādakāraṇamapi tanmādyakaṃ syāt | yathā dṛśyate yatsvedanaṃ calitasya sandheḥ punarghaṭṭanena tena samyagavasthānaṃ tadvadihāpi jñeyam ||4||5||
Gū. yādṛśe vāyau yādṛśaḥ svedo vihitaḥ tatprakāramāha – balāse iti | balāse kaphe | rūkṣaṇaḥ svedo vālukādibhirhitaḥ | kaphānile rūkṣasnigdhaṃ | kaphānile kaphavāte | kaphamedovṛte vāte koṣṇaṃ gehaṃ saṃseveta | raveḥ karān kiraṇān seveta | niyuddhaṃ bāhuyuddham | mārgagamanaṃ guruprāvaraṇam | kopaṃ balādidhāraṇaṃ śītādīnāṃ śamanaṃ , cintāvyāyāmabharāṃśca seveta | āmayamuktaye roganāśāya ||4||5||
prāksvedyāḥ - yeṣāṃ nasyaṃ vidhātavyaṃ bastiścāpi hi dehinām | śodhanīyāśca ye kecitpūrvaṃ svedyāśca te matāḥ ||6||
Dī. atha krameṇa ye pūrvaṃ svedyāstānāha - yeṣāṃ puruṣāṇāṃ nasyaṃ vidhātavyamiti nasyaṃ dātavyaṃ te nasyātmakasvedyā ityarthaḥ | na tu nasyaṃ vidhāya paścāt svedyā iti bhāvaḥ | yeṣāṃ dehināṃ bastiranuvāsananirūhākhyā dātavyā teṣāmapi pūrvaṃ svedakarma kāryaṃ, ye kiṃcicchodhanīyāḥ vamanavirekādiyogyāste'pi pūrvaṃ svedyā ityartha: | paramārthatastu virecane vidhātavye sati tryahaṃ snehapānaṃ paramam | vamane punarekāhoparame svedaḥ | yadāha kaścitsudāntasenaḥ - ekāhaṃ dinamanyacca kaphanut kleśyatakṣarairiti (?) || 6 ||
Gū. pūrvaṃ ye svedyāstānāha - yeṣāmiti | bhagandarī aśmaryāturaśca ete trayaḥ pūrvaṃ svedyāḥ || 6 ||
paścātsvedyāḥ - paścātsvedyā gate śalye mūḍhagarbhagade tathā | kāle prajātā'kāle vā paścātsvedyā nitambinī ||7||
Dī. idānīṃ ye paścāt svedyāstānāha - mūḍhagarbhādyupadrava ityeke | mūḍhagarbhagate tathetyapīti pāṭhaḥ kaścit | arthādraktasrāvādyupadravarahitāḥ paścāt svedyā ityarthaḥ | kāle prasavasamaye prajātakāle samyagjātasamaye | nitambinī strī | tantrāntare'pi ’paścātsvedyā hṛte śalye mūḍhagarbhā'nupadravā | samyakprajātakāle ca paścāt svedyā vijānateti’ | (cakradatte) ||7 ||
Gū. ye paścātsvedyāstānāha - paścāditi | gate śalye tathā mūḍhagarbhagade paścātsvedyāḥ | kāle prajātā navamamāsādagre prasūtā | akāle navamamāsādadhaḥ prasūtā vā nitambinī strī paścātprasavāt svedyā ||7||

Dī. athobhayataḥ śodhyāstānāha | plīhabhagandarārśasāṃ traya eva doṣāḥ | traya evopadravā vāte cāpi vātādidoṣajanitā ityarthaḥ | tathetigrahaṇānnaite pūrvaṃ svedyā: kiṃtu paścāt svedyā iti tātparyārthaḥ | tathā – coktaṃ - ’svedyāḥ punarayathādāhanāntaramuṣṇāmbutaptakaṭāhopaveśanarūpam’ | evamanyadapi yathāyogyaṃ boddhavyaṃ | janturāturaḥ śastrakarmaṇaḥ śastravidhānasya samaye kāle plīhabhagandarādīnityarthaḥ | śastrakarmaṇaḥ paścātpūrvaṃ caite svedyā ityabhiprāyaḥ || 8 ||

Gū. svedāddoṣaḥ kathaṃ yātītyāha - svedāditi | svedādvālukādisvedāt | tathā snehasvinnasya tailāvagāhanādyaiḥ svinnasya dehinaḥ puruṣasya dhātusthitā doṣāḥ koṣṭhāntargatvā dravatvaṃ prāpya virekatāṃ yāntīti yojanā || 9 ||

Dī. atha sveditasyāśvāsanārthaṃ viṣayamāha -- svidyamānaśarīrasya snehābhyaktaśarīrasyeti pādadvayasyetyayamabhiprāyaḥ - anena pūrvaṃ snehābhyaktaśarīrasya paścāt sveditasya puruṣasyetyarthaḥ | yataḥ sarvasvedeṣvapi snehābhyaṅgo vihita iti prasiddhaḥ | tathācoktaṃ dṛṣṭāntena | nānābhyaktenāpi ca snigdhadehe svedo yojyaḥ svedavidbhiḥ kathaṃcit | dṛṣṭaṃ loke kāṣṭhamasnigdhamāśu tāpādbhayaṃ sveda- yogairgṛhītamiti | śītairācchādya cakṣuṣī iti | sarvāṃgeṣu svede kriyamāṇe cakṣūrakṣaṇārthamuktaṃ śītairiti padmāpatrādibhiḥ | asyāgre ’hṛdayaṃ vṛṣaṇau dṛṣṭiṃ svedayenmṛdu naiva ve’ti kecitpaṭhanti vyākhyānayanti ca | atra cakṣuṣī ityupalakṣaṇatvāt hṛdayādirakṣaṇameva kāryamityanenaiva gṛhītārthatvāt || 10||
Gū. doṣavireke jāte tatsthāpanārthaṃ cikitsitamāha - svidyamānaśarīrasyeti | bālukādeḥ svinnasya vireke sati hṛdayaṃ śītalaiḥ candanādibhiḥ spṛśet | snehābhyaktaśarīrasya snehasvinnasya śītaiḥ kamalakadalīpatrādyaiḥ cakṣuṣī ācchādya virekāt upācaret ityuttarakriyāsaṃbandhaḥ || 10 ||
