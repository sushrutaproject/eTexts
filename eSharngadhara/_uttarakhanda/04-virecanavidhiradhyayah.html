--- title: "4. atha virecanavidhiradhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 4 order: 4 ---
atha virecanādhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ - atha virecanavidhiḥ |
Dī. sambandhastu prāgukta eva |
virecanaviṣayamāha - snigdhasvinnasya vāntasya dadyātsamyag virecanam |
Dī. pūrvasnehapānayuktasya svedayuktasya cetyarthaḥ | vāntasyeti vamanaṃ kṛtvā | etena snehapānādīnāmanantareṇa virecanaṃ na kāryamityabhiprāyaḥ | tathāhi - ‘pittamatyulbaṇīkṛtya snehasvede kṛte sati | vamane ca kṛte pūrvamataḥ samyagvirecayet’ iti | vamanāntaramapi snigdhasvinnasyeti | yaduktaṃ - ‘karmaṇāṃ vamanādīnāmantaraṃ tvantare punaḥ | snehasvedau prayuñjīta saṃśodhanamanantaram’ iti | snigdhasvinnamatra kimarthaṃ tat dṛṣṭāntena draḍhayati | yathā ‘snehasvedāvakṛtvā'gre yastu saṃśodhanaṃ pibet | dāruśuṣkamivānāmaṃ dehastasya viśīryata’ iti ||
Gū. vamanānantaraṃ virecanasya vihitatvādvirecanamāha - snigdhasya pītasnehasya, svinnasya kṛtasvedasya ||
vamane'pyakṛte sati doṣamāha - avāntasya tvadhaḥ srasto grahaṇīṃ chādayetkaphaḥ || 1 || mandāgniṃ gauravaṃ kuryājjanayedvā pravāhikām | athavā pācanairāmaṃ balāsaṃ ca vipācayet || 2 || snigdhasya snehanaiḥ kāryaṃ svedaiḥ svinnasya recanam |
Dī. avāntasyetyādi | adhaḥsrastodhaḥ prāptaḥ recanadravyaprabhāvavaśāt | etādṛśaḥ kaphastadā grahaṇīmācchādayet | grahaṇīmagnisthitānnasthānaṃ | yaduktam - ‘agnyadhiṣṭhānamannasya grahaṇādgrahaṇī mate’ti | ata evādau vamanenātra kaphanigraho vidheyaḥ | so'pyāśrayasamāśrayaṇātsroto'varodhahetuḥ na kevalaṃ grahaṇīcchādanaṃ karoti | mandāgnimityādi | atra gauravaṃ śarīrasyeti | pravāhikātīsārabhedaḥ | atha kāryāntaramāha - pācanairiti | pācanadravyaiḥ sāmamityaparipakvarasaṃ || tathāhi - ’jaṭharānaladaurbalyādavipakvastu yo rasaḥ | sa āmasaṃjñako dehe sarvadoṣaprakopaka’ iti | na kevalamāmaṃ śrleṣmāṇaṃ ca vipācayet | etena kṛtavamanasyāpi pūrvaṃ pācanaṃ dattvā paścādvirecayedityabhiprāyaḥ | athaveti grahaṇāttena vamanaṃ vinā pācanaṃ deyamityarthaḥ | saṃśodhanakrame'pi pācanasya prādhānyamuktaṃ | tathā tīsaṭe coktaṃ - ‘prāk pācanaṃ snehavidhirvidheyaḥ svedastataḥ syādvamanaṃ virekaḥ | nirūhaṇaṃ snehanabastikarma nasyaṃ kramaśceti bhiṣagvarāṇām’ iti | eṣāṃ kramābhiprāyeṇa vināpi virecanasya pūrvaṃ pācanaṃ kartavyameveti vyavahāraḥ saṅgata eva | atha pācanamabhidhāya virecanamapi kasya kathaṃ dātavyamityāha - snigdhasyeti | pūrvakṛtasnigdhapācanasya, kaiḥ snehanairghṛtādibhiḥ | na kevalaṃ snigdhasya recanaṃ kiṃtu svedairapi svinnasya sveditasya virecanaṃ praśastaṃ | yadvā snehanaiḥ svedairiti saṃbandhaḥ | snehasvedairityanena doṣāṇāṃ sukhamutpāṭanaṃ sūcitaṃ | yataḥ ‘snehasvedaiḥ pracalitā rasaiḥ snigdhairudīritāḥ | doṣāḥ koṣṭhagatā jantoḥ sukhaṃ haṃti virecanai’riti | pūrvamuktamapyadaḥ punareva draḍhayati na doṣaḥ paunaruktyena | kecidvakṣyamāṇena śaradṛtāviti ślokena militvā vyākhyānayanti | tatra śaradṛtau snigdhasya snehanaiḥ kṛtvā virecanaṃ kāryaṃ | vasante ca svedaiḥ svinnameva recanaṃ deyaṃ | na tu snigdhasya iti na sarvamatam || 1 || 2 ||
Gū. adhaḥsrastaḥ patitaḥ, grahaṇīṃ pittadhāriṇīṃ kṛtvā kapha ācchādayet | yadā kaphaḥ grahaṇīmācchādayet tadā kiṃ kuryāt ityāha - mandāgniṃ gauravaṃ ca kuryāt | pravāhikātīsārabhedastāṃ janayet | adhasrastasya kaphasya pratīkāramāha - athavā pakṣāntare | āmaṃ balāsaṃ kaphaṃ ca pācanaiḥ śuṇṭhyairaṇḍādibhiḥ pācayet | yadvā snehanairghṛtadugdhādibhiḥ snigdhasya sveditasya recanaṃ kāryamiti vidhiḥ tatrocyate | vamanādvirecanaṃ kadā deyamityapekṣāyāmatrānuktamiti likhyate | tatra bhedaḥ | samyakpravṛtte vamane saptarātre gate sati | susaṃskṛtaśarīrasya pradātavyaṃ virecanaṃ’ | carake ca ‘vātaṃ saṃsṛṣṭaṃ punaḥ saṃsnehitaṃ tathā | uṣṇaṃ laghu tryahaṃ bhuktaiḥ ṣoḍaśe'hni virecayet’ | ṣoḍaśadine yadvā pakṣāntare pakṣādvireko vāntasyeti suśrutavacanam | asyārthaḥ vātaṃkṛtaṃ samyagvamanaṃ ṣaḍahasaṃsṛṣṭaṃ ṣaḍahetyuktavamanaṃ punastryahaṃ saṃsnehitaṃ punastryahamuṣṇakṛtasvedaṃ punastryahaṃ laghubhuktaṃ athainaṃ vāmitaṃ bhūyaḥ snehasvedopapāditaṃ | śleṣmakāle gate jñātvā koṣṭhaṃ samyagvirecayet’ | vāgbhaṭe evaṃ likhitam | asyārthaḥ. athainaṃ puruṣaṃ vāmitaṃ kṛtavamanaṃ ṣaḍahānantaraṃ snehasvedopapāditaṃ bhūyaḥ punaḥ śleṣmakāle dvādaśadivase gate sati punastrayahaṃ laghu bhaktaṃ ṣoḍaśe hyeva koṣṭhaṃ samyagvirecayediti vāgbhaṭārthaḥ || 1 || 2 ||
svamate tu - virecanakālamāha - śaradṛtau vasante ca deyaṃ śuddhau virecanam || 3 || anyadātyayike kārye svedanaṃ śīlayedbudhaḥ |
Dī. atra śuddhau cāsvasthasya śodhanāya śaradṛtau vasante ca virecanaṃ deyaṃ | cakārādvarṣāsvapi | taduktam - ‘śītodbhavaṃ doṣacayaṃ vasante viśodhayan grīṣmajamabhrakāle | ghanātyaye vārṣikamāśu samyak prāpnoti rogānṛtujānna jātu’ iti | virecanamityanena viśeṣeṇa recanaṃ śaradāditriṣveva | anyadā himaśiśiragrīṣme kāle akārye tasmin virecanaṃ kṛte sati pūrvaṃ paścādvihitaśodhanaṃ śīlayedityabhiprāyaḥ | ata eva budha iti kālaviśeṣajño doṣaviśeṣajñaśca || 3 ||
Gū. idānīṃ virecanayogye virecanakālamāha - śaradṛtau āśvinakārtike tathā vasante caitravaiśākhe dehaśuddhau bhiṣagvirecayet | anyadā anyasminkāle vā satyapi rogotpattau budhaḥ śodhanaṃ śīlayet || 3 ||
śarīraśuddhau śodhanamuktaṃ tasya phalamāha - doṣāḥ kadācitkupyanti jitā laṅghanapācanaiḥ ||4|| ye tu saṃśodhanaiḥ śuddhā na teṣāṃ punarudbhavaḥ |
Dī. laṃghanamatropavāsaḥ, pācanasya pṛthugupādānāt | saptasu śamaneṣu dvayorgrahaṇaṃ pradhānatvāt | ye tu saṃśodhanaiḥ śuddhā doṣāsteṣāṃ na punarudbhavaḥ | ayamarthaḥ laṅghanādayaḥ śamanatvāt saṃcitān doṣān mūlān nonmūlayaṃti kiṃtu śamayanti te kadācitsvalpahetunā'pi kupyanti ye śodhanaiḥ śuddhāḥ teṣāṃ punarudbhavo na bhavati chinnasaṃcayatvāt | uktaṃ ca suśrute - ‘sañcayopahṛtā doṣā labhante nottarā gatīḥ’ iti | bahupustakeṣu ‘doṣāḥ kadācitkupyanti’ amuṃ ślokaṃ ‘pittaṃ virecanaṃ yuñjyāt’ asyāgre paṭhanti tadasaṅgatameva śodhanaparataivāsya ślokasya pūrvaślokaḥ | ‘śaratkāle vasante ca’ ityayaṃ śodhanapara eva tena śodhanaphalārtha evāyaṃ ślokaḥ | tena - ‘śaratkāle vasante ca’ asya ślokasyāgre ‘doṣāḥ kadācitkupyanti ayaṃ ślokaḥ paṭhanīyaḥ | virecanasyā'pi śodhanatvādyathākathañcitsamādheyaṃ vā || 4 ||
Gū. doṣanāśe śodhanotkṛṣṭamāha - sugamam ||4||
atha pittādau virecanam | pitte virecanaṃ yuñjyādāmodbhūte gade tathā || 5 || udare ca1 tathā'dhmāne koṣṭhaśuddhyai viśeṣataḥ ||
Dī. atha virecanaṃ keṣāṃ niyataṃ tānāha - ‘pittavirecanamiti | pittajanitānāmupadravāṇāṃ pittātmakānāṃ niyato vināśa iti kevalapitte'pi virecanamabhihitam | tathāca tantrāntare'pi -‘śarīrajātānāṃ doṣāṇāṃ krameṇa paramauṣadhaṃ | bastirvireko vamanaṃ tathā tailaghṛtaṃ madhviti’ | āmodbhūte gade tatheti | āmajanitavyādhau | āmavātavikārādāvityaryaḥ | udara iti udararogarūḍhi:| ādhmānodarāpūraḥ | koṣṭhāśuddhāviti | koṣṭhasya aśuddhiḥ baddhakoṣṭha iti prasiddhiḥ | viśeṣata ityanenaivamādīnāmapareṣāmapi virecanaṃ viśeṣeṇa kuryādityarthaḥ | atha śodhanasya prādhānyaviṣayatvena virecanamukhyatvamāha - ayaṃ ślokaḥ śodhanapadagrahaṇenānyacchodhanaparo'pi bhavati | atrācāryeṇa laṅghanapācanairityuktena vamanaviṣayasya niṣedhaparatvādvirecanādhyāye svīkṛtaḥ | nanu ye'pi laṅghanapācanairjitāste punaḥ kathaṃ saṃbhavanti ? ucyate | kadācidatisvalpahetulābhena jitā apyajitāḥ doṣamūlānucchedāt mūlabhūtāśayagatadoṣadūṣyasamudbhavāt samyagjitāḥ alpamapi hetuṃ prāpya punaḥ kupyantīti bhāvaḥ | tena teṣāṃ punarudbhava iti | saṃśodhanena mūladoṣajanyādalpahetunā na kadāpi prakopaḥ balavattarahetunā bhavatyeva || 5 ||
Gū. yeṣu rogeṣu virecanaṃ dīyate tānāha - pitte pittavikāre, āmodbhūte āmavātādau | udare udararoge | ādhmāne koṣṭhāśuddhau baddhakoṣṭhādau || 5 ||
idānīṃ ye virecane niṣiddhāstānāha - bālavṛddhāvatisnigdhaḥ kṣatakṣīṇo bhayānvitaḥ || 6 || śrāntastṛṣārtaḥ sthūlaśca garbhiṇī ca navajvarī | navaprasūtā nārī ca mandāgniśca madātyayī || 7 || śalyārditaśca rūkṣaśca na virecyā vijānatā |
Dī. virecyā iti | viśeṣeṇa recyā na bhavantītyabhiprāyeṇālparecanasyā prāptireva | ata eva bālavṛddhayorapyayaṃ niṣedhaḥ sāmānyaḥ | viśeṣeṇa tu virekasādhyavyādhyupagrastayoḥ kiṃciddeyamityuktaṃ | tathāca - ‘agnikṣāravirekaistu bālavṛddhau vivarjayet | tatsādhyeṣu vikāreṣu mṛdvīṃ kuryāt laghukriyām’ iti | niṣiddhācaraṇamanyatrāpyuktam - ‘atyantapittādviparītadehān vireca- yettānapi mandamandam’ iti | tānapi niṣiddhabālavṛddhādīn | atisnigdha ityatisnehapānādiyuktaḥ | tathāhi ‘navā'tisnigdhakāyasya dadyātsnehavirecanaṃ | doṣāḥ prabhāvitā bhūyo līyante tena vartmasu’ iti | kṣatakṣīṇā iti | dhātuśoṣaṇatvena śoṣī kathitaḥ | sa ca rājayakṣmabhedaḥ | bhayānvito bhayayuktaḥ | bhayagrahaṇatvena śokābhitaptasya dīnasya vā niṣedho darśitaḥ | yaduktaṃ ‘kṣīṇastathā rūkṣitabālavṛddhadīno'tha śoṣī bhayaśokataptaḥ’ ityādi | sthūlo bahumedonvitaḥ | mandāgnirapi kapholbaṇatvāt | rūkṣo vātolbaṇa iti | athavā rūkṣa ityasnigdhaḥ | tathāhyuktaṃ ‘virūkṣaṃ snehasātmyaṃ tu bhūyaḥ saṃsnihya recaye’diti | vijānatetyanena virecanaviṣayavikalpaviśeṣajñena vaidyenetyarthaḥ | yataḥ ‘virecanairyānti narā vināśamajñaprayuktairavirecanīyā’ iti || 6 || 7 ||
Gū. avirecanayogyānāha - ete bālādayo vijānatā viśeṣajñapuruṣeṇa na virecyāḥ || 6 || 7 ||
virecanayogyānāha - jīrṇajvarī garavyāpto vātaraktī bhagandarī || 8 || arśaḥ pāṇḍūdaragranthihṛdrogārucipīḍitāḥ | yonirogapramehārtā gulmaplīhavraṇārditāḥ || 9 || vidradhicchardivisphoṭavisūcīkuṣṭhasaṃyutāḥ | karṇanāsāśirovakragudameḍhrāmayānvitāḥ || 10 || plīhaśothākṣirogārtā: krimikṣīṇānilārditā: | śūlino mūtraghātārtā virekārhā narā matāḥ || 11 ||
Dī. virekasādhyān rogānāha - jīrṇajvarādayo virekārhāḥ narā matāḥ | ācāryāṇāmiti śeṣaḥ | jīrṇajvarīti purāṇajvarī tasya virekasādhyatvāt | garavyāpta iti garaṃ kṛtrimaṃ viṣaṃ tena vyāptaḥ duḥkhitaḥ | tathā'rśādipīḍitāḥ | tathā yonirogādibhirarditāḥ | tathā vidradhyādiyutāḥ | tathā karṇanāsāśirovakragudameḍhrāmayānvitā iti | karṇādijanitāmayānvitā ityarthaḥ | tenāmayaśabdo karṇādibhiḥ saha saṃbadhyate | plīhaśothākṣirogārtā iti plīhādirogavyāptā ityarthaḥ | krimikṣīṇānilārditā iti | kṛmyādibhirarditāḥ pīḍitāḥ | virekārhā iti virecanayogyā ityarthaḥ || 8-11 ||
Gū. jīrṇajvarī cirajvarī, garavyāpto viṣayukta:, udaram udararogiṇaṃ, granthirogaḥ | nanūdaraṃ pūrvamuktamatrāpyucyate tatkathaṃ na punaruktiḥ ? ucyate - udare punaruktirvirecanārthaṃ | yadvā udaragranthirbhinnarogaḥ | spaṣṭamanyat || 8-11 ||
atha virecanajñānārthaṃ koṣṭhabhedānāha - bahupitto mṛduḥ koṣṭho jahuśleṣmā ca madhyamaḥ | bahuvātaḥ krūrakoṣṭho durvirecya: sa kathyate || 12 ||
Dī. bahupittaḥ koṣṭhaḥ mṛduḥ, bahukapho madhyaḥ, bahuvātaḥ krūraḥ | madhyamena śodhanena hīnayogī krūraḥ | atiyogī mṛduḥ, samyagyogī madhyaḥ | saṃsargasannipātajakoṣṭhe yogavāhitvaṃ kaphasya jñeyaṃ vacanabalāt | yadāha khāraṇādiḥ - ‘vātolbaṇā syādbhahaṇī krūrakoṣṭhasya dehinaḥ | pittalā mṛdukoṣṭhasya yogavāhī tayoḥ kaphaḥ’ iti | tena vātakaphābhyāṃ krūraḥ, madhyo vātapittābhyāṃ, sannipātācca mṛduḥ | nanu bahuvātaśleṣmabhyāṃ krūraḥ, iti suśrutena bahukaphasya krūratvamuktam | iha tu madhyamatvamiti virodhaḥ ? maivaṃ - suśrutavākyasya saṃsargaparatvāditi bopadevapaṇḍitāḥ | durvirecya iti kṛcchrātsāmādiko'pyavirecyaḥ || 12 ||
koṣṭhānurūpāṃ mātrāmāha - mṛdvī mātrā mṛdukoṣṭhe madhyakoṣṭhe ca madhyamā | krūre tīkṣṇā matā dravyairmṛdumadhyamatīkṣṇakaiḥ ||13||
Dī. mṛdvādidravyaiḥ mṛdvādikoṣṭhakeṣu mṛdvādimātrā matā, ācāryāṇāmiti śeṣaḥ | nanu mṛdurdravyairmṛdvādyā mātrā bhavati kimarthaṃ mṛdvādimātretītyādi ? ucyate -hīnayogātiyogasamyagyogāya, te ca vaidyādhīnāḥ | uktaṃ ca - ‘alpasyāpi mahārthatvaṃ prabhūtasyālpakarmatām | kuryāt saṃśleṣaviśleṣakālasaṃskārayuktitaḥ’ ityādi mātrājñānārthamuktam || 13 ||
Gū. virecane mṛdumadhyakrūrakoṣṭhamāha - mṛdviti | mṛdumadhyamatīkṣṇakairdravyairmṛdvīmātrādayo matāḥ| sugamam ||13||
mṛdvādidravyāṇi kāni tāni ityata āha - mṛdurdrākṣāpayaścāmbutailairapi viricyate | madhyamastrivṛtātiktārājavṛkṣairviricyate || 14 || krūraḥ snukpayasāhemakṣīrīdantīphalādibhiḥ |
Dī. mṛduriti | mṛduḥ koṣṭhaḥ drākṣādibhiḥ samyak viricyate | tatra drākṣā gostanī, payaḥ godugdham uṣṇāmbu uṣṇodakam, pānīyatvena madhyame'pi grahaṇam | athavāmbuśabdaḥ sārakamastvādidravopalakṣaṇaḥ, tailamairaṇḍatailam | madhyastu madhyakoṣṭhaḥ na krūro na mṛduriti madhyapadābhidhānāt | sa ca trivṛtādibhiḥ samyak viricyate | trivṛtā śyāmā, tiktā kaṭukī, rājavṛkṣo girimālakaḥ| krūra iti | krūrakoṣṭhaḥ snukpayohemakṣīryādibhirviricyate | snuk sehuṇḍaḥ, hemakṣīrī svarṇakṣīrī, dantīphalaṃ jayapālaḥ | ādigrahaṇāt kumbhātilvakādayaḥ || 14 ||
Gū. tāni dravyāṇyevāha - payo dugdham, tailaiḥ eraṇḍāditailaiḥ, trivṛtā niḥsṛtā, tiktā kaṭukī, rājavṛkṣo girimālakaḥ, snuk sehuṇḍaḥ tasya dugdham, hemakṣīrī svarṇakṣīrī dantī jayapālaḥ | ādiśabdāt indravāruṇībrahmalavaṇyādayaḥ || 14 ||
virekasyottamādimātrāyai vāṅgakailiṅgakaparimāṇamāha - mātrottamā virekasya triṃśadvegaiḥ kaphāntagā || 15|| vegairviṃśatibhirmadhyā hīnoktā daśavegakaiḥ |
Dī. mātrottameti | pravarā vegamātrā triṃśadvegairjñaiyā, tathā kaphāntageti kailiṅgakaṃ mānam | tathā viṃśatibhirvegairmadhyā, tathā daśabhirvegairhīnā | uktaṃca vāgbhaṭe - ‘jaghanyamadhyapravare tu vegāścatvāra iṣṭā vamane ṣaḍaṣṭau | daśaiva te dvitriguṇā vireke prasthastathā śoṇitamokṣaṇe ca’ iti | tulāmānamapyuktaṃ tenaiva - ‘prasthastathā syāddvicaturguṇaśca | dvitrānsaviṭkānapanīya vegānmeyaṃ vireke vamane tu pītam’ iti | keciduttamasya vāyoḥ krūrakoṣṭasya ca uttamā mātrā tīkṣṇā'bhimatā, ata eva kaphāntagānmeyaṃ vireke vamane tu pītamiti, madhyamā'pi tasya mṛdukoṣṭhasya hīnā iti vyākhyānayanti || 15 ||
Gū. uttamādibhedena virekapramāṇamāha - mātrottameti | sugamam || 15 ||
idānīṃ kaṣāyaguṭikācūrṇātmakānāṃ virecanadravyāṇāṃ parimāṇamāha - dvipalaṃ śreṣṭhamākhyātaṃ madhyamaṃ ca palaṃ bhavet || 16|| palārdhaṃ ca kaṣāyāṇāṃ kanīyastu virecanam | kalkamodakacūrṇānāṃ karṣaṃ madhvājyalehataḥ || 17 || karṣadvayaṃ palaṃ vā'pi vayorogādyapekṣayā |

Dī. tasya vamanaṃ mṛdu kārayet | tasya virekārditasya vamanaṃ mṛdu kārayet | kiṃ kṛtvā ? śītāmbubhiḥ śītajalaiḥ śarīraṃ siktvā siñcayitvā | taṇḍulāmbubhiriti | taṇḍuladhāvanodakaiḥ | madhumiśrairiti | taṇḍuladhāvanairmadhumiśrairityarthaḥ | śītairiti | śītavīryadravyairlepamāha - sahakāratvaca ityādi | sahakāra āmraḥ tasya tvacaḥ kalkaḥ dadhnā sauvīrakeṇa kāñjikabhedena peṣayitvā nābhipralepena ulbaṇamatīsāraṃ hanyāt | bhojanamāha - ajākṣīramityādi | kṣīrādikaṃ raktaśālibhiḥ ṣaṣṭikairvā saha bhojayet | kṣīrasya kevalasyā'pi pānamāhureke saṃgrāhakatvāt | rasaṃ māṃsarasaṃ, vaiṣkiraṃ hāriṇaṃ vā | viṣkirā lāvatittirādayaḥ | śālibhiriti raktaśālyādibhiḥ | ṣaṣṭikairiti prasiddhaiḥ | svalpamityanena bhṛṣṭataṇḍulabhavaṃ śāktukamapi deyaṃ | masūrairapi svalpaṃ bhojanaṃ deyam | svalpamajīrṇabhayāt | athavā śītaiḥ śītavīryairdravyaiḥ saṃgrahaṇaṃ kuryāt iti || 41-43 ||
Gū. ativiriktopadrave pratīkāramāha - tasya śītāmbubhiḥ śītajalai: śarīraṃ siktaṃ sevitaṃ kuryāt | tathā madhumiśraistaṇḍulāmbubhirmṛdu vamanaṃ kārayet | pratīkārāntaramāha - sahakāraḥ āmraḥ tasya tvacaḥ dadhnā sauvīraṃ kāñjikaṃ tena piṣṭvā lepayet | sa lepaḥ ulvaṇamatīsāraṃ hanti | atirekagrahaṇārthaṃ pathyamāha - śīteḥ saṃgrāhibhirdravyaiḥ saṃgrahaṇaṃ bhiṣak kuryāt, ajākṣīraṃ pāyayet tathā hāriṇaṃ hariṇamāṃsajaṃ rasaṃ vaiṣkiraṃ kukkuṭāṇḍaṃ māṃsarasaṃ vā ṣaṣṭikaiḥ śālibhirmasūrairvā bhojayet | svalpaṃ tuccham | tathā śuṇṭhyādibhiḥ śītairdravyairdāḍimādyairbhiṣak saṃgrahaṇaṃ kuryāt || 41-43 ||
samyagviriktasya pācanādiprakāramāha - lāghavaṃ manasastuṣṭiranulomaṃ gate'nile || 44|| suviriktaṃ naraṃ jñātvā pācanaṃ pāyayenniśi |
Dī. lāghavaṃ śarīrasyeti | manasastuṣṭiriti | cittasaṃtoṣaḥ | anile vāyau anulomam adhobhāgaṃ gate sati etādṛśalakṣaṇena suviriktaṃ naraṃ jānīyāt | evaṃ jñātvā tasyā'pi pācanaṃ niśi dadyāt | pācanamaśeṣasyātra boddhavyaṃ | tacca mudgayūṣādikamityeke | yaduktaṃ ‘virecanānāṃ sarveṣāṃ dadhyannaṃ stambhane hitam | āmānte ca pradātavyamanyathā mudgamūṣaka’miti | kecittu pācanaṃ pāyayenniśītipadaṃ vinā paṭhanti na tu samyagviriktasyāyameva kramaḥ | yathāprāptiśca viṭpittakaphānilā na samyagviriktasya bhaveyuḥ krameṇeti | na caivaṃ kaphāntapadavyāghātaḥ | tena pācanapādaḥ keṣāṃcinmanoharaṃ | yato vāyurhi niḥśeṣaṃ kaphe kṛte āmāśaye śūnyatayā kevalaḥ saratīti | yadvā ācāryeṇa padapūraṇārthaṃ likhitam | tenāsyā'nyo'bhiprāyaḥ | pācanaṃ rātrāvevetyarthaḥ || 44||
Gū. idānīṃ suviriktalakṣaṇamāha - dehasya lāghave sati manaḥ saṃtuṣṭyāṃ satyām anile vāyau anulomaṃ gate svamārge prāpte sati lakṣaṇairnaraṃ suviriktaṃ jñātvā niśi pācanaṃ eraṇḍaśuṇṭhīdhānyajaṃ pāyayet || 44 ||
virecanasya phalamāha -- indriyāṇāṃ balaṃ buddheḥ prasādaṃ vahnidīpanam || 45 || dhātusthairyaṃ vaya:sthairyaṃ bhavedrecanasevanāt |
Dī. indriyāṇāmiti | cakṣurādīnāṃ balaṃ svasvaviṣayagrāhakatāśaktyatiśayaḥ | buddheḥ prasādaḥ prasannatā, vahneḥ kāyāgneḥ dīpanaṃ bhavati | dhātūnāṃ rasādīnāṃ sthairyaṃ mārgasya śuddhatvāt | tenaiva hetunā vayasaḥ sthairyaṃ karoti | uktaṃca vāgbhaṭe - ‘buddheḥ prasādaṃ balamindriyāṇāṃ dhātusthiratvaṃ jvalanasya dīptiḥ | cirācca pākaṃ, vayasaḥ karoti saṃśodhanaṃ samyagupāsyamānam’ iti ||45||
Gū. recanasevanātkiṃ bhavati ? ityāha - indriyāṇāṃ netrādīnāṃ balaṃ sthairyaṃ buddheḥ prasādam || 45 ||
kṛtavirecanasya pravātādīn niṣedhayati - pradātasevāśītāmbusnehābhyaṅgamajīrṇatām || 46 || vyāyāmaṃ maithunaṃ caiva na seveta virecitaḥ |
Dī. virecitaḥ kṛtavirecanaḥ pravātasevādīn na seveta, prakṛṣṭavātasevanaṃ tasya ca doṣakaratvāt | śītāmbusevanaṃ srotorodhādikāraṇāt | snehābhyaṅgasyāgnimāndyakaratvāt | uktaṃ ca - ‘varjyo'bhyaṅgaḥ kaphagrastastathāsaṃśuṣyajīrṇibhiḥ’ iti | cakārāt kṣaṇajāgaraṇavegānāṃ ca rodhanaṃ krodhaśokahimātapātibhāṣyatyāsanasaṃsthitinīcātyuccopavidhānāhaḥsvapnāni ||46||
Gū. virecananiṣedhavargamāha - virekataḥ pravātasevādyaṃ na seveta ityanvayaḥ || 46 ||
atha viriktasya pathyārthaṃ peyādi dravyāṇyāha - śāliṣaṣṭikamudgādyairyavāgūṃ bhojayetkṛtām || 47 || jaṅghālaviṣkirāṇāṃ vā rasaiḥ śālyodanaṃ hitam | iti śrīdāmodarasūnunā śārṅgadharapraṇītāyāṃ śārṅgadharasaṃhitāyāmuttarakhaṇḍe virecanavidhirnāma caturtho'dhyāyaḥ || 4 ||
Dī. yavāgūśabdaḥ peyādyupalakṣaṇārthaḥ | tena śālyādibhiḥ kṛtāḥ peyādayo sevyāḥ | sā yavāgū: nānāvidhaṃ pūrvaṃ vyākhyātā | tathā jaṅghālaviṣkirāṇāṃ rasaiḥ śālyodanaṃ hitam | uktaṃ ca -‘krameṇānena bhuñjāno bhajetprakṛtibhojanam’ iti | saṃśodhane peyādikrama eva hitaḥ | uktaṃ ca vāgbhaṭe -‘śodhanāsravavisrāvasnehayojanalaṅghanaiḥ | yātyagnirmandatāṃ tasmātkramaṃ peyādikaṃ bhajet’ | sa ca yathā - ‘peyāṃ vilepīmakṛtaṃ kṛtaṃ ca yūṣaṃ rasaṃ trīnubhayaṃ tathaikam | krameṇa seveta naro'nnakālānpradhānasādhyāvaraśuddhiśuddhaḥ’ iti | tatra jaṅghālā hariṇaiṇādayaḥ | viṣkirā lāvatittirādayaḥ | pradhānaśuddhyā śuddhaḥ puruṣaḥ trīn, annakālān peyādīn seveta | madhyaśuddhyā śuddhaḥ dvau dvau annakālau | avaraśuddhyā śuddhaḥ ekaikamannakālaṃ peyādikaṃ seveta iti || 47 || iti śrīvāstatryānvayaprakāśavaidyabhāvasiṃhātmajenāḍhamallena viracitāyāṃ śārṅgadharadīpikāyāmuttarakhaṇḍe virecādhyāya:||
Gū. vireke pathyavargamāha - virekānantaraṃ śālyādibhiḥ kṛtāṃ yavāgūṃ bhojayet jāṅgalairjāṅgalamāṃsarasairviṣkirāṇāṃ viṣkirasaṃjñānāṃ vā māṃsarasaiḥ śālyodanaṃ hitaṃ bhavati | virekauṣadhipīte asamyagvireke punaḥ kriyāmāha vireko nikṣepakaḥ || 47 || iti śrīkāśirāmavaidyaviracitāyāṃ śārṅgadhara- gūḍhārthadīpikāyāmuttarakhaṇḍe pañcakarmaṇi virecanādhyāyaḥ || 4 ||
