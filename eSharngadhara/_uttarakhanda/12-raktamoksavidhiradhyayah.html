--- title: "12. atha raktamokṣavidhiradhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 12 order: 12 ---
dvādaśo'dhyāyaḥ | atha śoṇitavisrāvavidhiradhunā vidhīyate atha śoṇitasrāvavidhiḥ | sambandhaścāyaṃ yathā lepādayo dehakāntikarāstadvacchoṇitavisrāvaṇamapi | ataḥ śoṇitasrāvaṇamāha - śoṇitaṃ srāvayejjantorāmayaṃ prasamīkṣya ca | prasthaṃ prasthārdhakaṃ vāpi prasyārdhārdhamathāpi vā || 1 || śaratkāle svabhāvena kuryādraktasrutiṃ naraḥ | tvagdoṣagranthiśothādyā na syū raktasruteryataḥ || 2 ||
Āḍhamalla. prasthamatra sārdhatrayodaśapalāni | tathācoktaṃ ‘vamane ca vireke ca tathā śoṇitamokṣaṇe | sārdhatrayodaśapalaṃ prasthamāhurmanīṣiṇa’ iti | nanu tantrāntare prasthaṃ prasthadvayaṃ ca krameṇa raktasrāvaṃ kathitaṃ tathāhi - ‘jaghanyamadhyaprabaleṣu vegāścatvāra iṣṭā vamane ṣaḍaṣṭau | daśaiva te dvitriguṇā vireke prasthastathā śoṇitamokṣaṇe ca’ iti | tatkkāthamatra prasthaṃ prasthārdhaṃ ca kathitaṃ, katham ? ucyate | tantrāntare yadidaṃ tadbahudoṣasyāvajinaḥ puruṣasya paraṃ pramāṇamāmananti | atra tu saśeṣadoṣaraktaṃ rakṣaṇīyamityālocyācāryeṇa prasthādisaṃgrahaḥ kṛta ityadoṣaḥ | tathāhi ‘raktaṃ svabhāvādṛtuvihitatvādanāturasyāpi raktasrutiḥ praśastā’ iti | yataḥ pittaṃ śaradi kupyatīti vacanādraktasyāpi kopo bhavatīti niyamaḥ | tathāca pittaprakopeṇaiva śoṇitaṃ ca prakupyatīti| tasmāccharadi śoṇitavisrāvaṇaṃ vihitam | tatsrāvaṇāt tvagdoṣādayo'pi na syu: | tvagdoṣā aṣṭādaśa kuṣṭhāni nyacchanīlikādayaśca | granthayo vātādimilitvā śirāgranthiśca | śothā doṣaduṣṭibhavā vraṇaśothādayaḥ | ādigrahaṇāt anye'pi śoṇitajā rogā raktagulmavidradhivisarpādayaḥ || 1 || 2 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. atha śoṇitasrāvavidhiṃ vyākhyāsyāma: | āmayaṃ raktajanyaṃ kuṣṭādyaṃ rogaṃ prasamīkṣya dṛṣṭvā janto: prāṇinaḥ śoṇitaṃ srāvayet | kiyatparimitaṃ srāvayedityāha - prasthaṃ mukhyaṃ prasthārdhaṃ madhyaṃ prasthārdhārdhaṃ nyūnaṃ vā kadā raktaṃ srāvayet svabhāvena raktasrāvasvabhāvena śaratkāle raktasrutiṃ srāvaṃ kuryāt, kutaḥ ? tvagdoṣā granthiśothādyā raktasruteryato na syuḥ kuṣṭhavātaraktādayaḥ | granthirvraṇādigranthiḥ || 1 || 2 ||
ādau raktasya rasavarṇaguṇādīnāha - madhuraṃ varṇato raktamaśītoṣṇaṃ tathā guru | śoṇitaṃ snigdhavisraṃ syādvidāhaścāsya pittavat ||3|| visratā dravatā rāgaścalanaṃ vilayastathā | bhūmyādipañcabhūtānāmete raktaguṇāḥ smṛtāḥ || 4 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha śoṇitasya prakṛtiṃ vaktumāha | madhuraṃ varṇata iti | makṣikāpipīlikādīnāṃ prācuryamupasarpaṇānmādhuryamasya pratītam | varṇato raktamiti | raktamāraktam indragopaprakāśamityeke, tadevapadmālaktaguñjāphalavarṇamapyuktam | athavā vastrādilagnaṃ satprakṣālyamānamapi na vivarṇatāṃ yātītyapare | aśītoṣṇamiti | na caikāntataḥ śītaṃ nātyuṣṇamiti | yato'gnīṣomīyādraktasya tattulyaguṇayoramlaśītayorabhilāṣo raktakṣaye bhavatīti darśanāt | athavā prakopo'sya doṣaireva tanmūlatvadoṣānurūpamanuṣṇaśītam | tathāhi - ‘yasmādraktaṃ vinā doṣairna kdācitprakupyati | tasmāttasya yathādoṣaṃ kālaṃ vidyātprakopanam’ iti | snigdhavisraṃ syāditi | snigdhaṃ vikṣuṇṇaṃ visramāmagandhi vidāhaścāsya pittavaditi pittasya yena vastunā yathā vidāhastena vastunā tathā vidāhaḥ śoṇitasyāpītyarthaḥ | atha śoṇite pāñcabhautikatvaṃ pratipādayannāha - yasmādeti visrādaya: pṛthivyādīnāṃ guṇāḥ śoṇite dṛśyante tasmādraktaṃ pāñcamautikamiti | tatra visratā vigandho bhūmiguṇaḥ dravatā dravabhāgo'yamambuguṇaḥ | rāgo raktatā tejoguṇaḥ | calanaṃ naikatra sthitiśīlatvamayaṃ vātaguṇaḥ sādanamityapare | vilaya iti vilayanopyākāśaguṇaḥ | eke vilayasthāne laghuteti paṭhanti vyākhyānayanti ca | bhūmyādipañcabhūtānāmiti | ādiśabdena aptejovāyvākāśāni gṛhyante ||3 || 4 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. raktam ādau rasato madhuraṃ, varṇato raktaṃ, guṇata aśītoṣṇaṃ, tathā gurumātrayā guru, tathā snigdhaṃ, sacikkaṇaṃ, visram āmagandhi, vidāho dāhakartṛtvaṃ ca asyāpi tadeva | rakte bhūmyādīnāṃ bhūtānāṃ guṇāśca santi tānāha - visratā āmagandhitā bhūmiguṇaḥ sacchidratā vā | ete guṇāḥ bhūmyādipañcabhūtānāṃ kramātsmṛtā: ||3||4||
duṣṭaraktalakṣaṇe kiṃ bhavatītyāha - rakte duṣṭe vedanā syātpāko dāhaśca jāyate | raktamaṇḍalatā kaṇḍūḥ śothaśca piṭikodgamaḥ || 5 || vṛddhe raktāṅganetratvaṃ sirāṇāṃ pūraṇaṃ tathā | gātrāṇāṃ gauravaṃ nidrā mado dāhaśca jāyate || 6 || kṣīṇe'mlamadhurākāṅkṣa mūrcchā ca tvaci rūkṣatā | śaithilyaṃ ca sirāṇāṃ syādvātādunmārgagāmitā ||7|| aruṇaṃ phenilaṃ rūkṣaṃ paruṣaṃ tanu śīghragam | askandi sūcinistodi raktaṃ syādvātadūṣitam ||8|| pittena pītaṃ haritaṃ nīlaṃ śyāvaṃ ca visrakam | askandyuṣṇaṃ makṣikāṇāṃ pipīlānāmaniṣṭakam ||9|| śītalaṃ bahalaṃ snigdhaṃ gairikodakasaṃnibham | māṃsapeśīprabhaṃ skandi mandagaṃ kaphadūṣitam || 10 || dvidoṣaduṣṭaṃ saṃsṛṣṭaṃ triduṣṭaṃ pūtigandhakam | sarvalakṣaṇasaṃyuktaṃ kāñjikābhaṃ ca jāyate || 11 || viṣaduṣṭaṃ bhavecchyāvaṃ nāsikonmārgagaṃ tathā | visnaṃ kāñjikasaṃkāśaṃ sarvakuṣṭhakaraṃ bahu || 12 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. idānīṃ duṣṭaraktasyā'pi lakṣaṇamāha - rakte duṣṭe iti | vātādidoṣadūṣite vedanā pīḍā pāko dāhaśca vraṇeṣveva prācuryamasyābhihitam | raktamaṇḍalateti raktavarṇamaṇḍalotpattiḥ | kaṇḍūḥ pāmāviśeṣaḥ, śotho vraṇaśothaḥ, piḍakā granthādayaḥ | atha raktātivṛddhau satyāṃ kiṃ lakṣaṇaṃ tadāha -raktāṅganetratvamiti | tena raktāṅganetratā ca bhavati, sirāṇāṃ pūrṇatā tatheti | sirāścātra raktavahā jñeyāḥ, tāsāṃ pūrṇatā raktatetyarthaḥ | medo medovṛddhiḥ prāyeṇātrāsthigatamedovṛddhiḥ syādityatra viṣayaḥ yataḥ ‘sthūlāsthiṣu viśeṣeṇa majjā tvabhyantarāśritā | athetareṣu sarveṣu satataṃ meda ucyata’ iti | kṣīṇe'mlamadhurākāṅkṣeti | śoṇitakṣaye sati vātavṛddhau satyāṃ tatpratīkārārthatvādamlarasecchā | tathā raktasya dravatvāttatkṣaye sati tejovṛddhau satyāṃ madhuraprārthanā | eke kṣīṇe'mlaśītalākāṅkṣeti paṭhanti | tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ | tvagvirūkṣateti vātavṛddhitaḥ sirāṇāṃ raktavahānāṃ ca śaithilyaṃ bhavati | atiraktatvāt | ghātātprahārāt unmārgagamitā raktasyetyarthaḥ | idānīṃ duṣṭaraktasya vātādīnāṃ pratyekalakṣaṇānyāha | aruṇādyuktalakṣaṇai: kṛtvā vātadūṣitaṃ raktaṃ jānīyāt | tatrāruṇamīṣadraktaṃ kṛṣṇābhāvaṃ vyākhyānayanti | phenilaṃ phenayuktaṃ paruṣamapicchilaṃ, tanu acchaṃ, śīghragamāśupravṛtti ata evāvaskandādīni | askandi styānarahitam | sūcībhiriva nistodanaṃ gātreṣu pittena yadā duṣṭaṃ raktaṃ syāttadā etādṛśaṃ bhavati kiṃ tatpītamiti|| pītaṃ pītavarṇam | tacca nirāmāvasthāyāṃ bhavati | haritaṃ śyāmavatpratibhāsam | nīlaṃ haritaśyāvam | tacca sāmāvasthāyāṃ bhavati | śyāvaṃ haritakṛṣṇam | visrakamāmagandhikam | askandi ceti styānarahitam | bhakṣikāpipīlikānāniṣṭamiti | aniṣṭamapriyam | yataḥ tāsāṃ mate'nusaraṇādaniṣṭatvamavagantavyaṃ kaphadūṣitaṃ raktamīdṛśaṃ bhavati śītalaṃ sparśādvīryāt bahalaṃ dhanaṃ gairikodakasannibhamiti pāṇḍulohitaṃ kathitam | māṃsapeśīprabhamitistyānatvāt | pāṇḍulohitamiti bhāvaḥ | ata eva skandi kathitam | skanditvādevamavargatvamasyoktam | atha saṃsargasannipātānvitaraktavikṛterlakṣaṇānyāha - dvidoṣaduṣṭaṃ śoṇitamiti śeṣaḥ, yato dvābhyāṃ doṣābhyāṃ dūṣitaṃ tatsaṃsṛṣṭamiti kathitam | etena dvābhyāṃ lakṣaṇābhyāṃ militaṃ bhavatīti bhāvaḥ | triduṣṭaṃ raktaṃ sannipātadūṣitaṃ kathitaṃ tribhirdoṣairdūṣitaṃ triduṣṭam | tacca pūtigandhikaṃ bhavati | sarvalakṣaṇasaṃyuktamiti | sarveṣāṃ vātapittakaphānāṃ lakṣaṇairyuktamiti | tadeva kāñjikābhaṃ ca bhavatīti | idānīṃ viṣaduṣṭasya raktasyākṛtiṃ darśayannāha - viṣaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamaṃ tena duṣṭaṃ dūṣitamityarthaḥ | īdṛśaṃ kena lakṣitaṃ tadāha - śyāvamiti | haritakṛṣṇaṃ varṇato bhavati | nāsikonmārgagaṃ tatheti | nāsikayā unmārgagāmi bhavati | tena nādhaḥ pravartate iti tātparyārthaḥ | viṣaprabhāvāt nāsikopalakṣitatvāttena mukhunetrakarṇairapi pravartata iti bhāvaḥ | visramityāmagandhi | sarvakuṣṭhakaramiti viṣaprabhāvāt | tathāhi ‘viṣeṇa dūṣitaṃ raktaṃ sarvakuṣṭhakaraṃ matam’ iti || 5-12 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. duṣṭe kupite rakte vedanā pīḍā syātpākādayaśca jāyante | raktavṛddhilakṣaṇamāha - vṛddhe rakte raktāṅgādilakṣaṇaṃ bhavati raktāṅgatvaṃ raktanetratvaṃ sirāṇāṃ dhamanyādīnāṃ pūraṇaṃ raktenaiva, gātrāṇāṃ gauravaṃ gurutā, madaḥ pūgabhakṣaṇavat, dāha uṣṇatvam | kṣīṇe rakte kiṃ lakṣaṇaṃ bhavatītyāha -kṣīṇe idaṃ bhavati - amlamadhurākāṅkṣā spṛhā, mūrcchā mūrcchitaṃ kṣīṇe tvaci rūkṣatā śaithilyaṃ vātasyaiva unmārgagāmitā ca | vātaduṣṭaraktalakṣaṇamāha - vātadūṣitaṃ raktam evaṃvidhaṃ syāt | kiṃbhūtaṃ tadāha - aruṇaraktaṃ phenilaṃ phenavat, rūkṣaṃ sneharahitaṃ, tanu adhanaṃ, paruṣaṃ karkaśaṃ, śīghragamanaṃ śīlaṃ askandi, ghanatvarahitaṃ, visrāvaviśeṣaḥ srāvayuktaṃ, pittadūṣitaraktamāha - pittena dūṣitaṃ pītaharitādivarṇaṃ raktaṃ bhavati tadāha - visrakaṃ srāvagandhi askandi cāñcalyarahitaṃ makṣikādīnāṃ aniṣṭakaṃ bhakṣaṇe hitaṃ na | kaphadūṣitaraktamāha - kaphadūṣitaṃ śītalādilakṣaṇaṃ bhavatītyāha - śītalaṃ śītaguṇaṃ sparśena vā śītalaṃ, bahulaṃ bahutaraṃ, snigdhaṃ sacikkaṇaṃ, gairikodakasannibhaṃ gairikajalasadṛśaṃ, punaḥ māṃsapeśīprabhaṃ granthirūpaṃ, skandi ghanatvasadṛśaṃ, mandagaṃ mandagamanaṃ, dvidoṣatridoṣaduṣṭalakṣaṇamāha – dvidoṣaduṣṭasaṃsṛṣṭadvidoṣalakṣaṇamāha - dvidoṣaduṣṭasaṃsṛṣṭaṃ dvidoṣalakṣaṇayuktaṃ bhavati triduṣṭaṃ tridoṣaduṣṭaṃ pūtigandhaṃ durgandhaṃ tathā kāñjikābhaṃ ca jāyate | viṣaduṣṭamāha - viṣaduṣṭaṃ viṣayuktaṃ śyāmavarṇaṃ bhavet nāsikāgandhaṃ tathā unmārgagam | visram āmagandhi, kāñjikasaṃkāśaṃ kāñjikatulyaṃ tathā sarvakuṣṭhakaraṃ, tathā bahu adhikam || 5-12 ||
prakṛtistharaktalakṣaṇamāha - indragopaprabhaṃ jñeyaṃ prakṛtisthamasaṃhatam |
Āḍhamalla. atha vikṛtaraktamabhidhāya prakṛtisthaṃ darśayannāha - yaḥ prāvṛṭkālajo jantuviśeṣaḥ indravadhūriti loke | sa cānekaprakāravarṇaḥ, atra tu rakta eva gṛhyate | asaṃhatamiti nātyacchaṃ nātighanaṃ na styāyatītyanye ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. indragopo vīravadhūṭī tadvat raktaṃ tathā asaṃhatam astyānam evaṃbhūtaṃ prakṛtisthaṃ raktaṃ jñeyam ||
yeṣu raktasrāvo vihitastānāha - śothe dāhe'ṅgapāke ca raktavarṇe'sṛjaḥ srutau || 13|| vātarakte tathā kuṣṭhe sapīḍe durjaye'nile | pāṇiroge ślīpade pade ca viṣaduṣṭe ca śoṇite || 14 || granthyarbudāpacīkṣudrarogaraktādhimanthiṣu | vidārīstanarogeṣu gātrāṇāṃ sādagaurave || 15 || raktābhiṣyandatandrāyāṃ pūtighrāṇāsyadehake | yakṛtplīhavisarpeṣu vidradhau piṭikodgame ||16|| karṇauṣṭhaghrāṇavaktrāṇāṃ pāke dāhe śiroruji | upadaṃśe raktapitte raktasrāva: praśasyate || 17|| eṣu rogeṣu śṛṅgairvā jalaukālābukairapi | athavāpi sirāmokṣaiḥ kuryādraktasrutiṃ naraḥ || 18 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha raktasrāvo yeṣāṃ vihitastānāha - śothe vraṇaśothe ekāṅgaje'pi srāvyaḥ | yataḥ sarvāṅgaśotho niṣiddhaśca sa vāmlabhojananimittaḥ | pāṇḍurogyarśasodariśoṣigarbhiṇīnāṃ ca śvayathau, aṅgadāhe aṅgapāke ca | raktavarṇe'sṛjaḥ srutāviti kevalaraktapitte | sapīḍe durjaye'nile iti | tathāhi - ‘uṣṇāmlalavaṇādervātaharasyopayogātkupitena śoṇitenāvṛto vāto yadā vedanākārī tadā sirāvyadha iti | pāṇiroge dāhādau tena bāhurogo'pi gṛhyate | yaduktaṃ -śoṇitāvṛtavātajanitayorbāhuśoṣāpabāhukayorapyaṃśayorantare sirāvyadhaḥ | apacyāmiti | utpannamātrāyāmityarthaḥ | na tu rūḍhāyāṃ tasyām | kṣudrarogeṣu arūṃṣikādiṣu | vidradhāviti ṣaḍvidhavidradhiṣu madhye pacca visrāvyāḥ taduktaṃ - ‘srāvyā vidradhayaḥ pañca bhaveyuḥ sarvapādata’ iti | upadaṃśe iti | upadaṃśagrahaṇāt parivartikaśūkadoṣaśukavyāpatsu ca meḍhramadhye sirāvyadhaḥ | raktapittamiti | romakūpodgatamityeke | śeṣaṃ subodham | atha śoṇitavisrāvaṃ yairvastubhiḥ kuryāttāni darśayannāha - eṣu pūrvokteṣu śothaprabhṛtiṣu rogeṣu śṛṅgādicatuḥprakārā yathāyogyānurūpato yojyā | śṛṅgaṃ tu gośṛṅgaṃ tacca saptāṅgulavistīrṇaṃ bhavati | tathāca ‘srāve tu śvetagośṛṅgaṃ vakraṃ saptāṅgulāyataṃ | kṣiptvāntaḥ picupeśīkaṃ yojyaṃ vātayute'sṛji | aṅguṣṭhamūlavanmūlaṃ chidramagre'sya mudgavat | vastrapaṭṭāvanaddhaṃ ca raktamācūṣaṇāddharet’ iti | jalaukāḥ prasiddhāḥ, tāśca dvādaśadhā bhavanti | tāsāṃ ṣaṭ saviṣā: tāvantya eva nirviṣāśca | vistāro'pi tāsāmanyatra1 jñātavyaḥ | atra granthagauravabhayānna likhitaḥ | alābukā tumbīśabdavācyā | tasyā lakṣaṇaṃ kenaciditi darśitam | tadyathā - aṅguṣṭhāṅgulapariṇāhā ca - turaṅgulā'nāśasaṃmitā sumukhā kṛṣṇamṛdāliptatanuḥ śreṣṭhā raktā ca sevane'lābūḥ | sāntardīrghamayutā tayā ca visrāvayedraktamiti | śirāmokṣairiti śirā raktavahāstāsāṃ mokṣaḥ śastreṇa kuryāditi | mokṣo rudhirasyeti || 13-18 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. śothe dāheti śothādirogeṣu nṛṇāṃ raktasrāvaḥ praśasyata ityanvayaḥ | raktavarṇe dehe asṛjaḥ srutau sādisrāve sapīḍe pīḍāsahite anile durjaye jetumaśakye gātrasādo gātraśaithilyaṃ raktābhiṣyandam āraktanetraṃ, spaṣṭamanyat | eṣu rogeṣu kena raktaṃ srāvayedityāha - jalaukā nirviṣā purātanāśca ‘mahyakāṃ vājayavanayājyapautanayā (?) śubhā’ iti suśrutoktatvāt alābu tumbīśirā tadvidhānasthānaṃ taddvidhā nipuṇairvaidyairjñeyam || 13-18 ||
yeṣu sirāmokṣo niṣiddhaḥ tānāha - na kurvīta sirāmokṣaṃ kṛśasyātivyavāyina:| klībasya bhīrorgarbhiṇyāḥ sūtikāpāṇḍurogiṇām ||19|| pañcakarmaviśuddhasya pītasnehasya cārśasām | sarvāṅgaśothayuktānāmudaraśvāsakāsinām ||20|| chardyatīsāraduṣṭānāmatisvinnatanorapi | ūnaṣoḍaśavarṣasya gatasaptatikasya ca || 21 || āghātasrutaraktasya sirāmokṣo na śasyate | eṣāṃ cātyayike roge jalaukābhistu nirharet ||22||
