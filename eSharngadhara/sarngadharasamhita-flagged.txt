# Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā: word spacing and verse tagging — spots to check

Word spacing was added in 2026-11 by 56 agents, one per piece of the TEI file
(tools/spacing/, see its README). Each agent reported up to five places it was
unsure of; they are listed below as "NNN l.LINE", where NNN is the piece and
LINE the line within that piece. Use the form quoted to find the place in
sarngadharasamhita-full.xml (search for it).

Decisions taken across the whole text after the agents had finished:
- avagraha: a space before ' after e/o everywhere (te 'pi, khaṇḍo 'yaṃ), as in
  the Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha; ā' (vā'pi, pācanā'dhyāya) stays joined.
- particles split everywhere: tathā ca, tathā hi, yad vā, na hi, yady api,
  ity arthaḥ, yad uktaṃ, tad yathā, tac ca, yas tu …; joined: tadanu, tadabhāve,
  ityādi(-vacanāt). The full list of these consistency fixes is at the end.
- forms where the doubled consonant at a word junction is written once
  (tailamānādviguṇitam, tasmātriguṇaṃ, rasonakalkādviguṇitena) are left joined.
- tat/tad before a noun is split or joined by context (rule 5); about 40 such
  pairs are treated differently in different places (tadrasaṃ / tad rasaṃ,
  taccūrṇaṃ / tac cūrṇaṃ, tanmānaṃ / tan mānaṃ, tatsiddhaṃ / tat siddhaṃ,
  tadūrdhvaṃ / tad ūrdhvaṃ) and were left so.

Root-text segments the verse tagger left as prose (headings, colophons, a few
corrupt half-verses) are listed in sarngadharasamhita-prose.txt.
