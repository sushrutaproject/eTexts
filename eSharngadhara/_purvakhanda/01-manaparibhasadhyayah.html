Kāśīrāma Vaidya. maṅgalācaraṇam - śārṅgadharasaṃhitārthamahaṃ vyākhyātumārabhe | tatra siddhiṃ diśatu māṃ vighnādhīśo gaṇādhipaḥ || paramanipuṇo granthakṛtprāripsitanirvighnaparisamāptaye tadadhyetṝṇāmapi pratyūhavyūhopaśāntaye adhyayananirvāhāya vyutpattaye ca āśīrvādarūpamaṅgalamācarati | tasya ca kartavyatāyāṃ śiṣṭācāraḥ pramāṇam | śriyaṃ sa dadyādityādi | saḥ bhavatāṃ śriyaṃ lakṣmīṃ dadyāt puro daityastasyāriḥ śrīmānmahādevaḥ, athavā śrīśabdena saṃpat | yaduktaṃ ‘lakṣmīsarasvatīdhātrivargasaṃpadvibhūtiśobhāsu | upakaraṇaveśaracanāvidhāsu ca śrīriti prathite’ti vyāḍiḥ | sa kaḥ | yadaṅgatejaḥprasare bhavānī virājate, yasya śaṃbhoryadaṅgaṃ tasya tejaḥprasaraḥ pracāraḥ tasminbhavānī pārvatī virājate śobhate | kasmin kasyāṃ keva? himādrau nirmalacandrikāyāṃ jvalitā mahauṣadhīva himādrau himaparvate nirmalacandrikāyāṃ candrajyotsnāyāṃ madhye yathā jvalitā mahauṣadhī rājate tadvadiyamapi karpūragauravattayā himādrivat śambho pratītasya pārśve mahauṣadhīva bhavānī virājate ityarthaḥ ||1|| ādau cikitsānimittamutkṛṣṭayogasaṃhārārthaṃ padyamāha -prasiddheti | munibhiḥ prayuktā ye siddhayogāḥ cikitsakairbahuśaḥ anubhūtāśca yogāḥ cikitsādhikāriṇaḥ teṣāṃ yogānāṃ susaṃgrahaḥ śārṅgadhareṇa vidhīyate kathyate | yujyata iti yogaḥ kvāthacūrṇādigaṇaḥ || 2 ||
prasiddhayogā munibhiḥ prayuktā- ścikitsakairye bahuśo'nubhūtāḥ | vidhīyate śārṅgadhareṇa teṣāṃ susaṃgrahaḥ sajjanarañjanāya || 2 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha grantho'yaṃ sakalajanopakārārthaṃ1 bhavatu iti matvā sadgranthasya sambandhaḥ kathanīyaḥ | tadyathā ‘siddhiḥ śrotṛpravṛttīnāṃ sambandhakathanādyataḥ | tasmāt sarveṣu śāstreṣu sambandhaḥ pūrvamucyate |’ iti | anena tāvat pūrvaṃ tamāha -prasiddhayogā ityādi | ye prasiddhayogāḥ santi teṣāṃ susaṃgrahaḥ sajjanarañjanāya mayā śārṅgadhareṇa vidhīyate viśeṣeṇa kriyata ityabhiprāyaḥ | natu kriyate yataścarakasuśrutādimunibhiritastato vistṛtā mayā saṃhṛtya prakārāntareṇa likhyata iti bhāvaḥ | etena granthasya viśiṣṭatvaṃ darśitam | kīdṛśā yogāḥ? munibhiḥ prayuktā iti | traikālikadarśī puruṣātiśayo hi munirucyate | taiḥ pūrvaṃ kathitā ityarthaḥ | etena granthasya prāmāṇyaṃ kathitam | punaḥ kīdṛśāḥ ? cikitsakairvaidyairbahuśo vāraṃvāramanubhūtā | nāmarūpayojanādibhirdṛṣṭapratyayā ityarthaḥ | etenātra sajjanānāṃ darśanādyutkaṇṭhā syādityabhiprāyaḥ || 2 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. ādau cikitsānimittamutkṛṣṭayogasaṃhārārthaṃ padyamāha - gū. - prasiddheti | munibhiḥ prayuktā ye siddhayogāḥ cikitsakairbahuśaḥ anubhūtāśca yogāḥ cikitsādhikāriṇaḥ teṣāṃ yogānāṃ susaṃgrahaḥ śārṅgadhareṇa vidhīyate kathyate | yujyata iti yogaḥ kvāthacūrṇādigaṇaḥ || 2 ||
hetvādirūpākṛtisātmyajāti- bhedaiḥ samīkṣyāturasarvarogān | cikitsitaṃ karṣaṇabṛṃhaṇākhyaṃ kurvīta vaidyo vidhivatsuyogaiḥ || 3 ||
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ ‘rogamādau parīkṣeta tato'nantaramauṣadham’ ityādi matamālocya śārṅgadharo'pyāha - hetviti | vaidya āturasarvarogān samīkṣya paścāt cikitsitaṃ vidhivat kurvīteti | anyathā doṣaḥ syāt | tathāhyuktam – ‘yastu rogamavijñāya karmāṇyārabhate bhiṣak | na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim’ iti | kaiḥ kṛtvā samīkṣyetyāha - heturnidānam, ādirūpaṃ pūrvarūpam, ākṛtiḥ svarūpam, sātmyamupaśayaḥ, jātiḥ samprāptiriti; eṣāṃ bhedaiḥ nānātmakatvena ete vyastāḥ samastāśca vyādhibodhakā jñeyā ityarthaḥ | yadvā heturādiryeṣāṃ te hetvādayaḥ, te ca rūpādayaśca tairityarthaḥ | etena hetvādipañcabhiḥ rūpādibhiśca āturarogān parīkṣyeti bhāvaḥ | tathāhi vāgbhaṭe –‘darśanasparśanapraśnaiḥ parīkṣetātha rogiṇam | rogaṃ nidānaprāgrūpalakṣaṇopaśayāptibhi:’ iti | hetvādipañcakaṃ pūrvaṃ vyākhyātameva | rūpāditrayaṃ yathārūpamiti cākṣuṣaṃ tena śarīrasya sthūlakṛśatāvarṇavikārādayaḥ, etena darśanajñānamalaṅkaroti | ākṛtiriti āsamantādeva kṛtiḥ kṛtikāraṇamiti samucitakauśalyenaiva nāḍyādibhiḥ śītoṣṇādikajñānarūpamityarthaḥ, etena sparśanajñānamalaṅkaroti | sātmyena hitaṃ kathyate bhavatāṃ kiṃ sātmyamityādivākyena praśnajñānamalaṅkaroti | athavā sātmyamabhilaṣitaṃ vadantyanye yato yena yadvāñchitaṃ bhavati tasya tenaiva doṣādīnāṃ kṣīṇavṛddhatvaṃ jñāyate, iti darśanādikaṃ trayaṃ kathitam | jātiśabdena viśiṣṭendriyajñānamabhidhīyate | yataḥ - suśrute ‘ṣaḍvidho hi rogāṇāṃ vijñānopāyaḥ, tadyathā pañcabhiḥ śrotrādibhiḥ praśnena ceti’ | ato darśanādayastraya evādau vyākhyātāḥ, śeṣāḥ śrotrādayo jātibhedena kathitāḥ | taduktaṃ suśrutācāryeṇa ‘tatra śrotrendriyavijñeyā viśeṣā rogeṣu praṇaṣṭaśalyavijñānīyādiṣu vakṣyante saphenaṃ raktamīrayannanilaḥ saśabdo nirgacchatītyevamādayaḥ | sparśanendriyavijñeyāḥ śītoṣṇaślakṣṇakarkaśamṛdukaṭhinatvādayaḥ sparśaviśeṣā jvaraśoṣādiṣu | cakṣurindriyavijñeyāḥ śarīropacayāpacayāyurlakṣaṇabalavarṇavikārādayāḥ | rasanendriyavijñeyāḥ pramehādiṣu rasaviśeṣāḥ | gandhendriyavijñeyā riṣṭaliṅgādiṣu vraṇānāmavraṇānāṃ ca gandhaviśeṣāḥ’ | iti hetvādayo vyākhyātāḥ, tatrādyaḥ pakṣo vyavahārasiddho dvitīyastu kalpito'pi sādhuḥ | samīkṣyeti samyak prakāreṇa vicārya, etadgrahaṇaṃ tajjñānavicchedārtham | yataḥ ‘mithyādṛṣṭā vikārā hi durākhyātāstathaiva ca | tathā duḥparidṛṣṭāśca mohayeyuścikitsakam’ iti | cikitsā rogāpanayanaṃ karma vyādhipratīkāraḥ dhātusāmyārthā kriyā cetyādiparyāyavācakaiḥ śabdaiścikitsā kathitā | tathācoktaṃ – ‘caturṇāṃ bhiṣagādīnāṃ śastānāṃ dhātuvaikṛte | pravṛttirdhātusāmyārthā cikitsetyabhidhīyate’ iti | taccātra dvividhaṃ karma karṣaṇabṛṃhaṇākhyamiti | karṣaṇamiti pravṛttadoṣāṇāṃ, bṛṃhaṇamiti kṣīṇānām; tathāca - ‘kṣīṇā vardhayitavyā’ ityādi | tathā –‘rogastu doṣavaiṣamyaṃ doṣasāmyamarogitā’ iti vacanāt | suyogairiti śobhanadravyakṛtaprayogaiḥ śīghramārogyotpādakairityarthaḥ ||3||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. auṣadhayogānāṃ prayogastu hetvādibhirvyādhiṃ jñātvā kartavyastadvijñānārthaṃ ślokamāha -hetvādītyādi | hetvādirūpākṛtisātmyajātibhedaiḥ āturasya sarvarogān samīkṣya dṛṣṭvā vaidyaḥ karṣaṇa-bṛṃhaṇākhyaṃ cikitsitaṃ kurvītetyanvayaḥ | karṣaṇaṃ kṛśīkaraṇaṃ bṛṃhaṇaṃ balavardhanaṃ ākhyā nāma yasya tat cikitsitaṃ hetvādisarūpaṃ ca ākṛtiśca sātmyaṃ ca jātibhedaśca te hetvādirūpākṛtisātmyajātibhedāstaiḥ hetvādīni nidānapañcakaheturnidānaṃ, rūpaṃ gaurādirvarṇaviśeṣaḥ | tathā cakṣuṣā yadgrāhyaṃ vastujātaṃ saṃsthānādi tadapi rūpam | ākṛtistu cakṣurādīndriyasvarapraticchāyākriyādiṣu anyeṣvapi bhāveṣu prākṛteṣu nimittataḥ vikṛtiryā samāse'riṣṭaṃ taditi lakṣayet | sātmyaṃ deśādi taiḥ, ādiśabdātprāgrūpārūpopaśayasaṃprāptayo gṛhyante hetvādirūpaṃ hetvādikārtsnyaṃ tena ākṛtyā ca sātmyena ca jātiḥ saṃprāptiḥ tasyā ye bhedāḥ saṃkhyāvikalpaprādhānyabalakālāḥ | vaidyaḥ āturasya sarvarogān samīkṣya proktaṃ cikitsitaṃ kurvīta vyādheḥ kiyanto'pyupāyā bhavanti || 3 ||
divyauṣadhīnāṃ bahavaḥ prabhedā vṛndārakāṇāmiva visphuranti | jñātveti saṃdehamapāsya dhīraiḥ saṃbhāvanīyā vividhaprabhāvāḥ || 4 ||
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ dravyāṇāṃ vividhaprabhāvāḥ santi teṣāṃ hetubhiḥ parīkṣāṃ nirākartumāha -divyauṣadhīnāmiti | divyauṣadhīnāṃ bahavo bhedā vṛndārakāṇāmiva visphuranti prakaṭībhavanti ityarthaḥ - iti jñātvā vicārya tadā sandehamapāsya dūrīkṛtya vividhaprabhāvā aparimitaśaktayo'vicchinnaśaktayo vā sambhāvanīyāḥ teṣāṃ sambhāvanā kartavyetyarthaḥ | dhīrairiti jñeyam | etenā'cintyo hi maṇimantrauṣadhīnāṃ prabhāva iti tātparyārthaḥ | divyauṣadhīnāmiti divyagrahaṇamatra dravyāṇāṃ viśiṣṭatvajñānārtham | vṛndārakāṇāmiti yathā devānāṃ bhedā aparimitāstathaiva dravyabhedā ityabhiprāyaḥ | prabhāvāścetyacintanīyāḥ | dhīrairiti gambhīrabuddhibhiḥ | dhīrā hi bāhyādhyātmikānāṃ hitāhitaparicchedakāriṇo bhavanti, ato dhīragrahaṇamatrāniścayārthajñāpakatvavyavacchedārtham || 4 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. divyeti | asyārthaḥ - divyauṣadhīnāṃ bahavaḥ prabhedāḥ visphuranti tān jñātvā saṃdehaṃ vāpāsya dhīraiḥ vividhaprabhāvāḥ saṃbhāvanīyā vicāraṇīyāḥ | vṛndārakā devāsteṣāmiva yathā devānāṃ bahavaḥ prabhedāstathauṣadhīnāmiti | divyā yā oṣadhyaḥ tāsāṃ bahavaḥ prabhedā visphuranti | keṣāmiva ? vṛndārakāṇāmiva tānbhedān vanasthānādibhyaḥ jñātvā saṃdehaṃ apāsya dūrīkṛtya karma kārayedityarthaḥ ||4 ||
