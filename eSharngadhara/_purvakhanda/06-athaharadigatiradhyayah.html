ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ | athāhārādigatiradhyāyaḥ | yātyāmāśayamāhāraḥ pūrvaṃ prāṇānilerita: | mādhuryaṃ phenabhāvaṃ ca ṣaḍraso'pi labheta saḥ || 1 ||
Āḍhamalla. idānīmāhārādigatiradhyāyaḥ pūrvaṃ darśitastamāha - atheti | yātītyādi | āhāraḥ pāñcabhautikasvarūpo'śanamityarthaḥ | taduktaṃ suśrute - ‘pañcabhūtātmake1 dehe āhāraḥ pāñcabhautikaḥ’ iti | evaṃvidhaḥ sa āhāraḥ pūrvamādau āmāśayaṃ yāti prāpnotītyarthaḥ | āmāśayaṃ ca nābhistanāntare koṣṭhaviśeṣamāhuḥ, yathā - ‘nābhistanāntare jantorāhurāmāśayaṃ budhāḥ’ iti | kīdṛśastamāha -prāṇānilerita iti | prāṇavāyunā prerita iti bhāvaḥ | ‘hṛdi prāṇānilo mataḥ’ iti vacanāt | tatra ṣaḍrasayukto'pi sa āhāro mādhuryaṃ madhurarasabāhulyaṃ phenabhāvaṃ ca labhate prāpnoti kaphasthānagatatvāt, kapho'pi madhura iti prasiddhaḥ; ata evāha tantrāntare - bhuktvādau kaphasya vṛddhiriti rasasādharmyāt asyaivāmājīrṇasaṃjñā, yataḥ ‘mādhuryamannaṃ smṛtamāmasaṃjñam iti || 1 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. yadā śarīrī jātastadā āhārāpekṣā jātā, ata āhārapākakramamāha - yātītyādi | pūrvaṃ prathamataḥ prāṇānileritaḥ prāṇavāyupreritaḥ āhāraḥ coṣmādipañcavidhalakṣaṇaḥ āmāśayaṃ yāti nābhistanāntaramāmāśayaḥ tatra gatvā mādhuryaṃ madhurabhāvaṃ phenabhāvaṃ ca labhate | kiṃbhūtaḥ āhāraḥ ṣaḍrasaḥ | vāgbhaṭe caivaṃ ‘ādau ṣaḍarasamapyannaṃ madhurībhūtamīrayet| phenabhāvaṃ rasaṃ jātaṃ vidāhi hyamlatāṃ gatam’ iti || 1 ||
atha pācakapattena vidagdhaścāmlatāṃ vrajet | tataḥ samānamarutā grahaṇīmabhinīyate || 2 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha sthānāntaraprāptimāha - atha pācaketi | athānantaram sa evāmāśayasthitāhāraḥ pācakapittena pācakāgnitejasā vidagdhaḥ san pacyamānā'bhimukhaḥ sannamlatāṃ vrajet, yato'mlātpittaṃ jāyate tatrāvasthāyāṃ vidagdhājīrṇasaṃjñaṃ gatamamlabhāvamiti | āmāśayāccalito'prāptagrahaṇīpado yadā bhavati tatra sthānāntaraṃ vadantyeke, naivaṃ sarvamataṃ, kintu pācakāgnisthānasamīpavartī yadā bhavati tadā vidagdho bhavatītyeke bhāṣante | svamate tu āmāśayāvasthāyāmeva pittavaidagdhyādamla eva syāt yataḥ pittavidagdhamamlatāmupaiti | idānīṃ tṛtīyāvasthāgatasya kāryamāha - tata ityādi | tatastasmātsthānāt samānamarutā nābhigatavāyunā kṛtvā grahaṇīṃ vahnisthānamabhinīyate prāpyate āhāra iti śeṣaḥ | etat sthānaṃ kalāśabdena vācyaṃ yataḥ ‘ṣaṣṭhī pittadharā nāma yā kalā parikīrtitā | pakvāmāśayamadhyasthā grahaṇī sā prakīrtitā |’ iti | grahaṇyāmapi koṣṭhavahninā kṛtvā pācyate, koṣṭhagrahaṇaṃ dhātvagninirāsārtham | tasmātkaṭuko jāyate iti tātparyārthaḥ | yatkaṭukājjāyate vāyuriti tadviṣṭabdhājī- rṇanāhurityeke | yaduktaṃ ‘kiñcidvipakvaṃ bhṛśatodaśūlaṃ viṣṭabdhamānaddhaviruddhavātam’ iti || 2 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. amlatvaṃ ca prāpnotītyāha -- athetyanantaraṃ pācakapittena vidagdhaḥ amlatāṃ vrajet tataḥ samānamarutā grahaṇī abhinīyate prāpyate | grahaṇī kalā | grahaṇyāṃ kiṃ bhavatītyāha - koṣṭhavahninā yadā grahaṇyāṃ pacyate tadā kaṭurjāyate tadvidho bhavati saṃpakvātsamyak pakvāt rasaḥ bhavati apakvādāmasaṃbhavo bhavatīti yojanā || 2 ||
(ṣaṣṭhī pittadharā2 nāma yā kalā parikīrtitā | pakvāmāśayamadhyasthā grahaṇītyabhidhīyate || 3 || grahaṇyāṃ pacyate koṣṭhavahninā jāyate kaṭuḥ | raso bhavati saṃpakvādapakvādāmasaṃbhavaḥ || 4 || vahnerbalena mādhuryaṃ snigdhatāṃ yāti tadrasaḥ | puṣṇāti dhātūnakhilānsamyakpakvo'mṛtopamaḥ ||5|| mandavahnividagdhaśca kaṭuścāmlo bhavedrasaḥ | viṣabhāvaṃ vrajedvāpi kuryādvā rogasaṃkaram || 6 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha tādṛśasyāhārasya dvividhāmavasthāṃ vaktumāha - rasa iti | vahnisthāne sthitasyāhārasya samyak pākādraso bhavati | asamyak pākādāmaḥ sambhavati iti | rasa iti samāgniparipācitatvāt sārabhūto yo dravāṃśaḥ sa rasa ityucyate, āmaśca aparipākādbhavati | taduktaṃ ‘jaṭharānaladaurbalyādavipakvastu yo rasaḥ | sa āmasaṃjñako dehe sarvadoṣaprakopakaḥ’ iti | idānīṃ tayo rasāmayorvyāpāraṃ darśayannāha - vahneriti | tadrasa iti pūrvoktaḥ, vahnerbaleneti rasagatā'gniprābalyāt madhuratāṃ snigdhatāṃ ca yāti prāpnoti sapta vākhilān raktādīn dhātūn puṣṇāti tasmāt samyak pakvo rasaḥ amṛtopamaḥ syāt | yathā ‘amṛto jīvano madhurādiguṇayuktaḥ’ iti śrutiḥ | tathāyamapi jīvanadhāraṇatarpaṇādiguṇayukta ityabhiprāyaḥ saumyatvāt | taduktaṃ suśrute - ‘sa khalu dravonusārī snehanajīvanatarpaṇadhāraṇādiviśeṣaiḥ saumya ityavagamyate’ iti | asyaiva svarūpāntaramāha - mandavahnividagdha ityādi | sa eva raso yadā mandavahnividagdho bhavati agnermāndyenāsamyak pakva iti bhāvaḥ, tadā kaṭuramlo vā bhūtvā viṣabhāvaṃ māraṇaṃ māraṇātmakarūpatāṃ vrajet prāpnoti | vā yadi māraṇātmako na bhavati alpatvāttadā rogasaṅkaraṃ rogasastaṃ kuryāt doṣadūṣitatvāt na tu svayameva, yataḥ snehadagdhavyapadeśavat, taduktaṃ ‘rasādistheṣu doṣeṣu vyādhayaḥ saṃbhavanti ye | te jñāyanta ityupacāreṇa tānāhurghṛtadagdhavat’ iti ||3-6||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. punaḥ vahninā pakvaḥ kiṃ bhavatītyāha - punaḥ vahnerbalena pakvaḥ mādhuryaṃ snigdhatāṃ ca yāti, cet samyak pakvaḥ tadā akhilān dhātūn puṣṇāti | mandavahnipakve doṣamāha - viṣabhāvaṃ viṣasvarūpaṃ vrajet viṣūcyādibhirmāraṇātmakatvāt | rogasaṃkaraṃ bahurogasamūhaṃ vā kuryāt || 3-6 ||
āhārasya rasaḥ sāraḥ sārahīno maladravaḥ | śirābhistajjalaṃ nītaṃ bastau mūtratvamāpnuyāt ||7|| tatkiṭṭaṃ ca malaṃ jñeyaṃ tiṣṭhetpakvāśaye ca tat | valitritayamārgeṇa yātyapānena noditam || 8 || pravāhiṇī sarjanī ca grāhiketi valitrayam |
Āḍhamalla. athāhārasya rasasāratvaṃ nirūpya nirasasya niḥsāratvaṃ darśayannāha - āhārasyeti | āhārasya pūrvapratipāditasya yo raso viditasvarūpaḥ sa sāra ityabhihitaḥ, sāro mukhyatvena tarpaṇādikṛtatvāt sārahīna iti rasarahita āhārasyāṃśo vartate sa maladravasaṃjñaḥ | malaṃ purīṣaṃ dravaṃ mūtraṃ | tayorbhinnagamanasthānaviśeṣamāha - śirābhirityādi | tajjalamiti purīṣaniḥsṛtaṃ dravaviśeṣaṃ śirābhirmūtravāhinībhiḥ kṛtvā bastau mūtrādhāre nītaṃ tadeva mūtrattvamāpnuyāditi bhāvaḥ | tat kiṭṭaṃ malamiti mūtrāvaśiṣṭaṃ malaṃ kiṭṭasaṃjñakaṃ jñeyaṃ nirdravatvāt tacca pakvāśaye tiṣṭhediti tātparyārthaḥ || atha pakvāśayasya sthitasya tādṛśasya tasyādhogamanaśīlatvaṃ darśayannāha - valīti | apānena gudasthitavāyunā noditaṃ preritaṃ malamiti śeṣaḥ, tacca valitritayamārgeṇa kṛtvā yāti bahirniḥsaratītyarthaḥ | valitritayanāmānyāha -pravāhiṇītyādi | pravāhiṇī sarjanīgrāhiketi tāsāṃ nāmabhirevākṛtayo boddhavyā: | valayastu gudasyāvayavabhūtāstāstisro valaya uparyuparivyavasthitāḥ śaṅkhavartanibhā iti || 7 || 8 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. āhārasya kaḥ sāraḥ kiṃ ca malamityāha - āhārasya sārabhūto rasaḥ sārahīno yaḥ sa maladravo bhavati tajjalaṃ śirābhiḥ bastau nītaṃ mūtratvaṃ āpnuyāt tatkiṭṭaṃ malaṃ bhavati | tasya vyavasthāmāha - tatkiṭṭaṃ tasyāhārapakvaṃ yatkiṭṭaṃ tanmalaṃ jñeyam ? tatpakvāśaye tiṣṭhet | tat apānena noditaṃ valitrayamārgeṇa yāti | tasya valitritayasya nāmānyāha - atra pramāṇaṃ noktaṃ tadgranthāntarādviśeṣato likhyate | tadyathā ‘gudasya mānaṃ sarvasya sārdhaṃ syāccaturaṅgulam | tetra syurvalayastisraḥ śaṅkhavartanibhāstu tāḥ || pravāhaṇī bhavet pūrvā sā sārdhāṅgulasaṃmatā | utsarjanī tu tadadhaḥ sāsārdhāṅgulasaṃmitā || tasyā adhaḥ saṃvaraṇī syādekāṅgulasaṃmitā | ardhāṅgulapramāṇaṃ tu budhairgudamukhaṃ matam | malotsargasya mārgo'yaṃ pāyurdehavinirmitaḥ ||7||8||
rasastu hṛdayaṃ yāti samānamaruteritaḥ || 9 || rañjitaḥ pācitastatra pittenāyāti raktatām | raktaṃ sarvaśarīrasthaṃ jīvasyādhāramuttamam || 10 || snigdhaṃ guru calaṃ svādu vidagdhaṃ pittavadbhavet |
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ maladravaṃ nirūpya sārabhūtasya rasasyāpi kāryatvena sthānāntaraprāptimāha - rasastviti | sa rasaḥ samānamarutā nābhigatavāyunā kṛtvā īritaḥ ūrdhvaṃ prerito hṛdayaṃ yāti prāpnoti, etena kimuktam ? sārabhūto raso grahaṇīsthānāt hṛdayamāgacchatīti bhāvaḥ | sakalaśarīragamanaśīlatvāt | taduktaṃ suśrute - ‘sarvadehānusāritve'pi tasya hṛdayaṃ sthānaṃ sa hṛdayāccaturviṃśatidhamanīranupraviśyordhvagā daśa daśa cādhogāminya- ścatasrastiryaggāstāḥ kṛtsnaṃ śarīramaharahastarpayanti, vardhayanti, dhārayanti, yāpayanti, jīvayanti cādṛṣṭahetukena karmaṇā tasya śarīramanudhāvato'numānādgatirupalakṣayitavyā’ iti | hṛdayaśabdopalakṣitamojaḥsthānamucyate | tatra so'pi rasaḥ pittena rañjakapittena kṛtvā pācitaḥ ata eva rañjitaḥ rāgaṃ prāptaḥ san raktatāṃ yāti prāpnoti | yato rasādraktaṃ bhavatīti vacanāt | raktasya bhāvo raktatā | tena ādāvīṣadraktabhāvaṃ prāpya paścātkrameṇa āraktaḥ syāt | taduktaṃ - ‘rasaḥ3 saptāhādarvāk parivartamānaḥ śvetakapotaharitahāridrapadmakiṃśukālaktakarasaprakhyaścāyaṃ yathākramaṃ divasaparivartādvarṇaparivartamāpadyamānaḥ pittoṣmoparāgāt śoṇitatvamāpadyate’ iti || atha raktaprādhānyaṃ darśayannāha - raktamiti | tadraktaṃ sarvaśarīrasthitaṃ bhavati pāñcabhautikatvāt, yataḥ ‘visratā dravatā rāgaḥ syandanaṃ laghutā tathā | bhūmyādīnāṃ guṇā hyete dṛśyante cātra śoṇite’ iti | jīvasyottamamādhāraśceti dehamūlatvāt | taduktaṃ - dehasya rudhiraṃ mūlaṃ rudhireṇaiva dhāryate | tasmādyatnena saṃrakṣyaṃ4 raktaṃ jīva iti sthitiḥ iti | punaḥ kīdṛśaṃ tadāha - snigdhamityādi | snigdhamarukṣaṃ, guru alaghu, calaṃ naikatrasthitiśīlaṃ dravatvāt | svādu madhuraṃ svabhāvāt yadā vidagdhaṃ tadā pittavadbhavet iti | yadā vidagdhaṃ pittamapyamlatāṃ vrajet kaṭukaṃ ca bhavet tathedamapīti bhāvaḥ | uktamanyatrā'pi ‘anuṣṇaśītaṃ madhuraṃ snigdhaṃ raktaṃ ca varṇataḥ | śoṇitaṃ guru visraṃ syādvidāhaścāsya pittavat ? iti | kāryatvenā'pi tasya laghutā ca darśitā iti na doṣaḥ || 9 || 10 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. iti pakvo rasaḥ kutra sthitvā raktādīn karotītyāha -- samānamaruteritarasaḥ ādau hṛdayaṃ yāti tatra pittena rañjitaḥ pācitaśca raktatāṃ yāti tatrobhayoḥ sthāne raktasya hṛdayapāke dūṣaṇaprasaṅgāt | tathācoktaṃ suśrute - ‘sa khalvāpyo raso yakṛtplīhānau prāpya rāgamupaitī’tyuktatvāt || 9 || 10 ||
pācitāḥ pittatāpena rasādyā dhātavaḥ kramāt ||11 || śukratvaṃ yānti māsena tathā strīṇāṃ rajo bhavet |
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ dhātūnāmutpattiparamparāpūrvakaṃ śukrasyāpi viṣayaṃ darśayannāha - pācitā iti | pittatāpena pittatejasā pācitāḥ paripācitā rasādyā dhātavaḥ krameṇa śukratvaṃ māsena yānti prāpnuvanti | rasādyā ityatra rasagrahaṇāt rasa eva śukratvaṃ prāpnoti | ata eva yātītyekavacanaṃ kṛtam | ādigrahaṇāt raktamāṃsamedomajjāsthīnīti | eke rasādyā dhātavo māsena śukratvaṃ yāntīti paṭhanti vyākhyānayanti ca, teṣāṃ mate tu kāryakāraṇayorabhedopacārāt na kevalaṃ śukratvaṃ strīṇāmapi rajo bhavet | taduktaṃ ‘rasādeva striyā raktaṃ rajaḥsaṃjñaṃ pravartate | tadvarṣādvādaśādūrdhvaṃ yāti pañcāśataḥ kṣayam iti | tatheti grahaṇāt śukratvamapi strīṇāmeva bhavati | yato dṛśyate hi tatsrāvaṇādibhiḥ prayogaiḥ, anyathā tāsāṃ saṃharṣaḥ kathaṃ syāditi | taduktaṃ - saumyaṃ tvagāśrayaṃ svacchaṃ snigdhaṃ yonimukhodgamam | strīṇāṃ śukraṃ na garbhāya bhavedgarbhāya cārtavam’ iti | māseneti | triṃśaddinairiti bhāvaḥ | triṃśaddinavivaraṇaṃ suśrutācāryeṇoktaṃ tadyathā - ‘sa khalu trīṇi trīṇi kalāsahasrāṇi pañca daśa ca kalā ekaikasmin dhātavastiṣṭhante, evaṃ māsena rasaḥ śukrībhavati strīṇāṃ cārtavam’ iti | uktamapi samuccayena darśayati - ‘aṣṭādaśa sahasrāṇi saṃkhyā yasmin samuccaye | kalaikā navatiścoktā svatantraparatantrayoḥ ||’ iti viṃśatikalo muhūrtaḥ kalā daśabhāgaścetyādinā māsasaṃkhyā jñātavyā | etena kimuktaṃ rasaḥ kilaikāhenaiva sampadyate anye ye dhātavaste pañcabhiḥ pañcabhirahobhiḥ sampadyanta iti bhāvaḥ || 11||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. raktaprasaṅgena anyeṣāṃ dhātūnāṃ pakvakramamāha - pittatāpena pācitā rasādyā dhātavaḥ māsena śukratvaṃ yāntīti yojyam | tathā retaḥprakāreṇa strīṇāṃ rajaśca māsena bhavet | viśeṣapākakramastu dhātuvicāraṇe proktaḥ || 11 ||
kāmānmithunasaṃyoge śuddhaśoṇitaśukrajaḥ ||12|| garbhaḥ saṃjāyate nāryāḥ sa jāto bāla ucyate | ādhikye rajasaḥ kanyā putraḥ śukrādhike bhavet||13|| napuṃsakaṃ samatvena yathecchā pārameśvarī |
Āḍhamalla. garbhotpattimāha - kāmāditi | śuddhaśoṇitaśukrajo garbho nāryāḥ striyāḥ saṃjāyata iti, kutaḥ ? kāmāt, kāmo'bhilāṣaḥ, kva ? mithunasaṃyoge, mithunaṃ dvandraṃ strīpuruṣaviṣayaṃ tasya saṃyogo melanaṃ tasmin sati | śuddhaśoṇitaśukrayorlakṣaṇaṃ ca tantrāntare yathā - ‘śaśāsṛkpratimaṃ yacca yadvā lākṣārasopamam | tadārtavaṃ praśaṃsanti yadvāso na virañjayet || dinatrayaṃ pravṛttiśca kurute śoṇitaṃ striyāḥ | vyavāyasrutaśukreṇa garbhastasyāstato bhavet || tadevā'tiprasaṅgena pravṛttamanṛtāvapi | asṛgdaraṃ vijānīyādatonyaduktalakṣaṇāt’ iti || śuddhaṃ śukraṃ yathā - ‘sphaṭikābhaṃ dravaṃ snigdhaṃ madhuraṃ madhuragandhi ca | śukramicchanti kecittu tailakṣaudranibhaṃ tathā’ iti | ‘vātādidūṣitaṃ pūti kuṇapaṃ granthirūpi ca | kṣīṇaṃ mūtrapurīṣābhyāṃ gandhiśukraṃ tu niṣphalam’ iti | garbha iti | śukraśoṇitaṃ garbhāśayasthamātmaprativikārasaṃmūrcchitaṃ garbha ityucyate | sa jāto bāla ucyate iti | sa garbho yadā jāto bahirniḥsarati tadā bāla ityucyate | bālaśabdaḥ kanyāpuruṣanapuṃsakeṣvapi vartate | ata evāhācāryaḥ - ādhikya iti | tatra ca garbhāśaye yadā rajasa ārtavasya ādhikyaṃ bhavati tadā kanyā jāyate utpadyate ārtavādhikaśukrādityabhiprāyaḥ | yadi śukādhikastarhi putraḥ śukrādhikārtavādityarthaḥ | samatveneti śukraśoṇitayoḥ sāmyena samabhāgena kṛtvā napuṃsakaṃ syāt | yathā pārameśvarīcchāsti tathaiva bhavati nātra vicāraḥ kartavya iti bhāvaḥ | icchetyanenaiva yamalajātamapi sūcitam | tadyathā - ‘bīje'ntarvāyunā bhinne dvau jīvau kukṣimāgatau | yamāvityabhidhīyete dharmetarapuraḥsarau’ iti|| 12 || 13 ||
