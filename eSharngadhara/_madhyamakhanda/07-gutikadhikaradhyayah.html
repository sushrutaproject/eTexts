saptamo'dhyāyaḥ | vaṭakāścātha kathyante tannāma guṭikā vaṭī | modako vaṭikā piṇḍī guḍo vartistathocyate ||1|| lehavatsādhyate vahnau guḍo vā śarkarā tathā | guggulurvā kṣipettatra cūrṇaṃ tannirmitā vaṭī ||2|| kuryādavahnisiddhena kvacidguggulunā vaṭīm | draveṇa madhunā vāpi guṭikāṃ kārayedbudhaḥ ||3||
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ guṭikāḥ kathyante - vaṭakāśceti | atheti cūrṇakalpanānantaraṃ vaṭakā guṭikādisaṃjñāḥ ata eva tannāma guṭikāvaṭīprabhṛtayo jñātavyāḥ | tasya vaṭakasya nāma paryāyavācakāḥ śabdā yathā tannāmeti tasya vaṭakasya nāma paryāyavākyaṃ guṭikādikamityarthaḥ | atha teṣāṃ kartavyavidhimāha - lehavatsādhyata iti | yathāvalehārthaṃ guḍādikaṃ sādhyate tathaivā'tra guḍaḥ, śarkarā, guggulurvāpi paktavya:, pāke sati cūrṇaṃ prakṣipya vaṭakādīn kuryāditi bhāva: | pakṣāntaramāha - kvaciditi | na sarvatra avahnisiddhena kuṭṭitena guggulunā vaṭakāḥ kāryāḥ guḍādikenāpi kvaciditi saṃcārādvā, athavā draveṇa toyakṣīrādinā madhunā kṣaudreṇāpi saṃplāvya guṭikāṃ kārayet budhaḥ paṇḍitaḥ atrāpi vahniṃ vineti sambandhaḥ ||1-3 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. atha guṭikādhikāraḥ || 1-3 ||
sitā caturguṇā deyā vaṭīṣu dviguṇo guḍaḥ | cūrṇāccūrṇasamaḥ kāryo guggulurmadhu tatsamam || 4 || dravaṃ ca dviguṇaṃ deyaṃ modakeṣu bhiṣagvaraiḥ |
Āḍhamalla. idānīmanukteṣu guḍaśarkarādīnāṃ mānamāha - siteti | sitā śarkarā caturguṇā deyā cūrṇādityarthaḥ | guḍo'tra dviguṇaḥ, atrā'pi cūrṇabhāgāditi bhāvaḥ | (gugguluśca1 cūrṇasamaḥ cūrṇabhāgasamāna ityarthaḥ | madhu tatsamamiti madhu kṣaudraṃ tatsamaṃ cūrṇasamaṃ dravyaṃ kṣīratoyādikaṃ dviguṇaṃ cūrṇabhāgādityarthaḥ) | cūrṇamatra sakaladravyāṇāṃ bhiṣagvarairiti grahaṇena svabuddhyā vā yathāyogyamavacārayediti sāṃpradāyikāḥ || 4 ||
karṣapramāṇaṃ tanmānaṃ balaṃ dṛṣṭvā prayujyate || 5 ||
Āḍhamalla. atha bhakṣaṇamānamanuktānāṃ darśayannāha - karṣapramāṇamiti | karṣapramāṇaṃ karṣamātraṃ tanmānamiti tat teṣāṃ vaṭakādīnāṃ mānaṃ parimāṇaṃ | balamiti dehabalaṃ doṣabalaṃ vyādhibalaṃ ca | balagrahaṇena karṣamānamatra sāmānyaṃ sūcitam | ata eva dṛṣṭvā vicāryetyarthaḥ | vicāraṇātra bālavṛddhānāmeva madhyānāṃ tu karṣamātraiva || 5 ||
atha śrībāhuśālo guḍaḥ arśādau - sa yathā - indravāruṇikā mustaṃ śuṇṭhī dantī harītakī | trivṛcchaṭhī viḍaṅgāni gokṣuraścitrakastathā || 6 || tejohvā ca dvikarṣāṇi pṛthagdravyāṇi kārayet | sūraṇasya palānyaṣṭau vṛddhadāru catuṣpalam ||7|| catuṣpalaṃ syādbhallātaḥ kvāthayetsarvamekataḥ | jaladroṇe caturthāṃśaṃ gṛhṇīyātkvāthamuttamam || 8 || kvāthyadravyāttriguṇitaṃ guḍaṃ kṣiptvā punaḥ pacet | samyakpakvaṃ ca taṃ jñātvā cūrṇametatpradāpayet ||9|| citrakatrivṛtādantītejohvā: palikāḥ pṛthak | pṛthaktripalikāḥ kāryā vyoṣailāmalakatvacaḥ2 ||10|| nikṣipenmadhu śīte ca tasminprasthapramāṇataḥ | evaṃ siddho bhavecchrīmānbāhuśālaguḍā'bhidhaḥ ||11|| jayedarśāṃsi sarvāṇi gulmaṃ vātodaraṃ tathā | āḍhyavātapratiśyāyaṃ mahāntaṃ kṣayapīnasam ||12|| halīmakaṃ pāṇḍurogaṃ pramehaṃ ca rasāyanam |
Āḍhamalla. sa yatheti vacanaṃ guṭikāsamūhapratipādanārthamabhihitamācāryeṇa | tatrādau śrībāhuśālo nāma guḍastaṃ darśayannāha - indravāruṇiketi | ayaṃ bāhuśālaguḍo'rśovyādhau śastaḥ, tathendravāruṇikā cirbhaṭī tasyā mūlaṃ, dantī viṭapaviśeṣaḥ tasyāpi mūlaṃ; trivṛnniśothamūlameva, śaṭhī karcūrabhedaḥ, tejohvā'tra cavyaṃ yatra ca tejohvā cavikā tatra tejohvā jyotiṣmatī evaṃ sarvatra | ( etatsarvaṃ3 pratyekaṃ dvikarṣaparimitaṃ bhavati | śūraṇasya palānyaṣṭāviti sūraṇaḥ kandaviśeṣaḥ prasiddhaḥ tasyāṣṭau palāni bhavanti; vṛddhadāru catuṣpalamiti niśothabhedaḥ tasya catuṣpalāni, bhallātamiti bhallātakaphalāni tāni catuṣpalamitāni bhavanti) etatsakalaṃ pānīyadroṇe saṃkvāthya caturthāṃśaṃ gṛhṇīyāt| jaladroṇamiti caturāḍhakaparimitaṃ jalaṃ tena ṣaṭpañcāśaduttaraśatadvayapalāni jalaparimāṇaṃ bhavati | u- ttamamiti vastragālitādikaṃ tenottamatā kvathitasyetyarthaḥ | caturthāṃśamityāḍhakamātraṃ kvāthyadravyāt triguṇitaṃ guḍa- miti guḍamatra jīrṇaṃ grāhyaṃ, kvāthyadravyāditi indravāruṇikāditejohvāntāt triguṇitaṃ tribhāgam etaduḍaṃ pāda- śeṣakāthe kṣiptvā punaḥ pacedyāvat sāndro bhavati | atra cūrṇamāha - citraketyādi | citrakaścitrakajaṭā, trivṛnniśothaḥ, dantī prasiddhā, tejohvā cavī etaddravyacatuṣṭayaṃ palapramāṇam, anyadapyāha - vyoṣailāmalakatvaca ityādi | vyoṣaṃ trikaṭukaṃ tvak guḍatvak idamapi dravyaṣaṭkaṃ tripalapramāṇaṃ pratyekaṃ | śrībāhuśālo guḍa evaṃ siddho bhavati | bāhuśāla ācāryaviśeṣastena nirmito guḍaḥ vaṭakasaṃjña ityarthaḥ | āḍhyavāta ūrustambhabhedaḥ | mahāntaṃ prabalamityarthaḥ | rasāyana iti prabhāvāt | tadyathā “yajjarāvyādhividhvaṃsi bheṣajaṃ tadrasāyanam” iti || 6-12 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. sa yathā sugamam | tejohvā4 tumburuḥ, kvāthāṃśadravyam indravāruṇikādyā tasmātriguṇaṃ guḍaṃ saṃpakve citrakādīni dāpayet | trivṛtā niḥsṛtā, dantī jayapālamūlaṃ, tejohvā tumburuḥ, vyoṣaṃ trikaṭu, elā sūkṣmā; maricaṃ ṣaṭpalaṃ trikaṭumadhye grahaṇāt “ekamapyauṣadhaṃ yasmin yoge yatpunarucyate | mānato dviguṇaṃ jñeyam” ityuktatvāt | dviguṇaṃ maricakaṃ yadvā maricakaṅkolaṃ, tvak5 prasiddhaṃ, madhu prasthapramitaṃ prasthamānam, evaṃ prasiddhaḥ śrībāhuśālaguḍaḥ bāhuśālo nāma kaścit, tadarthaṃ praṇītatvāt bāhuśālaḥ śrīmān śrīkaratvāt spaṣṭamanyat | nanu ādau jvaraghnī guṭikā kathaṃ noktā rogagaṇanāyāṃ jvarasyaiva prādhānyāt ? ucyate ? asya rasāyanatvāt jvarādivyādhināśakatvaṃ samāgataṃ, “yajjarāvyādhividhvaṃsi bheṣajaṃ tadrasāyana”mityukteḥ | vyādhimātragrahaṇe jvarasyā'pi grahaṇāt | yadvā arśorogasyāpi jvarādivyādhisaṃjñakatvādādāvabhidhānaṃ kramābhāvādvyatikramābhidhānam || 6-12 ||
atha maricādiguṭikā kāsādau - maricaṃ karṣamātraṃ syātpippalī karṣasaṃmitā || 13 || ardhakarṣo yavakṣāraḥ karṣayugmaṃ ca dāḍimam | etaccūrṇīkṛtaṃ yuñjyādaṣṭakarṣaguḍena hi ||14|| śāṇapramāṇāṃ guṭikāṃ kṛtvā vaktreṇa dhārayet | asyā: prabhāvātsarve'pi kāsā yāntyeva saṃkṣayam ||15||
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ maricādiguṭikāṃ darśayannāha - maricamiti | asyā maricādikāyā vaṭikāyāḥ prabhāvādacintyaśaktiḥ | sarve kāsā iti pañcaprakārāḥ | atra dāḍimaṃ dāḍimaphalabījāni karṣayugmamiti karṣadvayaṃ vakre dhārayediti, atra vakradhāraṇādidamauṣadhaṃ dhūmarūpamityāhureke, cakreṇa tu svasaṃgrahe bhakṣaṇacūrṇadvayaṃ vakre dhārayediti prastāve likhitaṃ vaidyaprasārake'pi ||
śvāsakāsādau guḍavaṭikā | guḍaśuṇṭhīśivāmustairguṭikāṃ dhārayenmukhe | śvāsakāseṣu sarveṣu bibhītaṃ vāpi kevalam ||16||
Āḍhamalla. ( guṭikāsvarūpabhakṣaṇamasyoktam, etadabhiprāyeṇātra likhitaṃ | guḍaśuṇṭhīti spaṣṭam) ||13-16||
āmalakyādiguṭikā tṛṣṇādau - āmalaṃ kamalaṃ kuṣṭhaṃ lājāśca vaṭarohakaḥ | etaccūrṇasya madhunā guṭikāṃ dhārayenmukhe ||17|| tṛṣṇā pravṛddhāṃ hantyeṣā mukhaśoṣaṃ ca dāruṇam |
Āḍhamalla. athāmalakyādiguṭikāmāha - āmalamiti | āmalamāmalakaphalaṃ kamalamutpalaṃ tadabhāve kamalabījāni grāhyāṇi, lājāḥ bhṛṣṭadhānyodbhavāḥ, vaṭarohaka iti vaṭavṛkṣaprarohaḥ sa ca vaṭarohaśabdavācya:, madhvatra guṭikākaraṇamānaṃ paribhāṣoktam, dhārayenmukha iti | etena yathā yathā guṭikā dravati tathā tathā śīlanīyā punarimāṃ sahasaivābhyavaharediti dṛṣṭaparicayaḥ | pravṛddhāmiti kairapi yogairanivāryām | mukhaśoṣaṃ ca dāruṇamiti dāruṇaṃ duḥkhakaramasādhyamityarthaḥ || 17 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. āmalam āmalakaṃ, kamalaṃ kamalapatraṃ phalaṃ vā, kuṣṭhaṃ prasiddhaṃ, lājā dhānyajā, vaṭarohakaṃ vaṭārohaḥ, kuṣṭhā'bhāve drākṣā vā deyā kuṣṭhasyātīvoṣṇatvāt ||17 ||
atha jvare sañjīvanīvaṭī tridoṣe ca - viḍaṅgaṃ nāgaraṃ kṛṣṇā pathyāmalabibhītakam ||18|| vacā guḍūcī bhallātaṃ sadviṣaṃ cātra yojayet | etāni samabhāgāni gomūtreṇaiva peṣayet||19|| guñjābhā guṭikā kāryā dadyādārdrakajai rasaiḥ | ekāmajīrṇagulmeṣu dve viṣūcyāṃ pradāpayet ||20|| tisraśca sarpadaṣṭe tu catasraḥ sāṃnipātike | vaṭī saṃjīvanī nāmnā saṃjīvayati mānavam ||2 1||
Āḍhamalla. atha sañjīvanīguṭikāṃ darśayannāha - viḍaṅgamiti | atra nāgaraṃ śuṇṭhī, kṛṣṇā pippalī, pathyā harītakī, āmalamāmalakaphalaṃ, bhallātaṃ bhallātakaphalaṃ, sadviṣamiti śuddhaṃ viṣaṃ eteṣāṃ samānamātrā boddhavyā | gomūtramatra guṭikārdhaparimitaṃ | guñjābhā guñjāparimāṇā vaṭī kāryā, ārdrakaraso'trānupānaṃ, ajīrṇagulmayorekaikāvaṭī deyā, visūcyāṃ dve vaṭyau, sarpadaṣṭe tisro guṭikāḥ, sannipāte catasra iti nāmnā kṛtvā sañjīvanī kathitā yato'syāḥ prabhāvānmānavo yadā mumūrṣustadā jīvatīti | vicāraṇātra na kāryā, “avicintyo hi maṇimantrauṣadhīnāṃ prabhāvaḥ” iti vacanāt ||18-21 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. nāgaraṃ śuṇṭhī, pathyā harītakī, āmalakaṃ, bibhītakam akṣa:; bhallātakaṃ prasiddhaṃ | tadabhāve bākucirdeyā, sadviṣam uttamaṃ viṣaṃ, spaṣṭamanyat || 18-21 ||
atha vyoṣādiguṭikā pīnasādau - vyoṣāmlavetasaṃ cavyaṃ tālīsaṃ citrakaṃ tathā | jīrakaṃ tintiḍīkaṃ ca pratyekaṃ karṣabhāgikam || 22 || trisugandhaṃ triśāṇaṃ syādguḍaḥ syātkarṣaviṃśatiḥ | vyoṣādiguṭikā seyaṃ pīnasaśvāsakāsajit ||23|| rucisvarakarī khyātā pratiśyāyapraṇāśinī |
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ vyoṣādiguṭikāmāha - vyoṣeti | vyoṣamatra trikaṭukam, amlavetasaḥ prasiddhaḥ, cavyaṃ loke cāvaśabdavācyaṃ, tālīsaṃ svanāmakhyātaṃ, tintiḍīkamityamlaphaladravyaṃ, trisugandhaṃ triśāṇamiti | elā-tvak-patrakaṃ, tacca militaṃ trikaṃ na tu pratyekaṃ, vyoṣādi tintiḍīkāntaṃ dravyaṃ ca pratyekaṃ karṣamātramityarthaḥ | guḍaḥ syāt karṣaviṃśatiriti | guḍo'tra purātano grāhyaḥ | karṣaviṃśatiriti pañcapalāni tena sakaladravyāddviguṇito guḍa ityarthaḥ, tathāhi - ‘vyoṣacitrakatālīsatintiḍīkāmlavetasam | sa cavyājājitulyāṃśamelātvakpatrapādikam | vyoṣādikamidaṃ cūrṇaṃ purāṇaguḍasaṃyutam’ iti ||22||23||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. vyoṣa trikaṭu; amlo'mlavetastasya cukraṃ vā rasaṃ vā deyaṃ, trisugandhaṃ tvak-tvaja-elā etat triśāṇam pratyekaṃ ṭaṅkaṃ | spaṣṭamanyat || 22 || 23 ||
atha guḍacatuṣṭayaguṭikā āmādau - āmeṣu saguḍāṃ śuṇṭhīmajīrṇe guḍapippalīm ||24|| kṛcchre jīraguḍaṃ dadyādarśaḥsu saguḍābhayām |
Āḍhamalla. atha guḍacatuṣṭayaguṭikāṃ vyācaṣṭe - āmeṣviti | guḍacatuṣṭayaguṭikā iti ko'rthaḥ ? guḍena saha catvāro yogāḥ kathitā ityarthaḥ | tadyathā āmeṣu saguḍāṃ śuṇṭhīmityeko yogaḥ, āmeṣu āmadoṣeṣu saguḍāmityatra guḍadvaiguṇyam evaṃ yogacatuṣṭayeṣu boddhavyam; ajīrṇe guḍapippalīmiti dvitīyaḥ, ajīrṇe viṣṭabdike, kṛcchre jīrakaguḍaṃ dadyāditi tṛtīyo yogaḥ, kṛcchre matrakṛcchre; arśaḥsu ca saguḍābhayāmiti caturtho yoga iti || 24 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. āmeṣu āmarogeṣu saguḍāṃ śuṇṭhīṃ dadyāt | vaṭīṃ tulyaguḍāṃ kṛtvā ajīrṇe ṣaḍvidhājīrṇe guḍapippalīṃ dadyāt; kṛcchre mūtrakṛcchre jīraguḍaṃ jīraṃ śvetajīrakam; arśaḥsu guḍābhayāṃ guḍayuktām abhayāṃ harītakīm ||24||
atha vṛddhadārumodakaḥ - vṛddhadārukabhallātaśuṇṭhīcūrṇena yojitaḥ || 25 || modakaḥ saguḍo hanyātṣaḍvidhārśaḥkṛtāṃ rujam |
Āḍhamalla. idānīṃ vṛddhadārukamodakamāha - vṛddhādāruketi vṛddhadāruko vikhyātaḥ tadabhāve niśothā grāhyā yato'syā bhedo vṛddhadāraka iti | eteṣāṃ dravyatrayāṇāṃ cūrṇa samamātramāhuḥ | saguḍa ityatra modakatvena guḍo dviguṇito jñeyaḥ | kecittu nādriyante yatastatra catuḥsamagrahaṇena sama eva guḍo yujyate sāmyenā'pi kiñciddravaṃ datvā agnau modakāḥ kāryāḥ | rujamiti pīḍām || 25 ||
