ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ | atyantaśuṣkaṃ yaddravyaṃ supiṣṭaṃ vastragālitam | tatsyāccūrṇaṃ rajaḥ kṣodastanmātrā karṣasaṃmitā || 1 ||
Dī. idānīṃ cūrṇakalpanāṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ | prathamatastatsvarūpamāha - atyantaśuṣkamiti | sūryāṃśubhiḥ śoṣitadravāṃśaṃ yaddravyamiti | puṣpaphalapatratvaṅmūlaniryāsādisaṃjñam | supiṣṭamityādi supiṣṭamityatiśayena cūrṇitaṃ śilāyāmiti śeṣaḥ | vastragālitaṃ vastrapūtaṃ, tattu cūrṇaṃ rajaḥ kṣoda iti paryāyanāmabhyāṃ saṃjñitam | tanmātreti bhakṣaṇārthaṃ mānaṃ karṣasaṃmitaṃ jñeyamityanuktaprayogeṣu || 1 ||
Gū. atha cūrṇakalpanā | yadatyantaśuṣkaṃ dravyaṃ cūrṇauṣadhaṃ saṃpiṣṭaṃ vastragālitaṃ vastrapūtaṃ taccūrṇaṃ syāttadeva rajaḥ kṣoda ityādīni cūrṇanāmāni bhavanti | tanmātrā bhakṣaṇamātrā karṣasaṃmitā balavāṃścet, alpabale ekaṭaṅkādvardhitavyā | cūrṇādīnāmanupānakam || 1 ||
cūrṇe guḍādimānakalpanā | idānīmaviśeṣokteṣu śarkarāguḍayormānapūrvakaṃ prakṣepavidhimāha - cūrṇe guḍaḥ samo deyaḥ śarkarā dviguṇā bhavet | cūrṇeṣu bharjitaṃ hiṅgu deyaṃ notkledakṛdbhavet ||2|| liheccūrṇaṃ dravaiḥ sarvairghṛtādyairdviguṇonmitaiḥ | pibeccaturguṇaireva cūrṇamāloḍitaṃ dravaiḥ ||3||
Dī. cūrṇa iti | guḍasāmyaṃ cūrṇāṃśāt, śarkarāpi dviguṇitā cūrṇaparimāṇādityarthaḥ | cūrṇeṣviti bahuvacanatvena guṭikālehādiṣvapi bhājataṃ hiṅgu boddhavyam | sarvairityato ghṛtādyairiti yat punargrahaṇaṃ kṛtaṃ tattoyakṣīramadyamūtrādīnāṃ vyavacchedārtham | yata etaiḥ saha cūrṇasya pānamuktametasyāgre, lehastu madhughṛtatailadīnāṃ praśastaḥ | pibedityādicaturguṇitātra cūrṇabhāgāt, dravairiti toyakṣīramadyamūtrādibhiḥ ||2||3||
atha cūrṇādīnāmanupānamātrāṃ darśayannāha - cūrṇāvalehaguṭikākalkānāmanupānakam | vātapittakaphātaṅke tridvyekapalamāharet || 4 || yathā tailaṃ jale kṣiptaṃ kṣaṇenaiva prasarpati | anupānabalādaṅge tathā sarpati bheṣajam || 5 ||
Dī. cūrṇāvaleheti | anupānamiti anu paścāt pānam | auṣadhabhakṣaṇopari yatpītaṃ tadanupānamityarthaḥ | “vātapittakaphātaṅke tridvyekapalamāharet” ityanukramo jñeyaḥ | ātaṅko rogaḥ tena vātātaṅke tripalaṃ, pittātaṅke dvipalaṃ, kaphātaṅke palaikamiti tātparyārthaḥ | anukteṣvanupāneṣu jalānupānaṃ praśastam | tathāhi - ‘sarveṣāmanupānaṃ syānmāhendraṃ toyamuttamam’ iti | suśrute'pi - “sarvānupāneṣu varaṃ vadanti medhyaṃ yadantaḥśucibhājanastham | lokasya janmaprabhṛtipraśastaṃ toyātmakāḥ sarvarasāḥ pradiṣṭāḥ” iti | athānupānasya prayojanaṃ nirdeṣṭumāha - yatheti | bhiṣajā saṃskṛtaṃ dravyaṃ tathā aṅgeṣu prasarpati, kutaḥ ? anupānabalādanupānaśaktitaḥ | yatheti dṛṣṭāntamāha - tailamityādi sugamam || 4 || 5 ||
Gū. pittakaphātaṅke pittādīnāṃ tridvyekapalaṃ haret pitte tripalaṃ, vāte dvipalaṃ, kapha ekapalaṃ | draveṇa nimbādidraveṇa || 4 || 5 ||
atha cūrṇārthaṃ bhāvanāparimāṇamāha - draveṇa yāvatā samyakcūrṇaṃ sarvaṃ plutaṃ bhavet | bhāvanāyāḥ pramāṇaṃ tu cūrṇe proktaṃ bhiṣagvarai: || 6 ||
Dī. draveṇeti | sāmānyena sarvaṃ toyādikam | anyadapi yaddravīkṛtaṃ tadapi grāhyaṃ na doṣaḥ | plutamiti plāvitaṃ, yathā manthasvarasasvarūpaṃ tadvaditi bhiṣagvarairityanena yuktyā kartavyamiti bhāvaḥ || 6 ||
Gū. yāvatplutaṃ vyāptaṃ bhavet tadbhāvanāpramāṇam | śeṣaṃ sugamam || 6 ||
athāmalakyādicūrṇaṃ jvarādau - āmalaṃ citrakaṃ pathyā pippalī saindhavastathā | cūrṇito'yaṃ gaṇo jñeyaḥ sarvajvaravināśanaḥ || 7 || bhedī rucikaraḥ śleṣmajetā dīpanapācanaḥ |
Dī. sa yathetyanena cūrṇāni darśayati granthakāraḥ | āmalamiti āmalādiko'yaṃ gaṇaḥ sarvajvaravināśano bhavati | āmalamityāmalakaphalaṃ, pathyā harītakī, etat sakalaṃ sāmyamanuktatvāt | sarvajvaramityaṣṭavidhaṃ vyavahāratastu viṣamajvareṣu prāyaśaḥ pracarati | bhedī rucikaraḥ ityādipadadvayaṃ kecit paṭhanti || 7 ||
Gū. āmalakaṃ,pathyā harītakī, śeṣaṃ sugamam ||7||
atha pippalīcūrṇaṃ jvare - madhunā pippalīcūrṇaṃ lihetkāsajvarāpaham || 8 || hikkāśvāsaharaṃ kaṇṭhyaṃ plīhaghnaṃ bālakocitam |
Dī. madhunā iti | ayaṃ madhupippalīyogaḥ kāsajvarādīn hanti | viśeṣeṇa bālānāmucitaḥ | bālaketi sāmānyena traividhyaṃ sūcayati | tadyathā āṣoḍaśādbālaḥ sa trividhaḥ kṣīrapaḥ, kṣīrānnādaḥ, annāda iti ||8||
Gū. pippalīcūrṇaṃ māṣamātraṃ madhunā lihet na tu karṣamātraṃ tīkṣṇadravyatvāt || 8 ||
mehādikeṣu triphalācūrṇam - ekā harītakī yojyā dvau ca yojyau bibhītikau ||9 || catvāryāmalakānyevaṃ triphalaiṣāṃ prakīrtitā | triphalā mehaśothaghnī nāśayedviṣamajvarān || 10 || dīpanī śleṣmapittaghnī kuṣṭhahantrī rasāyanī | sarpirmadhubhyāṃ saṃyuktā saiva netrāmayāñjayet || 11 ||
Dī. atha triphalācūrṇamāha - eketi | uktaparimāṇena mehādikān hantīti | yadā saiva triphalā sarpi- rmadhubhyāmavalehitā tadā netrāmayān jayediti | avalehastu sāyamupayojyaḥ | tathāhyuktam ‘ūrdhvajatrugarogaghnī sāyaṃ syādavalehikā’iti | kīdṛśamuktaparimāṇaṃ tadāha - ekā harītakītyādi | etadgaṇanāparimāṇaṃ triphalāyāḥ kathitaṃ na tu tulanāyāṃ viṣamamānaṃ, yataḥ guṇakasaṃkhyāmānenaiva samatvamasyāḥ, kutaḥ ? dvikārṣikī harītakīti vākyānurodhāt | tathāhi - ‘navādiguṇasampannaṃ tathaikatvaṃ dvikarṣatā | harītakyāḥ phale yatra trayaṃ tat śreṣṭhamucyate’ iti | navādiguṇo'pi prasaṅgato likhyate | yathā “navā snigdhā ghanā vṛttā gurvī kṣiptā tathā'mbhasi | nimajjedyā praśastatvādguṇakṛt sā prakīrtitā” iti | vibhītakamapi karṣākṣaparyāyanāmabhyāṃ kathitamiti prasiddhiḥ | uktaṃ nighaṇṭau ‘vibhītakaḥ karṣaphalo vāsanto'kṣaḥ kalidrumaḥ’ iti tena dve bibhītakaphale kathite | āmalamapi kolasaṃjñaṃ vadanti, kolastvardhakarṣasya paryāyanāmeti | tena catvāryāmalakāni bhavanti | etena triphalāyāstulanā sāmyamapi darśitam | gaṇanāyāṃ ca viṣamatvamiti, na tu tasyā bhāgeṣu viṣamatā, yato dṛśyate hi vṛddhavaidyānāṃ vyavahāro'yameva | yadyapi triphalā dvitīyāpyasti tathāpi sarvatraivāviśeṣoktau triphalāśabdena harītakyādikameva gṛhyate | yaduktam “drākṣākāśmaryakharjūraphalānīti phalatrikā | iyaṃ proktā ca dvitīyā triphalā carakādibhiḥ” iti || 9-11 ||
Gū. ekā harītakī, dvau bibhītakau, catvāri āmalakānyevaṃ triphalā syāt | anye tvevamāhuḥ ekā ekabhāgā haratikī, dvau vibhāgau vibhītakaṃ, āmalakaṃ catvāri bhāgāni, tathāca vacanaṃ - ‘bhāgamekaṃ harītakyā dvaubhāgau ca vibhītakāt | āmalakyāstrayo bhāgāḥ sahaikatra prayojayet’ iti | anye suśrutavāgbhaṭādayo bhāgapramāṇaṃ necchanti | tathāca ‘harītakī cāmalakaṃ bibhītakādibhistrayam| triphaleyaṃ smṛtā sadbhirāyurvedaviśāradaiḥ’iti | māne'nukte sāmyaṃ syādityukte: samabhāgā triphalā'tra suśrutādimate kecidbhāgamāhuḥ | ataḥ akṣadvayaṃ pramāṇaṃ bhūnimbo dṛśyate, śārṅgadhareṇa yuktabhāgavṛdvyā triphalā likhitā kevalaṃ triphalācūrṇakvāthaviṣayānupānaguṇaviṣayāṇyanyathā navāyasacūrṇādau virodhaḥ syāt, yataḥ kevalatriphalākvāthe cūrṇe ca bhāgavṛddhiḥ dṛśyate | tathāca “pathyāstisro bibhītakyaḥ ṣaḍ dhātryo dvādaśeti ca | prasthārdhasalile kvāthamaṣṭabhāgāvaśeṣita”miti, netraroge ekā harītakyādicūrṇamātraiva ato na virodhaḥ | sarpirmadhubhyāṃ netrabhayahāri sarpirghṛtaṃ madhu ca ato dvayam, atulyaṃ gṛhyate tulyayormadhusarpiṣorviruddhatvāt | atha ca anyairlikhitaṃ havirmadhubhyāṃ samābhyāmiti triphalāprayoge likhitam | iti triphalācūrṇam || 1-11 ||
atha tryūṣaṇacūrṇaṃ dīpanādau - pippalī maricaṃ śuṇṭhī tribhistryūṣaṇamucyate | dīpanaṃ śleṣmamedoghnaṃ kuṣṭhapīnasanāśanam || 12 || jayedarocakaṃ sāmaṃ mehagulmagalāmayān|
Dī. atha tryūṣaṇacūrṇamāha - pippalīti | idamapi trikaṭukaparyāyanāmnā kathitaṃ, trayāṇāṃ sāmyamityanuktatvāt | sāmamityarocakaṃ doṣaṃ vā | mehagulmagalāmayāniti mehāmayān gulmāmayān galāmayān || 12 ||
Gū. sugamam || 12 ||
atha pañcakolamarucyādau - pippalīcavyaviśvāhnāpippalīmūlacitrakaiḥ || 13 || pañjakolamiti khyātaṃ rucyaṃ dīpanapācanam | ānāhaplīhagulmaghnaṃ śūlaśleṣmodarāpaham || 14 ||
Dī. pañcakolamapyāha - pippalīti | atra cavyaṃ gajapippalīmūlaṃ, viśvāhvā śuṇṭhī, pippalīmūlaṃ pippalījaṭā, citrakaḥ svanāmakhyātaḥ | atrāpi pañcānāṃ samatvam | rucyaṃ rucikaraṃ, dīpanamagneḥ, pācanamāmādīnām, ānāha udarāpūraḥ | (ūṣakādigaṇaḥ1 -‘ūṣakaṃ2 saindhavaṃ hiṅgu kāśīsadvayagugguluḥ | śilājatu tutthakaṃ ca ūṣakādirudāhṛtaḥ | ūṣakādiḥ kaphaṃ hanti gaṇo medoviśodhanaḥ | aśmarīśarkarāmūtraśūlaghnaḥ kaphagulmanut’ iti | ayamapi pāṭhaḥ kvacit) || 13 || 14 ||
Gū. sugamam || 13 || 14 ||
idānīṃ trigandhacāturjātakacūrṇamāha - trigandhamelātvakpatraiścaturjātaṃ sakeśaraiḥ | trigandhaṃ sacaturjātaṃ rūkṣoṣṇaṃ laghu pittakṛt ||15|| varṇyaṃ rucikaraṃ tīkṣṇaṃ viṣaśleṣmāmayāñjayet |
Dī. trigandhamiti | elātvakpatraiḥ kṛtvā trigandhaṃ kathitaṃ, cāturjātaṃ tu sakeśarairelātvakpatraireva proktam, elā sūkṣmailā, tvak guḍatvak, patraṃ patrakaṃ, keśaraṃ nāgakeśaram | rūkṣoṣṇamiti vīryataḥ laghupākitvāt | varṇyaṃ kāntikaram| tīkṣṇaṃ marīcādivat || 15 ||
Gū. atha trisugandham | elā tvakpatrajam3 etaistribhistrigandhamucyate | caturjātakaṃ sakeśarairetaiścaturjātaṃ keśaram | spaṣṭamanyat || 15 ||
atha jīvanīyagaṇaḥ - kākolī kṣīrakākolī jīvakarṣabhakau tathā ||16|| medā cānyā mahāmedā jīvantī madhukaṃ tathā | mudgaparṇī māṣaparṇī jīvanīyo gaṇastvayam || 17 || jīvanīyagaṇaḥ svādurgarbhasaṃdhānakṛdguruḥ | stanyakṛdbṛṃhaṇo vṛṣyaḥ snigdhaḥ śītastṛṣāpahaḥ ||18|| raktapittaṃ kṣayaṃ śoṣaṃ jvaradāhanilāñjayet |
Dī. jīvanīyagaṇamāha - kākolītyādi | atra jīvanīyagaṇe kākolī kṣīrakākolī ca svanāmataḥ prasiddhā | kiñcidanayorjñāpanārthaṃ lakṣaṇamapyāha tadyathā - kākolī madhurā carvaṇataḥ kṣīraprāyā varṇataḥ śuklā ca | kṣīrakālolī atyantakṣīrayuktā | etaddvayaṃ viṭapaviśeṣeṇa dakṣiṇottaretīva prasiddham | jīvakarṣabhakāviti | jīvakaśca ṛṣabhakaścaitau, jīvako madhurarasaḥ hrasvaviṭapaḥ kūrcaśīrṣakaḥ, ṛṣabhako'pi hrasvavṛkṣaḥ sahasrachidro viṣāṇīti | tatheti grahaṇena etaddvayamapi dakṣiṇadeśe pracarati medā cānyā mahāmedā iti medā svalpaparṇī | medagandholpavṛkṣasaṃjñakaḥ4 , sa ca kāṣṭhaviśeṣo nakhacchedyo deśāntarādavagantavyaḥ | mahāmedā'ṣṭacchidrānvitā kāṣṭhaviśeṣaḥ, sa ca dakṣiṇottarapathe parvateṣu prasiddhaḥ | tatra devamaṇiriti prasiddhaḥ | jīvantī śākaviśeṣaḥ | sā cottarāpathe parvateṣu, vandā vṛkṣaśākhāsu tatraivotpadyate | madhukaṃ madhuyaṣṭīti jalajātā vallīviśeṣaḥ, madhurā bhakṣaṇataśca, asau vikhyātā sarvatraiva, mudgaparṇī māṣaparṇī ceti dravyayugmaṃ nijanāmnā prasiddham | mudgavatparṇaṃ yasyāḥ sā mudgaparṇī | evaṃ māṣaparṇīti sāpi kṣudrā vanasambhavā | mārjāramodinīti māṣaparṇī ca kṛṣṇanālā hayapucchikākārā parvatodbhavā5 madhurā ca | ebhirayaṃ jīvanīyagaṇasaṃjñaḥ svādurmadhurarasaḥ | garbhasandhānakṛditi vandhyāgarbhakaraḥ prabhāvāt, stanyakṛditi kṣīravṛddhikaraḥ, bṛṃhaṇo dhātuvṛddhikaraḥ, vṛṣyaḥ śukravṛddhikaraḥ || || 16-18 ||
Gū. ayaṃ jīvanīyagaṇaḥ kāśmīrādau prasiddhaḥ | kākolyādīnāṃ lakṣaṇamāha granthāntarāt - ”pīvarīsannibhaḥ kandaḥ śuṣkasāraṃ sugandhikam | kākolī kṣīrakākolī ardhordhaṃ taṃ vinirdiśet || īṣacchuklā tu kākolī kṣīravatpāṇḍurā parā | jīvakarṣabhakau śvetau hemādriśikharodbhavau || jīvakaḥ kūrcikākāra ṛṣabho vṛṣaśṛṅgavat || śuṣkārdrakanibhaṃ kandaṃ snigdhaṃ madhuraśītalam | mahāmedeti vikhyātā durgandho bheda ucyate” || jīvantī doḍīśākaḥ, madhukaṃ madhuyaṣṭī, mudgaparṇī mugonī, māṣaparṇī māṣonī || 16-18 ||
athāṣṭakavargaḥ - dve mede dve ca kākolyau jīvakarṣabhakau tathā ||19|| ṛddhivṛddhī ca taiḥ sarvairaṣṭavarga udāhṛtaḥ | aṣṭavargo budhaiḥ prokto jīvanīyasamo guṇaiḥ ||20||
Dī. athāṣṭavargamapyāha - dve mede iti| asau guṇaiḥ kṛtvā jīvanīyagaṇasāmya: sa cāṣṭabhirdravyairmilitaḥ | tadyathā - “dve mede dve ca kākolyau jīvakarṣabhakau tathā |” dvau ca ṛddhivṛddhī ca dve, atra ṛddhivṛddhī tu nāmnā prasiddhe | tayościhnamiti yasya gṛhe ṛddhivṛddhī staḥ tasya vasuvṛddhirbhavatīti prasiddhiḥ | ayaṃ ca suśrutagaṇasaṃgrahe jīvanīyāṣṭakasaṃjñaḥ | carakayoge tat pūrvoktaṃ jīvanīyadaśakamiti || 19 || 20 ||
