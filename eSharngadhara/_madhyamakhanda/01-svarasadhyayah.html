--- title: "1. atha svarasādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 1 order: 1 ---
śārṅgadharasaṃhitāyām - madhyamakhaṇḍa: atha svarasādhyāya : | athātaḥ svarasaḥ kalkaḥ kvāthaśca himaphāṇṭakau | jñeyāḥ kaṣāyāḥ pañjaite laghavaḥ syuryathottaram || 1 ||
Dī. atha madhyamakhaṇḍaḥ prārabhyate | tatrādau kaṣāyasaṃjñakavṛttamāha - athāta iti | athaśabdo maṅgalārthaḥ | ata iti prathamakhaṇḍānantaram | ete pañcakaṣāyā jñeyāḥ | te yathottaraṃ laghavaḥ syuriti | yo yasmāduttaro yathottaram | te ke ? svarasakalkakkāthahimaphāṇṭasaṃjñāḥ | svarasa iti sadyorasaḥ, kalko dṛṣadi peṣitaḥ, kvāthaḥ kkathitaḥ kaṣāyaḥ, hima iti śītakaṣāyaḥ, phāṇṭa iti jalaplāvitaṃ cūrṇam ||1|| atha teṣu pūrvaṃ svarasakalpanāmāha - tallakṣaṇaṃ yathā – āhatāditi - īdṛśāddravyāt samudbhavo rasaḥ sa svarasa ityucyate, kīdṛśāt ? tamāha - āhatāt kṣatāt śakalīkṛtāt tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāditi sadya utpāṭitāt | kṣuṇṇāditi ulūkhalādiṣu musalādibhiḥ kuṭṭitāt | vastraniṣpīḍita iti paṭena parisruto rasa iti śeṣaḥ | dravyāditi sāmānyagrahaṇenātra tvaṅmūlaphalādīnāṃ grahaṇam || lakṣaṇāntaramāha - kuḍavamityādi | ceti pakṣāntareṇāpi svarasaḥ kathitaḥ, sa yathā - kuḍavapramāṇaṃ cūrṇībhūtadravyaṃ dviguṇajale kṣipta- mahorātraṃ sthitaṃ bhavati tasmād yo raso jāta utpannaḥ so'pi svarasa uttamo jñeyaḥ | kuḍavagrahaṇamatra sakalamānadyotanārtham | ahorātragrahaṇenaiva himakaṣāyādbhinno jñātavyaḥ | matāntaramapi yathā - ādāyetyādi | svarasānāmasaṃbhave avidyamāne śuṣkadravyaṃ kṣuṇṇaṃ yathāparimā- ṇamādāya gṛhītvā aṣṭaguṇitapānīye sādhyaṃ kathitamityarthaḥ | tasmāt yat pādāvaśeṣaṃ bhavati tasyāpi svarasasaṃjñā | yathā parimāṇādinā kvāthādbhinnametat | idānīmanukteṣu pānārthaṃ parimāṇapūrvakaṃ prakṣepamāha - svarasasyeti | svarasasya ādye ardhaṃ palaṃ prayojayet | pānārthamityarthaḥ | niḥśoṣitamagnisiddhaṃ ca svarasaṃ palamātraṃ pibedityarthaḥ | tayoḥ kiṃcillaghutvāt || 2-5 ||
Gū. dvitīyakhaṇḍe ādau svarasādīnāmabhidhānātsvarasādīnāha - athāta ityādi | atheti rogagaṇanānantaraṃ svarasaḥ, kalkaḥ, kvāthaḥ, himaḥ, phāṇṭaḥ, ete pañca kaṣāyā jñeyāḥ – te ca yathottaraṃ laghavaḥ syuriti yojanā | yathottaramiti svarasātkalko laghuḥ evamuttaraṃ jñeyam | nanu svaraso gururbhavati tasyāpekṣayā anye laghavastatkathaṃ tasyaivādāvabhidhānam, svarasasya gurutve prathamatve pramāṇamāha - ‘svarasasya gurutvācca palamardhaṃ prayojayet’ (iti)| ucyate, svarasasya kvathitādigauravakriyābhāvādādāvabhidhānam | yadvā laghvapekṣayā guruḥ pūjyaḥ ataḥ svarasasya gurutvādādāvabhidhānaṃ, tathācoktaṃ vāgbhaṭe bheṣajakalpanāsthāne - ‘rasaḥ kalkaḥ śṛtaḥ śītaḥ phāṇṭaśceti prakalpanā | pañcadhaivaṃ kaṣāyāṇāṃ pūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ balādhikā’ | kaṣāyā iti kaṣāyadravyasādhitatvāt, yadvā kaṣāyāḥ kaṣāyanāmānaḥ | saṃjñāparo'yaṃ kaṣāyaśabdaḥ ||1|| teṣu svarasakalpanāmāha - evaṃbhūtāt kṣuṇṇātkṛṭṭitāddravyāt yaḥ vastraniṣpīḍito raso bhavetsa svarasa ucyate | kiṃbhūtāddravyāt | āhatāt śastrādinā chinnādityarthaḥ, ‘āhataḥ syātpratīghāte āhataṃ kṣatite'pi ce’ti viśvakoṣaḥ | āhatāt na hatam ahataṃ tasmāt | kṛmyagnyādibhiradūṣitāt kṛmyādidūṣitasya bheṣajakalpe niṣedhāt, yadvā tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāditi vā pāṭhaḥ | tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāt tatkṣaṇa ānītāt| svarasasya prakārāntaramāha - kuḍavamiti | catuḥpalaṃ cūrṇitadravyamauṣadhaṃ punardviguṇe aṣṭa- palamite jale kṣiptam ahorātraṃ sthitaṃ tasmāt pātrādvastrapūtaṃ gṛhītaṃ sa vā uttamo rasaḥ svaraso bhavet| ārdradravyābhāve śuṣkadravyasyāpi svaraso bhavedityāha - ādāyeti | sa rasaḥ svarasānāṃ ārdradravyāṇāṃ vā asambhave śuṣkaṃ ādāya aṣṭaguṇe jale sādhyaṃ pādaśiṣṭaśca gṛhyate punargṛhītatvātsvarasasya gurutvāt palamardhaṃ prayujyate cet niḥśoṣitaṃ rātristhāpitaṃ agnisiddhaṃ cet palamātraṃ pibediti yojanā | nanu idaṃ svarasalakṣaṇaṃ rātristhitatvāddhimalakṣaṇe agnipakvātkvāthakṣaṇe ca vyabhicarati | yataḥ vāgbhaṭe idaṃ svarasalakṣaṇaṃ likhitaṃ ‘sadyaḥsamuddhṛtakṣuṇṇāt yaḥ sravetpaṭapīḍanāt | vastraniṣpīḍito yaḥ sa svaraso rasa ucyate’ ato lakṣaṇe vyabhicāraḥ ? ucyate, idaṃ tantrāntaramataṃ, yadvā ṣoḍaśaguṇe nīre siddhaṃ bhavati, svaraso'ṣṭaguṇe nīre bhavatīti bhedaḥ ||2-5||
. āhatāttatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāddravyātkṣuṇṇātsamuddharet | vastraniṣpīḍito yaḥ sa rasaḥ1 svarasa uccate ||2|| kuḍavaṃ cūrṇitaṃ dravyaṃ kṣiptaṃ ca dviguṇe jale | ahorātraṃ2 sthitaṃ tasmādbhavedvā rasa uttamaḥ ||3|| ādāya śuṣkadravyaṃ vā svarasānāmasaṃbhave | jale'ṣṭaguṇite sādhyaṃ pādaśiṣṭaṃ3 ca gṛhyate ||4|| svarasasya gurutvācca palamardhaṃ prayojayet | niśoṣitaṃ cāgnisiddhaṃ palamātraṃ rasaṃ pibet ||5||
Dī. atha teṣu pūrvaṃ svarasakalpanāmāha - tallakṣaṇaṃ yathā – āhatāditi - īdṛśāddravyāt samudbhavo rasaḥ sa svarasa ityucyate, kīdṛśāt ? tamāha - āhatāt kṣatāt śakalīkṛtāt tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāditi sadya utpāṭitāt | kṣuṇṇāditi ulūkhalādiṣu musalādibhiḥ kuṭṭitāt | vastraniṣpīḍita iti paṭena parisruto rasa iti śeṣaḥ | dravyāditi sāmānyagrahaṇenātra tvaṅmūlaphalādīnāṃ grahaṇam || lakṣaṇāntaramāha - kuḍavamityādi | ceti pakṣāntareṇāpi svarasaḥ kathitaḥ, sa yathā - kuḍavapramāṇaṃ cūrṇībhūtadravyaṃ dviguṇajale kṣipta- mahorātraṃ sthitaṃ bhavati tasmād yo raso jāta utpannaḥ so'pi svarasa uttamo jñeyaḥ | kuḍavagrahaṇamatra sakalamānadyotanārtham | ahorātragrahaṇenaiva himakaṣāyādbhinno jñātavyaḥ | matāntaramapi yathā - ādāyetyādi | svarasānāmasaṃbhave avidyamāne śuṣkadravyaṃ kṣuṇṇaṃ yathāparimā- ṇamādāya gṛhītvā aṣṭaguṇitapānīye sādhyaṃ kathitamityarthaḥ | tasmāt yat pādāvaśeṣaṃ bhavati tasyāpi svarasasaṃjñā | yathā parimāṇādinā kvāthādbhinnametat | idānīmanukteṣu pānārthaṃ parimāṇapūrvakaṃ prakṣepamāha - svarasasyeti | svarasasya ādye ardhaṃ palaṃ prayojayet | pānārthamityarthaḥ | niḥśoṣitamagnisiddhaṃ ca svarasaṃ palamātraṃ pibedityarthaḥ | tayoḥ kiṃcillaghutvāt || 2-5 ||
Gū. teṣu svarasakalpanāmāha - evaṃbhūtāt kṣuṇṇātkṛṭṭitāddravyāt yaḥ vastraniṣpīḍito raso bhavetsa svarasa ucyate | kiṃbhūtāddravyāt | āhatāt śastrādinā chinnādityarthaḥ, ‘āhataḥ syātpratīghāte āhataṃ kṣatite'pi ce’ti viśvakoṣaḥ | āhatāt na hatam ahataṃ tasmāt | kṛmyagnyādibhiradūṣitāt kṛmyādidūṣitasya bheṣajakalpe niṣedhāt, yadvā tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāditi vā pāṭhaḥ | tatkṣaṇākṛṣṭāt tatkṣaṇa ānītāt| svarasasya prakārāntaramāha - kuḍavamiti | catuḥpalaṃ cūrṇitadravyamauṣadhaṃ punardviguṇe aṣṭa- palamite jale kṣiptam ahorātraṃ sthitaṃ tasmāt pātrādvastrapūtaṃ gṛhītaṃ sa vā uttamo rasaḥ svaraso bhavet| ārdradravyābhāve śuṣkadravyasyāpi svaraso bhavedityāha - ādāyeti | sa rasaḥ svarasānāṃ ārdradravyāṇāṃ vā asambhave śuṣkaṃ ādāya aṣṭaguṇe jale sādhyaṃ pādaśiṣṭaśca gṛhyate punargṛhītatvātsvarasasya gurutvāt palamardhaṃ prayujyate cet niḥśoṣitaṃ rātristhāpitaṃ agnisiddhaṃ cet palamātraṃ pibediti yojanā | nanu idaṃ svarasalakṣaṇaṃ rātristhitatvāddhimalakṣaṇe agnipakvātkvāthakṣaṇe ca vyabhicarati | yataḥ vāgbhaṭe idaṃ svarasalakṣaṇaṃ likhitaṃ ‘sadyaḥsamuddhṛtakṣuṇṇāt yaḥ sravetpaṭapīḍanāt | vastraniṣpīḍito yaḥ sa svaraso rasa ucyate’ ato lakṣaṇe vyabhicāraḥ ? ucyate, idaṃ tantrāntaramataṃ, yadvā ṣoḍaśaguṇe nīre siddhaṃ bhavati, svaraso'ṣṭaguṇe nīre bhavatīti bhedaḥ ||2-5||
prakṣepapramāṇam| madhu śvetā guḍaṃ kṣārān jīrakaṃ lavaṇaṃ tathā | ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ cūrṇādīnkolamātrānrase kṣipet || 6 ||
Dī. tatra prakṣepamāha - madhvityādi | madhvādikaṃ kolamātraṃ yathā syāttathā kṣipet | kolaṃ palasya cāṣṭāṃśam | madhvitisāmānyagrahaṇena tajjātayo'ṣṭau gṛhyante | tadyathā - “pautikaṃ bhrāmaraṃ kṣaudraṃ mākṣikaṃ chātrameva ca| ārghyamauddālakaṃ dālamityaṣṭau madhujātayaḥ” iti | evamaviśeṣoktau sarvatra | śvetā sitaśarkarā, guḍaṃ prasiddhaṃ, tadupalakṣitatvenānye ikṣuvikārā yathā doṣāpekṣayā gṛhyante | kṣārāniti bahuvacanatvena sarve kṣārāḥ, anukte yavakṣārameva sarvatra | jīrakaṃ prasiddhaṃ sāmānyagrahaṇena pītakṛṣṇādikam, aviśeṣe śvetajīrakameva | lavaṇamiti sāmānyena pañcalavaṇāni gṛjjate, tadyathā -‘sindhusauvarcalopetaṃ viḍaṃ sāmudrajodbhidaṃ’4 iti | anukte tu saindhavameva grāhyaṃ sarvatra | ghṛtatailaṃ pakvāpakvaṃ nānāvidham | tatrānukte tu gavyaṃ ghṛtaṃ tilatailaṃ ca pradhānamiti | tathāhi - ‘ghṛteṣu goghṛtaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ taileṣu tilajaṃ tathā’ iti | cūrṇādīniti vyoṣacūrṇādīn | ādigrahaṇena kṣāramūtrāriṣṭaśuktādayo rogāpekṣayā gṛhyante | yadvā guṭikāvalehādayo'pyādiśabdena grāhyāḥ || 6 ||
Gū. idānīṃ svarase prakṣepapramāṇamāha - rase svarase prakṣepaṇārthamuktān madhvādīn kolamātrān kṣipet | kolaśabdena ṭaṅkadvayamucyate | agnyapekṣayā vā deyaṃ, yataḥ ‘mātrāyā na vyavasthāsti vyādhiṃ koṣṭhaṃ balaṃ vayaḥ | ālocya deśakālau ca yojyā tadvacca kalpanā ||’ iti paribhāṣāyāmuktam|| 6 ||
amṛtāsvarasaḥ | sa yathā5 - amṛtāyā rasaḥ kṣaudrayuktaḥ sarvapramehajit | haridrācūrṇayukto vā raso dhātryāḥ samākṣikaḥ ||7||
Dī. svarasānāha - sa yatheti | amṛtā6 guḍūcī tasyā rasaḥ sadyorasaḥ kṣaudrayukta iti madhuprakṣiptaḥ | sarvagrahaṇeneti viṃśatirmehāḥ | prayogāntaramāha - dhātryā iti | dhātryā rasaḥ sadyaskaḥ kvathito vā haridrācūrṇayukto madhuyukto vā | sarvagrahaṇenāyamapi sarvapramehajidbhavatīti tātparyārthaḥ | vāgrahaṇena haridrāyā: kalkamapi deyam | tathāca tantrāntare - ‘niśākalkayuto dhātrīraso vā mākṣikānvita:| madhunā triphalācūrṇaṃ pramehāṇāṃ vināśanam’ iti ||7||
Gū. atha vyādhipratyanīkārthaṃ bahūn svarasān darśayannāha - sa yatheti | etasyāyamartha: sa: svarasaḥ yathā bhavati tathā ucyate, amṛtā guḍūcī tasyā rasa: svarasa: madhuyukta: sarvapramehajidbhavati athavā dhātryā āmalakyā rasaḥ haridrācūrṇayuktaḥ samākṣikaḥ sarvapramehajidbhavatīti pūrveṇānvayaḥ | kartavyatā tu pūrvoktaiva || 7 ||
raktapittādiṣu vāsakādisvarasaḥ | vāsakaḥ svarasaḥ peyo madhunā raktapittajit | jvarakāsakṣayaharaḥ kāmalāśleṣmapittahā || 8 || triphalāyā rasaḥ kṣaudrayukto dārvīraso'tha vā | nimbasya vā guḍūcyā vā pīto jayati kāmalām|| 9 ||
Dī. vāsaka ityādi | vāsakaḥ āṭarūṣakaḥ, tasya patrāṇi iti vyavahārāt | madhuneti prakṣepaḥ ayaṃ cordhvage raktapitte viśeṣeṇa yojyaḥ | śeṣaṃ sugamam || triphalāyā iti | triphalādīnāṃ caturṇāṃ svarasāḥ kāmalāṃ ghnantīti | tatra triphalādārvyo: kvathitarasaḥ, nimbaguḍūcyostu sadyorasa eva | madhuprakṣepa eṣu caturṣu yogeṣu jñeyaḥ | triphalā vikhyātā sātra harītakyādikā | yadyapi triphalāśabdena drākṣākāśmaryaparūṣakāṇyucyante | taduktaṃ - ‘pathyāvibhītadhātrībhistriphalā mahatī smṛtā | hrasvā kāśmaryamṛdvīkāparūṣakaphalairbhavet’ iti | tathāpi sarvatra guṇaprādhānyādaviśeṣoktau harītakyādīnyena triphalāśabdena gṛhyante | dārvī dāruharidrā tasyāstvak grāhyā, tvagabhāve kāṣṭhameveti sarvatra || 8 || 9 ||
Gū. atha vāsakādisvaraso raktapittādau | dārvī dāruharidrā spaṣṭamanyat | vāsakasvarasaḥ madhunā peyaḥ raktapittajidbhavati vāsakasvarasaḥ ardhapalaṃ madhu ṭaṃkadvayaṃmukhātplutaṃ raktaṃ tathā jvarādiharaḥ triphalāsvarasaḥ madhuyuta: tathā dārvī dāruharidrāsvarasaḥ madhuyuta: kāmalādināśako bhavati || 8 || 9 ||
viṣamajvare tulasīdroṇapuṣpīsvarasau | pīto maricacūrṇena tulasīpatrajo rasaḥ | droṇapuṣṣīraso vāpi nihanti viṣamajvarān ||10||
Dī. pīta iti - droṇapuṣpī ‘gumā’ iti loke | maricacūrṇamapyatra prakṣiptaṃ, tacca tīkṣṇatvānmāṣakadvayamiti vyavahāraḥ | viṣamajvarānityaikāhikādīn ||10||
Gū. atha viṣamajvare tulasīsvarasamāha - maricacūrṇena pītaḥ tulasīpatrajaḥ rasaḥ svarasaḥ droṇapuṣpīsvaraso vā pītaḥ viṣamajvarānsantatādīn nihanti | droṇapuṣpī gūmāśākaḥ kaṭūṣṇatvātkarṣa: rasaḥ maricaṃ māṣakamātraṃ mātrāyā: sthitirnāstītyuktatvāt || 10 ||
raktātisāre jambvādisvarasaḥ | jambvāsrāmalakīnāṃ ca pallavottho raso jayet | madhvājyakṣīrasaṃyukto raktātīsāramulbaṇam ||11||
Dī. jambvāmreti | jambvādīnāṃ pallavodbhavo raso madhvādiyukto'tīsāraṃ jayet | pallavagrahaṇena komalapatrāṇītyuktam | ājyaṃ ghṛtaṃ, kṣīraṃ dugdham | eke ‘madhvājakṣīrasaṃyukta’miti paṭhanti vyādhyukta- tvāt | tathāhi - ‘ājaṃ śoṣajvaraśvāsaraktapittātisārajit, iti | ulbaṇamiti vṛddhaṃ, cirakālasthitamityanye || 11 ||
Gū. atha raktātīsāre jambvādisvarasaḥ | jambvāmrāmalakīnāṃ ca pallavottharasaḥ svarasaḥ madhvājyakṣīrasaṃyuktaḥ ulbaṇaṃ raktātīsāraṃ jayet | jambvādīnāṃ pallavaṃ kisalayaṃ, ulbaṇam adhikam || 11 ||
babbūla – syonāka - kuṭajasvarasāḥ | sthūlababbūlikāpatrarasa: pānādvyapohati | sarvātisārānsyonākakuṭajatvagraso'thavā || 12 ||
Dī. sthūlababbūliketi | sthūlababbūlikā araṇyodbhavā | tasyātirūkṣatvādeke parvatīyāmiti vadanti, sā cordhvaśākhābhirupalakṣitā prasiddhaiva | ‘sthalababbūlikāpatrarasapānā’diti pāṭhāntaram| sthalavat sthalajavat śūlikā ānūpajāpi iti tātparyārthaḥ | śūliketi śūlikā kathitā | uktaṃ ca nāmamālāyām - ‘babbūlaḥ saralaḥ śūlī selakaṇṭo'dhikaṇḍakaḥ’ iti | śūlikā'lpaviṭapā, babbūrīti loke | prayogāntaramāha - syonāketyādi | syonākakuṭajayostvagudbhavo rasastatkāryakara iti | syonākatvagudbhavaḥ kuṭajatvagudbhavo veti yogadvayamiti kecit | atrāpi madhuprakṣepaḥ pūrvavadanusartavyaḥ | sarvātisārāniti saptaprakārānityarthaḥ || 12 ||
Gū. atha sarvātīsāre babbūlasvarasaḥ | sthūlababbūlikaṃ niṣkaṃṭakababbūla: tasya patrarasa: svarasa: tatpānātsarvātisārānṣaṅvidhāñjayati | athavā syonākaḥ araluḥ, tathā kuṭajaḥ kuṭaja: kūḍā tayoḥ tvagbhavo raso vā atīsārāñjayet ||12||
pratiśyāye - ārdrakasvarasaḥ | ārdrakasvarasaḥ kṣaudrayukto vṛṣaṇavātanut | śvāsakāsārucīrhanti pratiśyāyaṃ vyapohati || 13 ||
Dī. ārdraketi | ārdrakaṃ svanāmnā prasiddham | tadrasaḥ kṣaudrayuto madhuyutaḥ vṛṣaṇavātanudbhavati | vṛṣa- ṇavāto'ṇḍavātaḥ, kuraṇḍa iti kecit | pratiśyāyaṃ pratisrāvarūpam || 13 ||

atha mṛgaśṛṅgapuṭapākaḥ śūlādau -- śarāvasaṃpuṭe dagdhaṃ śṛṅgaṃ hariṇajaṃ pibet || 42 || gavyena sarpiṣā yuktaṃ hṛcchūlaṃ nāśayeddhruvam |iti śrīdāmodarasūnuśrīśārṅgadharācāryaviracitāyāṃ śārṅgadharasaṃhitāyāṃ madhyamakhaṇḍe svarasādikalpanā nāma prathamo'dhyāyaḥ || 1 ||
Dī. idānīṃ mṛgaśṛṅgapuṭapākamāha - śarāveti | hariṇājjjātaṃ hariṇajaṃ śṛṅgamiti viṣāṇam, tacca khaṇḍitaṃ kṛtvā grāhyam | dagdhamiti | puṭapākayuktyetyarthaḥ | ata eva mṛdāvaliptaṃ śarāvasampuṭaṃ kāryam | gavyagrahaṇaṃ anyaghṛtavyavacchedārthaṃ tasyātiśreṣṭhatvāt | bhakṣaṇamatra phāṇṭavajjñeyam | dhruvamiti - satyapratyayakārako yogo'yam || 42 || iti śrīvāstavyānvayaprakāśavaidyaśrībhāvasiṃhātmajenāḍhamallena viracitāyāṃ śārṅgadharadīpikāyāṃ madhyamakhaṇḍe svarasakalpanādhyāyaḥ prathamaḥ || 1 ||
Gū. śarāvasampuṭe dagdhaṃ bhasmasātkṛtaṃ hariṇajaṃ śṛṅgaṃ tadbhasma gavyena sarpiṣā ghṛtena yuktaṃ pibet | hṛcchūlaṃ naśyati dhruvamiti niścayena | nanu svarase puṭapāke ca ko bhedaḥ yataḥ puṭapāko bhinna evoktaḥ? ucyate, svarasakalpanāyāṃ rasa eva gṛhyate iti iyānbhedaḥ ato bhinna eva || 42 || iti śrīśārṅgadharasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne svarasādikalpanādhyāyaḥ || 1||
