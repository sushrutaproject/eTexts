Kāśīrāma Vaidya. atha dhātuśodhanam | svarṇādayaḥ ete saptadhātavo vijñeyāstān budhaḥ taddoṣaniḥsāraṇārthaṃ śodhayet | tāraḥ raupyam iti, pittalo nāgaḥ, sīsakaḥ sīsakaṃ, tīkṣṇaloham || 1 || atha dhātuśodhanaṃ | svarṇādidhātūnāṃ patrāṇi tailādau tridhā trivāraṃ agnau pratāpayet taptvā taptvā niṣiṃcayet nirvāpayet | evaṃ svarṇādilohānāṃ śuddhiḥ saṃprajāyate | nāgavaṅgayorviśeṣeṇa śodhanamāha - tadā viśuddhiḥ syāt| nanu pittalaḥ kṛtrimastatkathaṃ sapta ? -- ucyante, eke yaśadena saha sapta āhuḥ | tathāca ‘svarṇaṃ rūpyaṃ ca tāmraṃ ca raṅgaṃ yaśadameva ca | sīsaṃ lohaṃ ca saptaite dhātavo girisambhavā iti | athaṃ svarṇādidhātumāraṇaṃ tatrādau śārṅgadharānuktamapi svarṇotpattibhedaśuddhāśuddhalakṣaṇamāha - athotpattiḥ ‘purā nijāśramasthānāṃ saptarṣīṇāṃ jitātmanāṃ | patnyo vilokya lāvaṇyaṃ lakṣmīsampannayauvanāḥ || kandarpadarpavidhvastacetaso jātavedasaḥ | patitaṃ yaddharāpṛṣṭhe retaḥ sauvarṇatāmagāt ||’ atha bhedāḥ ‘prākṛtaṃ sahajaṃ vahnisaṃbhūtaṃ khanijaṃ tathā | rasendravedhasaṃjātaṃ svarṇaṃ pañcavidhaṃ mataṃ || brahmāṇḍaṃ saṃvṛtaṃ yena durlabhaṃ prākṛtaṃ hi tat | brahmavrataṃ tu sahajaṃ vahnijaṃ parikīrtitam | etatsvarṇatrayaṃ divyaṃ varṇairyaccāśubhiryutaṃ | dhāraṇādeva tatkuryāccharīramajarāmaraṃ || khanijaṃ khananājjātaṃ vedhajaṃ rasavedhataḥ | taccaturdaśavarṇāḍhyaṃ bhakṣitaṃ sarvarogajit’ || athāśuddhasvarṇaparīkṣā - ‘śvetāṅgaṃ kaṭhinaṃ rūkṣaṃ vivarṇaṃ ca samardalam | dāhe cchede sitaṃ śvetaṃ gharṣe cāpi malaṃ tyajet’ ||2-4||
atha svarṇamāraṇam -- svarṇāñca dviguṇaṃ sūtamamlena saha mardayet | tadgolakasamaṃ gandhaṃ nidadhyādadharottaram || 5 || golakaṃ ca tato rundhyāccharāvadṛḍhasaṃpuṭe | triṃśadvanopalairdadyātpuṭānyevaṃ caturdaśa ||6 || nirutthaṃ jāyate bhasma gandho deyaḥ punaḥ punaḥ |
Āḍhamalla. atha dhātumadhye svarṇamāraṇamāha - svarṇācceti | sūtaṃ pāradaṃ tacca suvarṇaparimāṇāt dviguṇaṃ grāhyam | amlena bījapūrādinā tadgolakasamaṃ gandhamiti tat svarṇapāradakṛtena golakena sāmyaṃ śodhitagandhakaṃ saṃgṛhya tadgolakasyādhaḥ upari ca datvā śarāvasaṃpuṭe saṃdhārya puṭediti granthābhiprāyaḥ | sampradāye kiñcidgandhakaṃ datvā golakaṃ kāryaṃ paścāt śeṣaṃ gandhamupari deyam | dṛḍhasaṃpuṭa iti | dṛḍhagrahaṇena mṛtkarpaṭakaṃ sūcyate tena mṛtkarpaṭakenā'tra dṛḍhatā kāryā | evaṃ caturdaśapuṭaiḥ kṛtvā nirutthaṃ jāyate suvarṇamiti śeṣaḥ | nirutthatā'trātyarthamūrcchanā kathyate na tu svarṇasya mṛtirbhavati2 | puṭamatra triṃśadvanopalaiḥ kṛtvā deyam | tacca kukkuṭapuṭasaṃjñayā vihitam | vanopalaṃ śuṣkagomayam, tacca karaṇḍāśabdavācyam | gandho deyaḥ punaḥ punaḥ iti | śuddhagandhakaṃ ca pūrvoktavidhānena pratyekaṃ puṭaṃ yojyamityarthaḥ || 5 || 6 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. svarṇasya bhūrjavatpatrīkṛtasya kuṭṭanenetyarthaḥ | sūtaṃ śuddhaṃ hiṅgulotthaṃ vā dviguṇam amlena nimbukādirasena saha mardayet | tadgolakasamaṃ gandhakam adharottaraṃ dadyāt ūrdhvādhaḥ sa eva madhye mudrayitvā triṃśadvanopalaiḥ puṭet | evaṃ caturdaśapuṭāt nirūtthaṃ jāyate | gandhakastulyaḥ puṭe puṭe deyaḥ || 5 || 6||
athāparo vidhiḥ- kāñcane gālite nāgaṃ ṣoḍaśāṃśena nikṣipet || 7 || cūrṇayitvā tathāmlena ghṛṣṭvā kṛtvā ca golakam | golakena samaṃ gandhaṃ dattvā caivādharottaram || 8 || śarāvasaṃpuṭe dhṛtvā puṭet triṃśadvanopalaiḥ | evaṃ saptapuṭairhema nirūtthaṃ bhasma jāyate || 9 || anyacca svarṇamāraṇamāha - kāñcanārarasairghṛṣṭvā samasūtakagandhayoḥ | kajjalyā hemapatrāṇi lepayetsamamātrayā || 10 || kāñcanāratvacaḥ kalkairmūṣāyugmaṃ prakalpayet | dhṛtvā tatsaṃpuṭe golaṃ mṛnmūṣāsaṃpuṭe pacet || 11 || nidhāya saṃdhirodhaṃ ca kṛtvā saṃśoṣya kokilaiḥ | vahniṃ kharataraṃ kuryādevaṃ dadyātpuṭatrayam || 12 || nirutthaṃ jāyate bhasma sarvakāryeṣu yojayet | kāñcanāraprakāreṇa lāṅgalī hanti kāñcanam || 13 || jvālāmukhī yathā hanyāttathā hanti manaḥśilā | athānyadapi svarṇamāraṇaprakāramāha - śilāsindūrayoścūrṇaṃ samayorarkadugdhakaiḥ ||14|| saptaiva bhāvanā dadyācchoṣayecca punaḥ punaḥ | tatastu gālite hemni kalko'yaṃ dīyate samaḥ || 15|| punardhamedatitarāṃ yathā kalko vilīyate | evaṃ velātrayaṃ3 dadyātkalkaṃ hemamṛtirbhavet || 16 || athānyaprakāramāha - pārāvatamalairlimpedathavā kukkuṭodbhavaiḥ | hemapatrāṇi teṣāṃ ca pradadyādantarāntaram ||17 || gandhacūrṇaṃ samaṃ dhṛtvā śarāvayugmasaṃpuṭe | pradadyātkukkuṭapuṭaṃ pañcabhirgomayopalaiḥ || 18 || evaṃ navapuṭāndadyāddaśamaṃ ca mahāpuṭam | triṃśadvanopalairdeyaṃ jāyate hemabhasmakam || 19 || (suvarṇaṃ4 ca bhavetsvādu tiktaṃ snigdhaṃ himaṃ guru | buddhividyāsmṛtikaraṃ viṣahāri rasāyanam || 20 ||
Āḍhamalla. aparamapi svarṇamāraṇamāha - kāñcana iti | kāñcane svarṇe gālite drāvite sati tatra nāgaṃ sīsakaṃ nikṣipet ṣoḍaśāṃśena kāñcanaparimāṇāt nāgaṃ grāhyam | amlaṃ mātuluṅgapratīkaṃ | adharottaramiti | golakasyopari cādhaśca gandhakaṃ deyam | saṃpuṭavidhānaṃ ca pūrvavat kāryam | kecidatra vanopalairviṃśatisaṃkhyakaireva manyante evamityanena prakāreṇa saptapuṭaiḥ kṛtvā hemabhasma bhavati ||7-5|| kāñcanāreti | kāñcanāro vṛkṣaviśeṣaḥ prasiddhaḥ | tasya patrāṇāṃ tvaco vā rasaḥ svarasaḥ, sūtakaḥ pāradaḥ gandhakaśca samānastayoḥ kajjalī kāryeti sambandhaḥ | hemapatrāṇi svarṇapatrāṇi samamātrayeti | tayā kajjalyā samayā svarṇapatraparimāṇayā śeṣaṃ vidhānaṃ pūrvavat | kokilairiti śuṣkagomayasaṃjñaiḥ aṅgārairvā yataḥ vahniṃ kharataraṃ kuryāditi grahaṇāt evamityamunā prakāreṇa puṭatrayamityatrāgnerboddhavyam | athavā kāñcanāraprakāreṇa lāṅgalīprakāramapi jānīyāt | lāṅgalī kalihārī, athavā jvālāmukhīprakāramapi kāñcanāraprakāravadboddhavyam| jvālāmukhī jayantī sā caturvidhā bhavati ‘śvetā raktā tathā pītā kṛṣṇā jvālāmukhī bhavet’ iti | etajjāticatuṣṭayaṃ śvetā raktā raktādikusumaiḥ kṛtvā jānīyāt || 10-13 | śileti | śilā manaḥśilā, sindūraḥ prasiddhaḥ, samayoḥ samānamānayoḥ, bhāvanāparimāṇaṃ ca yāvaddravyaṃ plutaṃ bhavati gālite pradrāvite hemni suvarṇe kalko'yamiti | ayaṃ kalkaḥ śilāsiṃdūrajaḥ | kalkagrahaṇena sāndro yojyaḥ | sama iti | svarṇamānasamaḥ kalka evamityamunā prakāreṇa velātrayaṃ trivāramityarthaḥ || 14-16 || pārāvateti | pārāvataḥ prasiddhaḥ malaṃ purīṣaṃ liṃpet iti hemapatrāṇītyarthaḥ | athavā kukkuṭodbhavairiti kukkuṭastāmracūḍaḥ tadudbhavairmalairiti sambandhaḥ, teṣāṃ svarṇapatrāṇāmantarāntarāntaraṃ yathā syāt tathā svarṇapatrasamaṃ gandhakacūrṇaṃ deyaṃ tāni ca śarāvayugmasaṃpuṭe dhṛtvā tatsaṃpuṭaṃ kukkuṭapuṭavidhānena pācyam, pañcabhirgomayopalairiti pañcasaṃkhyākaiḥ śuṣkagomayaiḥ evamityanena prakāreṇa navasaṃkhyākāni puṭāni dadyāt | daśamaṃ ca mahāpuṭamiti mahāpuṭaṃ triṃśadvanopalaiḥ kṛtvā deyam || 17-20 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. kāñcane svarṇe gālite nāgaṃ sīsakaṃ ṣoḍaśāṃśena nikṣipet | taccūrṇayitvā amlena ghṛṣṭvā golakaṃ kṛtvā samaṃ gandhakaṃ ūrdhvādhaḥ śarāvasaṃpuṭe pūrvavat || 7-9 || anyacca kāñcanārarasaiḥ samasūtakagandhayoḥ kajjalī hemapatrāṇi lepayet | samamātrayā svarṇasamamātrayā, spaṣṭamanyat || 10-13 || anyadapi svarṇamāraṇaṃ | śilā manaḥśilā sindūraṃ tayoḥ samayostulyayoścūrṇam arkadugdhaṃ dadyāt | dhamettato hemni dugdhakaiḥ samaiva bhāgato dadyāt dhamettato hemni gālite samaḥ hemasamaḥ kalkaḥ dīyeta tato dhamet | yathā kalko na tiṣṭhati evaṃ kṣāratrayaṃ dadyāt svarṇamṛtirbhavet || 14-16 || tathāca pārāvateti sugamam | anyacca granthāntarāt | daradaṃ hiṃgulaṃ svarṇatulyaṃ nāgaṃ sīsakaṃ mṛtanāgacūrṇaṃ vajrīkṣīreṇa marditam asya madhye suvarṇasya kalkaśca mriyate iti svarṇamāraṇam || 17-20 ||
idānīṃ raupyamāraṇaṃ prakaṭayannāha - bhāgaikaṃ tālakaṃ mardyaṃ jambenāmlena kenacit | tena bhāgatrayaṃ tārapatrāṇi parilepayet || 21 || dhṛtvā mūṣāpuṭe ruddhvā puṭet triṃśadvanopalaiḥ | samuddhṛtya punastālaṃ dattvā ruddhvā puṭe pacet || 22 || evaṃ caturdaśapuṭaistāraṃ bhasma prajāyate |
Āḍhamalla. bhāgaikamiti | tālakaṃ haritālaṃ bhāgaikaṃ tārapatrabhāgādityarthaḥ, jambena jambīrakarasena amlagrahaṇaṃ madhurajambīraniṣedhārthaṃ, kenacidityanena kenāpyamlena vā kutaḥ jambīrābhāvāt tena cukrādikamapi grāhyam, tena tālakena saha bhāgatrayamiti tārapatrāṇi raupyapatrāṇi pralepayet bhāgatrayaṃ tālakaparimāṇāt puṭavidhānaṃ pūrvavat triṃśadvanopalairityanena mahāpuṭaṃ darśitam | evamityuktapuṭavidhānena caturdaśapuṭakaṃ boddhavyam || 21 || 22 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. atha rajatotpattibhedānāha granthāntarāt - ‘tripurasya vadhārthāya nirnimeṣairvilocanaiḥ | nirīkṣayāmāsa śivaḥ krodhena paripūritaḥ || tatastūlkābhisamaye tasmāt kasmācca locanāt | tato rudraḥ samabhavadvaiśvānara iva jvalan || dvitīyasmāt paraṃ netrāddaśabindustu krāmukāt | tasmādrajatamudbhūtaṃ śukrakarmasu saṃsthitam’ iti | atha tadbhedāḥ - ‘sahajaṃ khanisaṃbhūtaṃ kṛtrimaṃ ca tridhā mataṃ | rajataṃ pūrvapūrvaṃ hi svaguṇairuttarottaraṃ | kṛtrimaṃ rasavādotthaṃ kiṃ vā rāmapadodbhavaṃ | ni:kalmaṣaṃ prabhāhīnaṃ dāhe cchede sitaṃ śubhaṃ | aśuddhaṃ sarvathā śodhyaṃ nāgaṃ dattvā vicakṣaṇaiḥ? iti | atha māraṇaṃ bhāgaikamiti | spaṣṭamanyat || 21 || 22 |
aparamapi tāramāraṇamāha - snuhīkṣīreṇa saṃpiṣṭaṃ mākṣikaṃ tena lepayet || 23 || tālakasya prakāreṇa tārapatrāṇi buddhimān | puṭeccaturdaśapuṭaistāraṃ bhasma prajāyate ||24 ||
Āḍhamalla. snuhīkṣīreṇeti | pūrvoktaharitālavidhānavanmākṣikavidhānena kṛtvā caturdaśapuṭāni yāvat bhavanti tāvat tāraṃ raupyaṃ bhasma syāt | mākṣikaṃ dhātumākṣikaṃ snuhīkṣīreṇeti snuhī sehuṇḍabhedaḥ || 23 || 24 ||
Kāśīrāma Vaidya. anyacca | snuhīkṣīreṇa sehuṇḍadugdhena saṃpiṣṭamākṣikaṃ svarṇamākṣikaṃ mūtrādau śuddhaṃ tālakasya prakāreṇa bhāgaikatārapatrāṇi vibhāgaṃ trilepayet punaḥ mūṣāsaṃpuṭena ruddhvā caturdaśapuṭaiḥ puṭet | tāraṃ bhasma prajāyate | granthāntare pāradena māraṇaṃ kṛtaṃ tadapi likhyate tathāca ‘vidhāya piṣṭaṃ sūtena rajatasyātha melayet| | tālaṃ gandhaṃ samaṃ paścānmardayennimbukadravaiḥ | dvitripuṭairbhavedbhasma yojyametadrasādiṣu’ iti | atha guṇāḥ ‘rūpyaṃ śītaṃ kaṣāyāmlaṃ svādupākarasaṃ saraṃ | vayasaḥ sthāpanaṃ snigdhaṃ lekhanaṃ vātapittajit | pramehādikarogāṃśca nāśayatyaciraṃ dhruvam || 23 || 24 ||
pītalohamāraṇamāha - arkakṣīreṇa saṃpiṣṭo gandhakastena lepayet | samenārasya patrāṇi śuddhānyamladravairmuhuḥ || 25 || tato mūṣāpuṭe dhṛtvā puṭedgajapuṭena ca | evaṃ puṭadvayenaiva bhasmāraṃ bhavati dhruvam || 26 || āravatkāṃsyamapyevaṃ bhasmatāṃ yāti niścitam | aparamapyāha - arkakṣīravadājaṃ5 syātkṣīranirguṇḍikā tathā || 27 || tāmrarītidhvanivadhe samagandhakayogataḥ |
