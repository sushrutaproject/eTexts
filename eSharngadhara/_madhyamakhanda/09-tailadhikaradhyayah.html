--- title: "9. atha tailādhikārādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 9 order: 9 ---
navamo'dhyāyaḥ | atha snehaparibhāṣā | kalkāccaturguṇīkṛtya ghṛtaṃ vā tailameva vā | caturguṇe drave sādhyaṃ tasya mātrā palonmitā ||1|| nikṣipya kvāthayettoyaṃ kvāthyadravyāccaturguṇam | pādaśiṣṭaṃ gṛhītvā ca snehaṃ tenaiva sādhayet ||2||
Dī. idānīṃ snehakalpanāṃ darśayannāha - kalkāditi | anukte kalkātkalkayogadravyāt ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ vā caturguṇaṃ grāhyaṃ, na tu dṛṣadi peṣitātkalkāt| dravamapi caturguṇaṃ snehaparimāṇāt dravaṃ toyādi | tasya mātrā palonmiteti | tasya saṃsiddhasnehasya pānārthaṃ palonmitā palapramāṇā mātrā jñeyā ityanukteṣu mānamidaṃ sāmānyaṃ | paribhāṣāyāmayamapi sāmānyasnehapākavidhau boddhavyaḥ, tathāhi - ‘kalkāccaturguṇaḥ snehaḥ snehātkvāthaścaturguṇaḥ | kvāthāccaturguṇaṃ vāri kvāthaḥ kvāthyasamo mataḥ’ iti | viśeṣo'pi kvacidvartate yathā tantrāntare vāsāghṛte balādye ghṛte vā tadyathā -“atrāpyaṣṭaguṇaistoyaiḥ kvathitaṃ kvāthadugdhayoḥ | pratyekaṃ dviguṇo bhāgo bhiṣak sarpiṣi nikṣipet” iti || 1 || 2||
Gū. atha sneheti | tatra tāvat prathamataḥ paribhāṣāmasya vyācaṣṭe | atha ghṛtādhikāraḥ | tatrādau ghṛtatailādisādhanamāha - tatkalketyādi | kalkāt dravyāt ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ caturguṇe dravye dugdhādau sādhyam agnidvārā kaṭāhādau paktvā siddhaṃ bhavati | tasya mātrā vātādirogaviṣaye mānamātrā palonmitā palapramāṇā | idānīṃ snehasādhane kvāthavidhānamāha - nikṣipyetyādi | kvāthadravyāccaturguṇaṃ toyaṃ jalaṃ nikṣipya kvāthayet| punaḥ pādaśiṣṭaṃ ca caturthāṃśāvaśeṣaṃ gṛhītvā'nena kvāthena snehaṃ ghṛtaṃ tailaṃ vā sādhayet | kvāthaṃ kvāthayogyaṃ kuṭṭitaṃ yaddravyaṃ daśamūlādi tasmādisyarthaḥ || 1 || 2 ||
mṛdudravyādau kvathane jalapramāṇamāha - caturguṇaṃ mṛdudravye kaṭhine'ṣṭaguṇaṃ jalam | tathāca madhyame dravye dadyādaṣṭaguṇaṃ payaḥ || 3 || atyantakaṭhine dravye nīraṃ ṣoḍaśikaṃ matam |
Dī. idānīṃ dravyaviśeṣe kvāthanimittaṃ jalamānamāha - caturguṇamiti | atra caturguṇaprabhṛtikaṃ dravyaparimāṇāddhoddhavyam | yāvatparimāṇaṃ kvāthyaṃ dravyaṃ bhavati tāvatparimāṇāccaturguṇitaṃ jalaṃ grāhyamityarthaḥ | mṛdu kaṭhinaṃ kaṭhinataraṃ ca dravyaṃ tatraivānumānato'vagantavyam | tathāca madhyame dravye dadyādaṣṭaguṇaṃ paya iti | madhyame mṛdvādisaṃbhāre payaḥ pānīyaṃ yatra mṛdukaṭhinataradravyamekatra militvā kvāthyaṃ tatrāṣṭaguṇaṃ pānīyaṃ datvā pādaśeṣaṃ grāhyamiti sarvatra | tathāhi - ‘mṛdvādikvāthyasaṃghāte mānānukte cikitsakāḥ | madhyasyobhayabhāgitvādicchantyaṣṭaguṇaṃ jalam’ iti || 3 ||
Gū. mṛdudravye guḍūcyādau caturguṇaṃ jalaṃ, kaṭhine daśamūlādau aṣṭaguṇaṃ, tathāca madhyamadravye āragvadhādau aṣṭaguṇaṃ payaḥ | payo'tra jalaṃ | atyantakaṭhine padmakādau ṣoḍaśikaṃ ṣoḍaśaguṇaṃ mataṃ kathitaṃ caturthāṃśaṃ gṛhyate || 3 ||
atha matāntare parimāṇaviśeṣamāha - karṣāditaḥ palaṃ yāvatkṣipetṣoḍaśikaṃ jalam || 4 || tadūrdhvaṃ kuḍavaṃ yāvadbhavedaṣṭaguṇaṃ payaḥ | prasthāditaḥ kṣipennīraṃ khārī yāvaccaturguṇam || 5 || ambukvātharasairyatra pṛthaksnehasya sādhanam | kalkasyāṃśaṃ tatra dadyāccaturthaṃ ṣaṣṭhamaṣṭamam || 6 || dugdhe dadhni rase nakre kalko deyo'ṣṭamāṃśakaḥ | kalkasya samyakpākārthaṃ toyamatra caturguṇam || 7 ||
Dī. karṣādita iti | karṣādita ityādiparimāṇaṃ sāmānyadravyaviṣaye vartate anyathā svatantravirodhaḥ, yāva- tkvāthyadravyaṃ karṣādiparimāṇātpalaṃ bhavati tāvattatra ṣoḍaśāṃśaṃ jalaṃ datvā kvāthaṃ pādaśeṣaṃ grāhyaṃ | ṣoḍaśikamatra kvāthyadravyaparimāṇāt, tatastu palādupari yāvatkuḍavaṃ kvāthyadravyaparimāṇaṃ bhavati tāvadatra toyaṃ pānīyamaṣṭaguṇaṃ bhavet, kvāthyadravyādityarthaḥ | atra kvāthaṃ caturthāṃśaṃ grāhyamiti sarvatra | prasthādita iti | prasthādiparimāṇāt yāvat khārī bhavati tāvadatra caturguṇaṃ nīraṃ pānīyaṃ prakṣipya saṃkvāthya ca pādaśeṣaṃ grāhyamiti tātparyārthaḥ | ādiśabdāt āḍhakadroṇaśūrpadroṇīprabhṛtayo grāhyāḥ | yadvā prasthasyādiḥ prasthādistasmāt tena śarāvamapi gṛhyate, śarāvādita ityarthaḥ || 4 || 5 ||aparamapyanuktamāha - ambukvātheti | yatra snehapāke pānīyāditrayāṇāṃ dravyāṇāṃ madhye yadā ekena pākaḥ syāt tadā kalkasya kalkadravyasya caturthāṃśaṃ ṣaṣṭhāṃśam aṣṭamāṃśaṃ yathā'nukrameṇa deyam | anukramo yathā - yadi ambunā kevalena pākastarhi kalkadravyasya caturthāśaṃ deyam, snehaparimāṇādityarthaḥ | yadā kevalena kvāthenaiva pākastadā kalkadravyasya ṣaṣṭhāṃśaṃ deyam, atrāpi snehaparimāṇādityarthaḥ | yatra tu kevalasvarasenaiva sādhyate srehaḥ tatra kalkadravyasyāṣṭamāṃśaṃ deyam | snehamānādityatrāpi sambandhaḥ ityanukramaḥ | aparamapi - dugdhe ityatrāpi ekadravyasādhyatā sūcyate tadā kalkadravyaṃ snehaparimāṇādaṣṭamāṃśaṃ deyamiti bhāvaḥ | kalkasyeti | yatra tu anyadravaiḥ kevalakalkenaiva sādhyate snehastadā caturguṇaṃ kalkadravyaṃ caturguṇaṃ toyaṃ nikṣipediti sarvatra | samyak pākārthaṃ boddhavyam | etena kimuktaṃ yadā kṣīrādibhiḥ pratyekena svakalkena pākastadā caturguṇaṃ caturthāṃśaṃ pānīyaṃ deyam | pānīyaṃ vinā pāko nāstīti sarvamatam || 6 || 7 ||
Gū. karṣādārabhya khārīparyantaṃ kvāthyadravye jalamānamāha - karṣāditaḥ yāvatpalaṃ pramāṇaṃ tāvat ṣoḍaśikaṃ ṣoḍaśaguṇaṃ jalaṃ kṣipet| tadūrdhvaṃ palādūrdhvaṃ yāvatkuḍavaṃ kuḍavapramāṇaṃ tāvadaṣṭaguṇaṃ payo jalaṃ bhavet | prasthāditaḥ yāvat khārīpramāṇaṃ tāvaccaturguṇaṃ jalaṃ kṣipet | pūrvaṃ caturguṇādijalaṃ yallikhitaṃ tanmṛdudravyādiviṣayaṃ jñeyaṃ | karṣādita idaṃ tu mānaviṣayam ataḥ kecinmṛdvādito gṛhṇanti kecinmānato gṛhṇanti || 4 || 5|| ambvādibhiryatra snehamānaṃ tatra kalkapramāṇamāha - ‘tatrāmbupiṣṭamuktaṃ syājjalaṃ cātra caturguṇam’, yatra bhinnaṃ sādhanaṃ bhavati, tatra kalkasyāṃśaṃ caturguṇaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ kramāt ambupāke caturthāṃśaṃ raso māṃsarasastatpāke aṣṭamāṃśam | ukto'pyambvādau pāke dugdhādau aṣṭamāṃśa eva kalkapāka ityāha - kalkasya samyagiti | dugdhe tathā dadhni tathā takre pākānantaraṃ kalkaḥ aṣṭamāṃśakādayaḥ raso māṃsarasaḥ | yadvā raso dhattūrarasaḥ māṃsarasasya pūrvoktatvāt kalkasya samyak pākārthaṃ caturguṇaṃ snehādityarthaḥ || 6 || 7 ||
dravāṇi yatra sneheṣu pañcādīni bhavanti hi | tatra snehasamānyāhuryathāpūrvaṃ caturguṇam || 8 ||
Dī. idānīmanuktadravāṇāṃ mānamāha - davāṇīti | yatra snehapakvadravāṇi kṣīratakrarasamūtrakvāthāni anyānyapi pañcādīni pañcaprabhṛtīni bhavanti anuktamānānīti viṣayaḥ | tatra tāni dravāṇi snehasamāni pratyekamāhurācāryāḥ | yathā vakṣyamāṇatriphalāghṛte arvāk dravaṃ catvārastrayo vā tarhi snehaparimāṇāccaturguṇaṃ jñeyaṃ pratyekamityarthaḥ | eke yathāpūrvaṃ caturguṇamiti paṭhanti tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ | kutracit dravyāṇīti pāṭho dṛśyate so'saṅgata eva || 8 ||
Gū. dravyāṇīti | yatra pañcādhikāni dravyāṇi tatra sneheṣu kiyatparimitaṃ dravyaṃ deyaṃ tadāha - sneheṣu ghṛtatailādiṣu dravāṇi dugdhādīni yatra pañcaprabhṛtīni bhavanti tatra snehasamānaṃ bhavati na caturguṇānīti yāvat | yathāpūrvaṃ caturguṇamityetasyāyamartha: | eko dravastatra pākena cāturguṇyaṃ, dvau dravau yatra tatra dvābhyāṃ cāturguṇyaṃ yaduktaṃ jaijjaṭe ‘ekadvitridravairdravyaiḥ kuryāt snehāccaturguṇaṃ | kṣīraṃ snehasamaṃ dadyāt caturbhiśca caturguṇam, iti | asyāyamarthaḥ yatraiko dravastatraikena cāturguṇyaṃ, evaṃ dvābhyāṃ, tribhirmilitvā cāturguṇyamityarthaḥ | yatra pañcādhikāni bhavanti tatra snehasamānīti pūrvārthaḥ| ‘tailasyārdhapramāṇena dadyādikṣurasaṃ budhaḥ | tathā māṃsarasaṃ cārdhaṃ datvā snehaṃ vipācayet’ snehasamānyāhurityasya truṭimāhurityarthaḥ ityanye | tadyathā - sakṛnmāṃsauṣadhirasaṃ mūtrasauvīrakādikaṃ | snehatulyaṃ dadhi kṣīraṃ jalaṃ caiva caturguṇam || granthajñānārthamedvyavākhyāprasaṅgenoktam ||8||
kevalakvāthapāke vidhānamāha - dravyeṇa kevalenaiva snehapāko bhavedyadi | tatrāmbupiṣṭaḥ kalkaḥ syājjalaṃ cātra caturguṇam || 9|| kvāthena kevalenaiva pāko yatreritaḥ kvacit | kvāthyadravyasya kalko'pi tatra snehe prayujyate ||10||
Dī. atha kevaladravyasya snehapākārthaṃ mānamāha - dravyeṇeti | atra dravyaṃ snehāccaturthāṃśaṃ kalkārthaṃ vāri tāvaddeyaṃ yāvat kalkaḥ syāt | yataḥ peṣaṇe jalasya parimāṇaṃ na syāt | jalaṃ cātra caturguṇamiti | atra kalke jalaṃ pānīyam | caturguṇaṃ deyaṃ snehamānādityarthaḥ | eke dravyamānāccaturguṇaṃ jalamiti vyākhyānayanti tanna manoharaṃ kalkadravyālpatvāt | aparamapyāha - kvātheneti | yatra snehe kvaciditi anukte kevalena kvāthena kṛtvā pākaḥ kathitaḥ tatra snehe kvāthyadravyasya kalka: kāryaḥ, kalkārthaṃ cātra caturguṇaṃ jalamiti tena yenaiva dravyeṇa kvāthaḥ kriyate tainaiva tatparimitena ca dravyeṇāyamapi kṛtvā'tra caturguṇena ca jalena saha sādhyaḥ | pūrvaṃ kvāthena sādhyaḥ paścātkalkenetyarthaḥ ||9||10||
Gū. kevalena kvāthena yadi snehapāko bhavet tatrāmbupiṣṭaḥ kalko jalaṃ caturguṇakvātheneti | idaṃ padyaṃ pūrvaślokasyaikārthatvāt prakṣiptaṃ jñeyaṃ || 9 || 10 ||
kalkahīnastu yaḥ srehaḥ sa sādhyaḥ kevale drave |
Dī. kalkahīna iti | yaḥ sneho ghṛtaṃ tailādikaḥ kalkahīnaḥ kalkarahito bhavet saḥ kevale drave sādhyaḥ| dravamatra caturguṇaṃ snehaparimāṇāt ||
Gū. atha kevaladravasādhanamāha - kalkahīne snehe sādhanamāha - yaḥ kalkahīnaḥ snehaḥ sa kevale drave sādhyaḥ|
puṣpakalkastu yaḥ snehastatra toyaṃ caturguṇam || 11 || snehe snehāṣṭamāṃśaśca puṣpakalkaḥ prayujyate |
Dī. idānīṃ puṣpakalkasya parimāṇamāha puṣpakalka iti | yaḥ puṣpakalkasaṃjñaḥ snehaḥ tatra snehe toyaṃ pānīyaṃ caturguṇaṃ deyaṃ caturguṇatā ca snehāt snehaparimāṇādityarthaḥ | saḥ puṣpakalkaḥ snehāṣṭamāṃśo yojyata ityarthaḥ | snehasyāṣṭamāṃśaṃ tena sāmyo yojya ityarthaḥ | puṣpāṇi yathā nāgakeśarakuṅkumalavaṅgadamanakasurapuṣpacampakotpalapuṇḍarīkaketakījātī-kusumbhaprabhṛtīni | eke puṣpakalkaḥ prayujyata ityasya sthāne'tra kalkaṃ prayujyate iti paṭhanti vyākhyānayanti tat patrakalkaṃ prakṣepamāhuḥ, tathāhi - ‘gandhaniryāsapuṣpāṇāṃ siddhaśīte'vatārite | prakṣepo gandhavṛddhyarthaṃ patrakalkaṃ tu tadviduḥ’ || 11 ||
Gū. puṣpakalkai: snehasādhanamāha - puṣpeti | yasminsnehe puṣpakalko dīyate sa puṣpakalkaḥ snehaḥ, tatra toyaṃ jalaṃ caturguṇaṃ snehātpuṣpakalkaḥ kiyatparimito dīyate ityatrāha - snehāṣṭamāṃśaśca puṣpakalkaḥ prayujyate iti || 11 ||
vartivatsnehakalkaḥ syādyadāṅgulyā vimarditaḥ ||12|| śabdahīno'gninikṣiptaḥ snehaḥ siddho bhavettadā| yadā phenodgamastaile phenaśāntiśca sarpiṣi ||13|| gandhavarṇarasotpattiḥ snehasiddhistadā bhavet | snehapākastridhā prokto mṛdurmadhyaḥ kharastathā ||14|| īṣatsarasakalkastu snehapāko mṛdurbhavet | madhyapākasya siddhiśca kalke nīrasakomalaḥ ||15|| īṣatkaṭhinakalkaśca snehapāko bhavetkharaḥ | tadūrdhvaṃ dagdhapākaḥ syāddāhakṛnniṣprayojanaḥ || 16 ||
Dī. idānīṃ snehānāṃ saṃsiddhilakṣaṇānyāha - vartivaditi | yadā snehe paripācitaḥ kalkaḥ aṅgulyā vimardito vartisadṛśo bhavati tadā siddho jñeyaḥ | athavā sa eva kalkaḥ yadā'gnau nikṣiptaḥ śabdarahito bhavati tadā siddho jñātavyaḥ iti sāmānyam | aparamapi viśeṣamāha - yadā snehasya pākānte taile phenodgamo bhavati sarpiṣi ca phenaśāntirbhavati tadā siddhalakṣaṇaṃ boddhavyaṃ | varṇagandharasotpattirapi snehasiddhiḥ syāt varṇādikamapi taile sarpiṣi ca jñātavyam | adhunā trividhasnehapākasya lakṣaṇamāha - īṣatsarasakalka iti | yadā kalke sāndratā kiñcidbhavati agnau nikṣipte sati caṭacaṭāśabdo bhavati tadā mṛdupāko jñeyaḥ, yadā nīraso kalko bhavati komalaśca nātikaṭhino nātimṛduriti tadā madhyapākaḥ, yadā kiñcit kaṭhinakalko jātaḥ nīrasaśca bhavati tadā kharapāka iti | eteṣāṃ parīkṣaṇamanumānato'vagantavyaṃ gurusakāśadarśanādvā | tadūrdhvaṃ kharapākādūrdhvaṃ yadā snehakalkaḥ kaṭhinataro bhavati tadā dagdho jñeyaḥ, sa ca sarvakarmasu niṣphalaḥ syāt dāhakaraśca bhavati ||12-16||
Gū. idānīṃ snehasiddhalakṣaṇamāha - yadā snehakalkaḥ aṅgulyā vartitaḥ vartīkṛtaḥ vartivadbhavet agninikṣiptaḥ śabdahīnaśca bhavet tadā siddhaṃ vadet | yadā varṇagandharasotpattistadāpi snehasiddhirbhavet| iti ca lakṣaṇaṃ varṇastailapakvavarṇo raktādiḥ, gandhaḥ sugandhatvaṃ, rasotpattirmadhurādirasotpattiḥ, tadā svedanāllakṣyate | snehapākasya trividhatvamāha - eko mṛdu:, eko madhyaḥ, ekaḥ kharaḥ; evaṃ tridhā snehapākaḥ | teṣāṃ lakṣaṇamāha - īṣatsarasakalkaḥ īṣajjalakalkaḥ mṛdusnehapākaḥ nīrasakomale kalke siddhaḥ pāko madhyapākaḥ nīrasakomale jalasahite ārdre īṣatkaṭhinakalkaḥ snehapākaḥ kharaḥ tadūrdhvaṃ dagdhapāko dāhakṛnniṣprayojanaḥ, na kāryakārī ||12||16||
