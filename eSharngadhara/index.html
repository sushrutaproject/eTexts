e-Śārṅgadhara

A reading edition of the Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā, in IAST

The Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā of Śārṅgadhara, a Sanskrit treatise on medicine and pharmacy, is organized into three khaṇḍas (sections). It is read here alongside two commentaries: Āḍhamalla's Dīpikā and Kāśīrāma's Gūḍhārthadīpikā. All three are distinguished typographically. Choose a khaṇḍa below to browse its adhyāyas (chapters).

The source of the etext is the 1931 edition of Paraśurāma Śāstrī.
