aparamapi cūrṇāñjanamāha- śītetyādi| śītaṃ rasañjanaṃ, karpūramanye| sauvīrakaṃ sauvīrāñjanam| rasabhāvitamiti catuṣpadamṛgapakṣimāṃsarasabhāvitamityarthaḥ| kecidrasa iti sāmānyoktyā rasāñjanasauvīrāñjanayoreva rasaṃ saṅgṛhṇanti, tadabhāve ca tayoreva cūrṇāḍhakamudakāḍhake prakṣipya ahorātraparyuṣitaṃ prātarmarditaṃ 3 pūtaṃ svarasavat prayojyamityarthaḥ| kecicca prastutatvādrasaśabdena gośakṛdrasaṃ gṛhṇanti||13-14||
