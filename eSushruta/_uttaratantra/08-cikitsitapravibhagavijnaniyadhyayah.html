--- title: "8. cikitsitapravibhāgavijñānīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 8 order: 8 ---

athātaścikitsitapravibhāgavijñānīyamadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ (suśrutāya) ||2||

dṛṣṭigatarogavijñānīyānantaraṃ sarvathā pratipannasakalanayanāmayasya bhiṣajaścikitsā nyāyyeti cikitsāpravibhāgīyārambhaḥ- athāta ityādi| pravibhāgaḥ prakarṣeṇa vibhajanaṃ pṛthakkaraṇamityarthaḥ||1-2||

ṣaṭsaptatirye'bhihitā vyādhayo nāmalakṣaṇaiḥ |
cikitsitamidaṃ teṣāṃ samāsavyāsataḥ [2] śṛṇu ||3||

cikitsā tu dvividhā samāsād vyāsācca; tatra samāsenehādhyāye vibhāgaṃ darśayannāha- ṣaḍityādi| lakṣaṇaiḥ hetupūrvarūparupopaśayasamprāptibhiḥ| samāsāt saṅkṣepādihaivādhyāye; vistarāt punarvātābhiṣyandādiṣu||3||

chedyāsteṣu daśaikaśca nava lekhyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ |
bhedyāḥ pañca vikārāḥ syurvyadhyāḥ pañcadaśaiva tu ||4||
dvādaśā'śastrakṛtyāśca yāpyāḥ sapta bhavanti hi |
rogā varjayitavyāḥ syurdaśa pañca ca jānatā |
asādhyau vā bhavetāṃ tu yāpyau cāgantusañjñitau ||5||

tatrāṣṭavidhaśastrakarmaṇāṃ madhye netrāmayeṣu caturvidhaṃ karma prayojyam, ataścaturvidhaśastrakarmārhān vyādhīn samāsārthaṃ vargīkṛtya prāha- chedyā ityādi| dvādaśā'śastrakṛtyāśceti cakārād dvau bāhyajāvaśastrakṛtyau| yāpyā iti snehādikriyayā yāpyatvaṃ, na tu chedyādikriyayā| yāpyasādhyatve ca dvayoravasthāvaśāt| varjayitavyā asādhyā ityarthaḥ||4-5||

arśo'nvitaṃ bhavati vartma tu yattathā'rśaḥ śuṣkaṃ tathā'rbudamatho piḍakāḥ sirājāḥ |
jālaṃ sirājamapi pañcavidhaṃ tathā'rma chedyā bhavanti saha parvaṇikāmayena ||6||

tāneva chedyān rogānnāmabhiḥ prāha- arśo'nvitamityādi| pañcavidhamarmeti prastāryarma, śuṣkārma, raktārma, adhimāṃsārma, snāyvarmetyevaṃ pañcavidham||6||

utsāṅginī bahalakardamavartmanī ca śyāvaṃ ca yacca paṭhitaṃ tviha baddhavartma |
kliṣṭaṃ ca pothakiyutaṃ khalu yacca vartma kumbhīkinī ca saha śarkarayā ca lekhyāḥ ||7||

lekhyānnāmabhiḥ prāha- utsaṅginītyādi| bahalakardamavartmanītyatra vartmeti padaṃ bahalakardamābhyāṃ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate||7||

śleṣmopanāhalagaṇau ca bisaṃ ca bhedyā granthiśca yaḥ kṛmikṛto'ñjananāmikā ca |8|

bhedyānnāmabhiḥ prāha- śleṣmetyādi| bisaṃ bisavartma|8|-

ādau sirā nigaditāstu yayoḥ prayoge pākau ca yau nayanayoḥ pavano'nyataśca ||8||
pūyālasānilaviparyayamanthasañjñāḥ syandāstu yāntyupaśamaṃ hi sirāvyadhena |9|

vyadhyānnāmabhiḥ prāha- ādāvityādi| ādau sirā nigaditāḥ ityādi yayorvikārayoḥ prayoge ādau sirā nigaditāstau vyadhyau, kau tau? sirotpātasirāharṣau| pavano'nyataḥ anyatovātaḥ| manthasañjñā adhimanthāścatvāraḥ; syandā abhiṣyandāstāvanta eva||8||-

śuṣkākṣipākakaphapittavidagdhadṛṣṭiṣvamlākhyaśukrasahitārjunapiṣṭakeṣu ||9||
aklinnavartmahutabhugdhvajadarśiśuktipraklinnavartmasu tathaiva balāsasañjñe |
āgantunā''mayayugena ca dūṣitāyāṃ dṛṣṭau na śastrapatanaṃ pravadanti tajjñāḥ ||10||

aśastrakṛtyānāha- śuṣketyādi| hutabhuk dhvajadarśī, dhūmadarśītyarthaḥ||9-10||

sampaśyataḥ ṣaḍapi ye'bhihitāstu kācāste pakṣmakopasahitāstu bhavanti yāpyāḥ |11|

yāpyānāha- sampaśyata ityādi| ṣaḍapi ye kācā abhihitāste sampaśyataḥ puruṣasya yāpyā bhavantīti sambandhaḥ|11|-

catvāra eva pavanaprabhavāstvasādhyā dvau pittajau kaphanimittaja eka eva |
aṣṭārdhakā rudhirajāśca gadāstridoṣāstāvanta eva gaditāvapi bāhyajau dvau ||11||

asādhyānāha- catvāra ityādi| catvāra eveti hatādhimanthanimiṣagambhīradṛṣṭivātahatavartmākhyāścatvāraḥ| dvāviti hrasvajāḍya(tya)jaladhisrāvākhyau| eka eva kaphasrāvākhyaḥ| aṣṭārdhakā iti catvāra ityarthaḥ| ke te catvāraḥ? raktasrāvājakājātaśoṇitārśovraṇānvitaśukrākhyāḥ| tāvanta eveti catvāra evetyarthaḥ| ke te catvāraḥ? pūyāsrāvanakulāndhyākṣipākātyālajyākhyāḥ| bāhyajau āgantujau||11||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre
cikitsitapravibhāgavijñānīyo nāmāṣṭamo'dhyāyaḥ ||8||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre'ṣṭamo'dhyāyaḥ||8||

1. `cikitsitapravibhāgīyaṃ’ iti pā.|
2. `samāsādvyāsataḥ\\’ iti pā.|
