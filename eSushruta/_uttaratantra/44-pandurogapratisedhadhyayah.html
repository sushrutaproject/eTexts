--- title: "44. pāṇḍurogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 44 order: 44 ---
athātaḥ pāṇḍurogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
hṛdrogapratiṣedhānantaraṃ saṅkhyāsāmyāt tīkṣṇāmlakaṭukādihṛdrogacikitsitasya hetutvāt pāṇḍurogapratiṣedhārambho yukta ityāha- athāta ityādi| atrādiśabdadvayaṃ luptaṃ draṣṭavyaṃ, tena pāṇḍvādirogādipratiṣedhamiti| tatra prathamenādiśabdena kṛṣṇapītamiśrā varṇā grāhyāḥ, kāmalādayo dvitīyena; apare tu pāṇḍuvarṇādhikyāt sarve eva pāṇḍurogāḥ procyanta iti matvā ekamevādiśabdaṃ luptaṃ varṇayanti; te ca ādiśabdāt kāmalādīn gṛhṇanti| apare tvādiśabdaṃ vinā'pi kāmalādīn gṛhṇanti, pāṇḍurogāntarbhāvātteṣāmiti||1-2||
vyavāyamamlaṃ lavaṇāni madyaṃ mṛdaṃ divāsvapnamatīva tīkṣṇam | niṣevamāṇasya vidūṣya raktaṃ kurvanti doṣāstvaci pāṇḍubhāvam ||3||
pāṇḍurogasya hetusamprāptī āha- vyavāyamamlamityādi| vyavāyādīni niṣevamāṇasya puruṣasya duṣṭā doṣā raktaṃ vidūṣya tvaci pāṇḍubhāvaṃ kurvantīti piṇḍārthaḥ| vyavāyaḥ strīsevā, sa ca sarvadoṣaprakopakaḥ, anye tu vātapittaprakopakatvaṃ vyavāyasya manyante bhāraharaṇādau krodhādau ca tatpāṭhāt| amlalavaṇayoḥ pittakaphakaratvam| mṛttikā sarvadoṣaprakopaṇī, nānāvidhatvāt; tathā coktam,- “kaṣāyā mārutaṃ, pittamūṣarā, madhurā kapham| kopayenmṛdrasādīṃśca raukṣyādbhuktaṃ virūkṣayet”- iti| divāsvapnaḥ tridoṣaprakopakaḥ| tīkṣṇaṃ pittakopi| evaṃ yathāsvaṃ yathāsambhavaṃ heturuktaḥ| vidūṣya raktamityādikā samprāptiḥ| atīva tīkṣṇamityatra ādiśabdo lupto draṣṭavyaḥ; tena piṣṭapayomāṣatilādīnāṃ grahaṇam| kecit ‘mṛnmadyamatsyāmiṣatīkṣṇapiṣṭapayodivāsvapnatilekṣumāṣān| niṣevamāṇasya’- iti paṭhanti, śeṣaṃ samam| te'pi luptādiśabdāt vyavāyādīni gṛhṇanti| apare ‘atīva tīkṣṇam’ ityatra ‘atīvarūkṣam’ iti paṭhanti||3||
pāṇḍvāmayo'ṣṭārdhavidhaḥ pradiṣṭaḥ pṛthaksamastairyugapacca doṣaiḥ |4|
saṅkhyāmāha- pāṇḍvityādi| aṣṭārdhavidhiḥ caturvidhaḥ; kathaṃ caturvidha ityāha- pṛthagityādi| pṛthagdoṣaiḥ trayaḥ, samastaiḥ ekaḥ| yugapaditi padaṃ doṣatrayasyaikakālaprakopasūcanārtham| yadyapi tantrāntare mṛjjaḥ pāṇḍurogaḥ pañcamaḥ kathitaḥ, tathā'pyatra mṛjjastridoṣaja evāntarbhūtaḥ, vakṣyamāṇāśca kāmalādayaḥ pāṇḍurogasyaiva paryāyāḥ; ato'tra catvāra eva| kecit pāṇḍvāmayastvaṣṭavidha iti paṭhanti| tanmate pṛthagdoṣaiḥ trayaḥ, samastaiḥ ekaḥ, mṛdbhakṣaṇāt ekaḥ, dve kāmale ca, eko halīmakaḥ, evaṃ pāṇḍurogo'ṣṭavidhaḥ| tathā ca tantrāntaram- “vātena pittena kaphena cāpi tridoṣamṛdbhakṣaṇasambhavaḥ syāt| dve kāmale caikahalīmakaśca sa cāṣṭadhaivaṃ tviha pāṇḍurogaḥ”- iti|4|-
sarveṣu caiteṣviha pāṇḍubhāvo yato'dhiko'taḥ khalu pāṇḍurogaḥ ||4||
yadyapi vātādibhedena kṛṣṇādivarṇatvamabhidhāsyati, tathā'pyasya vikārasya pāṇḍuvarṇādhikyāt pāṇḍurogavyapadeśa iti darśayannāha- sarveṣu caiteṣviha pāṇḍubhāva ityādi||4||
tvaksphoṭanaṃ ṣṭhīvanagātrasādau mṛdbhakṣaṇaṃ prekṣaṇakūṭaśothaḥ | viṇmūtrapītatvamathāvipāko bhaviṣyatastasya puraḥsarāṇi ||5||
idānīṃ pāṇḍurogapūrvarūpāṇyāha- tvagityādi| tvaksphoṭaḥ tvacāṃ sphuṭanam| ṣṭhīvanaṃ thūtkaraṇam| yadyapyatra mṛdbhakṣaṇaṃ kathitaṃ, tathā'pi mṛdbhakṣaṇe icchā gamyate| prekṣaṇam akṣi| avipāka āhārasya| puraḥsarāṇi agresarāṇi||5||
sa kāmalāpānakipāṇḍurogaḥ kumbhāhvayo lāghara(va)ko'lasākhyaḥ | vibhāṣyate lakṣaṇamasya kṛtsnaṃ nibodha vakṣyāmyanupūrvaśastat 1||6||
tasyaiva pāṇḍurogasya paryāyānāha- sa ityādi| sa rogaḥ kāmalādikābhiḥ sañjñābhirvibhāṣyate vividhaṃ bhāṣyate| asyeti kāmalādiparyāyairnirdiṣṭasya rogasya| anupūrvaśaḥ pūrvaṃ pūrvamanupūrveṇetyarthaḥ| taṃ pāṇḍurogam||6||
kṛṣṇekṣaṇaṃ kṛṣṇasirāvanaddhaṃ tadvarṇaviṇmūtranakhānanaṃ ca | vātena pāṇḍuṃ manujaṃ vyavasyedyuktaṃ tathā'nyaistadupadravaiśca ||7||
vātikapāṇḍurogalakṣaṇamāha- kṛṣṇekṣaṇamityādi| kṛṣṇekṣaṇaṃ kṛṣṇākṣikam| tadvarṇaviṇmūtranakhānanaṃ kṛṣṇavarṇaviṇmūtranakhamityarthaḥ| tadupadravaiḥ vātopadravaiḥ||7||
pītekṣaṇaṃ pītasirāvanaddhaṃ tadvarṇaviṇmūtranakhānanaṃ ca | pittena pāṇḍuṃ manujaṃ vyavasyedyuktaṃ tathā'nyaistadupadravaiśca ||8||
paittikapāṇḍurogalakṣaṇamāha- pītetyādi| tadvarṇaṃ pītavarṇam| tadupadravaiḥ pittopadravairityarthaḥ||8||
śuklekṣaṇaṃ śuklasirāvanaddhaṃ tadvarṇaviṇmūtranakhānanaṃ ca | kaphena pāṇḍuṃ manujaṃ vyavasyedyuktaṃ tathā'nyaistadupadravaiśca ||9||
ślaiṣmikapāṇḍurogalakṣaṇamāha- śukletyādi| tadvarṇaṃ śuklavarṇaṃ, tadupadravaiḥ kaphopadravaiḥ||9||
sarvātmake sarvamidaṃ vyavasyed... |10|
sānnipātikapāṇḍurogalakṣaṇamāha- sarvātmake sarvamidamityādi| sarvātmake sānnipātike pāṇḍuroge, sarvaṃ samastam, idaṃ vātādija pāṇḍurogalakṣaṇaṃ, vyavasyet jānīyāt|10|-
... vakṣyāmi liṅgānyatha kāmalāyāḥ | yo hyāmayānte sahasā'nnamamlamadyādapathyāni ca tasya pittam ||10|| karoti pāṇḍuṃ vadanaṃ viśeṣāt pūrveritau 2 tandribalakṣayau ca |11|
idānīṃ pāṇḍurogaparyāyabhūtāyā api kāmalāyā lakṣaṇamāha- vakṣyāmītyādi| nanu, yadi pāṇḍurogasya paryāyaḥ kāmalā, tat kathaṃ tasya lakṣaṇānyuktāni? satyaṃ, yathā pāṇḍurogāṇāṃ vātādijānāṃ pratisvaṃ bhedo vidyate tathā'syāpīti na doṣaḥ; etena kāmalādīnāṃ sañjñānāṃ viśiṣṭāvasthāviṣayatvādviśiṣṭatvaṃ, paryāyatvaṃ pāṇḍurogatvāparityāgāt| tānyeva liṅgānyāha- ya ityādi| ‘vakṣyāmi liṅgānyatha kāmalāyā’ ityabhidhāya kāmalākāraṇavacanaṃ bhaviṣyantyā api tasyāḥ parijñānārtham| āmayānte pāṇḍurogānte anyarogānte ca| sahasā śīghram| annamamlam amlabhojanam| pāṇḍumityatra dehamiti śeṣaḥ| ‘pūrveritau tandribalakṣayau ca’ iti śārīrasthāne garbhavyākaraṇādhyāye tandrā kathitā, sūtrasthāne doṣādikṣayavṛddhyadhyāye balakṣaya ityarthaḥ| nanu, tandrāśabdasya vyutpāditatvāt kathaṃ tandribalakṣayāviti? satyaṃ, kṛṣyāderākṛtigaṇatvenekpratyayāntatvena sādhu| anye ‘tandrā'balatvaṃ prathamoditaṃ ca’ iti paṭhanti; tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ| apare 'prathamoditāṃśca' iti paṭhanti; te ca pāṇḍurogoktaliṅgaviśeṣān gṛhṇanti| ‘pāṇḍuvadanam’ ityatra ‘pītavadanam’ iti kecit paṭhanti, taṃ cānārṣaṃ ṭīkākārā bhāṣante||10||-
bhedastu tasyāḥ khalu kumbhasāhvaḥ śopho mahāṃstatra ca parvabhedaḥ ||11||
idānīṃ kāmalābhedamāha- bheda ityādi| tatreti kumbhasāhve| parvabhedaḥ sandhipīḍā| kumbhasāhvaḥ tantrāntare kumbhakāmaleti kathyate||11||
jvarāṅgamardabhramasādatandrākṣayānvito lāghara(va)ko'lasākhyaḥ |12|
tasyaiva kumbhasāhvasyāvasthāyāṃ sañjñāntaramāha- jvarāṅgamardetyādi| sa eva kumbhasāhvo jvarādiyukto lāgharako'lasākhyaḥ syāditi piṇḍārthaḥ| lāgharakaḥ kathyate alasakaśca kathyata ityarthaḥ| aṅgamardo vedanāviśeṣaḥ, sphuṭaniketyapare| anye pāṇḍuroga eva jvarādiyuktaḥ kumbhasāhvaḥ kathyata iti manyante| asyaiva avasthābhedaṃ 3 pānakīti tantrāntarīyāḥ paṭhanti| tadyathā- “santāpo bhinnavarcastvaṃ bahirantaśca pītatā| pāṇḍutā netrarogaśca pānakīlakṣaṇaṃ vadet”- iti|12|-
taṃ vātapittāddharipītanīlaṃ 4 halīmakaṃ nāma vadanti tajjñāḥ ||12||
idānīṃ halīmakalakṣaṇamāha- tamityādi| taṃ pāṇḍurogam| kumbhasāhvo yadā haritapītanīlaḥ syāt, tadā tajjñāḥ kāyacikitsāvido halīmakaṃ nāma vadanti| hari haritaṃ, nīlaṃ śyāvam| kecittu ‘taṃ vātapittābhiparītaliṅgaṃ’ iti paṭhitvā vātapittābhyāmabhiparītaṃ sambaddhaṃ liṅgaṃ yasya tatheti vyākhyānayanti||12||
upadravāsteṣvaruciḥ pipāsā chardirjvaro mūrdharujā'gnisādaḥ | śophastathā kaṇṭhagato'balatvaṃ mūrcchā klamo hṛdyavapīḍanaṃ ca ||13||
idānīṃ pāṇḍurogopadravānāha- upadravā ityādi| teṣu pāṇḍurogeṣu| mūrdharujā śirovyathā| abalatvaṃ nirbalatvam| sopadravasya pāṇḍurogasya kṛcchrasādhyatvamasādhyatvaṃ ca yathāvasthaṃ boddhavyam||13||
sādhyaṃ tu pāṇḍvāmayinaṃ samīkṣya snigdhaṃ ghṛtenordhvamadhaśca śuddham | sampādayet kṣaudraghṛtapragāḍhairharītakīcūrṇayutaiḥ prayogaiḥ ||14|| pibedghṛtaṃ vā rajanīvipakvaṃ yattraiphalaṃ tailvakameva vā'pi | virecanadravyakṛtaṃ pibedvā yogāṃśca vairecanikān ghṛtena ||15||
idānīṃ cikitsitamāha- sādhyamityādi| sādhyapāṇḍurogajñānaṃ vakṣyamāṇāsādhyalakṣaṇaviparyayādariṣṭalakṣaṇavaiparītyācca| ghṛteneti kaṭvarakalyāṇakadādhikamahātiktapañcagavyādinā, anye kevalenaiva gavyaghṛtenetyāhuḥ| yadatra pāṇḍurogeṣu ghṛtenaiva snehanamuktaṃ tatteṣāṃ pittabhūyiṣṭhatvādraktadūṣyatvācca| yadyapi pāṇḍuroge ūrdhvaśodhanaṃ niṣiddhaṃ, tathā'pi ṛtudeśaprakṛtikālaśarīrāpekṣayā mṛdu vamanaṃ deyam| tathā ca tantrāntaram,- “kālaṃ ca deśaprakṛtī śarīraṃ samīkṣya dadyādvamanaṃ vidhijñaḥ| vāntasya tīkṣṇānyanulomanāni kalpopadiṣṭāni bhiṣagvidadhyāt”- iti| vamanānantaraṃ vakṣyamāṇaiḥ śāliprabhṛtibhiḥ kṛtasaṃsarjanakramasya doṣaśamanārthamāha- sampādayedityādi| śuddhaṃ pāṇḍurogiṇamevambhūtaiḥ prayogairvakṣyamāṇayogaiḥ śeṣadoṣaśamanārthaṃ sampādayet samyak yojayedityarthaḥ| nanu, sarpirmadhuharītakīyuktamekamevāyorajoyogamabhidhāsyati, tat kathaṃ prayogairiti bahuvacanaṃ? satyaṃ, pradeśāntaroktānāṃ navāyasaprabhṛtīnāmatra śastatvāt| pibedghṛtaṃ vetyādi| rajanīvipakvaṃ haridrākalkasādhitam|traiphalaṃ triphalāsādhitam| tailvakamiti tilvakaḥ paṭṭikārodhraḥ, tena sādhitamityarthaḥ; anye tailvakaṃ vātavyādhyuktamāhuḥ| virecanadravyakṛtamityādi|- trivṛdādisaṃskṛtaṃ ghṛtaṃ; vairecanikān yogān virecanadravyavikalpoktān, tāṃśca ghṛtena pibet||14-15||
mūtre nikumbhārdhapalaṃ vipācya pibedabhīkṣṇaṃ kuḍavārdhamātram | khādedguḍaṃ vā'pyabhayāvipakvamāragvadhādikvathitaṃ 5 pibedvā ||16||
idānīṃ pāṇḍuroge'parāṇyapi virecanānyāha- mūtre ityādi| nikumbhā dantī, tasyā ardhapalaṃ mahiṣīmūtre ṣoḍaśaguṇe vipācya, caturbhāgāvaśiṣṭaṃ kuḍavārdhamātraṃ paladvayamātraṃ, virekāya pibedityarthaḥ| guḍa ikṣuvikṛtiḥ| abhayāvipakvaṃ harītakīkvāthavipakvaṃ, anye abhayāvipakvaṃ guḍakaṃ saharītakīprabhṛtikamāhuḥ| apare ‘abhayāvimiśram’ iti paṭhanti| muhurvairecanikayoganirdeśāt 6 pāṇḍuroge virecanasyātyantahitatvaṃ jñeyam||16||
ayorajovyoṣaviḍaṅgacūrṇaṃ lihyāddharidrāṃ triphalānvitāṃ vā | sarpirmadhubhyāṃ vidadhīta vā'pi śāstrapradeśābhihitāṃśca yogān ||17||
kiṃ pāṇḍuroge pūrvoktameva kāryamutānyadapītyāha- aya ityādi| ayorajovyoṣaviḍaṅgacūrṇamityeko yogaḥ| haridrāṃ triphalānvitāmiti dvitīyo yogaḥ| ayorajo lohacūrṇam| vyoṣaṃ trikaṭukam| ‘lihyāt’ ityātra ‘madhusarpirbhyām’ iti sambandhanīyam| haridrāṃ piṇḍaharidrām| kecit haridrāśabdena ādau dāruśabdalopāt dāruharidrāṃ gṛhṇanti| vidadhīta vā'pītyādi| vidadhīta kurvīta| śāstrapradeśābhihitāṃśca yogān śāstre'nyapradeśoktān navāyasādīn||17||
harecca doṣān bahuśo'lpamātrān śvayeddhi doṣeṣvatinirhṛteṣu |18|
idānīṃ pāṇḍuroge yat śodhanamuktaṃ tadalpamātrayā kartavyamityāha- harecca doṣānityādi| bahuśo bahūn vārān| alpamātrān stokastokān| kasmāt stokān stokān haredityāha- śvayeddhi doṣeṣvityādi| śvayet śvayathuṃ prāpnuyāt| hi yasmādarthe| atra pāṭhāntaraṃ- ‘harecca doṣān bahuśo'lpamātrān śuddheṣu doṣeṣvabhinirhṛteṣu’ iti| śuddheṣu duṣṭirahiteṣu; abhinirhṛteṣvityabhiśabdaḥ samyagarthaḥ, atiyogasyāniṣṭatvāt|18|-
dhātrīphalānāṃ rasamikṣujaṃ ca manthaṃ pibet kṣaudrayutaṃ hitāśī ||18||
dhātrīphalānāmityādi| dhātrīphalānāmāmalakaphalānāṃ svarasaṃ kṣaudrayutaṃ pibedityeko yogaḥ| ikṣujaṃ rasaṃ kṣaudrayutamiti dvitīyaḥ| manthamiti “śaktavaḥ sarpiṣā'bhyaktāḥ śītavāripariplutāḥ”- ityādyuktalakṣaṇaṃ kṣaudrayutamiti tṛtīyaḥ| tathā ca tantrāntaram- “pibet suśītalān manthān ghṛtāktān madhusaṃyutān| sakṣaudraṃ vā rasaṃ dhātryā ikṣorvā'pi hitāśanaḥ”- iti| tantrāntare pāṇḍuroge manthaśabdena viśiṣṭo mantha ucyate| tadyathā- “dhātrīphalarase śaktūnikṣūṇāṃ ca rase tathā| pāṇḍurmadhusamāyuktaṃ pibenmanthaṃ suśītalam”- iti||18||
ume bṛhatyau rajanīṃ śukākhyāṃ kākādanīṃ cāpi sakākamācīm | ādāribimbīṃ sakadambapuṣpīṃ vipācya sarpirvipacet kaṣāye ||19|| tat pāṇḍutāṃ hantyupayujyamānaṃ kṣīreṇa vā māgadhikā yathāgni |20|
ume ityādi| śukākhyā carmakāravaṭaḥ, anye śukaśimbāmāhuḥ| kākādanī kākatindukaḥ| ādāribimbīṃ sakadambapuṣpīmityādi|- ādārī vellantarasadṛśapuṣpā āliriti loke, bimbī rāsnānukāriviṭapā lohitaphalā ca; kārtikakuṇḍastu ādāriśimbī āuliriti prasiddheti vyākhyānayati| kadambapuṣpī bhūmikadambaḥ, alambuṣāmapare| etāni kaṣāyakalpena vipācya, tasmin kaṣāye sarpirghṛtaṃ vipacet| kṣīreṇa vetyādi| māgadhikā vā kṣīreṇa saha yathāgni upayujyamānā paṇḍutāṃ hantītyarthaḥ||19||-
hitaṃ ca yaṣṭīmadhujaṃ 7 kaṣāyaṃ cūrṇaṃ samaṃ vā madhunā'valihyāt ||20||
hitaṃ cetyādi| yaṣṭīmadhukakaṣāyaṃ madhunā samamityeko yogaḥ| cūrṇaṃ vā yaṣṭīmadhukasya madhunā samaṃ madhunā saha lihyāditi dvitīyaḥ| anye ‘cūrṇaṃ samaṃ vā madhunā'valihyāt’ ityatra ‘cūrṇaṃ lihedvā madhunā dvitīyam’ iti paṭhanti; tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ||20||
gomūtrayuktaṃ triphalādalānāṃ dattvā''yasaṃ cūrṇamanalpakālam | pravālamuktāñjanaśaṅkhacūrṇaṃ lihyāttathā kāñcanagairikottham ||21||
gomūtrayūktamityādi| triphalādalānāṃ cūrṇamāyasaṃ cūrṇaṃ dattvā gomūtrayuktamanalpakālaṃ cirakālaṃ lihyāt| triphalādalānāmiti dalaśabdaḥ aṣṭhīlāparihārārtham| anye ‘gomūtrayuktaṃ triphalādalānāṃ’ ityatra ‘gomūtrapītatriphalādalānāṃ’ iti paṭhanti, gomūtrabhāvitatriphalādalānāmiti vyākhyānayanti| pravāletyādi| pravālo vidrumaḥ, muktā mauktikam, añjanaṃ rasāñjanam| tatheti gomūtrayuktamanalpakālaṃ lihyādityarthaḥ| kāñcanagairikotthamityatrāpi tatheti sambadhyate; tena suvarṇagairikacūrṇaṃ gomūtrayutaṃ cirakālaṃ lihyādityarthaḥ| ‘gomūtrapītāṃ triphalāṃ sapiṣṭāṃ’ iti kecit paṭhanti, gomūtrapītāṃ gomūtrabhāvitāṃ madhunā avalihyāditi vyākhyānayanti||21||
