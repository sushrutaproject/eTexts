--- title: "14. bhedyarogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 14 order: 14 ---

athāto bhedyarogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

lekhyapratiṣedhānantaraṃ bhittvā likhediti vacanādbhedyarogapratiṣedhārambhaḥ- athāta ityādi||1-2||

svedayitvā bisagranthiṃ chidrāṇyasya nirāśayam [1] |
pakvaṃ bhittvā tu śastreṇa saindhavenāvacūrṇayet ||3||
kāsīsamāgadhīpuṣpanepālyelāyutena tu |
tataḥ kṣaudraghṛtaṃ dattvā samyagbandhamathācaret ||4||

svedayitvetyādi| bisagranthiṃ pakvaṃ svedayitvā, anantaramasya pakvasya bisagrantheḥ svinnasya chidrāṇi nirāśayaṃ bhittvā, saindhavena kāsīsādiyuktenāvacūrṇayet| nirāśayamiti āśayasya utsaṅgasyābhāvo yathā syāttathā bhittvetyarthaḥ| puṣpaṃ puṣpāñjanaṃ, nepālī manaḥśilā| tataḥ pratisāraṇādanantaraṃ sandhānārthaṃ kṣaudraghṛtadānam| kecit ‘samyagbandhamathācaret’ ityatra ‘bandhenoktena yojayet’ iti paṭhanti| samyakśabdena śithilatā bandhasya khyāpyate||3-4||

rocanākṣāratutthāni pippalyaḥ kṣaudrameva ca |
pratisāraṇamekaikaṃ bhinne lagaṇa iṣyate ||5||
mahatyapi ca yuñjīta kṣārāgnī vidhikovidaḥ |6|

lagaṇasya cikitsāmāha- rocanetyādi| rocanā gorocanā, kṣāro yavakṣāraḥ| etāni pratisāraṇānyalpe lagaṇe bhinne sati yojyāni| ekaikamiti vacanena gorocanādibhirekaikaśaḥ pratisāraṇaṃ vidheyamityarthaḥ| alpalagaṇacikitsāmabhidhāya mahālagaṇacikitsāmāha- mahatyapi ca yuñjītetyādi| mahati gurutare iti| yuñjīteti bhinne sati lagaṇe kṣārāgnī prayuñjīta; tadanantaraṃ tatkālameva kṣārāgnidagdhavraṇavaccikitsā, paścāt sāmānyavraṇavat| kovidaḥ paṇḍitaḥ||5||-

svinnāṃ bhinnāṃ viniṣpīḍya bhiṣagañjananāmikām ||6||
śilailānatasindhūtthaiḥ sakṣaudraiḥ pratisārayet |7|

svayaṃ bhinnāyā añjananāmikāyāścikitsāmāha- svinnāmityādi| bhinnāṃ svayambhinnām| viniṣpīḍyeti snehasvedapūrvakaṃ niṣpīḍanaṃ kṛtvā, śilā manaḥśilā, elā sūkṣmailā, nataṃ tagaraṃ, sindhūtthaṃ saindhavam| pratisārayet gharṣayet||6||-

rasāñjanamadhubhyāṃ tu bhittvā vā śastrakarmavit ||7||
pratisāryāñjanairyuñjyāduṣṇairdīpaśikhodbhavaiḥ |8|

abhinnāñjananāmikācikitsāmāha- rasāñjanetyādi| svayamabhinnāmañjananāmikāṃ pūrvaṃ [2] svinnāṃ śastrakarmavidbhittvā rasāñjanamadhubhyāṃ pratisārya taduttarakālaṃ dīpaśikhodbhavaiḥ kajjalākhyairañjanairyojayediti piṇḍārthaḥ||7||-

samyaksvinne kṛmigranthau bhinne syāt pratisāraṇam ||8||
triphalātutthakāsīsasaindhavaiśca rasakriyā [3] |9|

krimigranthicikitsāmāha- samyagityādi| samyaksvinne iti locanocitasvedasvinne, anantaraṃ bhedite pratisāraṇamiti; anuktamapyañjananāmikoddiṣṭamañjanamadhubhyāṃ pratisāraṇaṃ, tadanantaraṃ triphalākvāthena tutthakāsīsasaindhavāvāpena rasakriyā||8||-

bhittvopanāhaṃ kaphajaṃ pippalīmadhusaindhavaiḥ ||9||
lekhayenmaṇḍalāgreṇa samantāt pracchayedapi |10|

śleṣmopanāhacikitsāmāha- bhittvetyādi| śleṣmopanāho bhedyaḥ, mahānnīrujaśca lekhyaḥ, raktānubandhaśca pracchanīyaḥ; śleṣmopanāhe bhinne likhite pracchite'pi ca pippalyādibhirekameva pratisāraṇam||9||-

saṃsnehya patrabhaṅgaiśca svedayitvā yathāsukham ||10||
āpākādvidhinoktena pañcamedyānupācaret |
sarveṣveteṣu vihitaṃ vidhānaṃ snehapūrvakam |
sampakve prayato bhūtvā kurvīta vraṇaropaṇam ||11||

eṣāṃ pañcānāmapi medyānāṃ bisagranthyādīnāmāmānāmādau saṃśamanaṃ nirdiśannāha- saṃsnehyetyādi| patrabhaṅgaiḥ patrakhaṇḍaiḥ; anye patrabhaṅgaśabdena pallavamāhuḥ| vidhinā apatarpaṇādinā sāmānyaśothapratīkāreṇa| snehapūrvakaṃ vidhānamiti snehasvedaraktāsrāvavirecanādi, nayanabudbudajānyatra vamanaṃ ca||10-11||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre bhedyarogapratiṣedho nāma caturdaśo'dhyāyaḥ ||14||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre caturdaśo'dhyāyaḥ||14||

1. ‘nirāśrayaṃ’ iti pā.|
2. ‘pūrvavat’ iti pā.|
3. ‘rasakriyām’ iti pā.|
