--- title: "2. saṃdhigatarogavijñānīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 2 order: 2 ---

athātaḥ sandhigatarogavijñānīyamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

pūrvasminnadhyāye sandhyādikrameṇa nayanagatarogā abhihitāḥ, ata aupadravikānantaraṃ prāk sandhigatā rogāḥ pratipādyā bhavantītyata āha- athāta ityādi| gataśabda āśrayavacanaḥ||1-2||

pūyālasaḥ sopanāhaḥ srāvāḥ parvaṇikā'lajī |
krimigranthiśca vijñeyā rogāḥ sandhigatā nava ||3||

srāvāḥ catvāraḥ pūyāsrāva ityādayaḥ||3||

pakvaḥ śophaḥ sandhijaḥ saṃsravedyaḥ sāndraṃ pūyaṃ pūti pūyālasaḥ saḥ |4|

idānīṃ teṣāṃ navānāmapi pratyekaṃ lakṣaṇamāha- pakva ityādi| sandhijaḥ kanīnasandhijaḥ| pūti durgandham| ayaṃ sannipātajaḥ sādhyaśca|4|-

granthirnālpo dṛṣṭisandhāvapākaḥ kaṇḍūprāyo nīrujastūpanāhaḥ ||4||

granthirityādi| nālpo mahānityarthaḥ| apāka īṣatpākaḥ| upanāhaḥ śleṣmopanāhaḥ| kaphajo'yaṃ sādhyaśca||4||

gatvā sandhīnaśrumārgeṇa doṣāḥ kuryuḥ srāvān rugvihīnān kanīnāt |
tān vai srāvān netranāḍīmathaike tasyā liṅgaṃ kīrtayiṣye caturdhā ||5||
pākaḥ sandhau saṃsravedyaśca pūyaṃ pūyāsrāvo naikarūpaḥ pradiṣṭaḥ |
śvetaṃ sāndraṃ picchilaṃ saṃsravedyaḥ śleṣmāsrāvo nīrujaḥ sa pradiṣṭaḥ ||6||
raktāsrāvaḥ śoṇitotthaḥ saraktamuṣṇaṃ nālpaṃ saṃsravennātisāndram |
pītābhāsaṃ nīlamuṣṇaṃ jalābhaṃ pittāsrāvaḥ saṃsravet sandhimadhyāt ||7||

sandhayo netrāntargatāścatvāraḥ| aśrumārgaśabdenātra sirā abhipretāḥ| tathāca videhaḥ,- “aśrusrāvaḥ sirā gatvā netrasandhiṣu tiṣṭhati| tataḥ kanīnakaṃ gatvā cāśru kṛtvā kanīnake|| tataḥ sravatyathāsrāvaṃ yathādoṣamavedanam”- iti| aśrusrāvaśabdenātra tatsahito doṣo'bhidhīyate| kathaṃ caturdhetyāha- pāka ityādi|- naikarūpa iti asya [1] sannipātajatvādbahurūpatvam| sandhiratra kanīnakasandhiḥ| asādhyo'yam| śveta ityādi|- ayamapyasādhyaḥ| raktetyādi|- asāvapyasādhyaḥ| pītetyādi|- kanīnakasandhimadhyādityarthaḥ| vyādhisvabhāvato vātāsrāvo nāsti, pittajagalagaṇḍacāturthikavat| eṣo'pyasādhyaḥ||5-7||

tāmrā tanvī dāhaśūlopapannā raktājjñeyā parvaṇī vṛttaśophā |
jātā sandhau kṛṣṇaśukle'lajī syāttasminneva khyāpitā pūrvaliṅgaiḥ ||8||

tāmretyādi| asādhyo'yam| alajītyādi| tasminneva kṛṣṇaśuklasandhāveva| pūrvaliṅgaiḥ parvaṇīliṅgairityarthaḥ| parvaṇyalajyoḥ sthānasya lakṣaṇasya caikye'pi tanutvasthūlatvābhyāṃ bhedaḥ| tanvī parvaṇī, tataḥ sthūlā punaralajī| iyaṃ sannipātajā asādhyā ca||8||

krimigranthirvartmanaḥ pakṣmaṇaśca kaṇḍūṃ kuryuḥ krimayaḥ sandhijātāḥ |
nānārūpā vartmaśuklasya sandhau [2] caranto'ntarnayanaṃ dūṣayanti ||9||

krimītyādi| krimīgranthirvartmaśuklayoḥ sandhau bhavati| tatraiva nānārūpāḥ krimayo vartmanaḥ pakṣmaṇaśca sandhiṃ prāptāḥ kaṇḍūṃ janayanti, antaścaranto bhakṣayanto nayanaṃ dūṣayanti| asau kaphajaḥ sādhyaśca||9||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantre sandhigatarogavijñānīyo nāma dvitīyo'dhyayaḥ ||2||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ||2||

1. ‘tasya’ iti pā.|
2. ‘vartmaśuklāntasandhau’ iti pā.|
