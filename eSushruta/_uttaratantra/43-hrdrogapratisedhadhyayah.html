--- title: "43. hṛdrogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 43 order: 43 ---

athāto hṛdrogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

gulmapratiṣedhānantaraṃ sthānasāmīpyānnāmasaṅkīrtanādvā hṛdrogapratiṣedho yukta ityata āha- athāta ityādi||1-2||

vegāghātoṣṇarūkṣānnairatimātropasevitaiḥ |
viruddhādhyaśanājīrṇairasātmyaiścāpi(ti) bhojanaiḥ ||3||
dūṣayitvā rasaṃ doṣā viguṇā hṛdayaṃ gatāḥ |
kurvanti hṛdaye bādhāṃ hṛdrogaṃ taṃ pracakṣate ||4||

idānīṃ pratijñānantaraṃ pūrvavaddhṛdrogasya hetusamprāptī āha- vegetyādi| etairvegāghātādihetubhirviguṇāḥ kupitā doṣā hṛdayaṃ gatvā rasaṃ dūṣayitvā hṛdaye bādhāṃ kurvanti, taṃ hṛdrogaṃ pracakṣate ācāryā iti sambandhaḥ| vegāghāta ityatra āghātaśabdo dvirāvartayitavyaḥ, tena vegāghāta āghātaśca; tatra vegāghāto vegāvarodhaḥ, āghāto laguḍādiprahāraḥ| viruddhaṃ hitāhitīyoktam, adhyaśanaṃ sājīrṇabhojanam, ajīrṇam āmavidagdhādikam| bādhāgrahaṇaṃ bādhāvaicitryajñāpanārtham| vegāghātādirasātmyabhojanānto hetuḥ, dūṣayitvetyādikā samprāptiḥ| hṛdroge pūrvarūpānabhidhānamavyaktalakṣaṇānāmeva pūrvarūpatvenābhimatatvāt; anye jātimātraparicchedāt pūrvarūpāvagamaḥ||3-4||

caturvidhaḥ sa doṣaiḥ syāt kṛmibhiśca [1] pṛthak pṛthak |
lakṣaṇaṃ tasya vakṣyāmi cikitsitamanantaram ||5||

tasya saṅkhyāmāha- caturvidha ityādi| sa hṛdrogaḥ| doṣaiḥ ekaikaiḥ sannipatitaiśca, kṛmibhiriti kṛmibhiḥ sahetyarthaḥ| nanu, tantrāntare pañca hṛdrogāḥ paṭhitāḥ, tat kathamatra catvāraḥ? satyaṃ, tridoṣahṛdrogottarāvasthaiva kṛmihṛdrogaḥ, tena na pañca hṛdrogāḥ| pṛthak pṛthagityādi| anantaraṃ lakṣaṇānantaramityarthaḥ||5||

āyamyate mārutaje hṛdayaṃ tudyate [2] tathā |
nirmathyate dīryate ca sphoṭyate pāṭyate'pi ca ||6||

vātikahṛdrogalakṣaṇamāha- āyamyata ityādi| āyamyate dīrghīkriyata iva, mārutaje hṛdroge, tudyate pratodena vyathyata iva, nirmathyate viloḍyata iva, dīryate ārayeva, pāṭyate dvidhā kriyata iva||6||

tṛṣṇoṣādāhacoṣāḥ syuḥ paittike hṛdayaklamaḥ |
dhūmāyanaṃ mūrcchā ca svedaḥ śoṣo mukhasya ca ||7||

paittikahṛdrogalakṣaṇamāha- tṛṣṇetyādi| ūṣā prādeśiko dāhaḥ| coṣaḥ cūṣyata iva vedanāviśeṣaḥ| paittike pittaje hṛdroge| dhūmāyanaṃ dhūmodvamanamiva||7||

gauravaṃ kaphasaṃsrāvo'ruciḥ stambho'gnimārdavam |
mādhuryamapi cā''syasya balāsāvatate hṛdi ||8||

ślaiṣmikahṛdrogalakṣaṇamāha- gauravamityādi| stambho jaḍimā| balāsāvatate śleṣmacchanne||8||

utkleśaḥ ṣṭhīvanaṃ todaḥ śūlo hṛllāsakastamaḥ |
aruciḥ śyāvanetratvaṃ śoṣaśca kṛmije bhavet ||9||

tridoṣajahṛdrogalakṣaṇamāha- utkleśa ityādi| ṣṭhīvanaṃ thūtkaraṇam| hṛllāso lālāsrutiḥ| tamaḥ andhakāradarśanaṃ śvāso vā| tama ityatra ‘sānnipātike bhavet’ iti vākyaśeṣaḥ| tatra todaśūlau vātena, utkleśahṛllāsaṣṭhīvanāni śleṣmaṇā, tamaḥ pitteneti| kecit ‘aruciḥ śyāvanetratvaṃ śoṣaśca kṛmije bhavet’ ityantaṃ sānnipātikahṛdrogalakṣaṇaṃ manyante| tridoṣajahṛdrogasyaivāpacārāt krimīn prāptasya lakṣaṇamāha- arucirityādi| kṛmije iti tridoṣajahṛdroga evāpacāreṇottarāvasthāyāṃ kṛminipātāt kṛmija ityucyate| tathā ca tantrāntaram- “tridoṣaje tu hṛdroge yo durātmā niṣevate| tilakṣīraguḍādīni granthistasyopajāyate|| marmaikadeśe saṅkledaṃ rasaścāpyupagacchati| saṅkledāt kṛmayaścāsya bhavantyupahatātmanaḥ”- iti, ato na saṅkhyātirekaḥ| kecit sānnipātiko hṛdrogo vyādhisvabhāvānna bhavatyevetyāhuḥ; tanna, samānatantre tridoṣajadarśanāt||9||

bhramaklamau sādaśoṣau jñeyāsteṣāmupadravāḥ |10|

vātādijānāṃ kṛmihīnānāṃ hṛdrogiṇāmupadravānāha- bhrametyādi| bhramaḥ cakrārūḍhasyeva bhramaṇaṃ, klamaḥ anāyāsaḥ śramaḥ| sādaḥ aṅgasādaḥ| śoṣo mukhasya dhātūnāṃ ca| yadyapi bhramādayo vātapittātmakāstathā'pi vyādhisvabhāvācchlaiṣmike'pi bhavanti|10|-

kṛmije kṛmijātīnāṃ ślaiṣmikāṇāṃ ca ye matāḥ ||10||

kṛmijasyopadravānāha- kṛmije kṛmijātīnāmityādi| ślaiṣmikāṇāmiti kṛmijātīnāmityasya viśeṣaṇam| tena ślaiṣmikāṇāṃ kṛmijātīnāṃ ya eva upadravāsta eva kṛmije hṛdroge matā ityarthaḥ||10||

vātopasṛṣṭe hṛdaye vāmayet snigdhamāturam |
dvipañcamūlakvāthena sasnehalavaṇena tu ||11||
pippalyelāvacāhiṅguyavabhasmāni saindhavam |
sauvarcalamatho śuṇṭhīmajamodāṃ ca cūrṇitam ||12||
phaladhānyāmlakaulatthadadhimadyāsavādibhiḥ |
pāyayeta viśuddhaṃ ca snehenānyatamena vā ||13||

idānīṃ vātahṛdrogacikitsāmāha- vātopasṛṣṭe ityādi| vātopasṛṣṭe hṛdaye vātahṛdroge ityarthaḥ| yadyapi vāte vamanaṃ na śastaṃ, tathā'pi vātasya kaphasthānagaṇatvādatra vamanaṃ śastameva, tathā pittaje'pi| vamanānantaraṃ yat kāryaṃ tadāha- pippalītyādi| yavabhasma yavakṣāraḥ| phalāni mātuluṅgādiphalāni, teṣāṃ raso'bhipretaḥ| madyāsavau madyavargoktau| snehenānyatamena vā catuḥsnehamadhye ekena snehenetyarthaḥ||11-13||

bhojayejjīrṇaśālyannaṃ jāṅgalaiḥ saghṛtai rasaiḥ |
vātaghnasiddhaṃ tailaṃ ca dadyādbastiṃ pramāṇataḥ ||14||

vātahṛdroge pathyamāha- bhojayedityādi| kiṃ vātahṛdroge etadeva kāryamutānyadapītyāha- vātaghnasiddhamityādi| vātaghnasiddhaṃ bhadradārvādipakvam| pramāṇataḥ yathāpramāṇam||14||

śrīparṇīmadhukakṣaudrasitotpalajalairvamet |
pittopasṛṣṭe hṛdaye seveta madhuraiḥ śṛtam ||15||
ghṛtaṃ kaṣāyāṃścoddiṣṭān pittajvaravināśanān |16|

pittahṛdroge vamanamāha- śrīparṇītyādi| śrīparṇī kāśmarī, madhukaṃ yaṣṭīmadhu, sitā śarkarā, utpalaṃ kuṣṭham| atra jalaśabdo na kvāthatvena sarvatra sambandhanīyaḥ, kṣaudrādīnāṃ kvāthe'nupayogāt| seveta madhurairityādi| madhuraiḥ śṛtaṃ kākolyādibhiḥ pakvam| pittajvaravināśanān kaṣāyān ‘candanośīraśrīparṇī’- ityādinoktān||15||-

tṛptasya ca rasairmukhyairmadhuraiḥ [3] saghṛtairbhiṣak ||16||
sakṣaudraṃ vitaredbastau [4] tailaṃ madhukasādhitam |17|

pittahṛdroge snehabastimāha- tṛptasyetyādi| rasairmukhyaiḥ eṇādimāṃsarasaiḥ| madhuraiḥ amlādisaṃskṛtaiḥ| madhukasādhitamekenaiva yaṣṭīmadhukena pakvaṃ; anye jīvanīyasādhitamāhuḥ||16||-

vacānimbakaṣāyābhyāṃ vāntaṃ hṛdi kaphātmake ||17||
cūrṇaṃ tu pāyayetoktaṃ vātaje bhojayecca [5] tam |
phalādimatha mustādiṃ triphalāṃ vā pibennaraḥ ||18||
śyāmātrivṛtkalkayutaṃ ghṛtaṃ vā'pi virecanam |
balātailairvidadhyācca [6] bastiṃ bastiviśāradaḥ ||19||

ślaiṣmikahṛdroge cikitsāmāha- vacetyādi| śleṣmahṛdroge vacānimbakaṣāyābhyāṃ vāntaṃ vātaje hṛdroge uktaṃ ‘cūrṇaṃ pippalyelā’- ityādikaṃ pāyayediti piṇḍārthaḥ| na kevalaṃ taccūrṇaṃ pāyayet bhojayecca tamiti taṃ vātaje hṛdroge uktaṃ cūrṇaṃ bhojayecca bhaktena saha khādedityarthaḥ| phalādimatha mustādimityādi|- phalādiḥ madanaphalādiḥ saṃśodhanasaṃśamanīyoktaḥ| mustādiḥ dravyasaṅgrahaṇīyoktaḥ| śyāmātrivṛtkalkayutamityādi|- śyāmā vṛddhadārakaḥ| atra pibediti sambadhyate| balātailairityādi| vātādicikitsitaṃ sarvamevānuktamapi tridoṣaje kāryam||17-19||

kṛmihṛdrogiṇaṃ snigdhaṃ bhojayet piśitaudanam |
dadhnā ca palalopetaṃ tryahaṃ, paścādvirecayet ||20||
sugandhibhiḥ salavaṇairyogaiḥ sājājiśarkaraiḥ |
viḍaṅgagāḍhaṃ dhānyāmlaṃ pāyayetāpyanantaram ||21||
hṛdayasthāḥ patantyevamadhastāt krimayo nṛṇām |
yavānnaṃ vitareccāsya saviḍaṅgamataḥ param ||22||

kṛmihṛdrogacikitsāmāha- kṛmihṛdrogiṇamityādi| piśitaudanaṃ sapalalaṃ dadhnā saha krimihṛdrogiṇaṃ tryahaṃ bhojayet kṛmīṇāmutkleśanārthaṃ; piśitaṃ māṃsaṃ, palalopetaṃ bhṛṣṭatilacūrṇayuktam| krimyutkleśānantaraṃ kiṃ kuryādityāha- paścādityādi| sugandhibhiryogaiḥ virecanakalpoktaiḥ ‘śarkarākṣaudrasaṃyuktaṃ’ ityādibhiḥ| salavaṇairyogairiti ‘samāstrivṛnnāgarakābhayāḥ syuḥ’ ityādibhiḥ| sājājiśarkarairiti sugandhiyogasalavaṇayogaviśeṣaṇaṃ, sājājikānāṃ vairecanikānāmanirdeśāt; tena, ajājiśarkarāsahitaiḥ sugandhibhiryogairathavā salavaṇairapyajādiyuktairvirecayet| pītavirekasyānupānamāha- viḍaṅgagāḍhamityādi| viḍaṅgagāḍhaṃ viḍaṅgotkaṭam| dhānyāmlaṃ kāñjikam| evaṃ pūrvoktena vidhānena| kecit ‘salavaṇaiḥ’ ityatra ‘sapalalaiḥ’ iti paṭhitvā sabhṛṣṭatilakalkaiḥ sugandhibhiryogairvirecayediti vyākhyānayanti| kṛmihṛdroge vamanaṃ na yojyaṃ, kṛmijarjarahṛdayatvāt; ata eva sugandhiyogairvirecanamuktaṃ, vamanaśaṅkābhayāt| dhānyāmlapānādanantaraṃ [7] kiṃ kuryādityāha- yavānnaṃ vitaredityādi| vitaret dadyāt| ata iti virekāt||20-22||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kāyacikitsātantre hṛdrogapratiṣedho nāma (pañcamo'dhyāyaḥ, āditaḥ) tricatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||43||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kāyacikitsātantre hṛdrogapratiṣedho nāma tricatvāriṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||43||

1. `pañcamaḥ krimibhistathā| pṛthagliṅgaṃ pravakṣyāmi' iti pā.|
2. pīḍyate' iti pā.|
3. `jāṅgalaiḥ' iti pā.|
4. `vitaredbasti' iti pā.|
5. bhojayeddhṛtam' iti pā.|
6. `balatailaṃ vidadhyāt' iti pā.|
7. `virecanādanantaraṃ' iti pā.|
