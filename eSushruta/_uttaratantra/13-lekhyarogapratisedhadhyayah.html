--- title: "13. lekhyarogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 13 order: 13 ---

athāto lekhyarogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

raktābhiṣyandānantaramauṣadhasādhyān gadāñcaturṣvabhiṣyandeṣvabhidhāya śastrasādhyān pratipādayitukāmo lekhyāñjanaprasaṅgena prathamaṃ lekhyarogapratiṣedhamāha- athāta ityādi| yadyapi cikitsāpravibhāgīyoddiṣṭāt ‘chedyāsteṣu daśaikaśca’ ityādivacanāt pūrvaṃ chedyarogāṇāmupādānaṃ prapnoti, tathā'pi lekhyāñjanaprasaṅgācchedyādiṣvalpāvasthāyāṃ [1] lekhanopayogācca likhyānāṃ prāgupādānam||1-2||

nava ye'bhihitā lekhyāḥ sāmānyasteṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ |
snigdhavāntaviriktasya nivātātapasadmani ||3||
(āptairdṛḍhaṃ gṛhītasya veśmanyuttānaśāyinaḥ) |
sukhodakaprataptena vāsasā susamāhitaḥ |
svedayedvartma nirbhujya vāmāṅguṣṭhāṅgulisthitam ||4||
aṅgulyaṅguṣṭhakābhyāṃ tu ni rbhugnaṃ vartma yatnataḥ |
plotāntarābhyāṃ [2] na yathā calati sraṃsate'pi vā ||5||
tataḥ pramṛjya plotena vartma śastrapadāṅkitam |
likhecchastreṇa patrairvā, tato rakte sthite punaḥ ||6||
svinnaṃ manohvākāsīsavyoṣārdrāñjanasaindhavaiḥ [3] |
ślakṣṇapiṣṭaiḥ samākṣīkaiḥ pratisāryoṣṇavāriṇā ||7||
prakṣālya haviṣā siktaṃ vraṇavat samupācaret |
svedāvapīḍaprabhṛtīṃstryahādūrdhvaṃ prayojayet ||8||
vyāsataste samuddiṣṭaṃ vidhānaṃ lekhyakarmaṇi |9|

tān sarvān saṅkhyayā'bhidhāya saṅkṣepārthaṃ teṣvabhinnavidhimupadiśannāha- navetyādi| tameva sāmānyavidhimāha- snigdhetyādi|- snigdhavāntaviriktasyetyatrānukto'pi snehānantaraṃ svedaḥ kāryaḥ| susamāhita ityanena yathā dṛśoḥ pīḍā na syāttathā svedayeditī dyotyate| nirbhujya parivartya, uttānīkṛtyetyarthaḥ| nirbhugnam uttānīkṛtam| plotāntarābhyāṃ vastraveṣṭitābhyāmaṅgulyaṅguṣṭhābhyāṃ yathā na calati na kampate, na vā sraṃsate nādhaḥ patati, tathā vartma nirbhujyetyarthaḥ| śastrapadāṅkitamiti pracchayitavye pracchitaṃ, bhedayitavye ca bhinnaṃ, chedanīye ca chinnamityarthaḥ| vātakaphavyādhikaṭhinaṃ dāruṇaṃ vartma śastreṇa likhet, pittaraktagadakrāntaṃ mṛduvartma punaḥ śephālikādīnāṃ patrairlikhet| paścātkarmāha- tata ityādi| svinnaṃ sveditam| manohvā manaḥśilā| ārdrāñjanaṃ rasāñjanam| mākṣīkaṃ suvarṇamākṣīkam| pratisārya avaghṛṣya| haviṣā ghṛtena| vraṇavat sadyovraṇavat saptāhaṃ yāvat, tadūrdhvaṃ śārīravraṇavat; tacca parṇabandhādinā krameṇa| svedāvapīḍetyādi| tryahādūrdhvaṃ punarvraṇavadeva svedāpīḍāprabhṛtīn prayojayediti tātparyārthaḥ| svedāvapīḍādikaṃ samāsenābhidhāya vistareṇātidiśannāhā- vyāsata ityādi| vidhānaṃ svedāvapīḍādikam| lekhyakarmaṇīti sakalalekhanārhagadakarmaṇītyartthaḥ||3-8||-

asṛgāsrāvarahitaṃ kaṇḍūśophavivarjitam ||9||
samaṃ nakhanibhaṃ vartma likhitaṃ samyagiṣyate |10|

idānīṃ samyaglikhitasya vartmano lakṣaṇamāha- asṛgāsrāvarahitamityādi| samaṃ nimnonnatatvarahitam| nakhanibhaṃ nakhasyeva nibhā yasya tattathā| tadeva vraṇavadupacaraṇīyam||9||-

raktamakṣi sravet skannaṃ kṣatācchastrakṛtāddhruvam ||10||
rāgaśophaparisrāvāstimiraṃ vyādhyanirjayaḥ |
vartma śyāvaṃ guru stabdhaṃ kaṇḍūharṣopadehavat ||11||
netrapākamudīrṇaṃ vā kurvītāpratikāriṇaḥ |
etaddurlikhitaṃ jñeyaṃ snehayitvā punārlikhet ||12||

durlikhitasya vartmano lakṣaṇamāha- raktamityādi| skannaṃ styānam| vyādhyanirjaya iti lekhanasādhyasya vyādheranupaśamaḥ| apratikāriṇa iti tasya pratikriyāmakurvata ityarthaḥ| tatra cikitsāmāha- snehatvetyādi| kecit ‘svedayitvā’ iti paṭhanti||10-12||

vyāvartate yadā vartma pakṣma cāpi vimuhyati [4] |
syāt saruk srāvabahulaṃ tadatisrāvitaṃ viduḥ ||13||
snehasvedādiriṣṭaḥ syāt kramastatrānilāpahaḥ |14|

atilikhitavartmano lakṣaṇamāha- vyāvartata ityādi| taccikitsitamāha- snehasvedādirityādi| anilāpahaḥ kramo'nyo'pi pariṣekādirbāhya ābhyantaro vā''hārādikaḥ| anilāpahena vāyuṃ jitvā paścādvraṇavadupacaraṇīyam||13||-

vartmāvabandhaṃ kliṣṭaṃ ca bahalaṃ yacca kīrtitam ||14||
pothakīścāpyavalikhet pracchayitvā'grataḥ śanaiḥ |15|

idānīṃ rogaviśeṣe kvacideva pracchannena raktaharaṇamṛdukaraṇapūrvakaṃ lekhanamatidiśannāha- vartmetyādi| avalikhet śastreṇa, na tu patrādibhiḥ||14||-

samaṃ likhettu medhāvī śyāvakardamavartmanī ||15||

kvacit punarlekhanamātrameva kartavyaṃ na punaśchedabhedapracchannānīti darśayannāha- samamityādi| samam ekakālamityarthaḥ; anye tu nātyavagāḍhaṃ nātyuttānamiti samamāhuḥ||15||

kumbhīkinīṃ śarkarāṃ ca tathaivotsaṅginīmapi |
kalpayitvā [5] tu śastreṇa likhet paścādatandritaḥ ||16||

kutracicchedapūrvakaṃ lekhanamāha- kumbhīkinīmityādi| kalpayitvā chittvetyarthaḥ| atandritaḥ analasaḥ||16||

bhaveyurvartmasu ca yāḥ piḍakāḥ kaṭhinā bhṛśam |
hrasvāstāmrāśca tāḥ pakvā bhindyādbhinnā likhedapi ||17||

kasmiṃścidbhedapūrvakaṃ lekhanamāha- bhaveyurityādi||17||

taruṇīścālpasaṃrambhā piḍakā bāhyavartmajāḥ |
viditvaitāḥ praśamayet svedālepanaśodhanaiḥ ||18||

kutrāpi vartmaroge na pūrvaṃ chedabhedapracchannāni, na cottarakālaṃ lekhanamiti darśayannāha- taruṇīścālpetyādi||18||

iti suśutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre lekhyarogapratiṣedho nāma trayodaśo'dhyāyaḥ ||13||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahakhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre trayodaśo'dhyāyaḥ||13||

1. ‘lekhanaprasaṅgāt’ iti pā.|
2. ‘plotāntarīkṛtaṃ naiva’ iti pā.|
3. vyoṣāñjanakasaindhavaiḥ’ iti pā.|
4. ‘vimuñcati’ iti pā.|
5. ‘kartayitvā’ iti pā.|
