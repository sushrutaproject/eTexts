athātaḥ karṇagatarogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
athāta ityādi| pratiṣedhaḥ cikitsitam||1-2||

nāḍīsvedopayogidravyāṇyāha- bilvetyādi| varṣābhūḥ śvetapunarnavā, dadhitthaḥ kapitthaḥ, unmatto dhattūrakaḥ, śigruḥ śobhāñjanakaḥ, viṭapaśākamanye| bastagandhā bobayikā| tarkārī araṇikā, yavaveṇubhiriti veṇuyavaḥ annajātiḥ| bilvairaṇḍādibhiḥ āranālasiddhaiḥ kevalavātārabdhe kaphāvṛtavātārabdhe ca karṇaśūle svedaḥ prayojyaḥ; paraṃ kevalavātaje śūle snehapūrvaḥ svedaḥ, kaphāvṛte punaḥ rūkṣaḥ sveda iti||6-7||
mīnakukkuṭalāvānāṃ māṃsajaiḥ payasā'pi vā | piṇḍaiḥ svedaṃ ca kurvīta karṇaśūlanivāraṇam ||8||
piṇḍasvedopayogidravyāṇyāha- mīnakukkuṭalāvānāmityādi| payasā'pi veti ghanībhūtakṣīrapiṇḍenetyarthaḥ| etairmīnādibhirmāṃsapiṇḍaiḥ pṛthak samastairvā svedaḥ kāryaḥ||8||

kaṇṭakārīmityādi| kaṇṭakārī catuṣpalapramāṇā, tadaṣṭaguṇaṃ kṣīraṃ, kṣīrāccaturguṇamudakaṃ dattvā vipacet, tena kṣīreṇa kukkuṭavasāṃ kuḍavāpramāṇāṃ pacet, tatkarṇayoḥ prapūraṇam||13||-
taṇḍulīyakamūlāni phalamaṅkolajaṃ tathā ||14|| ahiṃsrākendukānmūlaṃ saralaṃ devadāru ca | laśunaṃ śṛṅgaveraṃ ca tathā vaṃśāvalekhanam ||15|| kalkaireṣāṃ tathā'mlaiśca pacet snehaṃ caturvidham | vedanāyāḥ praśāntyarthaṃ hitaṃ tatkarṇapūraṇam ||16||
aparamapi pūraṇamāha- taṇḍulīyaketyādi| vaṃśāvalekhanaṃ vaṃśāccholanam| kalko dṛṣadi peṣitaḥ, sa cādravaḥ, sa cāṣṭapalapramāṇaḥ, amlaiḥ dadhitakrasurācukramātuluṅgarasaiḥ pratyekamaṣṭāviṃśatyadhikaśatapramāṇaiḥ, snehaścaturvidhaḥ sarpistailavasāmajjasañjño dvātriṃśatpalapramāṇaḥ||14-16||
laśunārdrakaśigrūṇāṃ muraṅgyā mūlakasya ca | kadalyāḥ svarasaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ kaduṣṇaḥ karṇapūraṇe ||17|| śṛṅgaverarasaḥ kṣaudraṃ saindhavaṃ tailameva ca | kaduṣṇaṃ karṇayordeyametadvā vedanāpaham ||18||
laśunārdraketyādi| śigruḥ madhuśigruḥ, muraṅgī dvitīyaḥ śobhāñjanakaḥ, kaduṣṇa īṣaduṣṇaḥ| eteṣāṃ laśunādīnāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ svarasā vyastāḥ samastā vā karṇapūraṇe deyāḥ||17-18||
vaṃśāvalekhanāyukte mūtre cājāvike bhiṣak | sarpiḥ pacettena karṇaṃ pūrayet karṇaśūlinaḥ ||19||
vaṃśetyādi| ‘mūtre cājāvike bhiṣak’ ityatra tantrāntaradarśanādviparyayeṇa sarpiḥpākaḥ kartavyaḥ; tena pūrvamāvike mūtre sarpiḥ paktavyaṃ, paścādāja iti| anye tu pūrvamāvikamūtrapakvena sarpiṣā karṇapūraṇaṃ, paścādājena mūtreṇeti viparyayaṃ vyākhyānayanti| kecit sarpiḥ pacedityatra tailaṃ pacediti paṭhanti; tanna, sarvanibandhaiḥ parihṛtatvāt||19||
mahataḥ pañcamūlasya kāṇḍamaṣṭādaśāṅgulam | kṣaumeṇāveṣṭya saṃsicya tailenādīpayettataḥ ||20|| yattailaṃ cyavate tebhyo dhṛtebhyo bhājanopari | jñeyaṃ taddīpikātailaṃ sadyo gṛhṇāti vedanām ||21||
dīpikātailamāha- mahata ityādi| kāṇḍaṃ khaṇḍaṃ, kṣaumeṇa atasīvastreṇa, āveṣṭya ‘tribhāgam’ iti śeṣaḥ| ghṛtebhyo ‘adhomukham’ iti śeṣaḥ| gṛhṇāti apaharati||20-21||
kuryādevaṃ bhadrakāṣṭhe kuṣṭhe kāṣṭhe ca sārale | matimān dīpikātailaṃ karṇaśūlanibarhaṇam ||22||
kuryādevamityādi| cakārādagurvairaṇḍāgnimanthakāṣṭhāni gṛhyante||22||
arkāṅkurānamlapiṣṭāṃstailāktān lavaṇānvitān | sannidadhyāt snuhīkāṇḍe korite tacchadāvṛte ||23|| puṭapākakramāsvinnān pīḍayedārasāgamāt | sukhoṣṇaṃ tadrasaṃ karṇe dāpayecchūlaśāntaye ||24|| kapitthamātuluṅgāmlaśṛṅgaverarasaiḥ śubhaiḥ | sukhoṣṇaiḥ pūrayet karṇaṃ tacchūlavinivṛttaye ||25|| karṇaṃ koṣṇena cukreṇa pūrayet karṇaśūlinaḥ | samudraphenacūrṇena yuktyā cāpyavacūrṇayet ||26||

pittajakarṇaśule sāmānyacikitsāmabhidhāya viśeṣacikitsāmāha- kākolyādāvityādi| kākolyādikalkena daśaguṇakṣīraṃ ghṛtaṃ siddhaṃ hitaṃ, tathā tiktaṃ tiktadravyasaṃskṛtam; anye tu tiktaṃ kuṣṭhacikitsitoktaṃ ghṛtaṃ kathayanti| kṣīravṛkṣā nyagrodhodumbarāśvatthaplakṣagardabhāṇḍāḥ, teṣāṃ pallavakvāthena kākolyādikalkena siddhaṃ ghṛtaṃ hitam| madhuke candane tatheti pratyekaṃ yaṣṭīmadhukacandanakvāthakalkābhyāṃ siddham| śarkarāmadhukairityatra yaṣṭīmadhukadvitayaṃ, ata eva bahutvam| saraiḥ virecanadravyairityarthaḥ| jejjaṭācāryastu kṣīravṛkṣapravāleṣvityekaṃ yogaṃ kathayati, bimbīkvātha iti dvitīyaṃ; tau ca yogāvanārṣau kathayanti||29-30||
