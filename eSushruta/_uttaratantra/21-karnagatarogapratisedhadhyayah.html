--- title: "21. karṇagatarogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 21 order: 21 ---

athātaḥ karṇagatarogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi| pratiṣedhaḥ cikitsitam||1-2||

sāmānyaṃ karṇarodheṣu ghṛtapānaṃ rasāyanam |
avyāyāmo'śiraḥsnānaṃ brahmacaryamakatthanam ||3||

karṇarogāṇāṃ sāmānyaṃ cikitsitamāha- sāmānyamityādi| ghṛtapānamatra ūrdhvabhaktikam| aśiraḥsnānaṃ na śiraḥsnānamityarthaḥ| akatthanam abhāṣaṇam| rasāyanamityatra ‘rasāśanam’ iti kecit paṭhanti, māṃsarasena saha bhojanamiti vyākhyānayanti||3||

karṇaśūle praṇāde ca bādhiryakṣveḍayorapi |
caturṇāmapi rogāṇāṃ sāmānyaṃ bheṣajaṃ viduḥ ||4||

vātajānāṃ caturṇāṃ karṇaśūlādīnāmekahetutvādekāmeva cikitsāmāha- karṇaśūla ityādi||4||

snigdhaṃ vātaharaiḥ svedairnaraṃ snehavirecitam |
nāḍīsvedairupacaretpiṇḍasvedaistathaiva ca ||5||

kiṃ tat sāmānyaṃ cikitsitamityāha- snigdhamityādi| snigdhaṃ snehapānenābhyaṅgena ca| snehavirecitam eraṇḍatailādibhiḥ| svedādhyāye vyāmārdhamātretyādinā nāḍīsvedaḥ proktaḥ; piṇḍasvedaḥ kṛśarāpāyasādibhiḥ||5||

bilvairaṇḍārkavarṣābhūdadhitthonmattaśigrubhiḥ |
bastagandhāśvagandhābhyāṃ tarkārīyavaveṇubhiḥ ||6||
āranālaśṛtairebhirnāḍīsvedaḥ prayojitaḥ |
kaphavātasamutthānaṃ karṇaśūlaṃ nirasyati ||7||

nāḍīsvedopayogidravyāṇyāha- bilvetyādi| varṣābhūḥ śvetapunarnavā, dadhitthaḥ kapitthaḥ, unmatto dhattūrakaḥ, śigruḥ śobhāñjanakaḥ, viṭapaśākamanye| bastagandhā bobayikā| tarkārī araṇikā, yavaveṇubhiriti veṇuyavaḥ annajātiḥ| bilvairaṇḍādibhiḥ āranālasiddhaiḥ kevalavātārabdhe kaphāvṛtavātārabdhe ca karṇaśūle svedaḥ prayojyaḥ; paraṃ kevalavātaje śūle snehapūrvaḥ svedaḥ, kaphāvṛte punaḥ rūkṣaḥ sveda iti||6-7||

mīnakukkuṭalāvānāṃ māṃsajaiḥ payasā'pi vā |
piṇḍaiḥ svedaṃ ca kurvīta karṇaśūlanivāraṇam ||8||

piṇḍasvedopayogidravyāṇyāha- mīnakukkuṭalāvānāmityādi| payasā'pi veti ghanībhūtakṣīrapiṇḍenetyarthaḥ| etairmīnādibhirmāṃsapiṇḍaiḥ pṛthak samastairvā svedaḥ kāryaḥ||8||

aśvatthapatrakhallaṃ vā vidhāya bahupatrakam |
tadaṅgāraiḥ susampūrṇaṃ nidadhyācchravaṇopari ||9||
yattailaṃ cyavate tasmāt khallādaṅgāratāpitāt |
tat prāptaṃ śravaṇasrotaḥ sadyo gṛhṇāti vedanām ||10||

śuddhavātaje śūle snehaviśeṣasvedamuddiśannāha- aśvatthapatretyādi| khallamiti dvikarṇikākāramityarthaḥ| abhyajya tailena ghṛtena vā, sātyakividehaproktatvāt||9-10||

kṣaumaguggulvagurubhiḥ saghṛtairdhūpayecca tam |
bhaktopari hitaṃ sarpirbastikarma ca pūjitam ||11||

karṇasvedasya paścātkarmāha- kṣaumetyādi| kṣaumam atasīvastraṃ, bastiratra śirobastiḥ||11||

niranno niśi tatsarpiḥ pītvopari pibet payaḥ |
mūrdhabastiṣu nasye ca mastiṣke pariṣecane ||12||
śatapākaṃ balātailaṃ praśastaṃ cāpi bhojane |13|

idānīṃ nirannasya sāyameva [1] koṣṇaṃ payo nirdiśannāha- niranna ityādi| nirannaḥ puruṣaḥ| niśi koṣṇaṃ payo'nupānaṃ ca sarpiḥ pibet| mūrdhetyādi| mastiṣkaḥ śirobastiviśeṣaḥ, sa ca snehapicuplotādinā'vacāryate| śatapākaṃ vātavyādhipaṭhitam| balātailaṃ mūḍhagarbhoktaṃ, anye tu balātailameva śatapākaṃ kṛtvopayujyata iti vyākhyānayanti||12||-

kaṇṭakārīmajākṣīre paktvā kṣīreṇa tena ca ||13||
vipacet kukkuṭavasāṃ karṇayostatprapūraṇam |14|

kaṇṭakārīmityādi| kaṇṭakārī catuṣpalapramāṇā, tadaṣṭaguṇaṃ kṣīraṃ, kṣīrāccaturguṇamudakaṃ dattvā vipacet, tena kṣīreṇa kukkuṭavasāṃ kuḍavāpramāṇāṃ pacet, tatkarṇayoḥ prapūraṇam||13||-

taṇḍulīyakamūlāni phalamaṅkolajaṃ tathā ||14||
ahiṃsrākendukānmūlaṃ saralaṃ devadāru ca |
laśunaṃ śṛṅgaveraṃ ca tathā vaṃśāvalekhanam ||15||
kalkaireṣāṃ tathā'mlaiśca pacet snehaṃ caturvidham |
vedanāyāḥ praśāntyarthaṃ hitaṃ tatkarṇapūraṇam ||16||

aparamapi pūraṇamāha- taṇḍulīyaketyādi| vaṃśāvalekhanaṃ vaṃśāccholanam| kalko dṛṣadi peṣitaḥ, sa cādravaḥ,
sa cāṣṭapalapramāṇaḥ, amlaiḥ dadhitakrasurācukramātuluṅgarasaiḥ pratyekamaṣṭāviṃśatyadhikaśatapramāṇaiḥ, snehaścaturvidhaḥ sarpistailavasāmajjasañjño dvātriṃśatpalapramāṇaḥ||14-16||

laśunārdrakaśigrūṇāṃ muraṅgyā mūlakasya ca |
kadalyāḥ svarasaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ kaduṣṇaḥ karṇapūraṇe ||17||
śṛṅgaverarasaḥ kṣaudraṃ saindhavaṃ tailameva ca |
kaduṣṇaṃ karṇayordeyametadvā vedanāpaham ||18||

laśunārdraketyādi| śigruḥ madhuśigruḥ, muraṅgī dvitīyaḥ śobhāñjanakaḥ, kaduṣṇa īṣaduṣṇaḥ| eteṣāṃ laśunādīnāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ svarasā vyastāḥ samastā vā karṇapūraṇe deyāḥ||17-18||

vaṃśāvalekhanāyukte mūtre cājāvike bhiṣak |
sarpiḥ pacettena karṇaṃ pūrayet karṇaśūlinaḥ ||19||

vaṃśetyādi| ‘mūtre cājāvike bhiṣak’ ityatra tantrāntaradarśanādviparyayeṇa sarpiḥpākaḥ kartavyaḥ; tena pūrvamāvike mūtre sarpiḥ paktavyaṃ, paścādāja iti| anye tu pūrvamāvikamūtrapakvena sarpiṣā karṇapūraṇaṃ, paścādājena mūtreṇeti viparyayaṃ vyākhyānayanti| kecit sarpiḥ pacedityatra tailaṃ pacediti paṭhanti; tanna, sarvanibandhaiḥ parihṛtatvāt||19||

mahataḥ pañcamūlasya kāṇḍamaṣṭādaśāṅgulam |
kṣaumeṇāveṣṭya saṃsicya tailenādīpayettataḥ ||20||
yattailaṃ cyavate tebhyo dhṛtebhyo bhājanopari |
jñeyaṃ taddīpikātailaṃ sadyo gṛhṇāti vedanām ||21||

dīpikātailamāha- mahata ityādi| kāṇḍaṃ khaṇḍaṃ, kṣaumeṇa atasīvastreṇa, āveṣṭya ‘tribhāgam’ iti śeṣaḥ| ghṛtebhyo ‘adhomukham’ iti śeṣaḥ| gṛhṇāti apaharati||20-21||

kuryādevaṃ bhadrakāṣṭhe kuṣṭhe kāṣṭhe ca sārale |
matimān dīpikātailaṃ karṇaśūlanibarhaṇam ||22||

kuryādevamityādi| cakārādagurvairaṇḍāgnimanthakāṣṭhāni gṛhyante||22||

arkāṅkurānamlapiṣṭāṃstailāktān lavaṇānvitān |
sannidadhyāt snuhīkāṇḍe korite tacchadāvṛte ||23||
puṭapākakramāsvinnān pīḍayedārasāgamāt |
sukhoṣṇaṃ tadrasaṃ karṇe dāpayecchūlaśāntaye ||24||
kapitthamātuluṅgāmlaśṛṅgaverarasaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
sukhoṣṇaiḥ pūrayet karṇaṃ tacchūlavinivṛttaye ||25||
karṇaṃ koṣṇena cukreṇa pūrayet karṇaśūlinaḥ |
samudraphenacūrṇena yuktyā cāpyavacūrṇayet ||26||

arkāṅkurānityādi| ayaṃ pāṭhaḥ sarvanibandhakārairanārṣīkṛtastasmānna vyākhyātaḥ||23-26||

aṣṭānāmiha mūtrāṇāṃ mūtreṇānyatamena tu |
koṣṇena pūrayet karṇaṃ karṇaśūlopaśāntaye ||27||

aṣṭānāmityādi| aṣṭānāṃ mūtrāṇāmanyatamenaikatamena||27||

mūtreṣvamleṣu vātaghne gaṇe ca kvathite bhiṣak |
paceccaturvidhaṃ snehaṃ pūraṇaṃ tacca karṇayoḥ ||28||

mūtreṣvityādi| mūtrāṇi gavādīnāmaṣṭānām| amlāni surāmastvāranālabījapūracukrāṇi| vātaghno gaṇo vidārigandhādiḥ||28||

etā eva kriyāḥ kuryāt pittaghnaiḥ pittasaṃyute |29|

vātajakarṇaroge kriyāmabhidhāya pittajakarṇaśūlacikitsāmāha- etā ityādi| etā eva dīpikādayaḥ kriyāḥ| tatra dīpikā aśvatthapatrakhallasnehasarpiṣā karṇapūraṇaṃ; kāñjikenāpi, tasya sparśane pittaharatvāt| pittasaṃyute karṇaśūle ityarthaḥ|29|-

kākolyādau daśakṣīraṃ tiktaṃ cātra hitaṃ haviḥ ||29||
kṣīravṛkṣapravāleṣu madhuke candane tathā |
kalkakvāthe paraṃ pakvaṃ śarkarāmadhukaiḥ saraiḥ ||30||

pittajakarṇaśule sāmānyacikitsāmabhidhāya viśeṣacikitsāmāha- kākolyādāvityādi| kākolyādikalkena daśaguṇakṣīraṃ ghṛtaṃ siddhaṃ hitaṃ, tathā tiktaṃ tiktadravyasaṃskṛtam; anye tu tiktaṃ kuṣṭhacikitsitoktaṃ ghṛtaṃ kathayanti| kṣīravṛkṣā nyagrodhodumbarāśvatthaplakṣagardabhāṇḍāḥ, teṣāṃ pallavakvāthena kākolyādikalkena siddhaṃ ghṛtaṃ hitam| madhuke candane tatheti pratyekaṃ yaṣṭīmadhukacandanakvāthakalkābhyāṃ siddham| śarkarāmadhukairityatra yaṣṭīmadhukadvitayaṃ, ata eva bahutvam| saraiḥ virecanadravyairityarthaḥ| jejjaṭācāryastu kṣīravṛkṣapravāleṣvityekaṃ yogaṃ kathayati, bimbīkvātha iti dvitīyaṃ; tau ca yogāvanārṣau kathayanti||29-30||

iṅgudīsarṣapasnehau sakaphe pūraṇe hitau |
tiktauṣadhānāṃ yūṣāśca svedāśca kaphanāśanāḥ ||31||

śleṣmaśūlasya cikitsāmāha- iṅgudīsarṣapetyādi| sakaphe ‘karṇaśūle’ ityadhyāhāraḥ| tiktetyādi| svedāśca kaphanāśanā iti tāpoṣmasvedā rūkṣāḥ||31||

surasādau kṛtaṃ tailaṃ pañcamūle mahatyapi |
mātuluṅgarasaḥ śuktaṃ laśunārdrakayo rasaḥ ||32||
ekaikaḥ pūraṇe pathyastailaṃ teṣvapi vā kṛtam |
tīkṣṇā mūrdhavirekāśca kavalāścātra pūjitāḥ ||33||

surasādau kṛtamityādi| tailadvitayametat| mātuluṅgarasa ityādi| mātuluṅgarasādinā ekaikenaiva siddhaṃ tailaṃ pūraṇe pathyamityarthaḥ| tīkṣṇā ityādi| tīkṣṇā mūrdhavirekā iti apāmārgabījādibhiḥ| tīkṣṇāḥ kavalāḥ pippalyādibhiḥ||32-33||

karṇaśūlavidhiḥ kṛtsnaḥ pittaghnaḥ śoṇitāvṛte |34|

śoṇitaśūlasya cikitsāmāha- karṇetyādi| kecidamuṃ pāṭhaṃ na paṭhanti|34|-

śūlapraṇādabādhiryakṣveḍānāṃ tu prakīrtitam ||34||
sāmānyato, viśeṣeṇa [2] bādhirye pūraṇaṃ śṛṇu |
gavāṃ mūtreṇa bilvāni piṣṭvā tailaṃ vipācayet ||35||
sajalaṃ ca sadugdhaṃ ca bādhirye karṇapūraṇam |36|

śūletyādi| pañjikākārastu ‘sajalaṃ ca sadugdhaṃ ca’ ityatra pādikaṃ jaladugdhābhyāṃ iti paṭhati||34-35||-

sitāmadhukabimbībhiḥ siddhaṃ vā''je payasyapi ||36||
bimbīkvāthe vimathyoṣṇaṃ śītībhūtaṃ taduddhṛtam |
punaḥ paceddaśakṣīraṃ sitāmadhukacandanaiḥ ||37||
bilvāmbugāḍhaṃ tattailaṃ bādhirye karṇapūraṇam |38|

sitāmadhuketyādi| sitāmadhukabimbībhiḥ kalkitābhiśchāgīkṣīre caturguṇe pakvaṃ tilatailamuṣṇameva bimbīkvāthe mathitvā śītībhūtaṃ pāṇinā''dāya punarapi bilvaphalakvāthe viloḍitaṃ sitaśarkarāyaṣṭīmadhuraktacandanaiḥ kalkīkṛtairdaśaguṇe kṣīre vipacet| kecid ‘bilvāmbugāḍhaṃ’ ityatra ‘bimbyambugāḍhaṃ’ iti paṭhanti||36-37||-

vakṣyate yaḥpratiśyāye vidhiḥ so'pyatra pūjitaḥ ||38||

idānīṃ bādhirye kaphānubandho'pyastītyanāgatāvekṣaṇena saṅkṣepārthamatideśena cikitsāmāha- vakṣyate ya ityādi| so'pyatra pūjita iti ‘navaṃ pratiśyāyamapāsya sarvamupācaret sarpiṣa eva pānaiḥ’ ityādinoktaḥ||38||

vātavyādhiṣu yaścokto vidhiḥ sa ca hito bhavet |39|

śuddhavātakṛte punaratītāvekṣaṇena saṅkṣepārthamatideśena nirdiśannāha- vātavyādhiṣvityādi| vātavyādhyukto vidhiḥ nivātabrahmacaryādikaḥ|39|-

karṇasrāve pūtikarṇe tathaiva kṛmikarṇake ||39||
samānaṃ karma kurvīta yogān vaiśeṣikānapi |40|

karma cikitsām||39||-

śirovirecanaṃ caiva dhūpanaṃ pūraṇaṃ tathā ||40||
pramārjanaṃ dhāvanaṃ ca vīkṣya vīkṣyāvacārayet |
rājavṛkṣāditoyena surasādigaṇena vā ||41||

kiṃ tat samānaṃ karmetyāha- śirovirecanamityādi| pramārjanaṃ picukūrcikayā karṇaproñchanaṃ, dhāvanaṃ prakṣālanam| vīkṣya vīkṣyeti pratyekaṃ karṇaprasrāvādiṣu doṣadūṣyādimalaviśeṣaṃ [3] dṛṣṭvā dṛṣṭvetyarthaḥ| rājavṛkṣāditoyenetyādi| rājavṛkṣādiḥ āragvadhādiḥ||40-41||

karṇaprakṣālanaṃ kāryaṃ cūrṇaireṣāṃ ca pūraṇam |
kvāthaṃ pañcakaṣāyaṃ tu kapittharasayojitam ||42||
karṇasrāve praśaṃsanti pūraṇaṃ madhunā saha |
sarjatvakcūrṇasaṃyuktaḥ kārpāsīphalajo rasaḥ ||43||
yojito madhunā vā'pi karṇasrāve praśasyate |
lākṣā rasāñjanaṃ sarjaścūrṇitaṃ karṇapūraṇam ||44||
saśaivalaṃ mahāvṛkṣajambvāmraprasavāyutam |
kulīrakṣaudramaṇḍūkīsiddhaṃ tailaṃ ca pūjitam ||45||

samānaṃ karmābhidhāya vaiśeṣikān yogānāha- karṇetyādi| pañcakaṣāyamasminnevādhyāye ‘tindukānyabhayā rodhraṃ samaṅgā''malakaṃ’ ityādinā paṭhiṣyati, anye tu āragvadhaśirīṣajambusarjāśvakarṇajaṃ pañcakaṣāyacūrṇaṃ kathayanti, tacca ṭīkāpañjikābhyāṃ nāṅgīkṛtam| sarjatvagityādi| kārpāsī vanakārpāsī| śaivalaṃ dūrvā, haṭhamityanye| mahāvṛkṣaḥ sehuṇḍaḥ, prasavāḥ pallavāḥ| kulīraḥ karkaṭaśṛṅgī, maṇḍūkī brāhmībhedaḥ| śaivalādikaṃ prasavaparyantaṃ kaṣāyadravyaṃ, kulīrādikaṃ ca kalkadravyam| pūjitaṃ ‘karṇasrāve karṇapūraṇam’ ityadhyāhāryam||42-45||

tindukānyabhayā rodhraṃ samaṅgā''malakaṃ madhu |
pūraṇaṃ cātra [4] pathyaṃ syātkapittharasayojitam ||46||

tindukānyabhayā rodhramityādi| samaṅgā añjalikārikā, ‘varāhakrāntā’ ityapare| idaṃ pañcakaṣāyacūrṇam||46||

rasamāmrakapitthānāṃ madhūkadhavaśālajam |
pūraṇārthaṃ praśaṃsanti tailaṃ vā tairvipācitam ||47||
priyaṅgumadhukāmbaṣṭhādhātakīśilaparṇibhiḥ |
mañjiṣṭhālodhralākṣābhiḥ kapitthasya rasena vā ||48||
pacettailaṃ tadāsrāvamavagṛhṇāti pūraṇāt |49|

rasamāmretyādi| āmrādipallavānāṃ rasaḥ svarasa eva ekaikaḥ samasto vā karṇapūraṇam, tairvipakvaṃ vā tilatailaṃ pūyasrāve pūraṇam| priyaṅgumadhukāmbaṣṭhetyādi| priyaṅgvādikalkaiḥ kapitthapallavarasena caturguṇena tilatailaṃ siddhaṃ karṇasrāvāpaham| ambaṣṭhā sāṅkuraḍā, śilā manaḥśilā, parṇī śālaparṇī||47-48||-

ghṛtaṃ rasāñjanaṃ nāryāḥ kṣīreṇa madhusaṃyutam ||49||
tatpraśastaṃ cirotthe'pi sāsrāve pūtikarṇake |
nirguṇḍīsvarasastailaṃ sindhurdhūmarajo guḍaḥ ||50||
pūraṇaḥ pūtikarṇasya śamano madhusaṃyutaḥ |51|

idānīṃ pūtikarṇasya viśeṣacikitsāṃ yogadvayenāha- ghṛṣṭamityādi||49-50||-

kṛmikarṇakanāśārthaṃ kṛmighnaṃ yojayedvidhim ||51||

kṛmikarṇe vaiśeṣikamāha- kṛmikarṇakanāśārthamityādi| kṛmighno vidhiḥ krimipratiṣedhe [5] vakṣyamāṇaḥ||51||

vārtākudhūmaśca hitaḥ sārṣapasneha eva ca |52|

aparamapi viśeṣacikitsitamāha- vārtākudhūma ityādi| pariṇataśuṣkavārtākudhūmasya pānaṃ karṇadhūpanaṃ ca, sārṣapasnehena karṇapūraṇaṃ, tantrāntaroktatvāt|52|-

kṛmighnaṃ haritālena gavāṃ mūtrayutena ca ||52||
gugguloḥ karṇadaurgandhye dhūpanaṃ śreṣṭhamucyate |
chardanaṃ dhūmapānaṃ ca kavalasya ca dhāraṇam ||53||

kṛmikṛtadaurgandhyacikitsāmāha- kṛmighnamityādi| kṛmighnaṃ viḍaṅgasāracūrṇaṃ, tacca haritālacūrṇena yutaṃ gomūtreṇa pūraṇaṃ, guggulunā dhūpanam| chardanamityādi| kecicchardanādikaṃ kriyātritayaṃ trayāṇāmāsrāvādīnāṃ hitamāhuḥ; tanna, yataḥ kṛmikṛtadaurgandhyasānnidhyādvidehādiṣvasya [6] darśanād daurgandhya eva chardanāditritayaṃ hitam||52-53||

karṇakṣveḍe hitaṃ tailaṃ sārṣapaṃ caiva pūraṇam |
vidradhau cāpi kurvīta vidradhyuktaṃ cikitsitam ||54||
