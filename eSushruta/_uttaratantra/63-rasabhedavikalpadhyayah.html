--- title: "63. rasabhedavikalpādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 63 order: 63 ---

athāto rasabhedavikalpamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

atha bhūtavidyāṃ parisamāpya aupadravike tantrabhūṣaṇānāṃ rasabhedādīnāmadhyāyānāṃ sūcitatvāt tantrabhūṣaṇānārabdhukāmastatroktakramānusāreṇa rasabhedavikalpādhyāyamāha- athāta ityādi| athāta ityādi pūrvavadvyākhyeyam [1] | rasabhedavikalpamiti rasā madhurādayaḥ pūrvaṃ vyākhyātāḥ, rasānāṃ bhedena dvitrikādibhedena vikalpo vibhajanaṃ yasmin sa tathā, athavā rasabhedānāṃ vikalpo doṣabhedavaśādavacāraṇaṃ yasmin sa tathā, tam||1-2||

doṣāṇāṃ pañcadaśadhā prasaro'bhihitastu yaḥ |
triṣaṣṭyā rasābhedānāṃ tatprayojanamucyate ||3||

rasabhedakathane prayojanamāha- doṣāṇāmitādi| yaḥ pañcadaśaprakāro vraṇapraśnādhyāye doṣāṇāṃ prasara uktaḥ, tadrasabhedānāṃ triṣaṣṭyāḥ prayojanamucyata iti sambandhaḥ| atra pañcadaśadhetyupalakṣaṇaṃ, tena doṣabhedānāṃ triṣaṣṭirapi gṛhyate| ayamabhiprāyaḥ- triṣaṣṭiprakārāṇāmapi doṣabhedānāmupayogārthaṃ [2] rasabhedā uktāḥ||3||

avidagdhā vidagdhāśca bhidyante te triṣaṣṭidhā |
rasabhedatriṣaṣṭiṃ tu vīkṣya vīkṣyāvacārayet ||4||

kīdṛśā rasāstriṣaṣṭibhedān [3] yāntītyāha- avidagdhā ityādi| avidagdhā asaṃyuktā ekākinaḥ samavāyato bhidyanta ityarthaḥ| vidagdhāḥ saṃyogataḥ samavāyataśca saṃyuktā rasāntarasaṃyogādbhidyante| vidagdhaśabdaḥ saṃyukte vartate, dhātūnāmanekārthatvāt| bhidyante ekaikenānugamanādbhedaṃ yāntītyarthaḥ| te rasāḥ| tatra yathāsambhavaṃ kecit saṃyogataḥ kecit samavāyataśca [4] bhidyante| saṃyuktā yathā- badarakapitthaphalādikaṃ madhurāmlaṃ(1), uṣṭrīkṣīrorabhramāṃsādikaṃ madhuralavaṇaṃ(2), kukkuraśṛgālamāṃsādikaṃ [5] madhurakaṭukaṃ(3), śrīvāsasarjarasādikaṃ madhuratiktaṃ(4) tailadhanvanaphalādikaṃ madhurakaṣāyaṃ(5); ūṣakādikam amlalavaṇaṃ(6), cukrādikam amlakaṭukaṃ(7), surādikam amlatiktaṃ(8), hastinīdadhiśukamāṃsādikam amlakaṣāyaṃ(9); trapusīsādikaṃ lavaṇatiktaṃ(10), gomūtrasvarjikādikaṃ lavaṇakaṭukaṃ(11), samudraphenādikaṃ lavaṇakaṣāyaṃ(12); karpūrajātīphalādikaṃ tiktakaṭukaṃ(13), lavalīphalahastinīghṛtādikaṃ tiktakaṣāyaṃ(14); bhallātakamajjāharitālādikaṃ kaṭukaṣāyam(15); evaṃ dvirasabhedāḥ [6] pañcadaśadhā darśitāḥ| ataḥ paraṃ rasatritayabhedā [7] vakṣyante- hastimāṃsādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇaṃ(1), śalyakamāṃsādikaṃ madhurāmlakaṭukaṃ(2), godhūmotthasurādikaṃ madhurāmlatiktakaṃ(3), mastutakrādikaṃ [8] madhurāmlakaṣāyaṃ(4), kāṇakapotamāṃsādikaṃ madhuralavaṇakaṭukaṃ(5), śambūkādimāṃsaṃ [9] madhuralavaṇatiktaṃ(6), padmakandādikaṃ guḍasaṃyuktaṃ madhuralavaṇakaṣāyaṃ(7), tṛṇaśūnyāphalaśuṣkakustumbaryādikaṃ madhurakaṭutiktakaṃ(8), godhāmāṃsairaṇḍatailādikaṃ madhurakaṭukaṭukaṣāyaṃ(9), guḍūcīśākhāmṛgāmiṣatuvarakatailādikaṃ madhuratiktakaṣāyaṃ(10), raupyaśilājatvādikam amlalavaṇakaṭukaṃ(11), hastimūtrādikam amlalavaṇatiktaṃ(12), saromakaṃ hastinīdadhyādi amlalavaṇakaṣāyaṃ(13), maricasaṃskṛtasurādikam [10] amlakaṭutiktakaṃ(14), amlavetasādikam amlakaṭukaṣāyaṃ(15), kīramāṃsayutasurādikam amlatiktakaṣāyaṃ(16), avimūtrādikaṃ lavaṇakaṭutiktaṃ(17), aruṣkaraṃ saromakaṃ lavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaṃ(18), samudraphenāvikaṃ lavaṇatiktakaṣāyaṃ(19), kṛṣṇāgarusuradārusnehādikaṃ [11] kaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(20); evaṃ trikabhedā viṃśatirdarśitāḥ| ataḥ paraṃ catuḥsaṃyogāḥ pañcadaśaprakārā vakṣyante- gomūtrānvitaśilājatuprabhṛtikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukaṃ(1), gomūtraikaśaphakṣīrādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇatiktakaṃ(2) saindhavānvitatakrādikaṃ [12] madhurāmlalavaṇakaṣāyaṃ(3), laśunānvitaṃ surādikaṃ madhurāmlakaṭutiktaṃ(4), kāñjikānvitairaṇḍatailādi khadirānvitaśilāhvādikaṃ ca madhurāmlakaṭukaṣāyaṃ(5), udumbarānvitaṃ yavāsaśarkarādikaṃ madhurāmlatiktakaṣāyaṃ(6), vārtākaphalādikaṃ madhuralavaṇatiktakaṭukaṃ(7), gomūtrānvitatailādikaṃ madhuralavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaṃ(8), tilaguggulvādikaṃ madhurakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(9), samudraphenaśarkarācitrakānvitabadarādi [13] madhuralavaṇatiktakaṣāyaṃ(10), suvarcalānvitahastinīdadhyādi [14] kṛtasurādikam amlalavaṇakaṭutiktaṃ(11), sauvarcalānvitahastinīdadhyādikam amlalavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaṃ(12), audbhidalavaṇānvitaṃ śukamāṃsādikam amlalavaṇatiktakaṣāyaṃ(13), bālamūlakahastinīdadhyādikam amlakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(14), saromakaṃ bālabilvādikaṃ lavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(15); evaṃ catuṣkarasasaṃyogāḥ pañcadaśa kathitāḥ| ataḥ paraṃ pañcarasasaṃyogāḥ ṣaḍ vakṣyante- āmakaramardānvitaṃ bhṛṣṭavārtākaphalādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇatiktakaṣāyaṃ(1), kaṭutrayayavakṣārānvitatakrādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukaṣāyam(2), audbhidānvitatakrādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇatiktakaṣāyaṃ(3), harītakīdhātrīphalādikaṃ madhurāmlakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(4), rasonādikaṃ madhuralavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(5), bhallātakaraupyaśilājatumiśritanimbādikam amlalavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaṃ(6); evaṃ pañcarasasaṃyogāḥ ṣaḍ darśitāḥ| ataḥ paraṃ ṣaḍrasaṃ vakṣyate- eṇamāṃsādikaṃ madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyam| ataḥ paramekaikarasānāha- santānikāgodugdhādikaṃ madhuraṃ(1) āmakaramardādikam amlaṃ(2), romakādikaṃ lavaṇaṃ(3), cavyādikaṃ kaṭukaṃ(4), nimbaparpaṭādikaṃ tiktaṃ(5), padmanyagrodhādyaṅkurādikaṃ kaṣāyaṃ(6); evaṃ ṣaṭ kathitāḥ| anena prakāreṇa triṣaṣṭiḥ kathitāḥ| kārtikakuṇḍastvamuṃ pāṭhamanyathā āpātanikāṃ kṛtvā vyākhyānayati; tadyathā- doṣāṇāṃ pañcadaśadhā prasaro bhedaḥ, tasya bahubhistriṣaṣṭirasabhedaiḥ saha prayogo durghaṭaḥ, ato doṣabhedānāmapi bahutvamāha- avidagdhā ityādi| avidagdhā aprakupitāḥ; vidagdhāḥ kupitā doṣāḥ; yadyasmin pāṭhe doṣāṇāṃ triṣaṣṭibhedā uktāḥ, tarhi pūrvapāṭhe pañcadaśadhā prasaraḥ kimityuktaḥ? satyaṃ, kupitadoṣasyātyantacikitsanīyatvāpādanārtham; anyat granthagauravabhayāt parityaktam||4||h

ekaikenānugamanaṃ [15] bhāgaśo yadudīritam |
doṣāṇāṃ [16] , tatra matimān triṣaṣṭiṃ tu prayojayet ||5||

pūrvamavidagdhā vidagdhāśca rasā ekaikenānugamanādbhidyanta ityuktaṃ, tadeva smārayati ekaikenānugamanamityādi| bhāgaśaḥ aṃśāṃśatayā| rasānāmekaikenānugamanaṃ pravartanaṃ yadudīritamasminnadhyāye bhaṇitaṃ, tadvaśātte rasāstriṣaṣṭidhā bhidyanta iti pūrvavākyena saha sambandhaḥ| tāṃ ca rasabhedatriṣaṣṭiṃ, matimān ūhāpohavidvaidyaḥ, tatra doṣabhedeṣu prayojayet| matimāniti padāt yādṛśā eva doṣāṇāṃ hīnādhikabhāvena bhedāstādṛśā eva rasabhedā yojyā iti sūcayati| anye tu ‘evamevānugamanaṃ’ iti paṭhitvā doṣāṇāmevaṃ bhāgaśoṃ'śāṃśabalavikalpenaikaṃ pratyekasyānugamanaṃ doṣabhedīye yadudīritaṃ tatra rasabhedatriṣaṣṭiṃ yojayediti| yadyapi doṣabhedīye yadvakṣyate iti sundaraṃ natu yadudīritamiti, tathā'pi bhāvini bhūtavadupacārāddoṣabhedīye yadudīritamiti kṛtam| apare'trāmuṃ pāṭhamanyathā paṭhitvā bahu jalpanti, tacca granthagauravabhayācchiṣyabuddhivyākulahetutvācca parityaktam||5||

yathākramapravṛttānāṃ dvikeṣu madhuro rasaḥ |
pañcānukramate yogānamlaścatura eva tu ||6||
trīṃścānugacchati raso lavaṇaḥ kaṭuko dvayam |
tiktaḥ kaṣāyamanveti te dvikā daśa pañca ca ||7||
tadyathā- madhurāmlaḥ(1), madhuralavaṇaḥ(2), madhurakaṭukaḥ(3), madhuratiktaḥ(4), madhurakaṣāyaḥ(5), etepañcānukrāntā madhureṇa; amlalavaṇaḥ(1), amlakaṭukaḥ(2), amlatiktaḥ(3), amlakaṣāyaḥ(4), ete catvāro'nukrāntā amlena; lavaṇakaṭukaḥ(1), lavaṇatiktaḥ(2), lavaṇakaṣāyaḥ(3), ete trayo'nukrāntā lavaṇena; kaṭutiktaḥ(1), kaṭukaṣāyaḥ(2), dvāvetāvanukrāntau kaṭukena; tiktakaṣāyaḥ(1) eka evānukrāntastiktena; evamete [17] pañcadaśa dvikasaṃyogā vyākhyātāḥ ||8||

idānīṃ rasabhedatriṣaṣṭiṃ granthenaiva vivṛṇvannāha- yathākramamityādi| yathākramapravṛttānāmiti madhurādikramapravṛttānāṃ rasānāmityarthaḥ| dvikeṣu dvikasaṃyogeṣu| anukramate anugacchati, anugato bhavatītyarthaḥ| daśa pañceti pañcadaśetyarthaḥ| yadyapi sūtrasthāne rasaviśeṣavijñānīyādhyāye pañcadaśa dvikā ityādinā dvikasaṃyogādīnāṃ saṅkhyā uktāḥ, tathā'pi punaratra saṅkhyākaraṇaṃ niyamārtham| tena iyanta eva rasabhedā gaṇanīyā na tantrāntaroktāstaratamayogādijā ityarthaḥ| teṣāmeva dvikānāṃ pañcadaśasaṅkhyākānāmudāharaṇānyāha- tadyathetyādi| madhurāmlau vidyete yatra saṃyoge sa madhurāmlaḥ| evaṃ śeṣeṣvapi jñeyam||6-8||

trikān vakṣyāmaḥ-
ādau prayujyamānastu madhuro daśa gacchati |
ṣaḍamlo lavaṇastasmādardhamekaṃ tathā [18] kaṭuḥ ||9||
tadyathā- madhurāmlalavaṇaḥ(1), madhurāmlakaṭukaḥ(2), madhurāmlatiktaḥ(3), madhurāmlakaṣāyaḥ(4), madhuralavaṇakaṭukaḥ(5), madhuralavaṇatiktāḥ(6), madhuralavaṇakaṣāyaḥ(7), madhurakaṭukatiktaḥ(8), madhurakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(9), madhuratiktakaṣāyaḥ(10), evameṣāṃ daśānāṃ trikasaṃyogānāmādau madhuraḥ prayujyate; amlalavaṇakaṭukaḥ(1), amlalavaṇatiktaḥ(2), amlalavaṇakaṣāyaḥ(3), amlakaṭutiktaḥ(4), amlakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(5), amlatiktakaṣāyaḥ(6), evameṣāṃ ṣaṇṇāmādāvamlaḥ prayujyate; lavaṇakaṭutiktaḥ(1), lavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(2), lavaṇatiktakaṣāyaḥ(3), evameṣāṃ trayāṇāmādau lavaṇaḥ prayujyate; kaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(1), evamekasyādau kaṭukaḥ prayujyate; evamete trikasaṃyogā viṃśatirvyākhyātāḥ ||10||

ādāvityādi| daśeti daśayogānityarthaḥ| tasmādardhamiti trīnityarthaḥ| teṣāmeva trikānāṃ viṃśatisaṅkhyākānāmudāharaṇānyāha- madhurāmletyādi| madhurāmlalavaṇā vidyante yatra sa madhurāmlalavaṇaḥ| evaṃ śeṣeṣvapi vyākhyeyam||9-10||

catuṣkān vakṣyāmaḥ |
catuṣkarasasaṃyogānmadhuro daśa gacchati |
caturo'mlo'nugacchecca lavaṇastvekameva tu ||11||
madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukaḥ(1), madhurāmlalavaṇatiktaḥ(2), madhurāmlalavaṇakaṣāyaḥ(3), madhurāmlakaṭukatiktaḥ(4), madhurāmlakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(5), madhurāmlatiktakaṣāyaḥ(6), madhuralavaṇakaṭukatiktaḥ(7), madhuralavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(8), madhuralavaṇatiktakaṣāyaḥ(9), madhurakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(10), evameṣāṃ daśānāmādau madhuraḥ prayujyate; amlalavaṇakaṭutiktaḥ(1), amlalavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(2), amlalavaṇatiktakaṣāyaḥ(3), amlakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(4), evameṣāṃ caturṇāmādāvamlaḥ; lavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(1), evamekasyādau lavaṇaḥ; evamete catuṣkarasasaṃyogāḥ pañcadaśa kīrtitāḥ ||12||

catuṣkānityādi| daśeti ‘yogān’ iti śeṣaḥ| teṣāṃ catuṣkarasasaṃyoge pañcadaśasaṅkhyākānāmudāharaṇānyāha- madhurāmletyādi| madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukā vidyante yatra yoge sa madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukaḥ; evaṃ śeṣeṣvapi||11-12||

pañcakān vakṣyāmaḥ |
pañcakān pañca madhura ekamamlastu gacchati ||13||
madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭutiktaḥ(1), madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭukaṣāyaḥ(2), madhurāmlalavaṇatiktakaṣāyaḥ(3), madhurāmlakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(4), madhuralavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(5), evameṣāṃ pañcānāmādau madhuraḥ prayujyate; amlalavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ(1), evamekasyādāvamlaḥ; evamete ṣaṭ pañcakasaṃyogā vyākhyātāḥ ||14||

pañcakānityādi| pañcakāniti pañcarasasamūhān| pañceti pañcasaṅkhyākān ‘yogān’ iti śeṣaḥ| teṣāmeva ṣaṇṇāmudāharaṇānyāha- madhurāmletyādi||13-14||

ṣaṭkamekaṃ vakṣyāmaḥ; ekastu ṣaṭkasaṃyogaḥ- madhurāmlalavaṇakaṭutiktakaṣāyaḥ; eṣa eka eva ṣaṭsaṃyogaḥ ||15||

tasya ṣaṭkasyodāharaṇamāha- madhurāmletyādi||15||

ekaikaśca ṣaḍrasā bhavanti- madhuraḥ(1), amlaḥ(2), lavaṇaḥ(3), kaṭukaḥ(4), tiktaḥ(5), kaṣāyaḥ(6), iti ||16||

eṣāmudāharaṇānyāha- madhuretyādi||16||

bhavanti [19] cātra-
eṣā triṣaṣṭirvyākhyātā rasānāṃ [20] rasacintakaiḥ |
doṣabhedatriṣaṣṭyāṃ tu prayoktavyā vicakṣaṇaiḥ ||17||

upasaṃhāramāha- bhavati cātretyādi| anuraso hīnarasaḥ| doṣabhedatriṣaṣṭyāṃ doṣabhedīyādhyāye vakṣyamāṇāyām| yadyapi parasparaṃ bhinnāḥ santo rasā bahavo bhavanti, tathā'pi, ṣaṭtvaṃ nātivartante; yathā vātādidoṣā bahudhā bhinnā api tritvaṃ na tyajanti tathā rasā api ṣaṭtvaṃ nātivartante||17||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantre tantrabhūṣaṇādhyāyeṣu rasabhedavikalpādhyāyo
nāma (prathamaḥ, āditaḥ) triṣaṣṭitamo'dhyāyaḥ ||63||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ
suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantre triṣaṣṭitamo'dhyāyaḥ||63||

6. `dvirasayonayaḥ' iti pā.|
7. `rasatrikasaṃyogo vakṣyate' iti pā.|
8. `siñcitikāphalādikaṃ' iti pā.|
9. `kaṭukāsantkṛtaguḍādikaṃ' iti pā.|
