--- title: "33. andhapūtanāpratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 33 order: 33 ---

athāto'ndhapūtanāpratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

pūtanāpratiṣedhānantaraṃ pūtanāsāmānyādandhapūtanāpratiṣedhārambho yujyata ityāha- athāta ityādi||1-2||

tiktakadrumapatrāṇāṃ kāryaḥ kvātho'vasecane |3|

tiktaketyādi| tiktakadrumā nimbādayaḥ, tatpatrakvāthena pariṣekaḥ kāryaḥ|3|-

surā sauvīrakaṃ kuṣṭhaṃ haritālaṃ manaḥśilā ||3||
tathā sarjarasaścaiva tailārthamupadiśyate |
pippalyaḥ pippalīmūlaṃ vargo madhurako madhu ||4||
śālaparṇī bṛhatyau ca ghṛtārthamupadiśyate |5|

suretyādi| sauvīrakaṃ virecanādhyāyopadiṣṭakāñjikaprakāraḥ| atra taile surāsauvīrake tailāt pratyekaṃ caturguṇe, kuṣṭhādīni kalkadravyāṇi caturthāṃśāni| pippalya ityādi| vargo madhurakaḥ kākolyādiḥ, vargagrahaṇaṃ yathālābhaniṣedhārthaṃ, pippalyādīnāṃ kalkakvāthābhyāṃ ghṛtaṃ vipācya śītībhūte ghṛte madhu kṣipet| idaṃ ghṛtaṃ pānāya||3-4||-

sarvagandhaiḥ pradehaśca gātreṣvakṣṇośca śītalaiḥ ||5||

sarvetyādi| pradeho lepaḥ| śītalairanyairapi dravyaiḥ||5||

purīṣaṃ kaukkuṭaṃ keśāṃścarma sarpatvacaṃ tathā |
jīrṇāṃ ca bhikṣusaṅghāṭīṃ dhūmanāyopakalpayet ||6||

purīṣaṃ kaukkuṭamiti kukkuṭapurīṣaṃ, keśāṃścarma ca kukkuṭasya, keśāścarma ca mānuṣasyetyeke; kecit carmeti sarpasya vadanti, tena carma tvak kañculikā sarpasya| jīrṇāṃ ca bhikṣusaṅghāṭīmiti bhikṣuratra śākyabhikṣurbauddhākhyaḥ parivrājakaśca, tayorjīrṇasaṅghāṭī jīrṇavastraṃ; tadekadeśo gṛhyate, ekadeśe'pi samudāyapravṛtteḥ; bhikṣuratra śākyabhikṣureveti jejjaṭaḥ||6||

kukkuṭīṃ markaṭīṃ śimbīmanantāṃ cāpi dhārayet |
māṃsamāmaṃ tathā pakvaṃ śoṇitaṃ ca catuṣpathe ||7||
nivedyamantaśca gṛhe śiśo rakṣānimittataḥ |
śiśośca snapanaṃ kuryāt sarvagandhodakaiḥ śubhaiḥ ||8||
karālā piṅgalā muṇḍā kaṣāyāmbaravāsinī |
devī bālamimaṃ prītā saṃrakṣatvandhapūtanā ||9||

kukkuṭīmityādi| kukkuṭī sphaṭikaracitakukkuṭāṇḍasadṛśakandā kukkuṭīśarīravat kusumacitrā vallī| markaṭī kapikacchuḥ, markaṭamūlītyanye, śukaśimbītyapare| anantā sārivā| antaśca khaṇḍagṛhābhyantare| śiśośceti cakārāt dhātryāśca snapanam| sarvagandhā elādikāḥ| devī prītā satī imaṃ bālaṃ rakṣatviti sambandhaḥ||7-9||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kumāratantre'ndhapūtanāpratiṣedho nāma (saptamo'dhyāyaḥ, āditaḥ) trayastriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||33||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kaumārabhṛtyatantre'ndhapūtanāpratiṣedho
nāma trayastriṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||33||
