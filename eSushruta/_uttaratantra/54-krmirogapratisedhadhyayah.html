--- title: "54. kṛmirogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 54 order: 54 ---

athātaḥ kṛmirogapratiṣedhamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
ajīrṇādhyaśanāsātmyaviruddhamalināśanaiḥ |
avyāyāmadivāsvapnagurvatisnigdhaśītalaiḥ ||3||
māṣapiṣṭānnavidalabisaśālūkaserukaiḥ |
parṇaśākasurāśuktadadhikṣīraguḍekṣubhiḥ ||4||
palalānūpapiśitapiṇyākapṛthukādibhiḥ |
svādvamladravapānaiśca śleṣmā pittaṃ ca kupyati ||5||
kṛmīn bahuvidhākārān karoti vividhāśrayān |
āmapakvāśaye teṣāṃ kaphaviḍjanmanāṃ punaḥ |
dhamanyāṃ raktajānāṃ ca prasavaḥ prāyaśaḥ smṛtaḥ ||6||

keṣāñcit kṛmīṇāṃ sāmānyaṃ hetumāha- ajīrṇetyādi| ajīrṇam āmavidagdhādibhedabhinnaṃ, adhyaśanam ajīrṇabhojanaṃ asātmyaṃyat sevitaṃ sukhārthaṃ na sampadyate, viruddhaṃ hitāhitīyoktaṃ, malinaṃ samalam| piṣṭānnaṃ taṇḍulapiṣṭādi, vidalaṃ makuṣṭhādi, bisaṃ bisāṇḍakaṃ, śālūḥ padmakandaḥ| parṇaśākaṃ patraśākaṃ, sūktaṃ sandhānakaviśeṣaḥ| palalaḥ tilakalkaḥ , ānūpapiśitam ānūpamāṃsaṃ, piṇyākaṃ yantrapīḍitatilakhalī, 'pītā' iti loke, pṛthukaḥ cipiṭakaḥ ādiśabdādevamprakārā anye'pi| bahuvidhākārān vividhākārān| vividhāśrayāniti nānāprakārāśrayānityarthaḥ| prasava utpattiḥ| prāyograhaṇādanyatrāpi sambhavo jñātavyaḥ| kecidatra ajīrṇādhyaśanādikaṃ hetugaṇaṃ na paṭhanti, teṣāṃ ca hetvavagatirvakṣyamāṇāt 'kṣīrāṇi māṃsāni' ityādipratiṣedhavidhānāt; mayā tu sukhabodhārthaṃ hetugaṇaḥ paṭhitaḥ| apare tu ‘ajīrṇabhojī madhurāmlanityo dravapriyaḥ piṣṭaguḍopabhoktā| vyāyāmajātāni [1] ca varjyamānaḥ svapnādivān vai labhate krimīnnā' iti paṭhanti||1-6||

viṃśateḥ kṛmijātīnāṃ trividhaḥ sambhavaḥ smṛtaḥ |
purīṣakapharaktāni, tāsāṃ vakṣyāmi vistaram ||7||

idānīṃ viṃśatisaṅkhyānāmapi kṛmijātīnāṃ kāraṇatraividhyamāha- viṃśaterityādi| jātigrahaṇaṃ kṛmīṇāmānantyakhyāpanārthaṃ; taccānantyaṃ viṃśatyāmevāvaruddhaṃ, ato viṃśatisaṅkhyākaraṇaṃ sārthakam| sambhavaḥ kāraṇam| kastrividhaḥ sambhava ityāha- purīṣetyādi| nanu, ajīrṇādhyaśanādayo hetava uktāḥ , kathaṃ purīṣādayaśca ucyante? satyaṃ, ajīrṇādayaḥ purīṣādīnāṃ hetavaḥ, purīṣādayaśca kṛmīṇāmiti| yadyapi kṛmyārambhakā na purīṣādayaḥ , tathā'pyupacārāt purīṣādīnāmapi kṛmyārambhakatvam| purīṣādyadhiṣṭhitā doṣāḥ purīṣādi, ghṛtādidagdhavat; etenājīrṇādibhiḥ prakupitā doṣāḥ purīṣādīnyadhiṣṭhāya kṛmīn janayanti tāsāṃ kṛmijātīnām| kecit ‘vistaraṃ’ ityatra ‘lakṣaṇaṃ’ iti pāṭhāntaraṃ savyākhyānaṃ paṭhanti; tacca śiṣyabuddhivyākulatvahetutvāt parityaktam||7||

ajavā vijavāḥ [2] kipyāścipyā gaṇḍūpadāstathā |
cūravo dvimukhāścaiva jñeyāḥ sapta purīṣajāḥ ||8||

purīṣajakṛmināmānyāha- ajavā ityādi| ajavādayo dvimukhāntāḥ sapta purīṣajāḥ kṛmayaḥ||8||

śvetāḥ sūkṣmāstudantyete gudaṃ pratisaranti ca |
teṣāmevāpare pucchaiḥ pṛthavaśca bhavanti hi ||9||
śūlāgnimāndyapāṇḍutvaviṣṭambhabalasaṅkṣayāḥ |
prasekārucihṛdrogaviḍbhedāstu purīṣajaiḥ ||10||

purīṣajānāṃ lakṣaṇamāha- śvetā ityādi| ya ete purīṣajāḥ kṛmayaḥ śvetāḥ sūkṣmāśca bhavanti, te bāhulyenāddaṣṭavaśādgudaṃ tudanti pratisaranti ca| teṣāmeva saptānāṃ madhye apare pucchaiḥ pṛthavo bhavanti| purīṣajaiḥ saptabhiḥ kṛmibhiḥ śūlāgnimāndyādayo bhavanti| viṣṭambho guḍaguḍāśabdaḥ| praseko lālāsrāvaḥ| viḍbhedaḥ dravapurīṣatā||9-10||

raktā gaṇḍūpadā dīrghā gudakaṇḍūnipātinaḥ |
śūlāṭopaśakṛdbhedapaktināśakarāśca te ||11||

teṣāmeva saptānāṃ purīṣajānāṃ madhye gaṇḍūpadānāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ karma cāha- raktā ityādi| paktināśakarā agnināśakarāḥ, kāraṇe [3] kāryopacārāt||11||

darbhapuṣpā mahāpuṣpāḥ pralūnāścipiṭāstathā |
pipīlikā dāruṇāśca kaphakopasamudbhavāḥ ||12||

kaphakṛmināmānyāha- darbhapuṣpā ityādi| darbhapuṣpādikā dāruṇaparyantāḥ kaphakopajāḥ ṣaṭ kṛmayaḥ||12||

romaśā romamūrdhānaḥ sapucchāḥ śyāvamaṇḍalāḥ |
rūḍhadhānyāṅkurākārāḥ śuklāste tanavastathā ||13||

teṣāmākṛtimāha- romaśā ityādi| romaśāḥ sarvato romānvitāḥ| romamūrdhānaḥ saromamastakāḥ| śyāvamaṇḍalāḥ śyāmamaṇḍalayuktāḥ||13||

majjādā netraleḍhārastāluśrotrabhujastathā |14|

teṣāmeva karmaviśeṣeṇa sañjñāntaramāha- majjādā ityādi|14|-

śirohṛdrogavamathupratiśyāyakarāśca te ||14||

teṣāṃ sarveṣāmeva karmāṇyāha- śirohṛdrogetyādi| rogaśabdaḥ śirohṛdbhyāṃ sambadhyate, tena śirorogo hṛdrogaśca, vamathuḥ chardiḥ| cakārāt anyānapi kaphajavyādhīn kurvanti| te kaphajāḥ kṛmayaḥ||14||

keśaromanakhādāśca dantādāḥ kikkiśāstathā |
kuṣṭhajāḥ saparīsarpā jñeyāḥ śoṇitasambhavāḥ ||15||

raktajakṛmināmānyāha- keśarometyādi| keśādādyāḥ parīsarpāntāḥ sapta raktajāḥ kṛmayaḥ||15||

te saraktāśca kṛṣṇāśca snigdhāśca pṛthavastathā |16|

teṣāmākṛtimāha- ta ityādi| te raktajāḥ kṛmayaḥ| pṛthavaḥ sthūlāḥ|16|-

raktādhiṣṭhānajān prāyo vikārān janayanti te ||16||

teṣāṃ karmāṇyāha- raktādhiṣṭhānajānityādi| raktādhiṣṭhānajān vyādhisamuddeśīyoktān kuṣṭhavisarpapiḍakādīn||16||

māṣapiṣṭānnavidalaparṇaśākaiḥ purīṣajāḥ |
māṃsamāṣaguḍakṣīradadhitailaiḥ kaphodbhavāḥ ||17||
viruddhājīrṇaśākādyaiḥ śoṇitotthā bhavanti hi |
jvaro vivarṇatā śūlaṃ hṛdrogaḥ sadanaṃ bhramaḥ ||18||
bhaktadveṣo'tisāraśca sañjātakṛmilakṣaṇam |19|

purīṣajādikṛmīṇāṃ sāmānyakāraṇamabhidhāyedānīṃ viśeṣakāraṇamāha- māṣapiṣṭānnavidaletyādi| piṣṭānnaṃ taṇḍulādipiṣṭajam||17-18||-

dṛśyāstrayodaśādyāstu kṛmīṇāṃ parikīrtitāḥ ||19||
keśādādyāstvadṛśyāste dvāvādyau parivarjayet |20|

idānīṃ kṛmīṇāṃ dṛśyādṛśyatvena sādhyāsādhyatvena ca vibhāgakaraṇārthamāha- dṛśyā ityādi| ādyāstrayodaśeti ajavādyā dāruṇāntā ityarthaḥ| keśādādyā iti keśādādyāḥ parīsarpāntā raktajāḥ kṛmaya ityarthaḥ| dvāvādyāviti raktajāpekṣayaiva ādyau, tena keśādaromādāvityarthaḥ| tathā coktam- “asādhyau dvau tu vijñeyau romakeśādasañjñitau” iti||19||-

eṣāmanyatamaṃ jñātvā jighāṃsuḥ snigdhamāturam ||20||
surasādivipakvena sarpiṣā vāntamāditaḥ |
virecayettīkṣṇatarairyogai ... |21|

nidānamabhidhāyedānīṃ cikitsāvasaraḥ- eṣāmanyatamamityādi| eṣāṃ krimīṇāṃ madhye anyatamamekatamaṃ jighāṃsuḥ, snigdhamāturaṃ surasādigaṇavipakvena sarpiṣā snigdhamāturaṃ jñātvā, āditaśca vāntaṃ tīkṣṇatarairyogaiḥ kaphaghnairvirecanakalpoktairvirecayediti piṇḍārthaḥ| ‘duṣṭe ca koṣṭhe kṛmibhirmanuṣyaṃ na vāmayet’ ityādinā kṛmiyuktasya puṃso vamananiṣedhaḥ, tathā'pi kṛmiyuktasya prathamāvasthāyāṃ vamanaṃ śastam, ato vāntamādita ityuktam||20||-

... rāsthāpayecca tam ||21||
yavakolakulatthānāṃ surasādergaṇasya ca |
viḍaṅgasnehayuktena kvāthena lavaṇena ca ||22||

virekānantaraṃ kiṃ kuryādityāha- āsthāpayedityādi| tam āturaṃ yavakolādīnāṃ kvāthena tathā surasādigaṇasya kvāthena viḍaṅgasādhitasnehayuktena saindhavalavaṇānvitenāsthāpayediti piṇḍārthaḥ| gaṇasya ceti cakārāt surasādikalko'pi grāhyaḥ||21-22||

pratyāgate nirūhe tu naraṃ snātaṃ sukhāmbunā |
yuñjyāt kṛmighnairaśanaistataḥ śīghraṃ bhiṣagvaraḥ ||23||
snehenoktena cainaṃ tu yojayet snehabastinā |24|

āsthāpanānantaraṃ kiṃ kuryādityāha- pratyāgata ityādi| pratyāgate vyāghuṭite patite ityarthaḥ| sukhāmbunā uṣṇodakena| kṛmighnairaśanaiḥ kṛmighnadravyasādhitaiḥ svabhāvataḥ kṛmighnaiśca| snehenoktena viḍaṅgasādhitasnehena| snehabastinā anuvāsanena||23||-

tataḥ śirīṣakiṇihīrasaṃ kṣaudrayutaṃ pibet ||24||

ato'nantaraṃ kṛtānnasaṃsargaḥ kiṃ kuryādityāha- tata ityādi| tata iti anuvāsanānantaram| kiṇihī kaṭabhī||24||

kevūkasvarasaṃ vā'pi pūrvavattīkṣṇabhojanaḥ |25|

pānāntaramāha- kevūketyādi| kevūkaḥ ‘kesura [4] ’ iti nāmnā prasiddhaḥ| pūrvavaditi kṣaudrayutamityarthaḥ| tīkṣṇabhojanaḥ tīkṣṇadravyasaṃskṛtāhāraḥ|25|-

palāśabījasvarasaṃ kalkaṃ vā taṇḍulāmbunā ||25||

aparamapi pānayogamāha- palāśabījetyādi| palāśabījasvarasaṃ kalkaṃ veti palāśabījakalkaṃ vā taṇḍulāmbunā pibedityarthaḥ||25||

pāribhadrakapatrāṇāṃ kṣaudreṇa svarasaṃ pibet |
pattūrasvarasaṃ vā'pi pibedvā surasādijam ||26||

aparamapi pānayogamāha- pāribhadrakapatrāṇāmityādi| pāribhadraḥ parvatanimbaḥ| pattūraḥ śiravālikā| surasādikaḥ surasādigaṇaḥ||26||

lihyādaśvaśakṛccūrṇaṃ vaiḍaṅgaṃ vā samākṣikam |27|

kṛmighnamavalehayogamāha- lihyādityādi| aśvaśakṛccūrṇaṃ ghoṭakapurīṣacūrṇam| vaiḍaṅgaṃ viḍaṅgacūm| ‘vaiḍaṅgam’ ityatra ‘baiḍālam’ iti kecit paṭhanti; tatra biḍālaviṭcūrṇamiti vyākhyānam|27|-

patrairmūṣikaparṇyā vā supiṣṭaiḥ piṣṭamiśritaiḥ ||27||
khādet pūpalikāḥ pakvā dhānyāmlaṃ ca pibedanu |28|

krimighnapūpalikāmāha- patrairmūṣikaparṇyā vetyādi| mūṣikaparṇī dantī, anye mūṣikakarṇākhyaṃ dravyāntaramāhuḥ| piṣṭamiśritaiḥ yavapiṣṭamiśritaiḥ, tasya viśeṣeṇa krimiharatvāt| dhānyāmlaṃ kāñjikam| anviti pūpalikābhakṣaṇānantaram| yadyapi ‘kṣīrāṇi māṃsāni ghṛtāni ca’ ityādigranthoktādamlavarjanādatra kāñjikasyāpi varjanaṃ prāpnoti, tathā'pi kāñjikasya prāyogikatvāt krimiharatvāccātra kāñjikānupānaṃ, tathā hi guṇopavarṇane kāñjikaṃ kṛmighnatvena dṛṣṭam||27||-

surasādigaṇe pakvaṃ tailaṃ vā pānamiṣyate ||28||
viḍaṅgacūrṇayuktairvā piṣṭairbhakṣyāṃstu kārayet [5] |29|

kṛmighnatailamāha- surasādigaṇe ityādi| tailamiti surasādigaṇe ‘kvāthakalkīkṛte’ iti śeṣaḥ| viḍaṅgetyādi| piṣṭaiḥ yavādipiṣṭaiḥ| bhakṣyān laḍḍūkādīn| vikalpavidhānaṃ pūrvayogāpekṣayā||28||-

tatkaṣāyaprapītānāṃ tilānāṃ snehameva vā ||29||

tatkaṣāyaprapītānāmityādi| tatkaṣāyaprapītānāṃ viḍaṅgakvāthabhāvitānām| atrāpi kārayediti sambandhanīyam||29||

śvāvidhaḥ śakṛtaścūrṇaṃ saptakṛtvaḥ subhāvitam |
viḍaṅgānāṃ kaṣāyeṇa traiphalena tathaiva ca ||30||
kṣaudreṇa līḍhvā'nupibedrasamāmalakodbhavam |
akṣābhayārasaṃ vā'pi... |31|

śvāvidha ityādi| śvāvidhaḥ seḍhāyāḥ, śakṛt viṣṭhā, tasyāścūrṇaṃ saptavārān viḍaṅgakvāthena bhāvitaṃ punastriphalākvāthena tathaiva bhāvitaṃ, kṣaudreṇa līḍhvā, anu paścāt, āmalakodbhavaṃ bibhītakodbhavaṃ vā harītakīrasaṃ vā'pi kṣaudreṇa pibediti sambandhaḥ||31||-

... vidhireṣo'yasāmapi ||31||

amumeva vidhi lauhacūrṇānāmapyatidiśannāha- vidhirityādi| eṣa vidhiḥ śvāvidhaḥ śakṛccūrṇavidhiḥ| ayasāmapīti trapusīsatāmrarajatakṛṣṇalohānāmapi| etenaitaduktaṃ bhavati- pratyekaṃ lohādicūrṇānāṃ śvāvitpurīṣacūrṇavidhānavadvidhiḥ kāryaḥ||31||

pūtīkasvarasaṃ vā'pi pibedvā madhunā saha |
pibedvā pippalīmūlamajāmūtreṇa saṃyutam ||32||
saptarātraṃ pibeddhṛṣṭaṃ trapu vā dadhimastunā |33|

pūtīkaḥ cirabilvaḥ| pibedityādi| ajāmūtreṇa chāgamūtreṇa| pibeddhṛṣṭamityādi| trapu raṅgaṃ, ‘kathīra’ iti loke| dadhimastugrahaṇam anyamastuniṣedhārthaṃ; anye ‘dadhimastunā’ ityatra dadhigrahaṇaṃ kūrcikāmastuparihārārthamiti vyākhyānayanti| tanna, dadhimastugrahaṇena kūrcikāmastugrahaṇamasambhavaṃ, sarvanibandhavirodhāt samānatantravirodhācca||32||

purīṣajān kaphotthāṃśca hanyādevaṃ kṛmīn bhiṣak ||33||

yat pūrvametaccikitsitamuktaṃ tat purīṣakaphajānāṃ kāryamiti darśayannāha- purīṣajānityādi| evamiti pūrvoktena vidhinetyarthaḥ||33||

śirohṛdghrāṇakarṇākṣisaṃśritāṃśca pṛthagvidhān |
viśeṣeṇāñjanairnasyairavapīḍaiśca sādhayet ||34||

purīṣakaphajānāṃ sāmānyacikitsitamabhidhāyedānīṃ kaphajānāmāśrayaviśeṣeṇa cikitsāmāha- śirohṛdghrāṇetyādi| śiraḥprabhṛtisaṃśritān nānāprakārān kṛmīn viśeṣeṇāñjanādibhiḥ sādhayediti piṇḍārthaḥ| cakārāt gaṇḍūṣakavalagrahādibhirapi| anye tu yadyapyañjanādikaṃ sāmānyenoktaṃ, tathā'pyañjanaṃ netrasthe, nasyāvapīḍau ghrāṇaśiraḥsaṃśrite ca, viśeṣeṇāñjanādīnāṃ netrādijakṛmiharatvāt; hṛdgatānāṃ karṇagatānāṃ cārthavaśādeva kṛmijahṛdrogakṛmikarṇakacikitsitaiścikitsā kāryā; cakārāt śiraḥsthitānāṃ kṛmīṇāṃ kṛmiśirorogacikitsitaiścikitsā kāryeti gṛhyata iti vyākhyānayanti| ‘saṃśritān’ ityatra ‘vraṇajān’ iti kecit paṭhanti; anye ‘saṃśritān’ ityatra ‘saṃsṛtān’ iti paṭhanti; karṇasthāne ca vaktraṃ paṭhanti||34||

śakṛdrasaṃ turaṅgasya suśuṣkaṃ bhāvayedati |
niṣkvāthena viḍaṅgānāṃ cūrṇaṃ pradhamanaṃ tu tat ||35||

śiraḥ prabhṛtisaṃśritakṛmighnaṃ pradhamanamāha- śakṛdrasamityādi| śakṛdrasaṃ turaṅgasyeti ghoṭakapurīṣasya rasamityarthaḥ| suśuṣkamiti purīṣarasasya viśeṣaṇam| pradhamanamiti nāsāyāṃ cūrṇaprakṣepaṇaṃ jñeyam||35||

ayaścūrṇānyanenaiva vidhinā yojayedbhiṣak |
sakāṃsyanīlaṃ tailaṃ ca nasyaṃ syātsurasādike ||36||

amumeva pradhamanavidhiṃ lohacūrṇeṣvatidiśannāha- ayaścūrṇānītyādi| aneneti ghoṭapurīṣasvarasapradhamanavidhānena| sakāṃsyanīlaṃ kāṃsyāpamārjanamasīsahitam| ‘surasādike’ ityatra ‘vipakvaṃ’ iti śeṣaḥ| tena surasādigaṇakvāthavipakvaṃ tailaṃ kāṃsyamasīsahitaṃ nasyaṃ syāditi| tathā coktāṃ,- ‘surasādiśṛtaṃ tailaṃ nasyaṃ kāṃsyamalānvitam’- iti||36||

indraluptavidhiścāpi vidheyo romabhojiṣu |37|

romādādīnāmatideśena cikitsāmāha- indraluptetyādi| romabhojiṣu romādeṣu| cakāreṇa keśabhojiṣvapīndraluptavidhiḥ kārya iti samuccīyate|37|-

dantādānāṃ samuddiṣṭaṃ vidhānaṃ mukharogikam ||37||

dantādānāmapyatideśena cikitsitamāha- dantādānāmityādi| mukharogikaṃ kṛmidantoktaṃ, viśeṣeṇa kṛmidantoktacikitsitasya dantānāṃ hitatvāt| yadyapi romādadantādānāṃ raktajatvāt raktajasāmānyacikitsānantaramindraluptavidhyādikaṃ viśeṣacikitsitaṃ kartumucitaṃ bhavati, tathā'pi kaphajā api romadantabhakṣaṇaṃ kurvantīti pravyaktakaṭukaṃ bhojanaṃ hitaṃ bhavediti pratipādanārthaṃ romadantādānāṃ prathamaṃ cikitsitamuktam||37||

raktajānāṃ pratīkāraṃ kuryāt kuṣṭhacikitsite |38|

idānīṃ sāmānyacikitsitaṃ raktajānāmatideśenāha- raktajānāmityādi| ‘kuṣṭhacikitsite’ ityatra pūrvoktameva samuddiṣṭamiti padaṃ sambandhanīyam| kecit ‘kuṣṭhacikitsitāt’ iti paṭhanti|38|-

surasādiṃ tu sarveṣu sarvathaivopayojayet ||38||

surasādiṃ tu sarveṣvityādi| sarveṣu purīṣajādiṣu| sarvathaiva annapānasnānādividhinā||38||

pāne'pi vidhyantaramāha- kulatthetyādi| kulatthakṣārasaṃsṛṣṭaṃ kulatthabhasmodakasaṃyuktam? kṣārapānaṃ yavakṣārapānaṃ, tathā coktaṃ viśvāmitreṇa- ‘yāvaśūkasya pānaṃ tu kulatthakṣāravāriṇā’- iti| kecit ‘kulatthakṣāra...’ ityatra ‘viḍaṅgakṣāra...’ iti paṭhanti||39||

samāsato'mlānmadhurān himāṃśca kṛmīn jighāṃsuḥ parivarjayettu ||40||

pravṛttilakṣaṇaṃ cikitsitamabhidhāyedānīṃ nivṛttilakṣaṇaṃ cikitsitamāha- kṣīrāṇi māṃsānītyādi| yadyapi ‘saṅkṣepataḥ kriyāyogo nidānaparivarjanam’ iti vākyādeva kṣīrādīnāṃ niṣedhavidhiḥ siddhaḥ, tathā'pi kṣīrādīnāṃ pratiṣedhakaraṇaṃ niyamārthaṃ; tena kṛmīṇāṃ nidānamapi kṣīrādikaṃ madātyaye madyavadbheṣajaṃ mā bhūdityarthaḥ||40||
