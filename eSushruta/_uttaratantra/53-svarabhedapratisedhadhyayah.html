--- title: "53. svarabhedapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 53 order: 53 ---

athātaḥ svarabhedapratiṣedhamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

śvāsakāsapratiṣedhānantaramūrdhvagatatvasāmānyāt śvāsakāsacikitsitasādhyatvācca svarabhedapratiṣedhārambho yukta ityata āha- athāta ityādi| svarabhedaḥ svaropaghātaḥ||1-2||

atyuccabhāṣaṇaviṣādhyayanātigītaśītādibhiḥ [1] prakupitāḥ pavanādayastu |
srotaḥsu te svaravaheṣu gatāḥ pratiṣṭhāṃ hanyuḥ svaraṃ bhavati cāpi hi ṣaḍvidhaḥ saḥ ||3||

svarabhedahetusamprāptisaṅkhyā āha- atyuccabhāṣaṇetyādi| adhyayanam anavarataṃ vedādipāṭhaḥ| atigītaṃ nirantaraṃ maṅgalādigānam| śītādibhirityatrādiśabdāt pittakaphakopahetavo gṛhyante| yadatra atyuccabhāṣaṇādīnāṃ vātakopahetūnāmeva sākṣāt kathanaṃ kṛtaṃ na pittakaphakopahetūnāṃ tat svaropaghātakartṛtve vāyoḥ prādhānyakhyāpanārtham| anye tu śītādītyatra ādiśabdena gurulaghusnigdharūkṣādīn guṇān gṛhṇanti| svaravaheṣu srotaḥsu śabdavāhinīṣu dhamanīṣu, te vātādayaḥ, gatāḥ prāptāḥ, pratiṣṭhāṃ sthitim| ṣaḍvidhaḥ ṣaṭprakāraḥ; pṛthak vātādibhistrayaḥ, sannipātenaikaḥ, medaḥkṣayābhyāṃ pratyekamekaḥ; evaṃ ṣaṭprakāraḥ| nanu, samānatantre ‘vātāt pittāt kaphāt kāsādraktavegāt sapīnasāt’ (ca. ci. 8) ityatra yathā ṣaḍvidhaḥ svarabheda uktastena sārdhaṃ kathaṃ na virodhaḥ| satyaṃ, kāsajapratiśyāyajayorliṅgatvenoktatvādgrahaṇaṃ, raktajasya tu kaphagrahaṇenaiva grahaṇaṃ, yato raktaṃ kaphayuktaṃ svarabhedaṃ karoti samānatantroktatvāt| yadyevaṃ tarhi kathaṃ na saṅkhyādhikyaṃ? styaṃ, kāsajapratiśyāyajayorvātikatvena grahaṇam| atyuccabhāṣaṇetyādiko hetuḥ, prakupitāḥ pavanādaya ityādikā samprāptiḥ, bhavati cāpi hi ṣaḍvidhaḥ sa iti saṅkhyā||3||

vātena kṛṣṇanayanānanamūtravarcā bhinnaṃ śanairvadati gadgadavat svaraṃ ca |4|

vātikasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- vātenetyādi| kṛṣṇaśabdo nayanādibhirvarcaḥparyantaiḥ sambadhyate| bhinnam anavasthitam| uktaṃ ca tantrāntare- “svarabhedo bhavedvātādrūkṣaḥ kṣāmaścalaḥ svaraḥ” (ca. ci. 8)- iti| ‘gadgadavat svaram’ ityatra kecit ‘gardabhavat svaraṃ’ iti paṭhanti|4|-

pittena pītavadanākṣipurīṣamūtro brūyādgalena paridāhasamanvitena [2] ||4||

pittajasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- pittenetyādi| pītaśabdo vadanādibhirmūtrāntaiḥ sambadhyate| 'sa vidāhasamanvitena’ iti kecit paṭhanti, tatra sa iti svarabhedī puruṣaḥ||4||

kṛcchrāt kaphena satataṃ kapharuddhakaṇṭho mandaṃ śanairvadati cāpi divā viśeṣaḥ [3] |5|

kaphajasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- kṛcchrādityādi| satatam anavaratam| kapharuddhakaṇṭhaḥ śleṣmāvṛtakaṇṭhaḥ| mandaṃ śanairiti mandaṃ mandamityarthaḥ| divā viśeṣa iti divase viśeṣo bhavet, kaphakṣayāt| ‘divā viśeṣāt’ iti kecit paṭhanti; rātrau tāvat kapharuddhakaṇṭhatvānna vadatyeva, divā punaḥ kaphāpacayādviśeṣo bhavati, tasmācca viśeṣānmandaṃ mandaṃ vadatīti vyākhyānayanti|5|-

sarvātmake bhavati sarvavikārasampadavyaktatā ca vacasastamasādhyamāhuḥ ||5||

sānnipātikasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- sarvātmaka ityādi| sarvātmake sannipātotthasvarabhede| sarvavikārasampad vātādiliṅgaprādurbhāvaḥ| avyaktatā ca vacasaḥ aspaṣṭavāktvam| taṃ sānnipātikasvarabhedam||5||

dhūpyeta vāk kṣayakṛte kṣayamāpnuyācca vāgeṣa cā(vā)pi hatavāk parivarjanīyaḥ |6|

kṣayajasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- dhūpyetetyādi| dhūpyeta mukhāddhūmamivodvamati, ‘bruvan’ iti vākyaśeṣaḥ| kṣayamāpnuyād vāgiti vacanakṣayaṃ prāpnuyāt ‘vātāt’ iti śeṣaḥ|6|-

antargalaṃ [4] svaramalakṣyapadaṃ cireṇa medaścayādvadati [5] digdhagalauṣṭhatāluḥ ||6||
kṣīṇasya vṛddhasya kṛśasya cāpi cirotthito yaśca sahopajātaḥ |
medasvinaḥ sarvasamudbhavaśca svarāmayo yo na sa siddhimeti ||7||

medojasvarabhedalakṣaṇamāha- antargalamityādi| antargalaṃ galasyāntare| svaraṃ vadati śabdaṃ karoti| ‘antargataṃ’ iti kecit paṭhanti, tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ| yato'ntargalaṃ svaraṃ vadati ata eva alakṣyapadamiti, na lakṣyante padāni yasyetyarthaḥ| bhedaścayāt medovṛddheḥ| digdhaṃ medodigdhaṃ galauṣṭhatālu yasya sa tathā| ‘medo'nvayāt’ iti kecit paṭhanti||6-7||

snigdhān svarāturanarānapakṛṣṭadoṣān nyāyena tān [6] vamanarecanabastibhiśca |
nasyāvapīḍamukhadhāvanadhūmalehaiḥ sampādayecca vividhaiḥ kavalagrahaiśca ||8||

svarabhedasya sāmānyacikitsāmāha- snigdhānityādi| yadyapi kaphajamedojayoḥ svarabhedayorapatarpaṇasādhyatvāt snehanaṃ na yuktaṃ, tathā'pi yathādoṣapratyanīkairyathādoṣapratyanīkasādhitaiśca snehaiḥ snehanaṃ yuktameva, athavā medaḥkaphajayorapi vāyusadbhāvāt snehanopadeśaḥ| apakṛṣṭadoṣān ākṛṣṭadoṣān; atra vamanavirecanabastibhiriti sambadhyate| nyāyena yathāvidhītyarthaḥ| vamanādibhirapakṛṣṭadoṣān svarabhedāturān kiṃ kuryādityāha- nasyāvapīḍetyādi| avapīḍya dīyate ityavapīḍo nasyabhedaḥ| mukhadhāvanaṃ gaṇḍūṣādi| sampādayet yojayet| vividhaiḥ doṣapratyanīkatvena nānāprakāraiḥ| kavalagrahaiḥ gaṇḍūṣabhedaiḥ| tathā ca- “sukhaṃ sañcāryate yā tu gaṇḍūṣe sā prakīrtitā| asañcāryā tu yā mātrā kavale sā prakīrtitā [7] ”- iti||8||

yaḥ śvāsakāsavidhirādita eva coktastaṃ cāpyaśeṣamavatārayituṃ yateta |9|

anyadapi svarabhedacikitsitamatideśenāha- ya ityādi| śvāsakāsapratiṣedhe sāmānyenādau yaḥ śvāsakāsavidhiruddiṣṭastaṃ vidhiṃ samastamapi yathādoṣaṃ kartuṃ yateteti piṇḍārthaḥ|9|

vaiśeṣikaṃ ca vidhimūrdhvamato vadāmi taṃ vai svarāturahitaṃ nikhilaṃ nibodha ||9||

vaiśeṣikamityādi| ata iti sāmānyacikitsānantaram| taṃ vai svarāturahitamiti svarabhedāturahitam| nibodha jānīhi||9||

svaropaghāte'nilaje bhuktopari ghṛtaṃ pibet |
kāsamardakavārtākamārkavasvarase śṛtam ||10||
pītaṃ ghṛtaṃ hantyanilaṃ siddhamārtagale rase |11|

tameva vaiśeṣikavidhimāha- svaropaghāta ityādi| bhuktopari bhojanopari| mārkavo bhṛṅgarājaḥ, śṛtaṃ pakvam| anilaṃ vātajasvarabhedam, upacārāt| ārtagalaḥ kakubhaḥ||10||-

yavakṣārājamodābhyāṃ citrakāmalakeṣu vā ||11||
devadārvagnikābhyāṃ vā siddhamājaṃ samākṣikam |12|

aparamapi vātaje ghṛtatrayamāha- yavakṣārājamodābhyāmityādi| agniko ajamodā| ājaṃ ‘ghṛtaṃ’ śeṣaḥ| samākṣikaṃ samadhu| atrāpi pītamiti yojyam||11||-

sukhodakānupāno vā sasarpiṣko guḍaudanaḥ ||12||

vātike pathyayogamāha- sukhodakānupāna ityādi| sukhodakam īṣaduṣṇodakam| kecidamuṃ yogaṃ paittike manyante; tanna, samānatantreṣu vātikasvarabheda eva prayuktatvāt||12||

kṣīrānupānaṃ pitte tu pibet sarpiratandritaḥ |13|

paittikasvarabhedacikitsāmāha- kṣīrānupānamityādi| atandritaḥ analasaḥ|13|-

aśnīyācca sasarpiṣkaṃ yaṣṭīmadhukapāyasam ||13||

paittike pathyayogamāha- aśnīyādityādi| yaṣṭīmadhukapāyasamiti yaṣṭīmadhukasādhitakṣīrasiddhāṃstaṇḍulān; athavā yaṣṭīmadhukānvitaṃ pāyasaṃ yaṣṭīmadhukapāyasam||13||

lihyānmadhurakāṇāṃ vā cūrṇaṃ madhughṛtāplutam |
śatāvarīcūrṇayogaṃ balācūrṇamathāpi vā ||14||

lihyādityādi| madhurakāṇāṃ kākolīprabhṛtīnām| śatāvarīcūrṇayogamiti śatāvarīcūrṇasya yogaḥ saṃyogo yatra tattathā, atrāpi madhughṛtāplutaṃ lihyāditi sambandhanīyam||14||

pibet kaṭūni mūtreṇa kaphaje svarasaṅkṣaye |
lihyādvā madhutailābhyāṃ bhuktvā khādet kaṭūni vā ||15||

kaphajasvarabhedacikitsāmāha- pibedityādi| kaṭūni trikaṭukādīni| mūtreṇa gomūtreṇa prādhānyāt| svarasaṅkṣaye svarabhede| lihyādvā madhutailābhyāmityādi madhutailābhyāṃ vā kaṭūni trikaṭukaprabhṛtīni lihyāt, bhuktvā vā tāni khādet||15||

svaropaghāte medoje kaphavadvidhiriṣyate |16|

medojasvarabhede kaphajacikitsāmatidiśannāha- svaropaghāta ityādi| kaphavat kaphajasvarabhedavat|16|-

sarvaje kṣayaje cāpi pratyākhyāyācaret kriyām ||16||

sānnipātikakṣayajayorasādhyayorapi cikitsā kartavyetyāha- sarvaja ityādi| pratyākhyāya asādhyatvena nirākṛtya| ācaret kuryāt| kriyāmiti sānnipātike sarveṣāṃ vātādisvarabhedānāṃ sarvameva cikitsitaṃ kāryaṃ, kṣayaje tu kṣayoktamiti; kecidubhayorapi sāmānyacikitsitaṃ kuryādityāhuḥ||16||

śarkarāmadhumiśrāṇi śṛtāni madhuraiḥ saha |
pibet payāṃsi yasyoccairvadato'bhihataḥ svaraḥ ||17||

atyuccabhāṣaṇotthasvarabhedasya vātātmakatve'pi nimittabhedamāśritya viśiṣṭāṃ cikitsāmāha- śarkaretyādi| madhuraiḥ kākolyādibhiḥ śṛtāni payāṃsi śarkarāmadhumiśrāṇi pibediti piṇḍārthaḥ| payāṃsītyatra bahuvacanaṃ gomahiṣyajānāṃ kṣīraparigrahāya||17||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kāyacikitsātantre chardipratiṣedho nāma (pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ, āditaḥ) tripañcāśattamo'dhyāyaḥ ||53||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kāyacikitsātantre svarabhedapratiṣedho nāma tripañcāśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||53||

1. `... viṣādhyayanābhighāta ...' iti pā.|
2. `ca vidāhasamanvitena' iti pā.|
3. `viśeṣāt' iti pā.|
4. `antargatasvaraṃ' iti pā.|
5. `medo'nvayāt' iti pā.|
6. `saṃyojayet' iti pā.|
7. `bhiṣagvaraiḥ' iti pā.|
