--- title: "59. mūtrakṛcchrapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 59 order: 59 ---

athāto mūtrakṛcchrapratiṣedhamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

mūtrāghātapratiṣedhānantaraṃ bastigatatvasāmānyānmūtrakṛcchrapratiṣedhārambhaḥ- athāta ityādi| mūtrakṛcchraṃ duḥkhena mūtrapravṛttiḥ| ‘mūtrakṛcchrapratiṣedham’ ityatra ‘mūtropaghātapratiṣedhaṃ’ ityanye paṭhanti, te'pyupaghātaśabdena kṛcchratāṃ vadanti; apare ‘mūtradoṣapratiṣedhaṃ’ iti paṭhanti, tatrāpi tādṛgevābhiprāyaḥ| nanu aśmarīmūtrāghātodāvartādiṣu mūtrakṛcchrasyoktatvāt kimartha punaratra tadabhidhānaṃ? satyaṃ, cikitsālakṣaṇakāryabhedāt tathā samānatantre'pi pṛthaṅnirdiṣṭatvācca punasteṣāmupādānam kecicca punaruktibhayādevāmumadhyāyaṃ na paṭhanti||1-2||

vātena pittena kaphena sarvaistathā'bhighātaiḥ śakṛdaśmarībhyām |
tathā'paraḥ śarkarayā sukaṣṭo mūtropaghātaḥ kathito'ṣṭamastu ||3||

vātenetyādi| mūtropaghāto mūtrakṛcchra evetyarthaḥ| aṣṭama iti saṅkhyākaraṇaṃ nānāprakārasyāpyaśmarījasya mūtrakṛcchrasyaikatvena grahaṇamiti pratipādanārtham| keciduttarārdhamīdṛśaṃ paṭhanti,- “śuktodbhavaṃ śarkarayā ca kaṣṭaṃ mūtrasya kṛcchraṃ pravadanti tajjñāḥ” iti| sa cānārṣaḥ pāṭhaḥ||3||

alpamalpaṃ samutpīḍya muṣkamehanabastibhiḥ |
phaladbhiriva kṛcchreṇa vātāghātena mehati ||4||

vātakṛcchralakṣaṇamāha- alpamalpamityādi| alpamalpaṃ stokaṃ stokaṃ, samutpīḍya arthavaśānmuṣkamehanādikameva pīḍayitvā| muṣkaḥ aṇḍaṃ, mehanaṃ liṅgaṃ, bastiḥ mūtrādhāraḥ| phaladbhiriva sphuṭadbhiriva| kṛcchreṇa duḥkhana; etat padaṃ mehatītyatra sambandhanīyam| vātāghātena vātakṛcchreṇetyarthaḥ| mehati mūtrayati| kārtikakuṇḍo'muṃ pāṭhāmanyathā paṭhati, sa cābhāvānna darśitaḥ||4||

hāridramuṣṇaṃ raktaṃ vā muṣkamehanabastibhiḥ |
agninā dahyāmānābhaiḥ pittaghātena mehati ||5||

pittakṛcchralakṣaṇamāha- hāridramuṣṇamityādi| hāridraṃ haridrāvarṇa, raktaṃ raktavarṇam
etaddvayaṃ nyūnādhikapittaprakopavaśat| dahyamānābhaiḥ atyantadāhaparītaiḥ| pittāghātena pittakṛcchreṇa||5||

snigdhaṃ śuklamanuṣṇaṃ ca muṣkamehanabastibhiḥ |
saṃhṛṣṭaromā gurubhiḥ śleṣmāghātena mehati ||6||

kaphakṛcchralakṣaṇamāha- snigdhamityādi| anuṣṇam īṣaduṣṇam| saṃhṛṣṭaromā uddharṣitaromā| gurubhiḥ bhārikaiḥ| śleṣmāghātena śleṣmakṛcchreṇa||6||

dāhaśītarujāviṣṭo nānāvarṇaṃ mahurmuhuḥ |
tāmyamānastu kṛcchreṇa sānnipātena mehati ||7||

sānnipātikalakṣaṇamāha- dāhaśītetyādi| āviṣṭo yuktaḥ|
nānāvarṇa pītaraktaśuklādivarṇam| tāmyamāno'tyartha tamaḥ praviśan| kṛcchreṇa atikaṣṭena||7||

mūtravāhiṣu śalyena kṣateṣvabhihateṣu ca |
srotaḥsu mūtrāghātastu jāyate bhṛśavedanaḥ [1] ||8||
vātabastestu tulyāni tasya liṅgāni lakṣayet |
śakṛtastu pratīghātādvāyurviguṇatāṃ gataḥ ||9||
ādhmānaṃ ca saśūlaṃ ca mūtrasaṅgaṃ karoti hi |10|

mūtravāhiṣu śalyenetyādi||8-9||

aśmarīhetukaḥ pūrvaṃ mūtrāghāta udāhṛtaḥ ||10||

aśmarītyādi| pūrva nidānasthāne'śmarīhetuko mūtrāghāta udāhṛtaḥ||10||

aśmarī śarkarā caiva tulye [2] sambhavalakṣaṇaiḥ |
śarkarāyā viśeṣaṃ tu śṛṇu kīrtayato mama ||11||
pacyamānasya pittena bhidyamānasya vāyunā |
śleṣamaṇo'vayavā bhinnāḥ śarkarā iti sañjñitāḥ ||12||
hṛtpīḍā vepathuḥ śūlaṃ kukṣau [3] vahniḥ sudurbalaḥ |
tābhirbhavati mūrcchā ca mūtrāghātaśca dāruṇaḥ ||13||
mūtraveganirastāsu tāsu śāmyati vedanā |
yāvadanyā [4] punarnaiti guḍikā srotaso mukham ||14||
śarkarāsambhavasyaitanmūtrāghātasya lakṣaṇam |15|

yadyapi śarkarākṛcchramaśmarījakṛcchraṃ ca tulyaṃ tathā'pi kiñcidbhedamāśritya śarkarākṛcchraṃ bhinnamāha- aśmarītyādi| pacyamānasyeti pakvasyetyarthaḥ, arthavaśāt śleṣmaṇa eva; bhidyamānasyeti bhinnasyetyarthaḥ, bhāvini bhūtavadupacārāt| vepathuḥ kampaḥ| sudurbalo mandaḥ| tābhiḥ śarkarābhiḥ| mūtraveganirastāsu mūtrasyādhovegena niḥsṛtāsvityarthaḥ| naiti nāgacchati| kārtikakuṇḍastvamuṃ pāṭhaṃ bahudhā vyākulīkṛtya paṭhati, sa tu granthagauravabhayāt parityaktaḥ| yadyapi aśmarīśarkarāmūtrakṛcchre nidānasthāne bhaṇite, tathā'pi prakaraṇavaśāt [5] kiñcillakṣaṇādhikyāccātrāpi bhaṇite||11-14||

cikitsitamathaiteṣāmaṣṭānāmapi vakṣyate ||15||

idānīṃ mūtrakṛcchracikitsāmāha- cikitsitamityādi| eteṣāṃ mūtrakṛcchrāṇām||15||

aśmarīṃ ca samāśritya yaduktaṃ prasamīkṣya tat |
yathādoṣaṃ prayuñjīta snehādimapi ca kramam ||16||

tadaiva cikitsitamatideśenāha- aśmarītyādi| aśmarīmāśritya yat prāk cikitsitamuktaṃ snehādiktamaśca, tadyathādoṣaṃ prasamīkṣya prayuñjīta kuryāt| etenaitaduktam- aśmarīcikitsite vātādibhedena yo vidhiruktastaṃ tathā snehādimapi ca kramaṃ prayuñjīta kuryāt| cakārāt prāguktamūtrāghātacikitsitamapi||16||

śvadaṃṣṭrāśmabhidau kumbhīṃ hapuṣāṃ kaṇṭakārikām |
balāṃ śatāvarīṃ rāsnāṃ varuṇaṃ girikarṇikām ||17||
tathā vidārigandhādiṃ saṃhṛtya traivṛtaṃ pacet |
tailaṃ ghṛtaṃ vā tat peyaṃ tena vā'pyanuvāsanam ||18||
dadyāduttarabastiṃ ca vātakṛcchropaśāntaye |19|

idānīṃ vātakṛcchracikitsitamāha- śvadaṃṣṭretyādi| śvadaṃṣṭrāgokṣurakaḥ| aśmabhit pāṣāṇabhedaḥ| kumbhī sthalakumbhī yasyāstvak vakrā [6] bhavati| girikarṇikā aparājitābhedaḥ [7] | vidārigandhādiḥ prathamo gaṇaḥ saṃhṛtya ekīkṛtya| traivṛtaṃ tailaṃ ghṛtaṃ veti tatra tribhirghṛtavasāmajjabhirvṛtaṃ tailaṃ, tailavasāmajjabhirvṛtaṃ ghṛtaṃ vā traivṛtam| tailaṃ ghṛtaṃ veti vikalpaḥ sātmyāpekṣayā vātādibalāpekṣayā vā jñeyaḥ||17-18||

śvadaṃṣṭrāsvarase tailaṃ saguḍakṣīranāgaram ||19||
paktvā tat pūrvavadyojyaṃ tatrānilarujāpaham |20|

aparamapi vātakṛcchracikitsitamāha- śvadaṃṣṭretyādi| kalkadravyamadhye paṭhitamapi kṣīraṃ dravatvena boddhavyam| yojyaṃ pānānuvāsanottarabastiṣu| anilarujāpahaṃ vātotthakṛcchranāśanamityarthaḥ||19||-

tṛṇotpalādikākolīnyagrodhādigaṇaiḥ kṛ(śṛ)tam ||20||
pītaṃ ghṛtaṃ pittakṛcchraṃ nāśayeta kṣīrameva vā |21|

pittatthakṛcchrabheṣajamāha- tṛṇotpalādītyādi| ghṛtaṃ tṛṇādisaṃskṛtaṃ grāhyāṃ; kṣīrameva veti vikalpaḥ sātmyāpekṣayā'gnibalāpekṣayā ca bhavati||20||-

dadyāduttarabastiṃ ca pittakṛcchropaśāntaye ||21||

anyadapi pittakṛcchrabheṣajamāha- dadyādityādi| dadyāduttarabastimiti ‘tṛṇādisaṃskṛtasnehena’ iti śeṣaḥ| yadyapi vakṣyamāṇatrividhabastigrahaṇenottarabastirgṛhītaḥ, tathā'pi pittakṛcchre uttarabastiratyantaṃ hita iti jñāpānārtha pṛthagapyuttarabastitarnirdiṣṭaḥ||21||

ebhireva kṛtaḥ snehastrividheṣvapi bastiṣu |
hitaṃ virecanaṃ cekṣukṣīradrākṣārasairyutam ||22||

aparamapi pittakṛcchravidhimāha- ebhirityādi| ebhireva anantaroktaistṛṇotpalādiprabhṛtibhireva| trividheṣu bastiṣu niruhānuvāsanottarabastiṣu| anye niruhasthāne śirobastiṃ manyante; tanna, samānatantravirodhāt| rasaśabda ikṣāvapi sambandhanīyaḥ||22||

surasoṣakamustādau varuṇādau ca yat [8] kṛtam |
tailaṃ tathā yavāgvādi kaphāghāte [9] praśasyate ||23||

kaphakṛcchracikitsitamāha- surasoṣaketyādi| surasādau ūṣakādau mustādau varuṇādau ca yat kṛtaṃ tailaṃ, tathā yavāgvādi, tat kaphāghāte praśasyate| yavāgvādirityatra ādiśabdādvilepyādayaḥ; te ca surasādisaṃskṛtā eva bodvavyāḥ| kaphāghāte kaphakṛcchre||23||

yathādoṣocchryaṃ kuryādetāneva ca sarvaje |24|

sannipātotthamūtrakṛcchracikitsāmāha- yathādoṣocchryamityādi|- yathādoṣādhikyam| etāneveti anantaroktavātādikṛcchraharayogān| sarvaje sānnipātike kṛcchre| etenaitaduktaṃ bhavati- sānnipātike kṛcchre yasya doṣasyādhikyaṃ ddaśyate taddoṣoktakṛcchraharayogotkaṭān
pūrvoktavātādikṛcchrayogānekīkṛtya kuryāditi|24|-

phalguvṛścīradarbhāśmasāracūrṇaṃ ca vāriṇā ||24||
surekṣurasadarbhāmbupītaṃ kṛcchrarujāpaham |25|

aparamapi sānnipātikakṛcchrayogamāha- phalguvṛścīretyādi| phalguvṛścīrādīnāṃ cūrṇa vāriṇā pītaṃ yathādoṣaṃ surekṣurasādibhiśca pītaṃ kṛcchrarujāpahaṃ, arthavaśāt sānnipātikakṛcchrarujāpahaṃ bhavatīti piṇḍārthaḥ| phalguḥ kākodumbarikā| vṛścīraḥ śuklapunarnavā| aśmasāraṃ śilājatu [10] ||24||-

tathā'bhighātaje kuryāt sadyovraṇacikitsitam ||25||

abhighātajakṛcchrasyātideśena cikitsitamāha- tathetyādi| abhighātaje abhighātajamūtrakṛcchre, sadyovraṇacikitsitamityatra luptacakāranirdeśāt vātakṛcchraśāntikriyā kāryā||25||

mūtrakṛcchre śakṛjjāte kāryā vātaharī kriyā |
svedāvagāhāvabhyaṅgabasticūrṇakriyāstathā ||26||

parīṣakṛcchre'tideśena cikitsitamāha- mūtretyādi| śakṛjje tāmeva vātaharīṃ kriyāṃ vivṛṇoti- svedāvagāhāvityādi| svedādayaścātra vātavyādhyuktā grāhyāḥ||26||

ye tvanye tu tathā kṛcchre tayo proktaḥ kriyāvidhiḥ ||27||

aśmarīśarkarotpannayoḥ kṛcchrayościkitsāmāha- ye ityādi| ye dve anye kṛcchre aśmarīśarkarotthe tayoḥ kriyāvidhiḥ proktaḥ aśmarīśarkarācikitsite| apare ‘dvau tathā'nyau tu yau kṛcchrau’ iti paṭhanti, tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ||27||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kāyacikitsātantre mūtrakṛcchrapratiṣedho nāma (ekaviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ, āditaḥ) ekonaṣaṣṭhatamo'dhyāyaḥ ||59||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ
suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantre ekonaṣaṣṭhitamo'dhyāyaḥ||59||

1. `bhṛśadāruṇaḥ' iti pā.|
2. ‘tulyasambhavalakṣaṇā' iti pā.|
3. `kukṣāvagniśca durbalaḥ' iti pā.|
4. `yāvadasyāḥ' iti pā.|
5. `prakaraṇāntaratvāt' iti pā.|
6. `vastraṃ' iti pā.|
7. `śvetasyandā' iti pā.|
8. `saṃskṛtam' iti pā.|
9. `kaphakṛcchre' iti pā.|
10. `lohaṃ' iti pā.|
