--- title: "23. nāsāgatarogapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 23 order: 23 ---

athāto nāsāgatarogapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi||1-2||

pūrvoddiṣṭe pūtinasye ca jantoḥ snehasvedau chardanaṃ sraṃsanaṃ ca |
yuktaṃ bhaktaṃ tīkṣṇamalpaṃ laghu syāduṣṇaṃ toyaṃ dhūmapānaṃ ca kāle ||3||

apīnasapūtinasyayościkitsāmāha- pūrvoddiṣṭe ityādi| pūrvoddiṣṭe apīnase ityarthaḥ| chardanaṃ vamanaṃ, sraṃsanaṃ virekaḥ| kṛtaśuddherantaḥsaṃsargaṃ niyamayannāha- yuktamityādi| toyasya pānaṃ niyamayannāha- uṣṇaṃ toyamityādi| kāle dhūmapānakāle||3||

hiṅgu vyoṣaṃ vatsakākhyaṃ śivāṭī lākṣā bījaṃ saurabhaṃ kaṭphalaṃ ca |
ugrā kuṣṭhaṃ tīkṣṇagandhā viḍaṅgaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ nityaṃ cāvapīḍe karañjam ||4||

tayorevāvapīḍamuddiśannāha- hiṅgu vyoṣamityādi| vyoṣaṃ trikaṭukam| śivāṭī śvetapunarnavā, śephāliketyanye| ugrā vacā, tīkṣṇagandhā śobhāñjanakaḥ| avapīḍya dīyata ityāvapīḍaḥ| kecidamumeva pāṭhamanyathā paṭhanti, sa cādarśanānna likhitaḥ||4||

etairdravyaiḥ sārṣapaṃ mūtrayuktaṃ tailaṃ dhīmānnasyahetoḥ paceta |5|

apīnasapūtinasyayoretairevāvapīḍanadravyairnasyārthaṃ tailasādhanamāha- etairdravyairityādi| kecit ‘asmin taile yuktitaḥ sampaceta mañjiṣṭhāṃ vai srotasi mrakṣaṇārthaṃ, iti paṭhanti, sa cācāryairanārṣīkṛtaḥ|5|-

nāsāpāke pittahṛtsaṃvidhānaṃ kāryaṃ sarvaṃ bāhmamābhyantaraṃ ca ||5||
hṛtvā raktaṃ kṣīravṛkṣatvacaśca sājyāḥ sekā yojanīyāśca lepāḥ |6|

nāsāpāke bheṣajamāha- nāsāpāke ityādi| bāhmaṃ lepābhyaṅgapariṣekādi, ābhyantaraṃ punarāhārasnehapānavirecanādi| sājyāḥ saghṛtāḥ||5||-

vakṣyāmyūrdhvaṃ raktapittopaśāntiṃ... |6|

raktapittasyānāgatāvekṣaṇena saṅkṣepārthaṃ cikitsitamāha- vakṣyāmyūrdhvamityādi| ūrdhvaṃ raktapittapratiṣedhe| upaśāntiṃ cikitsitaṃ ‘nāsāpravṛtte jalamāśu deyaṃ saśarkaraṃ nāsikayā payo vā’ ityādikam|6|-

... nāḍīvatsyāt pūyarakte cikitsā ||6||

pūyaraktasya vidhānamāha- nāḍīvadityādi| pūyarakte nāḍīvat nāḍītulyā cikitsā, yā visarpanāḍīstanarogacikitsite vyākhyātā||6||

vānte samyak cāvapīḍaṃ vadanti tīkṣṇaṃ ghūmaṃ śodhanaṃ cātra nasyam |7|

pūyarakte sāmānyāṃ cikitsāmabhidhāya viśeṣacikitsāmāha- vānte ityādi| atra pūyarakte; anye tu ‘atreti nāsāpāke’ iti vyākhyānayanti| kecit ‘vānte samyak’ ityādikaṃ kṣavathau bhraṃśathau ca yojayanti|7|-

kṣepyaṃ nasyaṃ mūrdhavairecanīyairnāḍyā cūrṇaṃ kṣavathau bhraṃśathau ca ||7||
kuryāt svedān mūrdhni vātāmayaghnān snigdhān dhūmān yadyadanyadvitaṃ ca |8|

kṣavathau bhraṃśathau ca cikitsāmāha- kṣepyamityādi| nasyamiti pradhamanamityarthaḥ, pañcavidhatvānnasyasya| mūrdhavairecanīyaiḥ śirovirecanadravyaiḥ| pradhamanena kṣīṇe kaphe vāte ca vṛddhe sati anilaghnān snigdhānityarthaḥ| anyaditi śirobastimastiṣkādikam||7||-

dīpte roge paittikaṃ saṃvidhānaṃ kuryāt sarvaṃ svādu yacchītalaṃ ca ||8||

dīptarogasya cikitsāmāha- dīpte ityādi| saṃvidhānaṃ karmetyarthaḥ, sarvaṃ bāhmābhyantaram||8||

nāsānāhe snehapānaṃ pradhānaṃ snigdhā dhūmā mūrdhabastiśca nityam |
balātailaṃ sarvathaivopayojyaṃ vātavyādhāvanyaduktaṃ ca yadyat ||9||

nāsānāhacikitsāmāha- nāsānāhe snehapānamityādi| snehapānam uttarabhaktakam| mūrdhabastiśceti cakārāt sālvaṇopanāhādayo gṛhyante| sarvathaiveti pānābhyaṅgānuvāsanaśirobastimastiṣkādibhiḥ sarvaireva prakāraiḥ| anyat aṇutailādikam||9||

nāsāsrāve ghrāṇataścūrṇamuktaṃ nāḍyā deyaṃ yo'vapīḍaśca tīkṣṇaḥ |
tīkṣṇaṃ dhūmaṃ devadārvagnikābhyāṃ māṃsaṃ vā''jaṃ yuktamatrādiśanti ||10||

nāsāsrāvacikitsāmāha- nāsasrāve ityādi| uktaṃ cūrṇaṃ śirovirecanadravyakṛtam| tīkṣṇo'vapīḍo ‘hiṅgu vyoṣaṃ vatsakākhyaṃ’ ityādinā ihaivādhyāye proktaḥ| agnikaḥ citrakaḥ, ajamodāmanye| ājaṃ māṃsaṃ ‘bhojane’ iti śeṣaḥ||10||

nāsāśoṣe kṣīrasarpiḥ pradhānaṃ siddhaṃ tailaṃ cāṇukalpena nasyam |
sarpiḥpānaṃ bhojanaṃ jāṅgalaiśca snehaḥ svedaḥ snaihikaścāpi dhūmaḥ ||11||

nāsāśoṣe cikitsāmāha- nāsāśoṣe kṣīrasarpirityādi| kṣīrasarpiḥ ādita uktam| pradhānaṃ śreṣṭham| nasye nasyaviṣaye| aṇukalpena aṇutailavidhānena vātavyādhyuktena| sarpiḥpānam antarbhaktakam| jāṅgalaiḥ eṇādimāṃsaiḥ, snehaḥ sveda ityarthaḥ| snaihiko dhūmo madhūcchiṣṭādibhiḥ||11||

śeṣān rogān ghrāṇajān sanniyaccheduktaṃ teṣāṃ yadyathā saṃvidhānam ||12||

arśaḥprabhṛtīnāṃ cikitsāmāha- śeṣānityādi| śeṣān rogānarśaḥśophādīn, sanniyacchet cikitset| uktaṃ saṃvidhānaṃ sve sve cikitsite||12||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre nāsāgatarogapratiṣedho nāma trayoviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||23||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre trayoviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ||23||
