--- title: "66. doṣabhedavikalpādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 66 order: 66 ---

athāto doṣabhedavikalpamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

tantrayuttayanantaraṃ pariśiṣṭatvāddoṣabhedādhyāyārambhaḥ- athāta ityādi| athetyādikaṃ pūrvavahyākhyeyam| dhātūn dūṣayantīti doṣā vātādayaḥ, teṣāṃ bhedaḥ pṛthaksaṃsargasannipātabhedena, tasya vikalpanamekaikadyanugamanena nānātvakaraṇaṃ prapañcanaṃ doṣabhedavikalpaḥ, tamadhikṛtya kṛtaḥ, tam||1-2||

aṣṭāṅgavedavidvāṃsaṃ [1] divodāsaṃ mahaujasam |
chinnaśastrārthasandehaṃ sūkṣmāgādhāgamodadhim ||3||
viśvāmitrasutaḥ śrīmān suśrutaḥ paripṛcchati |4|

aṣṭāṅgetyādi| aṣṭāṅgāni śalyādīni vājīkaraṇāntāni, tānyeva veda āyurvedaḥ, tena tatra vā vidvān yastam| mahaujasaṃ mahāprabhāvam| sūkṣmā līnārthāḥ [2] , agādhā duravagāhā ye āgamā evodadhayaste santyasminniti| śrīmāniti rājaśriyā brāhvayā vā alaṅkṛtaḥ| nanu, viśvāmitro gādhirājaḥ, tatsutatvena rājaśriyā yogo yuktaḥ, kathaṃ brāhmyā śriyeti? satyaṃ, viśvāmitrasya brāhmaṇyaṃ tapasā, tato brāhmyā śriyā yogo yukta eva| anye tu kṣatriyāṇāṃ brahmarṣijātatvenobhayayoga iti manyante, apare tu vidyāsamāptyā brāhmayā śriyā yoga iti manyante| tathā coktam- “vidyāsamāptau brāhmaṃ vā sattvamārṣamathāpi vā| dhruvamāviśati jñānāttasmādvaidyo dvijaḥ smṛtaḥ”- iti||3||-

dviṣaṣṭirdoṣabhedā ye purastātparikīrtitāḥ ||4||
kati tatraikaśo jñeyā dviśo vā'pyathavā triśaḥ |5|

dviṣaṣṭirityādi| purastātparikīrtitāḥ iti rasabhedavikalpe| kuto dviṣaṣṭirdoṣabhedāstriṣaṣṭī rasabhedā iti? ucyate- ‘dviṣaṣṭirdoṣabhedāḥ syū rasabhedāstriṣaṣṭidhā| svāsthyaṃ triṣaṣṭaṃ vijñeyaṃ tatra ṣaḍrasayojanam’- iti| kati tatraikaśo jñeyā dviśo vā'pyathavā triśa ityāha- ‘ekaśaḥ ṣaṭ dviśastvekaviṃśatiścaturanvitā| triśo dvātriṃśadityevaṃ trayo doṣāstriṣaṣṭidhā’- ita||4||-

tasya tadvacanaṃ śrutvā saṃśayacchinmahātapāḥ ||5||
prītātmā nṛpaśārdūlaḥ [3] suśrutāyāha tattvataḥ |6|

śiṣyapraśnamākarṇya gururāha- tasyetyādi| tasyeti suśrutasya| tat iti pūrvoktam| saṃśayacchiditi sandehacchettā| prītātmā tṛptāntaḥkaraṇaḥ| nṛpaśārdūlo rājasattamaḥ| tattvata iti yathārthabhāvena||5||-

trayo doṣā dhātavaśca purīṣaṃ mūtrameva ca ||6||
dehaṃ sandhārayantyete hyavyāpannā rasairhitaiḥ |7|

kimāha bhagavānityāha- traya ityādi| trayo doṣā vātādayaḥ, dhātavaḥ sapta rasādayaḥ| avyāpannā avikṛtiṅgatāḥ| rasairhitairiti madhurādirasaiḥ pathyairityarthaḥ| nanu, yathāpraśnaṃ pratyuttare vācye kimarthaṃ kati tatraikaśa ityādipraśnasyottarārthe trayo doṣā ityādyabhidhānam? ucyate- dhātūnāṃ malānāṃ ca doṣasaṃsarge doṣavyapadeśa ityādipratipādanārthaṃ; ata evāsṛjā saha purā pañcadaśa prasarā abhihitāḥ| yadyevaṃ tarhi māṃsādibhiḥ saha prasaraḥ kuto na kīrtitaḥ? ucyate- asaṅkhyeyatvāt| atha kathaṃ tadadhigantavyamiti cet, doṣaliṅgenaiva| tathā cātra vakṣyati- tasmāt prasaṅgaṃ saṃyamyetyādi| nanu, doṣadhātumalamūlaṃ hi śarīramityanenaiva doṣādikāryatvamabhihitaṃ; na ca śarīrāddeho bhinnārthaḥ, tayoḥ paryāyaśabdatvāt; tatkathaṃ punarucyate- ete dehaṃ dhārayantīti? ucyate- dehaśabdenātra karmapuruṣasya cikitsādhikṛtasya vivakṣitatvāt||6||-

puruṣaḥ ṣoḍaśakalaḥ prāṇāścaikādaśaiva ye ||7||
rogāṇāṃ tu sahasraṃ yacchataṃ viṃśatireva ca |
śataṃ [4] ca pañca dravyāṇāṃ trisaptatyadhikottaram ||8||
vyāsataḥ kīrtitaṃ taddhi... |9|

kīdṛśaḥ punarayaṃ dehaśabdābhigheyaḥ karmapuruṣa iti pūrvapratipāditasyaiva karmapuruṣasya svarūpaṃ vācyaṃ, tatprasaṅgenāparamapi prāṇādikaṃ yattatpratipādayituṃ sukhasmaraṇārthaṃ pratipādayati- puruṣa ityādi| atraike kalāśabdena pañcabhūtāni ekādaśendriyāṇīti ṣoḍaśavikārā iti vyācakṣate, parametannāyurvede pramāṇīkṛtam| anye tu kalāśabdamaṅgapratyaṅgeṣvāmananti- śirogrīvāpāṇipādapārśvapṛṣṭhodarāṃsītyaṣṭāṅgāni,
cibukanāsauṣṭhavaṅkṣaṇāṅguṣṭhāṅgulipārṣṇigulphāḥ pratyaṅgānīti| svatantraparatantrābhiprāyānuvartibhirācāryaiḥ kalāśabdo guṇavācī paṭhitaḥ| te ca sarvabhūtacintāśārīroktasukhaduḥkhecchodvegaprayatnādīn ṣoḍaśaguṇānācakṣate [5] | catuṣpādaṃ ṣoḍaśakalaṃ bheṣajaṃ carakācāryeṇoktaṃ; tatra ṣoḍaśakalaṃ ṣoḍaśaguṇamiti vyākhyānayanti| prāṇāścaikādaśeti agniḥ somo vāyuḥ sattvaṃ rajastamaḥ pañcendriyāṇi bhūtātmeti prāṇāḥ| yo'sau bhūtātmā sa eva ṣoḍaśakalaḥ, saṃyogina eva prāṇavyapadeśabhājaḥ, ityekādaśatvam| rogāṇāṃ tu sahasraṃ yacchataṃ viṃśatireva ceti viśatyadhikaikādaśaśatāni rogāṇām| atra sauśrute tantre ṣaṭsthāneṣu proktā rogāḥ ślokairabhidhīyante|-
‘hīnātidagdhaiḥ kṣāreṇa, trayaḥ pluṣṭādayo'gninā|
caturtho dhūmavihataḥ pañca śoṇitaduṣṭayaḥ||1||
doṣadhātumalādīnāṃ dvātriṃśat kṣayavṛddhitaḥ|
dve sthaulyakārśye trividho visraṃsādyo balakṣayaḥ||2||
ṣaṭ śophāḥ ṣaḍ vraṇā vahnitritayaṃ viṣamādikam|
āmaṃ vidagdhaṃ viṣṭabdhamajīrṇaṃ ca tathā tridhā||3||
iti ṣaṭṣaṣṭirātaṅkāḥ sūtrasthāne nidarśitāḥ|
āmapakvāśaye [6] śrotre tathendriyacatuṣṭaye||4||
tvagāmiṣasirāsnāyusandhyasthimajjasambhavāḥ [7] |
śukre caikāṅgasarvāṅgagatāḥ saptādhikā daśa||5||
trayodaśāvṛtairanyairdoṣaiḥ syurmārutaiḥ khalu|
caturvidhaṃ vātaraktamākṣepaścāpatānakaḥ|
pakṣāghāto'patantraśca manyāstambho'rditastathā||6||
gṛdhrasī saha viśvācyā śiraḥ kroṣṭukapūrvakam|
khañjaḥ paṅguḥ kalāyākhyaḥ kaṇṭakaḥ pādadāhakṛt||7||
pādaharṣo'vabāhuśca mūkaminmiṇagadgadāḥ|
tūnyādhmānadvaye'ṣṭhīlādvayamarśāṃsi ṣaṭ tathā||8||
carmakīlaścatasraścāśmaryaḥ pañca bhagandarāḥ|
tathā'ṣṭādaśa kuṣṭhāni kilāsāni punastridhā||9||
pramehā visṃhatiḥ proktāḥ piḍikā nava tatkṛtāḥ|
udarāṇi [8] tathā'ṣṭau ca mūḍhagarbhastathā'ṣṭadhā||10||
bāhyā vidradhayaḥ ṣaṭ syustathā'ntaḥsthāḥ smṛtā daśa|
visarpanāḍīstanajāstathaiva pañca pañca ca||11||
granthayaḥ sapta caikā syādapacī saptadhā'rbudam|
galagaṇḍāstrayaḥ sapta vṛddhayaḥ parikīrtitāḥ||12||
upadaṃśā matāḥ pañca ślīpadaṃ ca tathā tridhā|
bhagnā aṣṭādaśa jñeyāḥ śūkadoṣāstathaiva ca|
catvāriṃśattathā'ṣṭau ca kṣudrarogāḥ prakīrtitāḥ||13||
aṣṭāvoṣṭhabhavā dantamūleṣu daśa pañca ca||14||
aṣṭau danteṣu jihvāyāṃ pañca tālugatā nava|
kaṇṭhe cāṣṭādaśa jñeyāścatuḥsarvasarā gadāḥ||15||
evaṃ mukhe saptaṣaṣṭiriti sthāne dvitīyake|
dvācatvāriṃśadadhikā triśatī parikīrtitā||16||
aṣṭau śukragatā rogā aṣṭāvārtavaduṣṭayaḥ|
catvāro'sṛgdarāḥ proktā apātastvaparākṛtaḥ||17||
makkallalīnaśoṣāśca naigameśāhṛtastathā|
nāgodaraḥ srutirgarbhe śārīre saptaviṃśatiḥ||18||
atha medonilāvegācchvayathuḥ sarujaśca yaḥ|
āḍhyavātaḥ sarvasarāḥ śophāḥ pañca prakīrtitāḥ|
karṇapālyāmayāḥ pañca klaibyamuktaṃ caturvidham [9] ||19||
vāntarecitayoḥ proktā vyāpado daśa pañca ca|
ṣaṇnetrapraṇidhānasya netrasyaikādaśaiva tu||20||
pañca bastikṛtāstatra catvāraḥ pīḍane kṛtāḥ|
ekādaśa dravyakṛtāḥ sapta śayyākṛtāstathā||21||
catvāriṃśaccatasraśca vaidyato vyāpadastathā|
krodhāyāsādikāḥ pañca daśa cāturahetukāḥ||22||
snehasya kāraṇānyaṣṭāvapratyāgamakṛnti ca|
iti netrādidoṣeṇa ṣaṣṭiḥ sapta samāsataḥ||23||
evaṃ cikitsitasthāne rujo'ṣṭānavatistathā|
annādirakṣāvijñāne viṃśatirviṣahetukāḥ||24||
vegāḥ syuḥ sthāvare darvīkaramaṇḍalināṃ viṣe|
rājilavaikarañjānāṃ pratyekaṃ sapta sapta ca||25||
mūṣikāstu daśāṣṭau ca sapta vegā alarkajāḥ|
saptaṣaṣṭiśataṃ cātra kīṭānāṃ viṣadāyinām [10] ||26||
saptacatvāriṃśadyutaṃ kalpasthāne śatadvayam|
nava sandhyāśrayāḥ proktā vartmajāścaikaviṃśatiḥ||27||
śuklabhāge daśaikaśca catvāraḥ kṛṣṇabhāgajāḥ|
sarvāśrayāḥ saptadaśa dṛṣṭijā dvādaśaiva tu||28||
bāhyajau dvau netrarogāviti ṣaṭsaptatiḥ smṛtāḥ|
kukūṇakaḥ śiśoreva karṇe'ṣṭāviṃśatirnṛṇām||29||
ekatriṃśadghrāṇagatāḥ sapratiśyāyapañcakāḥ|
ekādaśa [11] śirorogāḥ paraṃ śālākyasañjñite||30||
ātaṅkānāṃ śataṃ proktaṃ ṣaṭcatvāriṃśatā yutam|
nava bālagrahā yonivyāpado viṃśatiḥ striyāḥ||31||
evaṃ kumāratantre'sminnekonatriṃśadāmayāḥ|
aṣṭau jvarā hyatīsārāḥ ṣaṭ catasraḥ pravāhikāḥ||32||
catvāro grahaṇīdoṣā yakṣmaiko gulmapañcakam|
hṛdrogāḥ pañca catvāraḥ pāṇḍvākhyāḥ kāmalādvayam||33||
halīmakaḥ pānakī ca raktapitaṃ caturvidham|
ṣaṭprakārā matā mūrcchā vikārāḥ sapta madyajāḥ||34||
dāhāḥ pañca tṛṣaḥ sapta chardayaḥ pañca dehinām|
hikkāḥ śvāsāstathā kāsāḥ pratyekaṃ pañca pañca ca||35||
svarabhedāstathā ṣaṭ syurviśatiḥ kṛmijātayaḥ|
navodāvartakā dṛṣṭā visūcyastisra eva ca||36||
ānāhau dvāvāmaviṭkau tathā'rocakapañcakam|
mūtrāghātā dvādaśa syuriti kāyacikitsite||37||
āmayānāṃ śataṃ proktaṃ catvāriṃśacca sapta ca|
devatā-daitya-gandharva-yakṣa-pitra-hi-rakṣasām||38||
piśacasyābhiṣaṅgeṇa gadāścāṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ|
apasmārāśca catvāra unmādāḥ ṣaḍudīritāḥ||39||
aṣṭādaśagadā bhūtavidyāyāṃ sūkṣmadarśibhiḥ|
evaṃ hi sauśrute tantre kāśīrājena kīrtitāḥ||40||
rogāṇāṃ tu sahasraṃ yacchataṃ viṃśatireva ca|
vyāsato vistarataḥ| hiśabdo hetvarthe, yasmāt kāraṇāt||7-8||-

... bhinnā doṣāstrayo guṇāḥ |
dviṣaṣṭidhā bhavantyete bhūyiṣṭhamiti niścayaḥ ||9||

bhinnā ityādi| bhinnā bhedaṅgatā dviṣaṣṭidhā bhavantītyarthaḥ| trayo doṣā vātapittaśleṣmāṇaḥ| guṇā guṇamayāḥ sattvarajastamomayāḥ| tadyathā- rajobhūyiṣṭho mārutaḥ, rajo hi pravartakaṃ sarvabhāvānāṃ; pittaṃ sattvotkaṭaṃ, laghuprakāśakatvāt, rajoyuktaṃ vetyeke; kaphaḥ tamobahulaḥ, guruprāvaraṇātmakatvādityāhurbhiṣajaḥ| yadyevaṃ tat kathaṃ kaphaprakṛtike puṃsi sattvaguṇopapannatā paṭhitā? ucyate- guṇādvitayamapi kaphe jñātavyaṃ, ‘sattvatamobahulā āpaḥ’ ityuktatvāt||9||

traya eva pṛthak doṣā dviśo nava samādhikaiḥ |
trayodaśādhikaikadvisamamadhyolbaṇaistriśaḥ ||10||
pañcāśadevaṃ tu saha bhavanti kṣayamāgataiḥ |
kṣīṇamadhyādhikakṣīṇakṣīṇavṛddhaistathā'paraiḥ [12] ||11||
dvādaśaivaṃ samākhyātāstrayo doṣā dviṣaṣṭidhā |12|

kathamete dviṣaṣṭidhā bhavantītyāha- traya evetyādi| tatra pṛthak pratyekaṃ, doṣā vātapittaśleṣmāṇastraya eva, itarau ca dvau svasthāviti jñeyau| vṛddho vātaḥ svasthau pittaśleṣmāṇau (1), vṛddhaṃ pittaṃ svasthau vātaśleṣmāṇau (2), vṛddhaḥ śleṣmā svasthe vātapitte (3), iti| dviśo nava samādhikairiti tatra samavṛddhābhyāṃ dvābhyāṃ doṣābhyāṃ trayaḥ saṃsargā bhavanti; yathā [13] - vātapitte same vṛddhe svasthaḥ śleṣmā (1), vātaśleṣmāṇau samau vṛddhau svasthaṃ pittaṃ (2), pittaśleṣmāṇau samau vṛddhau svastho vātaḥ (3), iti| anyatarādhikavṛddhābhyāṃ ṣaṭ [14] ; tadyathā- vātapittayorvṛddhayorvāto'dhiko vṛddhaḥ svasthaḥ śleṣmā (1), vātapittayorvṛddhayoḥ pittamadhikavṛddhaṃ svasthaḥ śleṣmā (2), vātaśleṣmaṇorvṛddhayorvāto'dhikavṛddhaḥ svasthaṃ pittaṃ (3), vātaśleṣmaṇorvṛddhayoḥ śleṣmā'dhikavṛddhaḥ svasthaṃ pittaṃ (4), pittaśleṣmaṇorvṛddhayoḥ pittamadhikaṃ vṛddhaṃ svastho vātaḥ (5), pittaśleṣmaṇorvṛddhayoḥ śleṣmā adhikavṛddhaḥ svastho vātaḥ (6)| triśastu vṛddhaistrayodaśa; tatra tribhiradhikairekaḥ adhikaikadoṣāstrayaḥ, adhikadvidoṣāstrayaḥ, samamadhyolbaṇadoṣāḥ ṣaṭ| tadyathā- vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ vāto'dhikavṛddhaḥ (1), vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ pittamadhikavṛddhaṃ (2), vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ śleṣmā'dhikavṛddhaḥ (3)| athādhikadvivṛddhadoṣāḥ; tadyathā- vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ vātapitte adhikavṛddhe (1), vātapittaśleṣmāṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ vātaśleṣmāṇau adhikavṛddhau (2), vātapittaśleṣmaṇāṃ vṛddhānāṃ pittaśleṣmāṇāvadhikavṛddhau (3)| kṣīṇo vātaḥ svasthau pittaśleṣmāṇau (1), kṣīṇaṃ pittaṃ svasthau vātaśleṣmāṇau (2), kṣīṇaḥ śleṣmā svasthe vātapite (3), evaṃ pṛthak traya eva| atha hīnamadhyolbaṇavṛddhāḥ ṣaḍucyante, tatra samaḥ samavṛddho hīnavṛddha ityarthaḥ; tadyathā- hīnavṛddho vātaḥ, madhyavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ, adhikavṛddhaḥ śleṣmā (1); hīnavṛddho vātaḥ, madhyavṛddhaḥ śleṣmā, adhikavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ (2); hīnavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ, adhikavṛddho vātaḥ, madhyavṛddhaḥ śleṣmā (3), madhyavṛddho vātaḥ, hīnavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ, adhikavṛddhoḥ śleṣmā (4); hīnavṛddhaḥ śleṣmā, adhikavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ, madhyavṛddho vātaḥ (5); adhikavṛddho vātaḥ, madhyavṛddhaṃ pittaṃ hīnavṛddhaḥ śleṣmā 6| sarvadoṣaiḥ samavṛddhairekaḥ| uktaṃ ca tantrāntare- ‘dvyulbaṇaikolbaṇaiḥ ṣaṭ syurhīnamadhyādhikaiśca ṣaṭ| samaiścaiko vikārāste sannipātāstrayodaśa”- iti| pañcāśadityādi| evamanena prakāreṇa kṣayamāgataiḥ pañcaviṃśatidoṣabhedaiḥ saha pañcāśadbhavanti| tadyathā- vātapittakaphairekaikaiḥ kṣīṇaistrayo bhedā bhavanti, dviśo nava procyante| tadyathā- vātapitte samakṣīṇe svasthaḥ śleṣmā (1), vātaśleṣmāṇau samakṣīṇau svasthaṃ pittaṃ (2), pittaśleṣmāṇau samahīnau svasthe vātaḥ 3| adhikakṣīṇāḥ ṣaṭ;
