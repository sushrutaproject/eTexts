--- title: "10. pittābhiṣyandapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 10 order: 10 ---

athātaḥ pittābhiṣyandapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

vātābhiṣyandapratiṣedhānantaraṃ ‘vātāddaśa tathā pittāt’ iti vacanāt pittābhiṣyandapratiṣedhārambhaḥ- athāta ityādi||1-2||

pittasyande paittike cādhimanthe raktāsrāvaḥ sraṃsanaṃ cāpi kāryam |
akṣṇoḥ sekālepanasyāñjanāni paitte ca syādyadvisarpe vidhānam ||3||

pittetyādi| sraṃsanaṃ virekaḥ| pittavisarpavidhānaṃ ‘uśīralāmajjakacandanāni’ ityādipralepāḥ| pūrvavadatrāpyapatarpaṇādibhiḥ kṛtāmapācanasya snehapūrvakaḥ sirāmokṣaḥ||3||

gundrāṃ śāliṃ śaivalaṃ śailabhedaṃ dārvīmelāmutpalaṃ rodhramabhram |
padmātpatraṃ śarkarā darbhamikṣuṃ tālaṃ rodhraṃ vetasaṃ padmakaṃ ca ||4||
drākṣāṃ kṣaudraṃ candanaṃ yaṣṭikāhvaṃ yoṣitkṣīraṃ rātryanante ca piṣṭvā |
sarpiḥsiddhaṃ tarpaṇe sekanasye [1] śastaṃ kṣīraṃ siddhameteṣu cājam ||5||
yojyo vargo vyasta eṣo'nyathā vā samyaṅnasye'ṣṭārdhasaṅkhye'pi nityam |6|

idānīṃ pittasyābhiṣyandayaugikadravyāṇyāha- gundrāmityādi| gundrā erakābhedaḥ, gavedhuketyanye, śaivalaṃ dūrvā, śailabhedaḥ pāṣāṇabhedaḥ, candanaṃ raktacandanaṃ, rātriḥ haridrā, anantā utpalasārivā, yoṣitkṣīraṃ strīstanyaṃ, śarkarādeḥ sāhacaryāt kalkadravye ikṣuriti ikṣurasena dravakāryam| vyastaḥ asamastaḥ, anyathā vā samasta ityarthaḥ| yadyapi vargatvāt ‘samastaṃ vargamardhaṃ vā yathālābhamathāpi vā’ iti śalyatantroktaparibhāṣayaiva samastavyastatā prāptā, tathā'pi śālākyatantratvādatra punaruktā| nasye aṣṭārdhasaṅkhye catuḥsaṅkhye pratimarṣāvapīḍanasyaśirovirecanākhye||4-5||-

kriyāḥ sarvā pittaharyaḥ praśastāstryahāccordhvaṃ kṣīrasarpiśca nasyam [2] ||6||

kriyā ityādi| kṣīrasarpiśceti ‘bhojane’ iti śeṣaḥ| nasyamityupalakṣaṇaṃ, tenāścyotanasekāñjanādi gṛhyate| yadyapi ‘paitte ca syādyadvisarpe vidhānaṃ’ ityanenaiva pittaharī kriyā kathitā, tathā'pi tatra vyādhipratyanīkatayoktā, atra tu doṣapratyanīkatveneti na doṣaḥ||6||

pālāśaṃ syācchoṇitaṃ cāñjanārthe śallakyā vā śarkarākṣaudrayuktam |7|

raktapittaprasādakāñjanānāṃ rasakriyā prathamā, atastāmāha- pālāśamityādi| pālāśaṃ śoṇitaṃ kiṃśukarasaḥ; anye palāśakhapuramāhuḥ|7|-

rasakriyāṃ śarkarākṣaudrayuktāṃ pālindyāṃ vā madhuke vā'pi kuryāt ||7||

aparāmapi rasakriyāmāha- rasakriyāmityādi| rasakriyāphāṇitākṛtiḥ| tathā coktam- “gṛhītvā kvāthakalpena kvāthaṃ pūtaṃ punaḥ punaḥ| kvāthayet phāṇitākārameṣā proktā rasakriyā” iti| pālindī śyāmā trivṛt, sārivāmanye||7||

mustā phenaḥ sāgarasyotpalaṃ ca kṛmighnailādhātribījādrasaśca |8|

aparamapi rasakriyāñjanamāha- mustetyādi| kṛmighnā viḍaṅgāḥ, dhātrī āmalakaṃ, bījaḥ pītasāraḥ ‘bījaka’ iti yāvat, mustādīnāṃ rase rasakriyā kāryā|8|-

tālīśailāgairikośīraśaṅkhairevaṃ yuñjyādañjanaṃ stanyapiṣṭaiḥ ||8||

vartyañjanamāha- tālīśetyādi| kāśīśaileti kecit paṭhanti||8||

cūrṇaṃ kuryādañjanārthe raso vā stanyopeto dhātakīsyandanābhyām |9|

cūrṇāñjanamāha- cūrṇamityādi| raso veti rasakriyā vetyarthaḥ| syandano'nenaiva nāmnā prasiddhaḥ| kecit candanābhyāmiti paṭhanti|9|-

yoṣitstanyaṃ śātakumbhaṃ vighṛṣṭaṃ kṣaudropetaṃ kaiṃśukaṃ cāpi puṣpam ||9||

yoṣidityādi| śātakumbhaṃ suvarṇaṃ, tacca yoṣitstanyena ghṛṣṭamañjanam| kṣaudrayuktaṃ vā kiṃśukapuṣpaṃ ghṛṣṭaṃ tāmrapātrādāvañjanam||9||

rodhraṃ drākṣāṃ śarkarāmutpalaṃ ca nāryāḥ kṣīre yaṣṭikāhvaṃ vacāṃ ca |
piṣṭvā kṣīre varṇakasya tvacaṃ ca toyonmiśre candanodumbare ca ||10||

rodhramityādi| rodhrādivacāntaṃ nārīkṣīre piṣṭamekamañjanam| varṇako rocanikā; tasyāstvak nārīkṣīreṇa piṣṭā dvitīyamañjanam| anye varṇakatvakśabdena karṇikāravṛkṣasya valkalaṃ kathayanti, tannecchati gayī| toyonmiśre candanodumbare ceti tṛtīyāñjanaṃ, toyonmiśre bālakayukte, candanaṃ kucandanaṃ; pañjikākārastu candanodumbaraṃ bhṛśataraṃ cūrṇitaṃ vastrabaddhaṃ toyaplutamāścyotanāñjanamāha||10||

kāryaḥ phenaḥ sāgarasyāñjanārthe nārīstanye mākṣike cāpi ghṛṣṭaḥ |
yoṣitstanye sthāpitaṃ yaṣṭikāhvaṃ rodhraṃ drākṣāṃ śarkarāmutpalaṃ ca ||11||
kṣaumābaddhaṃ pathyamāścyotane vā sarpirghṛṣṭaṃ [3] yaṣṭikāhvaṃ sarodhram |
toyonmiśrāḥ kāśmarīdhātripathyāstadvaccāhuḥ kaṭphalaṃ cāmbunaiva ||12||

kārya ityādi| aparamāścyotanamāha- nārītyādi| yaṣṭimadhukādidravyamatīva cūrṇitaṃ strīstanyasthāpitaṃ kṣaumavastrāvabaddhamāścyotane hitamityarthaḥ| yaṣṭikāhvaṃ sarodhramiti yaṣṭimadhukarodhre vā ghṛtaghṛṣṭe [4] pūrvavadāścyotanam||11-12||

eṣo'mlākhye'nukramaścāpi śuktau kāryaḥ sarvaḥ syātsirāmokṣavarjyaḥ ||13||

idānīmamlādhyuṣitaśuktyoḥ sāmānyacikitsāmāha- eṣa ityādi| kecidamlādhyuṣitaśuktyoraśastrakṛtyatvādeva sirāmokṣavarjanaṃ siddham, ataḥ ‘śirāmokṣavarjya’ iti pāṭhaṃ na paṭhanti||13||

sarpiḥ peyaṃ traiphalaṃ tailvakaṃ vā peyaṃ vā syāt kevalaṃ yat purāṇam |14|

amlādhyuṣite viśeṣacikitsitamāha- sarpirityādi| ādyaṃ ghṛtadvayaṃ saṃśodhanaṃ, purāṇaghṛtaṃ tu saṃśamanam|14|-

doṣe'dhastācchuktikāyāmapāste śītairdravyairañjanaṃ kāryamāśu ||14||

śuktikāyāṃ viśeṣacikitsitamāha- doṣe ityādi| adhastādapāste doṣe ‘virecanena’ iti śeṣaḥ||14||

vaidūryaṃ yat sphāṭikaṃ vaidrumaṃ ca mauktaṃ śāṅkhaṃ rājataṃ śātakumbham |
cūrṇaṃ sūkṣmaṃ śarkarākṣaudrayuktaṃ śuktiṃ hanyādañjanaṃ caitadāśu ||15||

tānyeva śītadravyāṇyāha- vaidūryamityādi||15||

yuñjyāt sarpirdhūmadarśī narastu śeṣaṃ kuryādraktapitte vidhānam |
yaccaivānyat pittahṛccāpi sarvaṃ yadvīsarpe paittike vai vidhānam ||16||

dhūmadarśinaścikitsitamāha- yuñjyādityādi| raktapitte vidhānamiti raktābhiṣyande pittābhiṣyande ca cikitsitaṃ yaduktaṃ tadityarthaḥ| anyat pittahṛditi pittavidagdhadṛṣṭeryadvidhānaṃ tadatra yojayet| yadvīsarpe paittike vai vidhānamiti pittavisarpoktaṃ vidhānaṃ yojayediti sambandhaḥ||16||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre pittābhiṣyandapratiṣedho nāma daśamo'dhyāyaḥ ||10||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre daśamo'dhyāyaḥ||10||

1. ‘nāvane ca’ iti pā.|
2. ‘śastā'śeṣā pittahantrī kriyā ca tryahādūrdhvaṃ kṣīrasarpiśca nasyam’ iti pā.|
3. ‘sarpirbhṛṣṭaṃ’ iti pā.|
4. ‘ghṛtabhṛṣṭe’ iti pā.|
