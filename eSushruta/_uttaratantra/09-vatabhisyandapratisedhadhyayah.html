--- title: "9. vātābhiṣyandapratiṣedhādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 9 order: 9 ---

athāto vātābhiṣyandapratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

cikitsitapravibhāgīyānantaraṃ sarveṣāṃ nayanāmayānāmabhiṣyandamūlatvāt, tatrāpi pittādīnāṃ vātasyaivāvaraṇatvena nidānabhūtatvādādau vātābhiṣyandapratiṣedho yukta ityata āha- athāta ityādi| atrādiśabdo lupto draṣṭavyaḥ, tena vātābhiṣyandādipratiṣedhaṃ vyākhyāsyāma ityarthaḥ; pratiṣedhaḥ cikitsitaṃ; evaṃ pittābhiṣyandapratiṣedhādiṣvādiśabdo draṣṭavyaḥ||1-2||

purāṇasarpiṣā snigdhau syandādhīmanthapīḍitau |
svedayitvā yathānyāyaṃ sirāmokṣeṇa yojayet ||3||
sampādayedbastibhistu samyak snehavirecitau |
tarpaṇaiḥ puṭapākaiśca dhūmairāścyotanaistathā ||4||
nasyasnehaparīṣekaiḥ śirobastibhireva ca |5|

tatra sirāvyadhasādhyeṣu pañcadaśasvabhiṣyandādhimanthau paṭhitau, sirāvyadhasādhyānāṃ cākṣirogāṇāṃ śoṇitamūlatvānmūlocchedārthaṃ pūrvaṃ dūṣyanirharaṇaṃ darśayannāha- purāṇetyādi| purāṇaṃ sarpiḥ saṃvatsaroṣitaṃ ghṛtam; anye daśavarṣasthitaṃ ghṛtaṃ purāṇaṃ kathayanti| yathānyāyaṃ yathāśāstramityarthaḥ| tena dṛṣṭeḥ samīpamuttamāṅgamalpaṃ svedayet, dṛṣṭirasvedyā| yathānyāyamiti padaṃ snehapānasirāmokṣābhyāṃ saha yojayet| tena snehopayaugikokto vidhiḥ ‘prāk pācitāmadoṣāya’ ityādiko'vadhāraṇīyaḥ| tenātrāpi doṣavyādhinirāmatāyāmeva sarpiḥpānādayo vidheyāḥ| tathā ca tantrāntaraṃ,- “svedaḥ pralepastiktānnaṃ dhūmo dinacatuṣṭayam| laṅghanaṃ cākṣirogāṇāmāmānāṃ pācanāni ṣaṭ|| añjanaṃ sarpiṣaḥ pānaṃ kaṣāyaṃ gurubhojanam| netrarogeṣu sāmeṣu snānaṃ ca parivarjayet”- iti| videhe'pi,- “prāgevākṣyāmaye bhaktaṃ trirātramaguru smṛtam| upavāsastryahaṃ vā syānnaktaṃ vā'pyaśanaṃ hitam|| tataścaturthe divase vyādhau sañjātalakṣaṇe| yathoktāstu kriyāḥ kāryā nasyasekāñjanādikāḥ”- iti| sirāmokṣeṇetyatra sirā upanāsikā lālāṭyā vā āpāṅgyā vā| dūṣyaharaṇaṃ pratipādya doṣacikitsitamāha- sampādayedityādi| sampādayet yojayet| bastibhiḥ snehabastibhirnirūhairvā| samyagiti hīnamithyātiyogarahitaiḥ| snehavirecitāviti snehavirecanaṃ tu pittasyānugate pittāsṛganugate vā vāyau balavato bahudoṣasya ceti| durbalasyālpadoṣasya ca puruṣasya svasthānagato malāvṛtaḥ svatantro vā bastibhirevāvajetavyaḥ| bastirapi malāvṛte vāte nairūhikaḥ, dhātukṣayaje snaihikaḥ| samyakśabdo bastyādiṣu śirobastyanteṣu sambadhyate| tarpaṇādividhānaṃ kriyākalpe bhaviṣyati| tatra sekaḥ sukhoṣṇaḥ, āścyotanaṃ ca śītameva||3-4||-

vātaghnānūpajalajamāṃsāmlakvāthasecanaiḥ ||5||
snehaiścaturbhiruṣṇaiśca tatpītāmbaradhāraṇaiḥ |
payobhirvesavāraiśca sālvaṇaiḥ pāyasaistathā ||6||
bhiṣak sampādayedetāvupanāhaiśca pūjitaiḥ |7|

pūrvaṃ svedayitvetyuktaṃ, tatra dravādisvedānupapādayannāha- vātaghnetyādi| vātaghnāni bhadradārvādīni| uṣṇaiḥ sukhoṣṇaiḥ| uṣṇairiti padaṃ vātaghnādibhiḥ sambadhyate| tatpītāmbaradhāraṇairiti catuḥsnehamitavastradhāraṇairityarthaḥ| sālvaṇairiti sālvaṇasyaikatvāt kathaṃ bahuvacanaṃ? satyaṃ, yaugikairanyairapi dravyaiḥ svedaḥ kārya iti bodhanārthaṃ bahuvacanaṃ, svedayitvā sirāmokṣeṇa yojayedityuktam||5-6||-

grāmyānūpaudakarasaiḥ snigdhaiḥ phalarasānvitaiḥ ||7||
susaṃskṛtaiḥ payobhiśca tayorāhāra iṣyate |8|

na cābhuktavataḥ śastrakarma kuryādityāśaṅkyāhāramāha- grāmyetyādi| phalarasānvitairiti dāḍimāmalakarasasaṃyuktaiḥ| susaṃskṛtaiḥ payobhiriti śatāvarīśṛṅgaveraprabhṛtidravyasaṃskṛtairityarthaḥ| tayoḥ syandādhimanthayoḥ||7||-

tathā copari bhaktasya sarpiḥpānaṃ(ne)praśasyate [1] ||8||
triphalākvāthasaṃsiddhaṃ kevalaṃ jīrṇameva vā |
siddhaṃ vātaharaiḥ kṣīraṃ prathamena gaṇena vā ||9||
snehāstailādvinā siddhā vātaghnaistarpaṇe hitāḥ |10|

purāṇasarpiṣā snigdhāvityuktaṃ tasya ca ghṛtasyāvacāraṇakālaṃ niyamayannāha- tathetyādi| kevalam asaṃskṛtam| vātaharaiḥ bhadradārvādibhiḥ| jejjaṭācāryastu kākolyādibhirvātaharairityāha| upari pānaṃ pibediti śeṣaḥ| prathamena gaṇena vidārigandhādinā| gayadāsācāryastu sarvatrāpi ghṛtaṃ manyate| tathā ca tadvyākhyānam- “triphalāghṛtaṃ vyādhipratyanīkaṃ, vātaharadevadārvādisiddhaṃ tu doṣapratyanīkaṃ, vātasyaiva pittānubandhe sati vidārigandhādisiddhaṃ”; īdṛśe ca vyākhyāne ‘siddhaṃ vātaharairvā'pi prathamena gaṇena vā’ iti pāṭhaḥ||8-9||-

snaihikaḥ puṭapākaśca dhūmo nasyaṃ ca tadvidham ||10||
nasyādiṣu sthirākṣīramadhuraistailamiṣyate |11|

pūrvaṃ tarpaṇairityuktaṃ, tatra tarpaṇadravyāṇyāha- snaihika ityādi| snaihikaḥ puṭapākaḥ kriyākalpe vakṣyamāṇaḥ| tadvidhaṃ snaihikamityarthaḥ||10||-

eraṇḍapallave mūle tvaci vā''jaṃ payaḥ śṛtam ||11||
kaṇṭakāryāśca mūleṣu sukhoṣṇaṃ secane hitam |12|

āntaramupakramamabhidhāya bāhyamāha- eraṇḍetyādi| secane ityatra cakāro lupto draṣṭavyaḥ; tena na kevalaṃ seke āścyotane cetyarthaḥ| tatra seke sukhoṣṇaṃ, āścyotane ca śītamevetyarthaḥ||11||-

saindhavodīcyayaṣṭyāhvapippalībhiḥ śṛtaṃ payaḥ ||12||
hitamardhodakaṃ seke tathā''ścyotanameva ca |13|

saindhavetyādi| ardhodakamiti ardhamudakaṃ yasmin kṣīre tat kṣīraṃ seke hitaṃ tathā āścyotane ca; na punaḥ pānādau| saindhavādipramāṇaṃ tu kṣīrādaṣṭamāṃśameva||12||-

hīberavakramañjiṣṭhodumbaratvakṣu sādhitam ||13||
sāmbhaśchāgaṃ payo vā'pi śūlāścyotanamuttamam |14|

hrīberetyādi| hrīberaṃ vālakam| vakraṃ tagaram| sāmbha iti caturguṇodakaṃ, ardhodakamiti kecit| āścyotanam akṣipūraṇam||13||-

madhukaṃ rajanīṃ pathyāṃ devadāruṃ ca peṣayet ||14||
ājena payasā śreṣṭhamabhiṣyande tadañjanam |15|

madhukamityādi| kecidamuṃ pāṭhaṃ na paṭhanti||14||-

gairikaṃ saindhavaṃ kṛṣṇāṃ nāgaraṃ ca yathottaram ||15||
dviguṇaṃ piṣṭamadbhistu guṭikāñjanamiṣyate |16|

gairikamityādi| yathottaraṃ dviguṇamiti gairikāddviguṇaṃ saindhavaṃ, saindhavāddviguṇā kṛṣṇā, tato'pi dviguṇaṃ nāgaram| guṭikāñjanamiṣyate iti snaihikapuṭapākādijanitakaphānubandhasya guṭikāñjanam| kecidatrāpyājena payaseti sambadhnanti; tenājapayasā piṣṭamadbhirvā| ‘nāgaraṃ ca yathottaraṃ’ ityatra ‘godantaṃ ca yathottaraṃ’ iti paṭhanti; sa ca pāṭho jejjaṭācāryeṇa dūṣitaḥ||15||-

snehāñjanaṃ hitaṃ cātra vakṣyate tadyathāvidhi ||16||

snehetyādi| yathāvidhīti vidhimanatikramya [2] , etenāpatarpaṇasirāvyadhakupitavāte'bhiṣyande snehāñjanaṃ hitamityarthaḥ| atraike ‘tāmrapātrasthitaṃ māsaṃ sarpiḥsaindhavasaṃyutaṃ’ [3] iti snehāñjanamāhuḥ| anye tu ‘vasā cānūpajalajā saindhavena samāyutā’ ityādinā vakṣyamāṇaṃ snehāñjanaṃ kathayanti||16||

rogo yaścānyatovāto yaśca mārutaparyayaḥ |
anenaiva vidhānena bhiṣak tāvapi sādhayet ||17||

vātābhiṣyandakriyāmanyasminnapi vātaje'kṣiroge'tidiśannāha- roga ityādi||17||

pūrvabhaktaṃ hitaṃ sarpiḥ kṣīraṃ vā'pyatha bhojane |
vṛkṣādanyāṃ kapitthe ca pañcamūle mahatyapi ||18||
sakṣīraṃ karkaṭarase siddhaṃ cātra ghṛtaṃ pibet |
siddhaṃ vā hitamatrāhuḥ pattūrārtagalagnikaiḥ ||19||
sakṣīraṃ meṣaśṛṅgyā vā sarpirvīratareṇa vā |20|

anyatovātamārutaparyayayoḥ sāmānyāṃ cikitsāmabhidhāya viśeṣacikitsāmāha- pūrvabhaktamityādi| tadeva ghṛtamāha- vṛkṣādanyāmityādi| vṛkṣādanī bandākaḥ, vṛkṣakalambuketyanye| vṛkṣādanyādīnāṃ saptadravyāṇāmatra kaṣāyaḥ| agarbhaṃ ghṛtamidam| siddhamityādi| pattūraḥ sitivāraḥ, ‘sirivālī’ iti loke prasiddhā| ārtagalaḥ kaṇṭakaphalo jalamuktadeśajaḥ, ‘kakubha’ ityanye| agnikaḥ ajamodā| pattūrādīnāṃ kaṣāye kṣīre ca sādhitaṃ sarpiḥ meṣaśṛṅgīkvāthe vā sakṣīre sādhitam| vīrataraḥ śaraḥ vellantaro vā, tasya kvāthe vā sakṣīre sādhitaṃ sarpiranyatovātamārutaparyayayorhitam| idamapi ghṛtatrayamagarbham||18-19||-

saindhavaṃ dāru śuṇṭhī ca mātuluṅgaraso ghṛtam ||20||
stanyodakābhyāṃ kartavyaṃ śuṣkapāke tadañjanam |
pūjitaṃ sarpiṣaścātra pānamakṣṇośca tarpaṇam ||21||
ghṛtena jīvanīyena nasyaṃ tailena cāṇunā |
pariṣeke hitaṃ cātra payaḥ śītaṃ sasaindhavam ||22||
rajanīdārusiddhaṃ vā saindhavena samāyutam |
sarpiryutaṃ stanyaghṛṣṭamañjanaṃ vā mahauṣadham ||23||
vasā vā''nūpajalajā saindhavena samāyutā |
nāgaronmiśritā kiñcicchuṣkapāke tadañjanam ||24||

saindhavamityādi| saindhavādidravyatrayāṇāṃ kalkena mātuluṅgādidravyacatuṣkeṇa rasakriyāṃ niṣpādya śuṣkapāke'ñjanaṃ kāryaṃ; yato rasakriyāñjanamidam| pūjitamityādi| sarpiṣo ‘jīvanīyasiddhasya’ iti śeṣaḥ| atra śuṣkapāke nasyaṃ jīvanīyena ghṛtenaiva; aṇunā tailena vā nasyaṃ pūjitam| aṇutailaṃ tu śālākyatantroktaṃ, na punarvātavyādhyuditam| pariṣeke ityādi| śītamiti śṛtaśītamityarthaḥ| sasaindhavamiti saśabda īṣadarthaḥ| rajanīdārusiddhaṃ vetyatra kṣīramiti sambadhyate| saindhavena samāyutam alpasaindhavayuktamityarthaḥ| sarpirityādi|- mahauṣadhaṃ vā ghṛtastrīstanyayuktaṃ pāṣāṇaghṛṣṭamityarthaḥ, añjanaṃ vā| vasetyādi|- ānūpajalajā vā vasā stokasaindhavayuktā kiñcicchuṇṭhīyuktā pāṣāṇaghṛṣṭā satī śuṣkapāke'ñjanamiti sambandhaḥ||20-24||

pavanaprabhavā rogā ye keciddṛṣṭināśanāḥ |
bījenānena matimān teṣu karma prayojayet ||25||

idānīṃ vātajeṣu timirakācādiṣveṣaiva kriyā kartavyeti darśayannāha- pavanetyādi| dṛṣṭināśanāḥ timirakācādayaḥ| bījena bījabhūtena| anena vātābhiṣyandoktakarmaṇā| matimān ūhāpohavit||25||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre
vātābhiṣyandapratiṣedho nāma navamo'dhyāyaḥ ||9||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate śālākyatantre navamo'dhyāyaḥ||9||

1. `bhaktasyopari sarpistau pibetāṃ cāpi mānavau’ iti pā.|
2. `vidheranatikramāt’ iti pā.|
3. `māṃsaṃ sarpiḥ saindhavasaṃyutam’ iti pā.|
