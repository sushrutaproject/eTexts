--- title: "27. navagrahākṛtivijñānīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 27 order: 27 ---

athāto navagrahākṛtivijñānīyamadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

śālākyatantrānantaraṃ kaumāratantraṃ prārabhyate- athāta ityādi| bālaśabdo'tra luptanirdiṣṭo draṣṭavyaḥ| tatra grahā bālānāṃ nava, aṣṭau punarabālānāṃ bhūtavidyāyāṃ nirdeṣṭavyāḥ| ākṛtiḥ ākāro liṅgaṃ kāryabhūtaṃ, natu svabhāvākhyam||1-2||

bālagrahāṇāṃ vijñānaṃ sādhanaṃ cāpyanantaram |
utpattiṃ kāraṇaṃ caiva suśrutaikamanāḥ śṛṇu ||3||

tatra prathamaṃ kumāratantrasyārthapiṇḍamuddiśannāha- bālagrahāṇāmityādi| vijñānaṃ viśiṣṭajñānam| sādhanaṃ cikitsitam| utpattiḥ prabhavaḥ| kāraṇaṃ hetuḥ||3||

skandagrahastu prathamaḥ skandāpasmāra eva ca |
śakunī revatī caiva pūtanā cāndhapūtanā ||4||
pūtanā śītanāmā ca tathaiva mukhamaṇḍikā |
navamo naigameṣaśca yaḥ pitṛgrahasañjñitaḥ ||5||

idānīṃ teṣāṃ navagrahāṇāṃ nāmānyāha- skandagraha ityādi| prathamaḥ pradhānamādirvā| navamo naigameṣa iti saṅkhyānirdeśādeva saṅkhyāyāṃ labdhāyāṃ bhūyo navama iti saṅkhyākaraṇaṃ kāśyapāditantrāntaroktādhikasaṅkhyānirākaraṇārtham| pitṛgrahasañjñita iti bālasyānyagrahebhyo rakṣakatvātpiteva grahaḥ saiva sañjñā sañjātā yasya sa tathā| (natu [1] bhūtavidyāvat pitṛlokābhihitagraha iti)||4-5||

dhātrīmātroḥ prākpradiṣṭāpacārācchaucabhraṣṭānmaṅgalācārahīnān |
trastān hṛṣṭāṃstarjitān tāḍitān vā pūjāhetorhiṃsyurete kumārān ||6||

kasmādgrahā bālān viśantītyāha- dhātrītyādi| dhātrī stanapāyinī poṣayitrītyarthaḥ, mātā prasavitrī| prākpradiṣṭāpacārāditi prāk śārīrasthāne pradiṣṭo'pacāraḥ akṛtyavidhiḥ māṃsasurādīnāṃ laulyādācaraṇaṃ vā| śaucaṃ mūtrapurīṣādiprakṣālanaṃ, maṅgalaṃ svastikasragdāmadūrvāvacāsarṣapādisparśanam, ācāraḥ pāpavirodhī, trastān udvignān, hṛṣṭān muditān, tarjitān anyena [2] kenacidvibhīṣitān| tāḍitān abhihatān| kathaṃ pūjā jāyate? kusumādinā bhojanādinā ca| ato hetoḥ dhātrīmātrorapacārāddūṣaṇādyanvitān bālān| pūjā cātropalakṣaṇaṃ; tena ratihetorapi hiṃsyuriti| nanu, dhātrīmātropacārādbālānāmanaparādhitvāt kathaṃ grahahiṃsanaṃ? atrocyate- bālānāṃ prāktanakarmayogata evāpacāraṃ kurutaḥ| kumārānityupalakṣaṇaṃ; tena kumārikā api hiṃsyuḥ| ete grahā ityarthaḥ||6||

aiśvaryasthāste na śakyā viśanto dehaṃ draṣṭuṃ mānuṣairviśvarūpāḥ |
āptaṃ vākyaṃ tatsamīkṣyābhidhāsye liṅgānyeṣāṃ yāni dehe bhavanti ||7||

kasmātte grahā dehaṃ viśanto nopalabhyante ityāha [3] - aiśvaryetyādi| eṣāṃ grahāṇāṃ yāni liṅgāni bhavanti tāni āptaṃ vākyaṃ samīkṣyābhidhāsye kathayiṣye iti piṇḍārthaḥ| āptaṃ vākyam āptairuktaṃ vākyam; āptāśca yathārthavādinaḥ [4] ||7||

śūnākṣaḥ [5] kṣatajasagandhikaḥ stanadviḍ vakrāsyo hatacalitaikapakṣmanetraḥ |
udvignaḥ sululitacakṣuralparodī skandārto bhavati ca gāḍhamuṣṭivarcāḥ ||8||

skandagrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- śūnākṣa ityādi| kṣatajasagandhikaḥ rudhirasamānagandhikaḥ, visragandha ityarthaḥ; anye tu kṣatajaṃ raktaṃ tatsagandhika īṣadrudhiragandhika ityarthaḥ| stanadviṭ stanadveṣī| vakrāsyaḥ vakramukhaḥ| hatacalitaikapakṣmanetra iti hataṃ calitaṃ caikapakṣma netre yasya sa tathā| udvignaḥ udvignacittaḥ| sululitacakṣuḥ kiñcinmilitalocanaḥ| skandārta iti skandavinodanārthaṃ rudreṇa sṛṣṭo grahaḥ tenārtaḥ, skando'tra na punarbhagavān kārtikeyaḥ| gāḍhe muṣṭivarcasī yasya sa tathā| gayadāsastu ‘stabdhāṅgaḥ’ kṣatajasagandhikaḥ stanadviṭ vakrāsyo hatacaraṇaikapakṣmanetraḥ’ iti paṭhitvā ‘stabdhamaṅgaṃ yasya sa tathā, hatāni ekapādaikapakṣmārdhanayanāni yasya sa tathā’ iti vyākhyānayati||8||

niḥsañjño bhavati punarbhavetsasañjñaḥ saṃrabdhaḥ karacaraṇaiśca nṛtyatīva |
viṇmūtre sṛjati vinadya jṛmbhamāṇaḥ phenaṃ ca prasṛjati tatsakhābhipannaḥ ||9||

skandāpasmārārtalakṣaṇamāha- niḥsañjña ityādi| saṃrabdhaḥ samyak kalitaḥ san, karacaraṇabhyāṃ hastapādābhyāṃ, nṛtyatīva nartanamiva karoti| vinadya śabdaṃ kṛtvā; kvacit ‘vinamya’ iti pāṭhaḥ, tatra gātravinamanaṃ kṛtvā; viṇmūtre muñcatīti vyākhyānam| tatsakhābhipannaḥ tacchabdena skandaḥ kathyate, tasya sakhā skandāpasmāraḥ, tenākrāntaḥ||9||

srastāṅgo bhayacakito vihaṅgagandhiḥ saṃsrāvivraṇaparipīḍitaḥ samantāt |
sphoṭaiśca pracitatanuḥ sadāhapākairvijñeyo bhavati śiśuḥ kṣataḥ śakunyā ||10||

śakunigrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- srastāṅga ityādi| srastāṅgaḥ śithilāṅgaḥ| bhayacakito bhītaḥ| vihaṅgā atra māṃsādā jalacarāśca| saṃsrāvivraṇaparipīḍitaḥ pūyāsrāvivraṇaparipīḍitaḥ| samantāt sarvataḥ| atroktavraṇasphoṭayorvidīrṇāvidīrṇatvena bhedaḥ| kṣataḥ abhibhūtaḥ||10||

raktāsyo haritamalo'tipāṇḍudehaḥ śyāvo vā jvaramukhapākavedanārtaḥ |
revatyā vyathitatanuśca karṇanāsaṃ mṛdnāti dhruvamabhipīḍitaḥ kumāraḥ ||11||

revatīgrahārtasya lakṣaṇamāha- raktāsya ityādi| haritamala iti malo'tra viṣṭhā mūtraṃ ca| jvaramukhapākavedanārtaḥ jvarārto mukhapākavedanārtaśca| vyathitatanuriti sarujaśarīraḥ| abhipīḍito revatyā iti sambandhaḥ||11||

srastāṅgaḥ svapiti sukhaṃ divā na rātrau viḍ bhinnaṃ sṛjati ca kākatulyagandhiḥ |
chardyā''rto hṛṣitatanūruhaḥ kumārastṛṣṇālurbhavati ca pūtanāgṛhītaḥ ||12||

pūtanāgrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- srastāṅga ityādi| na divā sukhaṃ svapiti na ca rātrau sukhaṃ svapiti| sṛjati muñcati| ‘srastāṅga’ ityatra kecit ‘svasthāṅga’ iti paṭhanti, svasthāṅgo'pi na divā na rātrau svapitīti vyākhyānayanti||12||

yo dveṣṭi stanamatisārakāsahikkācchardībhirjvarasahitābhirardyamānaḥ |
durvarṇaḥ satatamadhaḥśayo'mlagandhistaṃ brūyurbhiṣaja ihāndhapūtanārtam ||13||

andhapūtanārtalakṣaṇamāha- ya ityādi| adhaḥśayaḥ adhomukhaśāyī| amlagandhiḥ kāñjikādyamladravyagandhiḥ||13||

udvigno bhṛśamativepate prarudyāt saṃlīnaḥ svapiti ca yasya cāntrakūjaḥ |
visrāṅgo bhṛśamatisāryate ca yastaṃ jānīyādbhiṣagiha śītapūtanārtam ||14||

śītapūtanārtalakṣaṇamāha- udvigna ityādi| saṃlīnaḥ samyaglīnaḥ, śayyaikadeśe lagna ityarthaḥ| visrāṅgo visragandhāṅgaḥ| ‘udvigno bhṛśamati’ ityatra kecit ‘ākrandatyaticakitaḥ’ iti paṭhanti||14||

mlānāṅgaḥ surucirapāṇipādavaktro bahvāśī kaluṣasirāvṛtodaro yaḥ |
sodvego bhavati ca mūtratulyagandhiḥ sa jñeyaḥ śiśuriha vaktramaṇḍikārtaḥ ||15||

mukhamaṇḍikāgrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- mlānāṅga ityādi| mlānāṅgo durbalamadhyaśarīraḥ| surucirapāṇipādavaktra iti suṣṭhu ruciraṃ śobhanaṃ hastapādavaktraṃ yasya saḥ| vaktramaṇḍikarto mukhamaṇḍikārta ityarthaḥ||15||

yaḥ phenaṃ vamati vinamyate ca madhye sodvegam vilapati cordhvamīkṣamāṇaḥ |
jvaryeta pratatamatho vasāsagandhirniḥsañjño bhavati hi naigameṣajuṣṭaḥ ||16||

naigameṣagrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- ya ityādi| madhye madhyāṅge| sodvegaṃ satrāsaṃ, vilapati atiśayena śabdaṃ karoti, ūrdhvamīkṣamāṇaḥ ūrdhvaṃ paśyan| ‘vilapati’ ityatra kecit ‘vihasati’ iti paṭhanti| jvaryeta pratataṃ satatam| vasāsagandhiḥ vasayā tulyagandhiḥ| niḥsañjñaḥ nirgatasamyagjñānaḥ||16||

prastabdho yaḥ stanadveṣī muhyate cāviśanmuhuḥ |
taṃ bālamacirāddhanti grahaḥ sampūrṇalakṣaṇaḥ ||17||

sāmpratamasādhyagrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- prastabdha ityādi| prastabdho'tyarthaṃ stabdhāṅgaḥ| muhyate mohaṃ yāti| āviśan āveśaṃ kurvan| muhuḥ vāraṃvāram||17||

viparītamataḥ sādhyaṃ cikitsedacirārditam |18|

sādhyagrahārtalakṣaṇamāha- viparītamityādi| viparītaṃ prastabdha stanadveṣādilakṣaṇairanupahatam| acirārditam alpakālapīḍitam|18|-

gṛhe purāṇahaviṣā'bhyajya bālaṃ śucau śuciḥ ||18||
sarṣapān prakiretteṣāṃ tailairdīpaṃ [6] ca kārayet |
sadā sannihitaṃ cāpi juhuyāddhavyavāhanam ||19||
sarvagandhauṣadhībījairgandhamālyairalaṅkratam |
agnaye kṛttikābhyaśca svāhā svāheti santatam ||20||

sādhyasya cikitsāmāha- gṛhe ityādi| purāṇahaviṣā jīrṇaghṛtena, abhyajya bālaṃ cupāṭitvā| śucau pavitradeśe, śuciḥ ‘bhiṣag’ iti śeṣaḥ| teṣāṃ tailaiḥ sarṣapāṇāṃ tailaiḥ, dīpaṃ kārayet dīpaṃ prajvālayedityarthaḥ| sadetyādi| juhuyāt homaṃ kuryādityarthaḥ| havyavāhanam agnim| kairjuhuyāt? sarvagandhauṣadhībījairiti; sarvagandhā elādiparipaṭhitāḥ, oṣadhībījani yavadhānyatilādīni, tairjuhuyāt| gandhamālyairalaṅkṛtamagniṃ bālaṃ vā, satataṃ vāraṃvāraṃ na sakṛt| ayaṃ vidhiḥ sarvagrahāṇāṃ sāmānyaḥ| agnaya ityādi| agniṃ hutvā tato madhyāhne baliṃ dadyāt||18-20||

namaḥ skandāya devāya grahādhipataye namaḥ |
śirasā tvā'bhivande'haṃ pratigṛhṇīṣva me balim |
nīrujo nirvikāraśca śiśurme jāyatāṃ drutam ||21||

tatra yaṃ mantramuccarettamāha- nama ityādi||21||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kumāratantre navagrahākṛtivijñānīyo nāma (prathamo'dhyāyaḥ, āditaḥ) saptaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||27||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāmuttaratantrāntargate kaumārabhṛtyatantre navagrahākṛtivijānīyo nāma saptaviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ||27||

1. ayaṃ pāṭho hastalikhitapustake nopalabhyate|
2. ‘yena kenacit’ iti pā.|
3. ‘nanu pratyakṣāviṣayatvāt kṛtāveśānāṃ grahāṇāṃ kena jñānaṃ bhaviṣyatītyāha-’ iti pā.|
4. ‘tattvārthavedinaḥ’ iti pā.|
5. ‘stabdhākṣaḥ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘taile dīpaṃ’ iti pā.|
