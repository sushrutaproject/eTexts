saṃsvedī dvijagurudevadoṣavaktā jihmākṣo vigatabhayo vimārgadṛṣṭiḥ | santuṣṭo bhavati na cānnapānajātairduṣṭātmā bhavati ca devaśatrujuṣṭaḥ ||9||

bhūmau yaḥ prasarati sarpavat kadācit sṛkkiṇyau vilikhati 5 jihvayā tathaiva | nidrālurgaḍamadhudugdhapāyasepsurvijñeyo bhavati bhujaṅgamena juṣṭaḥ ||13||
