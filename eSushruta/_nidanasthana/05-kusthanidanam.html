athātaḥ kuṣṭhanidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
athetyādi||1-2|| atha bhagandarānantaramūrdhvādhodhamanīgatarogābhidhāne tiryaggatadhamanīrogapāriśeṣyād viruddhāśanasantarpaṇaikahetujanmatvāt saṃśodhanopakramasādhyatvānmahāvyādhisāmānyācca kuṣṭhanidānārambhaḥ||1-2||
mithyāhārācārasya 1 viśeṣādguruviruddhāsātmyājīrṇāhitāśinaḥ snehapītasya vāntasya vā vyāyāmagrāmyadharmasevino grāmyānūpaudakamāṃsāni vā payasā'bhīkṣṇamaśnato yo vā majjatyapsūṣmābhitaptaḥ sahasā chardiṃ vā pratihanti, tasya pittaśleṣmāṇau prakupitau parigṛhyānilaḥ pravṛddhastiryaggāḥ sirāḥ samprapadya samuddhūya bāhyaṃ mārgaṃ prati samantādvikṣipati, yatra yatra ca doṣo vikṣipto niścarati tatra tatra maṇḍalāni prādurbhavanti, evaṃ samutpannastvaci doṣastatra tatra ca parivṛddhiṃ prāpyāpratikriyamāṇo'bhyantaraṃ pratipadyate dhātūnabhidūṣayan ||3||
idānīṃ hetumāha- mithyetyādi| mithyāhārācārasya śāstroktavidhibhraṣṭāhārācārasyetyarthaḥ| samprāptimāha- tasyetyādi| tasya mithyāhārācārasyetyādikasya| samudbhūya ākṣipya, samantāt sarvatra; etena sarvadehagatatvaṃ rogasya sūcayati||3|| tasya prāk sthānasaṃśrayasvabhāvān hetvādipurassaraṃ darśayannāha- mithyāhārācārasyetyādi| mithyāśabda āhārācārābhyāṃ saha pratyekamabhisambadhyate| tatra dvādaśāśanapravibhāgoktavidhiviparītavidhirmithyāhāraḥ| ācāraḥ kāya vāṅmanobhedena trividho vihāraḥ, tasya svasthavṛttānāgatābādhavidhānādanyathākaraṇaṃ mithyācāraḥ| te ca prakopahetavaḥ sarveṣāmevātra gṛhyante mithyāhārācārabhedena dvividhyāt| pittaśleṣmāṇau prakupitau prakupitaḥ parigṛhya anila iti tridoṣajatvaṃ samprāptau sūcitaṃ; karmajatvaṃ punarbahmastrīsajjanavadhādibhiḥ parasvaharaṇāntaiḥ sūcayiṣyati| ata eva kuṣṭhānāṃ dvividhaṃ cikitsitaṃ yuktivyapāśritaṃ daivavyapāśritaṃ ca, doṣakarmasambhavatvāt kuṣṭhānām| kecidatra vyādhihetūnadhīyate- viśeṣādguruviruddhāsātmyājīrṇāhitāśina ityādi| yuktaṃ caitat, doṣarogobhayabhedena hetūnāṃ trividhatvāt, bhojādiṣu pāṭhācca| tathāhi bhojaḥ- “sevate yo viruddhānnamajīrṇe karṣatastathā” ityādi| tiryaggāḥ sirā iti nābhiprabhavāstrividhā hi sirāḥ sambhavanti ūrdhvagā adhogāstiryagāśceti| anye tu sirāśabdena dhamanīrapi sādṛśyādāhuḥ| uktaṃ ca “tiryaggāṇāṃ catasṝṇāmekaikā” (śā. 9) ityādi| samuddhūya ākṣipya| bāhyaṃ mārgaṃ pratīti lakṣaṇe dvitīyā| samantāditi sarvadeharogatvamasya sūcayati| ata eva yatra tatra grahaṇamaniyatadehasūcanaṃ; taduktaṃ carake- “ataḥ prabhavāṇyabhinivartamāni kevalaṃ śarīramupatapanti” (ca. ni. 5) iti; kevalamiti sakalaṃ doṣadūṣyagaṇam| tena raso raktaṃ māṃsamambu ca duṣṭamanugṛhyate| taduktaṃ carake- “vātādayastrayo duṣṭāstvagraktaṃ māṃsamambu ca| dūṣayanti sa kuṣṭhānāṃ saptako dravyasaṅgrahaḥ||”; tathā “raktaṃ lasīkā tvaṅmāṃsaṃ dūṣyaṃ doṣāstrayo malāḥ| visarpāṇāṃ samutpattau vijñeyāḥ sapta dhātavaḥ” (ca. ci. 7) iti| tatra ambu lasīkā udakameva| yadāha- “yattu tvagantare vraṇagatamudakaṃ tattu lasīkāśabdaṃ labhate” (ca. śā. 7) iti| tatra visarpasya pittaprabalaṃ saptadravyaṃ, kuṣṭhasya punarmandapittamiti| tathaiva dravyaṃ gurudravyādi, brahmastrīsajjanavadhādikarmaviśeṣo'pi viśeṣa iti| maṇḍalānyupajanayantīti sāmānyaṃ sarvakuṣṭhānāṃ lakṣaṇam| tathā carake'pi- “viruddhānyannapānāni dravasnigdhagurūṇi ca” (ca. ci. 7) ityādi| kuṣṭhasamprāptau bhojaḥ- “praduṣṭopacitā doṣā rasāsṛṅmāṃsasaṃsṛtāḥ| kuṣṭhāni janayantyāśu śarīreṣu śarīriṇām” iti||3||
tasya pūrvarūpāṇi- tvakpāruṣyamakasmādromaharṣaḥ kaṇḍūḥ svedabāhulyamasvedanaṃ vā'ṅgapradeśānāṃ 2 svāpaḥ kṣatavisarpaṇamasṛjaḥ kṛṣṇatā ceti ||4||
pūrvarūpamāha- tasyetyādi| akasmāt kāraṇaṃ vinā| svāpaḥ sparśājñānam||4|| sthānasaṃśrayalakṣaṇasamprāptau pūrvarūpaṃ- tvakpāruṣyamityādi| lomaharṣātisvedāsvedau ca kuṣṭhajanakadoṣaistiryaksvedavāhiṣu duṣṭeṣu| yaduktaṃ tantrāntare- “pārūṣyaṃ lomaharṣaṇam| atisvedanamasvedaḥ paridāhaśca jāyate||” iti| asṛkkṛṣṇatā doṣairasṛjo duṣṭatvāt| atraiva bhojaḥ- “atisvedo na vā svedaḥ kaṇḍūnistodasuptatāḥ| tvacaḥ pāruṣyamatyarthaṃ romaharṣo vivarṇatā|| śuklatā śoṇitaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vraṇānāmaprarohaṇam| dāhapākau ca samprāpte tvaci secanayo hitam(?)|| tasya kuṣṭhāni spaṣṭāni teṣāṃ nāmāni me śṛṇu||” iti||4||
tatra sapta mahākuṣṭhāni, ekādaśa kṣudrakuṣṭhāni, evamaṣṭādaśa kuṣṭhāni bhavanti | tatra mahākuṣṭhānyaruṇodumbararṣya(rkṣa)jihvakapālakākaṇakapuṇḍarīkadadrukuṣṭhānīti | kṣudrakuṣṭhānyapi sthūlāruṣkaṃ mahākuṣṭhamekakuṣṭhaṃ carmadalaṃ visarpaḥ parisarpaḥ sidhmaṃ vicarcikā kiṭibhaṃ (maṃ) pāmā rakasā ceti ||5||
idānīṃ saṅkhyāmāha- tatretyādi| mahattvamuttarottaradhātvanupraveśāt| ekādaśakuṣṭhānāṃ kṣudratvamuttarottaradhātvananupraveśāt| aṣṭādaśa kuṣṭhāni bhavantīti diṅmātramidaṃ darśitaṃ, kuṣṭhānāmaniyatasaṅkhyatvāt; tathā ca carakaḥ- “saptavidho'ṣṭādaśavidho'saṅkhyeyavidho vā” (ca. ni. 5) iti| mahākuṣṭhāni nāmataḥ 3 prāha- tatra mahākuṣṭhānītyādi| kṣudrakuṣṭhāni nāmataḥ 4 prāha- kṣudrakuṣṭhānyapītyādi||5|| teṣāṃ kuṣṭhānāṃ hetuliṅgacikitsopayoginīṃ kuṣṭhaviśeṣāśrayāṃ saṅkhyāṃ nirdiśannāha- tatra sapta mahākuṣṭhānītyādi| mahattvameṣāmuttarottaradhātvanupraveśāt| dhātvantarapraveśo'pyeṣāṃ bahubahuladoṣārambhāt| ata eva kriyā mahatī mahākuṣṭhe, kṣudre punaralpā| yadāha carakaḥ- “pracchannamalpe kuṣṭhe, mahati ca śastaṃ sirāvyadhanam” (ca. ci. 7) iti| doṣāṇāṃ prābalyaṃ doṣasvabhāvakāryaliṅgaiḥ kuṣṭheṣu jñeyam| atraivārthe kuṣṭhamadhikṛtya rogadoṣayoḥ kāryakāraṇabhūtayostathaiva doṣakāraṇayoranyonyajñāpakabhāve carakaḥ- “kuṣṭhaviśeṣairdoṣā doṣaviśeṣaḥ punaśca kuṣṭhāni| jñāyante te hetuṃ hetuśca tān prakāśayati” (ca. ci. 7) iti| tadyathā- aruṇakuṣṭhaṃ vāyoḥ svabhāvabhūtena raukṣyeṇa todasvāpādinā ca kāryeṇa vātaṃ kāraṇabhūtaṃ jñāpayati| hetuśceti cakāro'pyarthaḥ| tena heturapi tān doṣān prakāśayatīti| hetoḥ kāryasya prakāśena pratibandhaniyamaṃ sūcayati, kāryasya punaḥ prakāśane niyataḥ sambandha iti||5||
sarvāṇi kuṣṭhāni savātāni sapittāni saśleṣmāṇi sakrimīṇi ca bhavanti, utsannatastu doṣagrahaṇamabhibhavāt ||6||
sarveṣveva kuṣṭheṣu sarveṣāmeva doṣāṇāṃ krimīṇāṃ ca sambhavaṃ darśayannāha- sarvāṇītyādi| sarvāṇi kṣudrāṇi mahānti ca| kathaṃ tarhi vātenāruṇamityādītyāśaṅkyāha- utsannatastu doṣagrahaṇamabhibhavāditi; utsannata autkaṭyāt doṣagrahaṇam, abhibhavāt ‘itaradoṣayoḥ’ iti śeṣaḥ||6|| tatra sarveṣveva kuṣṭheṣu sarveṣāmeva doṣāṇāṃ krimīṇāṃ ca sambhavaṃ darśayannāha- sarvāṇītyādi| sarvaṇīti kṣudrāṇi mahānti ca| kathaṃ tarhi vātenāruṇāmityādi? utsannatastu doṣagrahaṇamiti| utsannamiti napuṃsake bhāve ktaḥ; tenotsannamutkarṣaḥ| utkarṣadoṣeṇa grahaṇaṃ vātenāruṇamityādi| abhibhavāditi lyablope pañcamī; tena abhibhavamutkarṣaṃ vīkṣya tena doṣeṇa grahaṇam| nanu cotkarṣo hi abhibhavamevāpekṣya bhavatīti abhibhavagrahaṇamantareṇa tadartho labhyata eveti abhibhavagrahaṇaṃ saṃsargajadoṣābhyāṃ grahaṇamityabhilambhayati||6||
tatra vātenāruṇaṃ, pittenodumbararṣya(rkṣa)jihvakapālakākaṇakāni, śleṣmaṇā puṇḍarīkaṃ dadrukuṣṭhaṃ ceti | teṣāṃ mahattvaṃ kriyāgurutvamuttarottaraṃ dhātvanupraveśādasādhyatvaṃ ceti ||7||
yasya doṣasya yasmin kuṣṭhe autkaṭyaṃ tena taddarśayannāha- tatretyādi| tatrāruṇādīnāṃ saptānāṃ mahattvādiṣu hetuṃ darśayannāha- teṣāmityādi| anuśabdena paścādarthavācinā mahatve'sādhyatve'pi kākaṇakavarjaṃ kālāntaraṃ ca sūcyate; kākaṇakaṃ punarutpattāvevāsādhyaṃ, vyādhisvabhāvāt| asādhyatvaṃ ceti cakāro dhātvanupraveśāccetyanantaraṃ draṣṭavyaḥ; tenānuktamārambhakadoṣamahattvaṃ samuccinoti||7|| tatrāruṇādīnāṃ saptānāṃ mahatve hetuṃ darśayannāha- teṣāṃ tu mahatvamityādi| asādhyatve'pi cāyameva hetuḥ| anuśabdena paścādarthavācinā asādhyatve'pi kāraṇavarjaṃ kālāntaraṃ ca sūcyate| yadāha- “sādhyā yāpyatvamāyānti yāpyāścāsādhyatāṃ tathā| ghnanti prāṇānasādhyāstu narāṇāmakriyāvatām” (sū. 26) ityādi| aruṇaṃ punarutpattāvevāsādhyaṃ, vyādhisvabhāvāt| asādhyatvaṃ ceti cakāro bhinnakrame dhātvanupraveśādityanantaraṃ draṣṭavyaḥ| tenānukramārambhakadoṣatve mahatvaṃ samuccinoti| bhedo hi bhedavatāṃ kāraṇabhedād viruddhadharmādhyāsādbhavatīti||7||
tatra, vātenāruṇābhāni tanūni visarpīṇi todabhedasvāpayuktānyaruṇāni | pittena pakvodumbaraphalākṛtivarṇānyaudumbarāṇi 5, ṛṣya(kṣa)jihvāprakāśāni 6 kharāṇi ṛṣya(kṣa)jihvāni, kṛṣṇakapālikāprakāśāni kapālakuṣṭhāni, kākaṇantikāphalasadṛśānyatīva raktakṛṣṇāni kākaṇakāni 7; teṣāṃ caturṇāmapyoṣacoṣaparidāhadhūmāyanāni kṣiprotthānaprapākabheditvāni krimijanma ca sāmānyāni liṅgāni | śleṣmaṇā puṇḍarīkapatraprakāśāni pauṇḍarīkāṇi, atasīpuṣpavarṇāni tāmrāṇi vā visarpīṇi piḍakāvanti ca dadrukuṣṭhāni; tayordvayorapyutsannatā parimaṇḍalatā kaṇḍūścirotthānatvaṃ ceti sāmānyāni rūpāṇi ||8||
tatra mahākuṣṭhānāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ darśayannāha- tatra vātenetyādi| ṛṣyajihvāprakāśānīti ṛṣyajihvāyā iva prakāśaḥ sādṛśyaṃ yeṣāṃ tāni kharatvayuktāni, ṛṣyo nīlāṇḍaḥ ‘roru 8 ’ iti prasiddhaḥ; anye ‘ṛkṣa’ iti paṭhanti, ṛkṣo ‘rīcha’ iti loke| kṛṣṇakapālikā kṛṣṇakarkarā| kākaṇantikā guñjā, ‘cirahaṇṭikā’ iti prasiddhā, tatphalavadraktāni madhye kṛṣṇāni| udumbarādīnāṃ pittakṛtatvaṃ darśayannāha- teṣāmityādi| uṣo'nalasamīpa ivopatāpaḥ, pluṣṭasyeva vedanāviśeṣa ityanye; coṣa ācūṣyata iva vedanāviśeṣaḥ, pipāsetyanye; paridāhaḥ sarvato dāhaḥ; dhūmāyanaṃ dhūmopahatasyeva, ‘dhūmodvamanamivāṅgānām’ ityeke| puṇḍarīkadadrukuṣṭhayorlakṣaṇaṃ darśayannāha- śleṣmaṇetyādi| puṇḍarīkaṃ śvetapadmam| dadrukuṣṭhaṃ dvividhaṃ- sitamasitaṃ ca; pṛṣodarāditvādākāranivṛttiḥ| tena vātena kharāṇi śyāvāṇyapi| lohitatvaṃ pittajatvādasya| atra carakaḥ- “karkaśaṃ raktaparyantamantaḥśyāvaṃ savedanam| yadṛkṣajihvāsaṃsthānamṛkṣajihvaṃ taducyate”- (ca. ci. 7) iti| atraiva doṣānubandhamāha carakaḥ- “vātapitte śleṣmapitte vātaśleṣmaṇi vā'dhike| ṛkṣa(ṣya)jihvaṃ puṇḍarīkaṃ sidhmakuṣṭhaṃ ca jāyate” (ca. ci. 7) iti kṛṣṇakapālikā kṛṣṇaśarkarā| atra vātānubandho'pyasti| taduktaṃ carake- “vāte'dhikatare kāpālaṃ, pitte tvaudumbaraṃ, kākaṇakaṃ tu tridoṣajam” (ca. ni. 5) iti| udumbarādīnāṃ pittakṛtatvaṃ darśayannāha- teṣāmityādi| sannipātajamapi kāraṇaṃ pittaje paṭhanti, pittotkaṭasannipātajatvāt| ūṣā analasamīpa ivopatāpaḥ| coṣaḥ cūṣaṇamiva, pipāsetyanye| paridāhaḥ sarvato dāhaḥ| dhūmāyanaṃ dhūmodvamanamivāṅgānāṃ, pittopatāpena| pittajeṣu ‘kṛmijanma ca’ iti kecit paṭhanti| puṇḍarīkāṇi sitāni kaphena, pittānubandhena raktāni ca, sitāruṇaṃ hi puṇḍarīkaṃ;
