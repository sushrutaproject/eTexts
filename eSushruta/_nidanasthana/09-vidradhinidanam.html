athāto vidradhīnāṃ nidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
athāta ityādi||1-2|| athāto vidradhernidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ| mūḍhagarbhānantaraṃ śastrakarmasādhyatvasādharmyāt, antarvidradhāvapyudarotsedhasya sāmānyād vidradherārambhaḥ| nirupādhirvidradhisañjñeti svamataṃ, “śīghravidāhādvidradhiḥ” ityanye; taduktaṃ- “sa vai śīghravidāhitvādvidradhīti nirucyate”- (ca. sū. 17) iti||1-2||
sarvāmaraguruḥ śrīmānnimittāntarabhūmipaḥ | śiṣyāyovāca nikhilamidaṃ vidradhilakṣaṇam ||3||
sarvāmaragururidaṃ nikhilaṃ vidradhīlakṣaṇaṃ śiṣyārthamuvāceti sambandhaḥ| amṛtadānena sarve devā amarāḥ kṛtāḥ, tenāsau dhanvantariḥ sarveṣāṃ devānāṃ gururityarthaḥ| śrīmān jñānātiśayenaiśvaryeṇa ca yukta ityarthaḥ| nimittāntarabhūmipa iti nimitāntareṇa kāraṇāntareṇa nareṣvapi bhaiṣajyāmṛtapradānalakṣaṇena bhūpālatāṃ gata ityarthaḥ| anye tu ‘aputrasya kāśirājasya paramārādhanayā nirvāṇe cittamadhiṣṭhāya 1 putratvamāpanna’ iti kāraṇāntaraṃ vyākhyānayanti| śiṣyāyovāca śiṣyārthaṃ kathitavānityarthaḥ| nikhilaṃ samastam| idaṃ vakṣyamāṇam| anena dhanvantarisvarūpopavarṇanena vidradhergarīyastvaṃ khyāpitaṃ bhavati||3||
tvagraktamāṃsamedāṃsi pradūṣyāsthisamāśritāḥ | doṣāḥ śophaṃ śanairghoraṃ janayantyucchritā bhṛśam ||4|| mahāmūlaṃ rujāvantaṃ vṛttaṃ cā(vā')pyathavā''yatam | tamāhurvidradhiṃ dhīrā, vijñeyaḥ sa ca ṣaḍvidhaḥ ||5|| pṛthagdoṣaiḥ samastaiśca kṣatenāpyasṛjā tathā | ṣaṇṇāmapi hi teṣāṃ tu lakṣaṇaṃ sampravakṣyate ||6||
samprāptiṃ sāmānyalakṣaṇaṃ ca darśayannāha- tvagraktetyādi| bhṛśamityatiśayenocchritā iti sambandhaḥ| dhīrā dhīmantaḥ| etena samprāptiruktā| mahāmūlamityādi sāmānyalakṣaṇamuktaṃ, viśiṣṭaṃ kṛṣṇo'ruṇa ityādi, kāraṇaṃ tu sāmānyaṃ śophakāraṇābhidhānenaivoktam| saṅkhyāmāha- vijñeyaḥ sa ca ṣaḍvidha iti| sa vidradhiḥ| tadeva ṣaḍvidhatvamāha- pṛthagityādi| samastaiścetyekaḥ, kṣatenāpyekaḥ, asṛjā'pyeka iti ṣaṭ||4-6|| sarvāmaraguruḥ śrīmāniti amarāṇāṃ gururiti amṛtoddhāraṃ smārayati; surā hyamṛtena dhanvantariṇoddhṛtena nirjarāmaraṇādamarīkṛtāḥ; ata eva sarvāmaraguruḥ| nimittāntarabhūmipa iti nareṣvapi svabhaiṣajyāmṛtapradānāya bhūpālatāṃ gata iti gamayati| yaduktaṃ prāk- “śalyaṃ mahacchāstravaraṃ gṛhītvā” (sū. 1) ityādi| etasyārthasya anya evādhyāye'pi pratipāditasya punaḥ pratipādanaṃ vidradhergarīyastvabodhanārtham; ata eva mahāvyādhīnāmanantarameva vidradherabhidhānam| vidradhilakṣaṇamiti lakṣyate aneneti lakṣaṇaṃ jñāpako heturiti| tacca lakṣaṇaṃ dvividhaṃ- sāmānyaṃ, viśiṣṭaṃ ca| tatra sāmānyaṃ ‘śothaṃ ghoraṃ mahāmūlam’ iti; viśiṣṭaṃ tu ‘kṛṣṇo'ruṇe vā’ ityādi| kārakahetavastu sāmānyaśothahetvabhidhānenoktāḥ| anye punarāhuḥ- vidradhiṣu paruṣādīni vātādīnāṃ svābhāvikaliṅgāni, kṛṣṇādīni kāryabhūtāni| sa vidradhiriti khyāta iti itiśabdena nirupādhireva vidradhisañjñā saṅketamātrākṛteti||3-6||
kṛṣṇo'ruṇo vā paruṣo bhṛśamatyarthavedanaḥ | citrotthānaprapākaśca vidradhirvātasambhavaḥ ||7||

āgantoḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- taistairityādi| taistairiti aśvapatanagū(mū)ḍhaprahārādibhirityarthaḥ; tenāsrutaśoṇitapiccitādīnāṃ 4 grahaṇam| kṣate vetyatra taistairbhāvairiti tīkṣṇaprahārādibhirityarthaḥ; anena ca srutaśoṇitānāṃ chinnādīnāṃ grahaṇam| kṣatoṣmā vāyuvisṛto vātena vistāritaḥ| pittavidradhilakṣaṇo ‘bhavati’ ityadhyāhāraḥ||11-12||- abhighātajasyāgantoḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- taistairbhāvairityādi| prapatanaprahārādibhirhate'tyantaduṣṭaśoṇitānām| hate iti mathitādīnāmapyupalakṣaṇam| kṣate iti taistairbhāvairityādibhiḥ| kṣata iti srutaśoṇitānāṃ chinnādīnāmupalakṣaṇam| tatraikatra mathitapiccitayoḥ sirāmārgāvaraṇena vātakopaḥ, anyatra chinnādau dhātukṣayeṇātiraktāsrāvanimittena| tatrābhighātajasyopacārārthaṃ doṣaliṅgaṃ darśayannāha- pittavidradhilakṣaṇa iti| raktaṃ ca raktaje eva sravatīti pittavidradhiliṅgātideśaḥ kṛtaḥ; pītatvasyāvayavavikṛtatvāt||11-12||-
kṛṣṇasphoṭāvṛtaḥ śyāvastīvradāharujājvaraḥ 5||13|| pittavidradhiliṅgastu raktavidradhirucyate |14|
raktavidradhilakṣaṇamāha- kṛṣṇasphoṭāvṛta ityādi||13||- raktaje dāhapākādīnāṃ tīvratvamadhikam| raktasya dūṣyatvāddoṣaliṅgamatideśena nirdiśannāha- pittasya vidradhairliṅgairityādi| bhojaprabhṛtayastu raktavidradhiṃ nādhīyate| anye tviha raktaje'dhikavidhānārthaṃ raktajaṃ paṭhantyeva||13||-

ābhyantarānatastūrdhvaṃ vidradhīn paricakṣate | gurvasātmyaviruddhānnaśuṣkasaṃsṛṣṭabhojanāt 6||15|| ativyavāyavyāyāmavegāghātavidāhibhiḥ | pṛthak sambhūya vā doṣāḥ kupitā gulmarūpiṇam ||16|| valmīkavatsamunnaddhamantaḥ kurvanti vidradhim | gude bastimukhe nābhyāṃ kukṣau vaṅkṣaṇayostathā ||17|| vṛkkayoryakṛti plīhni hṛdaye klomni vā tathā | teṣāṃ liṅgāni jānīyādbāhyavidradhilakṣaṇaiḥ ||18|| āmapakvaiṣaṇīyācca 7 pakvāpakvaṃ vinirdiśet |19|
