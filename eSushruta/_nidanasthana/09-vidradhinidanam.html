--- title: "9. vidradhinidānam" adhyaya: 9 order: 9 ---

athāto vidradhīnāṃ nidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi||1-2||

athāto vidradhernidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ| mūḍhagarbhānantaraṃ śastrakarmasādhyatvasādharmyāt, antarvidradhāvapyudarotsedhasya sāmānyād vidradherārambhaḥ| nirupādhirvidradhisañjñeti svamataṃ, “śīghravidāhādvidradhiḥ” ityanye; taduktaṃ- “sa vai śīghravidāhitvādvidradhīti nirucyate”- (ca. sū. 17) iti||1-2||

sarvāmaraguruḥ śrīmānnimittāntarabhūmipaḥ |
śiṣyāyovāca nikhilamidaṃ vidradhilakṣaṇam ||3||

sarvāmaragururidaṃ nikhilaṃ vidradhīlakṣaṇaṃ śiṣyārthamuvāceti sambandhaḥ| amṛtadānena sarve devā amarāḥ kṛtāḥ, tenāsau dhanvantariḥ sarveṣāṃ devānāṃ gururityarthaḥ| śrīmān jñānātiśayenaiśvaryeṇa ca yukta ityarthaḥ| nimittāntarabhūmipa iti nimitāntareṇa kāraṇāntareṇa nareṣvapi bhaiṣajyāmṛtapradānalakṣaṇena bhūpālatāṃ gata ityarthaḥ| anye tu ‘aputrasya kāśirājasya paramārādhanayā nirvāṇe cittamadhiṣṭhāya [1] putratvamāpanna’ iti kāraṇāntaraṃ vyākhyānayanti| śiṣyāyovāca śiṣyārthaṃ kathitavānityarthaḥ| nikhilaṃ samastam| idaṃ vakṣyamāṇam| anena dhanvantarisvarūpopavarṇanena vidradhergarīyastvaṃ khyāpitaṃ bhavati||3||

tvagraktamāṃsamedāṃsi pradūṣyāsthisamāśritāḥ |
doṣāḥ śophaṃ śanairghoraṃ janayantyucchritā bhṛśam ||4||
mahāmūlaṃ rujāvantaṃ vṛttaṃ cā(vā')pyathavā''yatam |
tamāhurvidradhiṃ dhīrā, vijñeyaḥ sa ca ṣaḍvidhaḥ ||5||
pṛthagdoṣaiḥ samastaiśca kṣatenāpyasṛjā tathā |
ṣaṇṇāmapi hi teṣāṃ tu lakṣaṇaṃ sampravakṣyate ||6||

samprāptiṃ sāmānyalakṣaṇaṃ ca darśayannāha- tvagraktetyādi| bhṛśamityatiśayenocchritā iti sambandhaḥ| dhīrā dhīmantaḥ| etena samprāptiruktā| mahāmūlamityādi sāmānyalakṣaṇamuktaṃ, viśiṣṭaṃ kṛṣṇo'ruṇa ityādi, kāraṇaṃ tu sāmānyaṃ śophakāraṇābhidhānenaivoktam| saṅkhyāmāha- vijñeyaḥ sa ca ṣaḍvidha iti| sa vidradhiḥ| tadeva ṣaḍvidhatvamāha- pṛthagityādi| samastaiścetyekaḥ, kṣatenāpyekaḥ, asṛjā'pyeka iti ṣaṭ||4-6||

sarvāmaraguruḥ śrīmāniti amarāṇāṃ gururiti amṛtoddhāraṃ smārayati; surā hyamṛtena dhanvantariṇoddhṛtena nirjarāmaraṇādamarīkṛtāḥ; ata eva sarvāmaraguruḥ| nimittāntarabhūmipa iti nareṣvapi svabhaiṣajyāmṛtapradānāya bhūpālatāṃ gata iti gamayati| yaduktaṃ prāk- “śalyaṃ mahacchāstravaraṃ gṛhītvā” (sū. 1) ityādi| etasyārthasya anya evādhyāye'pi pratipāditasya punaḥ pratipādanaṃ vidradhergarīyastvabodhanārtham; ata eva mahāvyādhīnāmanantarameva vidradherabhidhānam| vidradhilakṣaṇamiti lakṣyate aneneti lakṣaṇaṃ jñāpako heturiti| tacca lakṣaṇaṃ dvividhaṃ- sāmānyaṃ, viśiṣṭaṃ ca| tatra sāmānyaṃ ‘śothaṃ ghoraṃ mahāmūlam’ iti; viśiṣṭaṃ tu ‘kṛṣṇo'ruṇe vā’ ityādi| kārakahetavastu sāmānyaśothahetvabhidhānenoktāḥ| anye punarāhuḥ- vidradhiṣu paruṣādīni vātādīnāṃ svābhāvikaliṅgāni, kṛṣṇādīni kāryabhūtāni| sa vidradhiriti khyāta iti itiśabdena nirupādhireva vidradhisañjñā saṅketamātrākṛteti||3-6||

kṛṣṇo'ruṇo vā paruṣo bhṛśamatyarthavedanaḥ |
citrotthānaprapākaśca vidradhirvātasambhavaḥ ||7||

vātavidradhilakṣaṇamāha- kṛṣṇa ityādi| citrotthānaprapāka iti nānāprakārotthāno [2] nānāprakārapākaścetyarthaḥ||7||

citrotthānaprapāka iti citrau utthānaprapākau yasya, vāyorviṣamakriyatvāt||7||

pakvodumbarasaṅkāśaḥ śyāvo vā jvaradāhavān |
kṣiprotthānaprapākaśca vidradhiḥ pittasambhavaḥ ||8||

pittavidradhimāha- pakvetyādi||8||

paittike jvaradāhau sambhavamātrameva, pacyamāne punaratyantau bhavataḥ||8||

śarāvasadṛśaḥ pāṇḍuḥ śītaḥ stabdho'lpavedanaḥ |
cirotthānaprapākaśca sakaṇḍuśca kaphotthitaḥ ||9||

kaphajamāha- śarāvasadṛśa ityādi| śarāvasādṛśyena mahattvamuktam||9||

kaphaje sarāvasadṛśatvena mahattvasūcanam||9||

tanupītasitāścaiṣāmāsrāvāḥ kramaśaḥ smṛtāḥ |10|

teṣāṃ vidradhīnāṃ yathākramamāsrāvānāha- tanvityādi| eṣāṃ vātāpittakaphotthānāṃ vidradhīnām|10|-

pākānantarasambhavaṃ srāvaṃ darśayannāha- tanupītasitāścaiṣāmityādi| ete trayāṇāṃ vātapittakaphānāṃ yathāsaṅkhyena|10|-

nānāvarṇarujāsrāvo ghāṭālo viṣamo mahān ||10||
viṣamaṃ pacyate cāpi vidradhiḥ sānnipātikaḥ |11|

sānnipātikamāha- nānetyādi| nānāvarṇāḥ kṛṣṇapītaśuklāḥ, nānārujāstodadāhakaṇḍvādyāḥ, nānāsrāvāstanupītaśuklāḥ, te yasya santi sa yathoktaḥ| ghāṭāla iti ghāṭā ūrdhvabhāgo mahān yasyāstīti sa ghāṭālaḥ| viṣamo'sādhyatvāt| viṣamaṃ pacyate cāpīti cirāciragambhīrottānānunnatonnatabhedena [3] ||10||-

sānnipātikaṃ darśayannāha- nānetyādi| nānāvidhā varṇavedanāsrāvā yasya sa tathā| tatra nānāvarṇāḥ kṛṣṇapītaśuklāḥ; vedanāstodadāhakaṇḍvādikāḥ, nānāsrāvāḥ kṛṣṇapītasitāḥ| viṣamaṃ pacyate cirāciragambhīrottānakṛtena bhedenāsamatvāt| vātaje'pi pākasrāvavaiṣamyamasti, natu gāmbhīryādīnāmapi varṇarujāsrāvāṇām, ata eva vātajasya sādhyatvamuktam| ghāṭāla iti “prāṇisthādāto lajanyatarasyām” (pā. a. 5/2/96) iti lac| atyutthānādgurutvena ghāṭā iva| tathā ca bhojaḥ- “bahudoṣo rasān sarvān rasate yo yadā naraḥ| tasya dehe tvagādīni doṣāḥ sandūṣya dehinaḥ|| kurvanti sarvarūpotthaṃ śothaṃ sarvarujānvitam| viṣamaṃ pāki (?) viṣamaṃ ghāṭālaṃ cirapākinam|| vyāmiśravarṇasrutikaṃ bhiṣaktaṃ parivarjayet” iti||10||-

taistairbhāvairabhihate kṣate vā'pathyasevinaḥ ||11||
kṣatoṣmā vāyuvisṛtaḥ saraktaṃ pittamīrayet |
jvarastṛṣṇā ca dāhaśca jāyate tasya dehinaḥ ||12||
eṣa vidradhirāgantuḥ pittavidradhilakṣaṇaḥ |13|

āgantoḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- taistairityādi| taistairiti aśvapatanagū(mū)ḍhaprahārādibhirityarthaḥ; tenāsrutaśoṇitapiccitādīnāṃ [4] grahaṇam| kṣate vetyatra taistairbhāvairiti tīkṣṇaprahārādibhirityarthaḥ; anena ca srutaśoṇitānāṃ chinnādīnāṃ grahaṇam| kṣatoṣmā vāyuvisṛto vātena vistāritaḥ| pittavidradhilakṣaṇo ‘bhavati’ ityadhyāhāraḥ||11-12||-

abhighātajasyāgantoḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- taistairbhāvairityādi| prapatanaprahārādibhirhate'tyantaduṣṭaśoṇitānām| hate iti mathitādīnāmapyupalakṣaṇam| kṣate iti taistairbhāvairityādibhiḥ| kṣata iti srutaśoṇitānāṃ chinnādīnāmupalakṣaṇam| tatraikatra mathitapiccitayoḥ sirāmārgāvaraṇena vātakopaḥ, anyatra chinnādau dhātukṣayeṇātiraktāsrāvanimittena| tatrābhighātajasyopacārārthaṃ doṣaliṅgaṃ darśayannāha- pittavidradhilakṣaṇa iti| raktaṃ ca raktaje eva sravatīti pittavidradhiliṅgātideśaḥ kṛtaḥ; pītatvasyāvayavavikṛtatvāt||11-12||-

kṛṣṇasphoṭāvṛtaḥ śyāvastīvradāharujājvaraḥ [5] ||13||
pittavidradhiliṅgastu raktavidradhirucyate |14|

raktavidradhilakṣaṇamāha- kṛṣṇasphoṭāvṛta ityādi||13||-

raktaje dāhapākādīnāṃ tīvratvamadhikam| raktasya dūṣyatvāddoṣaliṅgamatideśena nirdiśannāha- pittasya vidradhairliṅgairityādi| bhojaprabhṛtayastu raktavidradhiṃ nādhīyate| anye tviha raktaje'dhikavidhānārthaṃ raktajaṃ paṭhantyeva||13||-

uktā vidradhayo hyete teṣvasādhyastu sarvajaḥ ||14||

bāhyeṣvasādhyamāha- uktā ityādi| sarvajaḥ sānnipātikaḥ||14||

bāhyeṣvasādhyaṃ nirdiśannāha- uktā vidradhaya ityādi| teṣvasādhyatvena tridoṣajaḥ pṛthak kriyate||14||

ābhyantarānatastūrdhvaṃ vidradhīn paricakṣate |
gurvasātmyaviruddhānnaśuṣkasaṃsṛṣṭabhojanāt [6] ||15||
ativyavāyavyāyāmavegāghātavidāhibhiḥ |
pṛthak sambhūya vā doṣāḥ kupitā gulmarūpiṇam ||16||
valmīkavatsamunnaddhamantaḥ kurvanti vidradhim |
gude bastimukhe nābhyāṃ kukṣau vaṅkṣaṇayostathā ||17||
vṛkkayoryakṛti plīhni hṛdaye klomni vā tathā |
teṣāṃ liṅgāni jānīyādbāhyavidradhilakṣaṇaiḥ ||18||
āmapakvaiṣaṇīyācca [7] pakvāpakvaṃ vinirdiśet |19|

antarvidradherhetusamprāptilakṣaṇānyāha- ābhyantarānityādi| saṃsṛṣṭabhojanāt [8] kadaśanabhojanāt| sambhūya militvā| gulmarūpiṇaṃ valmīkavatsamunnaddhaṃ vidradhiṃ kurvantīti sambandhaḥ; gulmarūpiṇaṃ kāṭhinyena, valmīkavatsamunnaddhaṃ sarvata unnatam| gurvasātmyetyādi vidāhibhiriti yāvaddhetuḥ, pṛthak sambhūya veti samprāptiḥ, gulmarūpiṇaṃ valmīkavatsamunnaddhamiti ca sāmānyalakṣaṇam| tatrāpi valmīkavatsamunnaddhatvaṃ pacyamānāvasthāyām| teṣāṃ vātādibhedabhinnānāṃ viśeṣavarṇaliṅgasaṅkhyādikaṃ bāhyavidradhivajjñeyam| jejjaṭastu ābhyantarasyāgantorabhāvameva manyate| teṣāṃ bāhyānāmābhyantarāṇāṃ ca sthānamāha- gude ityādi| gudādiṣu sthāneṣu doṣā vidradhiṃ kurvantīti sambandhaḥ| bastimukhe na tu bastau, tatra māṃsamedasorabhāvāt| vaṅkṣaṇa ūrumūlāśrayaḥ ‘gaṇḍaka’ iti loke| vṛkkau māṃsapiṇḍadvayam (eko vāmapārśvasthitaḥ, dvitīyo dakṣiṇapārśvasthitaḥ [9] )| yakṛt kālakhaṇḍaṃ dakṣiṇapārśvastham| plīhā udaravāmapārśve sthitaḥ| hṛdayaṃ kamalamukulākāramadhomukham| kloma kālakhaṇḍādadhastāt sthitaṃ dakṣiṇapārśvasthaṃ tilakaṃ prasiddham| teṣāmābhyantarāṇāmityarthaḥ||15-18||-

adhiṣṭhānaviśeṣeṇa viśeṣaliṅgajñānārthaṃ sādhyāsādhyaviniścayārthaṃ cābhyantarān vidradhīn darśayannāha- ābhyantarānityādi| abhyantarabhavān| pṛthak sambhūya veti samprāptiḥ| āganterābhyantarasya bāhyāgantuvidradhisamprāptyabhidhānenaiva samprāptiruktā| gulmarūpiṇamiti prathamaṃ sāmānyalakṣaṇaṃ gulmarūpamevābhyantaravidradhīnām| valmīkavatsamunnaddhatvaṃ sarveṣāmeva samānam| śeṣaṃ varṇarujāsrāvādi bāhyavidradhilakṣaṇenaivāvagantavyam| ata evoktaṃ “sarvaśothavikāreṣu vraṇavallakṣayet bhiṣak” (sū. 22) iti| teṣāṃ sādhyāsādhyatvavibhāgārthaṃ lakṣaṇaviśeṣajñānārthaṃ cādhiṣṭhānaṃ nirdiśannāha- gude bastimukhe ityādi| āmapakvaiṣaṇīyācceti cakāro bhinnakrame, āmaṃ pakvaṃ ca vibhāvayediti| āmapakvaiṣaṇīyāditi lyablope pañcamī; tenāmapakvaiṣaṇīyamavekṣyetyarthaḥ||15-18||-

adhiṣṭhānaviśeṣeṇa liṅgaṃ śṛṇu viśeṣataḥ ||19||
gude vātanirodhastu bastau kṛcchrālpamūtratā |
nābhyāṃ hikkā tathā''ṭopaḥ kukṣau mārutakopanam ||20||
kaṭīpṛṣṭhagrahastīvro vaṅkṣaṇotthe tu vidradhau |
vṛkkayoḥ pārśvasaṅkocaḥ plīhnyucchvāsāvarodhanam ||21||
sarvāṅgapragrahastīvro hṛdi śūlaśca dāruṇaḥ |
śvāso yakṛti tṛṣṇā ca pipāsāklomaje'dhikā ||22||

bāhyānāmābhyantarāṇāṃ cādhiṣṭhānaviśeṣeṇa lakṣaṇaṃ darśayannāha- adhiṣṭhānetyādi| āṭopaḥ saṅkṣobhaḥ| pārśvasaṅkocaḥ pārśvotpāṭanamiva| hṛdīti hṛdayotthe vidradhau, sarvāṅgapragrahādaya upadravā bhavantītyarthaḥ| ‘klomni pipāsā’ ityādi pāṭhāntaraṃ kecit paṭhanti, taccābhāvānna likhitam||19-22||

adhiṣṭhānaviśeṣeṇa liṅgaṃ darśayannāha- gude vātanirodha ityādi| tathā āṭopaḥ saṅkṣobhaḥ| klomni pipāsā mukhasya śoṣaḥ; ‘pepīyate payaḥ’ ityanye paṭhanti, tatrātyarthaṃ pīyate paya ityarthaḥ| kukṣau mārutakopanaṃ vidradhikṛtamārganirodhāt| sarvāṅgapragrahaḥ sarveṣāmaṅgānāṃ prakarṣeṇa grahaṇamiva| tīvro dussahaḥ, ūrdhvādhastiryaksirādhiṣṭhānatvāddhṛdayasya||19-22||

āmo vā yadi vā pakvo mahān vā yadi vetaraḥ |
sarvo marmotthitaścāpi [10] vidradhiḥ kaṣṭa ucyate ||23||

tatra marmotthitānāṃ sarvāvasthāsu kaṣṭatvaṃ darśayannāha- āma ityādi| yadi vetara iti itaro'lpaḥ||23||

marmotthitānāṃ sarvāsvavasthāsu kaṣṭatvaṃ darśayannāha- āmo vā yadi vā pakva ityādi||23||

nābheruparijāḥ pakvā yāntyūrdhvamitare tvadhaḥ |
jīvatyadho niḥsruteṣu sruteṣūrdhvaṃ na jīvati ||24||
hṛnnābhibastivarjyā ye teṣu bhinneṣu bāhyataḥ |
jīvet kadācit puruṣo netareṣu kadācana ||25||

avasthāviśeṣādasādhyatvamāha- nābherityādi| nābheruparijāḥ plīhayakṛtklomakukṣihṛdayajāḥ, pakvā ūrdhvaṃ yānti sravanti| itare gudabastivṛkkavaṅkṣaṇajā adho yānti| eṣu cādhoniḥsruteṣu jīvati, nordhvaniḥsruteṣu| hṛnnābhibastijān varjayitvā ye'nye gudakukṣivaṅkṣaṇavṛkkaplīhayakṛtklomajāsteṣvantarvidradhiṣu bāhyāto bhinneṣu kadāciddaivavaśājjīvet, na punaritareṣvabhyantarato bhinneṣvityarthaḥ| hṛnnābhibastijānāṃ cāsādhyatvamagrevācyam||24-25||

idānīṃ viśiṣṭāvayavavibhāgena sādhyāsādhyatvajñāpanārthamasya srāvanirgamamārgamaviśeṣaṃ darśayannāha- nābheruparijāḥ pakvā ityādi||24-25||

strīṇāmapaprajātānāṃ prajātānāṃ tathā'hitaiḥ |
dāhajvarakaro ghoro jāyate raktavidradhiḥ ||26||

ābhyantaraṃ raktajaṃ nārīṇāmeva darśayannāha- strīṇāmityādi| strīṇāṃ raktāvidradhirjāyata iti sambandhaḥ| kimbhūtānāṃ strīṇām? apaprajātānāṃ, patitagarbhaṇāṃ; punarapi kimbhūtānāṃ? prajātānāṃ samyakkālaprasūtānām| ahitaiḥ śītarūkṣāhāravihāraiḥ||26||

api samyakprajātānāmasṛk kāyādaniḥsṛtam |
raktajaṃ vidradhiṃ kuryāt kukṣau makkallasañjñitam ||27||
saptāhānnopaśāntaścettato'sau samprapacyate |28|

tasyaiva raktavidradherviśeṣamāha- api samyagityādi| makkallasañjñitaṃ makkallajanitaṃ, kāraṇe kāryopacārāt; ghṛtamāyuriti yathā| saptarātramapraśāntaḥ pittoṣmaṇā'ntaḥ pacyate, pakvaścāsādhyaḥ||27||-

ābhyantaraṃ raktajaṃ vidradhiṃ nārīṇāmeva darśayannāha- strīṇāmityādi| adhaḥprajātānām akālaprajātānām; ‘apaprajātānāṃ’ ityanye; tatra patitagarbhāṇāmityarthaḥ| prajātānāṃ samyakkāle prajātānāmityarthaḥ| tatrāpi raktanirodhe hetumāha- ahitairiti; śītarūkṣāhāravihāraiḥ| makkallasañjñitaṃ makkalajanitaṃ, kāraṇe kāryopacārāt; ghṛtamāyuriti yathā| tatra saptarātrādanupakrāntaḥ pittoṣmaṇā pacyate| atraivārthe bhojaḥ- “prajātānāṃ tu nārīṇāṃ makkallapratimaḥ sthiraḥ| saptarātrāt paraṃ kukṣau jāyate raktavidradhiḥ|| na cet praśāntaḥ saptāhādahitāśanataḥ kvacit| pacyate pittasaṃsargāttadā varjyo vijānatā” iti| kecidatrāpi “atha majjaparīpākaḥ” ityādikaṃ granthamadhīyate| prāyeṇa hi medosthisamāśraya eva vidradhiḥ sarvaḥ| yadāha- “tvagraktamāṃsamedosthi pradūṣyāsthisamāśritāḥ” iti| bhojenapyuktaṃ “sthitvā'sthni vā samuddhatasyuratyāt(?)| yasyāvapākakarmaṇā(?)'sthi vinirbhidya cirādvā prasavedyadi| vidagdho darśayedrūpaṃ vātādīnāṃ muhurmuhuḥ” iti| sarvavidradhiśeṣaṃ majjapākamicchanti||26-27||-

viśeṣamatha vakṣyāmi spaṣṭaṃ vidradhigulmayoḥ ||28||
gulmadoṣasamutthānādvidradhergulmakasya ca |
kasmānna pacyate gulmo vidradhiḥ pākameti ca ||29||
na nibandho'sti gulmānāṃ vidradhiḥ sanibandhanaḥ |
gulmākārāḥ svayaṃ doṣā vidradhirmāṃsaśoṇite ||30||
vivarānucaro granthirapsu budbudako yathā |
evamprakāro gulmastu tasmāt pākaṃ na gacchati ||31||
māṃsaśoṇitabāhulyāt pākaṃ gacchati vidradhiḥ |
māṃsaśoṇitahīnatvādgulmaḥ pākaṃ na gacchati ||32||
gulmastiṣṭhati doṣe sve vidradhirmāṃsaśoṇite |
vidradhiḥ pacyate tasmādgulmaścāpi na pacyate ||33||

yat prāguktaṃ “pṛthak sambhūya vā doṣāḥ kupitā gulmarūpiṇam” ityatraikarūpatvādantarvidradhigulmayoḥ kuto gulmādasya vidradheḥ pākakṛto viśeṣa ityāha- viśeṣamatha vakṣyāmītyādi| samutthānaṃ kāraṇaṃ dvayorvidradhigulmayoḥ samānaṃ, tat kiṃ gulmo na pacyata iti saṃśayamutpādya svayameva śiṣyāṇāṃ hitatayā siddhāntamātrasūtraṇamācāryeṇa kṛtam| evaṃ manyate- bhedo hi bhedavatāṃ kāraṇabhedādviruddhadharmādhyāsādvā, tatra kāraṇamabhinnamiti sarvathaivābhedena bhāvitavyamityāśaṅkyopādānakāraṇabhedenānayorbhedameva siddhāntaṃ darśayannāha- na nibandho'stītyādi| niścitya badhnātīti nibandho mūlaṃ rasaraktādi; dūṣyāṇāmabhāvādgulmānāṃ mūlaṃ nāstīti| kutaḥ punareṣāṃ mahattaraśotharūpāṇāṃ mūlaṃ nāstītyāha- gulmākārāḥ svayaṃ doṣā iti|- gulmākārā gulmarūpā ātmanaiva, doṣā vātādayaḥ; na punardūṣyasaṃśrayāḥ| vidradhestu mūlaṃ sahetumāha- vidradhirmāṃsaśoṇite; ‘bhavati’ iti śeṣaḥ| māṃsaśoṇite eva pākahetuḥ; tena cātropagīte| tatra kathaṃ pitte kaphe vā drave vātajanitasya gulmasya sthitirityāha- vivarānucaro gulmo hyapsu budbudako yathā| māṃsaśoṇitabāhulyādityādi| kinniviṣṭo gulmo bhavatītyāha- gulmastiṣṭhati sve doṣa iti; doṣe eva vātapittakaphasthānarūpe| doṣamātrāstitvaṃ punarapsu budbudako yathetyena pratipāditam| gulmaścāpi na pacyata ityapiśabdena pacyate'pi gulma iti kāyacikitsāsiddhaṃ gulmapākitvamuktaṃ samuccīyate| taduktaṃ carake- “raktapittātibāhulyāt kriyāmanupalabhya vā| gulmo yatra vidahyeta śastraṃ tatra bhiṣagjitam” (ca. ci. 5) iti| daivavaśādgulmo'pi kṛtaparigrahaḥ pacyate; sa khalu samūlatvādvidradhireveti dhānvantarīyāṇāṃ matamiti||28-33||
