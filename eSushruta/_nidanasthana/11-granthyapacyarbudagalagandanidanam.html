--- title: "11. granthyapacyarbudagalagaṇḍanidānam" adhyaya: 11 order: 11 ---

athāto granthapacyarbudagalagaṇḍānāṃ nidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi||1-2||

vātādayo māṃsamasṛk ca duṣṭāḥ sandūṣya medaśca kaphānuviddham [1] |
vṛttonnataṃ vigrathitaṃ tu śophaṃ kurvantyato granthiriti pradiṣṭaḥ ||3||

teṣāṃ hetusamprāptilakṣaṇaniruktīrāha- vātādaya ityādi| vātādayo doṣā duṣṭā māṃsamasṛk ca sandūṣya śophaṃ kurvantīti sambandhaḥ| etena trayo granthaya uktāḥ| medaśca kaphānuviddhaṃ kaphasaṃsṛṣṭaṃ vāyudūṣitatvācchophaṃ karotīti sambandhaḥ| bhoje'pi vātakartṛtvaṃ medogranthāvabhihitam| tathā ca,- “vāyurmedo yadā māṃse saṅkṣipedathavā tvaci| tatra medobhavo granthiḥ śyāvo bhavati kaṇḍulaḥ”- ityādi| ayaṃ caturtho granthiḥ| cakārāt sirājo granthiḥ pañcamo grāhyaḥ| duṣṭā vṛddhiṃ gatā ityarthaḥ| vṛtto vartulaḥ, unnata ucchritaḥ| vigrathito'tyarthaṃ kaṭhinaḥ| vātādayo duṣṭā ityanena hetūpadeśaḥ, māṃsamasṛk ca sandūṣyeti samprāptiḥ, vṛttonnataṃ viprathitaṃ tu śophamiti lakṣaṇopadeśaḥ, ato granthiriti pradiṣṭa iti niruktiḥ||3||

medogranthiḥ [2] pṛthageva samprāptyā'nvitaḥ; yaduktaṃ bhoje- “vāyurmedo yadā māṃse vikṣipedathavā tvaci| tatra medobhavo granthiḥ śyāvo bhavati kaṇḍuraḥ [3] || kṛśaḥ kṛśe mahān sthūle granthirbhinnaśca pīḍitaḥ| tilakalkanibhaḥ srāvo ghṛtavaccāsya jāyate” iti| sirāgranthestu pṛthageva samprāptiḥ||1-3||

āyamyate vyathyata [4] eti todaṃ pratyasyate kṛtyata [5] eti bhedam |
kṛṣṇo'mṛdurbastirivātataśca bhinnaḥ sraveccānilajo'sramaccham ||4||

teṣāṃ vātādijānāṃ pratyekaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ lakṣaṇamāha- āyamyata ityādi| āyamyate vāyorākarṣaṇena dīrgha iva kriyate, ‘granthiḥ’ iti śeṣaḥ| vyathyate vidhūyate| eti gacchati| todaṃ vyathām| pratyasyate kṣipyate| kṛtyate chidyate| eti bhedamiti bhedaṃ ca gacchati| amṛduḥ karkaśaḥ| bastirivātataśceti mūtrāśayavaddīrghaḥ| asraṃ rudhiram||4||

āyamyata iti vāyorākarṣaṇena dīrgha iva kriyate| ‘vyadhyate’ ityatra ‘vyāthyati’ ityeke paṭhanti||4||

dandahyate dhūpyati cūṣyate [6] ca pāpacyate prajvalatīva cāpi |
raktaḥ sapīto'pyathavā'pi pittādbhinnaḥ sraveduṣṇamatīva cāsram ||5||

dandahyate bhṛśaṃ dahyata iva| dhūpyati cātimātram atiśayena santāpaṃ karoti| pāpacyate bhṛśaṃ pacyata iva| prajvalatīva bhasmībhavatīva||5||

pittagranthau dandahyate bhṛśaṃ dahyata iva| pāpacyate bhṛśaṃ pacyate iva| prajvalatīva prakarṣeṇa jvalatīva||5||

śīto'vivarṇo'lparujo'tikaṇḍūḥ pāṣāṇavat saṃhananopapannaḥ |
cirābhivṛddhiśca kaphaprakopādbhinnaḥ sravecchuklaghanaṃ ca pūyam ||6||

avivarṇa īṣadvivarṇaḥ| pāṣāṇavat pāṣāṇa iva ‘kaṭhina’ ityadhyāhāraḥ| saṃhananopapannaḥ saṅghātānvito mahānityarthaḥ||6||

kaphagranthau śīto'vivarṇa iti īṣadvivarṇaḥ| pāṣāṇavat kaṭhinaḥ| saṃhananopapannaḥ saṃhatānvito mahānityarthaḥ||6||

śarīravṛddhikṣayavṛddhihāniḥ snigdho mahānalparujo'tikaṇḍūḥ |
medaḥkṛto gacchati cātra bhinne piṇyākasarpiḥpratimaṃ tu medaḥ ||7||

śarīravṛddhikṣayavṛddhihāniriti dehāpyāyanahrāsābhyāmāpyāyanahrāsau yasya sa tathā| gacchati sravati meda iti sambandhaḥ| atra medogranthau bhinne sati, piṇyākasarpiḥpratimaṃ tilakalkastyānaghṛtasadṛśam||7||

śarīravṛddhikṣayavṛddhihāniriti dehāpyāyanahrāsābhyāmāpyāyanahrāsau yasya sa tathā| gacchati cātra bhinne piṇyākasarpiḥpratimaṃ tu bhedaḥ| bhinne sati tilakalkastyānaghṛtasadṛśaṃ medo gacchati sravatītyarthaḥ||7||

vyāyāmajātairabalasya taistairākṣipya vāyurhi sirāpratānam |
sampīḍya saṅkocya viśoṣya cāpi granthiṃ karotyunnatamāśu vṛttam ||8||
granthiḥ sirājaḥ sa tu kṛcchrasādhyo bhavedyadi syāt sarujaścalaśca |
aruk sa evāpyacalo [7] mahāṃśca marmotthitaścāpi vivarjanīyaḥ ||9||
iti granthīnāṃ nidānaṃ samāptam |

sirājasya grantheḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- vyāyāmajātairityādi| vyāyāmajātaiḥ vyāyāmasamūhaiḥ| abalasya puruṣasya| sirāpratānaṃ sirāvistāram| sirājasya kṛcchrasādhyatvamāha- sa tu kṛcchrasādhya ityādi| tasyāsādhyatvamāha- arugityādi| mahāṃśceti cakāreṇa vātādijā apyevaṃrūpā varjanīyāḥ| marmotthitaśceti cakāreṇa arujo'pi vivarjanīyaḥ| anye punaratra “māṃsāsrajaṃ cārbudalakṣaṇena tulyaṃ hi dṛṣṭaṃ tvatha lakṣaṇajñaiḥ”- iti ṣaṣṭhaṃ granthiṃ paṭhanti| tanna, jejjaṭādibhiḥ parihṛtatvāt||8-9||

sirājasya grantheḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- vyāyāmajātairityādi| ayaṃ rujāyuktaḥ kṛcchrasādhyaḥ| sa cārujaścāpyacalo mahāṃśca marmotthitaścāpi vivarjanīyaḥ| taduktamanyatra- “sirājaścirasādhyastu sarvebhyo durupakramaḥ” iti| sa ceti cakāreṇa vātādijo'pyetādṛśalakṣaṇo'sādhya eva| tathā ca bhojaḥ- “durbalasya sirā vāyurmaithunādiprajāgaraiḥ| granthiṃ karoti prathitamākṣipan pariśoṣayan|| sirājālāvṛtaṃ kṛtsnaṃ pīḍayan sarujaṃ calam| pañcaitānarujān granthīnmarmajānacalāṃstyajet|| kapolagalamanyāsu duścikitsyāśca sandhiṣu| asādhyān varjayan sādhyān kṛcchrasādhyāṃśca sādhayet” iti| anye punaratra māṃsāsṛgjaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ granthiṃ paṭhanti- māṃsāsrajaṃ cārbudalakṣaṇena tulyaṃ hi ṣaṣṭhaṃ (dṛṣṭaṃ) tvatha lakṣaṇajñaiḥ” iti| ayamevārthaḥ samānatantreṣvapi pāramparyāt smaryate iti ca vadanti||8-9||

hanvasthikakṣākṣakabāhusandhimanyāgaleṣūpacitaṃ tu medaḥ |
granthiṃ sthiraṃ vṛttamathāyataṃ vā snigdhaṃ kaphaścālparujaṃ karoti ||10||
taṃ granthibhistvāmalakāsthimātrairmatsyāṇḍajālapratimaistathā'nyaiḥ [8] |
ananyavarṇairupacīyamānaṃ cayaprakarṣādapacīṃ vadanti ||11||
kaṇḍūyutāste'lparujaḥ prabhinnāḥ sravanti naśyanti bhavanti cānye |
medaḥkaphābhyāṃ khalu roga eṣa sudustaro varṣagaṇānubandhī ||12||

idānīmapacyāḥ samprāptyādīnāha- hanvasthītyādi| hanvorasthi hanvasthi; sandhiśabdo hanvasthyādibhirbāhvantaiḥ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; bāhusandhiśabdena kūrpasandhirucyate; akṣakaṃ koṣṭhakaṃ tasya sandhiḥ, etena grīvāgrakasandhirucyate [9] ; manyā grīvāpaścimabhāgasthā dhamanī| hanvasthītyādisamprāptiḥ, granthiṃ sthiraṃ vṛttamityādilakṣaṇam| taṃ granthibhirityādi taṃ pūrvoktaṃ granthim| ananyavarṇaiḥ samānavarṇaiḥ| upacīyamānaṃ vardhamānam| cayaprakarṣāditi upacayātiśayādityarthaḥ| cayaprakarṣādapacīmiti niruktiḥ| khaluśabdo'pyarthaḥ| tena medaḥkaphau vātādidoṣāntarānuviddhau jñeyau| tathāhi bhojaḥ,- “vātapittakaphā vṛddhā medaścāpi samācitam| jaṅghayoḥ kaṇḍarāḥ prāpya matsyāṇḍasadṛśān bahūn|| kurvanti grathitāṃstebhyaḥ punaḥ prakupito'nilaḥ| tān doṣānūrdhvago [10] vakṣaḥkakṣāmanyāgalāśritaḥ|| nānāprakārān kurute granthīn sā tvapacī smṛtā| vyāmiśradoṣajātā sā kṛcchrasādhyā prakīrtitā|| tāsāṃ vātottarā rūkṣā vātavedanayā'nvitā| kṣiprapākasamutthānā dāhayuktā tu paittikī|| gūḍhā vā'pākakaṭhinā kaphāt snigdhā'lparukkarā| medo'dhikā ślaiṣmikī ca viśeṣādatimārdavā|| apacī kaṇṭhamanyāsu kakṣāvaṅkṣaṇasandhisu| tāṃ tu mālākṛtiṃ vidyāt kaṇṭhahṛddhanusandhiṣu|| gaṇḍamālāṃ vijānīyādapacītulyalakṣaṇām”- iti| carake gaṇḍamālā gaṇḍe eva paṭhitā “medaḥkaphācchoṇitasañcayottho gaṇḍasya pārśve galagaṇḍa ekaḥ| syādgaṇḍamālā bahubhistu gaṇḍairdīptānale yūni balānvite ca|| sādhyā smṛtā pīnasapārśvaśūlakāsajvaracchardiyutā tvasādhyā” (ca. ci. 12)- iti| anye tu ‘medaḥkaphābhyāṃ punareṣa rogaḥ’ iti paṭhanti; punaḥśabdādadhikārthāddoṣāntarasambandhaḥ sthāpito bhavatīti ca vadanti| tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ| varṣagaṇānubandhī varṣasamūhasambandhītyarthaḥ| etena dīrgharogitvaṃ sūcitam| medaḥkaphābhyāmiti hetūddeśaḥ||10-12||

grantheranantaramapacyārambhaḥ, granthiviśeṣādapacyāḥ, medaḥsamucchrayagranthipratyāsannataśca| tasyāḥ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- hanvasthikakṣākṣaketyādi| hanvorasthi hanvasthi| sandhiśabdo hanvasthyādibhirbāhvantaiḥ saha pratyekamabhisambadhyate| bāhusandhiśabdena kūrparasandhirucyate| bāhumūlasandheḥ kakṣāśabdenaivopapannatvāt| akṣasandhiḥ grīvādhaḥsandhiḥ| eṣu sandhipradeśeṣūpacitaṃ dūṣyaṃ medaḥ, kaphaśca doṣo granthiṃ karoti| tamupacīyamānaṃ khalu anyairgranthibhirāmalakāsthipramāṇairmīnāṇḍākāraiḥ| cayaprakarṣādapacīti niruktiḥ| ‘cayāpakarṣāt’ iti kecit paṭhanti| sā punarupacayāpacayābhyāmapacīyate| upacayāpacayau darśayannāha- naśyanti bhavanti cānye iti| medaḥkaphābhyāmiti pūrvoktadoṣadūṣyayoreva niyamanam| khaluśabdo'pyarthaḥ, nipātānāmanekārthatvāt| tena medaḥkaphayoreva vātādidoṣāntarasaṃsargaṃ sūcayati| tathāhi bhojaḥ- “vātapittakaphā vṛddhā medaścāpi samācitam| jaṅghayoḥ kaṇḍarāḥ prāpya matsyāṇḍasadṛśān bahūn|| kurvanti grathitāṃstebhyaḥ punaḥ prakupito'nilaḥ| tān doṣānūrdhvago vakṣaḥkakṣāmanyāgalāśritaḥ|| nānāprakārāt kurute granthīn sā tvapacī smṛtā| vyāmiśradoṣajātā sā kṛcchrasādhyā prakīrtitā|| tāsāṃ vātottarā rūkṣā vātavedanayā'nvitā| kṣiprapākasamutthānā dāhayuktā tu paittikī|| gūḍhapākā ca kaṭhinā kaphāt snigdhā'lparuk sthirā| medodhikā ślaiṣmikī ca viśeṣādatimārdavā|| apacī kaṇṭhamanyāsu kakṣāvaṅkṣaṇasandhiṣu| tāṃ tu mālākṛtisamāṃ kaṇṭhahṛddhanusandhiṣu|| gaṇḍamālāṃ vijānīyātadapacītulyalakṣaṇām” iti| sthānakṛta eva bhedo'pacīgaṇḍamālayoḥ, grahaṇyatisārayoriva| gale eva mālāsthānā gaṇḍamālā carakeṇa paṭhitā- “medaḥkaphācchoṇitasañcayotthaḥ| galasya madhye galagaṇḍa ekaḥ syādgaṇḍamālā bahubhistu gaṇḍaiḥ|| sādhyāḥ smṛtāḥ pīnasapārśvaśūlakāsajvaracchardiyutāstvasādhyāḥ” iti| (ca. ci. ) ityapacīnāṃ nidānaṃ sampūrṇam||10-12||

gātrapradeśe kvacideva doṣāḥ sammūrcchitā māṃsamabhipradūṣya |
vṛttaṃ sthiraṃ mandarujaṃ mahāntamanalpamūlaṃ ciravṛddhyapākam ||13||
kurvanti māṃsopacayaṃ tu śophaṃ tamarbudaṃ śāstravido vadanti |14|

idānīmarbudādīnāṃ samprāptyādīnāha- gātrapradeśe ityādi| kvacideva gātrapradeśe doṣāḥ śophaṃ kurvantīti sambandhaḥ| sammūrcchitā vṛddhiṃ gatāḥ| kvacidevāniyatapradeśe, na punarapacīvaddhanvasthyādisandhiṣveva| apākam avidyamānapākam| māṃsopacayaṃ māṃsasaṅghātam| gātrapradeśe kvacideveti samprāptiḥ| sammūrcchitā doṣā iti hetuḥ| vṛttaṃ sthiramityādi svalakṣaṇam| gayī tu ‘kurvanti māṃsocchrayamatyagādham’ iti paṭhati||13||-

apacyanantaramarbudārambhaḥ, medaḥkaphādhikajanmasāmānyāt| tasya samprāptimāha- gātrapradeśa ityādi| kvacidevāniyatapradeśe na punarapacīvaddhanvasthyādiṣveva| sammūrcchitā vṛddhāḥ| apākamiti avidyamānapākam| māṃsocchrayaṃ māṃsasaṅghātam| atyagādhamāṃsaṃ tanmūlatvāt; tasmādeva hetorarbudamapākam; agādhamāṃsocchrayamarbudaṃ jāyate, vastusvabhāvāditi||13||-

vātena pittena kaphena cāpi raktena māṃsena ca medasā ca ||14||
tajjāyate tasya ca lakṣaṇāni grantheḥ samānāni sadā bhavanti |15|

tajjāyate tadarbudaṃ bhavatītyarthaḥ| tasya ca lakṣaṇāni tasya vātapittakaphamedojasya||14||-

teṣāṃ vātapittakaphamedojānāṃ vātādigranthilakṣaṇairlakṣaṇāni samānāni||14||-

doṣaḥ praduṣṭo rudhiraṃ sirāstu sampīḍya saṅkocya gatastvapākam ||15||
sāsrāvamunnahyati māṃsapiṇḍaṃ māṃsāṅkurairācitamāśuvṛddhim |
sravatyajasraṃ rudhiraṃ praduṣṭamasādhyametadrudhirātmakaṃ syāt ||16||
raktakṣayopadravapīḍitatvāt pāṇḍurbhavet so'rbudapīḍitastu |17|

raktajasyārbudasya lakṣaṇamāha- doṣaḥ praduṣṭa ityādi| praduṣṭo doṣaḥ sirāsu gato rudhiraṃ sampīḍya saṅkocya māṃsapiṇḍamunnahyatīti sambandhaḥ| unnahyati udbadhnātīva; anye tu ‘praduṣṭo doṣa unnahyati ucchrito bhavati, māṃsapiṇḍamāśuvṛddhiṃ śīghravardhanaśīlaṃ karoti’ iti vyākhyānayanti| apākam īṣatpākam||15-16||-

śoṇitajasya lakṣaṇaṃ darśayannāha- doṣaḥ praduṣṭa ityādi| apākamīṣatpākam, īṣadarthe nañ; alomikā piṇḍiketi yathā| doṣa unnahyati ucchrito bhavati| na kevalamunnahyati āśuvṛddhiṃ śīghravṛddhiṃ māṃsapiṇḍaṃ ca karoti| sravedajasram adhiṣṭhānabhūtasirārudhiraṃ; natu pākakṛtam, īṣadeva kledamātralakṣaṇasyāsrāvasyoktatvāt||15-16||-

muṣṭiprahārādibhirardite'ṅge māṃsaṃ praduṣṭaṃ prakaroti śopham ||17||
avedanaṃ snigdhamananyavarṇamapākamaśmopamamapracālyam |
praduṣṭamāṃsasya narasya bāḍhametadbhavenmāṃsaparāyaṇasya ||18||
māṃsārbudaṃ tvetadasādhyamuktaṃ... |19|

māṃsajasya samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- muṣṭiprahārādibhirityādi| samupaiti samyaggacchati| bāḍham atiśayena| māṃsaparāyaṇasya māṃsabhakṣaṇaśīlasya| ‘muṣṭiprahārādibhiḥ’ ityatra kecit ‘kāṣṭhaprahārādibhiḥ’ iti paṭhanti||17-18||-

...sādhyeṣvapīmāni vivarjayettu |
samprasrutaṃ marmaṇi yacca jātaṃ srotaḥsu vā yacca bhavedacālyam ||19||
yajjāyate'nyat [11] khalu pūrvajāte jñeyaṃ tadadhyarbudamarbudajñaiḥ |
yaddvandvajātaṃ yugapat kramādvā dvirarbudaṃ tacca bhavedasādhyam ||20||

sādhyeṣvapītyādi| sādhyeṣvapīmāni vivarjayediti sādhyeṣu madhye imāni vakṣyamāṇāni vivarjayedityarthaḥ| adhyarbudamapi dvirarbudameva, anye ‘atyarbudaṃ’ iti paṭhanti| yaddvandvajātamityādi dvandvena yugmena jātaṃ dvandvajātaṃ; na kevalamadhyarbudaṃ dvirarbudaṃ, yaddvandvajātaṃ tadapi dvirarbudamityarthaḥ| bhedaśca tayoruparijātatvena pārśvajātatvena ca| etena dvirarbudadvitayamapyasādhyamuktam||19-20||

māṃsajasya samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- kāṣṭhaprahārādibhirityādi| māṃsaparāyaṇasyeti māṃsaikaparasyātimāṃsavṛddhermāṃsajaṃ, tadasādhyaṃ; tathaiva samprasrutādīnyapyasādhyāni| adhyarbudam adhikamarbudam; adhyarbudaṃ yattadapi dvirarbudaṃ, yad dvandvena yugapadeva jātaṃ kramādvā tadadhyarbudaṃ dvirarbudamapi; dvayametadasādhyam| tathā ca bhojaḥ- “arbade tvarbudaṃ jātaṃ dvandvajaṃ vā'nujaṃ ca yat| dvirarbudamiti jñeyaṃ tadasādhyaṃ vinirdiśet” iti||17-20||

na pākamāyānti kaphādhikatvānmedobahutvācca viśeṣatastu |
doṣasthiratvādgrathanācca teṣāṃ sarvārbudānyeva nisargatastu ||21||

teṣāṃ pākābhāve hetuṃ darśayannāha- na pākamityādi| granthyādiṣvapi kaphādayaścatvāro hetavaḥ santi, paramarbudeṣu viśeṣataḥ kaphasya medasaścādhikyaṃ tasmāt, tathā viśeṣato doṣasthiratvād doṣakāryasya māṃsocchrayasya tenaiva svarūpeṇa cirakālāvasthitiśīlatvāt, tathā grathanād granthirūpatvātteṣāṃ medaḥprabhṛtīnāṃ; nisargatastviti tuśabdo'pyarthaḥ, tena svabhāvādapyarbudāni pākaṃ na gacchatītyarthaḥ||21||

teṣāṃ pākābhāve hetuṃ darśayannāha- na pākamāyāntītyādi| granthyādiṣvapi kaphādayaścatvāro hetavaḥ, arbudeṣu punaḥ kaphamedasorādhikyamiti viśeṣaḥ, tato na pākamāyānti| sarvāṇyevārbudāni pittaraktakṛtānyapi na pākamāgacchanti, kutaḥ? kaphādhikatvādityādi| viśeṣata iti viśeṣādatra kaphamedasorādhikyāt| tatraivāsiddhatāsādhanadoṣaṃ nirasyannāha- viśeṣato doṣasthiratvāt; kaphakāryasthiratvāt, tathā medaḥkāryake doṣakāryagrathanarūpāt; viśeṣeṇaivātra kaphamedasoḥ kāryasāmarthyenetyarthaḥ| nisargatastu atra tu śabdo'pyarthaḥ, svabhāvādapītyarthaḥ| bhoje'pyuktaṃ- “na pacyate sthiratvācca grathitatvāt svabhāvataḥ” iti| raktajamāṃsajayorapi doṣā evārambhakāḥ santi, tathā'pi tailadagdhavat raktajādivyapadeśa iti||21||

vātaḥ kaphaścaiva gale [12] pravṛddhau manye tu saṃsṛtya tathaiva medaḥ |
kurvanti gaṇḍaṃ kramaśaḥ svaliṅgaiḥ samanvitaṃ taṃ galagaṇḍamāhuḥ ||22||

idānīṃ galagaṇḍasamprāptiṃ darśayannāha- vāta ityādi| vātādayo gaṇḍaṃ kramaśaḥ kurvantīti sambandhaḥ| kiṃviśiṣṭaṃ gaṇḍaṃ? svaliṅgaiḥ samanvitam| vātena kaphena ca galagaṇḍo bhavati, na pittena; svabhāvena pittajasya galagaṇḍasyābhāvāt||22||

medaḥkaphavātārambhasamānārbudānantaraṃ galagaṇḍārambhaḥ| teṣāṃ samprāptiṃ nirdiśannāha- vātaḥ kaphaścetyādi| vātena kaphena ca galagaṇḍo bhavati na pittena, svabhāvena pittajasya galagaṇḍasyābhāvāt, pittajacāturthakavat| taduktaṃ carake- “caturthako darśayati prabhāvaṃ dvividhaṃ jvaraḥ| jaṅghābhyāṃ ślaiṣmikaḥ pūrvaṃ śirasto'nilasambhavaḥ” (ca. ci. 3) iti| atra ca gaṇḍaśabdasya sāmānyaśopharūpakārthavyudāsyarthaṃ svaliṅgairityuktaṃ; tāni tu galagaṇḍasya sāmānyaliṅgāni “nibaddhaḥ śvayathuḥ” ityādīni vakṣyamāṇāni||22||

todānvitaḥ kṛṣṇasirāvanaddhaḥ kṛṣṇo'ruṇo vā pavanātmakastu |
medonvitaścopacitaśca kālādbhavedatisnigdhataro'rujaśca [13] ||23||
pāruṣyayuktaściravṛddhyapāko yadṛcchayā pākamiyāt kadācit vairasyamāsyasya ca tasya jantorbhavettathā tālugalapraśoṣaḥ ||24||
sthiraḥ savarṇo'lparugugrakaṇḍūḥ [14] śīto mahāṃścāpi kaphātmakastu |
cirābhivṛddhiṃ kurute cirācca prapacyate mandarujaḥ kadācit ||25||
mādhuryamāsyasya ca tasya jantorbhavettathā tālugalapralepaḥ |
snigdho mṛduḥ pāṇḍuraniṣṭagandho medaḥkṛto [15] nīrugathātikaṇḍūḥ ||26||
pralambate'lābuvadalpamūlo dehānurūpakṣayavṛddhiyuktaḥ [16] |
snigdhāsyatā tasya bhavecca jantorgale'nuśabdaṃ [17] kurute ca nityam ||27||

tasyaiva vātādiviśeṣaliṅgāni nirdiśannāha- todānvita ityādi| aruja iti so'pi vātagalagaṇḍo yadā kālāt kālena kṛtvā medo'nvito bhavati, tadā arujo bhavatītyarthaḥ| yadṛcchayā ajñātahetuniyamena, pākamiyāt gacchet| kadācit na sarvakālam| vātajaṃ kaphajaṃ coktvā medojamāha- snigdha ityādi| gale'nuśabdamanunādaṃ pratiśabdamityarthaḥ||23-27||

tasyaiva vātādiviśeṣaliṅgāni nirdiśannāha- todānvita ityādi| śyāvāruṇyaṃ doṣadūṣyarāśeḥ, na punaravarṇasya vāyoreva| kadāciditi na sarvakālam| yadṛcchayā ajñātahetuniyamena| kaphajaḥ savarṇaḥ prakṛtisamānavarṇaḥ| tālugalapralepaḥ praliptatvam| vivardhate hīyati cātra dehaḥ dehavṛddhihānibhyāṃ lakṣaṇābhyāṃ vivardhate hiyati ca| hīyatīti divāderākṛtigaṇatvāt hidhātuṃ parasmaipadeṣu paṭhanti; tena hīyatīti rūpam| anye tu “vṛddhikṣayāvṛcchati cātra dehaḥ” iti paṭhanti| galena śabdamanunādam||23-27||

kṛcchrācchvasantaṃ mṛdusarvagātraṃ saṃvatsarātītamarocakārtam |
kṣīṇaṃ ca vaidyo galagaṇḍinaṃ tu bhinnasvaraṃ caiva vivarjayettu ||28||

idānīṃ galagaṇḍasyāsādhyatāmāha- kṛcchrādityādi||28||

idānīṃ galagaṇḍasyāsādhyatāṃ darśayannāha- kṛcchrācchvasantamityādi||28||

nibaddhaḥ śvayathuryasya muṣkavallambate gale |
mahān vā yadi vā hrasvo galagaṇḍaṃ [18] tamādiśet ||29||

na sarva eva galāśrayo gaṇḍo galagaṇḍaḥ, kiṃ tarhi kaścideva vakṣyamāṇasvarūpa iti darśayannāha- nibaddha ityādi| niyamena baddho nibaddhaḥ| muṣkavat aṇḍavat||29||

na galāśrayo galagaṇḍaḥ, kiṃ tarhi kaścideva pāribhāṣika iti darśayannāha- nibaddha ityādi| niyamena baddho nibaddhaḥ| muṣkavadaṇḍavat| bhojapyuktaṃ- “mahāntaṃ śothamalpaṃ vā hanumanyāgalāśrayam| lambantaṃ muṣkavaddṛṣṭvā galagaṇḍaṃ vinirdiśet” iti||29||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ nidānasthāne galagaṇḍagaṇḍamālāpacyarbudanidānaṃ nāmaikādaśo'dhyāyaḥ ||11||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ nidānasthāne ekādaśo'dhyāyaḥ||11||

iti sauśrute śalyatantre śrīgayadāsaviracitāyāṃ nyāyacandrikākhyāyāṃ pañjikāyāṃ nidānasthāne ekādaśo'dhyāyaḥ||11||
