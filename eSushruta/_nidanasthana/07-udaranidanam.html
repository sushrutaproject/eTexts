--- title: "7. udaranidānam" adhyaya: 7 order: 7 ---

athāta udarāṇāṃ nidānaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi||1-2||

pramehānantaramudarārambhaḥ, udakavahasrotoduṣṭisambhavasāmānyāt, tridoṣajasādharmyānmahāvyādhitvasāmānyācca||1-2||

dhanvantarirdharmabhṛtāṃ variṣṭho rājarṣirindrapratimo'bhavadyaḥ [1] |
brahmarṣiputraṃ vinayopapannaṃ śiṣyaṃ śubhaṃ suśrutamanvaśāt saḥ ||3||

yo dhanvantarirabhavat sa suśrutamanvaśāditi sambandhaḥ, śikṣitavānityarthaḥ| dharmabhṛtāṃ dhārmikāṇāṃ madhye| indrapratimo jñānaiśvaryādibhiḥ| brahmarṣirviśvāmitraḥ, rājarṣirapi tapasā brahmarṣitvaṃ prāptavān| vinayopapannaṃ jitendriyam| śubhamiti pūrvoktairanvayavayaḥśīlādiguṇairupetam||3||

brahmarṣiputramiti viśvāmitro hi rājarṣireva brahmarṣitvaṃ tapasā gataḥ| vinayopapannamityanena vinayānvitena guravaḥ praṣṭavyāḥ, vinītasyaivānuśāsanaṃ kartavyamiti darśayati||3||

pṛthak samastairapi ceha doṣaiḥ plīhodaraṃ baddhagudaṃ tathaiva |
āgantukaṃ saptamamaṣṭamaṃ ca dakodaraṃ ceti vadanti tāni ||4||

udarāṇāṃ saṅkhyāmāha- pṛthagityādi| pṛthagdoṣaistrīṇi, samastairdoṣairekam; etena catvāri doṣapradhānāni| iheti asmiñchāstre| tānīti udarāṇītyarthaḥ| plīhodarādīni catvāri dūṣyapradhānāni| aṣṭamamiti saṅkhyoktyā plīhodarayakṛddālyudarayoraikyameva jñātavyam||4||

tatra doṣaiścatvāri pṛthagekaśastrīṇi, sānnipātikaṃ ca dūṣyodaramityabhihitam| plīhodaramiti plīhodara eva yakṛdudbhavamapyavarudhyate, hetuliṅgacikitsitasāmānyāt| tatra plīhodare doṣau kaphapitte, dūṣyaṃ raktam| baddhagudamāgantuviśeṣaḥ, pṛthagliṅgacikitsitabhedāt| kṣatāntramapyāgantunimittaṃ kṣataje'bhihitam| dakodaram udakodaram, udakanimittaṃ srotovarodhajātam||4||

sudurbalāgnerahitāśanasya saṃśuṣkapūtyannaniṣevaṇādvā |
snehādimithyācaraṇācca jantorvṛddhiṃ gatāḥ koṣṭhamabhiprapannāḥ ||5||
gulmākṛtivyañjitalakṣaṇāni kurvanti ghorāṇyudarāṇi doṣāḥ |6|

udarāṇāṃ hetuṃ darśayannāha- sudurbalāgneritādi durbalāgneḥ atyantanaṣṭāgneḥ| ahitāśanasyeti viruddhasamaśanādhyaśanaviṣamāśanasya| snehādītyādiśabdādvamanavirecanāsthāpanānuvāsanaparigrahaḥ| mithyācaraṇaṃ yathoktānuṣṭānaparityāgaḥ| jantoḥ prāṇinaḥ| abhiprapannāḥ prāptā ityarthaḥ| kiṃviśiṣṭānyudaraṇi kurvanti? gulmākṛtivyañjitalakṣaṇāni gulmākṛtyā gulmarūpeṇa vyañjitāni prakaṭitāni lakṣaṇāni yeṣāṃ tāni tathā| punarapi kiṃviśiṣṭāni? ghorāṇi kṛcchratamāni||5||-

sthānasaṃśrayasyaiva samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- sadurbalāgnerityādi| ahitāśanasya durbalāgneriti- yadyapi hi vahnermāndyanimittānyanyānyapi santi, tathā'pyahitāśanamevāgnimāndyakāriṣu pradhānam| koṣṭhaṃ gatā iti sthānasaṃśrayaḥ| koṣṭhasthasattve'pi nātiśarīrādīnāmatinivṛttiḥ, udakavahasrotorodhaduṣṭisattvāt, sudurbalāgnitvādidoṣavat(?)| gulmākṛtivyañjitalakṣaṇāni gulmākṛtyā gulmarūpeṇa vardhate| tasya prāgutpattyākṛtirapi gulmena samānā||5||-

koṣṭhādupasnehavadannasāro niḥsṛtya duṣṭo'nilaveganunnaḥ ||6||
tvacaḥ samunnamya śanaiḥ samantādvivardhamāno jaṭharaṃ karoti |7|

samprāpti darśayannāha- koṣṭhādityādi| upasnehavaditi navaghaṭādyupasneho [2] yathā'ṇutamasrotobhirbahiḥsravad [3] dṛśyate tadvat koṣṭhādannasāro'nnaraso niḥsṛtya, śanaiḥ krameṇa, jaṭharamudarākhyaṃ rogaṃ karotīti sambandhaḥ| koṣṭhaśabdenātrāhārapākādhāra ucyate| kimbhūto'nnasāraḥ? duṣṭaḥ| punaḥ kimbhūtaḥ? anilaveganunno vātavegapreritaḥ| kiṃ kṛtvā? tvacaḥ samunnamya ānaddhāḥ kṛtvā| punaḥ kimbhūto rasaḥ? samantādvivardhamānaḥ samantāt sarvato vṛddhiṃ gacchan||6||-

sādhāraṇāṃ samprāptiṃ darśayannāha- koṣṭhādityādi| annasāro rasaḥ| koṣṭhānniḥsṛtyeti koṣṭhaśabdenāhārapākādhāro rasadoṣamūtrapurīṣavibhāgāśrayo grahaṇyabhidhāna ucyate, tatraiva sārākhyarasavibhāgāt| kathaṃ punaretat koṣṭhādantrāṇāṃ randhrābhāvādannarasasya niḥsaraṇamityāha- upasnehavat| nūtanaghaṭādupasneho yathā'ṇubhirbahiḥsrotobhirbahiḥ- sravad dṛśyate tadvat| yadyevaṃ nityamevodarāṇāṃ samprāptiḥ prāpnoti, upasnehena satatamevānnarasasya bahirnirgamanāt; naitadasti, udarārambhakadoṣadūṣitaśarīrasya rasasvedāmbuvahānāṃ srotasāṃ duṣṭeḥ, duṣṭiścaiteṣāṃ saṃvṛttamukhatvena vinivṛttamukhatvena ca; taduktaṃ carake- “svedavāhiṣu duṣṭeṣu pāruṣyaṃ lomaharṣaṇam| atisvedo na vā svedo paridāhaśca jāyate” iti| yadyevamata eva sroto'varodhānniḥsaraṇamavāpnoti| yathā''ha carakaḥ- “ruddhā svedāmbuvāhīni doṣāḥ srotāṃsi sañcitāḥ| prāṇāgnyapānān sandūṣya janayantyudaraṃ nṛṇām” (ca. ci. 13) iti| svedāmbuvāhisroto'varodhe punarambhaḥ| yadāha carakaḥ- “svedastu bāhyeṣu srotaḥsu pratihatagatistiryagavatiṣṭhamānastadevodakamāpyāyayati” (ca. ci. 13) iti| ata eva sarvāṅgakārśyamudariṇāmudarasya punaḥ pūrṇataiva| tasya dūṣyasya doṣān vinā vikārakāritā nāstītyāha- anilaveganunnastvacaḥ samunnamyeti| vṛddherayaṃ hetuḥ| sravan samantādannasāro'bhivardhamāno jaṭharamudaraṃ karoti| taduktaṃ carake- “mande'gnau malinairbhuktairapākāddoṣasañcayaḥ” (ca. ci. 13) ityādi||6||-

tatpūrvarūpaṃ balavarṇakāṅkṣāvalīvināśo jaṭhare hi rājyaḥ ||7||
jīrṇāparijñānavidāhavatyo bastau rujaḥ pādagataśca śophaḥ |8|

pūrvarūpamāha- tadityādi| vināśaśabdo balādibhiḥ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| kāṅkṣā annābhilāṣaḥ| valī tvaksaṅkocaḥ| rājyo rekhāḥ ‘bhavanti’ iti śeṣaḥ| jīrṇāparijñānavidāhavatyo bastau ruja iti jīrṇāparijñānavidāhau vidyete yāsu bastirujāsu tāstathā| jīrṇāparijñānamannapariṇāmāniścayaḥ; jīrṇāparijñānaṃ tu yathāsambhavatvāt puruṣasyaiva jñeyam||7||-

saṅgṛhya pārśvodarapṛṣṭhanābhīryadvardhate kṛṣṇasirāvanaddham ||8||
saśūlamānāhavadugraśabdaṃ satodabhedaṃ pavanātmakaṃ tat |9|

vātodaramāha- saṅgṛhyetyādi||8||-

yaccoṣatṛṣṇājvaradāhayuktaṃ pītaṃ sirā bhānti ca yatra pītāḥ ||9||
pītākṣiviṇmūtranakhānanasya pittodaraṃ tattvacirābhivṛddhi |10|

pittodaramāha- yadityādi| acirābhivṛddhi śīghravardhanaśīlam||9||-

yacchītalaṃ śuklasirāvanaddhaṃ guru sthiraṃ śuklanakhānanasya ||10||
snigdhaṃ mahacchophayutaṃ sasādaṃ kaphodaraṃ tattu cirābhivṛddhi |11|

śleṣmodaramāha- yacchītalamityādi| sthiraṃ kaṭhinam| sasādamaṅgaglāniyuktam||10||-

striyo'nnapānaṃ nakharomamūtraviḍārtavairyuktamasādhuvṛttāḥ ||11||
yasmai prayacchantyarayo garāṃśca duṣṭāmbudūṣīviṣasevanādvā |
tenāśu raktaṃ kupitāśca doṣāḥ kurvanti ghoraṃ jaṭharaṃ triliṅgam ||12||
tacchītavātābhrasamudbhaveṣu [4] viśeṣataḥ kupyati dahyate ca sa cāturo mūrcchati samprasaktaṃ pāṇḍuḥ kṛśaḥ śuṣyati tṛṣṇayā ca ||13||
prakīrtitaṃ [5] dūṣyudaraṃ tu ghoraṃ ... |14|

sannipātodaramāha- striya ityādi| striyo'nnapānaṃ nakharomamūtraviḍārtavayuktaṃ yasmai puruṣāya prayacchanti dadati| kimbhūtāḥ striyaḥ? asādhuvṛttā durācārāḥ| strīgrahaṇamatropalakṣaṇaṃ, tenānye'pi sannihitā avivekino grāhyāḥ| arayaḥ śatravaḥ| garān kṛtrimaviṣāṇi yasmai prayacchanti| duṣṭāmbu saviṣamatsyādisambandhāt| viṣameva dāvāgnivātātapādyabhibhavena mandatāṃ gataṃ viṣapītasya vā viṣaṃ dūṣīviṣaṃ; tena nakharomādinā viṣakalpena gareṇa dūṣīviṣeṇa vā''śu śīghraṃ raktaṃ kupitamiti boddhavyam| triliṅgaṃ vātapittaśleṣmaliṅgam| samprasaktaṃ nirantaram| dūṣyudaramiti yadeva sānnipātikodaraṃ tadeva dūṣyudaram, etena na saṅkhyātirekaḥ| ghoraṃ bhayānakaṃ, punarghoraṃ kaṣṭakāri||11-13||-

duṣṭāmbudūṣīviṣasevanādveti dūṣīviṣaṃ viṣameva dāvāgnivātātapādyabhibhavena mandatāṃ gatam| tenāśu raktaṃ kupitaṃ viṣasya raktadūṣaṇasvabhāvatvāt, āśukāritvācca| tat śītādiṣu viśeṣeṇa kupyati, dūṣīviṣakopasya tatraiva darśitatvāt| mūrcchatīti viṣasya mūrcchākaratvāt, prasaktaṃ nirantaraṃ mūrcchati| pāṇḍuśca bhavati, raktadoṣāt| dūṣyodarametadeva sānnipātikodaraṃ yadeva tadeva dūṣyodaram| etena na saṅkhyātirekaḥ| dūṣyantīti dūṣyāḥ, “kṛtyalyuṭo bahulam” (pā. a. 3/3/13) iti kapratyayaḥ; tasyodaraṃ, tena kṛtatvāt| atra bhojaḥ- “nakhalomārtavairyuktamannapānaṃ yadā striyaḥ| vaśīkartuṃ prayacchanti tadviṣatvāya kalpate|| dūṣīviṣaṃ sevitaṃ vā tasya raktaṃ prakopayet| raktaṃ viṣeṇa sanduṣṭaṃ trīn doṣān tattu kopayet” iti||7-13||-

... plīhodaraṃ kīrtayato nibodha |
vidāhyabhiṣyandiratasya jantoḥ praduṣṭamatyarthamasṛk kaphaśca ||14||
plīhābhivṛddhiṃ satataṃ karoti plīhodaraṃ tat pravadanti tajjñāḥ |
vāme ca pārśve parivṛddhimeti viśeṣataḥ sīdati cāturo'tra ||15||
mandajvarāgniḥ kaphapittaliṅgairupadrutaḥ kṣīṇabalo'tipāṇḍuḥ |16|

nibodha jānīhi| abhiṣyandi doṣadhātumalasrotasāṃ kledaprāptijanakam| asṛk kaphaśceti asṛggrahaṇena pittasyāpi grahaṇam| plīhā udarasya vāmapārśvasthitaḥ| eti gacchati| sīdati aṅgaglāniyukto bhavati| atra plīhodare||14-15||-

plīhodare vidāhiratatvena raktapittaṃ, tasya pittasya liṅgaṃ mando jvaraḥ; abhiṣyandiratatvena kaphaḥ, talliṅgaṃ mandāgnitvam||14-15||-

savyetarasmin yakṛti praduṣṭe jñeyaṃ yakṛddālyudaraṃ tadeva ||16||

yakṛddālyudarasya plīhodare evāvarodhaṃ darśayannāha- savyetarasminnityādi| savyetarasmin dakṣiṇapārśve| tadeva plīhodaraṃ yakṛddālyudaraṃ jñeyam| kva jñeyamityāha- yakṛti kālakhaṇḍe; kimbhūte? praduṣṭe; etena na saṅkhyātirekaḥ||16||

yakṛddālyudarasya plīhodara evāvarodhaṃ darśayannāha- savyetare ityādi| svyetare dakṣiṇe| tadeva plīhodarameva||16||

yasyāntramannairupalepibhirvā bālāśmabhirvā sahitaiḥ [6] pṛthagvā |
sañcīyate tatra malaḥ sadoṣaḥ krameṇa nāḍyāmiva saṅkaro hi ||17||
nirudhyate cāsya gude purīṣaṃ nireti kṛcchrādapi cālpamalpam |
hṛnnābhimadhye parivṛddhimeti ta(ya)ccodaraṃ viṭsamagandhikaṃ ca ||18||
pracchardayan baddhagudī vibhāvyaḥ, ... |19|

baddhagudaṃ darśayannāha- yasyetyādi| yasya puruṣasya antraṃ nirudhyata iti sambandhaḥ| kairnirudhyata ityāha- annairityādi| upalepibhiḥ picchilādibhiḥ| vā atra samuccaye| sahitairiti ekatra militaiḥ samuditairityarthaḥ| pṛthagveti ekaikairityarthaḥ| malaḥ purīṣam| sadoṣo doṣatrayasahitaḥ| nāḍyāṃ praṇālikāyām| saṅkaro'vakaraḥ, pracuratṛṇādisamūha ityarthaḥ| nireti niḥsarati| baddhagudī vibhāvyaḥ sa puruṣo baddhagudī jñeyaḥ||17-18||-

baddhagudaṃ darśayannāha- yasyāntramannairityādi| upalepibhiḥ picchilādibhiḥ| pihitaṃ yathāvat sakalamityarthaḥ| malaḥ purīṣam| sadoṣaḥ saha doṣairvātādirvartata iti| purīṣamantre krameṇa cīyate saṅkaravat, doṣaireva miśrīkṛtatvāt| taduktaṃ carake- “varcaḥpittakaphān ruddhvā janayatyudaraṃ tataḥ” (ca. ci. 13) iti| atra pā(va)kasthāne antrasya purīṣāyatanasya ruddhatvāt bahu(ddha)gudasambandhamantraṃ gudamucyate| taduktaṃ carake- “pakṣmabālaiḥ sahānnena bhuktairbaddhāyane gude” iti (ca. ci. 13)||17-18||-

...tataḥ parisrāvyudaraṃ nibodha |
śalyaṃ yadannopahitaṃ tadantraṃ bhinatti yasyāgatamanyathā vā ||19||
tasmāt sruto'ntrāt salilaprakāśaḥ srāvaḥ sravedvai gudatastu bhūyaḥ |
nābheradhaścodarameti vṛddhiṃ nistudyate'tīva vidahyate ca ||20||
etat parisrāvyudaraṃ pradiṣṭaṃ... |21|

parisrāvyudaramāha- tata ityādi| yacchalyamannopahitamannasambaddhaṃ bhuktaṃ, tadantraṃ bhinatti vidārayati| yasya puruṣasya| śalyaṃ śarkarākāṣṭhāsthikaṇṭakādikam| āgatamanyathā veti vāśabdo'vadhāraṇe, tenānyathaiva vilomena tiryagevāgataṃ, natvanulomena| sruto galitaḥ||19-20||-

kṣatāntraṃ nirdiśannāha- śalyaṃ yadannopahitamityādi| śalyaṃ śarkarātṛṇakāṣṭhāsthikaṇṭakādi| annopahitaṃ bhuktaṃ pakvāśayādanyathā vilomenāgataṃ, tadantraṃ bhinatti vidārayati| antrāt srutaḥ salilaprakāśaḥ srāvo bhūyo bahuśca, doṣajeṣu punarupasnehasrāveṇālpa eva gudataśca sravate| antrasrāvasya bahirgatatvāt kathaṃ gudataśca srāvaḥ? ucyate- salilabāhulyena tenaiva chidreṇa salilasya pratyāgatvādupasnehādgudasrāvaḥ; taduktaṃ carake- “pūrayan gudamantraṃ ca janayatyudaraṃ tataḥ” (ca. ci. 13) iti| tatastasmāt srāvānnābheradho jaṭharaṃ vṛddhimeti, jalasyādhogāmitvāt| chidrodarajalodarayoradūradoṣatrayavyapadeśaḥ| tathā''ha chidrodare eva carakaḥ- “tadadhonābheḥ prāyo'bhinirvartamānaṃ jalodaraṃ syād, yathābalaṃ ca doṣāṇāṃ rūpāṇi darśayati” (ca. ci. 13) iti| chidrodarajalodarayoradūradoṣatrayayorapi todādivedanā vāyunā mārgāvaraṇādikupitena||19-20||-

... dakodaraṃ kīrtayato nibodha |
yaḥ snehapīto'pyanuvāsito vā vānto virikto'pyathavā nirūḍhaḥ ||21||
pibejjalaṃ śītalamāśu tasya srotāṃsi duṣyanti hi tadvahāni |
snehopalipteṣvathavā'pi teṣu dakodaraṃ pūrvavadabhyupaiti ||22||
snigdhaṃ mahat samparivṛttanābhi bhṛśonnataṃ pūrṇamivāmbunā ca |
yathā dṛtiḥ kṣubhyati kampate ca śabdāyate cāpi dakodaraṃ [7] tat ||23||

udakodaramāha- dakodaramityādi| dakodaram udakodaramityarthaḥ| tadvahāni toyavahāni| snehopalipteṣvathavā'pi teṣviti snehādipānaṃ vinā'pi teṣu srotaḥsu snehopalipteṣu yadā śītalaṃ jalaṃ pibettadā'pītyarthaḥ| pūrvavaditi kṣatāntrodare yathā salilasyādhogāmitvādadho nābherudarotpattiryathā ca gudādāsrāvastadvaditi; jejjaṭena punaḥ ‘pūrvavadityatrānyānyudarāṇi yathā bhavanti tathedamapi’ iti vyākhyātaṃ, tannecchati gayī| parivṛttanābhi| ucchalitanābhi| dṛtiḥ khallā| kṣubhyati calati| kampate ceti kampanamatiśayena calanam||21-23||

dakodaraṃ darśayannāha- yaḥ snehapīta ityādi| yaḥ śītalamāśu jalaṃ pibet tasya snehapānānuvāsino'ntaḥsnehāvaliptasrotaso'tiśayavatī srotoduṣṭiḥ syāt; vāntaviriktanirūḍhāṇāmatiriktabhāvena vivṛtasrotasāṃ pūrvakarmapradhānakarmamandāgnīnāmatyambusevayā salilavahasrotasāṃ klomamūlānāṃ duṣṭirabhyupeyāt| dakodare eva samprāptivikalpaṃ darśayannāha- snehopalipteṣvityādi| athavā vinā'pi snehādipānaṃ śītalasalilasevākṛtāmudarasrotoduṣṭiḥ, snehopalipteṣvathavā'pi teṣu srotaḥsu dakodaramabhyupeti| pūrvavaditi pūrvavat kṣatāntrodare yathā salilasyādhogāmitvādadhonābherudarābhinirvṛttiḥ| taduktaṃ jalodare eva carake- “tasya rūpāṇi- anne'nākāṅkṣā pipāsā gudasrāvaḥ” ityādi| srotasāṃ duṣṭatvena bahirūpasnehena jalaniḥsaraṇaṃ sarveṣveva samam| pūrvavacchabdasyānya evārtho na punarvyākhyātaḥ||21-23||

ādhmānaṃ gamane'śaktirdaurbalyaṃ durbalāgnitā |
śophaḥ sadanamaṅgānāṃ saṅgo vātapurīṣayoḥ |
dāhastṛṣṇā ca sarveṣu jaṭhareṣu bhavanti hi ||24||

teṣāmaṣṭānāmapi sāmānyaṃ liṅgaṃ darśayannāha- ādhmānamityādi| jejjaṭena punarete upadravā vyākhyātāḥ; tannecchati gayī||24||

teṣāmaṣṭānāmapi sāmānyaliṅgaṃ darśayannāha- ādhmānamityādi| jaḍena (jejjaṭena) upadravā ete vyākhyātāḥ; tanna, carake vyādhīnāmapi rogasambhavānāmupadravatvena niṣiddhatvāt||24||

ante salilabhāvaṃ hi bhajante jaṭharāṇi tu |
sarvāṇyeva parīpākāttadā tāni [8] vivarjayet ||25||

sādhyāsādhyavibhāgāyāha- ante salilabhāvamityādi| paripākāt parimāṇena jalodaratā sarveṣāmeva| upasnehena niḥsṛtāntra(nna)rasātivṛddhena| sarveṣāmeva upasnehena niḥsṛtāntrarasamūlatvāttadeva kathamanudakatvaṃ? naiṣa doṣaḥ, tatra hyupekṣayā nātimandatvādagneḥ svalpatvāt prāgudarasyānudakatvamityucyate, alpatve'tadvyapadeśāt| tasya ca lakṣaṇaṃ carakeṇoktam- “aśothamaruṇābhāsaṃ saśabdaṃ nātibhārikam| sadā guḍaguḍāvacca sirājālagavākṣitam| nābhiṃ viṣṭabhya pāyau tu vegaṃ kṛtvā praṇaśyati| hṛdvaṅkṣaṇakaṭīnābhigudapratyekaśūlinaḥ|| karkaśaṃ sṛjato vātaṃ nāti mande ca pāvake| lolasyāvirase cāsye mūtre'lpe saṃhate viṣi| ajātodakamityetairyuktaṃ vijñāya lakṣaṇaiḥ| upācaret”- (ca. ci. 13) ityādi| ataḥ paramupasnehavanniḥsṛto'nnaraso nātyacchapicchilākāreṇa sambhavatyudare, sā punaravasthā pacyamānānāmudarāṇām| taduktaṃ carake- “tatra picchotpattau maṇḍalamudaraṃ guru stimitaṃ bhṛśamātatamākoṭitamaśabdaṃ mṛdusparśamapagatarājīkamākrāntaṃ nābhyāṃ parisarpati” (ca. ci. 13) iti| ataḥ paraṃ jātodakamudaraṃ; tasya lakṣaṇamudakodaranirdiṣṭaṃ “snigdhaṃ mahatsamparivṛtanābhi bhṛśonnataṃ pūrṇamivāmbunā ca” iti| carake'pi- “tasya rūpāṇi kukṣeratimātraṃ vṛddhiḥ sirāntardhānagamanamudakapūrṇadṛtisamasaṅkṣobhasparśaṃ ca bhavati| tathāvidhamacikitsyaṃ vidyāt (ca. ci. 13) iti||25||
