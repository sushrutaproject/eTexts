A reading edition of the Suśrutasaṃhitā, in IAST

The Suśrutasaṃhitā, a Sanskrit treatise on medicine and surgery, is organized into six sthānas (sections). It is read here alongside Ḍalhaṇa's twelfth-century commentary, the Nibandhasaṅgraha, with the two distinguished typographically. Choose a sthāna below to browse its adhyāyas (chapters).

The text below is based on the 1938 edition of the Suśrutasaṃhitā edited by Yādavaśarman Trivikrama Ācārya (see online scan at https://sushrutaproject1.github.io/Sushrutasamhita_Saktumiva2/06-su.ut-27-37-kumara/06-ut-vulgate-1938-27-37.xml?facs=1).
