--- title: "11. kṣārapākavidhyadhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 11 order: 11 ---

athātaḥ kṣārapākavidhimadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi| kṣāraśabdaḥ pākavidhibhyāṃ saha sambadhyate||1-2||

śastrānuśastrebhyaḥ kṣāraḥ pradhānatamaḥ, chedyabhedyalekhyakaraṇāt tridoṣaghnatvādviśeṣakriyāvacāraṇācca [2] ||3||

śastrānuśastrebhya iti śastrāṇi maṇḍalāgrādīni, anuśastrāṇi tvaksārādīni, tebhyaḥ kṣāraḥ pradhānatamaḥ, dve punaranuśastre [3] agnijalaukākhye pariśiṣṭe; taduktaṃ- ‘vidhiḥ pravakṣyate paścādagnikṣārajalaukasām’(sū. a. 8)’- iti; eṣāṃ trayāṇāmapi prādhānyamasti, ata evaiṣāṃ pṛthagadhyāyaḥ; atra jalaukasaḥ pradhānam, agniḥ pradhānataraḥ, kṣāraḥ pradhānatamaḥ; ‘kṣārādagnirgarīyān’ (sū. a. 12) ityatra yathā na virodhastathā tatraiva vakṣyāmaḥ| kasmāt kṣārasya prādhānyamityata āha- chedyetyādi| chedyādīni karmāṇyagnijalaukasorna sambhavanti, tena tābhyāṃ sakāśāt kṣārasya prādhānyaṃ, tridoṣaghnatvaṃ kṣārasya nānauṣadhisamavāyādityādinā sādhayiṣyati, viśeṣakriyāvacāraṇaṃ yathā pittārśaḥsu viśeṣeṇāvacāraṇam [4] ||3||

tatra kṣaraṇāt kṣaṇanādvā kṣāraḥ ||4||

kṣārasya niruktiṃ kurvannāha- tatra kṣaraṇādityādi| kṣaraṇād duṣṭatvaṅmāṃsādicālanāt, śātanādityarthaḥ; anye tu kṣaraṇāddoṣāṇāṃ cālanāt| kṣaṇanāt tvaṅmāṃsādihiṃsanāt| ‘kṣaraṇāt’ ityatra ‘kṣapaṇāt’ iti kecit paṭhanti; kṣapaṇaṃ śodhanamabhipretam||4||

nānauṣadhisamavāyāttridoṣaghnaḥ [5] , śuklatvāt [6] saumyaḥ; tasya saumyasyāpi sato dahanapacanadāraṇādiśaktiraviruddhā, āgneyauṣadhiguṇabhūyiṣṭhatvāt [7] kaṭuka uṣṇastīkṣṇaḥ pācano vilayanaḥ śodhano ropaṇaḥ śoṣaṇaḥ stambhano [8] lekhanaḥ kṛmyāmakaphakuṣṭhaviṣamedasāmupahantā puṃstvasya cātisevitaḥ ||5||

kṣāraguṇakarmāha- nānauṣadhisamavāyādityādi| samavāyaḥ saṃyogaḥ; yato'yaṃ kṣāro'nekauṣadhisamavāyāttridoṣaghnaḥ, ataḥ pittārśaḥsvapi yaugikatvamasya| śuklatvāt saumya ityasyāgre kecidapiśabdaṃ luptanirdiṣṭaṃ varṇayanti, vyākhyānayanti ca- na kevalamuṣṇaḥ saumyo'pīti, tenāsya vakṣyamāṇamuṣṇatvamapi na virudhyate| dahanapacanadāraṇaśaktiraviruddhā himānīvat| pācana iti dvividho'pi kṣāraḥ; atra vraṇaśothasya pratisāraṇīyaḥ pācanaḥ, annājīrṇasya pānīyaḥ| vilayanaḥ śophasya vātakaphabhūyiṣṭasya| śodhano duṣṭavraṇasya, ropaṇaḥ śuddhavraṇasya, atra yogaśaktiprabhāvāttilayavakalkavat kṣārasya śodhanaropaṇatvaṃ na
virudhyate; śodhana evāyaṃ kṣāraḥ, śuddhastu vraṇaḥ svayameva rohatītyanye; na hi śuddhe vraṇe ropaṇārthaṃ kṣāro'vacāryate sirādikṣaṇanabhayāt, tasmāt pāramparyeṇa ropaṇa iti; kecidasya ropaṇatvaṃ na paṭhantyeva| śoṣaṇo vraṇakledasya| stambhanaḥ śoṇitātipravṛtteḥ| upahantṛśabdaḥ kṛmyādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; kṛmayo bāhyābhyantarāḥ, teṣāṃ dvividhaḥ kṣāra upahantā; āmaḥ apakvarasaḥ, medasaḥ pānīya evopahantā; puṃstvasya śukrasya||5||

sa dvividhaḥ- pratisāraṇīyaḥ, pānīyaśca ||6||

idānīmuktaguṇakarmaṇaḥ kṣārasya dvaividhyaṃ darśayannāha- sa dvividha ityādi| pratisāraṇīyaḥ pratisāraṇārhaḥ; pānīyaḥ pānārhaḥ, kṣārodakamityarthaḥ; ye cānye'pi pākyasarjikāyavakṣārā yathārogaṃ vaktavyāsteṣāṃ cūrṇavaṭakāvalehādiṣu prayogaḥ kartavyaḥ||6||

tatra pratisāraṇīyaḥ kuṣṭhakiṭibhadadrumaṇḍalakilāsabhagandarārbudārśoduṣṭavraṇanāḍīcarmakīla- tilakālakanyacchavyaṅgamaśakabāhyavidradhikṛmiviṣādiṣūpadiśyate [9] , saptasu ca mukharogeṣūpajihvādhijihvopakuśadantavaidarbheṣu tisṛṣu ca rohiṇīṣu, eteṣvevānuśastrapraṇidhānamuktam [10] ||7||

pratisāraṇīyasya viṣayaṃ darśayannāha- tatra pratisāraṇīya ityādi| kiṭibhaḥ kuṣṭhaviśeṣaḥ; tasya pṛthagavidhānaṃ niyamena pratisāraṇīyasādhyatvajñāpanārthaṃ; dadru maṇḍalaṃ dadrumaṇḍalaṃ; kilāsaṃ tvaggataṃ śvitrakuṣṭhaṃ; carmakīlaḥ arśonidānapaṭhitaḥ; nyacchavyaṅgau kṣudraroganidānoktau; bāhyaśabdaḥ vidradhikṛmiviṣaiḥ [11] pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| viṣādiṣvityādiśabdādvalmīkādīnāṃ grahaṇam| ‘bāhyakṛmiviśeṣādiṣu’ iti kecit paṭhanti| ke te sapta mukharogā yeṣu kṣāra upayujyate, ityāha- upajihvetyādi|- upajihvādīn rogān mukharoganidāne vakṣyati| tisṛṇāṃ rohiṇīnāṃ grahaṇaṃ sannipātaraktajayorasādhyatvajñāpanārtham| kutaḥ punarupajihvādibhyo'nyeṣu mukharogeṣu kṣāro nopayujyata ityāha- eteṣvevetyādi| atrānuśastraṃ kṣāra eva| ‘eteṣvanuśastranipātanam’ iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ||7||

pānīyastu garagulmodarāgnisaṅgājīrṇārocakānāha- śarkarāśmaryābhyantaravidradhikṛmiviṣārśaḥsūpayujyate [12] ||8||

pānīyaviṣayaṃ darśayannāha- pānīyastvityādi| garaḥ kṛtrimaṃ viṣaṃ, dūṣīviṣamityanye; agnisaṅgaḥ iti agniḥ sajyate līyate yasmādyena yasmin vā so'gnisaṅgaḥ, tena vātaślaiṣmikagrahaṇīvisūcikālasakavilambikādivikārā gṛhyante; śarkarā vātena bhinnā'śmarī śarkaretyucyate||8||

ahitastu raktapittajvaritapittaprakṛtibālavṛddhadurbalabhrama- madamūrcchātimiraparītebhyo'nyebhyaścaivaṃvidhebhyaḥ ||9||

kṣārasya yeṣvayaugikatvaṃ tānāha- ahitastvityādi| bhramaścakrārūḍhasyeva bhramaṇaṃ, mado viṣamadyaśoṇitottho vikāraḥ pūgaphaleneva mattatā, mūrcchā cetanācyutiḥ, parītaśabdo bhramādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate||9||

taṃ cetarakṣāravaddagdhvā parisrāvayet; tasya vistaro'nyatra ||10||

tatra pānīyakṣārapākaṃ vidhiṃ ca saṅkṣepeṇa darśayitumāha- taṃ cetarakṣāravadityādi| itarakṣāravat pratisāraṇīyavat; yathā pratisāraṇīyasya kāṣṭhadāhaḥ ṣaḍguṇodakapākastribhāgāvaśeṣānantaraṃ parisrāvaṇaṃ ca, tadvat pānīyasyāpi pākavidhiḥ srāvaṇaṃ ca dvitīyapākavarjaṃ; pānīyakṣārodakapramāṇamuttamā [13] mātrā palaṃ, madhyamā karṣatrayam, adhamā ardhapalam| anyatra gulmādiṣvadhyāyeṣu||10||

athetarastrividho [14] mṛdurmadhyastīkṣṇaśca |
taṃ cikīrṣuḥ śaradi girisānujaṃ śucirupoṣya [15] praśaste'hani praśastadeśajātamanupahataṃ madhyamavayasaṃ mahāntamasitamuṣkakamadhivāsyāparedyuḥ [16] [17] [18] pāṭayitvā khaṇḍaśaḥ prakalpyāvapāṭya nivāte deśe nicitiṃ [19] kṛtvā sudhāśarkarāśca prakṣipya tilanālairādīpayet |
athopaśānte'gnau tadbhasma pṛthaggṛhṇīyādbhasmaśarkarāśca |
athānenaiva vidhānena [20] kuṭajapalāśāśvakarṇapāribhadrakabibhītakāragvadhatilvakārkasnuhyapāmārgapāṭalānaktamālavṛṣakadalī- citrakapūtīkendravṛkṣāsphotāśvamārakasaptacchadāgnimanthaguñjāścatasraśca kośātakīḥ samūlaphalapatraśākhā dahet |
tataḥ kṣāradroṇamudakadroṇaiḥ ṣaḍbhirāloḍya mūtrairvā yathoktairekaviṃśatikṛtvaḥ parisrāvya, mahati kaṭāhe śanairdarvyā'vaghaṭṭayan vipacet |
sa yadā bhavatyaccho raktastīkṣṇaḥ picchilaśca tamādāya mahati vastre parisrāvyetaraṃ vibhajya punaragnāvadhiśrayet |
tata eva kṣārodakāt kuḍavamadhyardhaṃ vā'panayet |
tataḥ kaṭaśarkarābhasmaśarkarākṣīrapākaśaṅkhanābhīragnivarṇāḥ kṛtvā''yase pātre tasminneva kṣārodake niṣicya piṣṭvā tenaiva dvidroṇe'ṣṭapalasammitaṃ śaṅkhanābhyādīnāṃ pramāṇaṃ prativāpya [21] , satatamapramattaścainamavaghaṭṭayan vipacet |
sa yathā nātisāndro nātidravaśca bhavati tathā prayateta |
athainamāgatapākamavatāryānuguptamāyase [22] kumbhe saṃvṛtamukhe nidadhyādeṣa madhyamaḥ ||11||
eṣa caivāpratīvāpaḥ [23] pakvaḥ saṃvyūhimo mṛduḥ ||12||

athetara ityādi| itaraḥ pratisāraṇīyaḥ; athetaro mṛdurityādyatra kecinna paṭhanti| pratisāraṇīyasya pākavidhiṃ nirdeṣṭumāha- taṃ cikīrṣurityādi| taṃ pratisāraṇīyaṃ, cikīrṣuḥ kartumicchuḥ; taṃ cikīrṣurityatra ‘athetaraṃ cikīrṣuḥ’ iti kecit paṭhanti; athaśabdo maṅgalārthaḥ, itaraṃ pratisāraṇīyam| girisānujaṃ parvatoparijātaṃ, “girisānujamiti jāṅgaladeśopalakṣaṇam” ityanye| ‘śucirupoṣya’ ityatra ‘śucirupavasan’ iti kecit paṭhanti| anupahataṃ kṛmidavāgnigarādyanupahatam| asitamuṣkakaṃ [24] kṛṣṇapuṣpamokṣakaṃ, na śvetādipuṣpam| adhivāsya mantravadbalikarmopaharedityarthaḥ| kecidasyāgre “mantreṇānenābhimantrya- agnivīrya! mahāvīrya! mā te vīryaṃ praṇaśyatu| ihaiva tiṣṭha kalyāṇa! mama kāryaṃ kariṣyasi|| mama kārye kṛte paścāddevalokaṃ gamiṣyasi| śvetaraktapuṣpasahasraṃ juhuyāt” iti paṭhanti; ayaṃ ca nibandheṣu na paṭhyate| anye tvatra “prāṅmukhopagatastatra baliṃ dattvā caturdiśam| vṛkṣābhyāśe tato brūyāt prāñjaliḥ prayataḥ śuciḥ|| nivasantīha bhūtāni yānyasmin kāniciddrume| apakrāmantvayaṃ chedyaḥ parārthe [25] śvo mayā drumaḥ| evamāmantritaṃ vṛkṣaṃ lājādhūpaiḥ surāsavaiḥ| prabhāte vaikṛtaṃ tatra na paśyecchedayettataḥ| śānte nīhāratimire yugamātrodite ravau| pūrvāgramuttarāgrasthaṃ taṃ taruṃ pāta(ṭa)yeddrutam” “ityadhivāsanamantraṃ paṭhanti, bhoje'pyevameva [26] paṭhyate” iti ca vadanti| aparedyuraparasmin dine| khaṇḍaśaḥ prakalpya bahūn khaṇḍān kṛtvā| avapāṭya tān khaṇḍān punaḥ pāṭayitvā; ‘aparedyuḥ pāṭayitvā ityasya sthāne ‘prabhāte vaikṛtamadbhutaṃ na paśyettadopakalpayet’ iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ| vaikṛtaṃ taruśākhābhaṅgādi, adbhutaṃ rudhiravarṣādi, iti ca tasya vyākhyānam| nicitiḥ kāṣṭhānāmauttarādharyeṇāvasthitiḥ; kecidatra ‘citiṃ kṛtvā’ iti paṭhanti, citiṃ dāhasthānamiti vyākhyānayanti| tilanālairādīpayet tilanāladīpanācchaktyatiśayo bhavati| nicitiṃ kṛtvetyasyāgre kecit ‘sudhāśarkarāḥ prakṣipya’ iti paṭhanti, vyākhyānayanti ca- sudhāśarkarāḥ sudhāpāṣāṇāḥ| bhasmaśarkarāśceti [27] dahyamānārdrakāṣṭharasāccharkarevānyonyasaṃhataṃ bhasmajātaṃ bhasmaśarkaretyucyate, tāṃ ca pṛthak gṛhṇīyāt; pṛthaggrahaṇaṃ bhinnaprayojanārthaṃ; tena bhasmanaḥ kṣārodakakaraṇaṃ, bhasmaśarkarā punaḥ kṣāradravyasamavāpinī| anye tu ‘pṛthak gṛhṇīyāt’ ityasyāgre ‘kaṭaśarkarā bhasmaśarkarāśca’ iti paṭhanti, ‘kaṭaśarkarā adagdhasudhāpāṣāṇāḥ, bhasmaśarkarāstu kṣāradravyaiḥ saha dagdhāścūrṇapāṣāṇāḥ’ iti ca vyākhyānayanti| eṣa dāhakalpaḥ| athānenaivetyādi| athānenaiva vidhānenetyanena nivātadeśanicitistilanāladīpanaṃ ca labhyate, girisānujamityasya jāṅgaladeśopalakṣaṇatvaṃ, tenehāpi kuṭajādīnyapi jāṅgalāni gṛhyante| muṣkakasya kuṭajādīnāṃ caikadeśakalpanatve'pi pṛthagdāhavidhānaṃ kuṭajādibhasmano muṣkakabhasmano bhāgādhikyakhyāpanārthaṃ, bhāgādhikyaṃ ca ‘tataḥ kṣāradroṇamudakadroṇaiḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ’- ityatra vakṣyāmaḥ| anye tu muṣkakeṇaikenāpi kṣāro bhavatīti pṛthagvidhānaṃ bodhayati| palāśaḥ kiṃśukaḥ; aśvakarṇaḥ pūrvadeśe gandhamuṇḍaḥ pippalasadṛśaḥ [28] , pāribhadrako devadāruḥ, anye tu raktakusumaḥ kaṇṭakīvṛkṣaviśeṣaḥ svanāmnā prasiddhaḥ, tilvakaḥ svanāmaprasiddhaḥ; indravṛkṣaḥ kuṭaja eva, dvividho hi kuṭajo bṛhatphalaḥ śvetapuṣpaḥ snigdhapatraḥ pumān, aṇuphalavṛntā śyāvāruṇapuṣpā strī; āsphotā sārivā; kośātakī ghoṣakaḥ; sā caturvidhā- bṛhatphalā, alpaphalā, pītapuṣpā, śvetapuṣpā, iti| samūlaphalapatraśākhā iti kuṭajādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| kecit kuṭajādikośātakyantaṃ samasya paṭhanti| tataḥ kṣāradroṇamityādi tato muṣkakakuṭajādidāhānantaram| kṣāradroṇamiti muṣkakabhasmano dvau bhāgau kuṭajādibhasmana eko bhāga iti miśrīkṛtaṃ kṣāradroṇamucyate| udakadroṇaiḥ ṣaḍbhiriti bhasmadravyāt ṣaḍguṇodakaparibhāṣeyam, anayaivānyatrāpi kṣārapākavidhau kṣārakalpanaṃ; pakṣāntaraṃ darśayati- mūtrairvā yathoktairiti dundubhisvanīyakalpoktairityarthaḥ [29] ; tathāhi- ‘gavāṃ mūtreṇa kṣārakalpena parisrāvya vipacet’ (ka. a. 6)- iti; atrāpi kṣārakalpeneti vacanādbhasmadravyānmūtrasya ṣaḍguṇatvamuktam| ekaviṃśatikṛtvaḥ parisrāvyeti ekaviṃśativārān vastreṇa gālayitvā| avaghaṭṭayan cālayan, tāvat pacet sa yadā bhavatyaccho raktastīkṣṇaḥ picchilaśceti| ete'cchādayo guṇāstribhāgāvaśeṣe kṣārodake bhavanti| taṃ kṣārakvātham| ādāya gṛhītvā| itaraṃ vibhajya kṣārabhasmakalkaṃ; ‘itaradvisṛjya’ iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ, tatretarat bhasma kiṭṭabhūtam| adhiśrayet āropayet| tata eva kṣārodakāt kuḍavamadhyardhaṃ vā'panayediti kuḍavamaṣṭau palānyapanayet; adhyardhamityatra kuḍavamiti sambadhyate, tenādhyardhaṃ kuḍavaṃ dvādaśapalāni vā'panayet| anye tvatra dvaiguṇyaṃ na manyante| etacca kṣārodakāpanayanaṃ kaṭaśarkarādīnāṃ niṣekārthaṃ peṣaṇārthaṃ ca; anye tu kaṭaśarkarādīnāṃ bhāgapramāṇajñāpanārthamiti vadanti, tena kaṭaśarkarādīni dravyāṇi pratyekaṃ kuḍavapramāṇānyadhyardhakuḍavapramāṇāni vā āvapedityarthaḥ| tataḥ kaṭaśarkaretyādi kaṭaśarkarā gāṅgeṣṭhī, bhasmaśarkarā atraivādhyāye pūrvaṃ vyākhyātā; anye tu kaṭaśarkarābhasmaśarkarayoranyathā vyākhyānaṃ kurvanti tadapyatraivādhyāye kathitam| kṣīrapāko jalaśuktiḥ| āyase pātre lauhe bhājane, ete agnivarṇāḥ kṛtvā, tasminneva kṣārodake pūrvopanīte kuḍave'dhyardhakuḍave vā, niṣicya prakṣipya| traiṣāṃ [30] kaṭaśarkarādīnāmanirdiṣṭapramāṇatvāt samā bhāgā ityeke; samabhāgaścātra pratyekamaṣṭau palāni; evaṃ ca dvātriṃśat [31] palāni bhavanti; etena dvidroṇe'ṣṭapalasammitamityapi pramāṇamanukūlitaṃ bhavati; dvidroṇe ṣoḍaśāṃśaścāvāpabhāgo jñāpito bhavati| anye tvaṣṭapalasammitamiti kaṭaśarkarādīnāṃ pratyekaṃ dve dve pale ityevamaṣṭapalatvaṃ manyante| tanna, śaṅkhanābhipradhānatvādeva kṣārasya; evaṃ ca viśvāmitrabhojoktabhāgakalpanā nirastā; taduktabhāgagrahaṇe'pi śaṅkhanābheraprādhānyaṃ syāt| dvidroṇagrahaṇena tribhāgāvaśeṣaṃ kṣārodakaṃ sarvatra paribhāṣayanti vṛddhāḥ, prāyeṇa hi vimiśritaṃ kṣāradroṇamāhuḥ; udakadroṇaiḥ ṣaḍbhiriti ṣaṭtriṃśadadhikapañcadaśaśatodakapalaiḥ saha vipacedityarthaḥ; tatastasya pacyamānasya taikṣṇyāccharaktapicchilādayo guṇāḥ ṣoḍaśāṃśāvaśiṣṭe ṣaṇṇavatipalapramāṇe bhavanti| anye tu vadanti- na cātra caturbhāgādyavaśeṣaṇādiparibhāṣā vyāpriyate, tasmācchanaiḥ śanaistāvadvipacedyāvadrāgādiguṇayuktaḥ kvātho bhavati kṣārasañjñita iti| ṣaṇṇavatipalapramāṇe tu kṣārodake kaṭaśarkarādīnāṃ samabhāgānāṃ caturthāṃśamāvāpamicchanti [32] ||11-12||

pratīvāpe yathālābhaṃ dantīdravantīcitrakalāṅgalīpūtikapravālatālapatrīviḍasuvarcikākanakakṣīrīhiṅguvacātiviṣāḥ samāḥ ślakṣṇacūrṇāḥ śuktipramāṇāḥ pratīvāpaḥ |
sa eva sapratīvāpaḥ pakvaḥ pākyastīkṣṇaḥ ||13||

sapratīvāpaṃ pākyasañjñaṃ tīkṣṇaṃ pratipādayannāha- pratīvāpe yathālābhamityādi| pratīvāpe iti kṣipte āvāpe punaraparadravyaprakṣepaḥ pratīvāpaḥ| dravantī dantībhedaḥ, saṃvarīti bhādānake, lāṅgalī kalikā(hā)rikā; pūtīkapravālāḥ kaṇṭakikarañjapallavāḥ, anye tu ‘ciribilvapallavapatraṃ ca’ iti paṭhanti; tālapatrī mūṣalī, kanakakṣīrī [33] suvarṇakṣīrī , kaṅguṣṭhamityanye| śuktipramāṇāḥ pratyekamardhapalapramāṇāḥ| sa eva sapratīvāpaḥ pakvaḥ pākyastīkṣṇa iti sa eva saṃvyūhimasañjñaḥ kṣāro dantyādidravyaprativāpena sahitaḥ pakvaḥ pākyasañjño bhavati tīkṣṇaścetyarthaḥ| na cāsya tīkṣṇadoṣaḥ, kutaḥ? atitīkṣṇaḥ kṣārasya doṣaḥ| anye tu ‘sa eva sapratīvāpa’ ityādipāṭhasthāne ‘sa ca pūrvavat pakvastīkṣṇa’ iti paṭhanti||13||

teṣāṃ yathāvyādhibalamupayogaḥ ||14||

teṣāṃ kṣārāṇām||14||

kṣīṇabale [34] tu kṣārodakamāvapedbalakaraṇārtham ||15||

kālavaśāddhīnauṣadhivaśādvā mṛdau kṣāre balakaraṇāyāvāpamāha- kṣīṇabale tvityādi| kṣīṇabale hīnaśaktau kṣāre kṣārodakamāvapet, pūrvakalpena kṛtaṃ kṣārodakaṃ prakṣipet, prakṣipya punarvipacediti| balakaraṇārthaṃ tīkṣṇādiśaktyupacayārtham||15||

bhavataścātra-
naivātitīkṣṇo [35] na mṛduḥ śuklaḥ ślakṣṇo'tha picchilaḥ |
aviṣyandī śivaḥ śīghraḥ kṣāro hyaṣṭaguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||16||

kṣāraguṇadoṣān pratipādayannāha- bhavataścātra naivātītyādi| atiśabdastīkṣṇamṛduśuklaiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; anatiśuklatvāt prativāpālpatā sūcitā bhavati; anye tvanatiśabdaṃ śuklena sambadhnanti| ślakṣṇaḥ akarkaśaḥ; anye tu ślakṣṇasthāne ‘mṛtsna’ iti paṭhanti, mṛtsnastvagindriyasukhaḥ| aviṣyandī yo'vacārito na prasarpati| śiva iti saumya ucyate; śivasthāne kecit ‘sama’ iti paṭhanti, tatra nātisāndro nātidrava ityarthaḥ| śīghraḥ śīghrakārī| kecit kṣāraguṇeṣu madhye ‘sukhanirgrāhyo doṣaghnaḥ’ iti guṇadvayaṃ paṭhanti; ‘sukhanirgrāhyaḥ sukhanirvartyaḥ, doṣaghno doṣamātrānusārī; doṣaśabdo'tra vyādhau vartate’ iti ca vyākhyānayanti| naivātitīkṣṇa ityādinaivāṣṭasaṅkhyā labdhā, ‘punaraṣṭaguṇa’ iti yat kṛtaṃ tat kaṭurasādiguṇātirekatvaniṣedhārtham||16||

atimārdavaśvaityauṣṇyataikṣṇyapaicchilyasarpitāḥ |
sāndratā'pakvatā hīnadravyatā doṣa ucyate ||17||

parihārārthaṃ doṣānadhikṛtyāha- atimārdavetyādi| atiśabdo mārdavaśvaityādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| śvaityaṃ śuklatā| kecit kṣāradoṣān sārdhaślokenādhīyate| tadyathā- “atyauṣṇyamatipaicchilyamatitaikṣṇyaṃ visarpitā| atyarthaṃ mārdavaṃ śvaityamatyarthaṃ sāndrameva ca| hīnauṣadhamavipakvatvaṃ kṣāradoṣā nava smṛtāḥ”- iti| atyarthaṃśabdaḥ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| saṅkhyeyanirdeśādeva saṅkhyā prāptā, punarnaveti saṅkhyākaraṇaṃ kṣārātisevayā puṃstvahantṛtvadoṣasaṅkhyāśaṅkānirāsārtham| kecit kṣāradoṣānna paṭhanti, uktaguṇaviparyayeṇaiva doṣāṇāṃ labdhatvāditi||17||

tatra kṣārasādhyavyādhivyādhitamupaveśya nivātātape deśe'sambādhe'gropaharaṇīyoktena vidhānenopasambhṛtasambhāraṃ, tato'sya tamavakāśaṃ nirīkṣyāvaghṛṣyāvalikhya pracchayitvā [36] , śalākayā kṣāraṃ pratisārayet, dattvā vākśatamātramupekṣeta ||18||

idānīṃ kṣārapratisāraṇavidhimadhikṛtyāha- tatra kṣārasādhyetyādi|
kṣārasādhyavyādhivyādhitamiti kṣārasya sādhyo yo vyādhistena vyādhitaṃ duḥkhitam| nivātātape deśe iti niśabdo vātātapābhyāṃ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| asambādhe'saṅkīrṇadeśe, agropaharaṇīyoktena vidhāneneti ‘praśasteṣu tithikaraṇamuhūrtanakṣatreṣu’ (sū. a. 5) ityādikena vidhinā; asya ca kṣāraṃ pratisārayedityanayā kriyayā sambandhaḥ| upasambhṛtasambhāramiti yantraśastrakṣārāgniśalāketyādisamīpasthāpitopakaraṇam| tataḥ sambhṛtasambhārānantaram| asya vyādhitasya, tamavakāśaṃ taṃ pradeśaṃ, nirīkṣyeti vyādhyākāravijñānārtham, avaghṛṣyeti pittaduṣṭam, avalikhyeti vātaduṣṭaṃ suptakaṭhinatvacaṃ, pracchayitveti kaphaduṣṭaṃ kaṇḍūśvayathuprabalaṃ, paścācchalākayā kṣāraṃ pratisārayedityarthaḥ; ‘kṣāraṃ pratisārayet’ ityasya sthāne ‘kṣāraṃ pātayitvā’ iti kecit paṭhanti| kālāvadhiṃ pratipādayati- dattvā vākśatamātramityādi|- vāk laghvakṣaroccāraṇaṃ [37] ; vākśatamātramiti mātragrahaṇenaitadbodhayati- etāvatā kālena yo dahati sa yathoktaguṇaḥ kṣāraḥ| anye mātrasthāne evaśabdaṃ paṭhanti, “vākśatameva nādhikaṃ na nyūnaṃ ca’’ iti ca vyākhyānayanti| idānīṃ kṣārasya pratidoṣaṃ lepapramāṇamavacāraṇakālaṃ ca niyamayannāha- padmapatrasama ityādi| imaṃ ca pāṭhaṃ kecidācāryā na paṭhanti, sa cābhāvāt samagro na likhita iti||18||

tasminnipatite vyādhau kṛṣṇatā dagdhalakṣaṇam [38] |19|

samyagdagdhalakṣaṇaṃ vaktumāha- tasminnipatita ityādi| vyādhiśabdo'tra duṣṭamāṃsādiṣu vartate, natu duḥkhasañjñike vyādhau; kutaḥ? tatra kṛṣṇatābhāvāt| etattu [39] sāmānyaṃ samyagdagdhalakṣaṇam|19|-

tatrāmlavargaḥ śamanaḥ sarpirmadhukasaṃyutaḥ ||19||
atha cet sthiramūlatvāt [40] kṣāradagdhaṃ na śīryate |
idamālepanaṃ tatra samagramavacārayet ||20||
amlakāñjikabījāni tilān madhukameva ca |
prapeṣya samabhāgāni tenainamanulepayet ||21||

samyagdagdhe paścātkarmāha- tatrāmlavarga ityādi| amlavargaḥ sauvīrakatuṣodakadhānyāmlādiḥ| sthiramūlatvāt dṛḍhamūlatvādityarthaḥ| na śīryate na śaṭati| idamiti agre vakṣyamāṇam| tatreti aśīryamāṇe kṣāradagdhe| samagraṃ sarvam| avacārayet prayojayet| tadevālepanamāha- amletyādi| amlakāñjikabījāniti amlakāñjikādhaḥsthitaṃ dravyam| teneti samabhāgapiṣṭenāmlakāñjikabījādinā| enaṃ kṣāradagdham||19-21||

tilakalkaḥ samadhuko ghṛtākto vraṇaropaṇaḥ |22|

kṣārakṛtavraṇaropaṇopāyamāha- tilakalka ityādi|22|-

rasenāmlena tīkṣṇena vīryoṣṇena ca yojitaḥ ||22||
āgneyenāgninā tulyaḥ kathaṃ kṣāraḥ praśāmyati |
evaṃ cenmanyase vatsa! procyamānaṃ nibodha me ||23||
amlavarjān rasān kṣāre sarvāneva vibhāvayet |
kaṭukastatra bhūyiṣṭho lavaṇo'nurasastathā |
amlena saha saṃyuktaḥ sa [41] tīkṣṇalavaṇo rase ||24||
mādhuryaṃ bhajate'tyarthaṃ tīkṣṇabhāvaṃ vimuñcati |
mādhuryācchamamāpnoti [42] vahniradbhirivāplutaḥ ||25||

agnitulyasya kṣārasyāgneyena kāñjikena kathaṃ śamanamityāśaṅkyāha- rasenetyādi| amlena tīkṣṇena vīryoṣṇenāgneyena rasena yojito'gnitulyaḥ kṣāraḥ kathaṃ praśāmyatītyarthapiṇḍaḥ| uttaramāha- evaṃ cenmanyase vatsetyādi| vibhāvayet jānīyādityarthaḥ| kaṭukastatra bhūyiṣṭha iti tatra pañcarase kṣāre kaṭuko'nurasaḥ, lavaṇastu bhūyiṣṭha iti yojyaṃ; kecit kaṭukameva bhūyiṣṭhaṃ manyante; tanna, yato janā amlabhakṣaṇe mādhuryārthaṃ lavaṇameva rasaṃ prayuñjate, lavaṇavarge kṣārasya paṭhitatvācca, kaṭuvarge cāpaṭhitatvāt; yadyevaṃ ‘sa khalvāgneyauṣadhibhūyiṣṭhatvāt kaṭuka’ iti tarhi katham? ucyate, lavaṇavargapāṭhādeva kṣārasya lavaṇatve siddhe kaṭuka iti vidhyarthameva| sa tīkṣṇalavaṇo rase iti sa kṣāra evaṅguṇaḥ| adbhiḥ pānīyaiḥ āpluto ‘boli(ḍi)ta’ iti loke||22-25||

tatra samyagdagdhe vikāropaśamo lāghavamanāsrāvaśca |26|

idānīṃ cikitsāntarakathanāya punaḥ samyagdagdhalakṣaṇamāha- tatra samyagdagdha ityādi| tatreti samyagdagdhādimadhye| lāghavamaṅgānām| kṛte kṣāranirvāpaṇa ete vikāropaśamādayo lakṣaṇānyuttarakālaṃ jñeyāni; “etadvikāropaśamādikaṃ kiñcittātkālikaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ, kiñciduttarakālikam” ityanye|26|-

hīnadagdhe todakaṇḍujāḍyāni vyādhivṛddhiśca |26|

hīnadagdhalakṣaṇamāha- hīnadagdhe ityādi| hīnadagdhe todādayo vātaślaiṣmikāḥ| jāḍyaṃ jaḍatā| anye tvatra ‘durdagdhe todadāhakaṇḍūjāḍyāni vyādhivṛddhiśca’ iti paṭhanti, “tasmin sthāne etāni vātapittaraktakaphaprakopakāryāṇi” iti ca vyākhyānayanti|26|-

atidagdhe dāhapākarāgasrāvāṅgamardaklamapipāsāmūrcchāḥ syurmaraṇaṃ vā ||26||

atidagdhalakṣaṇamāha- atidagdhe dāhetyādi| atidagdhe dāhapākādayaḥ paittikāḥ, rāgo raktatā, aṅgamardo vedanāviśeṣaḥ, klamo glāniḥ||26||

kṣāradagdhavraṇaṃ [43] tu yathādoṣaṃ yathāvyādhi copakramet ||27||

kṣāradagdhavraṇavyāpaccikitsāṃ vaktumāha- kṣāradagdhavraṇamityādi| anye tu ‘yathāvidhi yathādoṣam’ iti paṭhanti, “hetuvyādhipratyanīkena vidhinā” iti vyākhyānayanti||27||

atha naite kṣārakṛtyāḥ- tadyathā- durbalabālasthavirabhīrusarvāṅgaśūnodariraktapittigarbhiṇyṛtumatīpravṛddhajvaripramehirūkṣakṣatakṣīṇa- tṛṣṇāmūrcchopadrutaklībāpavṛttodvṛttaphalayonayaḥ ||28||

ataḥ kṣārasya dvividhasyāpi yeṣu pratiṣedhastānāha- atha naite kṣārakṛtyā ityādi| eṣu ca yathādoṣaṃ keṣucideva pratisāraṇīyakṣāraniṣedhaḥ, keṣucit pānīyaniṣedhaḥ, keṣucidubhayaniṣedhaḥ| sarvāṅgaśūnodarīti sarvāṅgena śūno ya udarī tasyaiva kṣāraniṣedhaḥ [44] kṛtaḥ; kṣata uraḥkṣataḥ, tena kṣīṇaḥ; tṛṣṇāmūrcchopadruta iti upadrutaśabdastṛṣṇāmūrcchābhyāṃ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; klībaḥ kṣīṇaśukraḥ, aśukrasya punaḥ kṣārādhikāra eva nāsti [45] ; udvṛttāpavṛttaphalayonaya iti udvṛttāpavṛttaśabdau phalayonibhyāṃ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyete; phalamaṇḍaṃ, yonirgarbhāśayaḥ; udvṛttaphalaḥ sthānādūrdhvagatāṇḍaḥ, apavṛttaphalaḥ sthānāt srastāṇḍaḥ; udvṛttayoniḥ sthānādūrdhvagatayoniḥ, apavṛttayoniḥ sthānāt srastayoniḥ; anye tu phalayoniśabdena samuditena garbhāśayamācakṣate, tatrāyamarthaḥ- udvṛttaphalayoniḥ sthānādūrdhvagatagarbhāśayā, apavṛttaphalayoniḥ sthānāt srastagarbhāśayetyarthaḥ; apare tu phalaśabdaṃ rajasi vartayanti, kutaḥ? garbhasya phalasādhanatvāt; tena yasyāḥ yoneḥ sakāśāt phalaṃ raja udvṛttamūrdhvagataṃ apavṛttamadhogatam, sā udvṛttāpavṛttaphalayoniḥ [46] ||28||

tathā marmasirāsnāyusandhitaruṇāsthisevanīdhamanīgalanābhinakhāntaḥśephaḥsrotaḥsvalpamāṃseṣu ca deśeṣvakṣṇośca na dadyādanyatra vartmarogāt ||29||

vyādhipuruṣaviśeṣeṇa niṣedhamuktvā pradeśaviśeṣeṇa vaktumāha- tathetyādi| taruṇāsthīni ghrāṇakarṇagrīvākṣipuṭeṣu taruṇasañjñānyasthīni; sevanyaḥ sapta, pañca śirasi, ekā jihvāyām, ekā śephasi; dhamanyaścaturviṃśatirnābhiprabhavāḥ; nābhiriti marmagrahaṇādeva nābhiḥ prāptā, punarnābhigrahaṇaṃ nābhyāṃ doṣātiśayasūcanārthaṃ; śepho meḍhraḥ| anuktamupagṛhṇannāha- alpamāṃseṣu ca pradeśeṣu jānupādopari lalāṭādiṣu ca| akṣṇośca na dadyādanyatra vartmarogāditi anyatrānyasmin roge, vartmaroge punarakṣṇorapi kṣāraṃ dadyādityarthaḥ||29||

tatra kṣārasādhyeṣvapi vyādhiṣu śūnagātramasthiśūlinamannadveṣiṇaṃ hṛdayasandhipīḍopadrutaṃ ca kṣāro na sādhayati (durbalabālasthavirādīn pratisāraṇīya [47] iti) ||30||

avasthāvaśānniṣedhārthaṃ darśayannāha- tatra kṣārasādhyeṣvityādi [48] ||30||

bhavati cātra-
viṣāgniśastrāśanimṛtyukalpaḥ kṣāro bhavatyalpamatiprayuktaḥ |
sa dhīmatā samyaganuprayukto rogānnihanyādacireṇa ghorān ||31||

idānīṃ samyakprayuktasya kṣārasya mahāguṇatvamasamyakprayuktasya tu mahādoṣatvaṃ nidarśayannāha- bhavati cātretyādi| viṣāgniśastrāśanimṛtyukalpa iti mṛtyuśabdo viṣādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate, tenāyamarthaḥ- viṣādibhiḥ kṛtvā yo mṛtyustena mṛtyunā kalpaḥ sadṛśo bhavati; tatra viṣavadvedanābhiḥ sarvāṅgānusārī bhūtvā mārayati, agnivadvisphoṭakādikāraṇo bhūtvā, śastravat sirādicchedī bhūtvā, aśanivat sadyo mārayatīti dṛṣṭāntacatuṣṭayamupanyastam||31||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne kṣārapākavidhirnāmaikādaśo'dhyāyaḥ ||11||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne kṣārapākavidhirnāmaikadaśo'dhyāyaḥ||11||

1. pacanaṃ pākaḥ, vidhiravacāraṇaṃ, kṣārasya pākavidhī yatra taṃ kṣārapākavidhim|
2. ‘chedyādikartṛtvaṃ kṣārasya bhagandaracchedanāḍīdāraṇakuṣṭhalekhanādinā” iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘te punardve anuśastre’ iti pā.|
4. viśeṣakriyāyāṃ pānākhyāyāmapyavacāryate kṣāraḥ, śastrānuśastrāṇi tu pāne nopayujyante, ataḥ siddhamasya viśeṣakriyāvacāraṇaṃ| taduktaṃ vāgbhaṭena- “sarvaśastrānuśastrāṇāṃ kṣāraḥ śreṣṭho bahūni yat| chedyabhedyādikarmāṇi kurute viṣameṣvapi| duḥkhāvacāryaśastreṣu tena siddhimayātsu ca| atikṛcchreṣu rogeṣu yacca pāne'pi yujyate (vā. sū. a. 30)” iti|
5. naca vācyaṃ yadvātaharādikṣāradravyāṇāṃ dagdhatvānna tadguṇānvayo bhasmanīti, dṛṣṭo hi bhasmanyapi prakṛtiguṇānvayaḥ, uktaṃ hi ‘sakṣaudramindīvarabhasmavāri nihanti raktaṃ’ (u. taṃ. a. 45) ityādi” iti cakraḥ|
6. ‘dahanapacanādikartṛtayā āgneyatvam kṣārasya sphuṭaṃ, pittajayāya svaguṇaṃ saumyatvamasphuṭaṃ sphorayannāha- śuklatvāt saumya iti| saumyatve'pyasya nahi śītatvam, uṣṇatvasyaiva darśanāt| bhūtānāṃ cādṛṣṭaviśeṣayantrito melakaviśeṣaḥ samānāśrayamapi guṇaṃ nārabhate| yathā āmalake amlatvamārabhate noṣṇatvam’ iti cakraḥ|
7. ‘sa khalvā...’ iti pā.|
8. ‘stambhanaḥ sirācchedādatipravṛttasya śoṇitasya, kṣāro hi chinnaṃ sirāmukhaṃ pācayitvā śoṇitagatiṃ niruṇaddhi’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
9. atra vidradhiśabdo na paṭhyate hastalikhitapustakeṣu|
10. ‘kṣāro hi śoṇitena saha sambhūya tāni tāni karmāṇi karotītyāha- eteṣvityādi| eteṣu kuṣṭhādiṣu, cakārāt kaṭhināśrayeṣu tādṛśeṣu kaṭhinotsannavraṇamāṃsādiṣu caivānuśastrapraṇidhānaṃ yathāyogaṃ śastrānuśastrapraṇidhānānantaraṃ kṣārapraṇidhānamuktaṃ pūrvācāryairiti śeṣaḥ”| iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
11. ‘kṛmiviṣābhyāṃ’ iti pā.|
12. atrārocakavidradhiśabdau na paṭhyete keṣucitpustakeṣu|
13. ‘mātrā tu palatrikarṣārdhapalarūpā uttamamadhyamādhamabaleṣu’ iti cakraḥ|
14. athetaro mṛdurmadhyastīkṣṇaśca’ iti pā. |
15. ‘śucirupavasanniti kṣāradrumasthitabhūtavighnopaśamārthaṃ, evameva ca grahaṇe bhaiṣajyaṃ guṇayuktaṃ bhavatīti darśayituṃ’ iti cakraḥ|
16. ‘asitamokṣakaṃ’ iti pā.|
17. ‘adhivāsyeti samantrabalikarmādinā'dhivāsanaṃ kṛtvā’ iti cakraḥ|
18. tato'paredyuḥ” iti pā.|
19. hārāṇacandrastu nicitaṃ kṛtvā’ iti paṭhati, vyākhyānayati ca -nicitaṃ kṛtvā rāśīkṛtya’|
20. kalpena iti pā.|
21. atra guṇotkarṣārthamanuktānyapi dakṣaśakṛdādīnyāvāpyānyāhuḥ- “ślakṣṇaṃ śakṛddakṣaśikhigṛdhrakaṅkakapotajam| catuṣpātpakṣipittālamanohvālavaṇāni ca’ (vā. sū. a. 30) iti prakṣepādhikāre tantrāntarānuśāsanāt’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
22. “āyase pātre iti lauhapātrasyaivātidṛḍhatvena kṣārābhedyatvāt| kiṃvā lauhapātre eva śaktyatiśayaḥ kṣārasya bhavatītyetadvacanādunnīyate’ iti cakraḥ|
23. kṣāro dvividhaḥ sapratīvāpo'pratīvāpaśca| āvāpānantaraṃ yatpunaḥ śaktyatiśayārthaṃ dīyate sa pratīvāpaḥ| tatrāpratīvāpo mṛduḥ saṃvyūhimasañjñaḥ, sapratīvāpaśca pākyasañjñastīkṣṇaḥ” iti cakraḥ|
24. ‘asitamokṣakaṃ’ iti pā.|
25. ‘pūjanārthe mayā’ iti pā.|
26. ‘bhoje'pyayameva’ iti pā.|
27. ‘kāṣṭharaso bhasmani galito dagdhaḥ śarkarāsadṛśatvādbhasmaśarkarocyate| tathāca nighaṇṭuḥ, - ‘muṣkakāddahyamānāttu rasaḥ pracyavate hi yaḥ| bhasmanā saha saṃyuktaḥ kāṭhinyamupagacchati| tāṃ bhasmaśarkarāmāhuḥ’ ityādi” iti cakraḥ|
28. aśvakarṇaḥ sarjabhedaḥ” iti cakraḥ| ‘śālabhedaḥ’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
29. annapānoktairgavādimūtrairityarthaḥ’ iti cakraḥ|
30. ‘athaiṣāṃ’ iti pā.|
31. ‘catvāriṃśatpalāni’ iti pā.|
32. ‘caturthāṃśe āvāpamicchanti’ iti pā.|
33. ‘kanakakṣīrī ‘kaṅkuṣṭha’ iti vyavahriyate’ iti cakraḥ|
34. kṣīṇabalatvamatra dravakṣayādeva lakṣaṇīyaṃ| taduktaṃ vāgbhaṭe- ‘balārthaṃ kṣīṇapānīye kṣārāmbu punarāvapet’ iti” iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
35. ‘kṣārasyātitīkṣṇatvaṃ vākśatāt prāgeraṇḍanāladāhāt, mṛdutvaṃ cārvāgapyadāhāllakṣaṇīyaṃ| yaduktaṃ ‘yadyeraṇḍajanālameṣa dahati kṣāro varo vākśatāt’ iti | vākśatādyadi dahati tarhi varaḥ śreṣṭho'nyathā tvavaro'titīkṣṇatvena mṛdutvena ceti tātparyam” iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
36. ‘avaghṛṣyetyādipakṣatraye pittaduṣṭamuttānamavaghṛṣya, vātaduṣṭamanatyavagāḍhaṃ vā'valikhya, kaphaduṣṭamavagāḍhaṃ ca pracchayitveti vyavasthā” iti cakraḥ|
37. ‘vākśatamātramiti śatākṣaroccāraṇaparyāptaṃ kālaṃ kṣārāvacāraṇam’ iti pā.|
38. ‘nipatite iti kṣārāpanīte vyādhau kṛṣṇatā dagdhalakṣaṇaṃ jñeyam, etacca dāhe jñeyaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ| vikāropaśamādyudarkajñeyamagre vakṣyati” iti cakraḥ|
39. ‘etattvasāmānyaṃ’ iti pā. |
40. “sthiramūlatvena dagdhalakṣaṇaṃ bhavati naca niḥśeṣavyādhiśātanaṃ, tatrāyaṃ lepaḥ- amlakāñjiketyādi” iti cakraḥ|
41. ‘sa tīkṣṇalavaṇo rasaḥ’ iti pā.|
42. ‘etacca bahirantaścāmlena kṣāramādhuryānayanāt kṣāravikārahananamucyate| yaduktaṃ carake madātyayacikitsite amlena madyenāntarakṣāravikāraśamanaṃ, tathā hi “kṣāro hi yāti mādhuryaṃ śīghramamlopasaṃhitaḥ” (ca. ci. a. 24) iti| bāhye tu kṣāre śītasparśatvādapi amlaṃ kṣāradāhaṃ hanti| yaduktaṃ viśvāmitre- “yataścoṣṇatamaḥ kṣāraḥ śaityaṃ cāmle rase'dhikam| tasmāt sekapradehābhyāmamlaḥ kṣāraṃ nivartayet” iti” cakraḥ|
43. kṣāradagdhavraṇamiti hīnātidāhajaṃ vraṇaṃ” iti cakraḥ|
44. “plīhodare kṣāravidhānāt” iti cakraḥ|
45. “klībo'trālpaśukraḥ, tasya kṣāraḥ sarvathā puṃstvaṃ hanyāt| aśukrasya puṃstvābhāvādeva na tadbādhā’ iti cakraḥ|
46. “kecittu phalaśabdenehāntraṃ grāhayantyupacārādgaṅgāyāṃ ghoṣa itivat” iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
47. ayaṃ pāṭho mudritapustakeṣu na paṭhyate|
48. asyāgre “cakārāddurbalabālasthavirādīn pratisāraṇīya iti’ ityadhikaṃ paṭhyate mudritapustakeṣu|
