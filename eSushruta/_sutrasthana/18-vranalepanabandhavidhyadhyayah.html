--- title: "18. vraṇālepanabandhavidhyadhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 18 order: 18 ---
athāto 1 vraṇālepanabandhavidhimadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2|| ālepa ādya upakramaḥ 2, eṣa sarvaśophānāṃ sāmānyaḥ pradhānatamaśca, taṃ ca pratirogaṃ vakṣyāmaḥ- tato bandhaḥ pradhānaṃ, tena śuddhirvraṇaropaṇamasthisandhisthairyaṃ ca ||3||
athāta ityādi| eṣa ālepaḥ| nanveṣa ālepa ādya upakramaḥ kathaṃ? dvivraṇīye'patarpaṇasyādyatvena paṭhitatvāt; ucyate- auṣadhadravyebhyo bahirantaḥpramārjanebhya ālepa ādya ityarthaḥ; ādyasamīpatvācchreṣṭhatvādvā''lepa ādya ityucyate| sarvaśophānāṃ sāmānya iti vātādiśophānāṃ tulyaḥ, ālepatvenābhedāt| pradhānatamaśca sarvopakramebhyaḥ āśupīḍāharatvāt| taṃ ca pratirogaṃ vakṣyāma iti tamālepaṃ, pratirogaṃ roge roge, vakṣyāmaḥ kathayiṣyāmaḥ, asmin sthāne cikitsite ca| tataḥ tasmādālepāt| asthisandhisthairyaṃ ceti asthnaḥ sandherdārḍhyaṃ ‘bhavati’ ityādhyāhāraḥ| kecidācāryā imaṃ na paṭhanti||1-3||
tatra 3 pratilomamālimpet | pratilome hi samyagauṣadhamavatiṣṭhate'nupraviśati romakūpān svedavāhibhiśca sirāmukhairvīryaṃ prāpnoti ||4||
idānīmālepāvacāraṇaṃ tatphalaṃ ca nirdeṣṭumāha- tatretyādi| pratilomamālimpediti lomābhimukhamālimpet| hi yasmādarthe| avatiṣṭhate avasthitiṃ karoti, anupraviśati ca ‘auṣadhaṃ’ ityanuvartate||4||
na ca śuṣyamāṇamupekṣeta, anyatra pīḍayitavyāt | śuṣko hyapārthako rukkaraśca 4||5||
ālepasyāvasthāyāṃ kartavyatāmāha- na cetyādi| anyatra pīḍayitavyāditi pīḍanadravyairmiśrakoktairyo vraṇaḥ pīḍanīyo bhavati tasmādanyatra śuṣyamāṇaṃ nopekṣetetyarthaḥ| hi yasmādarthe| apārthako niṣphalaḥ||5||
sa trividhaḥ- pralepaḥ, pradeha, ālepaśca | pralepapradehayorantaraṃ 5 - tatra pralepaḥ śītastanuraviśoṣī viśoṣī vā; pradehastūṣṇaḥ śīto vā bahalo'bahuraviśoṣī 6 ca; madhyamo'trālepaḥ | tatra, raktapittaprasādakṛdālepaḥ; pradeho vātaśleṣmapraśamanaḥ śodhano ropaṇaḥ śophavedanāpahaśca; tasyopayogaḥ kṣatākṣateṣu; yastu kṣateṣūpayujyate sa bhūyaḥ ‘kalka’ iti sañjñāṃ labhate niruddhālepanasañjñaḥ; tenāsrāvasannirodho mṛdutā pūtimāṃsāpakarṣaṇamanantardoṣatā vraṇaśuddhiśca bhavati ||6||
tadbhedānāha- sa trividha ityādi| pralepapradehayorvāstavābhede'pi vyāvahārikaṃ bhedaṃ darśayannāha- tatra 7 pralepa ityādi| antaraṃ bhedaḥ, sthānāsanavat| pralepaṃ lakṣaṇairnirdiśannāha- pralepa ityādi| aviśoṣī apīḍayitavye vraṇaśophe, viśoṣī pīḍayitavye; etena yena kalkena candanavallipyate sa pralepaḥ; anye ‘śītastanuḥ’ ityādilakṣaṇaṃ pradehasya iti vadanti| lakṣaṇaiḥ pradehaṃ nirdiśannāha- pradeha ityādi| uṣṇaḥ śīto veti viṣayāpekṣayā; tatra vātabahule kaphabahule vā uṣṇaḥ, pittaraktabahule śītaḥ| bahalaḥ sthūlaḥ, etena yastu kalkaḥ piṇḍīkṛtya vraṇasyopari dīyate sa pradehaḥ| ‘uṣṇaḥ śīto vā’ ityādilakṣaṇaṃ ‘pralepasya’ iti kecinmanyante| lakṣaṇāntaraṃ nirdiśya kāryabhedaṃ nirdiśannāha- tatretyādi| tatreti tayormadhye| kecidatrālepanasthāne ‘pradeha’ iti paṭhanti, anye tu pradehasthāne ālepaṃ paṭhanti| ālepasya sāmānyaviṣayaṃ darśayannāha- tasyetyādi| kṣatākṣateṣu vraṇavraṇaśopheṣvityarthaḥ| viṣayāntareṇālepasya nāmāntaramāha- yastvityādi| tuśabdo'vadhāraṇe| bhūyaḥśabdo'tra punararthe| niruddhā niṣiddhā| anye tvatra ‘nirodhyālepanasañjñāṃ’ iti paṭhanti; tatrāpi sa evārthaḥ| kalkasañjñasyālepasya karma āha- tenetyādi| āsrāvasannirodha iti āsrāvo raktādyāsrāvaḥ| anantardoṣateti doṣo'tra pūyaḥ, tenābhyantarapūyavarjitatvam| vraṇaśuddhiśceti doṣaduṣṭinivṛttiḥ; cakārādvraṇaropaṇasya grahaṇam||6||
avidagdheṣu śopheṣu hitamālepanaṃ bhavet | yathāsvaṃ doṣaśamanaṃ dāhakaṇḍūrujāpaham ||7|| tvakprasādanamevāgryaṃ māṃsaraktaprasādanam | dāhapraśamanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ rujākaṇḍūvināśanam ||8|| marmadeśeṣu ye rogā guhyeṣvapi tathā nṛṇām | saṃśodhanāya teṣāṃ hi kuryādālepanaṃ bhiṣak ||9|| (ṣaḍbhāgaṃ 8 paittike snehaṃ caturbhāgaṃ tu vātike | aṣṭabhāgaṃ tu kaphaje snehamātrāṃ pradāpayet) ||10||
ālepasyātmaviṣaye kālāvadhimāha- avidagdheṣvityādi| avidagdheṣvālepanaṃ hitaṃ, vidagdheṣu punaḥ pradeha eva, yasya ‘upanāha’ iti sañjñāntaram| yathāsvaṃ doṣaśamanamiti vātādidoṣeṣu madhye yadālepanaṃ dravyaguṇāpekṣayā yasya doṣasya śamanaṃ bhavet tat tatra hitamityarthaḥ| dāhakaṇḍūrujāpahamiti dāhaḥ pittasya, kaṇḍūḥ kaphasya, rujā vātasya, etā apahanti| dūṣyaprasādanatvamālepasya nirdeṣṭumāha- tvakprasādanamevāgryamiti|- agryaṃ pradhānam| dāhakaṇḍūrujāpahamiti pūrvasminneva śloke dāhādīnāṃ paṭhitatvādiha punaḥ pāṭho dūṣyakṛtā api dāhādayo bhavantīti bodhanārtham||7-10||
tasya 9 pramāṇaṃ mahiṣārdracarmotsedhamupadiśanti ||11||
tanutvamālepasya guṇaḥ, tanutvaṃ kiṃ pramāṇamityāha- tasyetyādi| pradehastu mahiṣārdracarmocchrāyāpekṣayā sthūla iti| ye tu tanutvaṃ pradehaguṇaṃ manyate, teṣāṃ mate pradehasyaitat pramāṇam||11||
na cālepaṃ rātrau prayuñjīta 10, mā bhūcchaityavihatoṣmaṇastadanirgamādvikārapravṛttiriti ||12|| pradehasādhye vyādhau tu hitamālepanaṃ divā | pittaraktābhighātotthe saviṣe ca viśeṣataḥ ||13|| na ca paryuṣitaṃ lepaṃ kadācidavacārayet | uparyupari lepaṃ ca na kadācit pradāpayet ||14|| ūṣmāṇaṃ vedanāṃ dāhaṃ ghanatvājjanayet sa hi | na ca tenaiva lepena pradehaṃ dāpayet punaḥ | śuṣkabhāvātsa nirvīryo yukto'pi syādapārthakaḥ ||15||
viśiṣṭe kāle ālepāvacāraṇavidhiniṣedhe, kathañcitkriyamāṇe ca doṣamāha- na cetyādi| etenāsya vidhiḥ sūcyate; tena vātaślaiṣmikāpākiśothe rātrau pradeha upayoktavyaḥ, tathā vidahyamāne śothe pradeho deya eva, kṣateṣu kalko'pi rātrau dātavya iti, tathā pittaje śothe vātapittaje ca śatadhautaghṛtābhyaṅgādi dātavyamiti| śaityāvihatoṣmaṇa iti ‘śophasya’ iti śeṣaḥ| tadanirgamāt śophoṣmāniḥsaraṇāt| vikārapravṛttirvikārābhivṛddhiḥ| śaityaruddharomakūpatvādanirgacchannūṣmā ālepenāntarnīto'tyarthaṃ śophaṃ pacatītyarthaḥ| svedavāhibhiḥ sirāmukhaiśca vīryaṃ prāpnotītyata ūrdhvaṃ bahuṣu pratiṣu nibandhapāṭhakramādvyatikrameṇa pāṭhakramo'dhikaśca pāṭho dṛśyate; sa ucyate- “tasya pramāṇaṃ mahiṣārdracarmotsedhamupadiśanti| na ca śuṣyamāṇamupekṣetānyatra pīḍayitavyāt; śuṣko hyapārtho rukkaraśca| sa trividha ālepaḥ pradeho lepaśca| teṣāmantaraṃ- ālepaḥ śītastanuraviśoṣī viśoṣī ca, pradehastūṣṇaḥ śīto vā bahulo bahuraviśoṣī ca, madhyamo lepa iti| viśeṣato raktapittaprasādakṛdālepaḥ; pralepo vātaśleṣmapraśamanaḥ śodhano ropaṇaḥ śophavedanāpahaśca, tasyopayogaḥ kṣatākṣateṣu; yastu kṣateṣūpayujyate sa bhūyaḥ ‘kalka’ iti sañjñā labhate niruddhālepanasañjñaḥ, tenāsrāvasannirodho mṛdutā pūtimāṃsāpakarṣaṇamanantardoṣatā vraṇaśuddhiśca bhavati| ubhayaguṇo lepaḥ| na cālepaṃ rātrau prayuñjīta, mābhūcchaityavihatoṣmaṇastadanirgamādvikārapravṛttiriti| tvakprasādanamevāgryaṃ māṃsaraktaprasādanam| dāhapraśamanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ todakaṇḍūvināśanam|| marmadeśeṣu ye rogā guhyeṣvapi tathā nṛṇām| saṃśodhanāya teṣāṃ hi kuryādālepanaṃ bhiṣak|| avidagdheṣu śotheṣu hitamālepanaṃ bhavet| yathāsvaṃ doṣaśamanaṃ dāhakaṇḍūvināśanam|| ṣaḍbhāgaṃ paittike snehaṃ caturbhāgaṃ tu vātike| aṣṭabhāgaṃ tu kaphaje snehamātrāṃ pradāpayet”- iti| ayaṃ pāṭho vartamāneṣu pustakeṣu sarveṣveva paṭhyata ityasmābhiḥ kramamātraṃ darśitaṃ; na tvayaṃ paṭhanīyaḥ; paṭhanīyaḥ punaḥ prāktanapāṭha eva, sarvapūrvācāryasammatatvāt||12-15||
ata ūrdhvaṃ vraṇabandhanadravyāṇyupadekṣyāmaḥ; tadyathā- kṣaumakārpāsāvikadukūlakauśeyapatrorṇacīnapaṭṭacarmāntarvalkalālābūśakalalatāvidalarajjutūlaphalasantānikālauhānīti 1112; teṣāṃ vyādhiṃ kālaṃ cāvekṣyopayogaḥ; prakaraṇataścaiṣāmādeśaḥ ||16||
idānīṃ vraṇabandhavidhiḥ prastūyate, sa ca dravyādhīnaḥ, atastānyeva prāgupadeṣṭumāha- kṣaumetyādi| kṣaumam atasītantubhirvyūtaṃ, anye vastrāntaramāhuḥ; kārpāsaṃ kārpāsatanturacitam; āvikam urabhraromavyūtaṃ; dukūlaṃ paṭṭavastram, anye valkalamāhuḥ; kauśeyaṃ kośakārakṛmilālodbhūtaṃ, yat ‘trasara’ iti loke; patrorṇo rallūkākhyaḥ kambalaḥ, anye tu ‘yairharitādīni devāṅgānyupyante te patrorṇāḥ’, ‘dhautakauśeyam’ ityapare, ‘māgadhapauṇḍrakādideśeṣu nāgavṛkṣādayaścatvāro vṛkṣāstatpatrebhyo jātairharitatantubhirūrṇārūpairvayate yattat patrorṇam’ ityeke; cīnapaṭṭaṃ cīnadeśa evotpadyate, taddeśajanāstu ‘cīnayugapaṭṭam’ iti vadanti, sa ca patrorṇasyaiva bhedaḥ, anye netramāhuḥ; carma eṇādīnām, apare carmavadbandhanadravyam, athavā suvarṇapiṣṭikāliptacarmatantubhisturuṣkairyāni vayante tāni carmaśabdenocyante; bāhyavalkalamantyaṃ ca tyaktvā madhyaṃ yadgṛhyate tadantarvalkalam; alābūśakalaṃ tumbīphalakarparikā; latāvidalaṃ śyāmalatādipāṭitārdhaṃ; kecidvidalaśabdaṃ carmādibhiḥ saha sambadhnanti, anye tu vidalaśabdena vaṃśavetrādikambikāmāhuḥ; latāśabdena ca śyāmalatādīnāṃ grahaṇaṃ; rajjurmuñjabalvajādivalitaḥ; tūlaphalaṃ śālmalīphalādi; santānikā kṣīrasantānikā, anye ‘kuśalasantānikā’ iti paṭhanti, kuśalasantānikā kolikavṛttiḥ dvitritanturacitā ‘vaya’ iti loke prasiddhā, markaṭavāsaṃ kecidāhuḥ; lohāni suvarṇatrapusīsādīni| kva punasteṣāmupayoga ityāha- teṣāmityādi| teṣāṃ kṣaumādīnām| vyādhiṃ kālaṃ cāvekṣyopayoga iti tatra vātaślaiṣmikaṃ vyādhiṃ kālaṃ ca śītamavekṣya vraṇabandhanadravyāṇāṃ ghanānāmupayogaḥ, raktapaittikaṃ vyādhimuṣṇaṃ ca kālamavekṣya tanūnāmupayogaḥ, cakārādgāḍhasthānamavekṣya ghanānāmupayogaḥ, śithilasthānamavekṣya tanūnāmupayoga iti| vargoktānāṃ vraṇabandhanadravyāṇāṃ saṅkṣepeṇādeśamāha- prakaraṇataścaiṣāmādeśa iti|- prakaraṇataḥ prakaraṇamavekṣya, eṣāṃ vraṇabandhanadravyāṇāmādeśanaṃ; tadyathā- sarpādidaṣṭasyāriṣṭābandho rakṣā(jvā)dibhiḥ, raktātipravṛttau raktanivṛttyarthaṃ santānikayā badhnīyāt, pāṭitaśyāmalatādyairbhagnasandhānāya bandhaḥ kartavyaḥ, lauhaiḥ suvarṇādibhirdantān badhnīyāt, trapvādibhirarbudagranthyādīn, alābuśakalena śirovraṇān badhnīyāt; evamanyeṣāmapi yathārhamādeśanam||16||
tatra kośadāmasvastikānuvellitamu(pra)tolīmaṇḍalasthagikāyamakakhaṭvācīnavibandhavitānagophaṇāḥ 13 pañcāṅgī ceti caturdaśa bandhaviśeṣāḥ | teṣāṃ nāmabhirevākṛtayaḥ prāyeṇa vyākhyātāḥ ||17||
idānīṃ bandhaviśeṣān saṅkhyayā nirdeṣṭumāha- tatra kośetyādi| eṣāmākāraṃ nirdeṣṭumāha- teṣāmityādi| prāyograhaṇānnāmnaiva sarveṣāmākṛtīrvaktuṃ na śakyate, tasmāt paratantroktalakṣaṇamapyatraiva samyagākṛtivijñānāya ślokairupadekṣyāmaḥ| tadyathā- “pūrayitvauṣadhairbandhaḥ kriyate kośakākṛtiḥ| kośakākhyaḥ sa vijñeyo, vaidyairbandhanavarjitaḥ|| dāmākṛtiṃ catuṣpādāṃ dāmākhyaṃ, svastikaṃ punaḥ| svastikākṛtimāsīvya 14, paścādāveṣṭya badhyate|| anuvellitaṃ tadvidyāt, mutolīlakṣaṇaṃ śṛṇu| bahvaṅghrirmuṭikākāro jālavadbahurandhrakaḥ|| mutolībandha eṣa syāt sañjñāntaramataḥ śṛṇu| gotuṇḍikāṃ cālanīṃ ca tāmevāhurbhiṣagvarāḥ|| maṇḍalaṃ veṣṭanākāraṃ vidadhyāt, sthagikāṃ punaḥ 15| sthagikāmiva cāpūrya bheṣajaiḥ paṭṭakena ca|| badhyate maṇḍalākāro yamalavraṇayostu yaḥ| bandhaṃ yamakanāmānaṃ bhiṣajo varṇayanti tam|| khaṭvākāraścatuṣpādaḥ khaṭvābandhaḥ prakīrtitaḥ| cīnabandhaṃ vijānīyāccīrābhirbahubhirvṛtam| vibandho vividho bandhaḥ sa ca ṣaṭcīndhi(ri)kāyutaḥ 16|| jñeyo vitānasañjñastu vitānākārasaṃyutaḥ| pāṣāṇaguḍakotkṣepakāriṇīṃ gophaṇāṃ viduḥ|| tadākṛtiṃ bhiṣakkuryādgophaṇāṃ, pañcakambibhiḥ| pañcāṅgīṃ tu vṛtāṃ kuryāt sandhānāya cikitsakaḥ”- iti||17||
tatra kośamaṅguṣṭhāṅguliparvasu vidadhyāt, dāma sambādhe'ṅge, sandhikūrcakabhrūstanāntaratalakarṇeṣu svastikaṃ, anuvellitaṃ śākhāsu, grīvāmeḍhrayoḥ mu(pra)tolīṃ, vṛtte'ṅge maṇḍalam, aṅguṣṭhāṅgulimeḍhrāgreṣu sthagikāṃ, yamalavraṇayoryamakaṃ, hanuśaṅkhagaṇḍeṣu khaṭvām, apāṅgayoścīnaṃ, pṛṣṭhodaroraḥsu vibandhaṃ, mūrdhani vitānaṃ, cibukanāsauṣṭhāṃsabastiṣu gophaṇāṃ, jatruṇa ūrdhvaṃ pañcāṅgīmiti; yo vā yasmin śarīrapradeśe suniviṣṭo bhavati taṃ tasmin vidadhyāt ||18||
bandhaviṣayamāha- tatretyādi| tatreti caturdaśabandheṣu madhye| parvaśabdo'ṅguṣṭhāṅgulibhiḥ saha sambadhyate| dāma sambādhe'ṅge iti saṅkaṭe'ṅge| sandhikūrcakabhrūstanāntaratalakarṇeṣviti kṣipramarmopariṣṭādubhayataḥ kūrcakaḥ, pādāṅguṣṭhāṅgulyormadhye kṣipramiti marma; bhrūstanāntaramiti antaraśabdaḥ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; talamiha hastapādatalam| śākhāsviti śākhāsu sakthibāhuṣu| bāhupārśvodarorupṛṣṭhādikaṃ vṛttamaṅgam| aṅguṣṭhāṅgulimeḍhrāgreṣviti agraśabdo'trāṅguṣṭhādibhiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| hanuśaṅkhagaṇḍeṣviti hanurmukhasandhiḥ, śaṅkho bhrūkarṇayorantaraṃ, gaṇḍo gallaḥ| apāṅgayoścīnamiti apāṅgayornetrāntapradeśayoḥ| hṛdayordhvaṃ stanayormadhyaṃ grīvāmūlādadhastāduraḥ| mūrdhā mastakam| cibukanāsauṣṭhāṃsabastiṣu gophaṇāmiti cibukamoṣṭhādho māṃsāsthipiṇḍikā 17, aṃso bāhuśiraḥ, bastirmūtrāśayaḥ| jatruṇa ūrdhvaṃ grīvāmūlādupari| ādyasūtrāt ‘vidadhyāt’ iti kriyāpadaṃ sarvatrānuvartate| uktaṃ deśaniyamaṃ prayojanavaśena vyabhicārayannāha- yo vetyādi| suniviṣṭaḥ śobhanākāraḥ||18||
yantraṇamūrdhvamadhastiryak ca ||19||
