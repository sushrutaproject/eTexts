--- title: "34. yuktasenīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 34 order: 34 ---

athāto yuktasenīyamadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

yuktasenīyamiti yuktā senā yasya sa yuktaseno rājetyarthaḥ; tamadhikṛtya kṛto'dhyāyo yuktasenīyaḥ||1-2||

nṛpateryuktasenasya parānabhijigīṣataḥ |
bhiṣajā rakṣaṇaṃ kāryaṃ yathā tadupadekṣyate ||3||
vijigīṣuḥ sahāmātyairyātrāyuktaḥ prayatnataḥ |
rakṣitavyo viśeṣeṇa viṣādeva narādhipaḥ ||4||

yuktasenasyeti sajjasenasya, gajaturagarathapadātilakṣaṇacaturaṅgabalasyetyarthaḥ| anye yuktasenasya militasenasyeti kathayanti| parān śatrūn; abhijigīṣataḥ jetumicchata ityarthaḥ| vijigīṣuḥ jetumicchuḥ| sahāmātyairiti kramāgataiḥ puruṣaiḥ sahetyarthaḥ| yātrāyukta iti dattaprayāṇa ityarthaḥ| rakṣitavya iti sarvebhya āgantumṛtyubhyaḥ| viśeṣeṇa atiśayena| viṣaṃ sthāvaraṃ, jaṅgamaṃ, kṛtrimaṃ ca||3-4||

panthānamudakaṃ chāyāṃ [2] bhaktaṃ yavasamindhanam |
dūṣayantyarayastacca jānīyācchodhayettathā ||5||

viṣaprayogabhedaṃ cikitsārthaṃ darśayannāha- panthānamityādi| panthānaṃ mārgaṃ; sa ca viṣeṇa dūṣito'śvādikhurakṣuṇṇareṇunā romakūpanāsāvivarādiṣu praviśan [3] vikāraṃ janayati| chāyāmiti viṣeṇa vṛkṣādidūṣaṇācchāyādoṣaḥ, dravyasvabhāvādvā chāyāmapi viṣaṃ dūṣayati; yadvā bhallātakacchāyā tatkṣaṇācchophakāriṇīti| bhaktam odanādikam| yavasaṃ busādi| dūṣayanti vikṛtiṃ kurvanti| arayaḥ śatravaḥ| tacceti panthādi| jānīyāt ‘liṅgaiḥ kṛtvā’ iti śeṣaḥ| śodhayet cikitset|||5||

tasya liṅgaṃ cikitsā ca kalpasthāne pravakṣyate |6|

kiṃ talliṅgaṃ kā vā cikitsetyāha- tasyetyādi|6|-

ekottaraṃ mṛtyuśatamatharvāṇaḥ pracakṣate ||6||
tatraikaḥ kālasaṃyuktaḥ [4] śeṣā āgantavaḥ smṛtāḥ |7|

nanu yadyakālamṛtyurasti tadā viṣādibhyo nṛpatirakṣaṇaṃ yuktaṃ, viṣabhakṣakasya liṅgacikitse ca yukte, atha nāstyakālamṛtyustadā kiṃ niṣphalaprayāsenetyāśaṅkyākālamṛtyuṃ pramāṇaiḥ sādhayannādāvāgamameva pramāṇamāha- ekottaramityādi| ekenādhikaṃ mṛtyuśatamatharvāṇo'tharvavedavidaḥ puruṣāḥ, pracakṣate kathayanti| tatraikaḥ kālasaṃyuktaḥ iti tatra teṣāṃ mṛtyūnāṃ madhye ekaḥ kālasaṃyuktaḥ kālamṛtyurityarthaḥ| śeṣā āgantavaḥ smṛtā iti śeṣā urvaritā āgantavo'pamṛtyavaḥ smṛtāḥ kathitāḥ ityarthaḥ| tatra garbhavarṣeṇa sahaikottaraśatavarṣairekottaraśataṃ mṛtyavaḥ pūryante; tataḥ śatādarvāgakālamṛtyavaḥ, śatādanantaraṃ kālamṛtyuriti kecinmanyante| apare bruvate- ‘śataśabdo'trāsaṅkhyāvācakaḥ, uttaraśabdastu pradhānavācakaḥ; tenāsaṅkhyā mṛtyavaḥ, kāraṇānāmasaṅkhyeyatvāt; ekaśca mṛtyuḥ pradhānaṃ, sa ca kālamṛtyuḥ kathyate| nanu, yo yasmin kāle mriyate sa tasya kālamṛtyureva, tat kathamakālamṛtyurityucyate? tathā coktaṃ- “nākāle mriyate kaścinnāsti mṛtyurakālajaḥ yo yasmin mriyate kāle mṛtyukālaḥ sa tasya hi- ” iti| vyāsabhaṭṭārakeṇāyuktaṃ- “nākāle mriyate kaccidviddhaḥ śaraśatairapi| kālaprāptasya kaunteya! vajrāyante tṛṇānyapi”- iti| naitadasti, upamānādipramāṇairbahudhā''cāryairakālamṛtyoḥ sthāpitatvāt| tathāca- “yathā varṣamakāle ca yathā puṣpaṃ yathā phalam| yathā syāddīpanirvāṇamakāle maraṇaṃ tathā”- iti| vyāsavacanamapyarjunaprotsāhakamakālamṛtyusthāpakaṃ dṛśyate; tathāhi- “jalamagnirviṣaṃ śastraṃ striyo rājakulāni ca| akālamṛtyavo hyete tebhyo bibhyati paṇḍitāḥ”- iti| anyairapyuktaṃ;- “kālaḥ surairapi hi vañcayituṃ na śakyo vakṣye vidhānamapamṛtyuvināśanāya| mṛtyurbhavedyadi kathañcana nāpamṛtyurvyarthāstadā carakasuśrutavāgbhaṭādyāḥ”- iti| tasmāt kālākālayormṛtyurbhavatyeveti| apare, ‘śeṣā āgantavaḥ smṛtāḥ’ ityasyāgrataḥ, “viṣvagvātādibhiryadvaddīpastailādisaṃyutaḥ| nirvātyate kṣaṇāddehī tathaivāgantumṛtyubhiḥ”- iti ślokaṃ paṭhanti, vyākhyānayanti ca- viṣvagvātaḥ prabalavātaḥ| ādiśabdena śalabhādinipātaḥ kathyate||6||-

doṣāgantujamṛtyubhyo rasamantraviśāradau ||7||
rakṣetāṃ nṛpatiṃ nityaṃ yattau [5] vaidyapurohitau |8|

atharvavedo'kālāmṛtyupraśamanahetuḥ, tadaṅgāni ca vaidyapurohitau paṭhataḥ, atastayo rājarakṣaṇe vyāpāraṃ darśayannāha- doṣetyādi| doṣāḥ vātādayaḥ, āgantavo viṣābhighātādayaḥ| rasamantraviśāradāviti yathāsaṅkhyena rasaviśārado vaidyaḥ, mantraviśāradaḥ purodhāḥ| tatra kecit ‘rasaviśārada’ ityatrādiśabdalopādrasavīryavipākaviśāradaḥ; athavā rasaśabdena rasavanti dravyāṇi kathyante, teṣāṃ viśāradaḥ, athavā rasaśabdo'tra viṣavācakaḥ tadviśārada iti kathayanti| viśāradaḥ paṇḍitaḥ| mantrāṇāmṛgyajuḥsāmoktānāmatharvavedoktānāṃ cetyarthaḥ| yattau yatnaparau||7||

brahmā vedāṅgamaṣṭāṅgamāyurvedamabhāṣata ||8||
purohitamate tasmādvarteta bhiṣagātmavān |9|

purohito vedānadhīte, vaidyastu tadaṅgamāyurvedamiti vaidyāt purodhasaḥ prādhānyaṃ darśayannāha- brahmetyādi| brahmā āyurvedamabhāṣata, kiṃviśiṣṭam? aṣṭāṅgaṃ; punaḥ kiṃviśiṣṭaṃ? vedāṅgam| varteta vyavaharedityarthaḥ| ātmavān buddhimān||8||-

saṅkaraḥ sarvavarṇānāṃ praṇāśo dharmakarmaṇām ||9||
prajānāmapi cocchittirnṛpavyasanahetutaḥ |10|

sarvaprajānāṃ rājāyattā sthitiriti rājarakṣaṇaṃ yatnena kāryamiti darśayannāha- saṅkara ityādi|| saṅkaraḥ ekatvaṃ brāhmaṇāśūdrādīnāṃ varṇānāṃ daṇḍābhāvenācāralopāt| dharmakarmaṇāmiti varṇānāmeva dharmasya karmaṇāmanyeṣāmapi| anye tu ‘dharmikarmaṇām’ iti paṭhanti, tatra dharmayogādāśramāścatvāro dharmiṇaḥ, brahmacārigṛhasthavānaprasthayatayaścatvāra āśramāḥ, tatkarmaṇāṃ prāṇāśaḥ| prajānāmucchittiriti daṇḍābhāvena kṛṣipāśupālyavāṇijyarājopasevānāmabhāvāducchittirucchedo bhavatītyarthaḥ| nṛpavyasanahetuta iti varṇasaṅkarādayo'narthā rājavyasanāt kāraṇādbhavanti, tasmādrājā yatnena rakṣaṇīya ityarthaḥ| anye ‘nṛpavyasanahetukā’ iti paṭhanti; tatra ucchittiriti sambadhyate||9||-

puruṣāṇāṃ nṛpāṇāṃ ca kevalaṃ tulyamūrtitā ||10||
ājñā tyāgaḥ kṣamā dhairyaṃ vikramaścāpyamānuṣaḥ |
tasmāddevamivābhīkṣṇaṃ vāṅmanaḥkarmabhiḥ śubhaiḥ ||11||
cintayennṛpatiṃ vaidyaḥ śreyāṃsīcchan vicakṣaṇaḥ |12|

rājño manujatve samāne'pyamānuṣānuvartibhirguṇerdevatvaṃ sādhayannāha- puruṣāṇāmityādi| kevalaṃ niścitaṃ, mūrtirākṛtiḥ; ājñā anullaṅghanīya ādeśaḥ, tyāgaḥ kīrtidharmārthaṃ bahuprakāro vittavisargaḥ, kṣāmā sarvaṃsahatā, dhairyaṃ vyasaneṣu dhīratā, vikramaḥ parākramaḥ, amānuṣaḥ eṣa guṇasamūho mānuṣeṣu na sambhavatītyarthaḥ| tasmādityādi|- abhīkṣṇamanavarataṃ, tasmāt kāraṇāddevamiva nṛpatiṃ vaidyaścintayediti sambandhaḥ| kaiḥ kṛtvā cintayedityāha- vāṅmanaḥ karmabhiḥ śubhairiti|- śubhaiḥ śobhanairityarthaḥ||10-11||

skandhāvāre ca mahati rājagehādanantaram ||12||
bhavetsannihito vaidyaḥ [6] sarvopakaraṇānvitaḥ |
tatrasthamenaṃ dhvajavadyaśaḥkhyātisamucchritam ||13||
upasarpantyamohena viṣaśalyāmayārditāḥ |
svatantrakuśalo'nyeṣu śāstrārtheṣvabahiṣkṛtaḥ ||14||
vaidyo dhvaja ivābhāti nṛpatadvidyapūjitaḥ [7] |15|

vaidyasya santatopayogitvāducitapradeśavāsaṃ nirdiśannāha- skandhāvāra ityādi| skandhāvāre kaṭake| mahati gurutare| anantaramiti avyavadhāne antarāyavarjite| sarvopakaraṇānvita iti yantraśastrakṣārauṣadhāgadādiyuktaḥ| tatretyādi|- tatrasthaṃ rājagṛhasamipastham| enamiti vaidham| dhvajavat patākāvat| nirmalaguṇakīrtanaṃ yaśaḥ, khyātiḥ svābhāvikī stutiḥ, samucchritaṃ vṛddhiṅgatam| upasarpanti samīpaṃ gacchanti| amohena niścintamanasā| viṣaśalyāmayārditā iti arditāḥ pīḍitāḥ; tena viṣārditāḥ śalyārditā āmayārditāścetyarthaḥ| vaidyaṃ bahuśrutaṃ stuvannāha- svatantretyādi| kuśalaḥ pravīṇaḥ| abahiṣkṛta iti nātyantamakuśalaḥ| nṛpatadvidyapūjita iti nṛpaistadvidyaiśca pūjitaḥ sammānitaḥ, tadvidyaiḥ vaidyairityarthaḥ||12-14||-

vaidyo vyādhyupasṛṣṭaśca bheṣajaṃ paricārakaḥ ||15||
ete pādāścikitsāyāḥ karmasādhanahetavaḥ |
guṇavadbhistribhiḥ pādaiścaturtho guṇavān bhiṣak ||16||
vyādhimalpena kālena mahāntamapi sādhayet |17|

pādacatuṣṭayaṃ cikitsāsādhakaṃ darśayannāha- vaidya ityādi| vyādhyupasṛṣṭo rogī; paricārakaḥ śuśrūṣakaḥ puruṣaḥ| karmasādhanahetava iti ārogyahetava ityarthaḥ||15-16||-

vaidyahīnāstrayaḥ pādā guṇavanto'pyapārthakāḥ ||17||
udgātṛhotṛbrahmāṇo yathā'dhvaryuṃ vinā'dhvare |18|

vaidyasya prādhānyaṃ darśayannāha- vaidyetyādi| apārthakā niṣphalāḥ| udgātā sāmāvit, hotā ṛgvedavit, brahmā tu trayīvit, adhvaryuryarvedavidupādhyāyaḥ sarveṣāmupadeṣṭā| adhvare yajñe||17||

vaidyastu guṇavānekastārayedāturāṃ sadā ||18||
plavaṃ pratitarairhīnaṃ [8] karṇadhāra ivāmbhasi |19|

pādatrayavyatireke'pi vaidyakriyāyāḥ sāphalyaṃ darśayannāha- vaidyastvityādi| plavaṃ nāvaṃ, pratitarairantaḥpraviṣṭajalotkṣepakaiḥ [9] puruṣaiḥ| karṇadhāro naukāyā netā||18||

tattvādhigataśāstrārtho [10] dṛṣṭakarmā svayaṅkṛtī ||19||
laghuhastaḥ śuciḥ śūraḥ sajjopaskarabheṣajaḥ |
pratyutpannamatirdhīmān vyavasāyī viśāradaḥ ||20||
satyadharmaparo yaśca sa bhiṣak pāda ucyate |21|

vaidyādīnāṃ guṇānāha- tattvetyādi| tattvādhigataśāstrārtha iti yathāvadadhītaśāstro yathāvajjñātatadarthaścetyarthaḥ| dṛṣṭakarmeti chedyādau snehādau ca kuśala ityarthaḥ| svayaṅkṛtī svaprajñayā cikitsāyāṃ pravṛttaḥ| laghuhasta iti chedyādikriyāsu vepanādidoṣarahitaḥ| śuciriti bāhyābhyantaraśaucayuktaḥ| śūro viṣādarahitaḥ| sajjopaskarabheṣaja iti sajjāni praguṇānyupastarāṇyagropaharaṇīyapaṭhitāni yantraśastrādīni bheṣajāni ca yasya sa tathā| pratyutpannamatiḥ yo'vasthāsu na muhyati| dhīmāniti anuktaduruktohāpohalakṣaṇabuddhiyuktaḥ| vyavasāyī viṣamāvasthāsvapi cikitsāṃ kurvanna muhyati; anye utsāhīti manyante| viśāradaḥ paṇḍitaḥ, kṛcchrapadamātreṣu na muhyati| satyadharmapara iti satyaṃ mithyābhāṣaṇanivṛttiḥ; dharmaḥ anekasādhanasādhyaḥ||19-20||

āyuṣmān sattvavān sādhyo dravyavānātmavānapi ||21||
āstiko vaidyavākyastho vyādhitaḥ pāda ucyate |22|

āyuṣmānityādi|- āyuṣmān dīrghāyuḥ| sattvavān kleśasahiṣṇuḥ| sādhyaḥ sādhyavyādhiyukta ityarthaḥ| ātmavān alolupaḥ| āstika iti dattamasti hutamasti paraloko'styevaṃ matiryasya sa āstikaḥ| vaidyavākyastho vaidyavacanakara ityarthaḥ||21||-

praśastadeśasambhūtaṃ praśaste'hani coddhṛtam ||22||
yuktamātraṃ [11] manaskāntaṃ gandhavarṇarasānvitam |
doṣaghnamaglānikaramavikāri viparyaye |
samīkṣya dattaṃ kāle ca bheṣajaṃ pāda ucyate ||23||

praśastadeśasambhūtamityādi|- yuktamātraṃ yuktapramāṇam| manaskāntaṃ manaḥpriyam| doṣaghnamiti doṣaśabdena vātādaya ucyante taddūṣitā dhātumalāśca, athavā doṣaharaṃ duṣṭiṃ haratyaduṣṭaṃ na ca kopayati| aglānikaraṃ harṣakṣayaṃ na karotītyarthaḥ| avikāri viparyaya iti viparyaye vyāpattāvapyalpavikāraṃ nirvikāraṃ vā| samīkṣya dattamiti kimabhisamīkṣya? āturopakramoktān parīkṣyānāyurādīn deśāntān, kālāvasthāḥ pūrvāhṇādikā vā||22-23||

snigdho'jugupsurbalavān yukto vyādhitarakṣaṇe |
vaidyavākyakṛdaśrāntaḥ pādaḥ paricaraḥ smṛtaḥ ||24||

snigdha itādi| snigdhaḥ prītiyuktaḥ| ajugupsuriti anindakaḥ; etena kaṣṭavyādhāvavasthāsvapyāturaṃ na nindatītyarthaḥ| balavāniti balavattvāt sarvakleśasahaḥ| yukto vyādhitarakṣaṇa iti yukto nipuṇaḥ, etena vyādhitasya rakṣaṇe tatpara ityarthaḥ| vaidyavākyakṛditi vaidyavākyamavikalpaṃ karotītyarthaḥ| aśrāntaḥ iti khaderahitaḥ| ‘pādaiścaturbhiḥ sampūrṇairebhiḥ karma prasidhyati’ iti kecidadhyāyānte paṭhanti| etacca na paṭhanīyaṃ, sarveṣu nibandheṣvadarśanāt||24||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne yuktasenīyo nāma catustriṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ ||34||

iti śrīḍalha(hṇa)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne catustriṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||34||

1. ‘vyādhitānāṃ mārakamupadravamabhidhāya svasthānāmapyāgantunimittaṃ yataḥ prāyo maraṇaṃ bhavati tadupadeṣṭuṃ yuktasenīyo'bhidhīyate| atra hi sūtrasthāne'vaśyaṃ sūtraṇīyaṃ vakṣyamāṇakalpasthānasya bījabhūtaṃ svasthopadravabhūtaṃ viṣamabhidhātavyaṃ, tathā cānye'pamṛtyava ekottaraśatamityādinā sūtrayitavyāḥ’ iti cakraḥ|
2. ‘chāyāśrayavṛkṣādīnāṃ dūṣaṇācchāyāduṣṭirbhavati| duṣṭavṛkṣādicchāyāśrayiṇo vṛkṣādigatena saviṣeṇa pavanena bādhyante| jānīyāditi jñātvā parihārārthaṃ| parihārāśaktau śodhayedapītyuktaṃ| śodhayediti nirviṣīkuryāt’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘praviśan’ iti hastalikhitapustake na paṭhyate|
4. ‘kālasañjñastu’ iti pā.|
5. ‘yatnāt’ iti pā.|
6. ‘nityaṃ’ iti pā.|
7. ‘nṛpatadvidhapūjitaḥ’ iti pā.|
8. ‘praticaraiḥ’ iti pā.|
9. ‘praticaraiḥ’ iti pā.|
10. ‘tīrthādhigataśāstrārthaḥ’ iti pā.|
11. cakrastu ‘alpamātraṃ bahuguṇaṃ’ iti paṭhati, vyākhyānayati ca ‘alpamātramapi praśastaguṇayogādbahuguṇakaram’ iti|
