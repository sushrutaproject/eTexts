--- title: "16. karṇavyadhabandhavidhyadhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 16 order: 16 ---

athātaḥ [1] karṇavyadhabandhavidhimadhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

atrādhikārānuvṛttiḥ sambandhahetuḥ| kathaṃ? svasthādhikāraḥ; svasthasya nirdoṣaśarīrasya karṇavyadhaḥ kartavyaḥ| taduktaṃ bhoje-
“kāleṣveteṣvadoṣāṇāṃ bālānāṃ karṇayorvyadhe| saha gātrairvivardhante karṇapālyaśca khāni ca” iti| tasmādatra svasthavṛttānuvṛttireva sambandhakāraṇam| pañcaiva hi sambandhakāraṇānyuktāni; tadyathā- adhikārānuvṛttiḥ, śeṣatvaṃ, tādarthyaṃ, jātyabhedo, nāmasaṅkīrtanaṃ ceti| bhavati cātra- “tādarthyaṃ jātyabhedaśca śeṣatvaṃ nāmakīrtanam| adhikārānuvṛttiśca pañca sambandhahetavaḥ”- iti||1-2||

rakṣābhūṣaṇanimittaṃ [2] bālasya karṇau vidhyete |
tau ṣaṣṭhe māsi saptame vā śuklapakṣe praśasteṣu tithikaraṇamuhūrtanakṣatreṣu kṛtamaṅgalasvastivācanaṃ dhātryaṅke kumāradharāṅke [3] vā kumāramupaveśya bālakrīḍanakaiḥ pralobhyābhisāntvayan bhiṣagvāmahastenākṛṣya karṇaṃ daivakṛte [4] chidra ādityakarāvabhāsite śanaiḥ śanairdakṣiṇahastenarju vidhyet, pratanukaṃ sūcyā, bahalamārayā, pūrvaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ kumārasya, vāmaṃ kumāryāḥ; tataḥ picuvartiṃ [5] praveśayet [6] ||3||

kinnimittaṃ bālasya karṇau vidhyedityāha- rakṣetyādi| rakṣābhūṣaṇanimittamiti nimittaśabdo rakṣābhūṣaṇābhyāṃ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate; anye tu rakṣaṇārthaṃ bhūṣaṇāni rakṣābhūṣaṇāni,tannimittamityarthaḥ| yadyapi viddhakarṇatvādvraṇini bāle raktamāṃsapriyatvādrakṣobhayamastyeva, tathā'pi vraṇasyālpakālatvāddaivakṛtacchidratvādvā rakṣaṇārthaṃ bhavati| kasmin kāle karṇasya vyadhaḥ kartavya ityāha- tau ṣaṣṭhe māsi saptame vā śuklapakṣa iti; tāviti tau karṇau, ṣaṣṭhe māse saptame veti nāyaṃ janmakālādūrdhvaṃ kintu saṃvatsarāderbhādrapadādyaḥ ṣaṣṭho māso māghaḥ saptamaḥ phālgunastayormadhya ekasminniti| maṅgalaṃ gītādi| svastivācanamāśīrvādoccāraṇam| dhātryaṅke iti dhātrī kumārasya stanapāyinī strī tasyā aṅke, kumāradharāṅke veti bālagrāhipuruṣasyotsaṅge| bālakrīḍanakairiti kṛtrimahastyaśvabalīvardaśukādibhiḥ| asyāgre‘bhakṣyaviśeṣairvā’ iti kecit paṭhanti, bhakṣyaviśeṣairmodakādibhiḥ| daivakṛte chidra iti prāktanajanmakarmakṛte ityarthaḥ| ṛju avakram| karṇaviśeṣeṇa sūcīviśeṣaviṣayaṃ niyamayannāha- pratanukaṃ sūcyeti|- prakarṣeṇa tanuṃ karṇaṃ sūcyā vidhyedityarthaḥ; bahalamārayeti sthūlakarṇamārayā vidhyedityarthaḥ; ārā carmakārāṇāṃ carmabhedanaṃ śastraṃ, bahalaṃ kaṭhinamiti lakṣmaṇaṭippaṇakenoktam| puruṣastrībhedena prākkarṇavyadhane praśastatvena viśeṣamāha- pūrvaṃ dakṣiṇamityādi| karṇe viddhe sati kiṃ kāryamityāha- tata ityādi| viddhvā ca picuvartiṃ praveśayet||3||

śoṇitabahutvena vedanayā cānyadeśaviddhamiti jānīyāt, nirupadravatayā taddeśaviddhamiti ||4||

anyadeśaviddhasya vijñānopāyamāha- śoṇitabahutvenetyādi| daivakṛtacchidraviddhasya vijñānamāha- nirupadravatayetyādi| taddeśaviddhamiti daivakṛtacchidraviddhaṃ, 'jānīyāt' ityanuvartate||4||

tatrājñena yadṛcchayā viddhāsu [7] sirāsu kālikāmarmarikālohitikāsūpadravā [8] bhavanti |
tatra, kālikāyāṃ jvaro dāhaḥ śvayathurvedanā ca bhavati; marmarikāyāṃ vedanā jvaro granthayaśca; lohitikāyāṃ manyāstambhāpatānakaśirograhakarṇaśūlāni bhavanti |
teṣu yathāsvaṃ pratikurvīta ||5||

sirāsu doṣaṃ vaktumāha- tatrājñenetyādi| jñena tu kālikālohitikayovarṇenaiva parihāraḥ kartavyaḥ, marmarikāyāstu varṇānuktau daivakṛtacchidravyadhaneneti| pratyekamupadravān vaktumāha- tatra kālikāyāṃ jvara ityādi| kālikādivyadhopadravacikitsāmāha- teṣu yathāsvamityādi| teṣu jvarādiṣu vyādhiṣu; yathāsvaṃ pratikurvīteti yasya jvarādivyādheryaḥ pratīkārastena taṃ pratikurvītetyarthaḥ||5||

kliṣṭajihmāpraśastasūcīvyadhādgāḍhataravartitvāddoṣasamudāyādapraśastavyadhādvā yatra saṃrambho [9] vedanā vā bhavati tatra vartimupahṛtyāśu madhukairaṇḍamūlamañjiṣṭhāyavatilakalkairmadhughṛtapragāḍhairālepayettāvadyāvat surūḍha iti; surūḍhaṃ cainaṃ punarvidhyet; vidhānaṃ tu pūrvoktameva ||6||

kālikādivyadhādanyathā'pi vyāpadaṃ taccikitsāṃ ca nirdiśannāha- kliṣṭajihmāpraśastetyādi| kliṣṭā amanojñā, jihmā vakratamā, apraśastā yathoktaguṇahīnā| doṣasamudāyāditi doṣaprakopāt| apraśastavyadhādveti adaivakṛtacchidre vyadhād garhitaśastrādivyadhādvā| saṃrambhaḥ śvayathuḥ| kecit 'kliṣṭajihmāpraśastasūcīvyadhādgāḍhataravartitvāt' iti na paṭhanti, ‘doṣasamudāyāt' ityādi ca paṭhanti, ‘surūḍhaṃ cainaṃ punarvidhyet' iti ca na paṭhanti||6||

tatra samyagviddhamāmatailena pariṣecayet, tryahāttryahācca vartiṃ sthūlatarāṃ dadyāt, pariṣekaṃ ca tameva ||7||

durviddhe vyāpaccikitsāṃ pratipādya samyagviddhe cikitsāmāha- tatretyādi| āmataileneti [10] apakvatailena, tilakalkaniṣpīḍanena muṣṭitaḥ parisrutenetyarthaḥ; tameveti āmatailapariṣekamevetyarthaḥ||7||

atha vyapagatadoṣopadrave karṇe vardhanārthaṃ laghu vardhanakaṃ kuryāt ||8||

gatopadrave karṇe vardhanopāyamāha- athetyādi| laghuvardhanakamiti doṣaviśeṣādapāmārganimbakārpāsādīnāṃ [11] kāṣṭhānāmanyatamasya, athavā sīsakādighaṭitaṃ [12] dhattūrapuṣpākṛtiṃ kuryāt||8||

evaṃ vivardhitaḥ karṇaśchidyate tu dvidhā nṛṇām |
doṣato [13] vā'bhighātādvā sandhānaṃ tasya me śṛṇu ||9||

evamityādi| doṣata iti doṣaśabdo vyādhiṣvapi vartate, tena doṣairvātādibhistajjanitavyādhibhirvetyarthaḥ| abhighātādveti patanapīḍanādibhiḥ||9||

tatra [14] samāsena pañcadaśakarṇabandhākṛtayaḥ |
tadyathā- nemisandhānaka utpalabhedyako vallūraka āsaṅgimo gaṇḍakarṇa āhāryo nirvedhimo vyāyojimaḥ kapāṭasandhiko'rdhakapāṭasandhikaḥ saṅkṣipto hīnakarṇo vallīkarṇo yaṣṭikarṇaḥ kākauṣṭhaka iti |
teṣu, pṛthulāyatasamobhayapālirnemisandhānakaḥ, vṛttāyatasamobhayapālirutpalabhedyakaḥ; hrasvavṛttasamobhayapālirvallūrakaḥ; abhyantaradīrghaikapālirāsaṅgimaḥ; bāhyadīrghaikapālirgaṇḍakarṇaḥ; apālirubhayato'pyāhāryaḥ; pīṭhopamapālirubhayataḥ kṣīṇaputrikāśrito nirvedhimaḥ, sthūlāṇusamaviṣamapālirvyāyojimaḥ; abhyantaradīrghaikapāliritarālpapāliḥ kapāṭasandhikaḥ; bāhyadīrghaikapāliritarālpapālirardhakapāṭasandhikaḥ |
tatra daśaite karṇabandhavikalpāḥ sādhyāḥ [15] ; teṣāṃ svanāmabhirevākṛtayaḥ prāyeṇa vyākhyātāḥ saṅkṣiptādayaḥ pañcāsādhyāḥ |
tatra śuṣkaśaṣkulirutsannapāliritarālpapāliḥ saṅkṣiptaḥ [16] ; anadhiṣṭhānapāliḥ paryantayoḥ kṣīṇamāṃso hīnakarṇaḥ; tanuviṣamālpapālirvallīkarṇaḥ; grathitamāṃsastabdhasirātatasūkṣmapāliryaṣṭikarṇaḥ; nirmāṃsasaṅkṣiptāgrālpaśoṇitapāliḥ kākauṣṭhaka iti |
baddheṣvapi tu śophadāharāgapākapiḍakāsrāvayuktā na siddhimupayānti ||10||

idānīṃ sandheyasya [17] sandhānāyāsandheyasya parihārāya cāha- tatretyādi| samāsena saṅkṣepeṇa, vistareṇa tu karṇabandhā amitāḥ, yadyapi pañcadaśasu karṇeṣu madhye daśaiva bandhyāḥ, tathā'pi bandhyabāhulyādabandhyeṣvapi bandhaśabdo vartate; yathā 'chatriṇo yānti' ityatra chatribāhulyādacchatriṣvapi chatrivyapadeśaḥ| 'tatra samāsena pañcadaśa karṇākṛtaya' ityanye paṭhanti| tāneva bandhānnāmabhirnirdiśannāha- tadyathā- nemisandhānaka ityādi| tadyatheti nidarśane| tatrāmī bandhavikalpāḥ nityakarmadarśanādabhyāsācca jāyante| yadyapyeṣāṃ nāmabhirevākṛtayaḥ prāyeṇa vyākhyātāstathā'pi lakṣaṇasūtraṃ nirdiśannāha- teṣu pṛthulāyatetyādi| pṛthule vistīrṇe, āyate dīrghe, same tulye, itthambhūte dve pālyau yasya sa tathā; pāliḥ karṇalatikā| nemiḥ cakradhārā, tadvat sandhānaṃ yasya sa nemisandhānakaḥ| vallūraka iti vallūraṃ śuṣkamāṃsaṃ, śuṣkamāṃsavadanekadhā vipāṭya yaḥ sandhīyate sa vallūrakaḥ| `samunnatasamobhayapāliḥ' ityeke paṭhanti| etat karṇabandhatrayaṃ samobhayapāliviṣayatayā jñeyam| abhyantaradīrghaiketyādi yastvabhyantaradīrghaikapāliḥ kathañcicchinno bhavati tasyetarapālyabhāvādbāhyapālimūle vyāsaṅga [18] āsaṅgimaḥ| gaṇḍakarṇa iti sānubandhakapolamāṃsamutkṛtya bāhyadīrghapālyagrabhāgaḥ sandhīyate yasmin sa gaṇḍakarṇaḥ| etadbandhadvayamekapālikam| apāliriti īṣatpāliḥ| ubhayato gaṇḍapradeśāt sānubandhamāṃsamāhṛtya pālyākāraṃ kṛtvā sandhānaṃ yasya kriyate sa āhāryaḥ| pīṭhopamapāliriti yasya tu pālidvayaṃ mūlata eva chinnaṃ bhavati sa pīṭhopamapāliḥ, nirvedhima iti vedhena niṣpanna ityarthaḥ, pālidvayahīnaṃ karṇaṃ putrikāyā upari viddhvā niṣpādanīya ityarthaḥ; idamapālikaṃ karṇabandhadvayam| sthūlāṇusametyādi sthūlāṇutvābhyāmekā pāliḥ samā, aparā viṣamā, te ullikhya nānāprakārayojanādvyāyojima iti| abhyantaradīrghaikapāliriti abhyantaradīrghaikapāliritarālpapālyā saha bahiḥ sandhānāt kapāṭavatsandhānaṃ yatra sa kapāṭasandhikaḥ| ardhakapāṭasandhika iti bāhyadīrghaikapāliritarālpapālyā sahābhyantarasandhānenārdhakapāṭavadyatra sandhīyate so'rdhakapāṭasandhikaḥ| idaṃ karṇabandhatrayaṃ viṣamobhayapālikam| tatretyādi tatra teṣu pañcadaśasu karṇabandhavikalpeṣu madhye| teṣāmākārajñānamadhikṛtyāha- teṣāṃ nāmabhirevetyādi| śeṣāṇāṃ parihārārthamāha- saṅkṣiptādaya ityādi| teṣāṃ lakṣaṇamāha- tatra śuṣketyādi| śuṣkaśaṣkuliriti śuṣkabāhyapālyadhiṣṭhānaḥ, itarālpapāliriti itarā gaṇḍadeśānugatā alpā pāliryasya sa tathā| anadhiṣṭhānapāliriti alpatvena avidyamāne adhiṣṭhānapālī yasya sa tathā, paryantayośceti gaṇḍabāhyapārśvayoḥ| viṣamā tanutvasthūlatvābhyām| ta ete pañca karṇabandhāḥ sapālayo'pyasādhyāḥ, asādhyā iti sādhanāyāśakyāḥ; kecidamuṃ pāṭhaṃ na paṭhanti| eteṣāṃ pañcānāmasādhyatve hetumāha- baddheṣvapītyādi| anye tu śophādyupadravopetānāṃ pūrveṣāṃ daśānāṃ bandhānāṃ sādhyānāmapyasādhyatvamāhuḥ||10||

bhavanti cātra-
yasya pālidvayamapi karṇasya na bhavediha |
karṇapīṭhaṃ same madhye tasya viddhvā vivardhayet ||11||

gadyoktānāṃ karṇabandhānāṃ madhye nirvedhimasya sandhānasūtramāha- yasyetyādi| iheti karṇabandhamadhye| karṇapīṭhaṃ putrikoparipradeśam||11||

bāhyāyāmiha dīrghāyāṃ sandhirābhyantaro bhavet |
ābhyantarāyāṃ dīrghāyāṃ bāhyasanghirudāhṛtaḥ ||12||

vyāyojimakapāṭasandhikārdhakapāṭasandhikānāṃ sandhānasūtramāha- bāhyāyāmityādi| yadā bāhyā pālirdīrghā bhavettadā ābhyantaraḥ sandhiḥ kartavya ityarthaḥ, ābhyantarā ca pāliryadā dīrghā syāttadā bāhyasandhirvidheya iti; bāhyāyāṃ gaṇḍāddūravartinyāṃ pālyāṃ dīrghāyām, ābhyantaro gaṇḍasannikṛṣṭo gaṇḍasaṃśrayī vetyarthaḥ| "kapāṭasandhikārdhakapāṭasandhikayorevedaṃ sandhānasūtraṃ, na tu vyāyojimasya" ityanye||12||

ekaiva tu bhavet pāliḥ sthūlā pṛthvī sthirā ca yā |
tāṃ dvidhā pāṭayitvā tu chittvā copari sandhayet ||13||

idānīmāsaṅgimasya sandhānasūtramāha- ekaivetyādi| "vallūrakasyedaṃ sandhānasūtraṃ sambhāvyate" ityanye vadanti||13||

gaṇḍādutpāṭya māṃsena sānubandhena jīvatā |
karṇapālīmapālestu kuryānnirlikhya śāstravit ||14||

āhāryasya sandhisūtraṃ nirdeṣṭumāha- gaṇḍādutpāṭhyetyādi| sānubandheneti gaṇḍapradeśalagnenetyarthaḥ| jīvateti śoṇitasahitenetyarthaḥ| anye tu "gaṇḍakarṇasyāpyetat sandhānasūtram" iti vadanti||14||

ato'nyatamaṃ bandhaṃ cikīrṣuragropaharaṇīyoktopasambhṛtasambhāraṃ [19] viśeṣataścātropaharet [20] surāmaṇḍaṃ kṣīramudakaṃ dhānyāmlaṃ kapālacūrṇaṃ ceti |
tato'ṅganāṃ [21] puruṣaṃ vā grathitakeśāntaṃ laghu bhuktavantamāptaiḥ suparigṛhītaṃ ca kṛtvā, bandhamupadhārya, chedyabhedyalekhyavyadhanairupapannairupapādya, karṇaśoṇitamavekṣya [22] duṣṭamaduṣṭaṃ veti; tatra vātaduṣṭe dhānyāmloṣṇodakābhyāṃ pittaduṣṭe śītodakapayobhyāṃ śleṣmaduṣṭe surāmaṇḍoṣṇodakābhyāṃ prakṣālya karṇau, punaravalikhyānunnatamahīnamaviṣamaṃ ca karṇasandhiṃ sanniveśya, sthitaraktaṃ sandadhyāt |
tato madhughṛtenābhyajya picuprotayoranyatareṇāvaguṇṭhya sūtreṇānavagāḍhamanatiśithilaṃ ca baddhvā kapālacūrṇenāvakīryācārikamupadiśet, dvivraṇīyoktena ca vidhānenopacaret ||15||

ataḥ paraṃ bandhavidhiḥ| agropaharaṇīyoktopasambhṛtasambhāramiti agropaharaṇīyokto yantraśastrādirupa samīpe sambhṛtaḥ samyakkṛtvā dhṛtaḥ sambhāraḥ samūho yatra| surāmaṇḍaṃ madyasyoparyacchabhāgaṃ, kapālacūrṇaṃ navamṛnmayabhāṇḍakhaṇḍacūrṇam| tato'ṅganāṃ puruṣaṃ veti aṅganāyā bhūṣaṇaprādhānyāt prāṅnirdeśaḥ| grathitakeśāntamiti grathitakeśāvayavam, anyathā keśavyākulatayā karmābhighātaḥ syādbandhaviśleṣo vā| laghubhuktavantamiti bhuktavān śastrakarmapīḍāṃ sahate, āhāralaghutvādvedanayā rātrijāgaritavato'pi nājīrṇabādhā syāt| āptaiḥ suparigṛhītaṃ ca kṛtveti āptairhitaiḥ| bandhamupadhāryeti ayaṃ bandho'tra kartavya iti niścitya| chedyādyairupapannairiti yuktaiḥ| upapādyeti saṃyojya, sanniveśya āropya, sandadhyāt sandhānaṃ kuryāt| picuprotayoriti tūlakarpaṭayorityarthaḥ| avaguṇṭhyeti āveṣṭhya| ācārikamiti āhāravihārākhyam| dvivraṇīyoktena vidhāneneti ālepanādinā||15||

bhavati cātra-
vighaṭṭanaṃ divāsvapnaṃ vyāyāmamatibhojanam |
vyavāyamagnisantāpaṃ vākśramaṃ ca vivarjayet ||16||

parihāryāṇyupadiśannāha- vighaṭṭanamityādi| vighaṭṭanaṃ cālanam||16||

na cāśuddharaktamatipravṛttaraktaṃ kṣīṇaraktaṃ vā sandadhyāt |
sa hi vātaduṣṭe rakte rūḍho'pi paripuṭanavān, pittaduṣṭe dāhapākarāgavedanāvān, śleṣmaduṣṭe stabdhaḥ kaṇḍūmān, atipravṛttarakte śyāvaśophavān, kṣīṇo'lpamāṃso na vṛddhimupaiti ||17||

parihāryasāmānyādanyadapi viruddhaṃ pratipādayannāha- na cetyādi|
aśuddharaktamiti duṣṭaraktam| atipravṛttaraktamiti śuddharaktamapi yadā'tipravṛttaṃ syāttadā na sandheyam| aśuddharaktasandhāne doṣamāha- sa hi vātaduṣṭe ityādi| paripuṭanaṃ tvaca uccaṭanaṃ, miśrakādhyāyoktaḥ saśophaḥ karṇalatikārogo vā| atipravṛttaraktasya sandhāne doṣamāha- atipravṛttarakte iti; atisravadrakte ityarthaḥ| śyāvaśopho'tra vātakṛtaḥ| kṣīṇakarṇasya sandhāne doṣamāha- kṣīṇa ityādi| kṣīṇo'tra śuddharaktātipravṛttyā; na tu doṣopadravaiḥ, sadoṣopadravavardhane chidyata eva||17||

(āmatailena [23] trirātraṃ pariṣecayet, trirātrācca picuṃ parivartayet) |
sa yadā surūḍho nirupadravaḥ savarṇo bhavati tadainaṃ śanaiḥ śanairabhivardhayet |
ato'nyathā saṃrambhadāhapākarāgavedanāvān, punaśchidyate vā ||18||
athāsyāpraduṣṭasyābhivardhanārthamabhyaṅgaḥ |
tadyathā- godhāpratudaviṣkirānūpaudakavasāmajjānau payaḥ sarpistailaṃ gaurasarṣapajaṃ ca yathālābhaṃ sambhṛtyārkālarkabalātibalānantāpāmārgāśvagandhāvidārigandhākṣīraśuklājalaśūkamadhuravargapayasyāprativāpaṃ tailaṃ vā pācayitvā svanuguptaṃ nidadhyāt ||19||
