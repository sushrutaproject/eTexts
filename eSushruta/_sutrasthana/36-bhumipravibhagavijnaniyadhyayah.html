--- title: "36. bhūmipravibhāgavijñānīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 36 order: 36 ---

athāto bhūmipravibhāgīyamadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

bhūmeḥ prakṛṣṭo vibhāgo bhūmipravibhāgaḥ, bhūmipravibhāgārtho'dhyāyo bhūmipravibhāgīyaḥ| pūrvādhyāye bhūmiparīkṣā bhūtakāryaiḥ śītoṣṇavarṣādibhistathā parvatavṛkṣādibhiḥ kṛtā, iha tvadhyāye bhūtaguṇaireva parīkṣā kartavyeti| kecidatrāntarāle miśrakādhyāyaṃ paṭhanti; tanna, pūrvācāryairapaṭhitatvāt||1-2||

śvabhraśarkarāśmaviṣamavalmīkaśmaśānāghātanadevatāyatanasikatābhiranupahatāmanūṣarāmabhaṅgurāmadūrodakāṃ [2] snigdhāṃ prarohavatīṃ mṛdvīṃ sthirāṃ samāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ gaurīṃ lohitāṃ vā bhūmimauṣadhagrahaṇāya [3] parīkṣeta |
tasyāṃ jātamapi kṛmiviṣaśastrātapapavanadahanatoyasambādhamārgairanupahatamekarasaṃ [4] puṣṭaṃ pṛthvavagāḍhamūlamudīcyāṃ cauṣadhamādadītetyeṣa bhūmiparīkṣāviśeṣaḥ sāmānyaḥ ||3||

sa bhūmivibhāgo dvividhaḥ- sāmānyo viśiṣṭaśca| tatra prathamaṃ sāmānyameva bhūmivibhāgamāha- śvabhretyādi| anupahatāmiti śvabhrādibhiḥ sikatāntaiḥ saha pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| śvabhraśarkarāśmabhirviṣamo nimnonnataḥ, tenānupahatāmiti sambandhaḥ| śvabhraṃ bilaṃ; śarkarā pakvabhāṇḍasūkṣmakhaṇḍaṃ śarkareti loke; aśmā pāṣāṇaḥ; valmīketi, nanu valmīkamūrdhanyeva śvetakapotī gṛhyate, tat kathaṃ valmīkajātauṣadhiniṣedhaḥ? satyam, āśīviṣaviṣaistasyā divyauṣadhervīryaṃ na hanyate, athavā tatraiva sā viśeṣeṇa vīryavatī bhavatityadoṣaḥ; śmaśānaṃ mṛtamartyadāhasthānam; āghātanaṃ [5] vadhasthānaṃ; sikatā vālukā; anupahatām; anupadrutām; anūṣarām ūṣaravarjitām; abhaṅgurāṃ rājirahitāṃ; adūrodakatvena sādhāraṇatvamuktaṃ; snigdhāṃ cikkaṇāṃ; prarohavatīṃ bījāṅkurajananīṃ; mṛdvīṃ komalāṃ; asthirāṃ vātatoyādibhiracalitamṛttikām; samām animnonnatāṃ; gaurīṃ suvarṇavarṇāṃ; auṣadhagrahaṇāyeti auṣadhagrahaṇārtham| na kevalaṃ bhūmiguṇaireva bheṣajāni vibhajanīyāni, kiṃ tarhi svadoṣaguṇairapīti darśayannāha- tasyāṃ jātamityādi| krimyādibhiḥ sambādhamārgāntaiḥ saha pratyekamanupahataśabdaḥ sambadhyate; sambādhaḥ saṅkaṭaḥ| ekarasam utkṛṣṭarasam| pṛthvavagāḍhamūlaṃ ceti pṛthu pṛthuvalkalaṃ, avagāḍhamūlaṃ bhūmyantaḥsthitaṃ mūlaṃ yasya tattathā; cakārādanupahatapraśastamūlatvaṃ ca samuccīyate| udīcyāmiti uttarasyāṃ diśi, etenottarābhimukho bhūtvā gṛhṇīyādityarthaḥ viśeṣo bhedaḥ||3||

viśeṣatastu [6] tatra, aśmavatī sthirā gurvī śyāmā kṛṣṇā vā sthūlavṛkṣaśasyaprāyā svaguṇabhūyiṣṭhā; snigdhā śītalā''sannodakā snigdhaśasyatṛṇakomalavṛkṣaprāyā śuklā'mbuguṇabhūyiṣṭhā; nānāvarṇā laghvaśmavatī praviralālpapāṇḍuvṛkṣaprarohā'gniguṇabhūyiṣṭhā; rūkṣā bhasmarāsabhavarṇā tanuvṛkṣālparasakoṭaravṛkṣaprāyā'nilaguṇabhūyiṣṭhā; mṛdvī samā śvabhravatyavyaktarasajalā sarvato'sāravṛkṣā mahāparvatavṛkṣaprāyā śyāmā cākāśaguṇabhūyiṣṭhā ||4||

sāmānyabhūmerguṇaṃ pradarśya viśiṣṭaṃ pratibhūtaṃ guṇaṃ nirdiśannāha- viśeṣatastvityādi| svaguṇabhūyiṣṭhetyarthaḥ| tanuvṛkṣā hrasvavṛkṣetyarthaḥ| asya pāñcabhautikaviśeṣaguṇakathanasyāyamabhiprāyaḥ-
pṛthivyādyutkaṭatvena dravyāṇāṃ svasyāṃ svasyāṃ bhūmau jātaṃ grāhyam, evaṃ balavattarāṇyauṣadhāni bhavanti||4||

atra kecidāhurācāryāḥ- prāvṛḍvarṣāśaraddhemantavasantagrīṣmeṣu yathāsaṅkhyaṃ mūlapatratvakkṣīrasāraphalānyādadīteti; tattu na samyak, saumyāgneyatvājjagataḥ |
saumyānyauṣadhāni [7] saumyeṣvṛtuṣvādadīta, āgneyānyāgneyeṣu; evamavyāpannaguṇāni bhavanti |
saumyānyauṣadhmani saumyeṣvṛtuṣu gṛhītāni somaguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ bhūmau jātānyatimadhurasnigdhaśītāni jāyante |
etena śeṣaṃ vyākhyātam ||5||

saumyeṣu varṣāhemantaśiśireṣvityarthaḥ| āgneyeṣu śaradvasantagrīṣmeṣu| ātyayike kārye punaḥ pūrvāhṇādiṣu vasantādiṣu yathāyathamauṣadhānyādadīta| ayamabhiprāyaḥ- āgneyānyauṣadhānyāgneyeṣu ṛtuṣu gṛhītānyāgneyaguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ bhūmāvatikaṭurūkṣoṣṇāni jāyanta iti||5||

tatra pṛthivyambuguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ bhūmau jātāni virecanadravyāṇyādadīta [8] , agnyākāśamārutaguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ vamanadravyāṇi, ubhayaguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāmubhayatobhāgāni, ākāśaguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ saṃśamanāni, evaṃ balavattarāṇi bhavanti ||6||

vamanādidravyāṇi kīdṛśāyāṃ bhūmau grahītavyānītyāha- tatretyādi| saṃśamanānīti yaddravyaṃ na vāmayati na virecayati vyādhinā sahaikībhūya tatsthameva vyādhimupaśamayati tat saṃśamanamucyate||6||

sarvāṇyeva cābhinavāni, anyatra madhughṛtaguḍapippalīviḍaṅgebhyaḥ ||7||
viḍaṅgaṃ pippalī kṣaudraṃ sarpiścāpyanavaṃ hitam |
śeṣamanyatvabhinavaṃ gṛhṇīyāddoṣavarjitam ||8||
(sarvāṇyeva sakṣīrāṇi vīryavanti;) teṣāmasampattāvatikrāntasaṃvatsarāṇyādadīteti [9] ||9||

sarvāṇītyādi| sarvāṇyeva cābhinavāni ‘balavattarāṇī’ ityatrāpi sambadhyate| abhinavamatra pariṇatikālānantaratvamabhipretaṃ, na punarjanmanavatvam| etasyaivāpavādasūtraṃ darśayannāha- anyatretyādi| doṣavarjitamiti krimiviṣaśastrādyupaghātādidoṣaistyaktam| atrāntare vṛddhaiḥ paṭhitaḥ pāṭhaḥ- sarvāṇītyādi| tatra sakṣīrāṇi sarasānītyarthaḥ| madhvādīni yathoktāni yadā na prāpyante tadā kiṃ kartavyamityāha- teṣāmityādi [10] | teṣāṃ madhughṛtādīnām asampattau atyantapurāṇānāmaprāptau||7-9||

bhavanti cātra-
gopālāstāpasā vyādhā ye cānye vanacāriṇaḥ |
mūlāhārāśca [11] ye tebhyo bheṣajavyaktiriṣyate ||10||

kutaḥ punarbheṣajāni jñātavyānītyāha- gopālā ityādi| gopālā gomahiṣyajādipālakāḥ procyante, tāpasāḥ tapasvino jaṭādhāriprabhṛtayaḥ, vyādhā vāgurikādayaḥ, vanacāriṇo mlecchādayaḥ||10||

sarvāvayavasādhyeṣu palāśalavaṇādiṣu |
vyavasthito na kālo'sti tatra [12] sarvo vidhīyate ||11||

tatra kecidāhurācāryāḥ prāvṛḍvarṣāśaraddhemantetyādi gadyena vyabhicārya, ślokairvyābhicārayitumāha- sarvāvayavasādhyeṣvityādi| sarvāvayavasādhyeṣu aśeṣamūlādyavayavagrāhyeṣu yogeṣvityarthaḥ| palāśalavaṇādiṣu patralavaṇādiṣu; patralavaṇayogaśca vātavyādhipaṭhito jñeyaḥ| tatra sarvo vidhīyata iti tatra teṣu patralavaṇādiyogeṣu, sarvaḥ samasto'pi kālaḥ prāvṛḍādirvidhīyate kathyata ityarthaḥ| etena sarvasminnapi kāle melāpakaḥ kriyata ityarthaḥ||11||

gandhavarṇarasopetā ṣaḍvidhā bhūmiriṣyate |
tasmādbhūmisvabhāvena bījinaḥ ṣaḍrasāyutāḥ ||12||

yena ca hetunā svaguṇaparityāgastamāha- gandhetyādi| gandhavarṇarasopeteti luptādiśabdanirdeśād gandhavarṇarasasparśaśabdopetā kathitā| ṣaḍvidhā pañcabhūtātmikā sarvalakṣaṇā ca| bījino vṛkṣādayaḥ| te ca bhūmisvabhāvena yutāḥ
ṣaḍrasopetā bhavanti; athavā ete ṣaḍrasā bhūmisvabhāvena yutā militāḥ santo bījino bhavanti vṛkṣādirūpā bhavantītyarthaḥ||12||

avyaktaḥ kila toyasya raso niścayaniścitaḥ |
rasaḥ sa eva cāvyakto vyakto bhūmirasādbhavet ||13||

yadi dravyāṇāṃ pariṇāmaviśeṣeṇa madhurādirasatā tatkathamucyate āpyo rasa ityāha- avyakta ityādi| avyakta iti madhurādibhāvenāparisphuṭa ityarthaḥ| kila avadhāraṇe| niścayaniścito raso'stītyarthaḥ| yadyevaṃ madhurādibhedānupapattirityata āha- rasaḥ sa evetyādi| sa evāvyakto raso vyakto bhavati madhurādibhāvaṃ yāti| kasmāt? bhūmirasāditi sambandhaḥ||13||

sarvalakṣaṇasampannā bhūmiḥ sādhāraṇā smṛtā |
dravyāṇi yatra tatraiva tadguṇāni viśeṣataḥ ||14||

bhūmidravyayoḥ sādhāraṇayoḥ kāraṇamāha- sarvetyādi|
sarvalakṣaṇasampanneti pṛthivyādipañcamahābhūtalakṣaṇayuktetyarthaḥ| tadguṇāni sādhāraṇāni dravyāṇītyarthaḥ||14||

vigandhenāparāmṛṣṭamavipannaṃ rasādibhiḥ |
navaṃ dravyaṃ purāṇaṃ vā grāhyamevaṃ vinirdiśet ||15||

navaṃ purāṇaṃ vā kīdṛśaṃ dravyaṃ gṛhyata ityāha- vigandhenetyādi| vigandhenāparāmṛṣṭamiti virodhinā gandhenāspṛṣṭamityarthaḥ| avipannaṃ rasādibhiriti akṣīṇaṃ rasādibhirityarthaḥ||15||

jaṅgamānāṃ vayaḥsthānāṃ raktaromanakhādikam |
kṣīramūtrapurīṣāṇi jīrṇāhāreṣu saṃharet ||16||

sthāvarāṇi niyamya jaṅgamāni niyantumāha- jaṅgamānāmityādi| vayaḥsthānāmiti taruṇānāṃ, na bālavṛddhānāmityarthaḥ||16||

plotamṛdbhāṇḍaphalakaśaṅkuvinyastabheṣajam |
praśastāyāṃ diśi śucau bheṣajāgāramiṣyate ||17||

gṛhītauṣadhasthāpanopāyaṃ darśayannāha- plotetyādi| plotaḥ; karpaṭaḥ| mṛdbhāṇḍaṃ mṛttikāpātraṃ, phalakaṃ paṭṭakaṃ, śaṅkuḥ kīlakaḥ eteṣu plotādiṣu vinyastaṃ ghṛtamauṣadhaṃ yasmin gṛhe tadbheṣajāgāraṃ bheṣajagṛhamiṣyata iti sambandhaḥ| praśastāyāṃ pūrvasyāmuttarasyāṃ vā| śucau deśe| bhūmipravibhāgīyādhyāye nibandheṣu vyatyayena pāṭho dṛśyate; asmābhistu vṛddhakramarītyaiva pāṭho likhita iti| sarvāvayavasādhyeṣvityādi yāvannavaṃ dravyaṃ purāṇaṃ veti pāṭhaṃ kecidācāryā na paṭhanti||17||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne bhūmipravibhāgīyo nāma ṣaṭtriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||36||

iti śrīḍalha(hṇa)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśruta vyākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne ṣaṭtriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ||36||

1. ‘miśrake bheṣajadravyamuktaṃ, dravyasaṅgrahaṇīyādau ca vaktavyaṃ, tacca praśastabhūmijātameva kāryakaraṃ bhavati, ato bheṣajagrahaṇārthaṃ praśastāpraśastabhūmivibhāgīyo'bhidhīyate| bhūmivibhāgopadarśanādhikāreṇa cānyadapi jantujagdhatvādibheṣajadoṣaguṇavarṇanamiha bhaviṣyati| ‘bhūmipravibhāgavijñānīyaṃ’ iti pā.|
2. ‘śvabhrādayaścālpavīryotpādakatayā bheṣajopaghātakāḥ, valmīkādayastu aprāśastyenāpraśastāḥ, aprāśastyaṃ ca teṣāṃ vacanādevonnīyate’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘bhūmimauṣadhārthaṃ’ iti pā.|
4. ‘ātapapavanayoratiyogena upaghātaḥ, toyasambādho'tijalasambādhaḥ| ekarasamiti vṛkṣāntarānākrāntam| avagāḍhamityanuttānamūlam’ iti cakraḥ|
5. ‘ādyatanaṃ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘viśeṣatastvityādinā bhūmiparīkṣāviśeṣaḥ kathyate| ayaṃ ca vakṣyamāṇo viśeṣaḥ ‘pṛthivyambuguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ bhūmau jātāni virecanadravyāṇyādadīta’ ityādau prayojanavān| sthireti kaṭhinā| nānāvarṇeti tejaso nānāvarṇakāraṇatvāt| praroho'ṅkuraḥ|’ iti cakraḥ|
7. ‘bheṣajadravyamapi kiñcit saumyaṃ kiñcidāgneyam| tena yat saumyaṃ tat somaguṇātirikteṣu saumyeṣu ṛtuṣu sampūrṇavīryatayā grāhyam, evamāgneyamāgneyeṣu, sarvathā mūlādyaṅge grāhyam| yattu grīṣmādiṣu mūlādigrahaṇaṃ tadanupapattikatvādasādhu| etadeva vyākaroti- tatra saumyetyādi| saumyānāṃ saumyagrahaṇe guṇātiśayaṃ darśayan samānanyāyatayā āgneyānyagniguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāṃ bhūmau kaṭutarāṇi rūkṣatarāṇi ca bhavanti’ iti cakraḥ| ‘teṣāṃ śākhāpalāśamaciraprarūḍhaṃ varṣāvasantayorgrāhyaṃ, grīṣme mūlāni śiśire ca śīrṇaprarūḍhaparṇānāṃ, śaradi tvakkandakṣīrāṇi, hemante sārāṇi, yathartu puṣpaphalam’ (ca. ka. a. 1) iti carakaḥ|
8. ‘virecanamadhobhāgaṃ tadadhogāmipṛthivyambuguṇabhūmiṣṭhāyāṃ balavattaraṃ bhavati| anilāgnī tūrdhvagau, tena tadguṇabhūyiṣṭhāyāmūrdhvagaṃ vamanaṃ balavattaraṃ bhavati, ubhayatobhāgaharāṇi vamanavirecanakarāṇi arkakṣīrādīni| ākāśasya sarvagatatvena śamanauṣadhakāritvaṃ, śamanaṃ hi sarvagataṃ doṣaṃ tatraiva śamayati| abhinavānīti abhinavatvamiha sampūrṇavīryatākāratvam| tena yattu mūlake bilve ca bālāvasthāyāmeva praśastatvaṃ tattvavasthāgṛhītamevābhinavatvaṃ jñeyam’ iti cakraḥ|
9. ‘anatikrāntasaṃvatsarāṇi’ iti pā.|
10. teṣāṃ sakṣīrāṇāṃ, asampattau aprāptau|
11. ‘mūlānyāharanti kāryārthamiti mūlahārāḥ| bheṣajavyaktiriti bheṣajākāranāmajñānaṃ, rasavīryādidvārā bheṣajavyaktiḥ śāstrādeva jñeyā’ iti cakraḥ|
12. tatra sarvo vivīyata iti sarva eva kālaḥ palāśalavaṇārthaṃ gṛhyamāṇapalāśe gṛhyate, yadi kālaniyamenāṅgagrahaṇaṃ tadā militavṛkṣagrāhakaḥ kālo nāstīti tatprayogo na syāt| vayaḥsthānāmiti taruṇānāṃ, bālavṛddhayostu asampūrṇadhātutvādevāpraśastatvam’ iti cakraḥ|
