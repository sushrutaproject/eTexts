--- title: "30. pañcendriyārthavipratipattyadhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 30 order: 30 ---

athātaḥ pañcendriyārthavipratipattimadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

pañcendriyāṇi śrotrādīni, teṣāmarthāḥ śabdādayaḥ, arthānāṃ śabdādīnāṃ vipratipattirviparītāvabodho hīnātiyogenetyarthaḥ| pañcagrahaṇaṃ karmendriyāṇāmubhayātmakasya manasaśca niṣedhārthaṃ; karmendriyāṇāṃ śarīragrahaṇenaiva grahaṇāt; manasastvindriyagrahaṇenaiva grahaṇaṃ, manovyatirekeṇendriyāṇāṃ viṣayagrahaṇābhāvāt||1-2||

śarīraśīlayoryasya [2] prakṛtervikṛtirbhavet |
tattvariṣṭaṃ samāsena, vyāsatastu nibodha me ||3||

prāgadhyāye dūtādijñeyabāhyāriṣṭaṃ pratipādyāsminnadhyāye ābhyantaramariṣṭamāśrayabhedena trividhaṃ saṅkṣepeṇa darśayannāha- śarīraśīlayorityādi| śarīraṃ pāñcabhautikaṃ prāṇikāyaḥ, śīlaṃ mānaso bhāvaḥ, anye tu `śīlaṃ samādhānayuktaṃ manaḥ' ityācakṣate; prakṛtiḥ svābhāvikaḥ prāṇino nijo dharmaḥ; eteṣāṃ śarīraśīlaprakṛtīnāṃ trayāṇāṃ bhāvānāṃ vikṛtirvaiparītyaṃ yadbhavettadariṣṭaṃ maraṇalakṣaṇaṃ bhavati| samāsena saṅkṣepeṇa| anye tu `śarīraśīlayordvayoryā [3] prakṛtistasyā yā vikṛtistadariṣṭaṃ maraṇalakṣaṇaṃ bhavati' ityācakṣate| prakṛteḥ prastāvāt kiñcidvicāraḥ kriyate, sā hi prakṛtirjātyādīn ṣaḍupādhīnapekṣate| taduktaṃ carake- “prakṛtirhi jātiprasaktā ca kulaprasaktā ca deśānupātinī ca kālānupātinī ca vayonupātinī ca pratyātmaniyatā ca”- (ca. i. a. 1) iti| sarvabhedeṣu cakārakaraṇamanyonyasamāveśārthaṃ; tatra jātiprasaktā nāma tāsu tāsu manuṣyādijātiṣu te te karacaraṇādayaḥ svabhāvaviśeṣāḥ; vacaneṣu ca nānāvividhā eva vyaktāvyaktādayo dharmāḥ, manasi ca dharmādharmamaithunāhāraprabhṛtayo'pi bhāvāḥ; kulaprasaktā nāma dvijakule tapaḥpriyatādayaḥ, kṣatriyakule samarapriyatādayo dharmaviśeṣā bhavanti; deśānupātinī nāma lāṭakarṇāṭādīnāṃ kāye vāci manasi ca te te svabhāvaviśeṣā viśeṣeṇa bhavanti; kālānupātinī nāma dehādiṣu daurbalyādyādānādyapekṣaṃ, gadeṣu ca doṣamalādyapekṣiṇī; vayonupātinī nāma bālyādyavasthāsu bālādau saukumāryādayo dharmāḥ; eteṣu triṣvanupātiśabdaprayogāt sañcāritvaṃ svabhāvaguṇānāmiṣṭaṃ; pratyātmaniyateti ātmanyātmani niyatā pratipuruṣaniyatetyarthaḥ| te punaḥ kāye vāci manasi ca svabhāvaviśeṣāḥ pratipuruṣabhedabhinnā dṛśyante; evaṃ tāvat ṣoḍhā prakṛtirbhavatyeva| anye tu “saptamyapi bhavet prāyo dehe lakṣaṇahaitukī| uttamādhamamadhyāyurliṅgānāṃ kāraṇaṃ hi sā”- iti manyante| anye tu prakṛtiṃ sattvarajastamomayīṃ vātapittaśleṣmamayīṃ vā manyante| tasyā [4] vikṛtirvaiparītyamanyathābhāvaḥ||3||

śṛṇoti vividhāñ śabdān yo divyānāmabhāvataḥ |
samudrapurameghānāmasampattau ca niḥsvanān ||4||
tān svanānnāvagṛhṇāti manyate cānyaśabdavat |
grāmyāraṇyasvanāṃścāpi viparītāñ śṛṇoti ca ||5||
dviṣacchabdeṣu ramate suhṛcchabdeṣu kupyati |
na śṛṇoti ca yo'kasmāttaṃ bruvanti gatāyuṣam ||6||

āturagatagrāhyaviruddhaśabdapratipattiṃ nirdiśannāha- śṛṇotītyādi| vividhān bahuprakārān sañjalpapāṭhagītavādyādīn| divyānāṃ siddhakinnaragandharvādīnām| samudrapurameghānāmityatra samudrādibhiḥ saha niḥsvanaśabdaḥ pratyekaṃ sambadhyate| etena samudrādīnāmasampattāvabhāve'pi teṣāṃ śabdāñchṛṇotītyarthaḥ| purasvanaḥ puravāsiprāṇiśabdaḥ; anye tu purasthāne `tūrya' iti paṭhanti; apare `vāta' iti paṭhanti| tān svanānityādi vidyamāneṣu vā samudrādisvaneṣu tānna gṛhṇāti, vā'nyaśabdavanmanyate| grāmyasvanān grāmavāsiprāṇiśabdān, āraṇyān araṇyavāsiprāṇidhvanīn; viparītāniti grāmyānāraṇyāniva śṛṇoti, āraṇyān grāmyāniva śṛṇotītyarthaḥ| dviṣacchabdeṣu śatruśabdeṣu| suhṛnmitrādiḥ| akasmācchravaṇarogaṃ vinā||4-6||

yastūṣṇamiva gṛhṇāti śītamuṣṇaṃ ca śītavat |
sañjātaśītapiḍako [5] yaśca dāhena pīḍyate ||7||
uṣṇagātro'timātraṃ ca yaḥ śītena pravepate |
prahārānnābhijānāti yo'ṅgacchedamathāpi vā ||8||

āturagatagrāhyaviruddhasparśapratipattiṃ darśayannāha- yastūṣṇamityādi| atra śītapīḍakā hi kaphakṛtāḥ, tāsāṃ jāḍyādayo vedanāviśeṣāḥ prākṛtāḥ, dāhastu vaikṛta iti| prahāro laguḍādikṛtaḥ| aṅgacchedaḥ śastrādikṛtaḥ||7-8||

pāṃśunevāvakīrṇāni yaśca gātrāṇi manyate |
varṇānyatā [6] vā rājyo vā yasya gātre bhavanti hi ||9||

āturagatagrāhyaviruddharūpapratipattiṃ darśayannāha- pāṃśunetyādi| pāṃśurdhūliḥ, avakīrṇāni vyāptāni| rājyo lekhā nīlalohitādayaḥ||9||

snātānuliptaṃ yaṃ cāpi bhajante nīlamakṣikāḥ [7] |10|

āturagatāmeva viruddharasapratipattiṃ darśayannāha- snātetyādi| pūrvaṃ snātaḥ paścādanulipta itthambhūtamapi sugandhikāyaṃ bahirmalavarjitaṃ yadā nīlamakṣikāḥ śrayante tadā'sau kālapakvatvādatimadhurībhūtaśarīraḥ, yena nirmalaśarīro'pi makṣikābhiḥ kāmyata iti| snātānuliptamityetadariṣṭaṃ varṣāvadhi|10|-

sugandhirvā'ti [8] yo'kasmāttaṃ bruvanti gatāyuṣam ||10||

āturagatagrāhyaviruddhagandhapratipattiṃ darśayannāha- sugandhirityādi| ati sugandhirvā'kasmāt `sugandhiprayogaṃ vinā bhavati' iti śeṣaḥ, idamapi vārṣikamariṣṭam||10||

viparītena gṛhṇāti rasān yaścopayojitān |
upayuktāḥ kramādyasya rasā doṣābhivṛddhaye ||11||
yasya doṣāgnisāmyaṃ ca kuryurmithyopayojitāḥ |
yo vā rasānna saṃvetti gatāsuṃ taṃ pracakṣate ||12||

āturagatapañcendriyārthavipratipattiṃ nirdiśyā''turagrāhyapañcendriyārthavipratipattinirdeśābhiprāyaḥ, tatra rasasya dehamūlatvena pradhānatvāttasyaiva vipratipattiṃ darśayannāha- viparītenetyādi| viparīteneti madhuro'mlaḥ, amlo madhura ityādi| upayuktāḥ kramāditi ‘pūrvaṃ madhuramaśnīyāt’ ityādikramaḥ| gatāsuṃ gataprāṇam| etadariṣṭaṃ māsikam||11-12||

sugandhaṃ vetti durgandhaṃ durgandhasya sugandhitām |
gṛhṇīte vā'nyathā gandhaṃ śānte dīpe ca nīrujaḥ ||13||
yo vā gandhaṃ na jānāti gatāsuṃ taṃ vinirdiśet |14|

gandhagrahaṇamadhikṛtyāha- sugandhamityādi| śānte dīpe nirvāṇe pradīpe, yo gandhaṃ na jānāti| nīrujo nīrogaḥ pīnasādirogavarjitaḥ||13||-

dvandvānyuṣṇahimādīni kālāvasthā diśastathā ||14||
viparītena gṛhṇāti bhāvānanyāṃśca yo naraḥ |15|

sparśagrahaṇamadhikṛtyāha- dvandvānītyādi| dvandvāni kaṣṭakarāṇi; ādigrahaṇāt sparśagrāhyāṇi snigdhādivīryāṇi| kālāvasthā pravātanirvātavarṣādyāḥ| diśa iti pūrvādikāḥ| anena mānasaḥ sparśa uktaḥ| dvividho hi sparśaḥ- sparśendriyagrāhyaḥ, mānasaśca| tathā ca tantrāntaram- “sparśenendriyasaṃsparśaḥ sparśo mānasa eva ca” (ca. śā. 1) iti| anyāṃśca bhāvān dravyaguṇakarmāṇi, viparītena gṛhṇāti| dvandvānyuṣṇahimādīnyetadariṣṭamekamāsikamiti; diśo vaiparītyena gṛhṇātyetadariṣṭaṃ trimāsikam||14||

divā jyotīṃṣi yaścāpi jvalitānīva paśyati ||15||
rātrau sūryaṃ jvalantaṃ vā divā vā candravarcasam |
ameghopaplave yaśca śakracāpataḍidguṇān ||16||
taḍittvato'sitān yo vā nirmale gagane ghanān |
vimānayānaprāsādairyaśca saṅkulamambaram ||17||
yaścānilaṃ mūrtimantamantarikṣaṃ ca paśyati |
dhūmanīhāravāsobhirāvṛtāmiva medinīm ||18||
pradīptamiva lokaṃ ca yo vā plutamivāmbhasā |
bhūmimaṣṭāpadākārāṃ lekhābhiryaśca paśyati ||19||
na paśyati sanakṣatrāṃ yaśca devīmarundhatīm |
dhruvamākāśagaṅgāṃ vā taṃ vadanti gatāyuṣam ||20||
jyotsnādarśoṣṇatoyeṣu [9] chāyāṃ yaśca na paśyati |
paśyatyekāṅgahīnāṃ vā vikṛtāṃ vā'nyasattvajām ||21||
śvakākakaṅkagṛdhrāṇāṃ pretānāṃ yakṣarakṣasām |
piśācoraganāgānāṃ bhūtānāṃ vikṛtāmapi ||22||
yo vā mayūrakaṇṭhābhaṃ vidhūmaṃ vahnimīkṣate |
āturasya bhavenmṛtyuḥ svastho vyādhimavāpnuyāt [10] ||23||

rūpagrahaṇamadhikṛtyāha- divetyādi| jyotīṃṣi tārakādīni| candravarcasamiti sūryameva candratejaḥsamaṃ paśyatītyarthaḥ| ameghopaplava ityādi| meghairanāvṛte'pi nabhasīndradhanurvidyullatāḥ `paśyati' iti śeṣaḥ| taḍittvato vidyudyuktān, asitān kṛṣṇān, meghāniti sambandhaḥ| ameghopaplave śakracāpataḍillatāvalokanamityariṣṭaṃ dvitrimāsikam| saṅkulaṃ vyāptam| ambaram ākāśam| mūrtimantaṃ devatākāram; anye puruṣākāramityāhuḥ| medinīṃ pṛthivīṃ, nīhāraḥ kuheḍī, yena pratyūṣakāle dinaṃ dhūmāvṛtamiva bhāti; netrarogaṃ vinā'pi dhūmādibhirāvṛtāṃ medinīṃ paśyatītyarthaḥ| lokaṃ jagat, pradīptamiva grīṣmakālādṛte'pi jvalitamiva| āplutamiva buḍitamiva, ambhasā pānīyena| bhūmimaṣṭāpadākārāmiti aṣṭāpadaśabdena lekhābhiḥ koṣṭhasantānanirmitaṃ caturaṅgakrīḍanārthamucyate| arundhatīṃ vasiṣṭhabhāryāṃ, dhruvaṃ tārakāviśeṣam, ākāśagaṅgāśabdena ślakṣṇaghanatārakāsantatirnadyākārocyate| ādarśaḥ pratibimbadarśanārthaṃ sampūrṇacandramākāraḥ kāṃsyamaya ucyate| uṣṇo gharmaḥ| jyotsnātapayostu praticchāyāṃ chāyāpuruṣaṃ kecinmanyante| vikṛtāṃ prakṛteranyathābhūtām| anyasattvajām anyebhyaḥ prāṇibhyo jātāmanyasattvajātām| śvā kukkuraḥ, kaṅkaḥ kaṅkamallaḥ; tasya lakṣaṇamāha- kaṅkaḥ syāt kaṅkamallākhyo bāṇapakṣārhapakṣakaḥ| lohapṛṣṭho dīrghapādaḥ pakṣādhaḥpāṇḍuvarṇabhāk- iti| bhūtānāṃ devayonīnām, athavā bhūtāni prāṇinasteṣāṃ; vikṛtāmapi vikṛtāṃ vikṛtiṃ gatāṃ chāyāṃ paśyatīti sambandhaḥ| yo vā mayūrakaṇṭhābhamityādi sudīptakhadirāṅgārajvalitaṃ dhūmarahitamapi mayūrakaṇṭhābhaṃ nīlaṃ dhūmasadṛśaṃ paśyatītyarthaḥ| atra `divā jyotīṃṣi' ityādinā `vidhūmaṃ vahnimīkṣate' ityetatparyantena granthena pañcavidhaṃ rūpamuktam| tathāhi- `divā jyotīṃṣi' ityādinā''kāśastharūpidravyarūpamuktam, ākāśarūpāsambhavāt; `yaścānilaṃ' ityādinā vāyurūpamuktaṃ; `dhūmanīhāra' ityādinā pṛthvīrūpamuktaṃ; `pradīptamiva' ityādinā vahnirūpamuktam, `āplutamivāmbhasā' ityanenāpāṃ rūpamuktaṃ, evaṃ pañcavidhaṃ rūpamuktam| `bhūmimaṣṭāpadākārām' ityanena bhūmervikṛtidarśanamuktaṃ, pūrvaṃ tu dhūmādyāvaraṇadarśanameveti na paunaruktyam| `na paśyati sanakṣatrām' ityanena vyomasthānāmadarśanamuktaṃ, `divā jyotīṃṣi' ityādinā tu pūrvaṃ darśanamuktam, ato na paunaruktyam| `jyotsnādarśoṣṇatoyeṣu' ityādinā rūpasyāyoga uktaḥ, `paśyatyekāṅgahīnāṃ vā' ityanena rūpahīnayoga uktaḥ, `vikṛtāṃ ca' ityanena mithyāyogo rūpasyoktaḥ| agnermithyāgrahaṇamadhikṛtyoktaṃ `yo vā mayūrakaṇṭhābham' ityādinā| etadariṣṭaphalaṃ darśayannāha- āturasya bhavenmṛtyurityādi||15-23||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne pañcendriyārthavipratipattirnāma
triṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||30||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne triṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||30||

1. ‘dvividhaṃ riṣṭaṃ- bāhyaṃ bhūtādigatamāntaraṃ śarīragataṃ ca| tatra vaidyena prathamamupasthitaṃ bhūtādigatameva parīkṣyata iti pūrvādhyāyenoktaṃ, tadanu cāturagṛhagatenāturagataṃ parīkṣaṇīyamiti āturagatariṣṭajñānārthaṃ pañcendriyārthavipratipattiradhyāyo'bhidhīyate| pañcendriyārthāḥ śabdādayaḥ, teṣu viruddhā pratipattiḥ tathā teṣāmevā'nyathābhāvo vipratipattiḥ|’ iti cakraḥ|
2. ‘tatra prathamataḥ śārīramānasasakalāriṣṭalakṣaṇameva tāvadāha- śarīraśīlayorityādi| śarīraṃ prāṇikāyaḥ, śīlaṃ manovṛttiḥ, śīlena ca mano lakṣyate| tena śarīramanasorityarthaḥ| anayoḥ prakṛtiḥ svabhāvaḥ| sā ca jātyādīnupādhīnapekṣate| vikṛtiranyathābhāvaḥ’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘dvandvayoḥ’ iti pā.|
4. ‘tasyā vikṛtirvaiparītyaṃ| yathā- prathamaṃ sattvaprakṛtistato rajastamaḥprakṛtirityādi| tathā, ādau vātaprakṛtistataḥ pittaśleṣmaprakṛtirityādi’ iti pā.|
5. ‘sañjātaśītapiḍako yaśca dāhena pīḍyate iti atra śītapīḍakocitāṃ śītavedanāṃ na vetti, dāhaṃ cānucitaṃ vettītīndriyavipratipattī riṣṭaṃ, tathā śītapiḍakocitaṃ śaityaṃ na bhavati dāhaścānucito bhavatīti sparśānyathābhāvo'pi riṣṭam| evamuṣṇagātra ityādāvapi yathāyogyatayā indriyasyārthasya vā vipratipattirvyākhyeyā’ iti cakraḥ|
6. ‘varṇānyabhāvo’ iti pā.|
7. ‘snātānuliptamityādau riṣṭe gandhasya rasasya cānyathābhāvo riṣṭam| nīlamakṣikā hi atyantamadhure durgandhe ca patanti’ iti cakraḥ|
8. ‘sugandhiṃ vātīti sugandhigandhamudvahati, ‘vā’ gatigandhanayoḥ, ityasya ‘vāti’ iti gandhanārthe rūpam’ iti cakraḥ|
9. ‘jyotsnādau uṣṇaśabdena ātapadīpayorgrahaṇam| yaduktaṃ carake- ‘jyotsnāyāmātape dīpe salilādarśayorapi’ (ca. i. a. 7) iti’ cakraḥ|
10. ‘svasthaḥ sa labhate vyādhimityatra so'pi vyādhirmaraṇāya bhavatīti jñeyam, anyathā maraṇavyabhicaraṇena riṣṭatvānupapattiḥ syāt’ iti cakraḥ|
