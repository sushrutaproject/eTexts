--- title: "9. yōgyāsūtrīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 9 order: 9 ---

athātō yōgyāsūtrīyamadhyāyaṁ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathōvāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta iti| yōgyā samyakkarmābhyāsaḥ, yōgyāyāḥ sūtraṁ, tadvidyatē yasmin sa tathā; anyē tu yōgyākhyāpakaṁ sūtraṁ yōgyāsūtraṁ, tadadhikr̥tya kr̥tō'dhyāyō yōgyāsūtrīyaḥ, taṁ tathā||1-2||

adhigatasarvaśāstrārthamapi śiṣyaṁ yōgyāṁ kārayēt |
snēhādiṣu chēdyādiṣu ca karmapathamupadiśēt |
subahuśrutō'pyakr̥tayōgyaḥ [2] karmasvayōgyō bhavati ||3||

adhigatētyādi| snēhādiṣviti ādiśabdāt svēdavamanavirēcanāsthāpanānuvāsanādīnāṁ grahaṇaṁ, chēdyādiṣviti ādiśabdādbhēdyādīnāṁ saptānāṁ grahaṇaṁ; yadyapyasmin tantrē chēdyādikarmaṇāṁ prādhānyaṁ tathāpi chēdyādiṣvapi prāk snēhādi prayujyata iti prāk snēhādigrahaṇam; anyē ‘chēdyādiṣu snēhādiṣu ca’ iti paṭhanti| karmapathaṁ kriyāmārgam| kutō'dhigatasarvaśāstrārthamapi śiṣyaṁ yōgyāṁ kārayēdityāha- subahuśrutō'pītyādi| akr̥tayōgyaḥ akr̥takarmābhyāsaḥ| karmasu kriyāsu||3||

tatra, puṣpaphalālābūkālindakatrapusai(sō)rvārukarkārukaprabhr̥tiṣu chēdyaviśēṣān darśayēt, utkartanāpakartanāni [3] cōpadiśēt; dr̥tibastiprasēvakaprabhr̥tiṣūdakapaṅkapūrṇēṣu bhēdyayōgyāṁ; sarōmṇi [4] carmaṇyātatē lēkhyasya; mr̥tapaśusirāsūtpalanālēṣu ca vēdhyasya; ghuṇōpahatakāṣṭhavēṇunalanālīśuṣkālābūmukhēṣvēṣyasya; panasabimbībilvaphalamajjamr̥tapaśudantēṣvāhāryasya; madhūcchiṣṭōpaliptē śālmalīphalakē visrāvyasya; sūkṣmaghanavastrāntayōrmr̥ducarmāntayōśca sīvyasya; pustamayapuruṣāṅgapratyaṅgaviśēṣēṣu bandhanayōgyāṁ; mr̥duṣu māṁsakhaṇḍēṣvagnikṣārayōgyāṁ; mr̥ducarmamāṁsapēśīṣūtpalanālēṣu ca karṇasandhibandhayōgyām; udakapūrṇaghaṭapārśvasrōtasyalābūmukhādiṣu ca nētrapraṇidhānabastivraṇabastipīḍanayōgyāmiti ||4||

śalyatantrasya chēdyādikarmaprādhānyāt prāk chēdyādiyōgyāmupadarśayannāha- tatra puṣpaphalētyādi| puṣpaphalaṁ kūṣmāṇḍaḥ, kālindakō jōnnālaḥ kūṣmāṇḍabhēdaḥ, trapusādayaḥ karkaṭībhēdāḥ, prabhr̥tigrahaṇācchīrṇavr̥ntādīnāṁ grahaṇam| chēdyaviśēṣāniti gōtīrthārdhalāṅgalakaprabhr̥tīn| utkartanam ūrdhvakartanam, aparakartanam [5] adhaśchēdaḥ| dr̥tibastītyādi dr̥tiḥ carmapuṭakaṁ [6] , bastiḥ mūtrapuṭakaṁ, prasēvakaḥ carmakhallapuṭaḥ [7] , udakapaṅkamiti udakamiśraḥ paṅkaḥ udakapaṅkaḥ| sarōmṇītyādi| ātatē prasāritē| mr̥tapaśusirāsviti mr̥tāḥ paśavō'jādayastēṣāṁ sirāsu| panasētyādi panasō bahiḥkaṇṭakī mahāphalaḥ ‘kaṭahala’ iti lōkē; panasādīnāṁ phalāni tēṣāṁ majjā| madhūcchiṣṭētyādi madhūcchiṣṭaṁ madhusikthakam| śālmalīphalakē śālmalīpaṭṭakē| pustamayētyādi pustamayaḥ puruṣō vastrādiracitō manuṣyaḥ| mr̥ducarmētyādi māṁsapēśī māṁsavartiḥ| udakētyādi| nētrapraṇidhānaṁ nētrapravēśanaṁ, bastivraṇabastipīḍanaṁ ca, anayōryōgyāṁ kārayēt; kva kārayēt? udakapūrṇaghaṭapārśvasrōtasi; na kēvalaṁ jalapūrṇaghaṭapārśvacchidrē alābūmukhādiṣu cētyarthaḥ||4||

bhavataścātra-
ēvamādiṣu mēdhāvī yōgyārhēṣu yathāvidhi |
dravyēṣu yōgyāṁ kurvāṇō na pramuhyati karmasu ||5||

bhavataścātrētyādi| ēvamādiṣviti ādiśabdaḥ prakārē, ēvamprakārēṣu yōgyārhēṣu puṣpaphalādiṣu||5||

tasmāt kauśalamanvicchan śastrakṣārāgnikarmasu |
yasya yatrēha sādharmyaṁ tatra yōgyāṁ samācarēt ||6||

tasmādityādi| yasya karmaṇaḥ, yatra yasmin dravyē, sādharmyaṁ sāmyaṁ, tasya tatra yōgyāṁ samācarēditi| kēcidēvamādiṣu mēdhāvītyādiślōkasyāgrē āpātanikāpūrvaṁ ślōkamēkaṁ paṭhanti; tadyathā- anuktaṁ yōgyāviṣayamupagrahītumāha- ‘dr̥ṣṭāntamātramuddiṣṭaṁ’- ityādi, sa ca vartamānēṣu pustakēṣu na paṭhyata ityasmābhirupēkṣitaḥ||6||

iti suśrutasaṁhitāyāṁ sūtrasthānē yōgyāsūtrīyō nāma navamō'dhyāyaḥ ||9||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṁ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṁ suśrutavyākhyāyāṁ sūtrasthānē yōgyāsūtrīyō nāma navamō'dhyāyaḥ|

1. “kartavyakarmaṇaḥ samyagyōgāya tatsadr̥śakarmābhyāsō yōgaḥ, tēna yōgāya prabhavatīti yōgyā| prabhavatītyarthē taddhitaḥ, strītvaṁ svabhāvāt maitrīvat| tasyāḥ sūtraṁ yōgyāsūtraṁ| tadadhikr̥tya kr̥tō granthastaṁ tathā” iti cakraḥ|
2. ‘yōgāya samyagyōgāya prabhavatīti yōgyaḥ, tadviruddhō'yōgyaḥ’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘utkartanaparikartanāni’ iti pā.|
4. ‘sarōmṇītyanēna lēkhyasyānulōmakartavyatāṁ sūcayati, pratilōmē hi lēkhyē rujāprādurbhāvō raktātipravr̥ttiśca’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
5. ‘parikartanaṁ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘khallāpadādi carma’ iti pā.|
7. ‘carmakhallaṭakaḥ’ iti pā.|
