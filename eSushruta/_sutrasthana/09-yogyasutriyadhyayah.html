--- title: "9. yogyāsūtrīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 9 order: 9 ---

athāto yogyāsūtrīyamadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta iti| yogyā samyakkarmābhyāsaḥ, yogyāyāḥ sūtraṃ, tadvidyate yasmin sa tathā; anye tu yogyākhyāpakaṃ sūtraṃ yogyāsūtraṃ, tadadhikṛtya kṛto'dhyāyo yogyāsūtrīyaḥ, taṃ tathā||1-2||

adhigatasarvaśāstrārthamapi śiṣyaṃ yogyāṃ kārayet |
snehādiṣu chedyādiṣu ca karmapathamupadiśet |
subahuśruto'pyakṛtayogyaḥ [2] karmasvayogyo bhavati ||3||

adhigatetyādi| snehādiṣviti ādiśabdāt svedavamanavirecanāsthāpanānuvāsanādīnāṃ grahaṇaṃ, chedyādiṣviti ādiśabdādbhedyādīnāṃ saptānāṃ grahaṇaṃ; yadyapyasmin tantre chedyādikarmaṇāṃ prādhānyaṃ tathāpi chedyādiṣvapi prāk snehādi prayujyata iti prāk snehādigrahaṇam; anye ‘chedyādiṣu snehādiṣu ca’ iti paṭhanti| karmapathaṃ kriyāmārgam| kuto'dhigatasarvaśāstrārthamapi śiṣyaṃ yogyāṃ kārayedityāha- subahuśruto'pītyādi| akṛtayogyaḥ akṛtakarmābhyāsaḥ| karmasu kriyāsu||3||

tatra, puṣpaphalālābūkālindakatrapusai(so)rvārukarkārukaprabhṛtiṣu chedyaviśeṣān darśayet, utkartanāpakartanāni [3] copadiśet; dṛtibastiprasevakaprabhṛtiṣūdakapaṅkapūrṇeṣu bhedyayogyāṃ; saromṇi [4] carmaṇyātate lekhyasya; mṛtapaśusirāsūtpalanāleṣu ca vedhyasya; ghuṇopahatakāṣṭhaveṇunalanālīśuṣkālābūmukheṣveṣyasya; panasabimbībilvaphalamajjamṛtapaśudanteṣvāhāryasya; madhūcchiṣṭopalipte śālmalīphalake visrāvyasya; sūkṣmaghanavastrāntayormṛducarmāntayośca sīvyasya; pustamayapuruṣāṅgapratyaṅgaviśeṣeṣu bandhanayogyāṃ; mṛduṣu māṃsakhaṇḍeṣvagnikṣārayogyāṃ; mṛducarmamāṃsapeśīṣūtpalanāleṣu ca karṇasandhibandhayogyām; udakapūrṇaghaṭapārśvasrotasyalābūmukhādiṣu ca netrapraṇidhānabastivraṇabastipīḍanayogyāmiti ||4||

śalyatantrasya chedyādikarmaprādhānyāt prāk chedyādiyogyāmupadarśayannāha- tatra puṣpaphaletyādi| puṣpaphalaṃ kūṣmāṇḍaḥ, kālindako jonnālaḥ kūṣmāṇḍabhedaḥ, trapusādayaḥ karkaṭībhedāḥ, prabhṛtigrahaṇācchīrṇavṛntādīnāṃ grahaṇam| chedyaviśeṣāniti gotīrthārdhalāṅgalakaprabhṛtīn| utkartanam ūrdhvakartanam, aparakartanam [5] adhaśchedaḥ| dṛtibastītyādi dṛtiḥ carmapuṭakaṃ [6] , bastiḥ mūtrapuṭakaṃ, prasevakaḥ carmakhallapuṭaḥ [7] , udakapaṅkamiti udakamiśraḥ paṅkaḥ udakapaṅkaḥ| saromṇītyādi| ātate prasārite| mṛtapaśusirāsviti mṛtāḥ paśavo'jādayasteṣāṃ sirāsu| panasetyādi panaso bahiḥkaṇṭakī mahāphalaḥ ‘kaṭahala’ iti loke; panasādīnāṃ phalāni teṣāṃ majjā| madhūcchiṣṭetyādi madhūcchiṣṭaṃ madhusikthakam| śālmalīphalake śālmalīpaṭṭake| pustamayetyādi pustamayaḥ puruṣo vastrādiracito manuṣyaḥ| mṛducarmetyādi māṃsapeśī māṃsavartiḥ| udaketyādi| netrapraṇidhānaṃ netrapraveśanaṃ, bastivraṇabastipīḍanaṃ ca, anayoryogyāṃ kārayet; kva kārayet? udakapūrṇaghaṭapārśvasrotasi; na kevalaṃ jalapūrṇaghaṭapārśvacchidre alābūmukhādiṣu cetyarthaḥ||4||

bhavataścātra-
evamādiṣu medhāvī yogyārheṣu yathāvidhi |
dravyeṣu yogyāṃ kurvāṇo na pramuhyati karmasu ||5||

bhavataścātretyādi| evamādiṣviti ādiśabdaḥ prakāre, evamprakāreṣu yogyārheṣu puṣpaphalādiṣu||5||

tasmāt kauśalamanvicchan śastrakṣārāgnikarmasu |
yasya yatreha sādharmyaṃ tatra yogyāṃ samācaret ||6||

tasmādityādi| yasya karmaṇaḥ, yatra yasmin dravye, sādharmyaṃ sāmyaṃ, tasya tatra yogyāṃ samācarediti| kecidevamādiṣu medhāvītyādiślokasyāgre āpātanikāpūrvaṃ ślokamekaṃ paṭhanti; tadyathā- anuktaṃ yogyāviṣayamupagrahītumāha- ‘dṛṣṭāntamātramuddiṣṭaṃ’- ityādi, sa ca vartamāneṣu pustakeṣu na paṭhyata ityasmābhirupekṣitaḥ||6||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne yogyāsūtrīyo nāma navamo'dhyāyaḥ ||9||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne yogyāsūtrīyo nāma navamo'dhyāyaḥ|

1. “kartavyakarmaṇaḥ samyagyogāya tatsadṛśakarmābhyāso yogaḥ, tena yogāya prabhavatīti yogyā| prabhavatītyarthe taddhitaḥ, strītvaṃ svabhāvāt maitrīvat| tasyāḥ sūtraṃ yogyāsūtraṃ| tadadhikṛtya kṛto granthastaṃ tathā” iti cakraḥ|
2. ‘yogāya samyagyogāya prabhavatīti yogyaḥ, tadviruddho'yogyaḥ’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘utkartanaparikartanāni’ iti pā.|
4. ‘saromṇītyanena lekhyasyānulomakartavyatāṃ sūcayati, pratilome hi lekhye rujāprādurbhāvo raktātipravṛttiśca’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ|
5. ‘parikartanaṃ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘khallāpadādi carma’ iti pā.|
7. ‘carmakhallaṭakaḥ’ iti pā.|
