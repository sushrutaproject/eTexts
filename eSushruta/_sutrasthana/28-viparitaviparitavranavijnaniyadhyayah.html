--- title: "28. viparītāviparītavraṇavijñānīyādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 28 order: 28 ---

athāto viparītāviparītavraṇavijñānīyamadhyāyaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
phalāgnijalavṛṣṭīnāṃ puṣpadhūmāmbudā [2] yathā |
khyāpayanti bhaviṣyattvaṃ tathā riṣṭāni pañcatām ||3||

niyatāniyatabhedena dvividhamariṣṭaṃ prakāśayitumāha- phalāgnītyādi| atra yathā puṣpaṃ phalasya bhaviṣyattvaṃ khyāpayati, ko'rthaḥ? puṣpaṃ phalaṃ bhaviṣyatīti; yathā ca dhūmo'gniṃ khyāpayati, yathā'mbudo jalavṛṣṭim, evamariṣṭāni pañcatāṃ mṛtyuṃ khyāpayanti; ‘bhāvinīm’ iti śeṣaḥ| ariṣṭānyatra maraṇalakṣaṇāni| nanu, atra kimarthaṃ puṣpadhūmāmbudā iti bahavo duṣṭāntā uktāḥ? ekenaiva gatārthatvāt| ucyate, puṣpāmbudau hetū phalajalavṛṣṭyorjñāpakāvaniyatau, na hyavaśyaṃ kāraṇena kāryaṃ kartavyameva; ityanenāniyatadṛṣṭāntena dūtadinimittajamariṣṭamaniyatamuktaṃ; dhūmāt kāryādagneḥ kāraṇasya yaj jñānaṃ tanniyatam, etena
niyatadṛṣṭāntena doṣajamariṣṭaṃ niyatamuktam; ityaniyataniyatabhedena dvividhamariṣṭaṃ prakāśayanti bahavo dṛṣṭāntā iti na doṣaḥ||1-3||

tāni [3] saukṣmyāt pramādādvā tathaivāśu vyatikramāt |
gṛhyante nodgatānyajñairmumūrṣorna tvasambhavāt ||4||
dhruvaṃ tu maraṇaṃ riṣṭe... |5|

avaśyamevāriṣṭasya maraṇabhavanaṃ darśayannāha- tānītyādi| mumūrṣoḥ martumicchoḥ prāṇina udgatānyapi prakaṭitānyapyariṣṭānyajñairmanuṣyairna gṛhyante na jñāyante| kuto hetorityāha- saukṣmyāditi; paramāṇuvat| pramādādveti madirāmadamattasya vastunyavimarśa iva| āśu vyatikramāditi yadaivāriṣṭamutpannaṃ tadaiva maraṇāditi, utpalaśatapatravyatibhedavat; anye tvāśuvyatikramaṃ yadaivāriṣṭamutpannaṃ [4] tadaiva nivartata iti manyante| na tvasambhavāditi na punarasambhavānna gṛhyante, apitu sambhavantyapyariṣṭānyuktaiḥ kāraṇairajñairna gṛhyanta ityabhiprāyaḥ| doṣajamariṣṭamadhikṛtya ariṣṭena maraṇasya niyatatvaṃ darśayannāha- dhruvamityādi||4||

... brāhmaṇaistat kilāmalaiḥ |
rasāyanatapojapyatatparairvā nivāryate ||5||

riṣṭe'pi maraṇanivṛttimupāyaviśeṣairāha- brāhmaṇairityādi| kilaśabdo'trāgamārthaṃ sūcayati; evaṃ kila āgame pratipāditamityarthaḥ| amalairiti rāgādidoṣarahitaiḥ| brāhmaṇebhyo'parānapi darśayannāha- rasāyanatapojapyetyādi|- rasāyanaparā iti svabhāvavyādhinivāraṇaviśiṣṭauṣadhacintakāḥ, te rasavīryauṣadhaprabhāveṇa maraṇaṃ nivārayanti| tathā tapaḥparāḥ tapasvinaḥ, te'pi viśiṣṭatapaḥprabhāveṇa maraṇaṃ nivārayanti | japyaparāḥ siddhamantracintakāḥ, te'pi mantraśaktiyuktāḥ pañcatvaṃ niṣedhayanti | etacca niyatāniyatariṣṭaviṣayaṃ jñeyam||5||

nakṣatrapīḍā [5] bahudhā yathā kālaṃ [6] vipacyate |
tathaivāriṣṭapākaṃ ca bruvate bahavo [7] janāḥ ||6||

kathaṃ punaḥ kālāntare pañcaviṃśativarṣādāvariṣṭapīḍā bhavatītyāha- nakṣatretyādi|- vipacyate phalatītyarthaḥ| pākaḥ phalam||6||

asiddhimāpnuyālloke pratikurvan gatāyuṣaḥ |
ato'riṣṭāni yatnena lakṣayet kuśalo bhiṣak ||7||

ariṣṭāparijñānadoṣaṃ darśayannāha- asiddhimityādi|asiddhiṃ dharmārthakāmādīnāṃ hānim||7||

gandhavarṇarasādīnāṃ viśeṣāṇāṃ svabhāvataḥ [8] |
vaikṛtaṃ yat tadācaṣṭe vraṇinaḥ pakvalakṣaṇam ||8||

asya tantrasya śalyatvāttatra ca vraṇasya prādhānyāttasyaivāriṣṭāni prāṅnirdeṣṭumāha- gandhetyādi | gandhavarṇarasādīnāmiti ādigrahaṇena śabdasparśayorapi saṅgrahaḥ; etena pañcāpīndriyārthā uktā bhavanti, rūpasya varṇagrahaṇena gṛhītatvāt| svabhāvataḥ prakṛtito yadvaikṛtamanyathātvaṃ, tadācaṣṭe tat kathayati, vraṇinaḥ puruṣasya, pakvalakṣaṇaṃ pātalakṣaṇaṃ, yathā pakvaṃ phalaṃ patatyeva tathā'sāvapi vraṇī patatyevetyarthaḥ||8||

kaṭustīkṣṇaśca visraśca gandhastu pavanādibhiḥ |
lohagandhistu raktena vyāmiśraḥ sānnipātikaḥ ||9||
lājātasītailasamāḥ kiñcidvisrāśca gandhataḥ |
jñeyāḥ prakṛtigandhāḥ syuḥ ... |10|

tatra prākṛtaṃ gandhaṃ darśayannāha- kaṭurityādi| atra lājātasītailasamāstrayo gandhā yathāsaṅkhyena vātapaittika-vātaślaiṣmika-pittaślaiṣmikā dvandvajā jñeyāḥ| ete trayo'pīṣadvisrāśca svabhāvājjñeyāḥ| atasīśabdenātasītailaṃ, tailaṃ tilatailam| jñeyāḥ prakṛtigandhāḥ syuriti jñātavyāḥ prakṛtigandhā bhaveyuriti| ‘kaṭustīvraśca’ iti kecit paṭhanti||9||-

... rato'nyadgandhavaikṛtam ||10||
madyāgurvājyasumanāpadmacandanacampakaiḥ |
sagandhā divyagandhāśca mumūrṣūṇāṃ vraṇāḥ smṛtāḥ ||11||
śvavājimūṣikadhvāṅkṣapūtivallūramatkuṇaiḥ |
sagandhāḥ paṅkagandhāśca bhūmigandhāśca garhitāḥ ||12||

vikṛtigandhān darśayannāha- ata ityādi| ato'nyadgandhavaikṛtamiti ata ebhyaḥ pūrvoktebhyaḥ kaṭvādiprākṛtagandhebhyo'nyadgandhavaikṛtam| tāneva vikṛtagandhānāha- madyetyādi| sumanā jātī, sagandhāḥ samagandhāḥ| divyagandhā iti aparo'pi ya uttamagandhaḥ| mumūrṣūṇāmiti martumicchūnāṃ prāṇinām| śvā kukkuraḥ, dhvāṅkṣaḥ kākaḥ, pūtivallūraṃ pūyasahitaṃ śuṣkamāṃsaṃ, matkuṇāḥ śayyādijāḥ kṣudrakīṭāḥ, etaiḥ samānagandhā ityarthaḥ||10-12||

kuṅkumadhyāmakaṅkuṣṭhasavarṇāḥ pittakopataḥ |
na dahyante na cūṣyante bhiṣak tān parivarjayet ||13||
kaṇḍūmantaḥ sthirāḥ śvetāḥ snigdhāḥ kaphanimittataḥ |
dūyante vā'pi dahyante bhiṣak tān parivarjayet ||14||
kṛṣṇāstu [9] ye tanusrāvā vātajā marmatāpinaḥ |
svalpāmapi na kurvanti rujaṃ tān parivarjayet ||15||

varṇānnirdeṣṭumāha- kuṅkumetyādi| dhyāma īṣatkṛṣṇaḥ| na dahyante na dāhayuktā bhavanti| cūṣyanta iti cūṣaṇamiva vedanāviśeṣaḥ| pittavarṇe sati yat pittavedanā dāhādikā na bhavanti, tadvaikṛtaṃ varṇasya| kecit “śyāmākuṅkumakaṅkuṣṭhasavarṇāḥ pittadoṣataḥ| na dahyante na dūyante bhiṣak tān parivarjayet”- iti pāṭhaṃ paṭhanti, vyākhyānayanti ca- śyāmā priyaṅguḥ, kaṅkuṣṭhaṃ suvarṇakṣīriniryāsaḥ, na dahyante nātitapyante na dūyante na paritapyante kintu paritāpamātrayuktā bhavanti” iti| sa ca nibandheṣu na dṛśyate; yatra tu dṛśyate, tatra gaḍḍarikāpravāhaḥ, tasmāt pūrvaḥ pāṭho nyāyyaḥ| kaṇḍūmanta ityādikāḥ kaphena, teṣāṃ dāharuje varṇavaikṛtaṃ; dūyante iti pīḍāṃ kurvanti| kṛṣṇāścetyādi ye vātajā vraṇāḥ kṛṣṇāstanusrāvā marmatāpinaśca yadā svalpāmapi pīḍāṃ na kurvanti tadā vātaje varṇavaikṛtam| atra rasavaikṛtaṃ yadyapi noktaṃ tathā'pi vātapittakaphavraṇeṣu yathākramaṃ kaṣāyakaṭumadhureṣu pipīlikādyupasarpaṇādanuktamapi jñeyaṃ, gandhavarṇarasādīnāmityatroddeśasūtre rasasyoktatvāt||13-15||

kṣveḍanti ghurghurāyante jvalantīva ca ye vraṇāḥ |
tvaṅmāṃsasthāśca pavanaṃ saśabdaṃ visṛjanti ye ||16||
ye ca marmasvasambhūtā bhavantyatyarthavedanāḥ |17|

śabdavaikṛtaṃ darśayannāha- kṣveḍantītyādi| kṣveḍanti khaṭakhaṭāśabdaṃ kurvanti| ghurghurāyanta iti śvamārjārādīnāṃ kopādantargataśabdo ghurghurāśabda ucyate| jvalantīveti jvalanta iva dṛśyante| tvaṅmāṃsasthānāṃ vraṇānāṃ saśabdapavanavāhitvaṃ śabdavaikṛtaṃ, koṣṭhagatānāṃ tu saśabdapavanavāhitvaṃ prakṛtireva||16||

dahyante cāntaratyarthaṃ bahiḥ śītāśca ye vraṇāḥ ||17||
dahyante bahiratyarthaṃ bhavantyantaśca śītalāḥ |18|

sparśavaikṛtamadhikṛtyāha- dahyante ityādi||17||-

śaktidhvajarathāḥ kuntavājivāraṇagovṛṣāḥ [10] ||18||
yeṣu cāpyavabhāseran prāsādākṛtayastathā |
cūrṇāvakīrṇā iva ye bhānticānavacūrṇitāḥ ||19||

ākṛtiviśeṣavaikṛtamadhikṛtyāha- śaktītyādi| śaktirāyudhaviśeṣaḥ, dhvajaḥ patākādaṇḍaḥ| yeṣu vraṇeṣu| avabhāseran śobhante| prāsādo devatānarendrāṇāmadhiṣṭhānam, ākṛtiratra rūpameva| rūpaṃ ca pañcendriyārthamadhye| aparamapi rūpaviśeṣamāha- cūrṇāvakīrṇā ityādi| atrāpi sāmānyākāreṇa rūpameva boddhavyam||18-19||

prāṇamāṃsakṣayaśvāsakāsārocakapīḍitāḥ |
pravṛddhapūyarudhirā vraṇā yeṣāṃ ca marmasu ||20||

gandhādivaikṛtaṃ samāpya, upadravāsādhyatvaṃ prasaṅgato yuktyāgataṃ darśayannāha- prāṇetyādi| prāṇakṣayaḥ śaktilakṣaṇabalakṣayaḥ, māṃsakṣaya upacayalakṣaṇabalakṣayaḥ||20||

kriyābhiḥ [11] samyagārabdhā na siddhyanti ca ye vraṇāḥ |
varjayettānapi prājñaḥ saṃrakṣannātmano yaśaḥ ||21||

anuktamapyaśeṣāriṣṭamupagṛhṇannāha- kriyābhirityādi||21||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ sūtrasthāne viparītāviparītavraṇavijñānīyo nāmāṣṭāviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ ||28||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ sūtrasthāne'ṣṭāviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ||28||

1. ‘pūrvādhyāyaiḥ samāsavistarābhyāṃ vraṇānuddiśyāturopakramaṇīyādhyāye cikitsā sūtrayitavyā| sā tu sāriṣṭatvādgatāyuṣi na sidhyati, ato'riṣṭajñānārthaṃ riṣṭapratipādakādhyāyeṣu sāmānyāriṣṭasvarūpābhidhāyakatayā viparītavraṇavijñānīyo'bhidhīyate| viparīto vraṇaḥ sādhyādanyaḥ sāriṣṭatayā vivakṣitaḥ’ iti cakraḥ| ‘viparītavraṇavijñānīyaṃ’ iti tāḍapatrapustake pāṭhaḥ|
2. ‘dhūma iha kāṣṭhamanthanajanyaḥ vahneśca pūrvabhāvī vivakṣito bhāvivahnigamakatayā’ iti cakraḥ|
3. ‘ariṣṭasya maraṇādavaśyaṃ prāgbhāvamāha- tāni saukṣmyādityādi| saukṣmyāt kaṇṭakādi sadapi sūkṣmajijñāsāṃ vinā nopalabhyate| tathā pramādādanavahitaiḥ sadapi nopalabhyate| āśu vyatikramāditi bhūtvā kṣaṇena nāśāt, etadapi sarvathā'vadhānaṃ vinā nopalabhyate| yathā vidyudādi kṣaṇamātrasthāyibhūtamapi nopalabhyate| na tvasambhavādityanena maraṇe'vaśyambhāvitvamuktaṃ, carake'pyuktaṃ ‘maraṇaṃ cāpi tannāsti yannāriṣṭapuraḥsaram’ (ca. i. a. 2)| tathā riṣṭe'pi maraṇaniyamamāha- dhruvamityādi|
4. ‘tadaiva’ iti pā.|
5. ‘nakṣatrapīḍeti janmakālasthitagrahaviśeṣairnakṣatrapīḍā yathā kālāntarabhāvimaraṇasūcikā kālāntareṇaiva pacyamānaduṣṭamaraṇaṃ gamayati| tathā'riṣṭānyapi kānicit cirakālapacyamānamārakakarmajanyāni cirabhāvimaraṇavyañjakānīti vākyārthaḥ| ariṣṭapākamiti riṣṭakāraṇamārakakarmaprapākam|’ iti cakraḥ|
6. ‘kālāt’ iti pā.|
7. ‘bahudhā’ iti pā.|
8. ‘svabhāvato vaikṛtamityanena asvābhāvikasya vikṛtasyāpi nātiṣṭatvamiti darśayati| etacca vaikṛtamadṛśyamānahetukaṃ mārakakarmakṛtameva riṣṭaṃ jñeyam| pakvaḥ pātonmukho maraṇonmukha iti yāvat’ iti cakraḥ| ‘samāsataḥ’ iti pā.|
9. ‘kṛṣṇā ye tvaruṇasrāvā’ iti tāḍapatrapustake pāṭhaḥ|
10. ‘vāraṇapakṣiṇaḥ’ iti tāḍapatrapustake pāṭhaḥ|
11. ‘bheṣajavikārayuktau riṣṭamāha- kriyābhirityādi| carake upadravāśca bheṣajavikārayuktiśca riṣṭādhikaraṇatayoktā’ iti cakraḥ|
