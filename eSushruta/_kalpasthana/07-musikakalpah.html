--- title: "7. mūṣikakalpaḥ" adhyaya: 7 order: 7 ---

athāto mūṣikakalpaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

athāta ityādi||1-2||

pūrvaṃ śukraviṣā uktā mūṣikā ye samāsataḥ |
nāmalakṣaṇabhaiṣajyairaṣṭādaśa nibodha me [1] ||3||
lālanaḥ putrakaḥ kṛṣṇo haṃsiraścikvi(kki)rastathā |
chucchundaro'lasaścaiva kaṣāyadaśano'pi ca ||4||
kuliṅgaścājitaścaiva capalaḥ kapilastathā
kokilo'ruṇasañjñaśca mahākṛṣṇastathonduraḥ ||5||
śvetena mahatā sārdhaṃ kapilenākhunā tathā |
mūṣikaśca kapotābhastathaivāṣṭādaśa smṛtāḥ ||6||

tānevāṣṭādaśabhedān nirdiśannāha- lālana ityādi||3-6||

śukraṃ patati yatraiṣāṃ śukraspṛṣṭaiḥ [2] spṛśanti vā |
nakhadantādibhistasmin gātre raktaṃ praduṣyati ||7||

nanu `daṣṭarūpaṃ samāsoktaṃ’ iti vakṣyati tatkathaṃ śukraviṣatvamevetyāha- śukramityādi| nakhadantādibhirityādiśabdāt purīṣamūtrābhyāṃ ca; tathā cālambāyane,- “śukreṇātha purīṣeṇa mūtreṇāpi nakhaistathā| daṃṣṭrābhirvā kṣipantīha [3] mūṣikāḥ pañcadhā viṣam”- iti| kasmiṃścittantrāntare viśeṣo'sti, tathā ca,- “garbhiṇyā mūṣikayā daṣṭe amlādidohadaḥ, ṛtumatyā daṣṭe raktamehanamādhmānaṃ ratiśīlatā ca” ityādi||7||

jāyante granthayaḥ śophāḥ karṇikā maṇḍalāni ca |
pīḍakopacayaścogro visarpāḥ kiṭibhāni ca ||8||
parvabhedo rujastīvrā mūrcchā'ṅgasadanaṃ [4] jvaraḥ |
daurbalyamaruciḥ śvāso vamathurlomaharṣaṇam ||9||
daṣṭarūpaṃ samāsoktametadvyāsamataḥ [5] śṛṇu |10|

sāmānyaṃ mūṣikadaṣṭalakṣaṇamabhidhātumāha- jāyante granthaya ityādi| karṇikā kamalamadhyabījakośākṛtiḥ| parvabhedaḥ sandheḥ sphoṭaḥ| aṅgasadanam aṅgaglāniḥ||8-9||-

lālāsrāvo lālanena hikkā chardiśca jāyate [6] ||10||
taṇḍulīyakakalkaṃ tu lihyāttatra samākṣikam |11|

teṣāṃ viśiṣṭaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ cikitsitaṃ ca nirdiśannāha- lālāsrāva ityādi| lālāsrāvacchardihikkā vātakopajā bhavanti| taṇḍulīyakakalkādikaṃ vyādhipratyanīkaṃ cikitsitaṃ, doṣapratyanīkaṃ tu sāmānyacikitsitoktaṃ `taptaghṛtena dagdhvā visrāvayeddaṃśaṃ’ ityādikam; evamanyatrāpi vyādhidoṣapratyanīkaṃ cikitsitaṃ svayamūhanīyaṃ; pratimūṣikoktaṃ tu cikitsitaṃ prāyo vyādhipratyanīkameva||10||-

putrakeṇāṅgasādaśca pāṇḍuvarṇaśca jāyate ||11||
cīyate granthibhiścāṅgamākhuśāvakasannibhaiḥ |
śirīṣeṅgudakalkaṃ tu lihyāttatra samākṣikam ||12||

putrakeṇāṅgetyādi| ākhuśāvakasannibhairmūṣikaputrakatulyairgranthibhiraṅgaṃ cīyate vyāpyata ityarthaḥ| ākhuśāvakanibhā granthayaḥ sarvamūṣikadaṃśeṣu jāyante viśeṣeṇa putrakadaṣṭe| nāgārjuno'trāha,- “tena darśayate daṣṭo vyaktaṃ mūṣikaputrakān| etat putrakadaṣṭasya vyaktaṃ bhavati lakṣaṇam”- iti| `śirīṣeṅgudacūrṇaṃ tu’ iti paṭhitvā gayadāsaḥ anayoḥ patracūrṇamiti vyākhyānayati; jejjaṭācāryastu `śirīṣaphalakalkaṃ tu’ iti paṭhati||11-12||

kṛṣṇena [7] daṃśe śopho'sṛkchardiḥ prāyaśca durdine |
śirīṣaphalakuṣṭhaṃ tu pibet kiṃśukabhasmanā ||13||

kṛṣṇenetyādi| granthyādīnyatrāpi sāmānyamūṣikalakṣaṇāni vijñeyāni; evaṃ sarvatronneyam| prāyaśceti vacanādanyatrāpyasṛkcchardiḥ| kiṃśukabhasmanā kiṃśukakusumabhasmodakenetyarthaḥ||13||

haṃsireṇānnavidveṣo jṛmbhā romṇāṃ ca harṣaṇam |
pibedāragvadhādiṃ tu suvāntastatra mānavaḥ ||14||

haṃsireṇetyādi| suvāntaḥ suṣṭhu vāntaḥ||14||

cikvi(kki)reṇa śiroduḥkhaṃ śopho hikkā vamistathā |
jālinīmadanāṅkoṭhakaṣāyairvāmayettu tam ||15||
yavanālarṣabhīkṣāraṃ bṛhatyoścātra dāpayet |16|

cikvireṇetyādi| jālinī kośātakī, ṛṣabhī kapikacchuḥ, bṛhatyośceti cakāreṇa kṣāramityatrāpi sambandhanīyam| kṣāraścātra kṣārodakam| gayadāsastu `jālinīmadanāṅkoṭha’ ityādisthāne `suvānto jālinīkvāthaiḥ sāramaṅkoṭhajaṃ pibet’ iti paṭhati na yavanālādi||15||-

chucchundareṇa [8] tṛṭ chardirjvaro daurbalyameva ca ||16||
grīvāstambhaḥ pṛṣṭhaśopho gandhājñānaṃ visūcikā |
cavyaṃ harītakī śuṇṭhī viḍaṅgaṃ pippalī madhu ||17||
aṅkoṭhabījaṃ ca tathā pibedatra viṣāpaham |18|

chucchundareṇetyādi| chucchundaradaṣṭalakṣaṇaṃ taccikitsitaṃ ca gayadāso'nyathā paṭhati; yathā- “bhramaśchucchundareṇogro grīvāstambho visūcikā| yavanālarṣabhīkṣāraṃ bṛhatyoścātra dāpayet” iti||16-17||-

grīvāstambho'lasenordhvavāyurdaṃśe rujā jvaraḥ ||18||
mahāgadaṃ sasarpiṣkaṃ lihyāttatra samākṣikam |
nidrā kaṣāyadantena hṛcchoṣaḥ kārśyameva ca ||19||
kṣaudropetāḥ śirīṣasya lihyāt sāraphalatvacaḥ |
kuliṅgena rujaḥ [9] śopho rājyaśca daṃśamaṇḍale ||20||
sahe sasindhuvāre ca lihyāttatra samākṣike |21|

grīvāstambha ityādi| sahe mudgaparṇīmāṣaparṇyau||18-20||-

ajitenāṅgakṛṣṇatvaṃ [10] chardirmūrcchā ca hṛdgrahaḥ ||21||
snukkṣīrapiṣṭāṃ pālindīṃ mañjiṣṭhāṃ madhunā lihet |22|

ajitenetyādi| pālindī trivṛt||21||-

capalena bhavecchardirmūrcchā ca saha tṛṣṇayā ||22||
kṣaudreṇa [11] triphalāṃ lihyādbhadrakāṣṭhajaṭānvitām |23|

capalenetyādi| bhadrakāṣṭhaṃ devadāru, jaṭā jaṭāmāṃsī| `jaṭānvitāṃ’ ityatra `ghanānvitām’ iti kecit||22||-

kapilena vraṇe kotho jvaro granthyudgamaḥ satṛṭ ||23||
lihyānmadhuyutāṃ [12] śvetāṃ śvetāṃ cāpi punarnavām |
granthayaḥ kokilenogrā jvaro dāhaśca dāruṇaḥ [13] ||24||
varṣābhūnīlinīkvāthakalkasiddhaṃ ghṛtaṃ pibet |25|

kapilenetyādi| kothaḥ pūtiprādurbhāvaḥ| śvetāṃ śvetasyandāṃ, `śvetāṃ’ ityatra gayadāsaḥ `śreṣṭhāṃ’ iti paṭhati||23-24||

aruṇenānilaḥ kruddho vātajān kurute gadān ||25||
mahākṛṣṇena pittaṃ ca śvetena kapha eva ca |
mahatā kapilenāsṛk kapotena [14] catuṣṭayam ||26||
bhavanti caiṣāṃ daṃśeṣu granthimaṇḍalakarṇikāḥ |
piḍakopacayaścograḥ śophaśca bhṛśadāruṇaḥ ||27||
dadhikṣīraghṛtaprasthāstrayaḥ pratyekaśo matāḥ |
karañjāragvadhavyoṣabṛhatyaṃśumatīsthirāḥ ||28||
niṣkvāthya caiṣāṃ kvāthasya caturthoṃ'śaḥ punarbhavet |
trivṛdgojyamṛtāvakrasarpagandhāḥ [15] samṛttikāḥ ||29||
kapitthadāḍimatvak ca ślakṣṇapiṣṭāḥ [16] pradāpayet |
tat sarvamekataḥ kṛtvā śanairmṛdvagninā pacet ||30||
pañcānāmaruṇādīnāṃ viṣametadvyapohati |
kākādanīkākamācyoḥ svaraseṣvathavā kṛtam ||31||
sirāśca srāvayet [17] prāptāḥ kuryāt saṃśodhanāni ca |32|

dadhyādīni prasthamātrāṇi, karañjādidravyāṇāṃ dvipalikaṃ bhāgaṃ, salilāḍhakena niṣkvāthya, kvathitaṃ prasthaśeṣaṃ, taistribhiḥ prasthaiḥ saha saṃyojya, trivṛnmūlādikalkapādaṃ ca vipacet; pakvaṃ ca ghṛtaṃ virecanaṃ bhavati| aṃśumatī śālaparṇī, gojī gojihvā, amṛtā guḍūcī, vakraṃ tagaraṃ, sarpagandhā sarpacchatrikā| samṛttikāḥ sakṛṣṇamṛttikāḥ, `ahimṛttikā’ iti kecit paṭhanti, atrāhimṛttikā valmīkamṛttikā; `agavṛttikā’ iti jejjaṭācāryaḥ paṭhati, tatrāgavṛttikā śallakī| dvitīyaṃ ghṛtamāha- kākādanītyādi| kākādanī kṛṣṇaśrīphalikā, kṛtaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ, ghṛtamiti śeṣaḥ| sirā ityādi| prāptā yuktā marmānāśritāḥ| saṃśodhanāni vamanādīni nasyaparyantāni, tānyapi prāptāni; tenāvamyāvirecyayoravarodhaḥ| anye tu viṣāturā avamyā api vamyā eva, `ete'pyajīrṇavyathitā vāmyā ye ca viṣāturā’ iti||25-31||-

sarveṣāṃ ca vidhiḥ kāryo mūṣikāṇāṃ viṣeṣvayam ||32||

aruṇādyāśritaṃ sirāvyadhādikaṃ sāmānyataḥ sarvatrātidiśannāha- sarveṣāmityādi| ayaṃ vidhiḥ sirāvyadhādikaḥ kartavya ityarthaḥ||32||

dagdhvā visrāvayeddaṃśaṃ pracchitaṃ ca pralepayet |
śirīṣarajanīkuṣṭhakuṅkumairamṛtāyutaiḥ ||33||

dagdhvetyādi| dagdhvā `ghṛtenāgnitaptena’ iti śeṣaḥ| visrāvayet pracchannena pracchitaṃ daṃśam| śirīṣādibhiḥ kalkairālepayet| tathaiva kecit pracchitaṃ daṃśaṃ visrāvayecchoṇitaṃ śirīṣādikalkairālepayeccetyāhuḥ||33||

chardanaṃ jālinīkvāthaiḥ śukākhyāṅkoṭhayorapi ||34||

śodhanaṃ sūtritaṃ dravyairvivṛṇvannāha- chardanamityādi| śukākhyaḥ carmakāravaṭaḥ||34||-

śukākhyākoṣavatyośca mūlaṃ madana eva ca |
devadālīphalaṃ caiva dadhnā pītvā viṣaṃ vamet |
sarvamūṣikadaṣṭānāmeṣa yogaḥ sukhāvahaḥ ||35||
phalaṃ vacā devadālī kuṣṭhaṃ gobhūtrapeṣitam |
pūrvakalpena yojyāḥ [18] syuḥ sarvonduruviṣacchidaḥ ||36||

aparāvapi vamanayogāvāha- śukākhyetyādi| koṣavatī ghoṣakaḥ| mūlamityatra phalamityanye| phalamityādi| pūrvakalpena dadhnā lehamityarthaḥ||34-36||

virecane trivṛddantītriphalākalka iṣyate |37|

virecanamāha- virecane ityādi| dantīsthāne kecinnīlīṃ paṭhanti|37|

śirovirecane sāraḥ śirīṣasya phalāni ca ||37||
hitastrikaṭukāḍhyaśca gomayasvaraso'ñjane |38|

śirovirecane ityādi| sāraḥ śirīṣasya, gayadāsastu śālatamālamadhūkānāmāha| phalāni ceti śirīṣasyeti sambandhaḥ| trikaṭukāḍhyaḥ trikaṭukotkaṭa ityarthaḥ| etacca yogāntaram||37||-

kapitthagomayarasau [19] lihyānmākṣikasaṃyutau ||38||
rasāñjanaharidrendrayavakaṭvīṣu vā kṛtam |
prātaḥ sātiviṣaṃ kalkaṃ lihyānmākṣikasaṃyutam ||39||
taṇḍulīyakamūleṣu sarpiḥ siddhaṃ pibennaraḥ |
āsphotamūlasiddhaṃ vā pañcakāpitthameva vā ||40||

saṃśamanayogānāha- kapitthetyādi| pratyekaṃ kapittharaso gomayarasaśca samadhurlehaḥ| rasāñjanetyādi| kaṭvī kaṭurohiṇī| taṇḍulīyaketyādi| kapitthaphalamūlapuṣpatvakpatrakalkakaṣāyasiddhaṃ pañcakāpitthaṃ ghṛtaṃ sarvamūṣikaviṣaharam||38-40||

mūṣikāṇāṃ viṣaṃ prāyaḥ kupyatyabhreṣvanirhṛtam [20] |
tatrāpyeṣa vidhiḥ kāryo yaśca dūṣīviṣāpahaḥ ||41||

praśāntakopasyāpyavaśyaṃ vamanādiśodhanaṃ karaṇīyamiti darśayannāha- mūṣikāṇāmityādi| eṣa vidhiḥ vamanavirecanaśirovirecananayanāñjanādiḥ| yaśca dūṣīviṣāpaho vidhiḥ so'pyatra kāryaḥ; etena dūṣīviṣatulyatā mūṣikaviṣasyoktā||41||

sthirāṇāṃ rujatāṃ vā'pi vraṇānāṃ karṇikāṃ bhiṣak |
pāṭayitvā yathādoṣaṃ vraṇavaccāpi śodhayet ||42||

idānīmākhuviṣavraṇe karṇikā bhavanti tathā'nyaviṣaduṣṭavraṇe'pi, taccikitsāmuddiśannāha- sthirāṇāmityādi| sthairyeṇa kaphātmakatā, rujatāmityanena vātātmakatā vraṇānāmuktā| karṇikāṃ māṃsakarṇīkam| pāṭayitvā vidārya, pāṭayitvetyatra pācayitveti kecit, pātayitvetyapare| lālanaputrakādimūṣikajātiviśeṣajñānaṃ nānādeśīyalokebhyo'vagantavyam||42||

śvaśṛgālatarakṣvṛkṣavyāghrādīnāṃ yadā'nilaḥ |
śleṣmapraduṣṭo muṣṇāti sañjñāṃ sañjñāvahāśritaḥ ||43||
tadā prasrastalāṅgūlahanuskandho'tilālavān |
atyarthabadhiro'ndhaśca [21] so'nyonyamabhidhāvati ||44||
tenonmattena daṣṭasya daṃṣṭriṇā saviṣeṇa tu |
suptatā jāyate daṃśe kṛṣṇaṃ cātisravatyasṛk ||45||
digdhaviddhasya liṅgena prāyaśaścopalakṣitaḥ |46|

idānīṃ mūṣakasya [22] catuṣpadajarāyujahiṃsratvasāmānyācchvādīnāṃ viṣaṃ lakṣaṇacikitsitairuddiśannāha- śvaśṛgālatarakṣvṛkṣetyādi| śvā kukkuraḥ, śṛgālaḥ prasiddhaḥ, tarakṣuḥ mṛgaśatrurvyāghraviśeṣaḥ `jarakha’ iti loke, ṛkṣo bhallūko'tilomaśaḥ `rīccha’ iti prasiddhaḥ; ādiśabdādvṛkacitrakādayo hiṃsrāḥ paśavo grāhyāḥ| śvādīnāṃ yadā'nilo vāyuḥ śleṣmapraduṣṭaḥ san, sañjñāvahāśrito sañjñāvahasrotogataḥ, sañjñāṃ samyagjñānaṃ, muṣṇāti harati| samyagjñānaharaṇācca kīdṛśo bhavatītyāha- tadetyādi| prasrastaṃ sthānacyutaṃ lāṅgūlaṃ bāladhirmehanaṃ vā hanuḥ skandhaśca yasya saḥ; prasrastaśabdo lāṅgūlādibhiḥ pratyekaṃ sambandhanīyaḥ| atilālavān atiśayena lālāsrāvayuktaḥ| atyarthabadhiro'ndhaśca [23] atiśayena badhiro'ndho vetyarthaḥ| anyonyamabhidhāvati parasparaṃ daṣṭumabhidhāvati| hanuskandhasthāne `galaskandha [24] ’ iti kecit paṭhanti| `pramūḍho'nyatamasteṣāṃ khādan viparidhāvati’ iti atra uttarārdhaṃ kecit paṭhanti| eṣāṃ madhye'nyatama ekatamaḥ pramūḍhaḥ san khādan viśeṣeṇa paridhāvati| tenonmattena daṣṭasyetyādi| tena daṃṣṭriṇā saviṣeṇonmattena daṣṭasya puruṣasya daṃśe suptatā bādhirya jāyate| upalakṣito yukta ityarthaḥ||43-45||-

yena cāpi bhaveddaṣṭastasya ceṣṭāṃ rutaṃ naraḥ ||46||
bahuśaḥ pratikurvāṇaḥ kriyāhīno vinaśyati |47|

atraivāriṣṭamuddiśannāha- yena cāpi bhavedityādi| ceṣṭā kriyā, rutaṃ śabdaḥ, bahuśaḥ anekavāraṃ, pratikurvāṇaḥ anukurvan, kriyāhīnaḥ svakīyakāyādivyāpārarahitaḥ||46||-

daṃṣṭriṇā yena daṣṭaśca tadrūpaṃ yastu paśyati ||47||
apsu [25] vā yadi vā''darśe'riṣṭaṃ tasya vinirdiśet |48|

idānīmaparamapyariṣṭaṃ nirdiśannāha- daṃṣṭriṇā yenetyādi| taddaṃṣṭrirūpadarśanamariṣṭaṃ maraṇalakṣaṇam||47||-

trasyatyakasmādyo'bhīkṣṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā [26] spṛṣṭvā'pi vā jalam ||48||
jalatrāsaṃ tu vidyāttaṃ riṣṭaṃ tadapi [27] kīrtitam |49|

punaraparamapyariṣṭaṃ nirdiśannāha- trasyatītyādi| akasmāt jalatrāsahetuṃ vinā| apiśabdācchrutvā'pi| jalatrāsaṃ jalatrāsarogaṃ jānīyāttasyeti sambandhaḥ| riṣṭamityādi| tadapi jalatrasanamariṣṭaṃ maraṇalakṣaṇaṃ kīrtitaṃ; etaccāriṣṭaṃ daṣṭasya, adaṣṭe jalatrasanāriṣṭasya vakṣyamāṇatvāt| atrārthe tantrāntaram,- “vyādhitena śvādinā daṣṭasya śleṣmā prakupitaścetovāhinīrdhamanīranupraviśya sañjñānāśamāpādayati sadyaḥ kālāntarādvā”; avabhṛte viśeṣataḥ- “tato naraḥ spṛṣṭvā dṛṣṭvā śrutvā vā jalaṃ trasyati, tasyāpi tadariṣṭaṃ jānīyāt” iti| gayadāsastu `yadi trasyatyadaṣṭo'pi’ ityādi paṭhati||48||-

adaṣṭo vā jalatrāsī na kathañcana sidhyati ||49||
prasupto'thotthito vā'pi svasthastrasto na sidhyati |50|

evamadaṣṭasyāpi jalatrāso'riṣṭameveti darśayannāha- adaṣṭa ityādi| asminnarthe tantrāntaram,- “adaṣṭasyāpi jantorhi jalatrāso bhavedyadi| tasya riṣṭaṃ hi bhiṣajo bruvate viṣacintakāḥ” iti| jalaṃ vinā'pi jalatrāso jāyate śleṣmasañcayāt| tathā coktaṃ,- “buddhisthānaṃ yadā śleṣmā kevalaṃ [28] pratipadyate| tadā buddhau niruddhāyāṃ śleṣmaṇā'dhiṣṭhito naraḥ|jāgrat supto'thavā''tmānaṃ majjantamiva manyate| salile trasyati tadā jalatrāsaṃ tu taṃ viduḥ|| śleṣmaghnaṃ tatra kartavyaṃ śodhanaṃ śamanāni ca| āhārasya vidhānena yāvat sa prakṛtiṃ vrajet”- iti| ayaṃ jalatrāsaḥ kupitakaphasya bhavatīti riṣṭaṃ na bhavati, ata evātra cikitsopadeśaḥ, daṣṭasya tu riṣṭameva| tathā svasthasya suptasya jāgrato vā jalamantareṇa trāso'riṣṭameveti darśayannāha- prasupto'thotthito vā'pītyādi||49||-

daṃśaṃ visrāvya tairdaṣṭe sarpiṣā paridāhitam ||50||
pradihyādagadaiḥ, sarpiḥ purāṇaṃ pāyayeta ca |
arkakṣīrayutaṃ hyasya [29] dadyāccāpi viśodhanam ||51||
