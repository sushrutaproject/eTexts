--- title: "30. nivṛttasaṃtāpīyarasāyanam" adhyaya: 30 order: 30 ---

athāto nivṛttasantāpīyaṃ rasāyanaṃ [1] vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

nivṛttasantāpaśabdamadhikṛtya kṛtaṃ rasāyanaṃ nivṛttasantāpīyam| tadadhikṛtya kṛto grantha itīyapratyayaḥ||1-2||

yathā nivṛttasantāpā modante divi devatāḥ |
tathauṣadhīrimāḥ prāpya modante bhuvi mānavāḥ ||3||

mahauṣadhirasāyanasyopādeyatvārthaṃ sāmānyaphalairauṣadhipraśaṃsāmuddiśannāha- yathetyādi| nivṛttavyādhitvena śārīramānasikaduḥkharahitāḥ, modante hṛṣyantiḥ, etena manaḥsukhaṃ darśayati| divi svarge||3||

atha khalu sapta puruṣā rasāyanaṃ nopayuñjīran; tadyathā- anātmavānalaso daridraḥ pramādī vyasanī pāpakṛd bheṣajāpamānī ceti |
saptabhireva kāraṇairna sampadyate; tadyathā- ajñānādanārambhādasthiracittatvāddāridryādanāyattatvādadharmādauṣadhālābhācceti [2] ||4||

anātmavān apraśastātmā mithyācārapravṛtto mūḍhabuddhiḥ| pramādī pramādaśīlo'navahitacitta ityarthaḥ| vyasanāni dyūtamadyaveśyādīni santyasyāsau vyasanī| sapta kāraṇāni ca santi anātmavadādīnāṃ saptānāṃ puṃsāmajñānādikāraṇaiḥ saptabhireva yathāyathaṃ rasāyanaṃ na prayuñjyāt| tatrānātmano'jñānāt, daridrasyālasasyānārambhānavasthitacittatvānna pravṛttiḥ, evaṃ pramādivyasanipāpakṛdādīnāmapyapravṛttiranumeyeti||4||

athauṣadhīrvyākhyāsyāmaḥ- tatrājagarī, śvetakāpotī, kṛṣṇakāpotī, gonasī, vārāhī, kanyā, chatrā, aticchatrā, kareṇuḥ, ajā, cakrakā, ādityaparṇī, brahmasuvarcalā, śrāvaṇī, mahāśrāvaṇī, golomī, ajalomī, mahāvegavatī, cetyaṣṭādaśa somasamavīryā mahauṣadhayo vyākhyātāḥ |
tāsāṃ somavat kriyāśīḥstutayaḥ śāstre'bhihitāḥ |
tāsāmāgāre'bhihutānāṃ yāḥ kṣīravatyastāsāṃ kṣīrakuḍavaṃ sakṛdevopayuñjīta [3] , yāstvakṣīrā mūlavatyastāsāṃ pradeśinīpramāṇāni trīṇi kāṇḍāni pramāṇamupayoge, śvetakāpotī samūlapatrā [4] bhakṣayitavyā, gonasyajagarīkṛṣṇakāpotīnāṃ sanakhamuṣṭiṃ khaṇḍaśaḥ kalpayitvā kṣīreṇa vipācya parisrāvyābhighāritamabhihutaṃ ca sakṛdevopayuñjīta, cakrakāyāḥ payaḥ sakṛdeva, brahmasuvarcalā saptarātramupayoktavyā bhakṣyakalpena, śeṣāṇāṃ pañca pañca palāni kṣīrāḍhakakvathitāni prasthe'vaśiṣṭe'vatārya parisrāvya sakṛdevopayuñjīta |
somavadāhāravihārau vyākhyātau, kevalaṃ navanītamabhyaṅgārthe, śeṣaṃ somavadānirgamāditi ||5||

somavaditi adhvaravidhivarjamanyatsarvaṃ somavat kriyāvidhānam| āśīrmaṅgalapāṭhādiḥ, stutiḥ phalavādaḥ; gayī tu kriyā karaṇaṃ vidhānaṃ, āśīḥ phalaṃ, tasya stutayaḥ somasyaiva; śāstre āgame abhihitāḥ| etena tanmate somasyevādhvaravidhirapi prāpyate| abhihutānāṃ kṛtahomānām| kṣīrakuḍavamaṣṭau palāni, mahatī mātrā| pradeśinīpramāṇāni āyāmavistārābhyām| kṣīreṇa vipācyeti “dravyādaṣṭaguṇaṃ kṣīraṃ kṣīrāttoyaṃ caturguṇam| kṣīrāvaśeṣaḥ kartavyaḥ kṣīrapāke tvayaṃ vidhiḥ” anena vidhinā| abhighāritamabhihutamiti pūrvamabhighāritaṃ paścādabhihutaṃ; abhighāritam īṣaddhṛtāktam| tatra cakrakāyāḥ payaḥ sakṛdeva| gayī tu ‘tatra cakrakāyāḥ [5] pāyasam’ iti paṭhati, vyākhyānayati ca- tatra cakrakāyāḥ [6] palakalkaṃ dviguṇitataṇḍulaṃ kṣīreṇa sādhyaṃ, sakṛdevopayuñjīta| bhakṣyakalpeneti apūpādividhānena| ānirgamāditi gūḍhāgāraṃ kṛtvā tatrāvāsaparyantamityarthaḥ||5||

bhavanti cātra-
yuvānaṃ siṃhavikrāntaṃ kāntaṃ śrutanigādinam |
kuryuretāḥ krameṇaiva dvisahasrāyuṣaṃ naram ||6||
aṅgadī kuṇḍalī maulī divyasrakcandanāmbaraḥ |
caratyamoghasaṅkalpo nabhasyambudadurgame ||7||
vrajanti pakṣiṇo yena jalalambāśca toyadāḥ |
gatiḥ sauṣadhisiddhasya somasiddhe gatiḥ [7] parā ||8||

ataḥ paraṃ phalaśrutimāha- yuvānamityādi| vikrāntaṃ vikramayuktam| kāntaṃ manojñam| krameṇaiveti śāstravihitasakalakrameṇa| aṅgadī keyūrayuktaḥ, kuṇḍalī kuṇḍalayuktaḥ, maulī mukuṭadhārī, srak mālā, ambaraṃ vastram, amoghasaṅkalpaḥ saphalakāmitaḥ| nabhasi ākāśe| ambudadurgame meghairduḥkhasañcāre| somasiddhe gatiḥ pareti somasiddhe puruṣe [8] punarevamutkṛṣṭaṃ gamanaṃ bhavatītyarthaḥ||6-8||

atha vakṣyāmi vijñānamauṣadhīnāṃ pṛthak pṛthak |
maṇḍalaiḥ kapilaiścitraiḥ sarpābhā pañcaparṇinī ||9||
pañcāratnipramāṇā ca vijñeyā'jagarī budhaiḥ |
niṣpatrā kanakābhāsā mūle dvyaṅgulasammitā ||10||
sarpākārā lohitāntā śvetakāpotirucyate |
dviparṇinīṃ mūlabhavāmaruṇāṃ kṛṣṇamaṇḍalām ||11||
dvyaratnimātrāṃ jānīyādgonasīṃ gonasākṛtim |
sakṣīrāṃ romaśāṃ mṛdvīṃ rasenekṣurasopamām ||12||
evaṃrūparasāṃ cāpi kṛṣṇakāpotimādiśet |
kṛṣṇasarpasvarūpeṇa vārāhī kandasambhavā ||13||
ekapatrā mahāvīryā bhinnāñjanasamaprabhā |
chatrāticchatrake vidyādrakṣoghne kandasambhave ||14||
jarāmṛtyunivāriṇyau śvetakāpotisaṃsthite |
kāntairdvādaśabhiḥ patrairmayūrāṅgaruhopamaiḥ ||15||
kandajā kāñcanakṣīrī kanyā nāma mahauṣadhī |
kareṇuḥ subahukṣīrā kandena gajarūpiṇī ||16||
hastikarṇapalāśasya tulyaparṇā dviparṇinī |
ajāstanābhakandā tu sakṣīrā kṣuparūpiṇī ||17||
ajā mahauṣadhī jñeyā śaṅkhakundendupāṇḍurā |
śvetāṃ vicitrakusumāṃ kākādanyā samāṃ kṣupām ||18||
cakrakāmoṣadhīṃ [9] vidyājjarāmṛtyunivāriṇīm |
mūlinī pañcabhiḥ patraiḥ suraktāṃśukakomalaiḥ ||19||
ādityaparṇinī jñeyā sadā''dityānuvartinī |
kanakābhā jalānteṣu sarvataḥ parisarpati ||20||
sakṣīrā padminīprakhyā devī brahmasuvarcalā |
aratnimātrakṣupakā patraidrvyaṅgulasammitaiḥ ||21||
puṣpairnīlotpalākāraiḥ phalaiścāñjanasannibhaiḥ |
śrāvaṇī mahatī jñeyā kanakābhā payasvinī ||22||
śrāvaṇī pāṇḍurābhāsā mahāśrāvaṇilakṣaṇā |
golomī cājalomī ca romaśe kandasambhave ||23||
haṃsapādīva vicchinnaiḥ patrairmūlasamudbhavaiḥ |
athavā śaṅkhapuṣpyā ca samānā sarvarūpataḥ ||24||
vegena mahatā''viṣṭā sarpanirmokasannibhā |
eṣā vegavatī nāma jāyate hyambudakṣaye ||25||

vistṛtakaniṣṭhāṅgulimuṣṭikahastaḥ aratniḥ| śvetakāpotisaṃsthite iti śvetakāpotikākṛtītyarthaḥ| rakṣoghne iti svabhāvena rakṣasāṃ pradhvaṃsakare| kāñcanavarṇaṃ kṣīraṃ yasyāḥ sā kāñcanakṣīrī| kṣuparūpiṇī viṭaparūpiṇī, baddhamuṣṭikaraḥ aratniḥ| nivāte'pi svabhāvavegenāviṣṭā| ambudakṣaye śaradi||9-25||

saptādau sarparūpiṇyo hyauṣadhyo yāḥ prakīrtitāḥ |
tāsāmuddharaṇaṃ kāryaṃ mantreṇānena sarvadā ||26||
mahendrarāmakṛṣṇānāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ gavāmapi |
tapasā tejasā vā'pi praśāmyadhvaṃ śivāya vai ||27||
mantreṇānena matimān sarvā evābhimantrayet |
aśraddadhānairalasaiḥ kṛtaghnaiḥ pāpakarmabhiḥ ||28||
naivāsādayituṃ śakyāḥ somāḥ somasamāstathā |29|

anena mantreṇoddharaṇamutpāṭanamauṣadhaparirakṣārthamabhimataphalaprāptyarthaṃ [10] ca| mantramāha- mahendretyādi| praśāmyadhvam upaśamaṃ kuruta; kimarthaṃ? śivāya kalyāṇāya| vai niścitam| naivāsādayituṃ naiva prāptum||26-28||-

pītāvaśeṣamamṛtaṃ devairbrahmapurogamaiḥ ||29||
nihitaṃ somavīryāsu some cāpyoṣadhīpatau |30|

paurāṇikīṃ śrutiṃ darśayannāha- pītetyādi| nihitaṃ dhṛtam| somavīryāsu auṣadhiṣu| some candramasi||29||-

devasunde hradavare tathā sindhau mahānade ||30||
dṛśyate ca jalānteṣu medhyā brahmasuvarcalā |
ādityaparṇinī jñeyā tathaiva himasaṅkṣaye ||31||
dṛśyate'jagarī nityaṃ gonasī cāmbudāgame |
kāśmīreṣu saro divyaṃ nāmnā kṣudrakamānasam ||32||
kareṇustatra kanyā ca chatrāticchatrake tathā |
golomī cājalomī ca mahatī śrāvaṇī tathā ||33||
vasante kṛṣṇasarpākhyā gonasī ca pradṛśyate |
kauśikīṃ saritaṃ tīrtvā sañjayantyāstu pūrvataḥ ||34||
kṣitipradeśo valmīkairācito yojanatrayam |
vijñeyā tatra kāpotī śvetā valmīkamūrdhasu ||35||
malaye nalasetau ca vegavatyauṣadhī dhruvā |
kārtikyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ ca bhakṣayettāmupoṣitaḥ [11] ||36||
somavaccātra varteta phalaṃ tāvacca kīrtitam |
sarvā viceyāstvoṣadhyaḥ somāścāpyarbude girau ||37||
sa śṛṅgairdevacaritairambudānīkabhedibhiḥ |
vyāptastīrthaiśca vikhyātaiḥ siddharṣisurasevitaiḥ ||38||
guhābhirbhīmarūpābhiḥ siṃhonnāditakukṣibhiḥ |
gajāloḍitatoyābhirāpagābhiḥ samantataḥ |
vividhairdhātubhiścitraiḥ sarvatraivopaśobhitaḥ ||39||
nadīṣu śaileṣu saraḥsu cāpi puṇyeṣvaraṇyeṣu tathā''śrameṣu |
sarvatra sarvāḥ parimārgitavyāḥ sarvatra bhūmirhi vasūni dhatte ||40||

deśamāha- devasunde ityādi| hrade jalāśayaviśeṣe| mahānada iti loke prasiddhaḥ| himasaṅkṣaye vasante| ambudāgame varṣāsu| kṛṣṇasarpākhyā vārāhī| vijñeyā anusaraṇīyāḥ| kutaḥ punaratra girau sarvā evānusaraṇīyā ityāha- sa ityādi| yato giriruktaviśeṣaṇaḥ [12] | ambudānīkaṃ meghaghaṭā| dhātubhiḥ gairikādibhiḥ| punaḥ kiṃviśiṣṭo giriḥ? guhābhiruktaviśeṣaṇābhirvyāptaḥ, siṃhonnāditakukṣibhiḥ siṃhairunnāditāḥ kukṣayo yāsāṃ tāstābhiḥ, punarāpagābhirnadībhiruktaviśeṣaṇābhirupalakṣitaḥ| hi yasmādarthe| yasmāt kāraṇāt sarvatra sarvadeśeṣu, bhūmiriyaṃ ratnāni dhārayatītyarthaḥ||30-40||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne nivṛttasantāpīyaṃ rasāyanaṃ nāma triṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||30||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne triṃśattamo'dhyāyaḥ||30||

2. ‘…danāyatanādanāyāsādau...’ iti pā.|

4. ‘samūlaphalavṛntā’ iti pā.|
5. ‘kanyakāyāḥ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘kanyakāyāḥ’ iti pā.|

8. ‘somasiddhasya’ iti pā.|
9. ‘takrakāṃ’ iti ‘nakrakāṃ’ iti ca pā.|
10. ‘bhiṣagāturaparirakṣārthaṃ’ iti pā.|
11. ‘dṛśyate nātra saṃśayaḥ’ iti pā.|
