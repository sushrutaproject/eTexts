--- title: "21. śūkarogacikitsitam" adhyaya: 21 order: 21 ---

athātaḥ śūkadoṣacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||

śūkādidoṣacikitsitamiti jñeyam, ādiśabdasya luptatvāt; tatrādiśabdena nidānoktā abhighātanimittā durbhaganimittāścātra jñātavyāḥ||1-2||

saṃlikhya sarṣapīṃ samyak kaṣāyairavacūrṇayet |
kaṣāyeṣveva tailaṃ ca kurvīta vraṇaropaṇam ||3||

saṃlikhyetyādi| kaṣāyairiti kaṣāyavṛkṣacūrṇairavacūrṇayet, kaṣāyavṛkṣairmiśrakoktaśodhanakaṣāyasādhanadravyaiścūrṇīkṛtairavacūrṇayedityarthaḥ| teṣu ropaṇakaṣāyasādhanadravyeṣu pādikeṣu jalaṃ caturguṇaṃ dattvā ropaṇaṃ tailaṃ kuryāt||3||

aṣṭhīlikāṃ jalaukobhirgrāhayecca [1] punaḥ punaḥ |
tathā cānupaśāmyantīṃ kaphagranthivaduddharet ||4||

aṣṭhīlikāmityādi| kaphagrantheriva śastreṇoddharaṇam||4||

svedayedgrathitaṃ śaśvannāḍīsvedena buddhimān |
sukhoṣṇairupanāhaiśca susnigdhairupanāhayet ||5||
kumbhīkāṃ pākamāpannāṃ bhindyācchuddhāṃ tu ropayet |
tailena triphalālodhratindukāmrātakena tu ||6||

svedayedityādi| śaśvat pratidinam| susnigdhairityupanāhaviśeṣaṇam| tailena triphalādikalkapādena jalacaturguṇasiddhena ropayet||5-6||

grāhayitvā jalaukobhiralajīṃ secayettataḥ |
kaṣāyaisteṣu siddhaṃ ca tailaṃ ropaṇamiṣyate ||7||

grāhayitvetyādi| kaṣāyaiḥ kaṣāyavṛkṣakaṣāyaiḥ, kaṣāyavṛkṣāḥ plakṣādayaḥ||7||

balātailena koṣṇena mṛditaṃ pariṣecayet |
madhuraiḥ sarpiṣā snigdhaiḥ sukhoṣṇairupanāhayet ||8||

baletyādi| madhurāṇi kākolyādīni||8||

sammūḍhapiḍakāṃ kṣipraṃ jalaukobhirupācaret |
bhittvā paryāgatāṃ cāpi lepayet kṣaudrasarpiṣā ||9||

sammūḍhetyādi| paryāgatā pakvā||9||

avamanthe gate pākaṃ bhinne tailaṃ vidhīyate |
dhavāśvakarṇapattaṅgasallakītindukīkṛtam ||10||
kriyāṃ puṣkarikāyāṃ tu śītāṃ sarvāṃ prayojayet |
jalaukobhirhareccāsṛk sarpiṣā cāvasecayet ||11||
sparśahānyāṃ haredraktaṃ pradihyānmadhurairapi |
kṣīrekṣurasasarpirbhiḥ secayecca suśītalaiḥ ||12||

avamanthetyādi| aśvakarṇaḥ pūrvadeśe ‘gandhamuṇḍa’ iti khyāto'śvatthasadṛśaḥ| pattaṅgaṃ raktacandanam||10-12||

piḍakāmuttamākhyāṃ ca baḍiśenoddharedbhiṣak |
uddhṛtya madhusaṃyuktaiḥ kaṣāyairavacūrṇayet ||13||

piḍakāmityādi| kaṣāyaiḥ cūrṇairityarthaḥ| śodhanakāle tu śodhanadravyaiścūrṇīkṛtairavacūrṇayet, ropaṇakāle tu ropaṇaireva||13||

rasakriyā vidhātavyā likhite śataponake |
pṛthakparṇyādisiddhaṃ ca deyaṃ tailamanantaram ||14||
kriyāṃ kuryādbhiṣak prājñastvakpākasya visarpavat |15|

rasakriyetyādi| rasakriyā phāṇitākṛtiḥ, śodhanakāle śodhanadravyakaṣāyakṛtā, ropaṇakāle ropaṇadravyakaṣāyakṛtā| pṛthakparṇyādisiddhaṃ tailaṃ sāmānyasnehaparibhāṣākalpena; pṛthakparṇyādīni miśrake ropaṇaghṛtapaṭhitāni kākolyādivarjitāni| visarpavat pittavisarpavadityarthaḥ||14||-

raktavidradhivaccāpi kriyā śoṇitaje'rbude ||15||
kaṣāyakalkasarpīṃṣi tailaṃ cūrṇaṃ rasakriyām |
śodhanaṃ ropaṇaṃ caiva vīkṣya vīkṣyāvacārayet ||16||
hitaṃ ca sarpiṣaḥ pānaṃ pathyaṃ cāpi virecanam |
hitaḥ śoṇitamokṣaśca yaccāpi laghu bhojanam ||17||
arbudaṃ māṃsapākaṃ ca vidradhiṃ tilakālakam |
pratyākhyāya prakurvīta bhiṣak samyak pratikriyām ||18||

raktetyādi| śoṇitaje arbude liṅgaje raktārbude| vīkṣya vīkṣyeti dvivraṇīyoktāmavasthāṃ samyak nirūpyetyarthaḥ| avacārayet kaṣāyādīnītyarthaḥ||15-18||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne śūkarogacikitsitaṃ nāmaikaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||21||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne ekaviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ||21||

1. ‘grāhayet kuśalo bhiṣak’ iti pā.|
