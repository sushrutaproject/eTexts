--- title: "19. vṛddhyupadaṃśaślīpadacikitsitam" adhyaya: 19 order: 19 ---

athāto vṛddhyupadaṃśaślīpadacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
antravṛddhyā vinā ṣaḍyā vṛddhayastāsu varjayet |
aśvādiyānaṃ vyāyāmaṃ maithunaṃ veganigraham ||3||
atyāsanaṃ caṅkramaṇamupavāsaṃ gurūṇi ca |4|

antravṛddhyetyādi| caṅkramaṇaṃ bhramaṇam||1-3||-

tatrādito vātavṛddhau traivṛtasnigdhamāturam ||4||
svinnaṃ cainaṃ yathānyāyaṃ pāyayeta virecanam |
kośāmratilvakairaṇḍaphalatailāni vā naram ||5||
sakṣīraṃ vā pibenmāsaṃ tailameraṇḍasambhavam |
tataḥ kāle'nilaghnānāṃ kvāthaiḥ kalkaiśca buddhimān ||6||
nirūhayennirūḍhaṃ ca bhuktavantaṃ rasaudanam |
yaṣṭīmadhukasiddhena tatastailena yojayet ||7||
snehopanāhau kuryācca pradehāṃścānilāpahān |
vidagdhāṃ pācayitvā vā sevanīṃ parivarjayet ||8||
bhindyāttataḥ prabhinnāyāṃ yathoktaṃ kramamācaret |9|

tatretyādi| traivṛtamapatānakaharaṃ vātavyādhyuktaṃ, naraṃ pāyayeteti sambandhaḥ| kāle āvasthike| nirūḍhaṃ ca yaṣṭīmadhukasiddhatailena yojayedanuvāsanakalpavidhinā| yathoktāmityādi| yathā yena prakāreṇokto dvivraṇīyakramastamācaret||4-8||-

pittajāyāmapakvāyāṃ pittagranthikramo hitaḥ ||9||
pakvāṃ vā bhedayedbhinnāṃ śodhayet kṣaudrasarpiṣā |
śuddhāyāṃ ca bhiṣagdadyāttailaṃ kalkaṃ ca ropaṇam ||10||
raktajāyāṃ jalaukobhiḥ śoṇitaṃ nirharedbhiṣak [1] |
pibedvirecanaṃ vā'pi śarkarākṣaudrasaṃyutam ||11||
pittagranthikramaṃ kuryādāme pakve ca sarvadā |12|

pittajāyāmityādi| kalkaṃ ca ropaṇaṃ dvivraṇīyoktakalkaṃ; gayī tu miśrakādhyāyoktamiti kathayati||9-11||-

vṛddhiṃ kaphātmikāmuṣṇairmūtrapiṣṭaiḥ pralepayet ||12||
pītadārukaṣāyaṃ ca pibenmūtreṇa saṃyutam |
vimlāpanādṛte vā'pi śleṣmagrānthikramo hitaḥ ||13||
pakvāyāṃ ca vibhinnāyāṃ tailaṃ śodhanamiṣyate |
sumanāruṣkarāṅkoṭhasaptaparṇeṣu sādhitam ||14||

vṛddhimityādi| uṣṇairuṣṇavīryairvacādipippalyādimuṣkakādigaṇaiḥ; anye tu, uṣṇavīryairajagandhādibhirmiśrakoktaiḥ| pītetyādi| gomūtrakvathitasyaiva dāruharidrākaṣāyasya vā pānaṃ, pītadārusthāne keciddevadāruṃ paṭhanti| vimlāpanādityādi| sumanāruṣkarādikalkakaṣāyasiddhaṃ tailam||12-14||

medaḥsamutthāṃ saṃsvedya lepayet surasādinā |
śirovirekadravyairvā sukhoṣṇairmūtrasaṃyutaiḥ ||15||
svinnāṃ cāveṣṭya paṭṭena samāśvāsya tu mānavam |
rakṣan phale sevanīṃ ca vṛddhipatreṇa dārayet ||16||
bhedastataḥ samuddhṛtya dadyāt kāsīsasaindhave |
badhnīyācca yathoddiṣṭaṃ śuddhe tailaṃ ca dāpayet ||17||
manaḥśilālalavaṇaiḥ siddhamāruṣkareṣu ca |18|

meda ityādi| surasādinā mūtrapiṣṭena lepaḥ| śirovirecanadravyāṇi pippalīviḍaṅgāpāmārgaśigruśirīṣasiddhārthakādīni saṃśodhanasaṃśamanīyoktāni| tairapi gomūtrapiṣṭaiḥ sukhoṣṇairlepaḥ| svinnāmityādi| yathoddiṣṭaṃ ca badhnīyāt gophaṇayā sthagikābandhena vā||15-17||-

mūtrajāṃ svedayitvā tu vastrapaṭṭena veṣṭayet ||18||
sevanyāḥ pārśvato'dhastādvidhyed vrīhimukhena tu |
athātra dvimukhāṃ nāḍīṃ dattvā visrāvayedbhiṣak ||19||
mūtraṃ, nāḍīmathoddhṛtya sthagikābandhamācaret |
śuddhāyāṃ ropaṇaṃ dadyādvarjayedantrahaitukīm ||20||
aprāptaphalakoṣāyāṃ vātavṛddhikramo hitaḥ |
tatra yā vaṅkṣaṇasthā tāṃ [2] dahedardhenduvaktrayā ||21||
samyaṅmārgāvarodhārthaṃ kośaprāptāṃ tu varjayet |
tvacaṃ bhittvā'ṅguṣṭhamadhye daheccāṅgaviparyayāt ||22||
anenaiva vidhānena vṛddhī vātakaphātmike |
pradahet prayataḥ kintu snāyucchedo'dhikastayoḥ ||23||
śaṅkhopari ca karṇānte tyaktvā yatnena sevanīm |
vyatyāsādvā sirāṃ vidhyedantravṛddhinivṛttaye ||24||

mūtrajāmityādi| varjayedantrahaitukīṃ ‘kośaprāptām’ iti vākyaśeṣaḥ| vātavṛddhikramo hita iti snehapānādiranuvāsanāntaḥ| tatretyādi| ardhenduvaktrayā śalākayā| tvaca bhittveti tvaṅmātraṃ chittvā| aṅgaviparyayāditi vāmāntravṛddhau [3] dakṣiṇāṅguṣṭhamadhye, dakṣiṇāntravṛddhau [4] vāmāṅguṣṭhamadhye| śaṅkhabhruvorantopari karṇalalāṭayormadhye antravṛddhernivṛttaye ‘vaṅkṣaṇasthāyā’ iti vākyaśeṣaḥ, sirāṃ vidhyet||18-24||

upadaṃśeṣu sādhyeṣu snigdhasvinnasya dehinaḥ |
sirāṃ vidhyenmeḍhramadhye pātayedvā jalaukasaḥ ||25||
haredubhayataścāpi doṣānatyarthamucchritān |
sadyo'pahṛtadoṣasya rukśophāvupaśāmyataḥ ||26||
yadi vā durbalo janturna vā prāptaṃ virecanam |
nirūheṇa harettasya doṣānatyarthamucchritān ||27||

upadaṃśeṣvityādi| tatra sirāṃ vidhyedbahudoṣe, alpadoṣe tu jalaukāpātaḥ; pittaraktajayormṛdutvājjalaukasetyanye; jejjaṭastu anavagāḍhe jalaukasā, avagāḍhe sirāvyadha iti| haredityādi| balavato virecyasya vamanavirecanābhyāṃ doṣaharaṇam| yadi vetyādi| durbalāvirecyayoḥ punaratyarthocchritadoṣayordoṣaharaṇānukūlyena nirūheṇa haraṇam| na vā prāptaṃ na yuktamityarthaḥ||25-27||

prapauṇḍarīkayaṣṭyāhvavarṣābhūkuṣṭhadārubhiḥ |
saralāgururāsnābhirvātajaṃ sampralepayet ||28||
niculairaṇḍabījāni yavagodhūmasaktavaḥ |
etaiśca vātajaṃ snigdhaiḥ sukhoṣṇaiḥ sampralepayet ||29||
prapauṇḍarīkapūrvaiśca dravyaiḥ sekaḥ praśasyate |30|

prapauṇḍarīketyādi| saralā trivṛt| niculetyādi| niculo vetasaḥ| prapauṇḍarīkapūrvaiḥ prapauṇḍarīkādibhiḥ, pūrvauktairvātopadaṃśoktaiḥ| sekaḥ pariṣekaḥ||28-29||-

gairikāñjanayaṣṭyāhvasārivośīrapadmakaiḥ ||30||
sacandanotpalaiḥ snigdhaiḥ paittikaṃ sampralepayet |
padmotpalamṛṇālaiśca sasarjārjunavetasaiḥ ||31||
sarpiḥsnigdhaiḥ samadhukaiḥ paittikaṃ sampralepayet |
secayecca ghṛtakṣīraśarkarekṣumadhūdakaiḥ ||32||
athavā'pi suśītena kaṣāyeṇa vaṭādinā |33|

gairiketyādi| añjanaṃ rasāñjanam| mṛṇālaṃ padmanālam| secayet pariṣecayedityarthaḥ||30-32||-

sālāśvakarṇājakarṇadhavatvagbhiḥ kaphotthitam ||33||
surāpiṣṭābhiruṣṇābhiḥ satailābhiḥ pralepayet |
rajanyativiṣāmustāsurasāsuradārubhiḥ [5] ||34||
sapatrapāṭhāpattūrairathavā sampralepayet |
surasāragvadhādyośca kvāthābhyāṃ pariṣecayet ||35||

śālaḥ śaṅkuḥ, aśvakarṇaḥ pūrvadeśaprasiddho'śvatthasadṛśaḥ, ajakarṇaḥ ‘piyāsāl’ iti loke| pattūraḥ sirivālikā||33-35||

evaṃ saṃśodhanālepasekaśoṇitamokṣaṇaiḥ |
pratikuryāt kriyāyogaiḥ prāksthānoktairhitairapi ||36||
na yāti ca yathā pākaṃ prayateta tathā bhiṣak |
vidagdhaistu sirāsnāyutvaṅmāṃsaiḥ kṣīyate dhvajaḥ ||37||
śastreṇopacareccāpi pākamāgatamāśu vai |
tadā'pohya tilaiḥ sarpiḥkṣaudrayuktaiḥ pralepayet ||38||
karavīrasya patrāṇi jātyāragvadhayostathā |
prakṣālane prayojyāni vaijayantyarkayorapi ||39||

evaṃ pūrvoktaprakāreṇa pratikuryāt, ‘āmopadaṃśam’ [6] iti śeṣaḥ| prāksthānaṃ sūtrasthānaṃ, tatra miśrakoktaiḥ; apiśabdād dvivraṇīyoktaiḥ| ‘tasmāt saṃśodhanālepasekaśoṇitamokṣaṇaiḥ| pratikuryāt kriyāyogaiḥ prāksthānoktairhitairapi’ iti kecit paṭhanti| tadā'pohya pūyamupahṛtya| prakṣālane vraṇasya karavīrapatrāṇi, teṣāṃ kaṣāya ityarthaḥ; tathaivāparayorvaijayantyarkayoḥ patrāṇāṃ kaṣāyaḥ; vaijayantī agnimanthavṛkṣaḥ [7] ||36-39||

saurāṣṭrīṃ gairikaṃ tutthaṃ puṣpakāsīsasaindhavam |
rodhraṃ rasāñjanaṃ dārvīṃ haritālaṃ manaḥśilām ||40||
hareṇukaile ca tathā sūkṣmacūrṇāni kārayet |
taccūrṇaṃ kṣaudrasaṃyuktamupadaṃśeṣu pūjitam ||41||

saurāṣṭrī tuvaramṛttikā| puṣpaṃ puṣpāñjanam||40-41||

jambvāmrasumanānimbaśvetakāmbojipallavāḥ |
śallakībadarībilvapalāśatiniśatvacaḥ ||42||
kṣīriṇāṃ ca tvaco yojyāḥ kvāthe triphalayā saha |
tena kvāthena niyataṃ vraṇaṃ prakṣālayedbhiṣak ||43||
asminneva kaṣāye tu tailaṃ dhīro vipācayet |
gojīviḍaṅgayaṣṭībhiḥ sarvagandhaiśca saṃyutam ||44||
etat sarvopadaṃśeṣu śreṣṭhaṃ ropaṇamiṣyate |45|

jambvāmretyādi| śvetaḥ śvetasyandaḥ; kāmbojikā kūvikā, gayī tu māṣaparṇīmāha| tiniśaḥ syandanaḥ| kṣīriṇo vaṭādayaḥ| asminnityādi| gojī gojihvā| sarvagandhairelādiparipaṭhitaiḥ| sāmānyakaṣāyapākakalpitena caturguṇena kaṣāyeṇa siddhaṃ tilatailamakalkameva, kaṣāyopādānāt, kalkamapyanantaroktauṣadhagaṇāt kartavyamityanye, kaṣāyaśabdasya kalke'pi vṛtteḥ||42-44||-

svarjikātutthakāsīsaṃ śaileyaṃ ca rasāñjanam ||45||
manaḥśilāsamaiścūrṇaṃ vraṇavīsarpanāśanam |
gundrāṃ dagdhvā kṛtaṃ bhasma haritālaṃ manaḥśilā ||46||
upadaṃśabisarpāṇāmetacchāntikaraṃ param |
mārkavastriphalā dantī tāmracūrṇamayorajaḥ ||47||
upadaṃśaṃ nihantyeṣa vṛkṣamindrāśaniryathā |48|

mārkavo bhṛṅgarājaḥ||45-47||-

upadaṃśadvaye'pyetāṃ pratyākhyāyācaret kriyām ||48||
tayoreva ca yā yogyā vīkṣya doṣabalābalam |49|

upadaṃśayordvayorapi raktasannipātajayostulyakāraṇatvāttulyaiva cikitsā| sannipātaprabhave tu vātādicikitsitamekīkṛtya dadyāt||48||-

upadaṃśe viśeṣeṇa śṛṇu bhūyastridoṣaje ||49||
duṣṭavraṇavidhiṃ kuryāt kuthitaṃ mehanaṃ tyajet |
jambvoṣṭhenāgnivarṇena paścāccheṣaṃ dahedbhiṣak ||50||
samyagdagdhaṃ ca vijñāya madhusarpiḥ prayojayet |
śuddhe ca ropaṇaṃ dadyāt kalkaṃ tailaṃ hitaṃ ca yat ||51||

upadaṃśe ityādi| kuthitaṃ mehanaṃ tyajediti yāvanmātraṃ mehanaṃ kuthitaṃ bhavettāvanmātraṃ chindyādityarthaḥ| jambvauṣṭhaḥ jambūphalasadṛśamukhāgrā kṛṣṇapāṣāṇaracitā vartiḥ||49-51||

snehasvedopapanne tu ślīpade'nilaje bhiṣak |
kṛtvā gulphopari sirāṃ vidhyettu caturaṅgule ||52||
samāpyāyitadehaṃ ca bastibhiḥ samupācaret |
māsameraṇḍajaṃ tailaṃ pibenmūtreṇa saṃyutam ||53||
payasaudanamaśnīyānnāgarakvathitena ca |
traivṛtaṃ copayuñjīta śasto dāhastathā'gninā ||54||

snehetyādi| sneho'tra śodhanārtha eva, ślīpadānāṃ rūkṣaṇīyatvāt, gayī tu snehasvedopanāhāṃśceti paṭhati| samāpyāyitadehamiti raktāpakarṣeṇa sthāpanāddehasyānukramādāpyāyitasya| bastiratra nirūho'bhipretaḥ| māsamityādi śuṇṭhīkarṣapakvenāṣṭaguṇitena taccaturguṇāvaśiṣṭena payasā bhojanaṃ kuryāt| traivṛtamityādi traivṛtamutpāditaṃ vātavyādhau, taccopayuñjīta snehaprayogakāle||52-54||

gulphasyādhaḥ sirāṃ vidhyecchlīpade pittasambhave |
pittaghnīṃ ca kriyāṃ kuryāt pittārbudavisarpavat ||55||

gulphasyetyādi| pittaje ślīpade sirāṃ vidhyet, gulphasyopari caturaṅgulam; anye tu gulphasyādha iti sirāvyadha eva sambadhnanti||55||

sirāṃ suviditāṃ vidhyedaṅguṣṭhe ślaiṣmike bhiṣak |
madhuyuktāni cābhīkṣṇaṃ kaṣāyāṇi pibennaraḥ ||56||
pibedvā'pyabhayākalkaṃ mūtreṇānyatamena ca |
kaṭukāmamṛtāṃ śuṇṭhīṃ viḍaṅgaṃ dāru citrakam ||57||
hitaṃ vā lepane nityaṃ bhadradāru sacitrakam |
viḍaṅgamaricārkeṣu nāgare citrake'thavā ||58||
bhadradārvelukākhye ca sarveṣu lavaṇeṣu ca |
tailaṃ pakvaṃ pibedvā'pi yavānnaṃ ca hitaṃ sadā ||59||
pibet sarṣapatailaṃ vā ślīpadānāṃ nivṛttaye |
pūtīkarañjapatrāṇāṃ rasaṃ vā'pi yathābalam ||60||
anenaiva vidhānena putrañjīvakajaṃ rasam |
prayuñjīta bhiṣak prājñaḥ kālasātmyavibhāgavit ||61||
kevukākandaniryāsaṃ lavaṇaṃ tvatha pākimam |
rasaṃ dattvā'tha pūrvoktaṃ peyametadbhiṣagjitam ||62||

sirāmityādi| sirāṃ suviditāmiti kṣipramarmaṇa upari caturaṅgule| madhvityādi| abhīkṣṇaṃ punaḥ punarityarthaḥ| pibedityādi| atra mūtreṇānyatamena ceti aṣṭamūtreṣvekenāpi harītakīṃ seveta| kaṭukāmityādi| anyatamena mūtreṇa pibedityatrāpi sambadhyate| hitamityādi| elukākhyam elavālukam| pibedityādi| kaṇṭakikarañjapatrasvarasaṃ yathābalaṃ analapuruṣādibalānatikrameṇa palamardhapalaṃ paladvayaṃ vā kaṭutailākṣaprakṣepaṃ pibet; kaṇṭakikarañjaḥ pūtīkarañjaḥ, cirabilva ityanye| anenaivetyādi| putrañjīvako'nenaiva nāmnā prasiddhaḥ| kevuketyādi| pākimaṃ lavaṇaṃ viḍalavaṇam| bhiṣagjitam auṣadham||56-62||

kākādanīṃ kākajaṅghāṃ bṛhatīṃ kaṇṭakārikām |
kadambapuṣpīṃ mandārīṃ lambāṃ śukanasāṃ tathā ||63||
dagdhvā mūtreṇa tadbhasma srāvayet kṣārakalpavat |
tatra dadyāt pratīvāpaṃ kākodumbarikārasam ||64||
madanācca phalāt kvāthaṃ śukākhyasvarasaṃ tathā |
eṣa kṣārastu pānīyaḥ ślīpadaṃ hanti sevitaḥ ||65||
apacīṃ galagaṇḍaṃ ca grahaṇīdoṣameva ca |
bhaktasyānaśanaṃ caiva hanyāt sarvaviṣāṇi ca ||66||

kākādanīmityādi| kākādanī kālāhiṃsretyarthaḥ| kākajaṅghā etannāmnā prasiddhaiva| kadambapuṣpī alambuṣā muṇḍatiketi loke prasiddhā| mandārī ālī, lambā kaṭukālābuḥ| śukanasā carmakāravaṭaḥ| dagdhvā kṣāraṃ ṣaṅguṇena mūtreṇaikaviṃśativārān srāvayet, kākodumbarikārasaṃ [8] madanaphalakvāthaṃ śukanasāsvarasaṃ ca pratyekaṃ gomūtreṇa samaṃ, pādikamapareṣāṃ matena prakṣipya, kṣārapākakalpakrameṇa vipakvaḥ siddhaḥ, ślīpadaharadraveṇa śāṇādimātrayā pānārhaḥ||63-66||

eṣveva tailaṃ saṃsiddhaṃ nasyābhyaṅgeṣu pūjitama
etānevāmayān hanti ye ca duṣṭavraṇā nṛṇām ||67||

kākādanyādīnāṃ kvāthena tathā kākodumbarikārasamadanaphalakvāthaśukanāsāsvaraseṣu pratyekaṃ sameṣu tailaṃ sādhyam||67||

dravantīṃ trivṛtāṃ dantīṃ nīlīṃ śyāmāṃ tathaiva ca |
saptalāṃ śaṅkhinīṃ caiva dagdhvā mūtreṇa gālayet ||68||
dadyācca triphalākvāthameṣa kṣārastu sādhitaḥ |
adho gacchati pītastu pūrvaiścāpyāśiṣaḥ samāḥ ||69||

dravantīmityādi| dravantī śambarī| trivṛtā trivṛt| dantī dantinī| nīlī śāradaṃ phalaṃ, śrīphaliketi loke| śyāmā vṛddhadārukaḥ| śaṅkhinī yavatiktābhedaḥ| pūrvaiścāpyāśiṣaḥ samā iti pūrvaiḥ pūrvakṣārayogaiḥ saha samāstulyā bhavanti, āśiṣa āśaṃsanāni arthavādāḥ| idamuktaṃ bhavati- ye kecidguṇāḥ pūrvakṣāreṣvabhihitāste'trāpi tulyā jñeyā ityarthaḥ||68-69||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne vṛddhyupadaṃśaślīpadacikitsitaṃ nāmaikonaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||19||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne ekonaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ||

1. ‘abhīkṣṇaṃ nirharedasṛk’ iti pā.|
2. ‘taptayā vaṅkṣaṇasthānaṃ’ iti pā.|
3. ‘vāmāṇḍapravṛddhau’ iti pā.|
4. ‘dakṣiṇāṇḍapravṛddhau’ iti pā.|
5. ‘saralā’ iti pā.|
6. ‘vātopadaṃśaṃ’ iti pā.|
7. ‘araṇikā’ iti pā.|
8. ‘goṣṭhodumbarikārasaṃ’ iti pā.|
