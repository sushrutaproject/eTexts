--- title: "15. mūḍhagarbhacikitsitam" adhyaya: 15 order: 15 ---

athāto mūḍhagarbhacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
nāto'nyat kaṣṭatamamasti yathā mūḍhagarbhaśalyoddharaṇam; atra hi yoniyakṛtplīhāntravivaragarbhāśayānāṃ madhye karma kartavyaṃ sparśena, utkarṣaṇāpakarṣaṇasthānāpavartanotkartanabhedanacchedanapīḍanarjūkaraṇadāraṇāni caikahastena garbhaṃ garbhiṇīṃ cāhiṃsatā, tasmādadhipatimāpṛcchya paraṃ ca yatnamāsthāyopakrameta ||3||

utkarṣaṇam adhogatasyordhvīkaraṇam, apakarṣaṇam ūrdhvagatasyādhaḥ karṣaṇaṃ, sthānāpavartanaṃ sthāneṣu saktasya garbhaśayyāta uttānasyāvāṅmukhīkaraṇam, udvartanam avāṅmukhasyottānīkaraṇam; atra kecit ‘sthānavyāvartanaṃ’ iti paṭhanti; tatra sthānaṃ sthitiḥ; sthāne garbhaśayyāyāṃ tiryagādisthitasyottānasyādhovyāvṛttyā sthāpanam; ūrdhvasya kasyacidaṅgasya chedanamutkartanaṃ, chedanamiti mṛtasya tu teṣu teṣu sthāneṣu saktasya tasya tasyāṅgasya chedanaṃ dvaidhīkaraṇaṃ, garbhakukṣyāderādhmātasya vidāraṇaṃ bhedanaṃ, pīḍanaṃ campanaṃ (valanam), ṛjūkaraṇaṃ vakrasya prāñjalīkaraṇaṃ, dāraṇaṃ vidāraṇam||1-3||

tatra samāsenāṣṭavidhā mūḍhagarbhagatiruddiṣṭā; svabhāvagatā api trayaḥ saṅgā bhavanti- śiraso vaiguṇyādaṃsayorjaghanasya vā ||4||

tatretyādi| śiraso vaiguṇyāt vakratvāt, aṃsayorjaghanasya ca vaiguṇyādityanuvartate; vaiguṇyaṃ vakratā| aṃso bāhuśiraḥ, jaghanaṃ kaṭeragrabhāgaḥ||4||

jīvati tu garbhe sūtikāgarbhanirharaṇe prayateta |
nirhartumaśakye cyāvanān mantrānupaśṛṇuyāt; tān vakṣyāmaḥ ||5||
‘ihāmṛtaṃ ca somaśca citrabhānuśca bhāmini |
uccaiḥśravāśca turago mandire nivasantu te ||6||
idamamṛtamapāṃ samuddhṛtaṃ vai
tava laghu garbhamimaṃ pramuñcatu stri |
tadanalapavanārkavāsavāste
saha lavaṇāmbudharairdiśantu śāntim ||7||
muktāḥ paśorvipāśāśca [1] muktāḥ sūryeṇa raśmayaḥ |
muktaḥ sarvabhayādgarbha ehyehi viramāvitaḥ ||8||

jīvatītyādi| prayateta yatnaṃ kurvīta| kecidācāryā mantravyākhyānaṃ na kurvanti, kecit kurvanti tadāha- ihetyādi| amṛtaṃ samudramanthanodbhūtaṃ, citrabhānurādityaḥ, bhāmini he varastri, uccaiḥśravā amṛtamanthanotpannasturagaḥ śakravāhanaḥ, laghu śīghraṃ, diśantu prayacchantu||5-8||

auṣadhāni ca vidadhyādyathoktāni |
mṛte cottānāyā ābhugnasakthyā vastrādhārakonnamitakaṭyā dhanvananagavṛttikāśālmalīmṛtsnaghṛtābhyāṃ [2] mrakṣayitvā hastaṃ yonau praveśya garbhamupaharet |
tatra sakthibhyāmāgatamanulomamevāñchet, ekasakthnā pratipannasyetarasakthi prasāryāpaharet, sphigdeśenāgatasya sphigdeśaṃ prapīḍyordhvamutkṣipya sakthinī prasāryāpaharet, tiryagāgatasya parighasyeva tiraścīnasya paścādardhamūrdhvamutkṣipya pūrvārdhamapatyapathaṃ pratyārjavamānīyāpaharet, pārśvāpavṛttaśirasamaṃsaṃ prapīḍyordhvamutkṣipya śiro'patyapathamānīyāpaharet, bāhudvayapratipannasyordhvamutpīḍyāṃsau śiro'nulomamānīyāpaharet, dvāvantyāvasādhyau mūḍhagarbhau, evamaśakye śastramavacārayet ||9||
sacetanaṃ ca śastreṇa na kathañcana dārayet |
dāryamāṇo hi jananīmātmānaṃ caiva ghātayet ||10||
aviṣahye vikāre tu śreyo garbhasya pātanam |
na garbhiṇyā viparyāsastasmātprāptaṃ na hāpayet ||11||

mantrānnirdiśyauṣadhāni nirdiśannāha- auṣadhānītyādi| tāni punaḥ śārīre aparāpātanoditāni pāṇipādalepā yonidhūpanamāturapānādikaṃ ca| niṣphale mantre tantre [3] ca vṛthābhūteṣu sūtījanavyāpāreṣu bhiṣajāṃ śalyāharaṇopāyaṃ nirdiśannāha- mṛte ceti| cakārādaphalībhūteṣu mantratantraprasūtījananavyāpāreṣu jīvatyapītyanuktaṃ samuccinoti| ābhugnasakthyā iti saṅkucitasakthyāḥ| dhanvano dhanurvṛkṣo badarīsadṛśapatraḥ, nagavṛttikā śallakī, yadyapi mṛtsnaśabdaḥpraśastāyāṃ mṛdi vartate tathā'pyatra dhanurvṛkṣaśallakīśālmalīnāṃ picchile cumpe mṛtsnaśabdo boddhavyaḥ| ghṛtaṃ gavyaṃ ghṛtaṃ, tābhyāṃ pāṇiṃ mrakṣayitvā vilipya, mūḍhagarbhamapaharediti sāmānyo vidhiḥ| mṛtaṃ ceti cakārādapaharaṇe viśeṣeṇa vidhimāha- tatretyādi| āñchet ākarṣet| ekasakthneti prathamamekasakthipratipannaṃ, dvitīyasakthi punarābhugnaṃ sthitvā''gatamityarthaḥ, tadapi prasārya praguṇībhūtasakthidvayamapaharaṇārthamāñchediti pūrvasmādviśeṣaḥ; sakthi jaṅghocyate, ayaṃ dvitīyaḥ| sphigityādi| ayaṃ tṛtīyaḥ| tiryagityādi| parigho'rgalādaṇḍaḥ| ārjavamānīya ṛjubhāvamānīyāpaharediti, ayaṃ caturthaḥ| pārśvetyādi| pārśvāpavṛttaṃ pārśvavalitaṃ śiro yasya, apatyapatho yoniḥ; ayaṃ pañcamaḥ| bāhudvayetyādi| ayaṃ ṣaṣṭhaḥ| tatra ādyaṃ mūḍhagarbhatritayaṃ pādābhyāmāharaṇīyaṃ, taditarattu tritayaṃ śirasā, dvāviti śeṣaṃ tu mūḍhagarbhadvayamasādhyam, evamaṣṭau mūḍhagarbhāḥ| dāryamāṇaḥ chidyamāno garbho mātaramātmānaṃ ca vyāpādayet| ghātayediti sarvathaivāśakyahastākarṣe dvayorapi garbhagarbhiṇyorvipattisiddheḥ| śastramantrādinopāyenāsiddhau garbhacyāvanaṃ garbhiṇīrakṣārthaṃ kāryamityāha- aviṣahya iti| soḍhumaśakye vikāre vyādhau sati kiṃ kartavyamityāha- śreyo garbhasyeti; śreyo bhavatītyarthaḥ| garbhapātanaphalamāha- na garbhiṇyā viparyāsa iti; viparyāso mṛtyurityarthaḥ| tasmāt prāptakālaṃ na hāpayet nollaṅghayedityarthaḥ||9-11||

tataḥ striyamāśvāsya maṇḍalāgreṇāṅgulīśastreṇa vā śiro vidārya, śiraḥkapālānyāhṛtya, śaṅkunā gṛhītvorasi kakṣāyāṃ vā'paharet; abhinnaśirasamakṣikūṭe gaṇḍe vā, aṃsasaṃsaktasyāṃsadeśe bāhū chittvā, dṛtimivātataṃ vātapūrṇodaraṃ vā vidārya nirasyāntrāṇi śithilībhūtamāharet, jaghanasaktasya vā jaghanakapālānīti ||12||
kimbahunā-
yadyadaṅgaṃ hi garbhasya tasya sajjati tadbhiṣak |
samyagvinirharecchittvā rakṣennārīṃ ca yatnataḥ ||13||
garbhasya gatayaścitrā jāyante'nilakopataḥ |
tatrānalpamatirvaidyo varteta vidhipūrvakam ||14||
nopekṣeta mṛtaṃ garbhaṃ muhūrtamapi paṇḍitaḥ |
sa hyāśu jananīṃ hanti nirucchvāsaṃ paśuṃ yathā ||15||
maṇḍalāgreṇa kartavyaṃ chedyamantarvijānatā |
vṛddhipatraṃ hi tīkṣṇāgraṃ nārīṃ hiṃsyāt kadācana ||16||
athāpatantīmaparāṃ pātayet pūrvavadbhiṣak |
hastenāpaharedvā'pi pārśvabhyāṃ paripīḍya vā ||17||
dhunuyācca muhurnārīṃ pīḍayedvāṃ'sapiṇḍikām [4] |
tailāktayonerevaṃ tāṃ pātayenmatimān bhiṣak ||18||
evaṃ nirhṛtaśalyāṃ tu siñceduṣṇena vāriṇā |
tato'bhyaktaśarīrāyā yonau snehaṃ nidhāpayet ||19||
evaṃ mṛdvī bhavedyonistacchūlaṃ copaśāmyati |20|

mṛte tu garbhe śastrāvacaraṇaṃ nirdiśannāha- tata iti| maṇḍalāgreṇāṅgulīśastreṇeti antarmukhasañjñitena kṛśataraśaśadharakānukāriṇā'rdhadvayasaṃhitamadhyakīlitena madhyamāṅgulyaṅguṣṭhapraviṣṭalohakaṭakaniyamitena, anye punarekāvayavena madhyamāṅgulīpraviṣṭalohakaṇṭakenetyāhuḥ| taddhi śastramantadvāraṃ hastāntargataṃ yonau supradeśapraveśitamapi sukhacchedakaraṃ garbhiṇīṃ na hinasti| śaṅkunā ākuñcitākāreṇa baḍiśaviśeṣeṇa samyaggṛhītaṃ hṛdaye kakṣe vā'paharet| śirasaḥ punaravaiguṇyena nirgataśiraso vipulāṃsadeśasaktasya vidhimupadiśannāha- abhinnaśirasamiti; kūṭe gaṇḍe vā saṅgṛhya śaṅkunā'paharaṇam| mṛtasyādhmātodarasya kramāgatamudarasaṅgaṃ nirdiśannāha- dṛtimivetyādi|- dṛtiḥ pānīyapūrṇakhallī| jaghanasaktasyetyādi|- jaghanasaktaṃ jaghanakapālāni vidārya, apahṛtyāpaharet| evamuddiṣṭānāmāharaṇopāyaṃ nirdiśyānuktānāmapi nānāprakārāṇāṃ nirdiśati- garbhasyetyādi| mṛte tu doṣaṃ nirdiśannāha- nopekṣetetyādi| saḥ garbhaḥ, hi yasmādarthe| maṇḍalāgreṇeti maṇḍalāgrāvacāraṇamuktamapi vṛddhipatraniṣedhopadeśārthaṃ punarucyate| athāpatantīmityādi| aparāṃ garbhāvaraṇaṃ jarāyusañjñaṃ yaṃ lokā āvalamityācakṣate| pūrvavat śārīroktavidhinā||12-19||-

kṛṣṇātanmūlaśuṇṭhyelāhiṅgubhārgīḥ sadīpyakāḥ ||20||
vacāmativiṣāṃ rāsnāṃ cavyaṃ sañcūrṇya pāyayet |
snehena doṣasyandārthaṃ vedanopaśamāya ca ||21||
kvāthaṃ caiṣāṃ tathā kalkaṃ cūrṇaṃ vā snehavarjitam |
śākatvagghiṅgvativiṣāpāṭhākaṭukarohiṇīḥ ||22||
tathā tejovatīṃ cāpi pāyayet pūrvavadbhiṣak |
trirātraṃ pañcasaptāhaṃ [5] tataḥ snehaṃ punaḥ pibet ||23||
pāyayetāsavaṃ naktamariṣṭaṃ vā susaṃskṛtam |
śirīṣakakubhābhyāṃ ca toyamācamane hitam ||24||
upadravāśca ye'nye syustān yathāsvamupācaret |
sarvataḥ pariśuddhā ca snigdhapathyālpabhojanā ||25||
svedābhyaṅgaparā nityaṃ bhavet krodhavivarjitā |
payo vātaharaiḥ siddhaṃ daśāhaṃ bhojane hitam ||26||
rasaṃ daśāhaṃ śeṣe tu yathāyogamupācaret |
vyupadravāṃ viśuddhāṃ ca jñātvā ca varavarṇinīm ||27||
ūrdhvaṃ caturbhyo māsebhyo visṛjet parihārataḥ |28|

vyavasthitavikalpā hyete deśaprakṛtisātmyāpekṣayā| kṛṣṇā pippalī, tanmūlaṃ pippalīmūlaṃ, dīpyakaḥ ajamodā| prasavakāla eva jātamūḍhagarbhāyāḥ kṛtakarmaṇā'pahṛtāparāyāḥ pārśvayoniśūlakāṭhinyaśāntyarthaṃ raktakledādidoṣaprasekārthaṃ ca tailāktayonestailena kṛṣṇādipānam; akālamūḍhagarbhāyāstu laṅghitāyāḥ punarannakāle uddālakādīnāmannānāmasnehalavaṇābhiryavāgūbhirupacāro yāvanto māsā garbhasya tāvantyevāhāni; anantaramāse tu snehādividhānamakālamūḍhagarbhāyā api pūrvavat| śākaḥ karkaśapatravṛkṣaḥ, ‘sāgavana’ iti loke| trirātramityādi|- evaṃ trirātraṃ kṛṣṇādicūrṇapānena śākatvagādicūrṇapānena vā''hṛtakoṣṭharaktakledādidoṣāyāḥ saptāhaṃ hrasvamātrayā snehapānam| tena viśuddhavātāśayāyā daśāhādūrdhvaṃ snigdhapathyālpabhojanam| tatra nityaṃ bāhyāvapi svedābhyaṅgāvavaśyaṃ kāryau, tatra dvitīyadaśāhe vātaharasiddhaṃ payo vakṣyamāṇaṃ, tṛtīye māṃsarasaḥ| kakubhaḥ sugandhimūlo viṭapaḥ| ācamanasnānādau ṣaḍaṅgakalpanākalpitaśirīṣakakubhasalilam| upadravā iti upadravā vātarogajvarādyāḥ| vātaharaiḥ śatapuṣpādibhiḥ| yathāyogamiti sātmyādyapakṣayeti bhāvaḥ||20-27||-

yonisantarpaṇe'bhyaṅge pāne bastiṣu bhojane ||28||
balātailamidaṃ cāsyai dadyādanilavāraṇam |
balāmūlakaṣāyasya daśamūlīśṛtasya ca ||29||
yavakolakulatthānāṃ kvāthasya payasastathā |
aṣṭāvaṣṭau śubhā bhāgāstailādekastadekataḥ ||30||
pacedāvāpya madhuraṃ gaṇaṃ saindhavasaṃyutam |
tathā'guruṃ sarjarasaṃ saralaṃ devadāru ca ||31||
mañjiṣṭhāṃ candanaṃ kuṣṭhamelāṃ kālānusārivām |
māṃsīṃ śaileyakaṃ patraṃ tagaraṃ sārivāṃ vacām ||32||
śatāvarīmaśvagandhāṃ śatapuṣpāṃ punarnavām |
tat sādhusiddhaṃ sauvarṇe rājate mṛnmaye'pi vā ||33||
prakṣipya kalaśe samyak svanuguptaṃ nidhāpayet |
balātailamidaṃ khyātaṃ sarvavātavikāranut ||34||
yathābalamato mātrāṃ sūtikāyai pradāpayet |
yā ca garbhārthinī nārī kṣīṇaśukraśca yaḥ pumān ||35||
vātakṣīṇe marmahate mathite'bhihate tathā |
bhagne śramābhipanne ca sarvathaivopayujyate ||36||
etadākṣepakādīn vai vātavyādhīnapohati |
hikkāṃ kāsamadhīmanthaṃ gulmaṃ śvāsaṃ ca dustaram ||37||
ṣaṇmāsānupayujyaitadantravṛddhimapohati |
pratyagradhātuḥ puruṣo bhavecca sthirayauvanaḥ ||38||
rājñāmetaddhi kartavyaṃ rājamātrāśca ye narāḥ |
sukhinaḥ sukumārāśca dhaninaścāpi ye narāḥ ||39||

balāmūlakvāthasya tathā daśamūlakvāthasya yavakolakulatthānāṃ kvāthasya payasaśca pratyekamaṣṭāvaṣṭau bhāgāḥ, ekaḥ snehāt; evaṃ snehāddvātriṃśadguṇo dravaḥ, snehacaturthāṃśo bheṣajakalkaḥ| madhuraḥ kākolyādigaṇaḥ| kālānusārivā kṛṣṇasārivā, ‘kālānusāri vā’ ityatra kecidācāryā asmin taile kṣīraśuklakaṃ paṭhanti, kṣīraśuklakaṃ kṣīravidārī| svanugupte nirupadravasthāne| adhīmanthaḥ akṣirogo'bhiṣyandapūrvakaḥ| ṣaṇmāsopayogāt kośamaprāptāmantravṛddhiṃ hanti, kośaprāptāṃ punarantravṛddhimasādhyāmāhuḥ||28-39||

balākaṣāyapītebhyastilebhyo vā'pyanekaśaḥ |
tailamutpādya tatkvāthaśatapākaṃ [6] kṛtaṃ śubham ||40||
nivāte nibhṛtāgāre prayuñjīta yathābalam |
jīrṇe'smin payasā snigdhamaśnīyāt ṣaṣṭikaudanam ||41||
anena vidhinā droṇamupayujyānnamīritam |
bhuñjīta dviguṇaṃ kālaṃ balavarṇānvitastataḥ ||42||
sarvapāpairvinirmuktaḥ śatāyuḥ puruṣo bhavet |
śataṃ śataṃ tathotkarṣo droṇe droṇe prakīrtitaḥ ||43||

balātailānantaraṃ balāsaṃskāreṇa śatapākavidhānamāha- baletyādi| pītebhyo bhāvitebhyaḥ saptakṛtvaḥ| tilatulyabalāmūlāṣṭaguṇasalilāṣṭāṃśaśeṣitakaṣāyabhāvitānāṃ tilānāṃ tailamādāya tatkvāthaṃ balākvāthaṃ sāmānyakvāthaparibhāṣārthakṛtakvāthena caturguṇena śataśaḥ pacet; tat kvathaṃ tatkalkameva vetyeke; pūrvabalātailasamuddiṣṭairmadhurādikalkairevetyanye||40-43||

balākalpenātibalāguḍūcyādityaparṇiṣu |
saireyake vīratarau śatāvaryāṃ trikaṇṭake ||44||
tailāni madhuke kuryāt prasāriṇyāṃ ca buddhimān |45|

uktabalātailadvayakalpena saṅkṣepārthaṃ dravyāntareṣvatideśamuddiśannāha- baletyādi| ādityaparṇī sūryāvartaḥ| vīrataruḥ svanāmaprasiddhaḥ, arjuna ityeke, kokilākṣa ityapare| atibalāditaileṣu ca balātailoktakalkaprakṣepaḥ; athavā'tibalādīnāmeva kvāthakalkābhyāṃ tailāni paktavyānīti||44||-

nīlotpalaṃ varīmūlaṃ gavye kṣīre vipācayet ||45||
śatapākaṃ tatastena tilatailaṃ pacedbhiṣak |
balātailasya kalkāṃstu supiṣṭāṃstatra dāpayet ||46||
sarveṣāmeva jānīyādupayogaṃ cikitsakaḥ |
balātailavadeteṣāṃ guṇāṃścaiva viśeṣataḥ ||47||

nīlotpalamityādi| varī śatāvarī||45-47||

iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne mūḍhagarbhacikitsitaṃ nāma pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ ||15||

iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ||15||

1. ‘muktāḥ pāśā vipāśāśca muktāḥ sūryenduraśmayaḥ| muktaḥ sarvabhayādgarbha ehyehi māciraṃ svāhā’ iti pā.|
2. ‘śālmalīṣu madhye marudeśīyaparvatasthā hi nitarāṃ picchilaniryāsā bhavanti, tadidamucyate-dhanveti| dhanvā marudeśaḥ, mṛtsnamatra picchilo bhāgaḥ| ‘viviktaṃ mṛtsnaṃ ca’ ityojolakṣaṇaṃ ca| tantrāntare'pyuktaṃ-‘hastamabhyajya yoniṃ ca sājyaśālmalipicchayā’ iti hārāṇacandraḥ| ‘dhanvanagavṛttikaśālmalīmṛtsnaghṛtābhyāṃ’ iti hārāṇacandrasammataḥ pāṭhaḥ|
3. ‘jalavyāpāre’ iti pā.|
4. hārāṇacandrastu ‘svedayedvā sapiṇḍikāṃ’ iti paṭhati, ‘sapiṇḍikāmaparāsannihitamāṃsapiṇḍikāsahitāmaparāmityarthaḥ’ iti ca vyākhyāti|
5. ‘trirātramevaṃ saptāhaṃ’ iti pā.|
6. ‘tatkvāthaśatapātaṃ śṛtaṃ’ iti pā.|
