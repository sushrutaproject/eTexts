--- title: "22. mukharogacikitsitam" adhyaya: 22 order: 22 ---

athāto mukharogacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1||
yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2||
caturvidhena snehena madhūcchiṣṭayutena ca |
vātaje'bhyañjanaṃ kuryānnāḍīsvedaṃ [1] ca buddhimān ||3||
vidadhyādoṣṭhakope tu sālvaṇaṃ copanāhane |
mastiṣke caiva nasye ca tailaṃ vātaharaṃ hitam ||4||
śrīveṣṭakaṃ sarjarasaṃ suradāru saguggulu |
yaṣṭīmadhukacūrṇaṃ tu vidadhyāt pratisāraṇam ||5||
pittaraktābhighātotthaṃ jalaukobhirupācaret |
pittavidradhivaccāpi kriyāṃ kuryādaśeṣataḥ ||6||
śirovirecanaṃ dhūmaḥ svedaḥ kavala eva ca |
hṛte rakte prayoktavyamoṣṭhakope kaphātmake ||7||
tryūṣaṇaṃ svarjikākṣāro yavakṣāro viḍaṃ tathā |
kṣaudrayuktaṃ vidhātavyametacca pratisāraṇam ||8||
medoje svedite bhinne śodhite jvalano hitaḥ |
priyaṅgutriphalālodhraṃ sakṣaudraṃ pratisāraṇam ||9||
etadoṣṭhaprakopānāṃ sādhyānāṃ karma kīrtitam |10|

caturvidhena catuṣprakāreṇa| snehena doṣādyapekṣayā samastavyastena snehapākaparibhāṣayā vātaharakaṣāyamadhūcchiṣṭakalkaśṛtenābhyaṅgaḥ| tataḥ snigdhaṃ nāḍīsvedena yathoktena svedayet| mastiṣkaḥ śirobastiprakāraḥ; badhyata ūrdhvaṃ śirobastiḥ, anavabaddho mastiṣka iti| anye tu paṭhanti ‘mastiṣkye [2] caiva nasye ca’ ityādi; atra mastiṣkyaṃ [3] mastiṣke [4] hitamabhyaṅgatailapicudhāraṇaśirobastyādi| śrīveṣṭakaḥ saralaniryāsaḥ| pratisāraṇaṃ gharṣaṇam| kriyāṃ kuryādaśeṣata iti saṃśodhanasaṃśamanarūpāṃ bāhyāmābhyantarīṃ ca| medoje punarasnehapūrvaṃ svedite bhittvā medaso'pakarṣaṇaṃ, śodhite cāgneravacāraṇaṃ pathyam||1-9||-

dantamūlagatānāṃ tu rogāṇāṃ [5] karma vakṣyate ||10||
śītāde hṛtarakte tu toye nāgarasarṣapān |
niṣkvāthya triphalāṃ mustaṃ gaṇḍūṣaḥ sarasāñjanaḥ ||11||
priyaṅgavaśca mustaṃ ca triphalā ca pralepanam |
nasyaṃ ca triphalāsiddhaṃ madhukotpalapadmakaiḥ ||12||
dantapuppuṭake kāryaṃ taruṇe raktamokṣaṇam |
sapañcalavaṇaḥ kṣāraḥ sakṣaudraḥ pratisāraṇam ||13||
hitaḥ śirovirekaśca nasyaṃ snigdhaṃ ca bhojanam |14|

dantetyādi| nasyamityādi| śoṇitāvasekaśodhanagaṇḍūṣakupitavātānubandhe snehanaṃ nasyam| tatra kaphānugate madhukādibhiḥ saha triphalāsiddhaṃ tailaṃ nasye prayojyaṃ, śoṇitānugame ca triphalādisaṃsiddhaṃ ghṛtamiti| taruṇe tatkālotpanne| nasyamiti ‘śītādoktasnehena’ iti śeṣaḥ, doṣadūṣyasthānānāmaikyāt||10-13||-

visrāvite dantaveṣṭe vraṇāṃstu pratisārayet ||14||
rodhrapattaṅgayaṣṭyāhvalākṣācūrṇairmadhūttaraiḥ |
gaṇḍūṣe kṣīriṇo yojyāḥ sakṣaudraghṛtaśarkarāḥ ||15||
kākolyādau daśakṣīrasiddhaṃ sarpiśca nasyataḥ |
śauṣire hṛtarakte tu rodhramustarasāñjanaiḥ ||16||
sakṣaudraiḥ śasyate lepo gaṇḍūṣe kṣīriṇo hitāḥ |
sārivotpalayaṣṭyāhvasāvarāgurucandanaiḥ [6] ||17||
kṣīre daśaguṇe siddhaṃ sarpirnasye ca pūjitam |
kriyāṃ paridare kuryācchītādoktāṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ ||18||

visrāvite ityādi| pattaṅgaṃ raktacandanaṃ, madhūttaraiḥ madhupradhānaiḥ| kṣīriṇo vaṭādayaḥ| daśakṣīrasiddhaṃ daśaguṇena gavyakṣīreṇa ghṛtaṃ sādhanīyam||14-18||

saṃśodhyobhayataḥ kāryaṃ śiraścopakuśe tathā |
kākodumbarikāgojīpatrairvisrāvayedasṛk ||19||
kṣaudrayuktaiśca lavaṇaiḥ savyoṣaiḥ pratisārayet |
pippalīḥ sarṣapāñ śvetānnāgaraṃ naiculaṃ phalam ||20||
sukhodakena saṃsṛjya kavalaṃ cāpi dhārayet |
ghṛtaṃ madhurakaiḥ siddhaṃ hitaṃ kavalanasyayoḥ ||21||
śastreṇa [7] dantavaidarbhe dantamūlāni śodhayet |
tataḥ kṣāraṃ prayuñjīta kriyāḥ sarvāśca śītalāḥ ||22||
uddhṛtyādhikadantaṃ tu tato'gnimavacārayet |
kṛmidantakavaccāpi vidhiḥ kāryo vijānatā ||23||

saṃśodhyobhayata iti vamanavirecanābhyāmūrdhvamadhaśca kāyaṃ śodhayitvā, śirovirecanena śiraśca saṃśodhayedityarthaḥ| raktapittotthite'pyupakuśe sthāninaḥ kaphasya pratīkārārthaṃ vamanaṃ yuktam| kākodumbarikā kāṣṭhodumbarikā, gojī gojihvā| niculo vetasaḥ| madhurakaiḥ kākolyādibhiḥ||19-23||

chittvā'dhimāṃsaṃ sakṣaudrairebhiścūrṇairupācaret |
vacātejovatīpāṭhāsvarjikāyāvaśūkajaiḥ ||24||
kṣaudradvitīyāḥ pippalyaḥ kavalaścātra kīrtitaḥ |
paṭolatriphalānimbakaṣāyaścātra dhāvane |
hitaḥ śiroviraikaśca dhūmo vairecanaśca yaḥ ||25||

chittvetyādi| tejovatī kākamardanikā| sakṣaudraśca sapippalīkaḥ sukhodake kavalaḥ||24-25||

sāmānyaṃ karma nāḍīnāṃ viśeṣaṃ cātra me śṛṇu |
nāḍīvraṇaharaṃ karma dantanāḍīṣu kārayet ||26||
yaṃ dantamabhijāyeta [8] nāḍī taṃ dantamuddharet |
chittvā māṃsāni śastreṇa yadi noparijo bhavet ||27||
śodhayitvā daheccāpi kṣāreṇa jvalanena vā |
bhinattyupekṣite dante hanukāsthi gatirdhruvam ||28||
samūlaṃ daśanaṃ tasmāduddharedbhagnamasthiram |
uddhṛte tūttare dante samūle [9] sthirabandhane ||29||
raktātiyogāt pūrvoktā rogā ghorā bhavanti hi |
kāṇaḥ sañjāyate janturarditaṃ cāsya jāyate ||30||
calamapyuttaraṃ dantamato nāpaharedbhiṣak |
dhāvane jātimadanasvādukaṇṭakakhādiram ||31||
kaṣāyaṃ jātimadanakaṭukasvādukaṇṭakaiḥ |
yaṣṭyāhvarodhramañjiṣṭhākhadiraiścāpi yat kṛtam ||32||
tailaṃ saṃśodhanaṃ taddhi hanyāddantagatāṃ gatim |
kīrtitā dantamūle tu kriyā danteṣu vakṣyate ||33||

sāmānyamityādi| sāmānyaṃ tulyam| yadeva vātādināḍīnāṃ karmoktaṃ tadeva dantanāḍīnāṃ sāmānyamekamityarthaḥ| yaṃ dantamabhijāyetetyādi yaṃ dantamabhi samīpe nāḍī syāttaṃ dantamuddharet| yadi noparijo bhavediti ūrdhvagatadantapaṅktigataścenna bhavati| sthirabandhane dṛḍhataradantaveṣṭane| ‘samūle sthirabandhane’ ityatra ‘śoṇitaṃ samprasicyate’ iti paṭhanti| dhāvane prakṣālane, jātimadanakaṭukasvādukaṇṭakakhādiraṃ kaṣāyamiti; anye tu kaṣāyamāmapūgaphalamiti manyante| svādukaṇṭako gokṣuraḥ, kaṇṭakārivṛkṣamanye||26-33||

snehānāṃ kavalāḥ koṣṇāḥ sarpiṣastraivṛtasya vā |
niryūhāścānilaghnānāṃ dantaharṣapramardanāḥ ||34||
snaihikaśca hito dhūmo nasyaṃ snigdhaṃ ca bhojanam |
raso rasayavāgvaśca kṣīraṃ santānikā ghṛtam ||35||
śirobastirhitaścāpi kramo yaścānilāpahaḥ |
ahiṃsan dantamūlāni śarkarāmuddharedbhiṣak ||36||
lākṣācūrṇairmadhuyutaistatastāḥ pratisārayet |
dantaharṣakriyāṃ cāpi kuryānniravaśeṣataḥ ||37||
kapālikā kṛcchratamā tatrāpyeṣā kriyā hitā |
jayedvisrāvaṇaiḥ svinnamacalaṃ kṛmidantakam ||38||
tathā'vapīḍairvātaghnaiḥ snehagaṇḍūṣadhāraṇaiḥ |
bhadradārvādivarṣābhūlepaiḥ snigdhaiśca bhojanaiḥ ||39||
calamuddhṛtya [10] ca sthānaṃ vidahet suṣirasya ca |
tato vidārīyaṣṭyāhvaśṛṅgāṭakakaserukaiḥ ||40||
tailaṃ daśaguṇe kṣīre siddhaṃ nasye hitaṃ bhavet |41|

sarpiṣastraivṛtasya veti vātavyādhipaṭhitasya [11] | niryūhāḥ kvāthāḥ| raso māṃsarasaḥ| rasayavāgvo māṃsarasayavāgvaḥ| kṣīraṃ dugdham| santānikā ‘sara’ iti bhāṣā, kṣīrasānnidhyāt santānikāghṛte kṣīrotthe eva| pratisārayet gharṣayedityarthaḥ| uddhṛtadaśanasya sthānaṃ dahecchuddhyarthaṃ śoṇitasrāvaniṣedhārthaṃ ca; suṣirasya ca pūyakṛtacchidrasya ‘sthānaṃ dahet’ iti śeṣaḥ||34-40||-

hanumokṣe samuddiṣṭāṃ kuryāccārditavat kriyām ||41||
phalānyamlāni śītāmbu rūkṣānnaṃ dantadhāvanam |
tathā'tikaṭhinān bhakṣyān dantarogī vivarjayet ||42||
sādhyānāṃ dantarogaṇāṃ cikitsitamudīritam |43|

arditavatsamuddiṣṭāmiti ardita iva samuddiṣṭāṃ kriyāṃ kuryāt| samuddiṣṭagrahaṇenaitajjñāpayati- arditoktāmātideśikīṃ “vātavyādhividhinopacaret” (ci.a.5) ityevaṃrūpāṃ vaiśeṣikīṃ vātārditoktāṃ mastiṣkaśirobastinasyadhūmopanāhādikāṃ dvividhāmapi kriyāṃ kuryāditi| sādhyānāṃ dantarogāṇāṃ cikitsitamuktaṃ, śyāvadantadālanabhañjanānāmasādhyatvānnodāhṛtam||41-42||-

jihvāgatānāṃ sādhyānāṃ karma vakṣyāmi siddhaye ||43||
oṣṭhaprakope'nilaje yaduktaṃ prāk cikitsitam |
kaṇṭakeṣvanilottheṣu tat kāryaṃ bhiṣajā bhavet ||44||
pittajeṣu vighṛṣṭeṣu niḥsṛte duṣṭaśoṇite |
pratisāraṇagaṇḍūṣaṃ nasyaṃ ca madhuraṃ hitam ||45||
kaṇṭakeṣu kaphottheṣu likhiteṣvasṛjaḥ kṣaye |
pippalyādirmadhuyutaḥ kāryastu pratisāraṇe ||46||
gṛhṇīyāt kavalāṃścāpi gaurasarṣapasaindhavaiḥ |
paṭolanimbavārtākukṣārayūṣaiśca bhojayet ||47||
upajihvāṃ tu saṃlikhya kṣāreṇa pratisārayet |
śirovirekagaṇḍūṣadhūmaiścainamupācaret ||48||
jihvāgatānāṃ karmoktaṃ... |49|

jihvetyādi| vighṛṣṭeṣu gojīśephālikādipatraghṛṣṭeṣu, pratisāraṇaṃ madhuraireva kākolyādibhiḥ; gaṇḍūṣo'pi taireva kvathitaiḥ; nasyamapi daśaguṇakṣīraṃ tatkalkasadhitaṃ hitam| likhiteṣu maṇḍalāgrādinā| asṛjaḥ kṣaye asṛjo'vasāne, virāme ityarthaḥ| kṣāro yavakṣāraḥ, tatpradhānaiḥ yuṣaiḥ kṣārayūṣaiḥ| upajihvāmityādi| gaṇḍūṣaḥ kaphavraṇaharadravyeṇa kaphakaṇṭakaharadravyeṇa vā| dhūmaiśca vairecanikadhūmaiḥ| alāsaḥ punaratra kapharaktakṛta āśukāritvāt pratyākhyāyaiva virecanakṣoṇitamokṣaṇakavalaśirovirecanadhūmavamanādibhirupacaryaḥ||43-48||-

... tālavyānāṃ pravakṣyate |
aṅguṣṭhāṅgulisandaṃśenākṛṣya galaśuṇḍikām ||49||
chedayenmaṇḍalāgreṇa jihvopari tu saṃsthitām |
notkṛṣṭaṃ caiva hīnaṃ ca tribhāgaṃ chedayedbhiṣak ||50||
atyādānāt sravedraktaṃ tannimittaṃ mriyeta ca |
hīnacchedādbhavecchopho lālā nidrā bhramastamaḥ ||51||
tasmādvaidyaḥ prayatnena dṛṣṭakarmā viśāradaḥ |
galaśuṇḍīṃ tu sañchidya kuryāt prāptamimaṃ kramam ||52||
maricātiviṣāpāṭhāvacākuṣṭhakuṭannaṭaiḥ |
kṣaudrayuktaiḥ salavaṇaistatastāṃ pratisārayet ||53||
vacāmativiṣāṃ pāṭhāṃ rāsnāṃ kaṭukarohiṇīm |
niṣkvāthya picumandaṃ ca kavalaṃ tatra yojayet ||54||
iṅgudīkiṇihīdantīsaralāsuradārubhiḥ |
pañcāṅgīṃ kārayet piṣṭairvartiṃ gandhottarāṃ śubhām ||55||
tato dhūmaṃ pibejjanturdvirahnaḥ kaphanāśanam |
kṣārasiddheṣu mudgeṣu yūṣaścāpyaśane hitaḥ ||56||
tuṇḍikeryadhruṣe kūrme saṅghāte tālupuppuṭe |
eṣa eva vidhiḥ kāryo viśeṣaḥ śastrakarmaṇi ||57||
tālupāke tu kartavyaṃ vidhānaṃ pittanāśanam |
snehasvedau tāluśoṣe vidhiścānilanāśanaḥ ||58||
kīrtitaṃ tālujānāṃ tu... |59|

aṅguṣṭhetyādi| chedayenmaṇḍalāgreṇeti bhāgadvayamardhaṃ [12] vā parityajya bhāgenaikenārdhena vā chedayet| bhramaścakrārūḍhasyeva bhramaṇam| tamaḥ andhakāradarśanam| prāptaṃ yuktam| kuṭannaṭaḥ śyonākaḥ| kiṇihī kaṭabhī, saralā trivṛt| pañcāṅgīmiti pañcabhiraṅgabhūtairdravyaiḥ kṛtā pañcāṅgī, tām| gandhottarāṃ gandhapradhānām| dvirahnaḥ ahni vāradvayamiti| kṣārasiddheṣu mudgeṣviti kṛto yūṣo yavakṣāreṇa lavaṇīkṛto bhojane hitaḥ pathyaḥ| tuṇḍikerītyādi| viśeṣaḥ śastrakarmaṇīti śastrakarmaṇi viśeṣo [13] bhedaḥ| tatra tuṇḍikerī medyā tālupuppuṭako'pi, adhruṣamāṃsasaṅghātau chedyau, māṃsocchrāyatvāllekhyaḥ kurmastato'nurūpāyāmavasthāyāṃ chedyo'pi| tālvityādi| cakāreṇātra tāluśoṣe pittaharo vidhiḥ samuccīyate, pittenāpi tāluśoṣajananāt| kīrtitaṃ tālujānāmiti atra raktārbudasyāsādhyatvāt karma nodāhṛtam||49-58||-

... kaṇṭhyānāṃ karma vakṣyate |
sādhyānāṃ rohiṇīnāṃ tu hitaṃ śoṇitamokṣaṇam ||59||
chardanaṃ dhūmapānaṃ ca gaṇḍūṣo nasyakarma ca |
vātikīṃ tu hṛte rakte lavaṇaiḥ pratisārayet ||60||
sukhoṣṇan snehagaṇḍūṣān dhārayeccāpyabhīkṣṇaśaḥ |
pataṅgaśarkarākṣaudraiḥ paittikīṃ pratisārayet ||61||
drākṣāparūṣakakvātho hitaśca kavalagrahe |
agāradhūmakaṭukaiḥ ślaiṣmikīṃ pratisārayet ||62||
śvetāviḍaṅgadantīṣu tailaṃ siddhaṃ sasaindhavam |
nasyakarmaṇi yoktavyaṃ tathā kavaladhāraṇe ||63||
pittavat sādhayedvaidyo rohiṇīṃ raktasambhavām |
visrāvya kaṇṭhaśālūkaṃ sādhayettuṇḍikerivat ||64||
ekakālaṃ yavānnaṃ ca bhuñjīta snigdhamalpaśaḥ |
upajihvikavaccāpi sādhayedadhijihvikām ||65||
ekavṛndaṃ tu visrāvya vidhiṃ śodhanamācaret |
gilāyuścāpi yo vyādhistaṃ ca śastreṇa sādhayet ||66||
amarmasthaṃ supakvaṃ ca bhedayedgalavidradhim |67|

śoṇitamokṣaṇādikarmapaccakaṃ sāmānyaṃ sādhyānām| abhīkṣṇaśaḥ vāraṃvāram| pataṅgaṃ raktacandanam| śvetā śvetasyandā| visrāvyetyādi| sādhayettuṇḍikerivaditi tuṇḍikeryāmapi galaśuṇḍīvidhyatideśāt sādhayedgalaśuṇḍīvaditi vācye tuṇḍikerīvidhyatideśo niḥśeṣacchedanārthaḥ, galaśuṇḍīvadatra tribhāgacchedo na kārya iti bodhayati, aśeṣacchedo na punastribhāgādibhiriti| visrāvya jalaukābhiḥ| śirovirecanadhūmalepanakṣārādibhiḥ śodhanam||59-66||-

vātāt sarvasaraṃ cūrṇairlavaṇaiḥ pratisārayet ||67||
tailaṃ vātaharaiḥ siddhaṃ hitaṃ kavalanasyayoḥ |
tato'smai snaihikaṃ dhūmamimaṃ dadyādvicakṣaṇaḥ ||68||
śālarājādanairaṇḍasāraiṅgudamadhūkajāḥ |
majjāno gugguludhyāmamāṃsīkālānusārivāḥ |
śrīsarjarasaśaileyamadhūcchiṣṭāni cāharet ||69||
tatsarvaṃ sukṛtaṃ cūrṇaṃ snehenāloḍya yuktitaḥ |
ṭiṇṭūkavṛntaṃ sakṣaudraṃ matimāṃstena lepayet ||70||
eṣa sarvasare dhūmaḥ praśastaḥ snaihiko mataḥ |
kaphaghno mārutaghnaśca mukharogavināśanaḥ ||71||
pittātmake sarvasare śuddhakāyasya dehinaḥ |
sarvaḥ pittaharaḥ kāryo vidhirmadhuraśītalaḥ ||72||
pratisāraṇagaṇḍūṣau dhūmaḥ saṃśodhanāni ca |
kaphātmake sarvasare vidhiṃ kuryāt kaphāpaham ||73||
pibedativiṣāṃ pāṭhāṃ mustaṃ ca suradāru ca |
rohiṇīṃ kaṭukākhyāṃ ca kuṭajasya phalāni ca ||74||
gavāṃ mūtreṇa manujo bhāgairdharaṇasammitaiḥ |
eṣa sarvān kaphakṛtān rogān yogo'pakarṣati ||75||

mukhāvayavagatānnirdiśya sakalavadanagatānnirdiśannāha- vātādityādi| lavaṇaiścūrṇaiḥ pañcalavaṇakṛtaiḥ| tailaṃ vātaharaiḥ siddhaṃ hitaṃ kavalanasyayoḥ vātaharabhadradārvādikalkakaṣāyasiddhaṃ tailaṃ kavale nasye ca hitamityarthaḥ| śālarājādanetyādi śālaphalādimajjānaḥ; śālaḥ śaṅkuḥ, rājādanaḥ kṣīrikā, sāraḥ sāravṛkṣaḥ khadirādiḥ| dhyāmaṃ gandhatṛṇaṃ [14] , kālānusārivā kālavallī| śrīḥ navanītakhoṭiḥ saralaniryāsaḥ, sarjarasaḥ rālā| snehenāloḍya yuktita iti yāvatā snehena nātighanatvaṃ nātitanutvaṃ lepayogyatā ca bhavati tāvatī snehamātretyarthaḥ| ṭiṇṭukaḥ śyonākaḥ| śuddhakāyasya vamanādibhiḥ| ativiṣādicūrṇadharaṇabhāgaṃ pratidivasaṃ ṣāṇmāsikaṃ gomūtreṇa peyam| dharaṇamānamapi kecit sauśrutīyāḥ paṭhanti; “madhyamā ekonaviṃśatirniṣpāvā dharaṇaṃ tānyardhatṛtīyāni karṣaḥ” (ci.a.31) iti||67-75||

kṣīrekṣurasagomūtradadhimastvamlakāñjikaiḥ |
vidadhyāt kavalān vīkṣya doṣaṃ tailaghṛtairapi ||76||
