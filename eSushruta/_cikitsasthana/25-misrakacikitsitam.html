--- title: "25. miśrakacikitsitam" adhyaya: 25 order: 25 ---
athāto miśrakacikitsitaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ ||1|| yathovāca bhagavān dhanvantariḥ ||2|| pālyāmayāstu visrāvyā ityuktaṃ prāṅnibodha tān | paripoṭastathotpāta unmantho duḥkhavardhanaḥ ||3|| pañcamaḥ parilehī ca karṇapālyāṃ gadāḥ smṛtāḥ | saukumāryāccirotsṛṣṭe sahasā'bhipravardhite ||4|| karṇaśopho bhavet pālyāṃ sarujaḥ paripoṭavān | kṛṣṇāruṇanibhaḥ stabdhaḥ sa vātāt paripoṭakaḥ ||5|| gurvābharaṇasaṃyogāttāḍanāddharṣaṇādapi | śophaḥ pālyāṃ bhavecchyāvo dāhapākarunānvitaḥ ||6|| rakto vā raktapittābhyāmutpātaḥ sa gado mataḥ | balādvardhayataḥ karṇaṃ pālyāṃ vāyuḥ prakupyati ||7|| gṛhītvā sakaphaṃ kuryācchophaṃ tadvarṇavedanam 1| unmanthakaḥ sakaṇḍūko vikāraḥ kaphavātajaḥ ||8|| vardhamāne yadā karṇe kaṇḍūdāharuganvitaḥ | śopho bhavati pākaśca tvakstho'sau duḥkhavardhanaḥ ||9|| kaphāsṛkkṛmayaḥ kuryuḥ sarṣapābhā vikāriṇīḥ | srāviṇīḥ piḍakāḥ pālyāṃ kaṇḍūdāharuganvitāḥ ||10|| kaphāsṛkkṛmisambhūtaḥ sa visarpannitastataḥ | lihyāt saśaṣkulīṃ pālīṃ parilehīti sa smṛtaḥ ||11|| pālyāmayā hyamī ghorā narasyāpratikāriṇaḥ | mithyāhārabihārasya pāliṃ hiṃsyurupekṣitāḥ ||12||
cirotsṛṣṭo bahukālaṃ yāvat parityaktaḥ, saukumāryahetutvāt| śaṣkulī bāhyapālyadhiṣṭhānaṃ||1-12||
tasmādāśu bhiṣak teṣu snehādikramamācaret | tathā'bhyaṅgaparīṣekapradehāsṛgvimokṣaṇam ||13|| sāmānyato viśeṣācca vakṣyāmyabhyañjanaṃ prati | kharamañjariyaṣṭyāhvasaindhavāmaradārubhiḥ ||14|| supiṣṭaiḥ sāśvagandhaiśca mūlakāvalgujaiḥ phalaiḥ | sarpistailavasāmajjamadhūcchiṣṭāni pācayet ||15|| sakṣīrāṇyatha taiḥ pāliṃ pradihyāt paripoṭake | mañjiṣṭhātilayaṣṭyāhvasārivotpalapadmakaiḥ ||16|| sarodhraiḥ sakadambaiśca balājambvāmrapallavaiḥ | siddhaṃ dhānyāmlasaṃyuktaṃ tailamutpātanāśanam ||17|| tālapatryaśvagandhārkabākucīphalasaindhavaiḥ | tailaṃ kulīragodhābhyāṃ vasayā saha pācitam ||18|| saralālāṅgalībhyāṃ ca hitamunmanthanāśanam | tathā'śmantakajambvāmrapatrakvāthena secanam ||19|| prapauṇḍarīkamadhukamañjiṣṭhārajanīdvayaiḥ | cūrṇairudvartanaiḥ pālīṃ tailāktāmavacūrṇayet ||20|| lākṣāviḍaṅgakalkena tailaṃ paktvā'vacārayet | svinnāṃ gomayapiṇḍena pradihyāt parilehike ||21|| piṣṭairviḍaṅgairathavā trivṛcchyāmārkasaṃyutaiḥ | karañjeṅgudibījairvā kuṭajāragvadhāyutaiḥ ||22|| sarvairvā sārṣapaṃ tailaṃ siddhaṃ maricasaṃyutam | sanimbapatrairabhyaṅge madhūcchiṣṭānvitaṃ hitam ||23|| pālīṣu vyādhiyuktāsu tanvīṣu kaṭhināsu ca | puṣṭyarthaṃ mārdavārthaṃ ca kuryādabhyañjanaṃ tvidam ||24|| lopākānūpamajjānaṃ vasāṃ tailaṃ navaṃ ghṛtam | paceddaśaguṇaṃ kṣīramāvāpya madhuraṃ gaṇam ||25|| apāmārgāśvagandhe ca tathā lākṣārasaṃ śubham | tatsiddhaṃ paripūtaṃ ca svanuguptaṃ nidhāpayet ||26|| tenābhyañjyāt sadā pālīṃ susvinnāmatimarditām | etena pālyo vardhante nirujo nirupadravāḥ ||27|| mṛdvyaḥ puṣṭāḥ samāḥ snigdhā jāyante bhūṣaṇakṣamāḥ |28|
tasmādityādi| sneha ādiryasya vamanādeḥ sa tathā| tatra vātike'bhyaṅgānuvāsanapradehādi, paittike virecanaṃ, ślaiṣmike vamanaṃ, raktapaittike raktāpakarṣaṇavirekapariṣekādikā kriyā, kṛmije kṛmighnaśca vidhiriti| kharamañjariḥ apāmārgaḥ| avalgujā bākucikā| paripoṭake pāliṃ catuḥsnehena sakiṭṭenaiva pradihyāt| tālapatrī muśalī| kulīragodhābhyāmiti kulīraḥ karkaṭaḥ, godhā ‘gosāp’ iti bhāṣā| saralā dhūpakāṣṭhaḥ| aśmantakādipatrakvāthapariṣiktāṃ lākṣāviḍaṅgakalkasiddhena tailenābhyaktāṃ prapauṇḍarīkādicūrṇairavacūrṇayet| trivṛdādisaṃyutairviḍaṅgairgomūtrapiṣṭaiḥ pradihyāt| gayī tu parilehike pippalīkṛmighnavacākalkena gomūtrapiṣṭena ca pradihyāditi vyākhyānayati; tadanugataśca pāṭho na labhyate| sarvairanantaroktaiḥ; gomūtrapiṣṭaireva karañjādibhiścaturbhiḥ| atrāpi gomūtraṃ caturguṇam| anye tvevaṃ paṭhanti- “nimbapatrairmūtrasikte hyaṅge cābhyañjanaṃ hitam” iti| lopākaḥ lāṅgaṭakaḥ| ānūpā mahiṣādayaḥ| vasā punasteṣāmeva| madhuragaṇaḥ kākolyādiḥ||13-27||-
nīlīdalaṃ bhṛṅgarajo'rjunatvak piṇḍītakaṃ kṛṣṇamayorajaśca | bījodbhavaṃ sāhacaraṃ ca puṣpaṃ pathyākṣadhātrīsahitaṃ vicūrṇya ||28|| ekīkṛtaṃ sarvamidaṃ pramāya paṅkena tulyaṃ nalinībhavena | saṃyojya pakṣaṃ kalaśe nidhāya lauhe ghaṭe 2 sadmani sāpidhāne ||29|| anena tailaṃ vipacedvimiśraṃ rasena bhṛṅgatriphalābha 3 vena | āsannapāke ca parīkṣaṇārthaṃ patraṃ balākābhavamākṣipecca ||30|| bhavedyadā tadbhramarāṅganīlaṃ tadā vipakvaṃ vinidhāya pātre | kṛṣṇāyase māsamavasthitaṃ tadabhyaṅgayogāt palitāni hanyāt ||31||
palite nīlītailamāha- nīlīdalamityādi| nīlī nīlāñjanikā śāradaphalā, ‘śrīphalī’ 4 iti loke| bhṛṅgarājo mārkavaḥ| piṇḍītakaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ, kṛṣṇapuṣpo madanaḥ| bījodbhavaṃ sāhacaraṃ ca puṣpaṃ bījakasahacarayoḥ puṣpam; anye tu bījodbhavamityāhuḥ, asmin yoge kecit sāhacaraṃ śyāmāpuṣpaṃ iti vadanti| ekīkṛtaṃ sarvamidaṃ pramāyeti adaḥ etat, pramāya tolayitvā, nalinībhavena paṅkena tulyaṃ padmatalakardamena sahetyarthaḥ| saṃyojya melayitvā| sadmani gṛhe| sāpidhāne suṣṭhu āvṛte sajhampane kalaśe iti jñeyam| anenetyādi| pakṣāduddhṛtyānena tailaṃ vipacet| snehacaturthāṃśo bheṣajakalkaḥ, snehāccaturguṇo drava iti nyāyaḥ svayamevohyaḥ| āsannapāke cetyādi| tāvat kālaṃ pācayed 5 yāvadeva balākāpakṣaṃ nīlaṃ bhavatīti; etena punaḥ punardravaṃ nikṣipya pacedityuktaṃ bhavati||28-31||
sairīyajambvarjunakāśmarījaṃ puṣpaṃ tilānmārkavacūtabīje | punarnave kardamakaṇṭakāryau kāsīsapiṇḍītakabījasāram ||32|| phalatrayaṃ loharajo'ñjanaṃ ca yaṣṭyāhvayaṃ nīrajasārive ca | piṣṭvā'tha sarvaṃ saha modayantyā sārāmbhasā bījakasambhavena ||33|| sārāmbhasaḥ saptabhireva paścāt prasthaiḥ samāloḍya daśāhaguptam | lauhe supātre vinidhāya tailamakṣodbhavaṃ tacca pacet prayatnāt ||34|| pakvaṃ ca lauhe'bhinave nidhāya nasyaṃ vidadhyāt pariśuddhakāyaḥ | abhyaṅgayogaiśca niyujyamānaṃ bhuñjīta māṣān kṛśarāmatho vā ||35|| māsopariṣṭāddhanakuñcitāgrāḥ keśā bhavanti bhramarāñjanābhāḥ | keśāstathā'nye khalatau bhaveyurjarā na cainaṃ sahasā'bhyupaiti ||36|| balaṃ paraṃ sambhavatīndriyāṇāṃ bhavecca vaktraṃ valibhirvimuktam | nākāmine'narthini nākṛtāya naivāraye tailamidaṃ pradeyam ||37||
sairīyakādīnāṃ puṣpāṇi| sairīyakaḥ kaṇṭaselīyākaḥ, mārkavacūtayorbīje, mārkavo bhṛṅgarājaḥ| cūta āmraḥ| punarnave dve śuklalohite| kardamaḥ padminīmūlapaṅkaḥ| piṇḍītako madanakaḥ, tasya yatphalaṃ pāṇḍuravarṇaṃ bhavati tadiha grāhyam| bījasāraṃ asanasāram| añjanaṃ nīlavarṇaṃ rasāñjanaṃ, anye sroto'ñjanamāhuḥ| nīrajaṃ nīlotpalam| piṣṭvā sarvaṃ karṣapramāṇamityarthaḥ| modayantī malliketi loke khyātā| sārāmbhasā bījakasambhavena bījakasārodakena| tailamakṣodbhavaṃ bibhītakatailaṃ, tadāḍhakapramāṇaṃ pacediti| lauhapātre nidhāya sthāpayet, ‘māsamātram’ iti śeṣaḥ| eṣa palite khalatau ca yogaḥ; valiḥ tvaksaṅkocaḥ, anarthini atātparyārthapare, akṛtāya kṛtaghnāya||32-37||
lākṣā 6 rodhraṃ dve haridre śilāle kuṣṭhaṃ nāgaṃ gairikā varṇakāśca | mañjiṣṭhogrā syāt surāṣṭrodbhavā ca pattaṅgaṃ vai rocanā cāñjanaṃ ca ||38|| hemāṅgatvak pāṇḍupatraṃ vaṭasya kālīyaṃ syāt padmakaṃ padmamadhyam | raktaṃ śvetaṃ candanaṃ pāradaṃ ca kākolyādiḥ kṣīrapiṣṭaśca vargaḥ ||39|| medo majjā sikthakaṃ goghṛtaṃ ca dugdhaṃ kvāthaḥ kṣīriṇāṃ ca drumāṇām | etat sarvaṃ pakvamaikadhyatastu vaktrābhyaṅge sarpiruktaṃ pradhānam ||40|| hanyād vyaṅgaṃ nīlikāṃ cātivṛddhāṃ vaktre jātāḥ sphoṭikāścāpi kāścit | padmākāraṃ nirvalīkaṃ ca vaktraṃ kuryādetat pīnagaṇḍaṃ manojñam ||41|| rājñāmetadyoṣitāṃ cāpi nityaṃ kuryādvaidyastatsamānāṃ nṛṇāṃ ca | kuṣṭhaghnaṃ vai sarpiretat pradhānaṃ yeṣāṃ pāde santi vaipādikāśca ||42||
lākṣāmityādi| valiḥ tvaksaṅkocaḥ||38-42||
harītakīcūrṇamariṣṭapatraṃ cūtatvacaṃ dāḍimapuṣpavṛntam | patraṃ ca dadyānmadayantikāyā lepo'ṅgarāgo naradevayogyaḥ ||43||
harītakītyādi| ariṣṭapatraṃ nimbapatraṃ, phenilavṛkṣapatraṃ vā| cūtatvak āmratvak| madayantikā ‘mendī’ iti loke, yasyāḥ piṣṭaiḥ patrairnakhānāṃ rāgaṃ striya utpādayanti||43||
iti suśrutasaṃhitāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne miśrakacikitsitaṃ nāma pañcaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ ||25||
iti śrīḍalha(hla)ṇaviracitāyāṃ nibandhasaṅgrahākhyāyāṃ suśrutavyākhyāyāṃ cikitsāsthāne pañcaviṃśatitamo'dhyāyaḥ||25||
