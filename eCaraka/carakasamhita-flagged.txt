# Carakasaṃhitā: word spacing and verse tagging — spots to check

Changed afterwards (2026-11, the editor's decision): ityādi is now ity ādi in all its
cases, and ityantena is ity antena; fused lemmata are cety ādi, kaṭv ity ādi. What is
said below about ityādi / ityantena being joined describes the first round. The forms
quoted in the flagged lines below are as the agents wrote them (ityādi joined).

Word spacing was added in 2026-11 by 125 agents, one per piece of the TEI file
(tools/spacing/, see its README). Each agent reported up to five places it was
unsure of; they are listed below as "NNN l.LINE", where NNN is the piece and
LINE the line within that piece. Use the form quoted to find the place in
carakasamhita-full.xml (search for it; after the avagraha pass te'pi is te 'pi).

Decisions taken across the whole text (the same as for the Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā
and the Mādhavanidāna):
- the notes are not spaced.
- avagraha: a space before ' after e/o everywhere (te 'pi, so 'yaṃ); ā'
  (tathā'pi, vā'pi) stays joined.
- particles split everywhere: tathā ca, tathā hi, yad vā, na hi, na ca, na tu,
  yady api, ity arthaḥ, ity āha, yad uktaṃ, tad uktaṃ, tad yathā, kiñ ca, kiṃ vā …;
  joined: tadanu, tadanantaraṃ, ityādi and its cases, ityantena, kintu, parantu,
  athavā, itastataḥ.
- forms where the doubled consonant at a word junction is written once are left
  joined; typos are left uncorrected and are usually left joined.
- commentary lemmata (pratīka) with a fused vowel stay joined (yathetyādi); with
  a consonant junction they are split (kaṭv ityādi, tasmād ityādi).

Policy questions for the editor (decided provisionally as stated):
- kiñca is split kiñ ca (47 places), kiṃvā is split kiṃ vā (551 places);
  ityantena is kept joined (47 places). As in the Mādhavanidāna.
- itipadena / itipadaṃ ("by the word …") is mostly left joined (itipadena ×12,
  itipadaṃ ×7, itipadam ×2) but split in a few places (iti padena ×2, iti padaṃ
  ×5, iti padam ×1). Left as the agents had it; say which you prefer.
- punarukta- (the technical term) is mostly joined (punaruktaṃ ×10, punaruktam
  ×4), sometimes split (punar uktaṃ ×3); punarabhidhānam ×4 / punar abhidhānam ×3;
  prāgutpatti- ×5 / prāg utpatti- ×2; prāguktam ×2 / prāg uktam ×2. Left so.
- kin tarhi is split everywhere (the source writes kintarhi); it could stay
  joined like kintu.
- yatkiñcit ×5 / yat kiñcit ×8 is left as the agents had it.
- numerals before a noun: triṃśatpalāni ×4 / triṃśat palāni ×2, ṣaḍrasā /
  ṣaḍ rasā, ṣaḍudāvartā / ṣaḍ udāvartā. Left so.
- tat/tad/etad before a noun is split or joined by context (rule 5):
  tatsiddhaṃ ×5 / tat siddhaṃ ×1, tallakṣaṇaṃ ×3 / tal lakṣaṇaṃ ×2,
  tadbheṣajaṃ ×1 / tad bheṣajaṃ ×4, etaddvayam ×3 / etad dvayam ×3 and about
  ten other pairs occurring once each way.
- list items that may be dvandvas are treated differently in a few places:
  sarpistailaṃ ×1 / sarpis tailaṃ ×6, sarpirguḍā ×2 / sarpir guḍā ×1,
  rajastamaś / rajas tamaś, dadhyamlaṃ / dadhy amlaṃ, jvarastṛṣṇā / jvaras tṛṣṇā.

Root-text segments the verse tagger left as prose are listed in
carakasamhita-prose.txt: 2,563 segments, most of them the prose of the Caraka
(sūtras, lists, dialogue) and the chapter formulas and colophons; among them are
a few hundred single half-verses that do not scan as they stand (readings in
parentheses, hypermetric or defective lines).
