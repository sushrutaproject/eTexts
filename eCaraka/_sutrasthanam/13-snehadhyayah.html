--- title: "13. snehādhyāyaḥ" adhyaya: 13 order: 13 ---
athātaḥ snehādhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ||1|| iti ha smāha bhagavānātreyaḥ||2||
nirdeśavātakalākalīye vātādayo'bhihitāḥ, teṣāṃ bheṣajaṃ yathā kalpanīyaṃ tadupadeṣṭuṃ kalpanācatuṣko'bhidhīyate| bheṣajānāṃ kalpanā bheṣajakalpanā, sā ca kalpanā āśrayadravyābhidhānaṃ vinā na pāryate kalpayitum 1; ataḥ snehādidravyagocarā sneha-sveda-vamana-virecanakalpanehābhidhīyate, bastikalpanā tviha bahuvaktavyatvānnoktā| atrāpi vamanādipravṛttau snehasyaiva prathamaṃ vidhīyamānatayā tathā doṣapradhānasya vātasya pradhānabheṣajatvācca tatpratipādaka eva snehādhyāyo'bhidhīyate| snehasya pratipādako'dhyāyaḥ snehādhyāyaḥ||1-2||
sāṅkhyaiḥ saṅkhyātasaṅkhyeyaiḥ sahāsīnaṃ punarvasum| jagaddhitārthaṃ papraccha vahniveśaḥ svasaṃśayam||3||
saṅkhyā samyagjñānaṃ, tena vyavaharantīti sāṅkhyāḥ| saṅkhyātaṃ jñātaṃ saṅkhyeyaṃ jñeyaṃ yaiste tathā; yadi vā ‘saṅkhyātasaṅkhyeyam’ iti pāṭhaḥ, tadā punarvasuviśeṣaṇametat; arthastu samānaḥ| sāṅkhyaiḥ sahāvasthānopadarśanamātreyasya kartavyapraśnānuguṇamanaḥsamādhānopadarśanārtham| saṃśayamiti saṃśayena 2 viṣayiṇā viṣayaṃ lakṣayati, tena saṃśayamiti saṃśayaviṣayamityarthaḥ||3||
kiṃyonayaḥ kati snehāḥ ke ca snehaguṇāḥ pṛthak| kālānupāne ke kasya kati kāśca vicāraṇāḥ||4|| kati mātrāḥ kathammānāḥ kā ca keṣūpadiśyate| kaśca kebhyo hitaḥ snehaḥ prakarṣaḥ snehane ca kaḥ||5|| snehyāḥ ke ke na ca snigdhāsnigdhātisnigdhalakṣaṇam| kiṃ pānāt prathamaṃ pīte jīrṇe kiñca hitāhitam||6|| ke mṛdukrūrakoṣṭhāḥ kā vyāpadaḥ siddhayaśca kāḥ| acche saṃśodhane caiva snehe kā vṛttiriṣyate||7|| vicāraṇāḥ keṣu yojyā vidhinā kena tat prabho!| snehasyāmitavijñāna jñānamicchāmi veditum||8||
ko'sau saṃśaya ityāha- kiṃyonaya ityādi| kiṃyonayaḥ kimādhārakāraṇāḥ| kālaścānupānaṃ ca kālānupāne| vicāraṇā dravyāntarāsaṃyuktasnehapānaṃ varjayitvā snehopayogaḥ| kathammānā kīdṛṅmānā| kā ceti mātrā| prakarṣaḥ kālaprakarṣaḥ| snehane snehayuktikriyāyām| ke naceti ke na ca snehyāḥ| kiṃ pānāt snehapānāt prathamaṃ pūrvaṃ hitāhitaṃ; kiñca pīte snehe tathā jīrṇe ca snehe hitāhitamiti yojyam| siddhayo vyāpatsādhanāni bheṣajāni| acche iti saṃśodhanapṛthaṅnirdeśādgobalīvardanyāyena saṃśamana iti bhavati| vṛttiriti vṛttiḥ upacāravidhānam| jñānaṃ śāstram||4-8||
atha tatsaṃśayacchettā pratyuvāca punarvasuḥ| snehānāṃ dvividhā saumya yoniḥ sthāvarajaṅgamā||9|| tilaḥ priyālābhiṣukau bibhītakaścitrābhayairaṇḍamadhūkasarṣapāḥ| kusumbhabilvārukamūlakātasīnikocakākṣoḍakarañjaśigrukāḥ||10|| snehāśayāḥ sthāvarasañjñitāstathā syurjaṅgamā matsyamṛgāḥ sapakṣiṇaḥ| teṣāṃ dadhikṣīraghṛtāmiṣaṃ vasā sneheṣu majjā ca tathopadiśyate||11||
abhiṣuka auttarāpathikaḥ| citrā gorakṣakarkaṭī, tadbījamiha; yadi vā citrā lohitairaṇḍaḥ| atasī ‘umā’ iti khyātā 3| ārukanikocakākṣoḍā auttarāpathikāḥ| snehāśayāḥ snehasthānāni| ete cāviṣkṛtatamatvenoktāḥ, tena nimbatailādayo boddhavyāḥ| āmiṣaṃ māṃsam||9-11||
sarveṣāṃ tailajātānāṃ tilatailaṃ viśiṣyate| balārthe snehane cāgryamairaṇḍaṃ tu virecane||12|| (kaṭūṣṇaṃ tailamairaṇḍaṃ vātaśleṣmaharaṃ guru | kaṣāyasvādutiktaiśca yojitaṃ pittahantrapi ||1||)|
tailajātānāmiti jātaśabdaḥ prakāravacanaḥ; yathā- “yadāhārajātamagniveśa” (sū.a.25) ityādi| atra yadyapi yogāttilabhavameva tailaṃ, tathā'pi rūḍhyeha sarva eva sthāvarasnehāstailamityucyante| yaduktaṃ suśrute- “niṣpattestadguṇatvācca tailatvamitareṣvapi” (su.sū.a.45) iti| viśiṣyate atiricyate| agryamityairaṇḍena sambadhyate||12||
sarpistailaṃ vasā majjā sarvasnehottamā matāḥ| eṣu caivottamaṃ sarpiḥ saṃskārasyānuvartanāt||13||
sarvasnehottamā ityatra sarvaśabdena dadhikṣīrādayo gṛhyante; sarvasnehottamatvaṃ ca sarpirādīnāṃ snehaguṇaprakarṣavattvena| saṃskāro guṇāntarāropaṇaṃ, tasyānuvartanamanuvidhānaṃ svīkaraṇamiti yāvat| etaduktaṃ bhavati- yat- na tathā tailādayo dravyāntarasaṃskṛtāḥ saṃskāraguṇān vahanti yathā sarpiriti| ata evoktaṃ- “nānyaḥ snehastathā kaścit saṃskāramanuvartate| yathā sarpirataḥ sarpiḥ sarvasnehottamaṃ matam” (ni. a.1)| ata eva saṃskārakadravyacitrakādiguṇānuvidhāne'pi sarpirna svaguṇān snehaśaityādīn jahāti, kiñca svaguṇān tadguṇāṃśca vahati, yato'nuśabdena paścādvācinā svaguṇavartanasya paścāt saṃskārakaguṇavartanamucyate| ata evoktaṃ- “snehādvātaṃ śamayati pittaṃ mādhuryaśaityataḥ| ghṛtaṃ tulyaguṇaṃ doṣaṃ saṃskārāttu jayet kapham” (ni. a.1) iti| na ca vācyaṃ rūkṣoṣṇacitrakādisaṃskārādrūkṣoṣṇaṃ sarpirbhūtaṃ, tataśca sarpiṣaḥ snehaśaitye tadviruddhe kathaṃ tiṣṭhataḥ? iti; yataḥ sarpiḥsambaddhacitrakāvayavānugataṃ hi rūkṣoṣṇatvaṃ, sarpirgate ca snehaśaitye iti bhinnāśrayatvānna virodhaḥ; idameva ca sarpiṣaḥ saṃskārānuvartanaṃ yat- svaguṇaviruddhasyāpi tasyānupaghātena dhāraṇaṃ; saṃskārakacitrakādiguṇavahane'pi sarpiṣaḥ śaityādayaḥ kadācidabhibhūyante saṃskārakaguṇairuṣṇādibhirna punaḥ snehaguṇaḥ, tasya sneheṣu sarvātmanā vyavasthitasya prabalatvāt; ata eva sarpirādayaḥ snehapradhānatvāt ‘snehā’ ityucyante| anye tu saṃskārānuvartanaṃ svaguṇopaghātena saṃskārakaguṇavahanaṃ bruvate, etacca taile tiṣṭhati na sarpiṣīti vadanti; sarpiṣi tu saṃskārānuvartanamuktaṃ yat tat sarpiṣi sarvathā kasyacidguṇasya saṃskāraguṇenānupaghātādbhavati 4; tathāhi- dāhapraśamanārthaṃ jvare candanādiśītadravyasādhitatailamuktaṃ, yataḥ śītena sādhitaṃ tailamuṣṇamapi svabhāvācchītameva bhavatīti||13||
ghṛtaṃ pittānilaharaṃ rasaśukraujasāṃ hitam| nirvāpaṇaṃ mṛdukaraṃ svaravarṇaprasādanam||14|| mārutaghnaṃ na ca śleṣmavardhanaṃ balavardhanam| tvacyamuṣṇaṃ sthirakaraṃ tailaṃ yoniviśodhanam||15|| viddhabhagnāhatabhraṣṭayonikarṇaśiroruji| pauruṣopacaye snehe vyāyāme ceṣyate vasā||16|| balaśukrarasaśleṣmamedomajjavivardhanaḥ| majjā viśeṣato'sthnāṃ ca balakṛt snehane hitaḥ||17||
snehaguṇānabhidhatte- ghṛtamityādi| nirvāpaṇaṃ dāhapraśamanam| mārutaghnamityādi tailaguṇāḥ| sthirakaram aṅgasthairyakaram| viddhetyādi vasāguṇāḥ| pauruṣopacayaḥ śukropacayaḥ| snehe śarīrasnehane kartavye| balaśukretyādi majjaguṇāḥ||14-17||
sarpiḥ śaradi pātavyaṃ vasā majjā ca mādhave| tailaṃ prāvṛṣi nātyuṣṇaśīte snehaṃ pibennaraḥ||18||
sarpirādipānakālamāha- sarpiḥ śaradītyādi| mādhave vaiśākhe| prāvṛḍāṣāḍhaśrāvaṇau, yaduktaṃ- “prāvṛṭ śukranabhau jñeyau” (si.a.6) iti| śaradi bahupittatvena pittaviruddhaṃ ghṛtameva yaugikaṃ, nānyastailādiḥ| vasāmajjñostu nātiśītoṣṇatvāt sādhāraṇe; yatastayoranupāne śītatvena voṣṇatvena vā nirdeśo na kṛtaḥ; “yathāsattvaṃ tu śaityauṣṇye vasāmajjñorvinirdiśet” (sū.a.27) iti tu vacanena śaityauṣṇyābhidhānaṃ sāmānyena, tailasarpirvadauṣṇyaśaityānabhidhānānna prakarṣaprāptaśaityoṣṇatvaprāpakam| ata evoktaṃ- “tailavasāmajjasarpiṣāṃ tu yathāpūrvaṃ śreṣṭhatvaṃ vātaśleṣmavikāreṣu bhavati, yathottaraṃ pittavikāreṣu” (vi.a.8) iti| atrottarasya sarpiṣaḥ śaityāt pittaharatvaṃ, tailasya tūṣṇatvādvātaśleṣmaharatvaṃ, vasāmajjñostu sādhāraṇatvena madhyagatatvamiti sthitam| evaṃ vasāmajjñoḥ sādhāraṇatvena tathā balyatvadhātuvṛddhikaratvābhyāmanatiśītoṣṇe tathā balakṣayadhātukṣayayukte mādhave prayogo yuktaḥ| caitrastu mukhyasādhāraṇaguṇo'pi prabhūtaśleṣmatayā na snehaviṣaya ityanuktaḥ| sāmānyena snehopayoge śastaṃ kālamāha- nātyuṣṇetyādi| nātyuṣṇe grīṣme, nātiśīte hemante śiśire ca tathā varṣajanitaśaitye varṣākāle| ayaṃ cānātyayikavikāre sati kālaniyamo jñeyaḥ, agre uṣṇe śīte ca kāle snehopadeśāt||18||
vātapittādhiko rātrāvuṣṇe cāpi pibennaraḥ| śleṣmādhiko divā śīte pibeccāmalabhāskare||19||
kālaviśeṣe doṣaviśeṣe ca pānakramaṃ darśayati- vātetyādinā| vātaśca pittaṃ ca vātapittaṃ, tadadhiko vātapittādhikaḥ| rātrāviti sāyam| uṣṇe grīṣme| śleṣmādhikagrahaṇamatyantaśītavikāragṛhītapuruṣopalakṣaṇārthaṃ, tena vātaśleṣmādhikaḥ śleṣmādhikaśca gṛhyate; ata eva suśrute'pyuktaṃ- “vātapittādhiko rātrau vātaśleṣmādhiko divā” (su.ci.31) iti| kevalavātādhikasya tathā pittādhikasya śleṣmādhikasya ca sādhāraṇe śaradādau kāle utsargasiddha eva pānakālo vakṣyamāṇo bhavati| vakṣyati hi- “pibet saṃśamanaṃ snehamannakāle” ityādi| anye tu bruvate- vātādhikagrahaṇena kevalavātasyāpi grahaṇaṃ, śleṣmādhikavyapadeśācca vātaśleṣma-pittaśleṣmaṇorapi grahaṇamiti| śīte hemantādau| amalaḥ prabalaraśmiryasmin dinasya bhāge bhāskaraḥ so'malabhāskaro madhyāhna iti yāvat||19||
atyuṣṇe vā divā pīto vātapittādhikena vā| mūrcchāṃ pipāsāmunmādaṃ kāmalāṃ vā samīrayet||20|| śīte rātrau piban snehaṃ naraḥ śleṣmādhiko'pi vā| ānāhamaruciṃ śūlaṃ pāṇḍutāṃ vā samṛcchati||21||
uktakālaniyamaviparyaye doṣamāha- atyuṣṇe vetyādi| atyuṣṇe kāle divā pītaḥ snehaḥ ślaiṣmikasyāpi yathoktavikārakaraḥ, tathā vātapittādhikena pītaḥ śītakāle'pi yathoktavikārakaraḥ; evaṃ niśāpāne'pi vākyārthaḥ; paraṃ niṣiddhasya kālasya doṣasya ca melake'tyaye coktavikāraprakarṣāprakarṣau tarkaṇīyau||20-21||
jalamuṣṇaṃ ghṛte peyaṃ yūṣastaile'nu śasyate| vasāmajjñostu maṇḍaḥ syāt sarveṣūṣṇamathāmbu vā||22||
anupānamāha- jalamityādi| taile'nviti anupāne| anupānaparimāṇaṃ tu samyagbheṣajapākārthaṃ kriyamāṇaṃ bheṣajāvaikārikapākenaivonneyaṃ, vṛddhavaidyavyavahārācca||22||
odanaśca vilepī ca raso māṃsaṃ payo dadhi| yavāgūḥ sūpaśākau ca yūṣaḥ kāmbalikaḥ khaḍaḥ||23|| saktavastilapiṣṭaṃ ca madyaṃ lehāstathaiva ca| bhakṣyamabhyañjanaṃ bastistathā cottarabastayaḥ||24|| gaṇḍūṣaḥ karṇatailaṃ ca nastaḥkarṇākṣitarpaṇam| caturviṃśatirityetāḥ snehasya pravicāraṇāḥ 5||25||
vicāraṇāḥ saṅkhyayā svarūpeṇa caikagranthenāha- odanaścetyādi| vilepī viraladravā yavāgūrbahusikthasamanvitā jñeyā| saśākapallavena kṛto yūṣaḥ khaḍaḥ| kāmbaliko dadhilavaṇasnehatilādikṛta īṣadamlaḥ| etayorudāharaṇaṃ yathā- “takre kapitthacāṅgerīmaricājājicitrakaiḥ 6| supakvaḥ khaḍayūṣo'yamayaṃ kāmbaliko mataḥ|| dadhyamlo lavaṇasnehatilamāṣānvitaḥ śṛtaḥ” iti| lehaḥ śarkarādīnāṃ pākāt kṛtaḥ; lihyata iti lehaḥ| pravicāryate avacāryate'nukalpenopayujyate'nayeti pravicāraṇā odanādayaḥ; odanādayaśca snehapravicāraṇāyāṃ snehayuktā eva boddhavyāḥ; abhyañjanādayastu yadyapi śuddhasnehasampādyāstathā'pi jaṭharāgnisambandhe na vyāpriyanta iti tantre vicāraṇāśabdenocyante||23-25||
acchapeyastu yaḥ sneho na tāmāhurvicāraṇām| snehasya sa bhiṣagdṛṣṭaḥ 7 kalpaḥ prāthamakalpikaḥ||26||
kevalasnehapānaṃ tu snehane śaktyatiśayavattvena na vicāraṇāsañjñayocyate, etadevāha- acchapeya ityādi| acchaśca peyaśca acchapeyaḥ, odanādyasambandhe sati peya ityarthaḥ| na tāmāhurvicāraṇāmiti vacanena vaidyaparamparāsiddho'yaṃ vyavahāra iti darśayati| bhiṣagbhirdṛṣṭo bhiṣagdṛṣṭaḥ| prathame śreṣṭhe kalpe pakṣe bhavatīti prāthamakalpikaḥ, śreṣṭha ityarthaḥ||26||
rasaiścopahitaḥ snehaḥ samāsavyāsayogibhiḥ| ṣaḍbhistriṣaṣṭidhā saṅkhyāṃ prāpnotyekaśca kevalaḥ||27|| evametāścatuḥṣaṣṭiḥ snehānāṃ pravicāraṇā 8| okartuvyādhipuruṣān prayojyā jānatā bhavet||28||
prakārāntareṇa vicāraṇābhedamāha- rasaiścetyādi| samāso rasānāmanyonyamelakaḥ, vyāso'melakaḥ; samāsavyāsavadbhiḥ ṣaḍbhī rasairodanādigatairupahito yuktaḥ san snehastriṣaṣṭisaṅkhyāṃ prāpnoti| ekaśca kevala ityacchapeyaṃ varjayitvā'bhyañjanādiprayojyaḥ| triṣaṣṭhī rasabhedā ātreyabhadrakāpyīye “svāduramlādibhiryogaṃ” (sū.a.26) ityādivākye vakṣyamāṇā boddhavyāḥ| evaṃ snehānāṃ rasayuktānāṃ catuḥṣaṣṭiḥ pravicāraṇā bhavanti| catuḥṣaṣṭiriti snehānāmityanena sambadhyate, tena pravicāraṇetyekavacanamupapannaṃ bhavati| na sarvavicāraṇā sarvatra kartavyā, kintu sātmyartuvyādhidoṣapuruṣān parīkṣya yā yatra yujyate sā tatra kartavyetyāha- okartvityādi| okaḥ abhyāsaḥ| puruṣagrahaṇena kasmin deśe'yaṃ puruṣo vartata iti parīkṣayā deśo'pyavaruddho boddhavyaḥ, vayobalaprabhṛtayaśca boddhavyāḥ||27-28||
ahorātramahaḥ kṛtsnamardhāhaṃ ca pratīkṣate| pradhānā madhyamā hrasvā snehamātrā jarāṃ prati||29|| iti tisraḥ samuddiṣṭā mātrāḥ snehasya mānataḥ| tāsāṃ prayogān vakṣyāmi puruṣaṃ puruṣaṃ prati||30|| prabhūtasnehanityā ye kṣutpipāsāsahā narāḥ| pāvakaścottamabalo yeṣāṃ ye cottamā bale||31|| gulminaḥ sarpadaṣṭāśca visarpopahatāśca ye| unmattāḥ kṛcchramūtrāśca gāḍhavarcasa eva ca||32|| pibeyuruttamāṃ mātrāṃ tasyāḥ pāne guṇāñchṛṇu| vikārāñchamayatyeṣāṃ śīghraṃ samyakprayojitā||33|| doṣānukarṣiṇī mātrā sarvamārgānusāriṇī| balyā punarnavakarī śarīrendriyacetasām||34|| aruṣkasphoṭapiḍakākaṇḍūpāmābhirarditāḥ| kuṣṭhinaśca pramīḍhāśca vātaśoṇitikāśca ye||35|| nātibahvāśinaścaiva mṛdukoṣṭhāstathaiva ca| pibeyurmadhyamāṃ mātrāṃ madhyamāścāpi ye bale||36|| mātraiṣā mandavibhraṃśā na cātibalahāriṇī| sukhena ca snehayati śodhanārthe ca yujyate||37|| ye tu vṛddhāśca bālāśca sukumārāḥ sukhocitāḥ| riktakoṣṭhatvamahitaṃ yeṣāṃ mandāgnayaśca ye||38|| jvarātīsārakāsāśca yeṣāṃ cirasamutthitāḥ| snehamātrāṃ pibeyuste hrasvāṃ ye cāvarā bale||39|| parihāre sukhā caiṣā mātrā snehanabṛṃhaṇī| vṛṣyā balyā nirābādhā ciraṃ cāpyanuvartate||40||
mātrāyāḥ saṅkhyāṃ pramāṇaṃ cāha- ahorātramityādi| ahorātraśabdo'ṣṭapraharopalakṣaṇaḥ, evamahaḥśabdārdhāhaśabdau catuḥpraharadvipraharopalakṣaṇau, tena praharādyatīte'pyahani pītā mātrā yathoktapraharakālaprāptyā dināntare rātrau vā jīryamāṇā mantavyā; anye tvahorātraśabdena nyūnenāpyahnā yuktā rātrirahorātraśabdenocyate 9, kṛtsnārdhāhau tu praharopalakṣaṇāviti vadanti| atra yadā ahorātrapariṇāminī mātrā kriyate, tadā tadaharāhāro na kartavyaḥ; ata evaitatpānaṃ puruṣaviśeṣaviṣayakathane uktaṃ- “kṣutpipāsāsahānarāḥ” iti| prabhūtasnehanityāḥ prabhūtasnehasātmyāḥ| samyagyo jitetivacanena mahāvyāpattitvamasyāḥ sūcayati| sarvamārgāḥ koṣṭhasandhimarmaśākhāḥ| punarnavakarī niḥśeṣadoṣaharatvena| aruṣketyādi madhyamamātrāguṇaḥ; aruṣkā arūṃṣikā| śodhanārtha iti śodhanārtha(ṅga)snehakaraṇe; etena uttamamātrā saṃśamane 10 eva paraṃ, na tu śodhanāṅgasnehe kartavyeti darśayati| ye tvityādi hrasvamātrāguṇaḥ| parihāre sukheti alpaparihāratvena||29-40||
vātapittaprakṛtayo vātapittavikāriṇaḥ| cakṣuḥkāmāḥ kṣatāḥ kṣīṇā vṛddhā bālāstathā'balāḥ||41|| āyuḥprakarṣakāmāśca balavarṇasvarārthinaḥ| puṣṭikāmāḥ prajākāmāḥ saukumāryārthinaśca ye||42|| dīptyojaḥsmṛtimedhāgnibuddhīndriyabalārthinaḥ| pibeyuḥ sarpirārtāśca dāhaśastraviṣāgnibhiḥ||43||
